# Evidence & Design Ledger: Don's Restaurant

## Creative Brief
- **Client / Business:** Don's Restaurant (Don's Charlotte)
- **Address:** 321 N Caldwell St, Charlotte, NC 28202 (Uptown Charlotte)
- **Phone:** (704) 376-2909
- **Email:** info@donscharlotte.com
- **Operating Hours:** Wednesday - Thursday: 5:00 PM - 11:00 PM; Friday: 5:00 PM - 1:00 AM; Saturday: 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Brunch), 5:00 PM - 1:00 AM (Dinner / Lounge); Sunday: 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Soulful Sunday Brunch); Monday - Tuesday: Closed (Available for Private Buyouts)
- **Concept & Cuisine:** Upscale Southern cuisine, high-energy weekend soul brunch, and craft cocktail lounge located in Uptown Charlotte's historic First Ward. Renowned for elevated Southern classics including jumbo shrimp & stone-ground cheddar grits, crispy buttermilk chicken & signature red velvet waffles, creamy collard green dip, crispy salmon bites, soulful seafood gumbo, whole crispy red snapper, and smoked artisanal craft cocktails.
- **Design Philosophy:** Midnight luxury lounge aesthetics combining deep midnight navy, rich burnished copper, champagne gold accents, and warm alabaster stone. High-contrast editorial typography, refined card layouts, interactive VIP bottle and dining planner, and zero emojis.

## Claim Ledger
- **Elevated Southern Gastronomy:** Classic comfort dishes reimagined through fine-dining technique, including 24-hour buttermilk brined poultry, scratch-made andouille roux, stone-ground Carolina grits, and hand-cut salmon bites.
- **Signature Red Velvet Waffles:** Made-to-order Belgian pearl sugar red velvet waffles served with organic bourbon-infused maple syrup and whipped sweet cream butter.
- **Craft Mixology & Spirits Vault:** Custom cocktail program featuring smoked bourbon old fashioneds, house-infused lavender gin fizzes, fresh blackberry lemon drop martinis, and curated agave selections.
- **Soulful Weekend Brunch:** Live jazz and R&B brunch experience every Saturday and Sunday in Uptown Charlotte with mimosa towers and signature breakfast platters.
