Guest Dress Code & Policies

  • Attire: Upscale casual / smart evening wear requested. Athletic wear, sleeveless shirts on gentlemen, and beachwear are not permitted in the dining room.
  • Age Policy: Family-friendly during brunch and early dinner. 21+ in the lounge after 10:00 PM on Friday and Saturday evenings.
  • Seating Grace Period: Reserved tables are held for 15 minutes past the reservation time.

Arrival, Parking & Valet

  • Valet Service: Complimentary valet drop-off available Friday and Saturday evenings at the main Caldwell Street entrance.
  • Street & Deck Parking: Metered street parking available along N Caldwell Street and E 7th Street, plus nearby public parking decks within two blocks.
  • Rideshare Drop-off: Dedicated passenger loading zone located directly outside our front entrance.
