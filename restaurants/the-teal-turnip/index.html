Fine Dining in a T-Shirt.

Welcome to The Teal Turnip, Chef Taylor Kastl and Steve McGinley’s whimsical culinary sanctuary in Charlotte’s Oakhurst neighborhood. We serve playful, pun-filled small plates grounded in rigorous French technique—from sage-seared duck breast with blackberry gastrique to Cajun-grilled Scottish salmon and bourbon cola chicken—in an intimate, adults-only atmosphere.
