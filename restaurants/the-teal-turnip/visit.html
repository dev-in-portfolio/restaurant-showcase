Dining Sanctuary

Adults-Only Atmosphere Directives

To preserve our intimate environment and intentional dining rhythm:

  • Age Policy: The Teal Turnip operates as an adults-only establishment. All seated guests must be 18 years of age or older (21+ for bar seating).
  • Reservations: Given the intimate scale of our dining room, table reservations are strongly encouraged, especially for Friday and Saturday evening seatings.
  • Dining Pace: We recommend planning 90 to 120 minutes for your dinner experience to allow our multi-course small plates and craft cocktails to unfold at a leisurely rhythm.
Midweek Sessions

Tuesday Live Music & Bar Bites

Experience our relaxed Tuesday evening format:

  • Hours & Format: Every Tuesday from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM, we host local Charlotte acoustic and jazz musicians in our bar and lounge.
  • Menu: A curated selection of small plate bar snacks, warm shareables, and weekly feature bourbon highballs.
  • Seating: Bar and lounge tables are seated on a casual, walk-in basis with no cover charge.
