Kitchen Manifesto

The Foundations of Our Kitchen

Farm relationships, culinary integrity, and joyful execution.

Tenet 01

Five Seasonal Menus

Rather than forcing out-of-season produce from thousands of miles away, our kitchen rewrites our offerings five times a year to honor the natural cycles of Carolina growers.

Tenet 02

French Discipline & Searing

Every score on our duck breast, every house gastrique reduction, and every velvety vegetable purée reflects classical French technique executed with contemporary boldness.

Tenet 03

Unpretentious Humor

Food is meant to bring joy. By naming dishes "Duck Season", "I Yam What I Yam", and "I'm Having a Lentil Breakdown", we invite our guests to smile before their first bite.

Signature Dish Breakdown

The Anatomy of "Duck Season"

Our sage-seared duck breast represents the zenith of our culinary balance. We begin with a cross-hatch diamond score along the duck breast skin to render out fat and create a crackling crispy exterior while preserving a tender, succulent medium-rare interior. It is plated over a velvety roasted parsnip purée, caramelized leek rounds, fresh microgreens, and a glossy reduction of local wild blackberries and aged vinegar.
