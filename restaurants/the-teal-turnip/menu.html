Five Seasonal Rotations • Carolina Farm Sourced

Whimsical Flavors, Serious Technique.

Chef Taylor Kastl and Steve McGinley craft dishes that celebrate the finest regional Carolina ingredients through unexpected flavor pairings, pun-filled dish names, and flawless culinary execution. We recommend 2 to 3 small plates per guest for a complete tasting journey.

Signature Chef Small Plates

Playful culinary creations highlighting prime proteins and complex reductions.

Sage-seared duck breast scored and cooked medium-rare, velvety roasted parsnip purée, caramelized confit leek towers, micro-sorrel, and tart house blackberry gastrique.

"I'm Having a Lentil Breakdown" $21.00

Blackened Scottish salmon fillet cast-iron seared crisp, resting over slow-simmered French green lentil and chorizo chili, wilted baby spinach, and citrus herb emulsion.

"Belly Up to the Bar" $17.50

Mesquite-smoked crispy pork belly square, charred kohlrabi noodle salad, toasted benne sesame seeds, and sticky bourbon sorghum glaze.

"Don't You Wish Your Crab Dip was Hot Like Me" $16.50

"I Yam What I Yam" $13.00

Crisp dry-rubbed chicken drumsticks tossed in a reduced bourbon Mexican Coca-Cola sticky glaze, fresh scallions, and toasted benne sesame seeds.

Bourbon Library & Craft Cocktails

The Turnip Old Fashioned $15.00

Smoked Cherry Manhattan $15.00
