# Creative Design Brief: Intermezzo Pizzeria

## 1. Restaurant Identity & Intelligence
- **Name**: Intermezzo Pizzeria
- **Cuisine**: Artisanal Brick-Oven Pizzeria & Italian Table
- **Tagline**: Authentic Artisanal Brick-Oven Pizzeria & Italian Table in Charlotte, NC
- **Location**: Charlotte Metro Region, NC

## 2. Three Concept Directions Evaluated

### Concept A: Bespoke Artisanal Brick-Oven Pizzeria & Italian Table Identity (SELECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Bespoke Artisanal Brick-Oven Pizzeria & Italian Table aesthetics. Primary accent `#6366f1`, deep background `#0b1329`.
- **Typography**: Display font `'Syne', sans-serif` paired with `'Inter', sans-serif`.
- **Signature Feature**: Intermezzo Pizzeria Interactive Flavor Explorer.
- **Header Strategy**: Brand badge + adaptive navigation drawer activating at 1024px to prevent horizontal crowding.

### Concept B: Contemporary Urban Dining Concept (REJECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Rejected for lack of distinct regional identity.

### Concept C: Classic Neighborhood Gathering Space (REJECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Rejected as too generic for Intermezzo Pizzeria.

## 3. Art Direction Rationale
- **Color Logic**: Accent `#6366f1` for callouts with `#0b1329` background.
- **Typography Logic**: Hierarchy using `'Syne', sans-serif` for titles and `'Inter', sans-serif` for legibility.
- **Responsive Navigation**: Adaptive breakpoint at 1024px tested across 1440px to 360px with zero overflow.
