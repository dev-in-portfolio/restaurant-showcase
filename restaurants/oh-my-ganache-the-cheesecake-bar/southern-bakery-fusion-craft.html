🥧 Soulful Southern Bakery Fusion

Sweet Potato Pie Meets Cheesecake

Our signature Sweet Potato Pie Cheesecake took Charlotte by storm. By uniting the velvety tang of classic New York cheesecake with the rich, nutmeg-spiced warmth of heirloom Southern sweet potato pie, we created an unforgettable Southern confectionery icon.

Southern Heritage

The 3 Pillars of Southern Bakery Fusion

1. Heirloom Sweet Potato Puree

Roasting Carolina sweet potatoes and blending with cinnamon, nutmeg, Madagascar vanilla, and brown sugar for a decadent top layer.

2. Spiced Peach Cobbler Bread Pudding

Soaking buttery bakery brioche in sweet cream and baking with caramelized peaches and crispy cobbler crust toppings.

3. Bourbon Salted Caramel Glaze

Simmering pure cane sugar and butter with Kentucky bourbon and sea salt for an aromatic, buttery dessert crown.
