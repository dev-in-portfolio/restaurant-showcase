The Art of the Cheesecake Bar

At Oh My Ganache, we believe dessert should be as expressive and individual as you are. Our custom cheesecake bar lets you design your ultimate confection—starting from slow water-bath-baked vanilla custard bases and topping with warm Belgian ganache, scratch fruit compotes, and crunchy crumbles.

Confectionery Pillars

The 3 Pillars of Oh My Ganache Custom Slices

1. Water-Bath Slow Baking

Baking every whole cheesecake gently in steam water-baths to achieve an ultra-smooth, silky texture without cracks or dry edges.

2. Small-Batch Belgian Ganache

Melting premium Belgian dark, milk, and white chocolate with fresh heavy cream and sea salt for rich, glossy dessert drizzles.

3. Scratch Southern Toppings

Simmering bourbon caramel apples, strawberry compotes, cinnamon oat streusel, and strawberry shortcake crunch fresh daily.
