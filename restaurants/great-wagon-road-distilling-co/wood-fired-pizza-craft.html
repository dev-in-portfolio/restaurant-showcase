Neapolitan Wood Oven Craft

Great whiskey deserves great pizza. At Great Wagon Road Distilling Co., our sourdough pizza dough undergoes 48-hour cold fermentation before baking in our 800°F wood-fired oven fueled by local oak logs, creating blistered crusts & bubbly cheese.

Oven Craft Science

The 3 Pillars of GWR Wood-Fired Pizza

1. 48-Hour Sourdough Ferment

Long fermentation creates a light, digestible crust with complex tang & airy leoparding structure.

2. 800°F Hardwood Flame Bake

Oak hardwood embers blast pizzas at 800°F, cooking pies in under 90 seconds to lock in fresh tomato moisture.

3. Artisanal Toppings & Hot Honey

Imported San Marzano tomatoes, fresh fior di latte mozzarella, spicy soppressata, & local wildflower hot honey.
