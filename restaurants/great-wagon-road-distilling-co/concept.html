NoDa Craft Distillery Roots

Great Wagon Road Distilling Co. was founded at 610 Anderson St to honor generations of Irish moonshining & whiskey distilling history. Named after the historic Great Wagon Road that brought Irish settlers south, we bring authentic pot still craft to Charlotte's NoDa neighborhood.
