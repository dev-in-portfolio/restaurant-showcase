## Original Audit Weakness

Great Wagon Road Distilling Co. relies on basic event listings and third-party distillery tour pages. Located at 610 Anderson St in NoDa, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its 100% malted barley copper pot distillation process, wood-fired pizza kitchen, Saturday tour booking directives, and private barrel room event hosting.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **NoDa Arts District Location:** 610 Anderson St, Charlotte NC 28205 (NoDa Arts District / Anderson St Corridor).
2. **Irish-Heritage Craft Spirits:** RUAH Single Malt Irish Whiskey, Salamander Vodka, Bán Poitín, & Handcrafted Distillery Cocktails.
3. **Wood-Fired Neapolitan Pizza & Tours:** Wood-Fired Soppressata & Honey Pizza, Margherita Pizza, Warm Pretzel Bites, & Saturday Public Distillery Tours.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Industrial Copper Display Serif Typography:** Industrial copper serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and barrel mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Copper Charcoal & Irish Emerald Palette:** High-energy distillery palette anchored in copper charcoal (`#14110F`), oak copper bronze (`#B45309`), Irish emerald (`#15803D`), flame gold (`#F59E0B`), and warm malt parchment (`#FFFBEB`).
3. **"The Copper Pot Still & Wood-Fired Oven Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`distillery-pot-still-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing copper-distilled spirits alongside wood-fired Neapolitan pizzas & handcrafted cocktails.

- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **GWR Vault Cards** (`gwr-vault-card`) and NoDa craft highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`gwr-header`, `gwr-brand`, `copper-hero-stage`, `flame-stamp-badge`, `gwr-vault-card`, `gwr-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
