🥃 100% Malted Barley Distillation

The Science of Copper Pot Stills

Distilling is an art form rooted in Irish history. At Great Wagon Road Distilling Co., we craft RUAH Single Malt Irish Whiskey using 100% malted barley, open fermentation, & hand-hammered copper pot stills that remove unwanted sulfur to deliver unmatched smoothness.

Distillation Science

The 3 Pillars of GWR Pot Still Craft

1. 100% Malted Barley Mash

We source premium malted barley, mashing & fermenting in small open-top vessels to develop deep cereal sweetness.

2. Copper Pot Catalytic Reflex

Our tall copper swan neck stills maximize vapor interaction, stripping harsh congeners while capturing delicate floral esters.

3. NoDa Charred Oak Maturation

Spirits are barrel-aged in charred virgin American white oak casks, absorbing vanilla, caramel, & oak tannins over time.
