The Art of Calzones & Pasta

Authentic Italian comfort food goes beyond the pizza wheel. At Lorenzo's Pizzeria, our kitchen hand-stuffs calzones with seasoned ricotta & mozzarella, rolls savory stromboli with Italian meats, & bakes rich ziti casseroles golden brown.

The 3 Pillars of Italian Kitchen Tradition

1. Hand-Folded Calzone Dough

Fresh pizza dough stuffed with seasoned ricotta, mozzarella, & ham, vented & baked golden.

2. Rolled Meat Stromboli

Pepperoni, Italian sausage, & peppers rolled tight with cheese & sliced into hot savory spirals.

3. Classic Baked Casseroles

Penne tossed with ricotta marinara, covered in mozzarella, & baked until bubbly & golden.
