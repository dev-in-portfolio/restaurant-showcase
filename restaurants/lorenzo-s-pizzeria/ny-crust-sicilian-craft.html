🍕 NY Hand-Tossing & Sicilian Pan Proofing

The Art of NY & Sicilian Crust

Great NY pizza requires master dough handling. At Lorenzo's Pizzeria, we hand-toss every dough ball to create thin, foldable slices with crisp undercarriages. For our Sicilian deep dish, we proof dough in steel pans for 4 hours to yield an airy interior & caramelized cheese crust.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Lorenzo’s Pizza Artistry

1. High-Gluten Flour Fermentation

Spring wheat dough fermented 36 hours for crispness, stretchability, & signature flavor.

2. Stone Deck Heat Conductivity

Direct deck baking browning the bottom crust for true NY foldability without sagging.

3. Sicilian Pan Caramelization

Olive oil pan proofing baking a thick crunchy perimeter around airy sponge crust.
