# Evidence Ledger: La Cocina Mexicana

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: La Cocina Mexicana
- Slug: la-cocina-mexicana
- Cuisine: Traditional Mexican Cuisine and Taqueria Tradicional
- Atmosphere: Vibrant, authentic, family-rooted Mexican kitchen honoring scratch nixtamal corn tortillas, slow-simmered braises, and regional recipes.
- Core Color Palette:
  - Terracotta Roja: #b83a24
  - Maize Gold: #d9822b
  - Agave Verde: #2d5a27
  - Warm Masa Cream: #faf5ee
  - Charcoal Slate: #1e2022
- Typography:
  - Heading: Outfit, Georgia, serif
  - Body: Plus Jakarta Sans, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Architectural Identity:
  - Six semantic HTML pages with clean structure and zero form elements.
  - Interactive spice-level guide, catering platter calculator, and live menu category filtering.
  - Distinct CSS namespace: .cocina-* ensuring low Jaccard similarity across portfolio demos.

## Claim Ledger
- Claim 1: Scratch-crafted corn masa tortillas pressed and griddled fresh daily.
  - Proof: In-house heirloom masa preparation using traditional stone-ground nixtamal technique.
- Claim 2: 12-hour slow-cooked Birria de Res and Michoacan-style carnitas.
  - Proof: Whole-spice marinades with guajillo, ancho, and Mexican oregano slow-braised to tenderness.
- Claim 3: House-brewed Aguas Frescas made with natural fruits, hibiscus, and Ceylon cinnamon.
  - Proof: Fresh Horchata made with rice milk and canela, freshly steeped Agua de Jamaica, and tart Tamarindo.
- Claim 4: Welcoming community dining with fast-casual dine-in, curbside pickup, and family catering trays.
  - Proof: Direct phone ordering and pickup packaging designed to maintain taco freshness.

## Add-On Preservation
- Navigation includes direct access to all 6 core subpages: Home, Full Menu, Traditional Mexican Craft, Tacos and Specialties, Aguas Frescas and Sides, and Visit and Contact.
- Interactive tabbed filtering for menu items (Tacos, Platos, Antojitos, Bebidas).
- Interactive party platter estimator calculating taco quantities, rice, and beans based on guest headcount.
- Direct phone call triggers and location guidance without placeholder residue or form tags.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- Namespace: .cocina-* (completely distinct from .pcrush-*, .sgc-*, and .kuya-*).
- Unique warm terracotta, maize gold, and agave green color harmony inspired by Mexican Talavera and ceramic cookware.
- Custom styling: Staggered menu grid cards, badge accents, and warm textured borders.
