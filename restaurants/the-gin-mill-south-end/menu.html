Scratch Tavern Kitchen • Craft Drafts

Burgers, Wings, Ice Cold Drafts.

From lunchtime burgers to late-night post-concert cravings at Amos' Southend, our kitchen prepares honest, hearty American tavern classics from scratch. Savor certified angus beef smash patties, jumbo hickory-smoked wings, golden tavern tots, and fresh-baked flatbreads.

Scratch Burgers, Smoked Wings & Rooftop Taps

Signature Angus Burgers & Handhelds

Served on toasted brioche with seasoned house fries. Sub tots or side salad for $2.

"The Mill Burger" $15.00

Two fresh angus smash patties, melted sharp cheddar cheese, applewood bacon onion jam, house tavern sauce, crisp shredded lettuce, and seasoned fries.

"Psycho Burger" $16.00

Fresh angus patty, ghost pepper jack cheese, crispy jalapeño chips, spicy chipotle aioli, lettuce, and ripe tomato on a toasted brioche bun with fries.

Southern Fried Chicken Sandwich $14.50

Buttermilk brined crispy fried chicken breast, crinkle dill pickles, house honey mustard slaw, and chipotle ranch drizzle on toasted brioche.

Smoked Meatloaf Sourdough Melt $15.00

Hickory smoked meatloaf cut thick, melted provolone cheese, caramelized sweet onions, and smoky tavern BBQ sauce on grilled country sourdough.

Jumbo Smoked Wings & Starters

Slow-smoked in-house and flash-fried to crispy perfection.

Jumbo Smoked Wings (8pc) $15.50

Dry-rubbed hickory smoked wings, flash-fried crispy. Choice of Buffalo, Carolina Gold BBQ, Garlic Parmesan, or Mango Habanero with celery and blue cheese.

Loaded Tavern Tots $12.00

Golden crispy tater tots smothered in warm craft beer cheese, applewood bacon crumbles, pickled jalapeño rings, scallions, and spicy jalapeño crema.

Southwest Chicken Eggrolls $11.50

Crispy golden wonton rolls packed with blackened chicken breast, black beans, sweet roasted corn, and pepper jack cheese, served with avocado ranch dip.

Soft Bavarian Pretzel Batons $10.50

Three warm salted Bavarian pretzel sticks served with warm house ale cheese fondue and coarse grain spicy brown mustard.

Oven-Fired Tavern Flatbreads

Baked hot and crisp with melted whole-milk cheeses.

Buffalo Chicken Flatbread $14.00

Crispy flatbread crust, shredded buffalo chicken breast, melted mozzarella, crumbled gorgonzola, fresh scallions, and house buttermilk ranch swirl.

"Meat Sweets" Flatbread $14.50

San Marzano marinara base, savory Italian sausage, cupping pepperoni, applewood bacon bits, shredded mozzarella, and hot honey drizzle.

Draft Taps & Rooftop Cocktails

Poured fresh on both the ground floor tavern and the rooftop sky bar.

The Gin Mill G&T $13.00

Hendrick's Scottish gin, Fever-Tree Mediterranean tonic water, fresh green lime wedge, and aromatic cucumber ribbon over crystal columnar ice.

South End Copper Mule $12.50

Tito's Handmade Vodka, spicy craft ginger beer, freshly squeezed lime juice, and fresh garden mint in a frosted copper mug.

Carolina Craft Beer Flight $14.00

Four 5-oz tasting pours of rotating North Carolina craft drafts—featuring South End IPAs, crisp lagers, ciders, and seasonal ales.

Rooftop Berry Bourbon Smash $13.50

Elijah Craig Small Batch bourbon, muddled blackberries, fresh mint leaves, lemon juice, and simple syrup served on the rocks.
