Second-Story Open-Air Terrace • South Tryon

Skyline Views, Open-Air Pours.

Perched above South Tryon Street, The Gin Mill’s expansive second-floor rooftop deck is one of South End’s favorite open-air destinations. Complete with a dedicated full-service rooftop bar, string lighting, high-definition sports screens, and communal tables, it offers prime vistas of the Uptown skyline and South End energy.

Full-Service Sky Bar

Feature 02

Take in sweeping sightlines of Uptown Charlotte’s towering skyline as the sun sets and the city lights reflect across the South Tryon district.

All-Weather Comfort
