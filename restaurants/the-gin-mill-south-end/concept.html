1423 South Tryon • Established 1997

Pioneering Spirit, South End Roots.

Before South End was filled with sleek glass high-rises and trendy boutiques, The Gin Mill was here pouring cold drafts, grilling burgers, and serving as the neighborhood’s beloved living room. Established in 1997, our tavern has grown with Charlotte—expanding to include our signature rooftop deck while preserving the unpretentious warmth that makes us legendary.

Core Values

The Soul of The Gin Mill

Authentic community, scratch hospitality, and Queen City pride.

Value 01

Neighborhood Anchor

We are proud to be a genuine neighborhood tavern where newcomers and longtime Charlotteans share the same bar rail and feel instantly at home.

Value 02

Scratch Craftsmanship

Even in a high-volume pub environment, we insist on scratch cooking—grinding beef, smoking wings in-house, and hand-crafting sauces every day.

Value 03

Live Entertainment Synergy

Partnered in spirit with Amos' Southend next door, we keep South End's rich live music and nightlife tradition alive with every draft and pre-show toast.

Live Music Nexus

In the Shadow of Amos' Southend

For decades, Amos' Southend has hosted some of rock, indie, and hip-hop's most iconic touring acts. Situated directly in front of the venue at 1423 South Tryon, The Gin Mill serves as the undisputed pre-show and post-show headquarters for music lovers across the region.
