Entertainment Headquarters

Game Day Watch Parties & Weekly Events

What to expect during live entertainment and game days at The Gin Mill:

  • Panthers & Charlotte FC: Stadium sound on, full bar service across both floors, and game day wing and beer bucket specials.
  • Tuesday Trivia: Live team trivia every Tuesday evening with prizes, drink specials, and scratch pub bites.
  • Weekend Rooftop DJs: Rotating Charlotte DJs spinning on the rooftop deck Friday and Saturday nights from 10:00 PM to close.
Neighborhood Logistics

Transit, Parking & Amos' Southend Access

Navigating arrival and concert nights in South End:

  • Amos' Southend Shows: The Gin Mill is positioned directly in front of the venue gates—making it the ideal meeting point before doors open and for post-show celebrations.
  • Parking Options: Paid public surface parking lots and parking decks are available nearby on South Tryon, Camden Road, and at The RailYard deck.
  • Rideshare: Direct curb drop-off and pickup zone along South Tryon Street in front of the tavern.
