Rooftop Views, Legendary Tavern.

Welcome to The Gin Mill, South End Charlotte’s definitive two-story tavern and open-air rooftop destination. Positioned right in front of the legendary Amos' Southend music hall, we serve handcrafted double angus smash burgers, slow-smoked jumbo chicken wings, loaded tavern tots, and icy local craft beers with unbeatable skyline views.

Tavern Signature

Jumbo Hickory Smoked Wings

Our award-winning chicken wings are dry-rubbed and hickory-smoked low and slow before being flash-fried to golden perfection. Tossed in house buffalo sauce, sweet Carolina gold BBQ, or roasted garlic parmesan, served with crisp celery sticks and chunky blue cheese dip.
