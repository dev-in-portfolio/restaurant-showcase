## Original Audit Weakness

Curry Junction relies on basic ordering links (`curryjunction.net`, ChowNow). Located in the Arboretum at 8200 Providence Rd, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its tandoori clay-oven roasting craft, hand-pleated Himalayan momo technique, rich spice roasting tradition, and South Charlotte Arboretum dining experience.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Arboretum South Charlotte Location:** 8200 Providence Rd, Charlotte NC 28277 (Arboretum Shopping Center at Providence Rd & Pineville-Matthews Rd).
2. **Signature Himalayan & Royal Indian Fare:** Steamed & fried Himalayan momo dumplings with spicy sesame tomato chutney, clay-oven tandoori chicken, lamb seekh kebabs, creamy chicken tikka masala, lamb vindaloo, and garlic butter naan.
3. **Dining Ambiance & Service:** Warm, authentic dining room ambiance with weekday lunch thali/buffet specials and evening dinner service.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Stately Elegant Serif Typography:** Stately elegant display serif (*Marcellus*) paired with refined body sans (*Outfit*) and spice ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Royal Plum & Saffron Copper Palette:** Regal royal palette anchored in deep plum charcoal (`#1F0812`), rich saffron amber (`#D97706`), warm tandoori terracotta (`#B45309`), polished antique gold (`#F59E0B`), and soft silk cream (`#FFFDF9`).
3. **"The Clay-Oven Tandoori & Hand-Pleated Momo Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`tandoori-momo-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing hand-pleated Himalayan momos alongside clay-oven tandoori grills and rich curries.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `CupLux Coffee Drive-Thru` — Modern vibrant drive-thru with Plus Jakarta Sans fonts and Freedom Navy/Yellow.
2. `Crisp Kitchen & Cocktails` — Modern midnight emerald lounge with DM Sans fonts and Amber Copper.
3. `Courtyard Hooligans` — British soccer pub with Unbounded display fonts and Stadium Green/Gold.

### Divergent Choices for Curry Junction

- **Hero Composition:** Regal Himalayan Saffron & Tandoori Copper Vault split-hero layout: left side features an ornate copper stamp badge & spice crest (`"HIMALAYAN & ROYAL INDIAN KITCHEN • ARBORETUM CHARLOTTE"`), stately elegant serif display (*Marcellus*), deep royal plum & saffron copper palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of steamed Himalayan momos in a bamboo steamer, tandoori chicken legs, chicken tikka masala, and garlic butter naan.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Curry Junction Vault Cards** (`junction-vault-card`) and **Arboretum Dining Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`junction-header`, `royal-brand`, `plum-hero-stage`, `copper-stamp-badge`, `junction-vault-card`, `junction-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
