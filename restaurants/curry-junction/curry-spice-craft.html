🍫 Spice Tempering & Slow Gravies

The Alchemy of Curry Spices

Great curries are built layer by layer. We bloom whole spices—cardamom pods, cinnamon bark, & cumin seeds—in hot oil before simmering rich gravies with fresh ginger, garlic, tomatoes, and cashew cream.

Spice Science

The 3 Pillars of Curry Spice Craft

1. Whole Spice Blooming (Tadka)

Whole cinnamon, green cardamom, cloves, & star anise are bloomed in hot oil to release essential oils.

2. Slow-Simmered Tomato-Onion Base

Fresh onions, tomatoes, & cashew cream are simmered for hours until thick, velvety, and sweet.

3. Custom Heat Level Balance

Every curry can be tailored precisely to your preference—from mild butter masalas to hot Goan vindaloos.
