# Evidence Document: Cedar Street Tavern

## Creative Brief
- **Business Name:** Cedar Street Tavern
- **Location:** 120 N Cedar St, Suite 755, Charlotte, NC 28202 (Corner of W 5th St & N Cedar St, Uptown Charlotte)
- **Phone:** (704) 333-3448
- **Hours of Operation:**
  - Monday to Wednesday: 11:00 AM - 12:00 AM
  - Thursday to Saturday: 11:00 AM - 2:00 AM
  - Sunday: 10:00 AM - 12:00 AM
  - Weekend Brunch: Saturday 11:00 AM - 2:00 PM, Sunday 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM
- **Concept & Vibe:** Welcoming Uptown Charlotte neighborhood sports tavern and grill featuring hearty handcrafted burgers, legendary Maryland crab dip, jumbo double-fried wings, craft beers on tap, fireplace lounge, outdoor patio, and lively game day watch parties.
- **Design Aesthetic:** Warm rustic timber, deep forest cedar green, roasted amber malt, weathered brick, and aged parchment with chamfered geometry, brass accents, and responsive tavern grid layouts.

## Claim Ledger
- **Fact 1 (Address & Neighborhood):** Situated at 120 N Cedar St in Uptown Charlotte at the intersection of 5th and Cedar Streets, bridging Fourth and Third Wards.
- **Fact 2 (Phone & Contact):** Direct telephone line (704) 333-3448 and official web portal at cedarstreettavern.com.
- **Fact 3 (Operating Hours):** Open daily for lunch, dinner, and late night until midnight Monday-Wednesday, 2:00 AM Thursday-Saturday, and midnight Sunday with Saturday/Sunday weekend brunch.
- **Fact 4 (Signature Tavern Fare):** Renowned for the Cedar Street Burger (custom half-pound beef blend on brioche), warm jumbo lump Maryland crab dip with pita points, double-tossed jumbo chicken wings (buffalo, hot honey, Carolina gold BBQ), fireplace flatbreads, and hand-cut pub fries.
- **Fact 5 (Events & Entertainment):** Monday Night Music Bingo, comprehensive NFL Sunday Ticket and Premier League sports viewing across high-definition screens, outdoor pet-friendly patio, and cozy fireplace lounge.

## Add-On Preservation
- **Interactive Game Day Watch Party Planner:** Real-time calculator for tailgates, watch parties, and group draft packages with cost breakdown.
- **Wing Heat & Flavor Index:** Interactive flavor matrix detailing heat ratings, sweet/savory profiles, and suggested craft beer pairings.
- **Draft Beer Flight Customizer:** Interactive builder allowing guests to compose 4-pour craft beer flights across local Carolinas breweries.
- **Direct Action Channels:** Click-to-call direct links and direct email reservation links without standard web forms.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- **Palette Uniqueness:** Forest cedar green (#183324), amber malt (#d9822b), warm brick crimson (#8a2e1b), creamy parchment (#f7f4ed), and dark iron slate (#151b18).
- **Typography:** Display font 'Bebas Neue' paired with crisp body font 'Plus Jakarta Sans', contrasting against prior Japanese modern and Italian classical styles.
- **Geometry & Accents:** Chamfered badge corners, wood plank borders, brass divider rules, and chalkboard-inspired event slates.
