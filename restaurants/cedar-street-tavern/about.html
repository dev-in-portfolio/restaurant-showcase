Uptown Roots

THE NEIGHBORHOOD SPORTS TAVERN

Cedar Street Tavern was created to fill a vital role in Uptown Charlotte: an authentic, welcoming neighborhood bar where neighbors, office workers, sports fans, and visitors can pull up a stool and feel instantly at home.

Positioned at 120 N Cedar Street at the junction of Fourth and Third Wards, our tavern sits within easy walking distance of Bank of America Stadium, Truist Field, and Uptown's core financial towers.

We believe that great taverns are built on three fundamental pillars: uncompromising scratch kitchen quality, an ever-rotating lineup of fresh regional craft beer, and sincere, unpretentious hospitality.

The Scratch Kitchen

We do not cut corners. Our burger patties are freshly ground from whole choice chuck and brisket, our wings are double-fried for maximum crunch, and our Maryland crab dip follows a time-honored coastal family recipe.

Carolina Craft Beer

Our 16 tap lines are dedicated to championing the best independent breweries across Charlotte, the Piedmont, and Western North Carolina, alongside iconic classic tavern lagers and seasonal ciders.

Fireplace & Patio Vibe

Relax inside by our brick fireplace lounge or catch the evening breeze on our tree-lined outdoor patio. Whether it is a chilly autumn Panthers game or a sunny summer brunch, we have the ideal seat.

Community & Sports

WHERE CHARLOTTE GATHERS FOR THE GAME

With more than 20 high-definition screens strategically arrayed throughout the tavern and patio, there is no bad angle. From NFL Sunday Ticket and Premier League mornings to college football Saturdays and Charlotte FC matches, we bring stadium energy to your table.
