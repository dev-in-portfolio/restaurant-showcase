The Rhino Heritage

Neighborhood Soul, Queen City Roots.

Rhino Market & Deli opened its first doors on West Morehead Street in Wesley Heights with a bold vision: to give Charlotte the authentic, bustling neighborhood bodega it was missing. A place where you could grab a morning coffee, crush a hot sandwich at lunch, pick up local groceries, and enjoy a draft beer with neighbors.

The Values of Rhino Market

Neighborhood Inclusivity

Creating an unpretentious, welcoming hub where neighbors from every walk of life gather, converse, and feel at home.

Championing homegrown producers by dedicating our shelves and draft lines to North Carolina coffee roasters, brewers, and bakers.
