The 3 Pillars of Rhino Sandwich Craft

1. The Chicken Torta Architecture

Balancing slow-pulled seasoned chicken breast with rich sliced avocado, melted provolone, and pickled jalapeño crunch on crusty ciabatta.

2. Artisan Local Bakery Breads

Sourcing fresh ciabatta, marbled rye, buttery croissants, and rustic sourdough loaves baked specifically to hold generous layers without collapsing.

3. Made-to-Order Speed

Pressing and building every hot melt and cold grinder fresh in minutes for busy Charlotte commuters and neighborhood regulars.
