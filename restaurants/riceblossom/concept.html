Chengdu Roots, Queen City Craft.

Known among Chinese cuisine connoisseurs as 川王府 ("King of Szechuan"), Riceblossom was established with a singular mission: to bring the uncompromised depth, intense wok fire, and electrifying mala spice profiles of Sichuan province directly to South Charlotte.
