The 3 Pillars of Riceblossom Dim Sum & Drinks

1. Hong You Chao Shou Wontons

Hand-pinched daily with seasoned Berkshire pork and ginger, tossed in our proprietary spiced red chili oil reduction.

2. Sizzling Cast Iron Flaming Pans

3. House-Brewed Boba & Fruit Teas

Shaking fresh milk teas with slow-cooked brown sugar tapioca pearls, refreshing mango green teas, and chilled Tsingtao lager.
