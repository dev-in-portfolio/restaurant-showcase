Chengdu Specialties Mala & Wok Hei

Chongqing Mala Spicy Chicken (辣子鸡) #1 Signature

Crispy bite-sized dark meat chicken flash-fried and wok-tossed with a mountain of sun-dried red chilies, numbing Sichuan green peppercorns, toasted sesame, and garlic scallions.

Szechuan Boiled Fish in Chili Oil (水煮鱼) Authentic Chengdu

Delicate tender white fish fillets poached in a bubbling spicy broth over Napa cabbage and bean sprouts, finished with a pour of sizzling aromatic chili oil, minced garlic, and Sichuan peppercorns.

Traditional Mapo Tofu (麻婆豆腐) Mala Classic

Silky soft tofu cubes simmered with seasoned minced pork in rich fermented Pixian broad bean paste (Doubanjiang), garlic ginger sauce, and toasted ground red Sichuan peppercorns.

Three Pepper Sizzling Beef High Heat Wok

Tender flank steak wok-seared over blazing flame with fresh jalapeños, bell peppers, dried red chilies, and sweet onions in savory brown garlic sauce.

Dim Sum & Beverages Handmade & Teas

Wontons in Red Chili Oil (红油抄手) Hand-Pinched

Silky handmade pork dumplings boiled fresh and served in our signature house-infused aromatic red chili oil with toasted garlic, sweet soy reduction, and green scallions.

Crispy Scallion Pancakes Flaky Layers

Golden pan-fried savory layered flatbread stuffed with fragrant chopped green scallions and sesame, served with sweet ginger soy dipping sauce.

Sizzling Cumin Flaming Lamb Cast Iron Skillet

Tender sliced lamb tossed with toasted whole cumin seeds, dried red chilies, cilantro, and onions, served on a sizzling hot cast iron pan.

Artisanal Boba Milk Tea & Tsingtao House-Brewed

Freshly shaken brown sugar boba milk tea with chewy tapioca pearls, refreshing passionfruit jasmine green tea, and ice-cold imported Tsingtao beer.
