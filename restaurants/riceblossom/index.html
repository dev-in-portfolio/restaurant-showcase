Ancient Clay Pot Discipline

Authentic Chengdu Mapo Tofu

Simmered in an artisanal black clay pot, our authentic Mapo Tofu features tender silken tofu cubes suspended in fiery Sichuan chili bean paste (Pixian Doubanjiang), minced beef, fermented black beans, and scallions, crowned with freshly ground red and green Sichuan peppercorn powder that delivers that authentic tingling numbness.

Handmade Dough & Broth Mastery

Handmade Dan Dan Noodles & Fiery Broths

Every bowl of Dan Dan noodles is layered with house-made chili oil, dark aromatic soy sauce, toasted sesame paste, seasoned minced pork, pickled mustard greens (Yacai), and crisp baby bok choy. Pair with our famous Szechuan Boiled Fish or cumin lamb flaming iron skillets.
