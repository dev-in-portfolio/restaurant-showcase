🍗 Iconic Southern Comfort Science

The Art of Fried Chicken & Cornbread

Southern fried chicken is a culinary craft perfected through patience. At Mert’s Heart & Soul, our chicken is soaked in cultured buttermilk & spices overnight before being tossed in seasoned flour & fried golden. Served alongside warm cornbread waffles & honey butter.

Kitchen Craft

The 3 Pillars of Mert's Fried Chicken

1. 24-Hour Cultured Buttermilk Soak

Marinating fresh chicken in garlic-paprika cultured buttermilk to tenderize meat & infuse deep flavor.

2. Secret Seasoned Flour Dredge

Hand-dredging chicken in double-sifted flour seasoned with black pepper, cayenne, garlic, & sea salt.

3. Waffle-Pressed Sweet Cornbread

Baking sweet cornmeal batter inside heavy cast-iron waffle irons until crispy outside & fluffy inside.
