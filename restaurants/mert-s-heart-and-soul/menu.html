Lowcountry Shrimp & Yellow Grits #1 House Special

Jumbo Atlantic shrimp sautéed with smoked tasso ham, bell peppers, scallions, & rich garlic gravy over creamy stone-ground buttered yellow grits with cornbread.

Famous Southern Deep-Fried Chicken Food Network Fav

Half chicken marinated in buttermilk & house secret herbs, fried golden crisp, served with baked mac & cheese & collard greens.

Fresh Carolina catfish fillets crusted in seasoned yellow cornmeal, fried crispy, served with house tartar sauce, hushpuppies, & red beans & rice.

St. Louis Slow-Smoked Pork Ribs Smoked Daily

Tender pork rib rack dry-rubbed & hickory-smoked low for 8 hours, glazed in sweet tangy Carolina barbecue sauce.
