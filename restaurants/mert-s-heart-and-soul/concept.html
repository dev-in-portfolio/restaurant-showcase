Heart, Soul & Southern Passion

Mert’s Heart & Soul was founded by James & Sammy Bazzelle to serve genuine Lowcountry Southern comfort food in Center City Charlotte. Located at 214 N College St, Mert's has grown into an Uptown institution beloved by locals, celebrities, & food travelers.
