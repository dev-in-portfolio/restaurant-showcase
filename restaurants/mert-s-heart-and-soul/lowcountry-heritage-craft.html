🥩 Coastal Carolina Heritage

The Art of Lowcountry Cooking

Lowcountry cuisine is born from the coastal marshes, rice plantations, & sea islands of South Carolina & Georgia. At Mert’s Heart & Soul, we bring Charleston's rich culinary traditions to Uptown Charlotte—sautéing Atlantic shrimp in smoked tasso ham gravy over stone-ground buttered grits.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Mert's Lowcountry Craft

1. Stone-Ground Yellow Grits

Cooking coarse Carolina corn grits low & slow with heavy cream, butter, & sharp cheddar cheese.

2. Smoked Tasso Ham Gravy

Building a savory roux with diced Cajun-style smoked tasso pork ham, holy trinity veggies, & seafood stock.

3. Wild Atlantic Seafood Sourcing

Sourcing sweet wild Atlantic white shrimp & fresh Carolina catfish for traditional pan-searing.
