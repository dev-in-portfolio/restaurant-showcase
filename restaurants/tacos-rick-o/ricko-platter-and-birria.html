The Legend of The Ricko Platter.

Created by Chef Ricky Ortiz, the Ricko Platter is Charlotte's ultimate culinary celebration. Loaded with three crispy Jalisco birria quesatacos, golden wings, caramelized sweet plantains, and queso fries, it delivers an explosion of texture and flavor in every bite.

Platter Elements

Anatomy of the Ricko Platter

Each component is prepared fresh to order on the food truck flattop.

3 Crispy Quesatacos

Molten Birria Tacos

Hand-dipped corn tortillas stuffed with 12-hour braised shredded beef and melted Chihuahua cheese, griddled golden brown and served with hot consommé.

4 Seasoned Wings & Fries

Crispy Wings & Queso

Jumbo chicken wings fried crisp with Latin spice rub, paired with golden crinkle-cut fries drizzled in warm melted queso blanco.

Sweet Contrast

Plátanos Maduros

Caramelized sweet ripe plantains topped with crema and condensed milk, providing the perfect sweet balance to the savory birria.

Tradition & Passion

The 12-Hour Birria Braise

Our birria starts with choice beef chuck cut into large roasts and marinated with guajillo, ancho, and chile de árbol peppers. Simmered low and slow for twelve hours with roasted garlic, Mexican cinnamon, and cloves, the meat becomes extraordinarily tender while the broth concentrates into our signature dipping consommé.
