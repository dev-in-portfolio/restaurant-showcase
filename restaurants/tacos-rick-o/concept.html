Our Heritage

The Journey of Tacos Rick-O

The culinary journey that turned a food truck into a Charlotte sensation.

Roots

Family Kitchen Traditions

Chef Ricky grew up cooking alongside his family, mastering the delicate art of balancing dried chilies, whole Mexican spices, and slow braising.

Innovation

Birria Revolution

Recognizing Charlotte's craving for authentic Jalisco quesatacos, Ricky perfected his 12-hour recipe, quickly generating massive buzz across the city.

Community Love

Queen City Dedication

Serving thousands of guests every weekend with genuine smiles, generous portions, and consistent culinary excellence.

Fresh Daily

Zero Shortcuts, Pure Craft

We believe great street food takes time. From toasting our guajillo chilies to hand-shredding slow-braised beef and whipping up fresh batches of guacamole and salsa verde, we cook with unconditional pride every single day.
