Directions & Parking

Arriving at Charlotte Premium Outlets

  • Driving Directions: Located off I-485 Exit 4 (Steele Creek Rd) in southwest Charlotte. Turn onto Dixie River Rd and follow signs into Charlotte Premium Outlets.
  • Locating The Truck: Park in the main lot outside Saks Fifth Avenue OFF 5TH. Look for our bright orange and red Tacos Rick-O trailer.
  • Express Pick-Up: Call ahead at (980) 339-1279 for fast curbside pickup during shopping trips.
