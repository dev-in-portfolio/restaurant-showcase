Royal Tradition, Heartfelt Hospitality.

Tabla Indian Restaurant was established to bring an elevated, comprehensive Indian dining experience to Ballantyne and the greater South Charlotte community. Named after the revered classical Indian percussion drum that sets the rhythm of timeless melodies, our kitchen strikes a perfect rhythm between the royal Mughal traditions of the North and the bold, coastal spices of the South.

Core Values

The Foundations of Tabla Indian Restaurant

The culinary commitments that guide our kitchen every day.

Value 01

Authentic Whole Spices

We roast and grind whole spices in-house—cinnamon quills, black cardamom, star anise, and cloves—yielding nuanced masalas without artificial colors or preservatives.

Value 02

Halal & Vegetarian Inclusivity

We proudly serve 100% Halal certified meats and offer dedicated vegetarian and vegan kitchens, ensuring every member of our diverse South Charlotte community dines with confidence.

Value 03

Family & Corporate Anchor

From bustling weekday corporate executive lunches to grand weekend multi-generational family banquets, we provide warm, generous hospitality that feels like home.

South Charlotte Community

A Place for Connection

Conveniently located near the North Carolina–South Carolina state line, Tabla serves guests from Ballantyne, SouthPark, Matthews, Pineville, and Fort Mill. Whether unwinding over spiced masala chai, enjoying a quick lunch buffet special, or celebrating a family milestone, our doors are open daily.
