Welcome to Tabla Indian Restaurant, South Charlotte’s premier dining destination for royal North and South Indian cuisine. Located on the Lancaster Highway corridor in Ballantyne, our kitchen celebrates the heritage of subcontinental cooking: blistered garlic naans pulled from blazing clay tandoor ovens, slow-steamed Hyderabadi dum biryanis, velvety butter chicken, and crisp Punjabi samosas crafted from scratch daily.

Clay Tandoor Hearth

Crispy Punjabi Samosas

Folded by hand daily in our kitchen, our samosas feature a flaky pastry shell packed with spiced Russet potatoes, green peas, roasted cumin, and whole coriander seeds. Fried to a golden crunch and accompanied by freshly made mint-coriander and sweet tamarind chutneys.

Our signature mixed grill brings together spiced tandoori chicken leg quarters, succulent chicken tikka morsels, and skewered seekh kebabs directly from the clay hearth. Served with warm sesame naan bread and fresh sliced salad.
