Transit & Parking

Arriving at Tabla Indian Restaurant

Visiting our Ballantyne dining room is effortless and convenient:

  • Plaza Surface Parking: Ample, free well-lit parking spaces are available directly outside Suite D8 across the plaza surface lot.
  • Dedicated Curbside Bays: Reserved curbside spaces near the front entrance allow quick grab-and-go pickup for online and phone orders.
  • Highway Corridor Access: Easily accessible from I-485 via US-521 (Johnston Rd) and minutes from Fort Mill and Indian Land.
Corporate & Event Feasts

Catering & Large Party Directives

Guidelines for group dining and catering orders:

  • Corporate Lunch Trays: Packaged individual boxed lunches and buffet trays for corporate parks throughout Ballantyne and South Charlotte.
  • Halal & Vegetarian Assurance: All meat dishes are 100% Halal certified, and vegetarian curries are prepared in dedicated cookware.
  • Large Event Planning: Full-service chafing dish setups, live dosa stations, and custom menu planning for weddings and private parties.
