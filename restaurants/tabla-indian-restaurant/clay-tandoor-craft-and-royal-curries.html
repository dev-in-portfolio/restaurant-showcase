Radiant Terracotta Heat • 800 Degrees

Live Clay Tandoor, Velvet Gravies.

The heartbeat of Tabla Indian Restaurant is our traditional cylindrical clay tandoor. Fueled to searing heat exceeding 800 degrees Fahrenheit, it cooks leavened flatbreads in seconds and locks succulent juices into spiced skewered meats. Paired with our slow-simmered handi curries, it represents the pinnacle of North Indian culinary tradition.

Kitchen Heritage

The Craft Behind Every Naan & Curry

Techniques perfected across centuries of subcontinental royal cooking.

Pillar 01

Terracotta Wall Slapping

Naan dough is hand-stretched onto cotton gaddi pads and slapped directly against the vertical clay interior. Intense radiant heat puffs bubbles in seconds, forming a crisp charred bottom and pillow-soft crumb.

Pillar 02

Vertical Skewer Roasting

Meats marinated in hung yogurt, freshly ground garam masala, and Kashmiri chilies are threaded onto three-foot stainless skewers and lowered into the center of the pit for a tender, charred exterior.

Pillar 03

Slow Reduction Gravies

Our royal makhanis and kormas simmer for hours in heavy-bottom handis with whole spices, pure butter, and cashew purees, finished with crushed sun-dried fenugreek leaves.

Royal North Indian Tradition

Butter Chicken Perfection

Murgh Makhani originated in old Delhi as the definitive royal curry. At Tabla, we roast marinated chicken thighs in the clay oven until charred and tender, then fold them into a silky reduction of ripe vine tomatoes, butter, ginger, cardamom, and heavy cream. Accompanied by fragrant saffron basmati rice and piping-hot garlic naan.
