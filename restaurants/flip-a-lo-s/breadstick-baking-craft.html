The 3 Pillars of Flip-a-Lo’s Baking Craft

1. Daily Kneaded Yeast Dough

Unbleached bread flour, warm water, extra virgin olive oil, & yeast kneaded daily for pillowy interior pull.

2. Clarified Garlic Butter Bath

Straight out of the oven, every order is brushed with clarified sweet cream butter infused with minced garlic.
