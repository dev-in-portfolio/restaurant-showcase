## Original Audit Weakness

Flip-a-Lo’s relies on basic ordering templates and basic social listings. Located at 7629 Pineville-Matthews Rd in South Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its 24-hour marination wing process, hand-breaded tender crisping technique, scratch garlic butter breadstick baking process, and game-day party platter order directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South Charlotte Location:** 7629 Pineville-Matthews Rd, Suite A, Charlotte NC 28226 (Carmel Commons Shopping Center).
2. **Buffalo Wings & Crispy Tenders:** Jumbo Buffalo Wings (Sauced in Buffalo Medium, Hot Cayenne, Honey BBQ, Garlic Parmesan, Lemon Pepper Dry Rub), Hand-Breaded Crispy Tenders, & Loaded Crinkle Fries.
3. **Scratch Garlic Breadsticks & Sweets:** House-made garlic butter breadsticks with warm marinara, blue cheese celery dips, & hot cinnamon sugar funnel cake sticks.

### Core Design Moves

1. **High-Velocity Athletic Display Sans Typography:** Athletic high-contrast display sans (*Space Grotesk*) paired with clean body sans (*Inter*) and game-day mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Midnight Charcoal & Cayenne Red Palette:** High-energy wing vault palette anchored in midnight charcoal (`#0C0A09`), cayenne red (`#DC2626`), buffalo orange (`#EA580C`), honey butter gold (`#F59E0B`), and crisp blue cheese cream (`#F8FAFC`).
3. **"The 24-Hour Wing Toss & Scratch Breadstick Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`wing-sauce-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing jumbo tossed wings alongside hand-breaded tenders, garlic butter breadsticks, & game-day party platters.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Fiesta Maya Mexican Restaurant` — Opulent Mayan Stone with Playfair Display fonts and Deep Obsidian/Mayan Jade.
2. `Felix Empanadas` — Warm Vibrant Argentine Street Food with Plus Jakarta Sans fonts and Terracotta Rust/Chimichurri Emerald.
3. `Exotica Indian Kitchen & Bar` — Royal Indian Fine Dining with Cinzel Decorative fonts and Imperial Navy/Copper Gold.

### Divergent Choices for Flip-a-Lo’s

- **Hero Composition:** High-Velocity Buffalo Wing & Crispy Tenders Vault split-hero layout: left side features an electric cayenne stamp badge (`"SOUTH CHARLOTTE BUFFALO WINGS & TENDERS • 7629 PINEVILLE-MATTHEWS RD"`), athletic display sans (*Space Grotesk*), midnight charcoal & cayenne red palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of golden tossed wings, crispy tenders, garlic breadsticks, marinara dip, & blue cheese celery sticks.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Flip-a-Lo’s Vault Cards** (`flipalos-vault-card`) and South Charlotte game-day highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`flipalos-header`, `flipalos-brand`, `cayenne-hero-stage`, `electric-stamp-badge`, `flipalos-vault-card`, `flipalos-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
