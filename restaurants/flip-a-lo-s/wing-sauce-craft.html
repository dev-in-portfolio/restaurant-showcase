🍗 24-Hour Buttermilk Marination

The Art of Wing Tossing

Great wings require patience. We steep our jumbo chicken wings in a seasoned buttermilk brine for 24 hours, flash fry them at 375°F for maximum skin crunch, & toss them in stainless bowls with warm house sauces.

Wing Science

The 3 Pillars of Flip-a-Lo’s Wing Craft

1. 24-Hour Spiced Buttermilk Brine

Breaks down proteins so every bite of chicken meat stays juicy, tender, & seasoned to the bone.

2. 375°F Flash Frying Crunch

High-heat frying renders out skin fat quickly, creating an ultra-crispy shell that holds sauce without getting soggy.

3. Small-Batch Stainless Bowl Toss

Wings are tossed per order in hot stainless bowls with real butter cayenne & honey BBQ glazes.
