French Confit & Frying Technique

The Science of Duck Fat Frying

Frying chicken in pure rendered duck fat elevates comfort food to haute cuisine. Duck fat has a high smoke point and rich savory depth that creates a shatteringly crisp golden crust while locking in succulence.

Culinary Technique

The 3 Pillars of Coquette Duck Fat Craft

1. 24-Hour Herb Buttermilk Brine

Whole chickens are steeped for 24 hours in cultured buttermilk, thyme, rosemary, & garlic to tenderize every bite.

2. Pure Rendered Duck Fat

Fried in 100% rendered duck fat at 350°F, imparting a rich nutty aroma and crisp texture impossible with plant oils.

3. Lavender Honey Drizzle

Drizzled hot out of the fryer with raw North Carolina wildflower honey infused with French provencal lavender.
