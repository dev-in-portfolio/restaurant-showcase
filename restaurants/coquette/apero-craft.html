Midday Apéro Experience • Tue-Sat 3pm-5pm

French Apéro & Raw Bar

"L'Apéro" is the cherished French ritual of unwinding between afternoon and evening. Walk in Tuesday through Saturday from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM for freshly shucked coastal oysters, French charcuterie, aperitif cocktails, and Champagne.
