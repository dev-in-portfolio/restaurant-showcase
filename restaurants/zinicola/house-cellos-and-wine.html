Classic Limoncello

Hand-zested organic lemons steeped for 40 days in pure spirits and blended with cane sugar. Brilliantly tart, aromatic, and invigorating.

Strawberrycello

Fresh Carolina strawberries steeped slowly to extract vibrant natural color and sweet summer fruit character with a crisp citrus finish.

Tour Italy by the Glass

Our wine program spans the storied wine appellations of the Italian peninsula: crisp Pinot Grigio from Alto Adige, structured Nebbiolo from Piedmont, earthy Sangiovese from Chianti Classico and Montalcino, and bold Nero d'Avola from Sicily.
