The Craft

The Elements of Our Pasta

Every step is calibrated for maximum sauce adherence and toothsome texture.

Flour Sourcing

Caputo Tipo 00 & Semolina

Imported stone-milled Italian wheat flours mixed with fresh farm egg yolks create silky, golden doughs with exceptional elasticity.

Bronze Extrusion

Textured Sauce Grip

Our bronze-die extruded shapes possess a micro-porous surface that allows velvety sauces to cling tightly to every curve.

Pan Emulsion

Mantacatura Technique

Each order is finished in the sauté pan, vigorously tossing starch-rich pasta water, butter, and sauce together into a glossy emulsion.

Pasta Geometry

Matching Shape to Sauce

In Italy, pasta shape is never an afterthought. We pair our wide, sturdy pappardelle ribbons with 8-hour braised beef ragù Bolognese, delicate tonnarelli with Roman pecorino and black pepper, and extruded rigatoni with creamy tomato vodka sauce.
