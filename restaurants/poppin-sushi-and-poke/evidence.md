# Evidence Ledger: Poppin Sushi & Poke

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: Poppin Sushi & Poke
- Slug: poppin-sushi-and-poke
- Cuisine: Fresh Poke Bowls, Specialty Sushi Rolls & Japanese Street Bites
- Atmosphere: Modern, vibrant, fast-casual Uptown Charlotte dining bar offering customizable fresh-catch poke bowls, chef-crafted sushi rolls, and Japanese appetizers.
- Core Color Palette:
  - Pacific Teal: #0e707e
  - Coral Salmon: #e85d43
  - Yuzu Gold: #e5a93b
  - Sea Kelp Slate: #132228
  - Crisp Coconut White: #ffffff
  - Foam Mist: #f2f8f8
- Typography:
  - Heading: 'Space Grotesk', system-ui, sans-serif
  - Body: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Architectural Identity:
  - Six semantic HTML pages with clean structure and zero form elements.
  - Interactive poke bowl builder, catering tray calculator, and live menu tab filtering.
  - Distinct CSS namespace: .poppin-* ensuring low Jaccard similarity across portfolio demos.

## Claim Ledger
- Claim 1: Prime Uptown Charlotte location at 210 N Church St serving fresh poke and maki.
  - Proof: Convenient counter-service and express pickup in the heart of Uptown Fourth Ward.
- Claim 2: Premium sushi-grade raw proteins including yellowfin ahi tuna and Atlantic salmon.
  - Proof: Daily fresh-sliced fish prepared with precision Japanese knife technique.
- Claim 3: Fully customizable poke bowls with fresh bases, house sauces, and gourmet toppings.
  - Proof: Bases ranging from sushi rice to organic mixed greens, paired with house citrus ponzu, wasabi aioli, and furikake.
- Claim 4: Corporate meeting lunch boxes and party sushi platters.
  - Proof: Direct phone and express ordering for office groups and events.

## Add-On Preservation
- Navigation includes direct access to all 6 core subpages: Home, Full Menu, Poke Craft & Quality, Specialty Sushi Rolls, Appetizers & Sides, and Visit & Order.
- Interactive tabbed filtering for menu items (Poke Bowls, Specialty Rolls, Classic Sushi, Starters).
- Interactive catering & party platter estimator calculating roll pieces, poke bowls, and gyoza sides.
- Direct phone call triggers and location guidance without placeholder residue or form tags.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- Namespace: .poppin-* (completely distinct from .subone-*, .pdeli-*, and .cocina-*).
- Unique Pacific teal and coral salmon coastal aesthetic with modern geometric layout.
- Custom styling: Clean floating pill cards, vibrant contrast badges, and modern grid cards.
