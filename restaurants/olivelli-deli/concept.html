A Love Letter to the Italian Deli

Created by the culinary team behind Charlotte landmarks Supperland and Ever Andalo, Olivelli Deli brings the vibrant spirit of a traditional Italian neighborhood salumeria to 3120 N Davidson St in NoDa. Combining artisanal scratch cooking with casual walk-up window accessibility, we craft unforgettable food for daily living.

The Values of Olivelli Deli

Scratch Craft Standards

Baking benne seed rolls in-house, making fresh tapenades, and sourcing the finest imported salumi to ensure superior flavor in every sandwich.
