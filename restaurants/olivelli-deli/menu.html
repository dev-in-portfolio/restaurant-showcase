Italian Sandwiches On Benne Rolls

The "Oli Italiano" #1 Signature Sandwich

Prosciutto di Parma, spicy capicola, mortadella, Genoa salame, aged provolone cheese, finely shredded cabbage, marinated red onion, and house Castelvetrano olive tapenade on a fresh benne seed roll.

Roasted Turkey & Provolone Deli Classic

Slow-roasted herb turkey breast, aged sharp provolone, crisp romaine, ripe tomato, Calabrian chili mayo, and red wine vinaigrette on toasted benne roll.

Porchetta & Salsa Verde Italian Specialty

Crisp-skinned roasted spiced pork belly and loin, fresh herb salsa verde, arugula, and lemon garlic aioli on crusty Italian bread.

Classic Chicken Salad Sandwich House Recipe

Tender shredded chicken breast blended with celery, fresh herbs, and light lemon mayo, served with bibb lettuce on benne seed bread.
