🥖 Artisan Bread & Salumi Slicing

The Art of the Benne Seed Roll

Every unforgettable sandwich starts with extraordinary bread. At Olivelli Deli, our signature rolls are baked fresh daily, crusted with toasted heirloom benne seeds, and layered with thin-sliced Prosciutto di Parma, spicy capicola, mortadella, aged provolone, and our signature Castelvetrano olive tapenade.

Salumeria Craft

The 3 Pillars of Olivelli Sandwiches

1. Toasted Heirloom Benne Seeds

Baking bread rolls coated with aromatic benne seeds, lending a deep, nutty crunch that elevates classic Italian deli sandwiches.

2. Premium Italian Salumi Slicing

Slicing authentic Prosciutto di Parma, mortadella with pistachio, Genoa salame, and capicola paper-thin to create airy, melt-in-your-mouth layers.

3. Castelvetrano Olive Tapenade

Coarsely grinding bright green Sicilian Castelvetrano olives with extra virgin olive oil, garlic, and herbs for the ultimate briny sandwich spread.
