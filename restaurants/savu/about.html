The Vision

Where Culinary Excellence Meets Charlotte Nightlife

Located in the heart of Uptown Charlotte at 445 W Trade St, SAVU was founded with an ambitious mission: to unite the precision and depth of pan-Asian culinary traditions with the high-voltage excitement of global nightlife culture.

Our culinary team sources premium King Crab, Maine lobster, sashimi-grade Pacific fish, and Miyazaki Wagyu, preparing each dish with modern flair. From our signature crab crispy rice to royal lobster lo mein and theatrical smoke cocktails, every sensory detail is designed to amaze.
