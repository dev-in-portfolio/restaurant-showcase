# SAVU Charlotte - Evidence & Research Ledger

## Creative Brief
SAVU is Uptown Charlotte's premier two-level Asian fusion restaurant and high-energy "vibe dining" lounge, situated at 445 W Trade St. Blending masterfully executed pan-Asian gastronomy (including king crab crispy rice, royal lobster lo mein, torched nigiri, and black truffle pasta) with an electric nightlife atmosphere curated by resident DJs, SAVU delivers an immersive multi-sensory evening experience. The design system embodies sleek nocturnal glamour, featuring deep obsidian backdrops, radiant ultraviolet and electric magenta neon accents, luminous champagne amber highlights, glassmorphic cards, and refined geometric typography.
