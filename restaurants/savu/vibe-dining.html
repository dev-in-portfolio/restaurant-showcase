What is Vibe Dining?

At SAVU, dinner is merely the opening act. As the night progresses, ambient lighting deepens, basslines elevate, and our two-level venue transforms into Uptown's most stylish nightlife destination.

01. Dynamic Soundscapes

Curated nightly sets by premier regional and guest DJs spanning melodic deep house, soulful afrobeat, upscale hip-hop, and modern edits.

Live DJ Nightly

02. Two-Level Architectural Layout

Choose between the pulsating main dining floor with direct bar access or the elevated VIP Mezzanine offering birds-eye views of the room.

Mezzanine & Lounge

03. Bottle Shows & Sparklers

Elevate your milestone birthday or corporate celebration with theatrical parade presentations, custom LED displays, and premium champagne showers.
