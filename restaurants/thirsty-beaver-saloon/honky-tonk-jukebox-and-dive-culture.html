Outlaw Country, Mick Jagger Lore.

What makes The Thirsty Beaver special isn’t just its architectural defiance—it is the living spirit inside. From our lovingly curated country jukebox and walls plastered with signed dollar bills to the night Mick Jagger drank a beer on the patio unnoticed by anyone, the Beaver is authentic Charlotte at its rawest.

The Cultural Heart of Plaza Midwood

Vintage analog jukeboxes, dollar bill wallpaper, and famous patrons.

Culture 01

Mick Jagger's Visit

On September 29, 2021, Mick Jagger stopped by the Beaver for an evening beer before the Rolling Stones' Charlotte concert, hanging out on the patio unnoticed by nearby regulars.

Culture 02

The Outlaw Jukebox

Drop your coins and choose from classic cuts by Waylon Jennings, Merle Haggard, Johnny Cash, George Jones, and Patsy Cline. No internet streams—just pure tactile jukebox joy.

Culture 03

Living Wall Gallery

Signed one-dollar bills, eclectic velvet paintings, bumper stickers, hanging brassieres, and taxidermy contribute to a visual history written directly by our patrons since 2008.

Plaza Midwood Soul

Come As You Are

At The Thirsty Beaver, pretense is left at the door. You will find construction workers, bankers, musicians, neighborhood artists, and college students sitting shoulder-to-shoulder at the bar. Everyone is treated with the exact same warmth, honest hospitality, and cold beer.
