Plaza Midwood History • Est. 2008

Defending Character, Preserving Soul.

The Thirsty Beaver was founded in 2008 by brothers Brian and Mark Wilson on a simple, timeless truth: every great neighborhood needs a gathering place that values people over profits, cold beer over fancy cocktails, and independent character over corporate conformity.

Enduring Values

The Values That Keep the Beaver Standing

Authenticity, loyalty to Plaza Midwood, and welcoming hospitality for all.

Value 01

Steadfast Independence

We stood our ground when multi-million dollar corporate developers attempted to wipe out our history. We remain proudly independent, owner-operated, and true to our roots.

Value 02

The Real Third Place

As genuine gathering spaces disappear, the Beaver provides an egalitarian watering hole open 365 days a year where everyone is welcome and conversations happen face-to-face.

Value 03

Community Loyalty

From our neighborhood regulars and local musicians to curious travelers from across the globe, we treat every customer like an old friend who just walked into our living room.
