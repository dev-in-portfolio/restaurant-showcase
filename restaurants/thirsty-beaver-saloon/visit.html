Payment Directives

Cash & On-Site ATM

Tips for smooth bar service at The Beaver:

  • Cash Is King: Cash speeds up service during busy nights, concerts, and weekend afternoons.
  • Indoor ATM: We have an on-site automated teller machine inside next to the jukebox.
  • Credit Cards: Cards are accepted at the bar with standard tab pre-authorization.
Neighborhood Synergy

Bring Your Own Food & Transit

Making the most of your trip to Plaza Midwood:

  • Bring Outside Food: Feel free to bring takeout pizza, burritos, barbecue, or sandwiches from nearby Plaza Midwood eateries directly to our bar tables.
  • Parking Options: Free public street parking is available along Central Avenue and adjacent residential streets.
  • Rideshare & Bus: Convenient rideshare drop-off zone directly on Central Ave, and CATS bus Route 9 stops nearby.
