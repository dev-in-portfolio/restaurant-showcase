Founded 1977 • Charlotte NC Music History

Nearly Five Decades of Pure Heart.

Founded in 1977 by Tommy Sossamon on Central Avenue, Tommy's Pub has stood as an unwavering anchor of Charlotte's working-class and musical spirit. Relocated to Eastway Crossing and guided by Jamie Starks, we celebrate nearly 50 years of unforgettable nights.

The History

The Journey of a Charlotte Landmark

Key chapters that shaped Tommy's Pub into the cultural icon it is today.

1977 – 2013

The Central Avenue Era

Tommy Sossamon opens the original pub on Central Avenue in Plaza Midwood, creating a legendary haven for neighborhood characters and touring musicians.

A New Home

Eastway Crossing Move

When redevelopment struck Central Avenue, the pub successfully relocated its bar, racing memorabilia, and musical spirit to 3124 Eastway Drive.

Present Day

Jamie Starks Stewardship

Under owner Jamie Starks, Tommy's Pub continues as an independent live music pillar, fostering new generations of Carolina musicians and songwriters.

Community Heart

Grassroots Resilience

From pandemic survival fundraisers and makers markets to benefit shows for local causes, Tommy's Pub is more than just a bar—it’s an essential community hub that takes care of its neighbors.
