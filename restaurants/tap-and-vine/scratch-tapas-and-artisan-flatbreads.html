Mediterranean-American Small Plates

Flavors Made for Sharing & Savoring.

The kitchen at Tap & Vine operates on the belief that dining is best enjoyed as a shared journey. Rather than large, heavy entrees, our scratch tapas format encourages guests to order a variety of vibrant dishes—from tender grilled lamb lollipops and crispy fontina-stuffed arancini to blistered artisan flatbreads pulled fresh from our ovens.

Kitchen Craftsmanship

The Benchmarks of Tap & Vine Tapas

Fresh ingredients, culinary precision, and harmonious wine pairings.

Technique 01

High-Heat Flame Searing

Our lamb lollipops and filet mignon medallions are hard-seared over flame to lock in moisture, developing an herbaceous herb-and-garlic crust while maintaining medium-rare tenderness.

Technique 02

Hand-Rolled Arancini

Made from slow-cooked arborio rice folded with wild forest mushrooms and fontina, hand-shaped, breaded in panko, and fried golden for a crisp exterior and molten cheese center.

Technique 03

Slow-Fermented Flatbreads

Our flatbread dough rests through a multi-day cold fermentation process, producing a thin crust with crisp blistered bubbles that stands up to rich cheeses, prosciutto, and wild arugula.

Stonecrest Oven

Artisan Flatbread Perfection

Every flatbread is assembled to order with top-tier ingredients: sweet Italian prosciutto di Parma, milky fresh mozzarella, earthy white truffle oil, and peppery wild baby arugula. Crisped in our ovens and sliced for effortless sharing across your table.
