South Charlotte Gathering • Stonecrest & Quail Corners

A Living Room for Wine Lovers.

Tap & Vine was created with a clear mission: provide South Charlotte with a stylish, warm, and welcoming neighborhood enoteca where exceptional wines and creative scratch tapas could be enjoyed without pretension. From lively happy hours and casual patio lunches to celebratory weekend dinners and late-night sips, our lounge is where community gathers.

Core Values

The Foundations of Tap & Vine

The culinary commitments and hospitality philosophy behind our doors.

Value 01

Accessible Wine Culture

We demystify wine. Whether you are an experienced collector seeking an allocated Napa Cabernet or a casual sipper exploring a new Pinot Grigio, our team guides you with warmth and insight.

Value 02

Scratch Kitchen Integrity

We never cut corners with premade sauces or frozen appetizers. From rolling our own arancini to hand-carving domestic lamb chops, every small plate is made from scratch.

Value 03

Late-Night South Charlotte Vibe

While many suburban dining rooms close early, Tap & Vine remains vibrant until midnight on Friday and Saturday, providing a chic haven for after-dinner drinks and dessert.

Two Convenient Charlotte Locations

Stonecrest & Quail Corners

With our flagship lounge in Stonecrest at Piper Glen on Rea Road and our sister neighborhood location at Quail Corners on Park Road, Tap & Vine offers convenient access across Ballantyne, SouthPark, Piper Glen, and surrounding neighborhoods.
