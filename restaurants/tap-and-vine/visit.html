Transit & Parking

Arriving at Stonecrest at Piper Glen

Visiting our Rea Road lounge is hassle-free:

  • Ample Surface Parking: Free, lighted plaza parking is available directly in front of the restaurant within the Stonecrest shopping center.
  • EV Charging: Multiple vehicle charging stations are located within a short stroll in the shopping complex.
  • Corridor Access: Rapid access from Ballantyne Commons Parkway, I-485 exit 59 (Rea Rd), and Piper Glen.
Atmosphere Directives

Lounge Seating & Shaded Patio

Guidelines for your visit:

  • Wine Bar Counter: First-come, first-served seating along our polished hardwood bar with full cocktail and tapas service.
  • Outdoor Patio: Dog-friendly outdoor patio featuring soft lounge seating and fire tables for pleasant Carolina evenings.
  • Weekend Reservations: Peak hours occur between 6:30 PM and 9:00 PM on Friday and Saturday. Calling ahead or booking online is recommended for dining tables.
