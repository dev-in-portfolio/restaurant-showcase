Stonecrest at Piper Glen • 7828 Rea Road

Curated Cellar Wines, Artisan Scratch Tapas.

Welcome to Tap & Vine, South Charlotte’s destination for sophisticated evening dining, curated cellar pours, and scratch Mediterranean-American small plates. Situated in Stonecrest at Piper Glen (with a sister location at Quail Corners), our lounge pairs over 60 sommelier-selected global wines by the glass and bottle with grilled rosemary lamb lollipops, crispy truffle arancini, and oven-crisped flatbreads.

House Specialty

Grilled Rosemary Lamb Lollipops

Our signature tapas dish features tender domestic lamb loin chops marinated in fresh rosemary, roasted garlic, and olive oil. Seared to a caramelized crust over open flame and served with roasted fingerling potatoes and a rich red wine reduction.

Prosciutto & Wild Arugula Flatbread

Baked in high-heat ovens until the thin crust shatters with every bite, this guest favorite features melted fresh mozzarella, savory prosciutto di Parma, and peppery wild baby arugula lightly dressed in extra virgin olive oil and fresh lemon juice.
