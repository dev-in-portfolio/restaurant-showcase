🎳 The Queen Park Story

LoSo Soul, Social Energy.

Queen Park Social was built on a simple premise: bringing people together in an authentic, high-energy environment free from the distractions of everyday life. Located at 4125 Yancey Road, our converted warehouse space blends mid-century bowling nostalgia with modern Queen City craft culture.
