The Art of Birria & Consomé

Real Mexican birria requires relentless patience. At El Veneno, beef brisket is marinated with roasted guajillo chilies, garlic, & Mexican spices before slow-braising for 8 hours until tender enough to shred by hand.

Street Taco Science

The 3 Pillars of El Veneno Gastronomy

1. Guajillo Chili Marinade

Dried guajillo & ancho chili pods are toasted, rehydrated, & blended with cinnamon & cloves for our signature marinade.

2. Chili-Oil Plancha Sear

Corn tortillas are dipped directly into rendering birria chili oil before being seared crisp on hot flat-top grills.

3. 8-Hour Consomé Reduction

The pan drippings & bone marrow broth reduce for 8 hours, yielding a deep, savory dipping consomé.
