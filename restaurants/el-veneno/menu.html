Street Tacos & Birria Menu

Hand-pressed corn tortillas, slow-simmered birria consomé, quesabirria, & refreshing horchata at 101 N Tryon St.

Street Tacos & Birria Corn Tortillas

Signature Birria de Res Tacos (3 Trio) #1 Best Seller

Crispy guajillo chili-dipped corn tortillas stuffed with slow-braised beef brisket, melted Oaxaca cheese, cilantro, & diced onions. Served with 8-oz warm consomé dipping broth.

Suadero Street Tacos (3 Trio) Street Favorite

Tender confit beef brisket seared crisp on the flat-top, topped with fresh cilantro, diced raw white onion, & roasted salsa verde.

Tacos al Pastor (3 Trio) Popular

Achiote & guajillo marinated pork sliced thin, seared with fire-roasted pineapple, fresh cilantro, & salsa roja.

Carnitas Michoacán Tacos (3 Trio) Traditional

Slow-confited citrus & spice pork shoulder crisp-seared, topped with pickled red onions & cilantro.

Quesabirria Mulita House Specialty

Double layered corn tortilla sandwich loaded with melted chihuahua cheese, shredded birria beef, cilantro, onions, & consomé cup.

Antojitos & Drinks Scratch Made

Mexican Esquites Street Corn Cup Market Favorite

Charred roasted corn off the cob tossed with cotija cheese, chili powder, lime juice, & garlic cilantro crema.

Fresh Guacamole & House Chips Scratch Side

Hass avocados mashed daily with lime, cilantro, jalapeno, & sea salt served with warm tortilla chips.

Scratch Cinnamon Rice Horchata Fresh Made

House-steeped rice milk, Mexican cinnamon, vanilla bean, & cane sugar served cold over ice.

Agua de Jamaica (Hibiscus) Refreshing

Steeped organic hibiscus calyces sweetened with raw sugar & fresh lime juice.
