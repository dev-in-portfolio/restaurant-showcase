The House-Made Syrup Lab

We refuse artificial flavor squeezes. Every syrup at Astoria is slow-simmered in our kitchen using pure botanical spices, real nuts, and raw cane sugar.

Syrup Profiles

Our Four Core House Infusions

1. Pistachio Cardamom

Ground green cardamom pods and roasted pistachios steeped with organic cane sugar for earthy floral warmth.

2. Lavender Honey

Dried French lavender blossoms infused with local North Carolina wildflower honey for delicate herbal sweetness.

3. Brown Sugar Cinnamon

Dark muscovado brown sugar and Saigon cinnamon sticks simmered into a rich caramel spiced glaze.

4. Madagascar Vanilla Bean

Whole split Madagascar bourbon vanilla beans scraped and infused for authentic fragrant vanilla notes.
