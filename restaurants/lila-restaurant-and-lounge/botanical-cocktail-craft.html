The Art of Botanical Lounge

The bar at LILA is an sensory sanctuary. Mixologists infuse Japanese Roku gin, green tea syrup, fresh yuzu, & smoked hibiscus teas to create cocktails served over hand-carved crystal ice spheres in velvet booth surroundings.
