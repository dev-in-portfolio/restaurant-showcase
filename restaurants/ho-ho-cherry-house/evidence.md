## Original Audit Weakness

Ho Ho Cherry House relies on basic third-party delivery portal links (BeyondMenu/Grubhub). Located at 2001 E 7th St in Elizabeth, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its Elizabeth neighborhood history, high-heat wok hei searing craft, dim sum specialties, and family feast takeout packs.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Elizabeth Neighborhood Location:** 2001 E 7th St, Ste G, Charlotte NC 28204 (Corner of E 7th St & Hawthorne Ln).
2. **Traditional Cantonese & Sichuan Specialties:** Cherry House General Tso's Chicken, Szechuan Crispy Shredded Beef, Peking Duck with Lotus Buns, House Special Lo Mein, Steamed Pork Dumplings, & Hand-Folded Wonton Soup.
3. **Hours of Operation:** Mon-Sun 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM (7 days a week).

### Core Design Moves

1. **Elegant Display Serif Typography:** High-fashion serif (*Cormorant Garamond*) paired with clean body sans (*Inter*) and wok kitchen mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Cherry Lacquer & Crimson Red Palette:** Oriental culinary palette anchored in cherry lacquer (`#14070A`), cherry crimson red (`#C51E3A`), silk gold (`#E5A93C`), and cherry blossom blush (`#FDF2F4`).
3. **"The Cherry Blossom Wok & Dim Sum Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`elizabeth-wok-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing 700°F wok-hei Cantonese entrees alongside handcrafted dim sum & Peking duck.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

### Divergent Choices for Ho Ho Cherry House

- **Hero Composition:** Elizabeth Neighborhood Cherry Blossom & Imperial Red Wok Gastronomy Vault split-hero layout: left side features a cherry crimson stamp badge (`"TRADITIONAL CHINESE & WOK CRAFT • ELIZABETH (2001 E 7TH ST)"`), display serif (*Cormorant Garamond*), cherry lacquer & crimson red palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of high-heat wok searing & dim sum.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **HHCH Vault Cards** (`hhch-vault-card`) and Elizabeth culinary highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`hhch-header`, `hhch-brand`, `cherry-hero-stage`, `blossom-stamp-badge`, `hhch-vault-card`, `hhch-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
