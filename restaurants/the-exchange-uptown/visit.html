Uptown Business Services

Getting Here

Transit, Parking & Overstreet Access

Seamless connections to the entire center city:

  • Charlotte Overstreet Mall: Direct climate-controlled skybridge pedestrian access connecting neighboring towers directly into One Independence Center.
  • Parking Garages: On-site covered parking available within the One Independence Center deck with entrances on Trade and 5th Streets.
  • Light Rail & Streetcar: Steps from the CTC/Arena LYNX Blue Line station and Tryon Street Gold Line streetcar stops.
