Our Core Beliefs

Values That Shape Our Hospitality

Approachability, local culinary advocacy, and thoughtful design in the heart of Uptown.

Pillar 01

Accessible Sophistication

We offer fine wines and gourmet foods in an informal, welcoming setting with unpretentious pricing—including $7 glasses of wine and $5 craft drafts.

Pillar 02

Celebrating Carolina

We partner directly with North Carolina producers, from Charlotte cheese artisans to regional bakeries and craft breweries, to showcase regional terroir.

Pillar 03

Convenience Without Compromise

Fast service should never mean sacrificing quality. Every sandwich, salad, and snack is made from scratch with premium ingredients.

In the Heart of Uptown

Connected to the Rhythm of Charlotte

Situated within One Independence Center directly adjacent to Monarch Market and steps from the Charlotte Overstreet Mall, The Exchange is positioned at the intersection of business and culture, offering a tranquil sanctuary amidst the bustling energy of North Tryon Street.
