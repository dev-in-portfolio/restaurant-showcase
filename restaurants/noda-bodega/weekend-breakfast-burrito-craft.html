🌮 Friday & Saturday 8:00 AM Morning Tradition

The NoDa Morning Burrito Ritual

Every Friday and Saturday morning starting at 8:00 AM, NoDa Bodega opens its doors for Charlotte's most sought-after breakfast burritos. Wrapped tight and toasted on the flat-top, each massive burrito is stuffed with sizzling chorizo, crispy potatoes, farm eggs, and scratch salsas.

Morning Standards

The 3 Pillars of Weekend Breakfast

1. Crispy Flat-Top Toasting

Griddling every wrapped burrito on the flat-top with butter until the exterior develops a golden, crunchy seal that locks in melted cheese.

2. Spiced Local Chorizo & Bacon

Cooking robust Mexican chorizo and thick-cut hardwood smoked bacon fresh every morning alongside buttery scrambled farm eggs.

3. Scratch Salsas & Hot Coffee

Serving each burrito with house-roasted tomatillo salsa verde and pairing it with fresh local roasted coffee on our shaded porch.
