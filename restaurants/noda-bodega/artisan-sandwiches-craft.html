🥪 The Art of the Colossal Sub

Crafting Charlotte's Best Sandwiches

At NoDa Bodega, a sandwich is not an afterthought—it is a culinary masterpiece. From sourcing fresh hearth loaves from local Duke's Bread to slow-roasting top rounds of beef and curing peppery pastrami, every bite delivers bold flavor and satisfying heft.

Deli Philosophy

The 3 Pillars of Bodega Sandwiches

1. Fresh Local Duke's Bread

Partnering with Charlotte's legendary bakery for crusty sub rolls, rustic ciabatta, and fragrant marbled rye baked daily.

2. Scratch-Cured & Braised Meats

Roasting beef in-house, slicing hot capicola and prosciutto thin to order, and slow-cooking mojo seasoned pork.

3. Punchy House Relishes & Kimchi

Balancing rich meats with fermented spicy kimchi, house-pickled giardiniera, spicy cherry pepper relish, and scratch vinaigrettes.
