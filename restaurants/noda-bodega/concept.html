🎨 Bohemian Soul & Neighborhood Roots

The Heart of 36th Street

NoDa Bodega was born out of a deep love for the North Davidson arts district and the timeless tradition of the neighborhood corner store. Located at 1200 E 36th St, we created a gathering place where artists, musicians, neighbors, and sandwich aficionados connect over honest, colossal food.

Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of NoDa Bodega

Neighborhood Community

Serving as the living room of 36th Street, welcoming regulars by name and supporting local Charlotte bakers, brewers, and artists.

Uncompromised Quality

Refusing mass-produced deli shortcuts—crafting scratch dressings, fermenting custom toppings, and using fresh local bread daily.

NoDa Arts Culture

Preserving the colorful, quirky, and independent spirit that makes Charlotte's historic arts district one of the South's greatest cultural gems.
