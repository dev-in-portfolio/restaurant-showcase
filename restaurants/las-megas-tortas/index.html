Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before tackling our legendary tortas.

How big are the tortas at Las Megas Tortas? +

Our giant tortas are built on oversized 10-inch fresh Mexican telera bread and weigh between 1.5 to 2 pounds when fully stacked. One torta is easily enough for two meals or to share between two people!

Is your Pastor roasted on a traditional vertical trompo? +

Yes! We use an authentic vertical gas spit trompo topped with a whole pineapple. The outer caramelized layers of achiote-marinated pork are shaved fresh directly into warm corn tortillas.

Do you take phone orders for fast pickup? +

Yes, call us directly at (704) 527-2285 and we will have your hot tortas, tacos, and huaraches packed and ready when you arrive at our South Blvd location.
