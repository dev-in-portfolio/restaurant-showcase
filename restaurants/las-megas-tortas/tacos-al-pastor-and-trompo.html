The Vertical Trompo Tradition

Slow-spinning marinated pork shoulder, caramelized open flame heat, and fresh roasted pineapple.

Pure Taqueria Craft

Real Spit-Roasted Al Pastor

True Al Pastor cannot be rushed or simulated on a flat grill. We stack thinly sliced pork shoulder onto a vertical spit (trompo), seasoning each layer with our house marinade of ground achiote paste, guajillo chiles, cumin, Mexican oregano, and sour orange juice.

A fresh whole peeled pineapple crowns the top of the spit. As the trompo spins against radiant heat, pineapple juices baste the roasting pork, creating caramelized crispy edges while keeping the core succulent and juicy.

  • Carved paper-thin directly into double warm corn tortillas
  • Finished with a swift flick of fresh roasted pineapple
  • Garnished with finely minced white onion, cilantro, and fiery salsa
