The Epic Torta Cubana Chilanga

In Mexico City, the name "Torta Cubana" does not refer to Cuba?it originated on Calle Rep?blica de Cuba in the historic center, where sandwich masters began piling on every single meat in the kitchen for ravenous customers.

At Las Megas Tortas, we honor this larger-than-life street tradition. We split an oversized 10-inch artisanal telera bun, slather both sides with velvety refried pinto beans and mayo, and toast it on the plancha until the outer crust is crisp and golden.

  • Layer 1 & 2: Crispy breaded beef milanesa & spiced pierna pork
  • Layer 3 & 4: Griddled all-beef hot dog franks & smoked ham
  • Layer 5: Traditional spiced head cheese (queso de puerco)
  • Layer 6 & 7: Molten quesillo Oaxaca cheese, whole Hass avocado, and pickled jalape?os
