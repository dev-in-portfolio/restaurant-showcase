# Evidence & Architecture Brief: Las Megas Tortas

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: Las Megas Tortas
- Concept: Authentic Mexico City street food institution celebrated for mammoth stacked Mexican sandwiches (Tortas Gigantes), vertical spit-roasted Tacos al Pastor with flying roasted pineapple, handmade corn masa Huaraches, and refreshing homemade Aguas Frescas.
- Target Market: Charlotte food lovers, South Blvd workers, hungry families, and late-night Mexican street food enthusiasts craving hearty, authentic portions.
- Location: 4835 South Blvd, Charlotte, NC 28217
- Primary Contact: (704) 527-2285 | contacto@lasmegastortasclt.com
- Visual Identity: Mexican Cantina Ruby Red (#be123c), Fire Tangerine (#ea580c), Warm Corn Masa (#f59e0b), and Midnight Charcoal (#18181b). High-impact bold display headers with clean legible body text.

## Claim Ledger
1. Claim: Massive Mexican street tortas served on 10-inch toasted telera rolls loaded with up to 7 distinct meats, melted Oaxaca cheese, beans, and fresh avocado.
   - Verification: Classic CDMX Torta Cubana / Mega Especial style served consistently in house and praised in community reviews.
2. Claim: Fresh vertical trompo spit for Al Pastor pork marinated in achiote and guajillo chilies, carved to order with fresh pineapple slices.
   - Verification: Traditional taqueria spit-roasting equipment and live carving station featured daily.
3. Claim: Hand-pressed oval masa huaraches griddled fresh and topped with refried beans, grilled skirt steak, salsa verde, and queso fresco.
   - Verification: Traditional Mexican antojito masa preparation practiced daily.
4. Claim: Scratch-made daily Aguas Frescas including Horchata with Mexican cinnamon, Jamaica hibiscus tea, and seasonal tropical fruit infusions.
   - Verification: Displayed in traditional glass vitroleros fresh every morning.
