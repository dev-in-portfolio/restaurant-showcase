Antipasti & Insalate

Classic Italian starters and garden salads featuring imported specialties.

Creamy artisanal Pugliese burrata cheese paired with aged Prosciutto di Parma, roasted Campari tomatoes, and Genovese basil pesto.

Primi Piatti: Artisanal Pastas & Risotti

Handmade daily in our cucina using imported Italian flours and farm eggs.

Tagliolini al Tartufo Nero $32.00

Delicate thin egg pasta ribbons tossed in a velvety cream sauce with shaved black summer truffles, parmigiano, and sweet cream butter.

Pappardelle con Ragù di Cinghiale $28.00

Broad hand-cut egg pappardelle noodles with slow-braised Tuscan wild boar ragù, rosemary, juniper berries, and Chianti reduction.

Ravioli di Vitello al Burro e Salvia $29.00

Handmade pasta pillows filled with braised milk-fed veal, finished in browned butter, crispy garden sage, and toasted pine nuts.

Imported Carnaroli rice slow-cooked in rich saffron shellfish broth with jumbo tiger prawns, diver sea scallops, calamari, and PEI mussels.
