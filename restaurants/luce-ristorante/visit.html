Dress Code & Valet Parking

  • Dining Room Dress Code: We request business casual or elegant evening attire. Athletic wear (including gym shorts, leggings, and ball caps), graphic tees, and flip-flops are strictly prohibited.
  • Evening Valet Parking: Complimentary valet parking is provided during dinner service at the Truist Center porte-cochère on 5th Street and College Street.
  • Plaza Pedestrian Access: Guests can enter via the North Tryon Street plaza walkway between 5th and Trade Street.
