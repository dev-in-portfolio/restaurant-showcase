The Neighborhood

The constant on a changing block.

Plaza Midwood is one of Charlotte's most eclectic neighborhoods — breweries, tattoo parlors, new kitchens along Central and Commonwealth — and The Diamond is its throughline: the diner that was here before all of it and fed most of the people who built it.

  • Commonwealth Avenue, front and center

    The diner sits right on 1901 Commonwealth with its patio facing the street — close enough to the Central Avenue strip to walk the whole corridor on a Friday night.

  • The brewery-hop anchor

    Reviewers make the same loop: brewery hopping through Plaza Midwood, then the Winky Dinky Dog and a booth to close it out.

  • A block with memory

    Charlotte has lost legendary diners and dives over the years — the Diamond is the one still cranking, 80 years in, with its neon sign intact.

  • Family-friendly, dog-friendly, everyone-friendly

    Kids' plates with two-side options, a dog-friendly patio, high chairs, and a come-as-you-are room — the neighborhood's actual living room.
