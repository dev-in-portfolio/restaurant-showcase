First Timer's Card

Order it all the way.

That's not a suggestion — it's the local dialect. Here's how the regulars say it.

  • "All The Way"

    Lettuce, tomato, onion, pickle, and mayo on your burger — or your fried bologna sandwich, which reviewers call one of the best anywhere.

  • "Southern Style"

    Chili, mustard, onions, and slaw. The Carolina answer to every topping question.

  • Chips & Dip To Start

    Freshly made potato chips with the Vidalia onion dip — or the hot pimento dip with pita, per The Infatuation.

  • Banana Pudding To Finish

    Homestyle, with chewy, crumbly 'Nilla wafers. Every order should be capped off with it.
