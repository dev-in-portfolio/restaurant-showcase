## Original Audit Weakness

Curry n Cake relies on basic template delivery links and social pages. Located at 2015 E Arbors Dr Suite 270 in Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its dual identity — authentic clay-oven tandoori curry craft paired with artisan 100% eggless fusion bakery cakes.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Mallard Creek Location:** 2015 E Arbors Dr, Suite 270, Charlotte NC 28262 (Mallard Creek / University City Corridor).
2. **Authentic Indian Curries & Tandoori Entrees:** Chicken Tikka Masala, Lamb Rogan Josh, Butter Chicken, Samosa Chaat, Paneer Butter Masala, & Garlic Naan.
3. **100% Eggless Artisan Fusion Bakery:** Signature Eggless Mango Mousse Pastry, Black Forest Cake, Gulab Jamun Cake, & Custom Birthday Celebrations.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Royal Indian Serif Typography:** Royal serif (*Fraunces*) paired with clean body sans (*Inter*) and bakery mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Warm Saffron Charcoal & Royal Amber Palette:** Rich culinary palette anchored in saffron charcoal (`#1C1917`), royal saffron amber (`#D97706`), cardamom green (`#166534`), turmeric gold (`#EAB308`), and spiced ivory (`#FFFBEB`).
3. **"The Clay Tandoori Oven & Eggless Bakery Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`tandoori-curry-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing clay-oven curries alongside artisan 100% eggless fusion pastry cakes.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Great Wagon Road Distilling Co.` — Industrial Copper Distillery with Cinzel fonts and Copper Charcoal/Oak Bronze.
2. `Fumée Kitchen & Cocktails` — 21+ Vibe Lounge with Syne fonts and Deep Obsidian Onyx/Smoked Rose Gold.
3. `Food For Your Soul CLT` — Southern Soul Food with Playfair Display fonts and Cast-Iron Charcoal/Burnt Molasses.

### Divergent Choices for Curry n Cake

- **Hero Composition:** Warm Saffron Spice & Eggless Bakery Vault split-hero layout: left side features a vibrant saffron stamp badge (`"AUTHENTIC INDIAN CURRIES & EGGLESS BAKERY • 2015 E ARBORS DR"`), royal Indian serif (*Fraunces*), warm saffron charcoal & royal amber palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of tandoori curries, naan bread, & eggless pastry cakes.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **CNC Vault Cards** (`cnc-vault-card`) and Mallard Creek culinary highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`cnc-header`, `cnc-brand`, `saffron-hero-stage`, `spice-stamp-badge`, `cnc-vault-card`, `cnc-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
