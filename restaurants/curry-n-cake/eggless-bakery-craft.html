🍰 100% Vegetarian Eggless Bakery

Artisan Eggless Fusion

Baking without eggs requires extraordinary technique. At Curry n Cake, our master bakers combine Indian sweet traditions with European pastry arts, crafting moist 100% eggless cakes infused with mango mousse, cardamom, & Belgian chocolate.

Bakery Craft Science

The 3 Pillars of Eggless Bakery Craft

1. 100% Vegetarian Purity

Every cake, slice, & pastry is strictly 100% eggless & vegetarian, crafted in a dedicated egg-free bakery bay.

2. Alphonso Mango & Indian Fusion

We infuse real Alphonso mango pulp, saffron strands, cardamom pods, & Gulab Jamun into light sponge layers.

3. Custom Birthday Pre-Orders

We design custom multi-tier eggless celebration cakes for birthdays, anniversaries, & weddings across Charlotte.
