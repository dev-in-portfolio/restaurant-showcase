🍫 Clay Tandoori Oven Heat

The Art of Tandoori Spices

Authentic Indian cooking relies on the elemental power of the clay tandoor oven. At Curry n Cake, our meats & naan breads are seared over 700°F hardwood charcoal embers before being combined with 12-hour simmered spice gravies.

Spice Science

The 3 Pillars of Curry n Cake Gastronomy

1. Whole Spice Tempering (Tadka)

Whole cardamom pods, cumin, cloves, & cinnamon sticks are bloomed in hot ghee to release essential oils.

2. 700°F Clay Tandoor Sear

Tandoori meats & naan flatbreads are cooked inside earthen clay pots, locking in smoky charcoal flavor.

3. 12-Hour Gravy Simmer

Curry bases are slow-cooked for half a day with fresh ginger, garlic, tomatoes, & roasted fenugreek leaves.
