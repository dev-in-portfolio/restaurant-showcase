Arrival Directives

South End Parking & Transit Access

Visiting Stable Hand is straightforward with multiple access options:

  • On-Site Surface Lot: Dedicated parking stalls for Stable Hand customers are located directly in front of the building at 125 Remount Road.
  • Lynx Light Rail Access: We are located just two blocks from the New Bern Station on the Charlotte Lynx Blue Line, offering an easy car-free commute from Uptown or South Charlotte.
  • Street Parking: Unmetered street parking is available along Remount Road and adjacent neighborhood side streets.
Space & Seating

Seating, Wi-Fi & Dog-Friendly Patio

How our space functions throughout the day:

  • Indoor Tables & Power: Ample blonde wood tables and bar counter stools equipped with accessible power outlets and high-speed customer Wi-Fi.
  • Outdoor Dog-Friendly Patio: Open-air patio seating bathed in morning sunshine, welcoming four-legged companions with water bowls.
  • Evening Wine Hangouts: From Tuesday through Saturday we remain open until 7:00 PM for after-work natural wine pours, beer, and light bites.
