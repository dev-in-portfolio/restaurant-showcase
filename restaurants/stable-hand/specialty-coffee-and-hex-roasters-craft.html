Brewing Standards

The Four Tenets of HEX Coffee Sourcing

Meticulous sourcing and preparation define every cup served over our counter.

Standard 01

Direct Trade Terroir

HEX partners directly with smallholder farmers, cooperatives, and washing stations across East Africa and Latin America, paying equitable premiums for top-tier quality.

Standard 02

Dialed-In Calibration

Every morning our barista team tastes, weighs, and calibrates grinder burrs and brew temperatures to account for humidity, bar pressure, and degas timelines.

Standard 03

House-Steeped Syrups

We never use commercial flavored syrups. Our kitchen steeps real garden herbs, whole spices, and fermented miso to create balanced, culinary-minded seasonal drinks.

Signature Beverage

The Story of Miso Butterscotch

Our most celebrated beverage was born from culinary cross-pollination. By blending savory red miso with caramelized butterscotch, vanilla, and espresso, we created a latte that delivers rich umami depth without overwhelming the natural stone-fruit brightness of HEX roasted beans.
