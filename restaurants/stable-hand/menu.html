Cafe, Kitchen & Cellar Offerings.

At Stable Hand, everything begins with thoughtful sourcing and kitchen craftsmanship. From meticulously roasted coffees by sister roaster HEX to from-scratch breakfast sandwiches on buttery brioche, nourishing rice porridge, and a hand-selected natural wine list, our menu nourishes from early morning to twilight.
