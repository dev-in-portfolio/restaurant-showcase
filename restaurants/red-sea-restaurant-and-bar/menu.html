Authentic Horn of Africa Stews, Skillet Tibs, & Vegan Feasts.

Meat Specialties Berbere & Skillet

Doro Wat (National Dish) #1 Signature

Tender chicken drumsticks slow-simmered for hours in rich berbere chili sauce with sweet caramelized onions, spiced clarified butter (niter kibbeh), and a whole hard-boiled egg on fresh injera.

Sizzling Beef Tibs Skillet Seared

Prime cubes of tender beef pan-seared over high flame with fresh rosemary sprigs, sliced jalapeño peppers, garlic, and red onions in spiced herb butter, served sizzling hot.

Yebeg Alicha (Mild Lamb Stew) Aromatic Mild

Succulent bone-in lamb simmered gently in mild turmeric, fresh ginger, garlic, and sweet onion broth with whole cloves and cardamom.

Kitfo (Spiced Minced Beef) Habesha Classic

Lean minced prime beef warmed gently in mitmita chili powder and seasoned clarified butter, served with house-made ayib farmer's cheese and collard greens.

Vegan Platters & Drinks Plant-Based & Bar

Grand Vegetarian Combo Platter 100% Vegan

Vibrant sampler platter featuring Misir Wat (spicy red lentils), Kik Alicha (yellow split peas), Gomen (braised collards), Shiro Wat (seasoned chickpea stew), Fasolia (green beans & carrots), and Atkilt Wat (cabbage & potatoes).

Shiro Wat (Ground Chickpea Stew) Clay Pot Stew

Roasted ground chickpeas and split peas simmered with garlic, ginger, and aromatic Ethiopian herbs into a velvety, deeply savory clay pot stew.

Tej (Ethiopian Honey Wine) Traditional Mead

Ancient fermented honey wine brewed with gesho leaves, offering a naturally sweet, aromatic golden beverage served in traditional round-bottom flask glasses.

Habesha Coffee Ceremony Fresh Roasted

Fresh green Ethiopian coffee beans pan-roasted, freshly ground, and brewed in a clay jebena pot, served in small cups with burning frankincense aromatics and popcorn.
