Tradition & Hospitality

The 3 Pillars of Red Sea Tibs & Ritual

1. High-Heat Skillet Tibs

Flash-searing tender prime beef or lamb with fresh rosemary sprigs, sliced jalapeños, and garlic in spiced niter kibbeh butter.

2. Plant-Based Fasting Feasts

Offering one of Charlotte's most extensive vegan menus with split peas, red lentils, collard greens, and cabbage slow-stewed with herbs.

3. 3-Round Coffee Ceremony

Pan-roasting raw green beans, brewing in clay jebenapot, and serving three sacred rounds: Abol (strength), Tona (transformation), and Baraka (blessing).
