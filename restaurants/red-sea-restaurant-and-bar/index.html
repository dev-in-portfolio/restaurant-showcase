Ancient Grain Fermentation

Naturally Fermented Teff Injera

Injera is not merely bread—it is the plate, the utensil, and the heart of every meal. Crafted from iron-rich teff grain, our batter undergoes slow natural sourdough fermentation before being poured onto clay griddles. The resulting spongy texture captures every drop of spicy doro wat and savory tibs.

The Habesha Hospitality Ritual

Traditional Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony (Jebena Buna)

Coffee was born in Ethiopia, and sharing a pot of freshly brewed Jebena buna is the highest sign of hospitality. Raw green beans are pan-roasted until aromatic, ground by hand, and slow-steeped in a clay Jebena pot, served in small finjan cups with roasted barley.
