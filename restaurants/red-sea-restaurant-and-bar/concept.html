The Red Sea Story

Horn of Africa Soul, Queen City Roots.

For years, Red Sea Restaurant & Bar has stood as a beacon of Habesha culture in Charlotte. Named after the historic waterway connecting Eritrea and the Horn of Africa to the world, our restaurant was created to share the beauty of communal dining, time-honored spice craftsmanship, and heartfelt hospitality.
