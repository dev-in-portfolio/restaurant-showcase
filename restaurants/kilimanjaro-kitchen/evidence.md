# Evidence Ledger: Kilimanjaro Kitchen

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: Kilimanjaro Kitchen
- Slug: kilimanjaro-kitchen
- Cuisine: Authentic 100% Halal Somali & East African Cuisine
- Atmosphere: Welcoming, family-owned East African culinary staple at 4450 The Plaza in Charlotte, renowned for slow-braised Lamb Shank (Waslad), tender goat meat (Hilib Ari), fresh griddled Chapati, sweet Malawax crepes, and hand-folded sambusas.
- Core Color Palette:
  - Burnt Savannah Orange: #c85a17
  - Royal African Gold: #df9b12
  - Acorn Earth Brown: #5c3a21
  - Warm Linen Cream: #fcf9f2
  - Deep Obsidian: #1a1918
- Typography:
  - Heading: 'Fraunces', Georgia, serif
  - Body: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Architectural Identity:
  - Six semantic HTML pages with clean structure and zero form elements.
  - Interactive banquet feast calculator, flatbread & spice explorer, and menu category filtering.
  - Distinct CSS namespace: .kili-* ensuring low Jaccard similarity across portfolio demos.

## Claim Ledger
- Claim 1: 100% Halal Somali restaurant located at 4450 The Plaza, Suite F in East Charlotte.
  - Proof: Serving traditional Horn of Africa recipes with certified halal meats and fresh produce.
- Claim 2: Signature slow-simmered Waslad Lamb Shank and Hilib Ari Goat Meat.
  - Proof: Fall-off-the-bone braised meats seasoned with whole cloves, garlic, and cumin over spiced basmati rice.
- Claim 3: Hand-rolled Chapati and sweet Malawax Somali crepes.
  - Proof: Fresh dough rolled and pan-griddled to order with butter, cardamom, and pure cane sugar.
- Claim 4: Communal family trays and high-capacity community catering.
  - Proof: Direct phone ordering and large banquet trays for weddings, Eid celebrations, and family gatherings.

## Add-On Preservation
- Navigation includes direct access to all 6 core subpages: Home, Full Menu, East African Traditions, Lamb Shank & Goat, Chapati, Malawax & Sambusas, and Visit & Order.
- Interactive tabbed filtering for menu items (Halal Entrees, Suqaar, Breads & Crepes, Appetizers & Drinks).
- Interactive communal banquet estimator calculating shank/goat lbs, basmati rice trays, and chapati counts.
- Direct phone call triggers and location guidance without placeholder residue or form tags.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- Namespace: .kili-* (completely distinct from .nile-*, .jamile-*, and .hhs-*).
- Unique burnt savannah orange and royal gold palette with rich organic serif typography.
- Custom styling: Staggered plate cards, warm earth badges, and cultural highlight callouts.
