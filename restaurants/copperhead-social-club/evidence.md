## Original Audit Weakness

Copperhead Social Club relies on basic toasttab ordering pages (`copperheadsocial.com`, `toasttab.com`). Located at 2810 N Davidson St in NoDa, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its house-turned-social-club vibe, signature griddled smash burger alchemy, craft Carolina draft selection, and late-night patio energy.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **NoDa N Davidson St Location:** 2810 N Davidson St, Charlotte NC 28205 (historic converted bungalow in NoDa).
2. **Signature Smash Burgers:** "The Copperhead" with house secret sauce & banana peppers, Oklahoma Onion Burger, Green Chile Double, and Kimchi Bacon Double.
3. **Bar Sides & Craft Drafts:** Loaded crinkle-cut fries, poutine, chicken wings, Carolina craft drafts, outdoor patio garden, and indoor dart lounge.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Industrial Metal Typography:** Raw industrial display sans (*Space Grotesk* / *Outfit*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and pub ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Burnished Copper & Neon Amber Palette:** High-energy palette anchored in scorched copper charcoal (`#0F1416`), burnished copper amber (`#D96B27`), electric neon flame (`#FF3D00`), aged copper rust (`#A84318`), and soft foam linen (`#F7F4EF`).
3. **"The Griddled Smash Burger & Craft Taproom Science Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`smash-burger-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing crispy lace smash burgers alongside loaded fries and Carolina craft drafts.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Cool Idiot Coffee` — Playful specialty coffee bar with Bricolage Grotesque/Outfit fonts and Cake Pop Magenta.
2. `Comal Taco Co.` — Sonoran comal taco stall with Syne/Outfit fonts and Habanero Amber.
3. `Coffey Creek Café` — 1987 Southern diner with Lora/Outfit fonts and Copper Chestnut.

### Divergent Choices for Copperhead Social Club

- **Hero Composition:** Industrial Metal & NoDa Neon Social Club Vault split-hero layout: left side features a copper coil & neon club stamp badge (`"NODA SOCIAL CLUB • 2810 N DAVIDSON ST"`), ultra-bold industrial typography (*Space Grotesk*), electric copper & neon amber palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of signature double smash burgers, loaded crinkle fries, and craft beers on a wooden patio table.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Copperhead Vault Cards** (`copperhead-vault-card`) and **NoDa Patio Garden Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`copperhead-header`, `club-brand`, `copperhead-hero-stage`, `copper-stamp-badge`, `copperhead-vault-card`, `copperhead-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
