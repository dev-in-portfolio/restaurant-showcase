Flat-Top Griddle Science

The Science of The Smash Sear

Great smash burgers are an exact science. Smashed hard against a 450°F steel griddle with a custom iron press, the beef caramelizes instantly, creating razor-thin lacy edges and locking in maximum savory juiciness.

Sear Technique

The 3 Pillars of Copperhead Smash Craft

1. Custom 80/20 Beef Blend

We grind chuck and brisket daily for the ideal fat-to-lean ratio needed for high-heat griddling.

2. High-Pressure Iron Press

Beef balls are smashed paper-thin within the first 10 seconds to maximize Maillard browning surface area.

3. Steam-Melted American Cheese

Real American cheese slices are layered over hot patties and steamed under a metal dome for gooey meltiness.
