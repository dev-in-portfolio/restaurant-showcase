24-Hour Broths, Pristine Nigiri.

Welcome to YUME Ramen Sushi & Bar, South End’s premier destination for authentic Japanese ramen, traditional nigiri, and izakaya small plates. Founded by Chef Tony Zheng and Michiko Zheng, we slow-simmer our pork bone tonkotsu for 24 continuous hours, hand-roll delicate sushi, and pour curated Japanese whiskies and sakes.

The Sushi Bar

Artisan Nigiri & Specialty Rolls

Every slice of sashimi and piece of nigiri at YUME represents knife precision and seasonal freshness. Savor our Chef's Nigiri Moriawase, featuring hand-pressed sweet ebi shrimp, fatty salmon, yellowtail hamachi, and white tuna alongside specialty rolls garnished with house-grated wasabi root and pickled gari ginger.

Izakaya Favorite

Pan-Seared Pork Gyoza Dumplings

Our handmade dumplings are folded by hand with seasoned ground pork, crisp napa cabbage, fresh garlic, and toasted sesame oil. Pan-seared until the bottoms develop a golden, shatteringly crisp lace crust while the interior stays succulent and savory, served with our house scallion-soy dipping sauce.
