Our signature Tonkotsu broth requires a continuous 24-hour rolling simmer to break down heritage pork marrow and collagen into an unctuous, milky emulsion. The Five Elements

Anatomy of an Authentic Ramen Bowl

Every element is balanced to complement the broth's savory weight.

Element 01

24-Hour Rolling Boil

Pork femur and knuckle bones are soaked, blanched, and boiled furiously for 24 continuous hours. The intense convective heat emulsifies natural fats and collagen into a creamy, luxurious broth.

Element 02

Ajitsuke Tamago Egg

Soft-boiled with surgical precision to ensure a firm, tender white around a golden jammy molten yolk, then steeped for 24 hours in a sweet dashi-soy-mirin marinade.

Element 03

Black Garlic Mayu Oil

Whole garlic cloves are slow-roasted in sesame oil across seven distinct temperature stages until completely charred and black, yielding a complex, smoky, bittersweet aromatic oil.

Craftsmanship

Braised Chashu Pork Belly

Our chashu is hand-rolled and simmered for five hours in a reduction of Japanese sake, mirin, dark soy sauce, ginger root, and scallions. Sliced thick, each portion is lightly seared with a culinary torch just before landing in the steaming bowl, releasing deep caramelization and melt-on-the-tongue tenderness.
