The Dream (夢) • Established in Charlotte

The Culinary Dream of Tony & Michiko Zheng.

In Japanese, the word "Yume" means "Dream." When husband-and-wife team Tony and Michiko Zheng founded YUME, their vision was singular: to create a genuine Japanese culinary sanctuary in Charlotte where craft ramen, pristine raw fish, and izakaya culture could coexist without compromise.

Guiding Principles

The Values of YUME Hospitality

Rooted in Japanese craftsmanship and genuine Queen City warmth.

Principle 01

Authentic Extraction

We never use bouillon powder, flavor boosters, or shortcuts. Our 24-hour broths are slow-extracted from bones, konbu, and aromatics, exactly as done in traditional Tokyo ramen shops.

Principle 02

Seafood Freshness

We maintain rigorous direct relationships with reputable coastal fishmongers, ensuring each piece of tuna, salmon, and hamachi arrives at peak freshness and flavor.

Principle 03

Omotenashi Spirit

In Japanese hospitality, omotenashi means caring for guests with complete mindfulness and attention to detail. Every visit to our Wilmore dining room is tailored with warmth.

Neighborhood Vibe

The Wilmore & South End Connection

Situated on South Mint Street between the historic tree-lined streets of Wilmore and the bustling energy of South End, YUME provides a serene, stylish escape. Guests gather at our open ramen counter to watch steam rise from noodle baskets, relax at the sushi bar with sashimi moriawase, or enjoy hand-shaken cocktails in our modern dining room.
