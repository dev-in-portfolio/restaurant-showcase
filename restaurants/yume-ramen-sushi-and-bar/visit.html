Parking & Neighborhood Access

Visiting YUME in South End / Wilmore is convenient:

  • Dedicated Customer Parking: A designated surface parking lot is located directly adjacent to our building on South Mint Street.
  • Street Parking: Ample, free neighborhood street parking is available along South Mint St and West Spruce St.
  • LYNX Light Rail: Located just a few blocks from the Bland Street and East/West Boulevard LYNX Blue Line stations.
