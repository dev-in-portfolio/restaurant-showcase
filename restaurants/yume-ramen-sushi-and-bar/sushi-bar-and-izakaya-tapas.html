The Izakaya Ritual

Shared Plates & Raw Bar Precision

Designed for social dining, pairings with sake, and lingering evenings.

Ritual 01

Seasoned Akazu Sushi Rice

Our sushi rice is seasoned with traditional red sake lees vinegar (akazu), giving the warm grains a subtle savory sweetness that harmonizes with fatty tuna, salmon, and yellowtail.

Ritual 02

Crispy Pork Lace Gyoza

Dumplings crimped by hand each afternoon, seared in cast-iron skillets with a splash of starch water to create a shatteringly crisp lace veil on the bottom.

Ritual 03

Steamed Kakuni Baos

Tender lotus leaf steamed bao buns stuffed with thick-cut 12-hour braised pork belly, house-pickled cucumber ribbons, and sweet scallion hoisin glaze.

Izakaya Excellence

The Perfect Drink Pairing

In Japan, an evening at an izakaya begins with an order of sizzling hot pork gyoza or karaage fried chicken alongside a frosty draft beer or a chilled whisky highball. At YUME, our bar team crafts refreshing highballs using Suntory Toki whisky and super-chilled soda, cutting cleanly through the richness of our braised meats.
