The Anatomy of Pollo con Tajadas

Layered with precision to create the ultimate balance of crunch, savory chicken, and tangy slaw.

Layer 1: The Foundation

Tajadas de Guineo Verde

Green bananas shaved ultra-thin and fried crisp on demand, forming a crunchy, savory bed that absorbs the savory chicken drippings.

Layer 2: Golden Chicken

Spiced Crispy Chicken

Marinated in garlic, cumin, oregano, and achiote, then fried to golden perfection with juicy tender meat and shattering skin.

Layer 3 & 4: Slaw & Pink Sauce

Curtido, Chimol & Aderezo

Topped with finely shredded cabbage, fresh chimol salsa, ruby pickled onions, and our creamy signature Honduran pink sauce.
