Menu Catracho
Asados, Pollo con Tajadas & Baleadas

Asados a la Parrilla & Pollo con Tajadas

Charcoal-grilled meats and crispy chicken served San Pedro Sula style.

Pollo con Tajadas (Pollo Chuco) $14.50

Crispy spiced quarter chicken served over a bed of fried green banana slices (tajadas de guineo), shredded cabbage, pickled pink onions, fresh chimol, and house pink sauce.

Carne Asada Catracha $15.50

Tender flank steak marinated in citrus and garlic, grilled over charcoal embers. Served with sweet fried plantains, refried red beans, chimol, queso duro, and handmade tortillas.

Chuleta con Tajadas $14.00

Charcoal-grilled seasoned pork chop served over green plantain slices, curtido cabbage salad, chimol, and dressing.

Plato Mixto Catracho $16.50

Combination feast of grilled carne asada, smoked chorizo catracho, and crispy fried chicken with tajadas, beans, avocado, and tortillas.

Baleadas Hechas a Mano

Freshly stretched giant flour tortillas cooked on hot griddles.

Baleada Sencilla $4.50

Handmade thick flour tortilla folded with warm stewed Honduran red beans, rich mantequilla blanca, and grated queso duro.

Baleada con Huevo $6.00

Handmade flour tortilla stuffed with refried red beans, mantequilla blanca, cheese, and fluffy scrambled eggs.

Baleada Mixta con Carne $8.50

Loaded with refried red beans, mantequilla blanca, dry cheese, scrambled eggs, and tender chopped grilled carne asada.

Baleada Especial Completa $9.50

The ultimate Honduran baleada: carne asada, grilled chorizo, scrambled eggs, fresh avocado slices, mantequilla, and cheese.

Antojitos Catrachos & Bebidas Naturales

Street appetizers and refreshing tropical juices.

Yuca con Chicharrón $11.00

Boiled and lightly fried yuca root topped with crispy golden pork cracklings, pickled cabbage slaw, and savory tomato sauce.

Pastelitos de Carne (3 pcs) $7.50

Crispy fried cornmeal turnovers stuffed with seasoned minced beef and rice, served with cabbage slaw and salsa chismol.

Horchata de Morro $4.00

Traditional Honduran spiced seed and rice beverage infused with cinnamon, vanilla, and sweet milk.

Jugo Natural de Maracuyá $4.00

Freshly pressed natural passion fruit juice served chilled over ice.
