Imported Heritage

Mantequilla Blanca

True Honduran sour cream—richer, thicker, and saltier than common crema—providing the essential creamy base for our handmade baleadas.

Slow Stewed

Frijoles de Seda

Traditional small red silk beans simmered for hours with garlic, onion, and lard, then mashed and refried until velvety smooth.

Fresh Cut

Curtido & Chismol

Our house-made chimol salsa and pickled red cabbage curtido add bright acidity and crunch to cut through rich charcoal-grilled meats.
