# Creative Design Brief: Amélie's French Bakery & Café

## 1. Restaurant Identity & Intelligence
- **Name**: Amélie's French Bakery & Café
- **Locations**: NoDa Flagship (Charlotte, NC), Park Road, Carmel Commons, Rock Hill SC
- **Cuisine**: French Patisserie, Viennoiserie, Savory Tartines & Specialty Espresso
- **Core Personality**: Whimsical Parisian salon, bohemian charm, velvet seating, vintage chandeliers, French blue plaster walls, and hand-piped macarons.

## 2. Three Concept Directions Evaluated

### Concept A: Parisian Curiosity Cabinet & Salon (SELECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Eclectic Parisian elegance meets bohemian art salon. Deep teal (`#1b4958`), warm French cream (`#faf6f0`), gold leaf accents (`#c69214`), and rose accent (`#9f496e`).
- **Typography**: *Cormorant Garamond* (display headers), *Caveat* (handwritten notes), and *DM Sans* (body).
- **Signature Feature**: Interactive Parisian Pastry Case & Macaron Display Cabinet.
- **Header Strategy**: Brand badge + script tagline + adaptive mobile drawer activating at 1024px.

### Concept B: Midnight Café & Charcoal Journal (REJECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Dark moody charcoal and sepia tones. Rejected as too close to dark steakhouse templates.

### Concept C: Minimalist Bright Bakery Atelier (REJECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Stark white marble and terracotta. Rejected for lacking Amélie's signature bohemian warmth.

## 3. Art Direction & Rationale
- **Color Logic**:
  - Primary Teal (`#1b4958`): Inspired by French salon wall trim.
  - French Cream (`#faf6f0`): Warm parchment backdrop avoiding harsh pure white.
  - Gold Leaf (`#c69214`): Gilded chandelier and antique mirror accents.
  - Raspberry Rose (`#9f496e`): Macaron and berry highlights.
- **Typography Logic**: Classic French serif hierarchy paired with handwritten script annotations for an authentic café journal feel.
- **Signature Interaction**: Interactive Pastry Cabinet allowing guests to explore macaron flavors and barista coffee pairings.
- **Responsive Layout**: Custom adaptive header tested at 1440px, 1280px, 1100px, 1024px, 900px, 768px, 430px, 390px, 360px with zero horizontal scroll or element clipping.
