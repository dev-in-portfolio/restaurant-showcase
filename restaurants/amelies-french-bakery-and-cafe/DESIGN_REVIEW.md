# Design Review & Quality Gate: Amélie's French Bakery & Café

## 1. Initial Failure Pattern Critique
- **Previous Defect**: The initial rebuild relied on a generic dark navy steakhouse template with orange buttons, generic emoji cards, and generic "Chef Special Creation" copy.
- **Root Cause**: Over-reliance on uniform layout templates without grounding the design in Amélie's real NoDa bohemian salon identity.
- **Correction Executed**: Replaced generic template with Concept A ("Parisian Curiosity Cabinet & Salon"), featuring French cream parchment, duck-egg teal, gold filigree accents, French typography, and an interactive macaron cabinet.
