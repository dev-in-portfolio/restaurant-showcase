Born in NoDa, Loved Across the Carolinas

The Amélie’s Story

What began as a whimsical dream in Charlotte's historic NoDa arts neighborhood has grown into a landmark French bakery celebrated for its Parisian salon atmosphere, velvet sofas, hand-picked chandeliers, and unyielding commitment to scratch pastry craftsmanship.

THE CRAFT PHILOSOPHY

Scratch-Baked Without Shortcuts

Every morning before dawn, our master bakers fold French butter into laminated doughs, whip fresh fruit curds, and hand-pipe delicate almond meringue shells. We believe true French patisserie requires patience, precision, and the finest ingredients.

THE SALON AESTHETIC

Curiosity & Bohemian Comfort

Our interiors are designed as living art galleries — eclectic combinations of French blue plaster walls, ornate gold mirrors, mismatched velvet armchairs, and glowing crystal chandeliers that encourage guests to linger, create, and connect.

A Legacy of Sweetness

From One Small Case to a Carolinas Institution

Over the past two decades, Amélie's has expanded to multiple locations across Charlotte and Rock Hill while retaining the soul of a friendly neighborhood bakery where students, artists, families, and travelers feel equally at home.
