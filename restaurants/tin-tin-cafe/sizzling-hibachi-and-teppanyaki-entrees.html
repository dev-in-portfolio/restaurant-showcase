The secrets behind our flavorful, juicy hibachi entrees.

High-Heat Searing

Tender Sirloin & Poultry

We cut our sirloin steak into bite-sized morsels and sear them rapidly on iron flat tops with garlic butter and soy to caramelize the exterior while preserving tenderness inside.

Complete Platter

House Fried Rice & Veggies

Every hibachi entree includes a generous mound of egg fried rice, fresh sautéed zucchini and sweet onions, and slowly simmered sweet glazed carrots.

Secret Recipe

Homemade White Sauce

Our house-blended white yum yum sauce is whisked fresh daily with subtle garlic, sweet paprika, and rich creaminess, providing the ideal accompaniment for dipping.

Popular Duos

Hibachi Steak & Shrimp Combination

For diners who want the best of land and sea, our Steak & Shrimp Combo ($18.95) pairs seared sirloin cubes with succulent jumbo shrimp seasoned with fresh lemon and garlic butter. Served with steaming fried rice and crisp vegetables, it remains one of University City’s top dinner favorites.
