The Sushi Bar Standards

Craftsmanship Behind the Counter

Freshness, knife precision, and authentic seasoning in every bite.

Seafood Integrity

Sushi-Grade Sourcing

We slice only clean, pristine salmon, yellowfin tuna, and yellowtail daily, maintaining strict temperature controls so that every piece of nigiri and sashimi tastes clean and delicate.

Balanced Seasoning

Seasoned Sushi Rice

Proper sushi starts with rice. Our sushi rice is cooked with short-grain japonica rice and gently folded with brewed rice vinegar, sea salt, and a hint of sugar to achieve the perfect bite.

Crunch & Texture

Tempura-Fried Rolls

From our classic Spider Roll with whole fried soft-shell crab to tempura shrimp rolls with sweet eel sauce, we deliver a delightful interplay of crispy crunch and tender seafood.

Crowd Favorite

The Signature Spider Roll

Our Spider Roll ($13.95) features whole tempura-fried soft-shell crab, crisp cucumber, fresh lettuce, and ripe avocado rolled in sushi rice, finished with a generous drizzle of savory sweet kabayaki eel sauce. It offers an irresistible contrast of hot, crispy crunch and cool sushi rice.
