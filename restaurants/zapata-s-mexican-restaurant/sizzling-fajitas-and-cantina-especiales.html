The Hallmarks of Our Mexican Kitchen

The Sizzle

Fajitas Texanas

Marinated skirt steak, seasoned chicken breast, and tender shrimp seared over screaming-hot cast iron with caramelized bell peppers and sweet onions ($22.99), served with guacamole salad.

Charlotte Staple

Jalapeño Cheese Dip

Our iconic creamy white cheese dip infused with pickled jalapeños and roasted spices ($7.99). Served piping hot with endless crispy corn tortilla chips.

Jalisco Tradition

Tacos de Birria

Three crispy griddled corn tortillas filled with slow-cooked shredded beef, melted Oaxaca cheese, cilantro, and diced onions, served with savory dipping consommé ($17.99).

Platos Fuertes

Carne Asada a la Tampiqueña

A classic Mexican feast ($23.99). Premium marinated skirt steak charbroiled over open flames, paired with a traditional cheese enchilada, grilled green onions, refried pinto beans, Mexican rice, and fresh guacamole.
