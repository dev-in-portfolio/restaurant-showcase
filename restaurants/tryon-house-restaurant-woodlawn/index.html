From morning breakfast platters with crispy thick bacon and skillet hash browns to evening char-broiled ribeyes, chopped hamburger steaks, Greek gyros, and daily homemade pies, we deliver honest comfort on every plate.

Char-Broiled Ribeye Steak Dinner

Seared over intense open grates to lock in rich marbling and savory juices, our hand-cut ribeye steak is seasoned with our signature house steak rub. Served with a sea-salt crusted baked potato loaded with sour cream and scallions, hot dinner roll, and house crisp garden salad.

Fresh-Baked Southern Pecan Pie

Baked fresh in our kitchen each morning following a heritage Southern recipe, our pecan pie features caramelized pecan halves layered over a rich brown sugar and cane syrup custard in a tender, flaky pastry crust. Served warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
