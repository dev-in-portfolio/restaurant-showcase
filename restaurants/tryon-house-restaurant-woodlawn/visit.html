Effortless Arrival

On-Site Free Diner Parking Lot

Parking is simple and stress-free at Tryon House:

  • Private Surface Lot: A large, well-lit free parking lot surrounds our building on E Exmore Street, with designated accessible spaces.
  • Easy Highway Access: Conveniently accessible from I-77 (Exit 6B Woodlawn Road), South Boulevard, and Tyvola Road.
  • LYNX Blue Line: Located within walking distance of the Woodlawn LYNX light rail station.
Call Ahead Takeout

Fresh Diner Meals to Go

Enjoy our homestyle cooking wherever you are:

  • Call Ahead: Dial (704) 527-6545 to place your breakfast, lunch, or dinner order for fast pickup at our register.
  • Hot & Fresh: Breakfast plates, burger baskets, and ribeye dinners are packed carefully to stay steaming hot.
  • Whole Pies to Go: Whole homemade pecan, lemon meringue, and coconut cream pies available for pickup with 24 hours notice.
