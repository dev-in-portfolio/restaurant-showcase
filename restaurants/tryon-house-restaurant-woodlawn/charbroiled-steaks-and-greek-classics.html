Char-Broiled Cuts • Greek Family Tradition

Open-Flame Grilling, Timeless Classics.

Like the landmark family diners of Charlotte’s mid-century culinary heyday, Tryon House brings together two great traditions: classic Southern meat-and-potato comfort and time-honored Greek family recipes. From thick char-broiled ribeyes and smothered chopped steaks to rotisserie gyro platters, dinner at Tryon House is generous, authentic, and satisfying.

Dinner Specialties

The Pillars of Our Evening Menu

Hand-cut butcher steaks, scratch pan gravies, and authentic Greek hospitality.

Dinner 01

Open-Flame Char-Broiling

Our ribeyes are trimmed in-house and char-broiled over high-heat flames, rendering the outer fat ribbon into a crisp caramelized crust while locking in rich tenderness.

Dinner 02

Greek Gyro & Tzatziki Craft

We slice seasoned rotisserie gyro meat paper-thin, griddle it for crisp edges, and fold it into warm pita bread with house tzatziki yogurt cucumber sauce and Greek salad.

Dinner 03

Scratch Bakery Showcase

Save room for dessert. Our rotating glass case holds daily homemade Southern pecan, lemon meringue, and coconut cream pies baked from family recipe books.

Southern Dessert Tradition

Carolina Pecan Pie

Every slice of our famous pecan pie is loaded with whole Georgia pecans baked slowly inside an egg and cane syrup custard until set, surrounded by a delicate, flaky pie crust. Warm, sweet, and nutty, it represents the heart of Charlotte Southern baking.
