Butcher Cuts & Culinary Precision

Prime Steaks, Double-Fried Frites.

At The Public House, we reject freezer-to-fryer shortcuts. Our culinary team approaches American comfort tavern food with classic French technique: sourcing prime butcher cuts, grinding custom beef blends in-house, slowly simmering reductions, and double-frying hand-cut potatoes to crispy perfection.

Kitchen Tenets

The Foundations of Our Tavern Fare

Technique, ingredient integrity, and scratch preparation in every dish.

Discipline 01

Prime Flat Iron Searing

Known by butchers as the second most tender cut on the animal, our flat iron steaks are seared cast-iron hot to develop a deep savory crust with tender, juicy beef interior.

Discipline 02

Double-Fried Crispy Frites

Idaho russet potatoes are washed, cut into slender frites, blanched at low heat, rested, and then flash-fried to order for a shattering exterior and pillowy center.

Discipline 03

Proprietary Burger Blends

Double patties smashed hard on chrome planchas to achieve caramelized crispy lacing, stacked with savory bacon jam and aged sharp Wisconsin cheddar.

Tavern Essential

The Remedy Burger Heritage

Named as the ultimate cure for long Uptown workweeks and late-night cravings, The Remedy Burger represents the pinnacle of tavern sandwich engineering. We begin with two custom-ground patties seared with razor-thin caramelized edges. We add savory house-rendered applewood bacon and onion jam, melted Wisconsin cheddar, garlic aioli, and crispy fried shallots, all nestled inside a griddled buttered brioche bun.
