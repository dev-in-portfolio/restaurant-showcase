300 South Brevard • Founded 2019

A Living Room for Uptown Charlotte.

Historically, the great public houses of the world were community epicenters—places where townspeople from all walks of life converged over hot food, honest drink, and spirited debate. Founded in 2019, The Public House was designed to fulfill that exact mission in the center of Uptown Charlotte.

Our Story
Uptown Charlotte's Public Tavern Since 2019
Core Principles

The Values Driving The Public House

Commitment to scratch culinary standards, inclusive gathering, and late-night warmth.

Principle 01

Scratch Craft Over Shortcuts

We make our aiolis in-house, render our bacon jam from scratch, double-fry hand-cut potatoes, and butcher our steaks fresh daily rather than relying on pre-packaged frozen goods.

Principle 02

A True Public Gathering Space

No pretentious dress codes or velvet ropes. We welcome corporate professionals, game-day crowds, convention attendees, and late-night service industry staff with equal enthusiasm.

Principle 03

Late-Night Culinary Haven

While most Uptown hotel kitchens shut down early, our scratch kitchen serves hot Steak N' Frites, juicy smash burgers, and loaded tots until 2:00 AM on weekends.

Transit & Architecture

At the Center of Queen City Motion

Situated on the ground floor of 300 South Brevard, The Public House sits right where Charlotte works, commutes, and celebrates. Directly adjacent to the LYNX Blue Line 3rd St / Convention Center station and two blocks from Spectrum Center, we connect transit convenience with neighborhood tavern soul.
