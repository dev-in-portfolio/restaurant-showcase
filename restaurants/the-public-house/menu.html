Tavern Plates & Scratch Entrees

Butcher-cut prime meats and hearty chef-driven tavern staples.

Fish N' Frites $18.50

Flaky Atlantic cod fillet coated in crisp craft beer batter, served golden with hand-cut seasoned french fries, house sweet caper tartar sauce, and grilled lemon.

Bangin' Crispy Shrimp $14.50

Tender wild shrimp dusted in seasoned cornstarch flour, flash-fried crispy, and tossed in our signature sweet and spicy chili remoulade with freshly sliced scallions.

Blackened Scottish Salmon $23.50

Classic Chicken Parmesan $19.50

Signature Burgers & Artisan Handhelds

Served on griddled brioche buns with a generous basket of seasoned frites.

The Remedy Burger $16.50

Our flagship burger. Proprietary double beef blend smashed with caramelized edges, melted sharp aged cheddar, savory bacon onion jam, garlic aioli, and crispy fried shallots on toasted brioche.

Classic Tavern Cheeseburger $14.95

Double Angus patties grilled juicy with melted American cheese, crisp green leaf lettuce, sliced ripe tomato, tangy pickles, and house secret pub sauce on brioche.

Hot Buttermilk Chicken Sandwich $15.50

Tender chicken breast soaked in seasoned buttermilk, fried crunchy, brushed with Nashville hot honey glaze, and topped with house dill pickles and cool creamy slaw.

Prime Rib French Dip $17.50

Thinly shaved slow-roasted prime rib, melted provolone cheese, and caramelized sweet onions on a toasted artisan hoagie roll with piping hot rosemary beef au jus for dipping.

Handcrafted Cocktails & Carolina Drafts

Uptown Espresso Martini $13.50

Smoked Cherry Manhattan $14.50

Carolina Craft Draft Taps $6.50 – $8.00
