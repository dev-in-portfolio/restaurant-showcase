Elevated Tavern Fare, Late-Night Uptown Craft.

Welcome to The Public House, Uptown Charlotte’s premier destination for elevated tavern classics, premier sports viewing, and handcrafted cocktails. Located at 300 South Brevard Street—steps from the Charlotte Convention Center, Spectrum Center, and LYNX light rail—we serve prime Steak N' Frites, the double-patty Remedy Burger, Asian loaded tots, and hand-carved ice Old Fashioneds well past midnight.

Culinary Crown

Prime Butcher's Cut Steak N' Frites

Our 10-ounce prime flat iron steak is seared cast-iron crisp to order, locking in succulent juices and rich marbling. Crowned with melting roasted garlic herb compound butter, a fragrant pool of rich red wine demi-glace, and accompanied by a towering portion of crispy golden parmesan frites.

Artisan Mixology

Hand-Carved Ice & Smoked Bourbons

We take cocktail craft seriously. Our signature Old Fashioned combines small-batch Kentucky bourbon, aromatic bitters, and raw turbinado syrup stirred silky smooth and poured over a hand-carved crystal ice sphere, finished with flame-expressed orange oils.
