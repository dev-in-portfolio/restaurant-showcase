Weekday Ritual

Uptown Happy Hour Directives

Guidelines for our celebrated Monday through Friday happy hour:

  • Days & Hours: Monday through Friday from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM.
  • Drink Specials: Discounted select Carolina craft draft beers, house red and white wines by the glass, and signature well spirits.
  • Bar Bites: Special pricing on Loaded Asian Tots ($8), warm Bavarian Pretzels ($8), and crispy tavern sliders.
Stadium Protocol

Game-Day & Arena Directives

Visiting for a Hornets game, Panthers tailgate, or concert:

  • Seating Policy: Bar and high-top tables are first-come, first-served on major event days. Early arrival (at least 90 minutes before tip-off or kickoff) is highly recommended.
  • Group Bookings: Table reservations are accepted for dining groups of 6 or more during non-peak hours.
  • Post-Game Kitchen: Full dinner and shareable menus are served right through the final buzzer and well after concerts conclude.
