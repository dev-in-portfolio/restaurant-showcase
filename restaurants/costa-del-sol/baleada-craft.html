Handmade Flour Tortilla Tradition

Hand-Folded Honduran Baleadas

The "Baleada" is Honduras' iconic street food staple. We hand-knead wheat flour dough and press soft, thick tortillas on a hot griddle before spreading rich refried red kidney beans, Central American cream, scrambled eggs, and avocado.

Explore Baleada Menu
Baleada Alchemy

The 3 Pillars of Costa Baleada Craft

1. Hand-Kneaded Flour Dough

Fresh wheat flour dough balls are rested and hand-stretched into thick, soft tortillas griddled to golden spots.

2. Creamy Refried Red Beans

Honduran red kidney beans are slow-simmered with onions & garlic, mashed silky smooth, and spread hot.

3. Mantequilla & Queso Seco

Drizzled with authentic Central American sour cream (*mantequilla*) and sprinkled with salty dry curd cheese.
