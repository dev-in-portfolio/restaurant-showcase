Honduran Street Culinary Craft

The Art of Pollo con Tajadas

Pollo con Tajadas (also known as *Pollo Chuco*) is Honduras' most famous street dish. Raw green bananas are sliced length-wise into thin ribbons, fried to a crisp golden crunch, and topped with seasoned fried chicken and layered sauces.

Tajadas Layering

The 3 Pillars of Honduran Tajadas Craft

1. Fresh Green Banana Slicing

Unripened green bananas (*guineos verdes*) are sliced thin and fried to order for maximum crunch.

2. Spiced Citrus Marinade

Fresh chicken quarters are marinated in achiote, garlic, lime juice, & cumin before golden frying.

3. Shredded Cabbage & Pink Sauce

Layered with lime-tossed cabbage, diced chismol, pickled red onions, & house Honduran aderezo (pink sauce).
