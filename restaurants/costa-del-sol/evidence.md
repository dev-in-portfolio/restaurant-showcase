## Original Audit Weakness

Costa del Sol relies on basic template sites (`costadelsolus.com`, order.online). Located at 8045 South Blvd in Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its rich Honduran & Central American culinary heritage, handmade baleadas pressing technique, crispy pollo con tajadas craft, and vibrant Latin family atmosphere.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South Blvd Charlotte Location:** 8045 South Blvd, Charlotte NC 28273 (Archdale & South Blvd).
2. **Signature Honduran Dishes:** *Pollo con Tajadas* (crispy fried chicken over green banana chips with pickled cabbage & pink salsa), *Baleadas Sencillas y Completas* (flour tortillas with refried beans, egg, avocado, & queso seco).
3. **Central American Specialties:** *Sopa de Mondongo*, pupusas, carne asada, sweet fried plantains, and ice-cold horchata & maracuyá drinks.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Warm Latin Serif Typography:** Warm expressive serif display (*Lora*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and kitchen ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Honduran Terracotta & Caribbean Turquoise Palette:** Vibrant high-energy palette anchored in deep Caribbean espresso (`#1A120B`), sunlit Honduran terracotta (`#C2410C`), tropical Caribbean teal (`#0D9488`), golden plantain amber (`#D97706`), and soft corn silk cream (`#FFFBEB`).
3. **"The Pollo con Tajadas & Handmade Baleada Craft Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`pollo-tajadas-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing crispy green banana tajadas alongside handmade flour baleadas and Latin soups.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Coquette` — Parisian haute couture brasserie with Playfair Display/Plus Jakarta Sans fonts and Parisian Burgundy.
2. `Copperhead Social Club` — Industrial smash burger pub with Space Grotesk/Outfit fonts and Burnished Copper.
3. `Cool Idiot Coffee` — Playful specialty coffee bar with Bricolage Grotesque/Outfit fonts and Cake Pop Magenta.

### Divergent Choices for Costa del Sol

- **Hero Composition:** Honduran Coastal Sol & Central American Kitchen Vault split-hero layout: left side features a tropical sun emblem & banana palm stamp badge (`"HONDURAN & CENTRAL AMERICAN KITCHEN • SOUTH BLVD CHARLOTTE"`), warm Latin serif display (*Lora*), sunlit terracotta & Caribbean teal palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of signature Pollo con Tajadas with green banana chips, loaded Baleadas Completas, and iced horchata.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Costa Vault Cards** (`costa-vault-card`) and **South Blvd Latin Kitchen Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`costa-header`, `sol-brand`, `honduran-hero-stage`, `sun-stamp-badge`, `costa-vault-card`, `costa-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
