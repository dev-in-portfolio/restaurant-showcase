Authentic Honduran Flavors

Honduran & Latin Menu

Pollo con Tajadas, hand-folded baleadas, Salvadoran pupusas, Sopa de Mondongo, and tropical fruit drinks on South Boulevard.

Especialidades Catrachas Honduran Dishes

Pollo con Tajadas Complete House Specialty

Crispy spiced fried chicken quarter served over crunchy green banana chips, topped with pickled red cabbage, chismol, & signature pink sauce.

Baleada Especial Completa Handmade Tortilla

Large warm hand-pressed flour tortilla filled with savory refried red beans, scrambled eggs, fresh avocado, Honduran mantequilla, & queso seco.

Sopa de Mondongo y Mariscos Weekend Soup

Hearty traditional beef tripe broth simmered with yuca, plantains, cilantro, and spices (or fresh seafood soup option).

Carne Asada Catracha Grilled Steak

Char-grilled marinated flank steak served with green banana tajadas, chimichurri, refried beans, avocado, & fried cheese slice.

Central American Favorites Sides & Drinks

Pupusas Revueltas (3 Pack) Salvadoran Style

Hand-stuffed thick corn tortillas filled with melted cheese, chicharrón pork, & beans, served with curtido cabbage salad & salsa roja.

Plátanos Fritos con Mantequilla Sweet Side

Golden fried ripe sweet plantains served with rich cream (mantequilla centralamericana) and refried beans.

Fresh Iced Horchata & Maracuyá Tropical Juice

Traditional Honduran morro seed & rice horchata spiced with cinnamon, or fresh passion fruit (maracuyá) natural juice.

Glass Bottle Mexican Coke & Jarritos Cane Sugar

Imported Mexican Coca-Cola made with real cane sugar, plus Tamarindo, Mandarin, & Piña Jarritos fruit sodas.
