Tailored to You

How to Build Your Drink

Every beverage can be tailored across four key dimensions.

1. Sweetness Level

  • 0% Sweetness: Unsweetened pure tea flavor
  • 25% Sweetness: Subtle hint of cane sweetness
  • 50% Sweetness: Balanced half-sweet (Recommended)
  • 75% Sweetness: Rich sweet profile
  • 100% Sweetness: Traditional full sweetness

2. Artisan Toppings (+$0.75)

  • Brown Sugar Boba: Chewy warm tapioca pearls
  • Silky Egg Pudding: Rich French-custard texture
  • Herbal Grass Jelly: Traditional cooling herbal jelly
  • Salted Cheese Foam: Whipped savory-sweet cream
  • Crystal Boba: Crisp low-calorie konjac jelly
  • Aloe Vera Gel: Refreshing juicy fruit cubes

3. Milk & Ice Choice

  • Dairy Base: Organic whole milk or half & half
  • Plant Milks: Creamy Oatly oat milk or soy
  • Ice Levels: Regular ice, Light ice, or No ice
  • Temperature: Served iced cold or steaming hot
