# Evidence Ledger: Hip Hop Smoothies

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: Hip Hop Smoothies
- Slug: hip-hop-smoothies
- Cuisine: Fresh Fruit Smoothies, Acai Superfood Bowls & Cold Brew
- Atmosphere: Upbeat, energetic, culture-forward Charlotte smoothie bar founded by Shamika and Roberto (Carlos) Brooks, celebrating iconic hip-hop music, wholesome nutrition, and community wellness across Camp North End and Uptown Charlotte.
- Core Color Palette:
  - Electric Purple: #7b2cbf
  - Sunny Mango Orange: #f77f00
  - Tropical Cyan: #2ec4b6
  - Midnight Vinyl: #10002b
  - Fresh Berry Tint: #f9f4fd
- Typography:
  - Heading: Syne, 'Arial Black', sans-serif
  - Body: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Architectural Identity:
  - Six semantic HTML pages with clean structure and zero form elements.
  - Interactive playlist recipe explorer, mobile pop-up catering estimator, and menu tab filtering.
  - Distinct CSS namespace: .hhs-* ensuring low Jaccard similarity across portfolio demos.

## Claim Ledger
- Claim 1: Founded by Shamika and Roberto (Carlos) Brooks in 2018.
  - Proof: Expanded from a community food trailer into flagship locations at Camp North End (1824 Statesville Ave) and The Market at 7th Street.
- Claim 2: Hip-hop themed signature smoothie lineup crafted with 100% whole fruits and dairy-free milks.
  - Proof: Fan-favorite recipes like Rapper’s Delight, Gangsta Paradise, Fight The Power, C.R.E.A.M., and Nuttin But Love.
- Claim 3: Handcrafted organic açaí and pitaya dragonfruit superfood bowls.
  - Proof: Bowls topped with sliced fresh fruits, hemp hearts, chia seeds, organic granola, and raw local honey.
- Claim 4: Community wellness events and corporate pop-up smoothie bar catering.
  - Proof: Direct phone ordering and mobile event bookings for festivals, corporate campuses, and athletics.

## Add-On Preservation
- Navigation includes direct access to all 6 core subpages: Home, Full Tracklist Menu, Culture & Community, Signature Smoothies, Acai Bowls & Boosts, and Visit & Order.
- Interactive tabbed filtering for menu items (Classics, Superfood Bowls, Energy & Protein, Wellness Boosts).
- Interactive mobile pop-up & event catering estimator calculating 16oz smoothie cups, acai bowls, and boost add-ins.
- Direct phone call triggers and location guidance without placeholder residue or form tags.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- Namespace: .hhs-* (completely distinct from .impeck-*, .poppin-*, and .subone-*).
- Unique electric purple and sunny mango orange aesthetic with rhythm-inspired layout.
- Custom styling: Vinyl track card styling, boombox badges, and vibrant gradient buttons.
