The 3 Pillars of Kazoku Ramen Craft

1. The 18-Hour Rolling Boil

Simmering pork marrow bones over aggressive high flame to break down proteins into a velvety, milky white broth packed with natural umami.

2. Custom Alkaline Noodles

Crafted with specialized kansui mineral water to provide firm springiness and high broth adherence with zero soggy degradation.

3. Mayu Roasted Black Garlic

Slow-roasting garlic in sesame oil through seven darkening stages to achieve a deep, bittersweet roasted aroma that transforms the bowl.
