Architectural Heritage & Cantina Energy

A 1920s Tuscan Villa, Reborn as a Fiesta.

Set within the historic Reynolds-Gourmajenko estate at 715 Providence Road in Myers Park, RuRu’s occupies one of Charlotte’s most breathtaking architectural landmarks. We preserved the historic masonry, wrought-iron archways, and sprawling tree-shaded courtyard, breathing new life into the property with vibrant Mexican cantina culture, laughter, and craft tequila.

The RuRu's DNA

Three Principles That Define Our Vibe

Unpretentious hospitality meets historic garden charm.

Principle 01

Courtyard Patio Culture

Centered around our iconic carved stone fountain, our expansive garden terrace offers breezy outdoor dining shaded by mature trees by day and glowing with string lights by night.

Principle 02

Pop-Culture Playfulness

We never take ourselves too seriously. Our menu is an affectionate homage to our favorite classic comedies, movies, and quotes, sparking instant conversations around every table.

Principle 03

Pure Agave Devotion

We are fiercely dedicated to authentic agave spirits. No artificial mixers, no inferior syrups—just 100% blue agave tequilas, fresh-pressed citrus, and passionate bartenders.

Walk-In Community Vibe

No Reservations, Just Walk In & Celebrate

RuRu's operates entirely on a walk-in model because we believe great dining should be spontaneous and inclusive. Whether stopping in for a quick weekday lunch on the terrace or joining a buzzing Friday evening crowd around the fountain, you are always welcome at our table.
