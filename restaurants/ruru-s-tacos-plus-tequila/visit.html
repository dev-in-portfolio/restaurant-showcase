Arrival Directives

On-Site Parking & Villa Accessibility

Arriving at 715 Providence Road is straightforward:

  • Dedicated Surface Lot: A paved on-site parking lot is located directly behind and beside the historic villa with direct entrance through the courtyard gate.
  • Peak Evening Valet: Complimentary valet attendants are frequently stationed at the front portico on busy Friday and Saturday evenings.
  • Neighborhood Street Parking: Additional legal neighborhood parking is available along Providence Road and adjoining residential avenues where designated.
Fiesta Catering

Taco Bars & Event Packages

Bring the electric RuRu's cantina experience to your home, office, or private celebration:

  • Build-Your-Own Taco Bars: Chafing racks with slow-braised carnitas, seasoned ground beef, or grilled chicken, warm double tortillas, and all classic toppings.
