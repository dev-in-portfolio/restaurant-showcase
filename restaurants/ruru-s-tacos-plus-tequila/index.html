Historic Myers Park Villa • Charlotte NC

Electric Tacos, Historic Villa Patio.

Welcome to RuRu’s Tacos + Tequila, housed inside the historic 1920s Reynolds-Gourmajenko Tuscan villa at 715 Providence Road. Step through our arched iron gates into Charlotte's most celebrated outdoor courtyard terrace centered around a bubbling stone fountain. We pair playful pop-culture-named tacos with 100+ curated agave tequilas, fresh-pressed citrus margaritas, and scratch Mexican cooking.

Scratch Mixology

Hand-Shaken Margaritas & Fine Sea-Salt Rims

We reject bottled sweet-and-sour mixes and commercial syrups. Every RuRu’s margarita is hand-shaken with 100% blue agave tequila, fresh freshly squeezed Mexican limes, and light organic agave nectar. Served in a chilled crystal coupette glass with a pristine rim of fine sea salt.
