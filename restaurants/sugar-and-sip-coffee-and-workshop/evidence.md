# Evidence & Design Ledger: Sugar & Sip Coffee & Workshop

## Creative Brief
- **Client / Business:** Sugar & Sip Coffee and Workshop
- **Address:** 120 N Cedar St, Ste 725, Charlotte, NC 28202 (Fourth Ward / Uptown West Charlotte)
- **Phone:** (704) 919-0143
- **Email:** hello@sugarandsipclt.org
- **Operating Hours:** Monday - Friday: 7:00 AM - 4:00 PM; Saturday - Sunday: 9:00 AM - 2:00 PM
- **Concept & Offerings:** Black-owned boutique craft espresso bar and collaborative creative workshop space in Fourth Ward Uptown Charlotte. Specializing in imaginative seasonal lattes (Fruity Pebble Latte, Blueberry Lemon Latte, Tropical Matcha Splash, Salted Coconut Chai, Pina Colada Cold Brew, Lavender Honey Latte), classic espresso extractions, loose-leaf botanicals, and scratch pastries (warm cinnamon rolls, pearl sugar waffles, stuffed mini bagels, raspberry cheesecake). The venue also serves as a vibrant community hub hosting creative workshops, open mic sessions, coworking days, latte art masterclasses, and mobile espresso cart catering across the Charlotte metro.
- **Design Philosophy:** Warm bohemian cafe minimalism meets urban creative loft aesthetic. Caramel latte tones, rose blossom pink, warm linen cream, espresso dark roast, and muted sage botanical accents. Clean typography, interactive Mobile Coffee Cart & Workshop Event calculator, and zero emojis.

## Claim Ledger
- **Craft Roast & Specialty Mixology:** Direct-trade specialty beans extracted with barista precision, paired with house-made syrups and layered botanical infusions like the Tropical Matcha Splash and Fruity Pebble Latte.
- **Community Workshop & Creative Space:** Beyond a coffee shop; a welcoming, inclusive Fourth Ward hub featuring community open mic nights, craft workshops, poetry readings, and creative workspace.
- **Artisan Scratch Bakery:** Freshly baked daily small bites including Belgian pearl sugar waffles, warm gourmet cinnamon rolls, stuffed savory mini bagels, and cheesecake bars.
- **Mobile Coffee Cart & Event Bar:** Fully equipped mobile espresso bar and barista service for weddings, corporate meetings, brand launches, and campus celebrations.
