Our Coffee Craft & Community Roots

Born from a passion for authentic coffee culture, artistic collaboration, and fostering genuine human connection in Uptown Charlotte.

The Sugar & Sip Vision

Cultivating Culture, One Cup at a Time

Located at 120 North Cedar Street in Fourth Ward, Sugar & Sip was created to reimagine what an urban neighborhood coffee shop could be — an unpretentious, culturally vibrant third place where everyone feels seen, welcomed, and inspired.

We partner with ethical, direct-trade roasters and source organic ingredients for our house-made syrups, from real lavender blossoms and Ceylon cinnamon to steeped cereal milks.

Beyond our espresso bar, our workshop space hosts local creators, book clubs, poetry open mics, and artistic skill-shares, making Sugar & Sip a living community hub for the Queen City.

Guiding Values

The 4 Pillars of Sugar & Sip

What drives our craft and community commitment every day.

1. Ethical Sourcing

Direct-trade relationships supporting smallholder coffee farmers across Africa and the Americas.

2. Flavor Innovation

Pushing beverage boundaries with imaginative, nostalgia-driven recipes made with real whole ingredients.

3. Creative Access

Providing an accessible, welcoming platform for artists, writers, and community organizers to gather.

4. Warm Hospitality

Treating every customer like family from the moment they step through our Fourth Ward doors.
