The Sugar & Sip Difference

More Than Just a Daily Cup

We built Sugar & Sip to be a creative sanctuary where authentic community connection, artistic expression, and exceptional coffee thrive together.

01.

Inventive Specialty Lattes

From nostalgia-infused Fruity Pebble Lattes and Blueberry Lemon creations to ceremonial Tropical Matcha Splashes, each beverage is crafted with scratch syrups and culinary balance.

02.

Creative Workshop Loft

An inclusive community platform hosting live poetry, open mic nights, book discussions, watercolor workshops, latte art tutorials, and collaborative coworking.

03.

Mobile Espresso Cart Bar

Full-service mobile espresso cart and professional barista service bringing handcrafted cafe experiences to weddings, corporate events, and private gatherings across Charlotte.
