## Original Audit Weakness

Seoul Good CLT brings authentic Korean night market energy and beloved street food staples to Uptown Charlotte with a 30-year local culinary pedigree. However, its previous online footprint was minimal and fragmented across food hall listings. This 6-page responsive web experience gives Seoul Good an energetic, vibrant digital identity showcasing its cheese-pull K-Dogs, sizzling bulgogi Kupbap, and catering boxed sets.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Viral Korean K-Dogs (Corn Dogs):** Yeasted rice-flour batter coated in crunchy panko breadcrumbs or crispy diced potatoes, filled with stretchy whole-milk mozzarella and savory beef sausage, rolled in sugar and drizzled with spicy gochujang mayo and honey mustard.
2. **Kupbap Rice Bowls & Korean Fried Chicken:** Sizzling beef bulgogi, spicy pork, and double-fried crispy chicken tossed in sweet chili Yangnyeom glaze over rice, glass noodles (japchae), fried egg, and aged kimchi.
3. **Monarch Market Street Counter:** Fast, high-energy counter service inside One South Tryon, offering quick lunch bowls for Uptown professionals and late-night weekend snacks.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Seoul Neon Orange & Night Charcoal Palette:** Neon K-Street Orange (`#f97316`), Sizzling Gochujang Crimson (`#dc2626`), Night Market Charcoal (`#18181b`), Crispy Corn Dog Gold (`#eab308`), and Sesame Cream (`#fefce8`).
2. **K-Street Food Customization Architecture:** Interactive K-Dog cheese/potato build diagrams, Kupbap protein matrices, and spice heat index breakdowns.
3. **Punchy Modern Typography:** Bold Syne / Bebas Neue condensed headers paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for prices and flavor callouts.
