The K-Dog Breakdown

Every K-Dog is skewered by hand and dipped into our sweet yeasted batter before receiving its custom exterior coating and seasoning:

  • Core Selection: 100% Mozzarella cheese stick, 100% Beef hot dog, or the classic Half-and-Half (cheese on top, beef on bottom).
  • Textural Coating: Traditional panko breadcrumb crust, diced crispy Idaho potato cubes (Gamja), or crunchy crushed dry ramen.
  • The Sugar Dusting: Highly recommended! Rolling the hot dog in fine sugar creates the signature sweet-savory balance.
  • The Sauces: Finished with zigzags of house spicy gochujang mayo, sweet honey mustard, and smoky ketchup.
All-In-One Hearty Meal

The Anatomy of a Kupbap Bowl

Kupbap is designed to deliver complete Korean comfort food in an easy-to-enjoy bowl format:

  • Jasmine Rice Bed: Warm, fluffy seasoned rice dressed with sesame oil and light tamari.
  • Japchae Glass Noodles: Stir-fried sweet potato starch noodles with carrots, spinach, and sesame.
  • Sizzling Protein: Sliced ribeye beef marinated in Asian pear and soy, spicy gochujang pork, or crispy double-fried chicken bites.
  • Garnishes: A sunny-side fried egg with golden yolk, fermented spicy napa cabbage kimchi, green onions, and toasted nori strips.
