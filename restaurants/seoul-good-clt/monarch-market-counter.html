Center City Charlotte

A Night Market in the Heart of Uptown

Monarch Market at One South Tryon is Charlotte's most exciting multi-vendor culinary space. Seoul Good stands out with its glowing neon signs, sizzling woks, and buzzing street counter:

  • Rapid Counter Service: Step right up to our counter. Hot K-Dogs are fried and sauced in 3-4 minutes; Kupbap bowls are assembled immediately.
  • Spacious Seating Choices: Take your food to communal dining tables, high-top bar perches, or outdoor patio seating.
  • Craft Cocktail Pairings: Pair spicy chicken or bulgogi bowls with beers, cocktails, and wines from Monarch Market's main bars.
Convenient Access

Location & Center City Transit

Effortless access whether you walk from your office or ride the light rail.

1. Trade & Tryon Center

Located at 101 North Tryon Street inside One South Tryon, directly at the central nexus of Charlotte's business and entertainment district.

2. LYNX Rail Access

Two blocks from both the Charlotte Transportation Center (CTC/Arena) and 7th Street light rail stations on the Blue Line.

3. Parking Options

Convenient garage parking available in the One South Tryon parking deck, as well as metered street parking along Church and 4th streets.
