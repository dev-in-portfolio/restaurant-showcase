🍸 Spice-Infused Craft Cocktails

The Art of Indian Mixology

Our bar program elevates traditional cocktail craft with ancient Indian spices. We steep real Kashmiri saffron, crush green cardamom pods, simmer tamarind reductions, & infuse applewood smoke into every glass.

Mixology Principles

The 3 Pillars of Exotica Mixology

1. Real Spice & Botanical Infusions

Green cardamom pods, star anise, cinnamon sticks, & Kashmiri saffron steeped directly into artisanal spirits.

2. Scratch Jaggery & Tamarind Syrups

Unrefined unbleached jaggery cane sugar & tangy cooked tamarind reduction cooked daily behind the bar.

3. Palate-Cooling Spice Pairings

Cocktails specifically crafted to complement high-heat tandoori dishes & rich velvet Mughlai curries.
