## Original Audit Weakness

Exotica Indian Kitchen & Bar relies on third-party ordering platforms and basic site (`exoticaclt.com`). Located at 500 S College St in Uptown, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its opulent royal Mughlai culinary heritage, live clay tandoor copper baking process, botanical craft mixology program, and corporate executive dinner reservation directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Uptown Charlotte Location:** 500 South College Street, Suite #150, Charlotte NC 28202 (Uptown Charlotte across from Charlotte Convention Center & Overstreet Mall).
2. **Royal Mughlai Culinary Heritage:** Signature Royal Velvet Butter Chicken, Tandoori Lamb Chops, Smoked Cardamom Dum Biryani, Royal Saffron Salmon, & Garlic Butter Naan.
3. **Botanical Mixology & Uptown Ambiance:** Custom Indian-inspired botanical craft cocktails (Cardamom Old Fashioned, Saffron Tamarind Fizz), copper water goblets, & elegant Uptown lounge seating.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Luxury Display Serif Typography:** Elegant luxury serif (*Cinzel Decorative* / *Cinzel*) paired with refined body sans (*Outfit*) and mixology mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Imperial Navy & Copper Gold Palette:** High-energy royal lounge palette anchored in deep imperial navy (`#0B132B`), regal copper gold (`#D97706`), jewel emerald (`#059669`), warm silk ivory (`#FAFAF9`), and royal crimson (`#9F1239`).
3. **"The Royal Tandoor & Botanical Mixology Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`royal-tandoor-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing live clay tandoor specials alongside royal curries, saffron biryanis, & craft cocktails.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Everybody Eatz` — Bold Southern Soul Food with Outfit fonts and Cajun Charcoal/Electric Amber.
2. `Emmy Lou's Wine Bar` — Dilworth Velvet Wine Bar with Cormorant Garamond fonts and Velvet Bordeaux/Champagne Gold.
3. `Elizabeth Creamery` — Warm Sweet Shop with Fraunces fonts and Strawberry Velvet/Waffle Gold.

### Divergent Choices for Exotica Indian Kitchen & Bar

- **Hero Composition:** Opulent Royal Indian Gastronomy & Craft Cocktail Lounge Vault split-hero layout: left side features a polished emerald brass stamp badge (`"UPTOWN CHARLOTTE ROYAL INDIAN & CRAFT COCKTAILS • 500 S COLLEGE ST"`), luxury display serif (*Cinzel*), imperial navy & copper gold palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of royal brass karahi butter chicken, sizzling tandoori lamb chops, saffron biryani, steam garlic naan, & a botanical cocktail against the Uptown skyline.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Exotica Vault Cards** (`exotica-vault-card`) and Uptown Indian lounge highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`exotica-header`, `exotica-brand`, `imperial-hero-stage`, `emerald-stamp-badge`, `exotica-vault-card`, `exotica-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
