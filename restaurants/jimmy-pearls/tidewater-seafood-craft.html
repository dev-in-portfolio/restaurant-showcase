🦀 Virginia Tidewater Heritage

The Art of Tidewater Frying

Virginia Tidewater cooking celebrates the natural sweetness of fresh coastal seafood. At Jimmy Pearls, we coat fresh catfish fillets & soft-shell crabs in coarse yellow cornmeal seasoned with Old Bay & cayenne, frying at high heat to lock in juicy ocean flavor.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Tidewater Seafood Craft

1. Wild Atlantic Catch Sourcing

We source sustainable Atlantic blue crabs, Carolina catfish, & Chesapeake Bay oysters directly from coastal dock markets.

2. Stone-Ground Cornmeal Crust

Coarse yellow cornmeal mixed with Old Bay, black pepper, & sea salt creates an ultra-crispy, non-greasy crust.

3. Scratch Remoulade & Slaw

Every fish basket is paired with house-made tartar sauce, tangy pickle remoulade, & fresh shredded cabbage slaw.
