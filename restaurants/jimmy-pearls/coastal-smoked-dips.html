🥚 Oak-Smoked Trout & Oysters

The Art of Smoked Dips

Smoked fish is a staple of Coastal Virginia gatherings. At Jimmy Pearls, we smoke fresh trout over hardwood oak embers, blending it with fresh dill, lemon zest, & cream cheese to create Charlotte's favorite seafood spread.

Explore Seafood Dips
Kitchen Craft

The 3 Pillars of House Smoked Seafood Dips

1. Oak-Hardwood Slow Smoking

Fresh trout fillets are cured in sea salt & brown sugar before being smoked low-and-slow over oak logs.

2. Hand-Whipped Dill Mousse

Smoked fish flakes are whipped with cultured cream cheese, fresh dill sprigs, capers, & fresh lemon juice.

3. Classic Saltine & Pickle Pairing

Served traditionally with crispy saltine crackers, house-pickled red onions, & dashes of Southern hot sauce.
