Tidewater Roots & Pride

Jimmy Pearls was founded to tell the rich story of Virginia Tidewater & Southern coastal foodways. From our kitchen stall at Market at 7th Street in Uptown Charlotte, we serve seafood rooted in history, family fish fries, & coastal community traditions.
