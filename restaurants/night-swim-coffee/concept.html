A New Current in Specialty Coffee

Night Swim Coffee was born from a unified vision to build Charlotte's premier roasting infrastructure. Founded by the creators behind Not Just Coffee and Undercurrent Coffee, Night Swim brings decades of barista expertise, ethical sourcing passion, and minimalist aesthetic design into a singular roasting collective.
