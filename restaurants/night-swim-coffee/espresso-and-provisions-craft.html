The 3 Pillars of Night Swim Café Craft

1. Calibrated Espresso Extraction

Weighing and timing every espresso dose to hit exacting brew ratios, delivering velvety body and sweet caramel crema.

2. In-House Botanical Syrups

Simmering real vanilla beans, cracked green cardamom pods, and organic floral botanicals without artificial preservatives.

3. Scratch Sourdough & Bakery

Partnering with regional grain millers to bake flaky butter croissants, brioche breakfast sandwiches, and savory toasts.
