Naturally Leavened, Heirloom Grains.

Welcome to Verdant Bread, Charlotte’s dedicated artisanal sourdough bakery. Tucked behind the Common Market on Monroe Road, we mill heritage grain varieties from North Carolina farmers and bake 100% naturally leavened country boules, open-crumb sourdough, flaky laminated croissants, and English muffins with zero commercial baker’s yeast.

The Mother Starter

100% Wild Fermentation

No commercial yeast ever enters our kitchen. Every loaf rises solely through our living sourdough starter, producing deep organic acidity, superior shelf-life, and easy digestibility.

House Signature

Carolina Country Sourdough

Our hallmark country loaf features a blistered mahogany crust, pronounced scoring ear, and a tender, custard-like crumb riddled with open alveoli air pockets. Crafted from stone-milled wheat, spring water, and sea salt, it stays fresh on your kitchen counter for days and toasts to crisp perfection.

Viennoiserie Program

Hand-Laminated All-Butter Croissants

Our viennoiserie requires three days of patient folding, sheeting, and temperature-controlled resting. Laminated with 84% European-style butter, our croissants, pain au chocolat, and seasonal morning pastries bake to a golden sheen with hundreds of delicate, shatteringly crisp layers.
