The Hallmarks of Our Pastry & Subscription Programs

Precision butter layering and guaranteed fresh bread reservations.

Program 01

Three-Day Lamination

We laminate sourdough croissant dough with 84% butterfat European-style butter, folding and resting across three consecutive days to develop hundreds of paper-thin honeycomb layers.

Program 02

Weekly Bread Subscription

Never worry about loaves selling out. Bread Club members enjoy a reserved weekly fresh sourdough boule baked that morning and ready for convenient pickup at designated hubs.

Program 03

Regional Pickup Hubs

Pick up your fresh weekly subscription at our Oakwold bakery window in Charlotte, or at partner hubs including The Pickled Peach in Davidson and High Branch Brewing Co. in Concord.

How the Bread Club Works

Signing up is simple: choose your preferred pickup location (Oakwold Bakery Window, Davidson, or Concord), select your bread tier, and your artisan loaf will be bagged and awaiting you every week. It connects home kitchens directly to North Carolina grain farmers and old-world sourdough baking.
