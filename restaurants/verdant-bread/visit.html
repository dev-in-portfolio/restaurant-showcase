Direct Bakery Access

The Oakwold Walk-Up Window

What to expect when visiting Verdant Bread on Monroe Road:

  • Walk-Up Orders: Walk up to our service window to pick up warm morning country boules, baguettes, and fresh croissants.
  • Pre-Order Pickups: Bread Club members and pre-orders have dedicated fast-track pickup bags ready at the counter.
  • Arrive Early for Pastries: Laminated croissants, pain au chocolat, and seasonal Danish pastries frequently sell out by midday.
Beyond Monroe Road

Farmers Markets & Regional Hubs

Find Verdant Bread throughout the greater Charlotte region:

  • Uptown Farmers Market: 300 S Davidson St, Saturdays 8:00 AM – 12:30 PM (Fresh boules, focaccia, English muffins).
  • Davidson Hub: The Pickled Peach (Weekly Bread Club pickup partner in North Mecklenburg).
  • Concord Hub: High Branch Brewing Co. at Gibson Mill (Weekly Bread Club pickup partner in Cabarrus County).
