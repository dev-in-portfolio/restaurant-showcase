Oakwold East Charlotte • Rebuilding Regional Agriculture

Reviving Carolina Grain, One Loaf at a Time.

Verdant Bread was founded with a profound mission: to reconnect Charlotte with the historic agricultural heritage of the American South. By sourcing organic heirloom wheat, spelt, and rye from independent North Carolina grain growers and stone-milling on site, we prove that real bread is an agricultural product, not an industrial commodity.

Milling Philosophy

The Values Guiding Every Verdant Bake

Commitment to farmer partnerships, ancient grains, and true community nourishment.

Value 01

Living Nutrient Density

Stone milling crushes grain slowly at low temperatures, keeping the wheat germ and nourishing oils intact. Our flour provides real fiber, natural B-vitamins, and deep earthy flavor.

Value 02

Carolina Farmer Alliances

We pay fair farmgate prices to local growers across North Carolina who cultivate sustainable, non-GMO heirloom grains without synthetic chemicals or industrial monoculture.

Value 03

Community Accessibility

Whether you visit our Monroe Road pickup window or grab a loaf at the Uptown Farmers Market, we believe handcrafted sourdough should be an accessible daily staple for every family.

The Oakwold Bakehouse

Behind the Monroe Road Pickup Window

Our bakery is a dedicated production space designed for efficiency, flour dusting, and hearth deck ovens. By serving our community through a direct walk-up pickup window, our bakers stay focused on timing every mix, coil fold, and bake to absolute perfection. It ensures every loaf you carry home was handled with individual care.
