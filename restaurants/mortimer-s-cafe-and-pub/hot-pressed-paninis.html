Culinary Standards

The Art of the Press

Why a Mortimer's panini tastes different from any ordinary sandwich.

Boar’s Head Deli Cuts Premium Quality

We use only premium whole-muscle Boar’s Head turkey, pastrami, and roast beef with zero fillers or artificial binders.

Cast-Iron Even Heat 375°F Sear

Our commercial cast-iron presses create a uniform golden crust that stays crunchy down to the final bite without getting soggy.

Scratch Au Jus Reduction Simmered Daily

Simmered with roasted beef bones, red wine, garlic, and fresh thyme, providing deep savory richness to our dip sandwiches.

House-Seasoned Kettle Chips Included Side

Every panini comes with a generous mound of extra-crunchy kettle-cooked chips seasoned with our house blend of herbs and sea salt.
