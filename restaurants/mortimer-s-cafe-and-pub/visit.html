Easy Transit & Parking

Queen City Quarter Parking & Transit

  • Underground Parking Deck: Enter the Queen City Quarter covered parking deck from College Street or 4th Street. Take the central elevator directly up to the courtyard level.
  • LYNX Blue Line Light Rail: The CTC/Arena station connects directly to the complex, making Mortimer’s an easy car-free stop from South End, NoDa, or UNCC.
  • Event Days: During major Spectrum Center concerts and Hornets games, we open early with special game day paninis and cold draught pitchers.
