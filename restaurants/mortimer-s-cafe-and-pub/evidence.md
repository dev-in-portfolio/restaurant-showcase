## Original Audit Weakness

Mortimer’s Café & Pub has been a cornerstone of Uptown Charlotte hospitality for over 16 years, surviving the transformation of the EpiCentre into Queen City Quarter with a fiercely loyal following for its hot-pressed paninis and welcoming neighborhood vibe. However, their online footprint was fragmented across third-party delivery services without a dedicated multi-page website showcasing their signature panini creations, French dip au jus craft, craft beer selections, or Queen City Quarter courtyard access. This 6-page concept provides an authentic, cozy, and high-converting digital home.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Gourmet Hot-Pressed Paninis & Dips:** Signature sandwiches made with premium Boar's Head deli meats, sliced cheeses, and artisan breads pressed crisp on cast-iron grills, served with warm house au jus and crispy kettle chips.
2. **Classic British-American Tavern Fare:** Panko-crusted smoked gouda mac & cheese bites, hand-breaded fried dill pickles, loaded bleu cheese kettle chips, and warm pretzel dips.
3. **Queen City Quarter Courtyard & Est. 2010 Heritage:** Over 16 years of community memories, outdoor plaza patio seating, craft draughts on tap, and pre-event dining for Hornets games at the Spectrum Center.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **British Racing Emerald & Vintage Brass Palette:** Racing Emerald Green (`#064e3b`), Vintage Copper/Brass (`#d97706`), Dark Mahogany (`#1c1917`), and Warm Linen White (`#f8fafc`).
2. **Literary Pub Visual Architecture:** Named panini cards ("The Dickens", "The Mortimer", "Sir Walter"), French Dip au jus spotlight, and Queen City Quarter courtyard timeline.
3. **Refined Tavern Typography:** Playfair Display classic serif display paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for the Est. 2010 founding stamp.
