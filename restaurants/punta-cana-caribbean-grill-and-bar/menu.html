Mofongos & Asados Pilón Mortar

Mofongo con Camarones Criollos #1 Signature

Fried green plantains mashed in a wooden pilón with garlic, roasted pork chicharrón, and olive oil, crowned with sautéed jumbo shrimp in a rich tomato, garlic, and bell pepper salsa criolla.

Pernil Asado Tradicional Dominican Icon

Pork shoulder slow-roasted for 12 hours with sour orange mojo, oregano, and crushed garlic, served with crispy blistered cuerito skin, arroz con habichuelas, and sweet maduros.

Rabo Encendido (Stewed Oxtail) Slow-Braised

Tender, fall-off-the-bone beef oxtails simmered in a spiced red wine, scotch bonnet, and aromatic herb reduction, served over seasoned yellow rice and yuca con mojo.

Chivo Guisado Liniero Country Recipe

Traditional Dominican braised goat meat stewed in bitter orange, dark Dominican rum, oregano, and chili pepper until deeply savory and tender.

Tropical Drinks & Sides Island Bar

Morir Soñando Tradicional Island Elixir

The beloved Dominican refreshment blending freshly squeezed Florida orange juice with sweetened condensed milk, pure vanilla extract, and crushed ice.

Passion Fruit Chinola Mojito Tropical Rum

Dominican white rum muddled with fresh mint, raw cane sugar, tart passion fruit chinola pulp, and fresh lime juice topped with club soda over ice.

Tostones & Sweet Maduros Duo Side Classic

Crispy twice-fried green plantain tostones served with house garlic mojo dipping sauce alongside caramelized sweet ripe maduros.

Sancocho Dominicano Especial Weekend Pot

Hearty traditional stew with braised meats, yuca, plantains, calabaza squash, and sweet corn on the cob, served with white rice and fresh avocado slices.
