Community & Celebrations

The 3 Pillars of Punta Cana Hospitality

1. The Iconic Morir Soñando

Freshly squeezed orange juice skillfully blended with sweetened milk and real vanilla over ice without curdling—the undisputed national drink of the Dominican Republic.

2. Weekday Lunch Specials

Available Monday through Saturday from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM: generous platters of roast pork, stewed chicken, rice, beans, and fresh salad for an exceptional value.

3. Large Family Feasts

Catering whole roast pernil, sweet plantains, moro de guandules, and empanadas for family birthdays, weddings, and Charlotte community celebrations.
