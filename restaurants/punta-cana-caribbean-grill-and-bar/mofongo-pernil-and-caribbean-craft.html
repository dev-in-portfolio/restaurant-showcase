🌴 The Art of the Wooden Pilón

Mashed in Pilón, Roasted to Crisp.

Dominican gastronomy is built on deep heritage and patient technique. At Punta Cana, we hand-pound fresh green plantains in rustic wooden pilón mortars with garlic and crackling chicharrón, alongside pork shoulders slow-roasted for half a day until the meat melts and the skin turns to golden glass.

Culinary Heritage

The 3 Pillars of Punta Cana Kitchen Fire

1. The Wooden Pilón

Crushing fresh fried plátano verde directly to order with roasted garlic paste, sea salt, and crispy pork cracklings for authentic texture and flavor.

2. 24-Hour Mojo Pernil

Marinating pork shoulders in bitter Seville orange juice, crushed garlic cloves, and Dominican wild oregano before slow-roasting for 12 hours.

3. Rich Criollo Reductions

Simmering Rabo Encendido oxtails and Chivo Guisado goat in spiced sofrito, scotch bonnet peppers, and dark Dominican rum until fork-tender.
