Mexican Street Tradition • NoDa Culture

Street Soul, Artisanal Sweet.

Ricko's Churro Bar was created to celebrate the vibrant dessert culture of Mexican street plazas. From the team behind Charlotte's beloved Tacos Rick-O, we brought a dedicated artisanal churreria to North Davidson Street, transforming humble choux dough into an unforgettable culinary destination.

The Values of Ricko's Churro Bar

Authentic Craft Over Shortcuts

Refusing pre-packaged frozen dough; mixing, resting, piping, and frying every single churro fresh to order every day.

NoDa Community Heart

Creating a welcoming, late-night sweet gathering haven where families, art wanderers, and neighbors come together.

Cultural Flavor Storytelling

Honoring traditional Mexican spices, cajeta, guava, and cafe de olla while innovating with playful modern toppings.

From Plaza To NoDa

A Charlotte Sweet Tooth Destination

What started with our family's passion for bold Mexican street food evolved into a mission to bring true artisanal churreria craft to North Carolina. When you smell the canela cinnamon and sizzling dough on North Davidson Street, you know you've arrived.
