House-Steeped Horchata • Cafe de Olla • Ice Cream

Cold Horchata, Hot Churros.

Nothing pairs better with hot cinnamon-sugar churros than our handcrafted Mexican beverage bar. From traditional rice milk horchata slow-steeped with Ceylon cinnamon sticks to refreshing strawberry horchata frappes, rich loaded ice cream sundaes, and spiced Iced Cafe de Olla.

The 3 Pillars of Ricko's Drinks & Sundaes

1. Authentic Cinnamon Horchata

Soaking long-grain rice with whole cinnamon bark overnight before blending with pure vanilla and sweetened milk for a velvety sip.

2. Iced Spiced Cafe de Olla

Brewing rich dark roast Mexican coffee with unrefined piloncillo cane sugar, Mexican cinnamon, and cloves served chilled over ice.

3. The Loaded Churro Sundae

Building dual scoops of artisanal vanilla ice cream anchored by hot crispy churro loops, warm dulce de leche, and whipped cream.

Slow Steeped Discipline

Real Ingredients, Pure Tradition

We believe authentic Mexican refreshments require patience. We never use powdered horchata mixes or artificial flavor syrups. From crushing genuine Ceylon canela to melting traditional dark piloncillo cones, every cup honors heritage recipes.
