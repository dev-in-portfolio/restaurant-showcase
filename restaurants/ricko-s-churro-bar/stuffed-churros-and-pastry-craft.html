Star-Nozzle Extrusion • Pure Canela Sugar

Extruded Hot, Filled Warm.

The secret to exceptional churros is immediacy. At Ricko's Churro Bar, we never pre-fry or hold dough under heating lamps. Every order begins with scratch-made choux dough extruded into fluted star shapes, fried to deep golden perfection, tossed in cinnamon sugar, and custom-filled on the spot.

Culinary Discipline

The 3 Pillars of Ricko's Churro Craft

1. The Golden Star Extrusion

Pressing fresh choux dough through precision fluted star nozzles creating deep ridges that maximize crispy surface area and hold canela cinnamon sugar.

2. Direct-Core Warm Injection

Piping rich fillings—from caramelized dulce de leche to tart guava cream—directly into the steaming core of hollowed churro loops.

3. Artisanal Sweet Glazes

Coating crispy loops in rich Mexican dark chocolate, condensed sweet milk, crushed Oreos, and toasted coconut flakes.

Warm Fillings & Ice Cream

The Art of the Stuffed Churro

Every bite delivers a contrasting sensation—the blistering crunch of hot cinnamon-sugar dough followed by an explosion of warm, velvety filling. Paired with rich vanilla bean ice cream and Mexican hot chocolate, Ricko's transforms simple street pastry into fine culinary craft.
