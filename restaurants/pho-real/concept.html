🍜 The Pho Real Story

Authentic Soul, Modern Dining.

Pho Real was born from a simple belief: genuine Vietnamese comfort food requires real ingredients, authentic patience, and openhearted hospitality. Located in the heart of University City, we provide a welcoming neighborhood hub where students, families, and professionals gather for restorative bone broth and crispy street bites.
