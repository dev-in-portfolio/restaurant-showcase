🍗 Street Appetizers & Craft Bar

Wok Sizzle, Saigon Brews.

Beyond our iconic noodle bowls, Pho Real brings the vibrant energy of Saigon street markets to University City. From blistering hot woks tossing prime Bo Luc Lac (Shaking Beef) to crisp golden Cha Gio egg rolls and imported Vietnamese lagers at our full bar.

Street Craft

The 3 Pillars of Pho Real Bar & Bites

1. Wok-Tossed Street Flavors

Flash-searing marinated meats at intense heat to capture authentic 'wok hei' breath in our Shaking Beef, fried rices, and lemongrass chicken.

2. Hand-Rolled Crispy Cha Gio

Rolling delicate rice paper with seasoned pork, wood ear mushrooms, and glass noodles, fried until crisp and served with herbs and nuoc cham.

3. Full Vietnamese Bar

Featuring Saigon 333 lagers, local North Carolina craft beers, lychee martinis, and traditional iced Vietnamese milk coffee brewed to order.
