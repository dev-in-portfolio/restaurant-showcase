🍜 24-Hour Bone Marrow Extraction

Patience in the Pot

Great pho cannot be rushed. At Pho Real, our master broth kettles simmer constantly for 24 uninterrupted hours. We gently extract deep gelatin and collagen from prime beef marrow bones, balanced by open-flame charred onions, ginger roots, whole star anise, and Saigon cinnamon.

Kitchen Craft

The 3 Pillars of Pho Real Culinary Standards

1. 24-Hour Low & Slow Simmer

Simmering beef knuckles, marrow bones, and brisket continuously to produce a broth of deep clarity, rich mouthfeel, and natural umami sweetness.

2. Fire-Charred Aromatics

Roasting whole yellow onions and unpeeled ginger directly over open flames before toasting star anise, cloves, and cardamom in dry cast iron pans.

3. The Real Deal Sampler

Hand-stuffing chicken wings with glass noodles, wrapping golden Cha Gio, and roasting whole quails to create our signature feast platter.
