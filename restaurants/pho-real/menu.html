Full Vietnamese Dining Menu

Slow-Simmered Pho, Sizzling Shaking Beef, & The Legendary Pho Real Deal Sampler.

Bone Broth Pho 24-Hour Craft

The Pho Real Special (Pho Dac Biet) #1 Signature

Generous combination of thinly sliced rare eye round steak, well-done flank, brisket, soft tendon, and Vietnamese beef meatballs in 24-hour simmered beef bone broth over fresh banh pho rice noodles.

Pho Ga (Free-Range Chicken Pho) House Favorite

Tender poached all-white free-range chicken breast served in golden spiced chicken broth infused with ginger, coriander, and scallion oil, accompanied by fresh lime and Thai basil.

Pho Chay (Vegetarian Lotus Pho) Plant-Based

Organic fried tofu, king oyster mushrooms, crisp lotus root, baby bok choy, and carrots in a 100% vegetarian charred onion and daikon radish broth.

Pho Tai Bo Vien (Steak & Meatball) Classic Bowl

Tender slices of rare eye round steak cooked gently in piping hot beef marrow broth paired with seasoned beef meatballs and fresh bean sprouts.

Street Feasts & Drinks Appetizers & Bar

The "Pho Real Deal" Sampler Platter Feast Starter

Our crowd-favorite shareable appetizer tower featuring crispy stuffed chicken wings, golden pork egg rolls (Cha Gio), roasted quail, and fresh shrimp spring rolls (Goi Cuon).

Bun Thit Nuong Cha Gio Vermicelli Bowl

Char-grilled lemongrass pork and crispy egg rolls over chilled rice vermicelli noodles, shredded lettuce, mint, pickled carrots, crushed peanuts, and house nuoc cham.

Bo Luc Lac (Shaking Beef) Wok Sizzled

Prime Angus beef tenderloin cubes flash-seared in a smoking wok with garlic, butter, and sweet soy sauce, served over a bed of watercress and steamed jasmine rice.

Ca Phe Sua Da (Vietnamese Iced Coffee) House Brew

Dark French roast coffee slowly dripped through a traditional metal phin filter directly over sweet condensed milk, poured over crushed ice.
