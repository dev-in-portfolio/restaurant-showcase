Artisanal Ramen 16-Hour Broths

Signature Tonkotsu Black Garlic Ramen #1 Signature

Rich 16-hour pork bone broth drizzled with roasted black garlic oil (mayu), served with thick braised pork belly chashu, seasoned ajitsuke tamago egg, menma bamboo shoots, wood ear mushrooms, and spring scallions.

Fiery Spicy Miso Tonkotsu Ramen Spicy Favorite

Creamy pork broth blended with fermented red miso paste, house chili oil, ground spicy pork, charred corn kernels, bok choy, and crispy nori seaweed sheets.

Tokyo Shoyu Chicken Ramen Clear Broth

Clear simmered chicken dashi broth seasoned with aged Japanese soy sauce, tender sliced chicken breast, soft egg, scallions, and wavy wheat noodles.

Pan-Fried Japanese Pork Gyoza (6pc) Crispy Starter

Handmade pork and cabbage dumplings pan-seared to a golden crisp lace and served with house garlic sesame soy dipping sauce.

Island Poke & Boba Fresh Sashimi

Classic Ahi Tuna & Salmon Poke Bowl Poke Star

Sushi-grade yellowfin tuna and fresh Atlantic salmon over seasoned sushi rice, topped with avocado cubes, edamame, sweet mango, cucumber ribbons, masago, and citrus ginger ponzu.

Spicy Salmon Crunch Poke Bowl House Favorite

Fresh diced salmon tossed in house Sriracha aioli over mixed greens and rice, topped with jalapeño rounds, crispy fried onions, furikake seasoning, and eel sauce drizzle.

Dragon Sushi Roll Specialty Roll

Crispy tempura shrimp and cucumber inside, draped with whole barbecued freshwater eel, sliced avocado, toasted sesame seeds, and sweet kabayaki glaze.
