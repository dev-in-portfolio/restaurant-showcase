🏛 The Pokebowl Ramen Story

Two Traditions, One Table.

Pokebowl Ramen was created around a simple culinary dream: bringing together the slow-cooked, soul-warming comfort of authentic Japanese ramen with the refreshing, colorful energy of Hawaiian poke bowls. Located at 9621 Brookdale Dr in Northeast Charlotte, we refuse shortcuts in our stock pots or our raw fish bars.

Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of Pokebowl Ramen

Authentic Time & Heat

Never using artificial soup bouillon powders or shortcuts—our 16-hour bone simmer is what gives our tonkotsu broth its legendary richness.

Supreme Freshness

Inspecting every salmon and yellowfin tuna loin upon arrival so your poke bowl delivers ocean-fresh sashimi quality in every bite.

Warm Neighborhood Spirit

Welcoming UNC Charlotte students, neighborhood families, and lunchtime commuters with fast, friendly service and customizable bowls.
