University Place Boardwalk • Charlotte NC

Lakeside Living, Four-Legged Friends.

The Wine Vault’s outdoor patio is situated directly along the charming wooden boardwalk of University Place lake. Bring your dogs, open a chilled bottle of Sauvignon Blanc or Pinot Noir, order a charcuterie board, and soak in the fresh air and live acoustic tunes.

100% Dog Friendly

Well-behaved pups on leash are warmly welcomed. We provide fresh water bowls and plenty of shaded spots along the boardwalk.

Atmosphere

Lakeside Sunsets

Watch the sun go down over the water as the boardwalk lights reflect on the lake. Outdoor heaters keep the patio comfortable during crisp autumn evenings.

Live Tunes

Weekend Acoustic Sets

Enjoy free live acoustic performances by talented local Charlotte songwriters, bluegrass duos, and folk guitarists on weekend afternoons.

Retail Bottles & Small Corkage

Drink Any Bottle on the Boardwalk

Pick any bottle from our expansive retail inventory of 1,000+ global wines, and our bartenders will open and serve it for you at your patio table with proper glassware for a modest corkage fee.
