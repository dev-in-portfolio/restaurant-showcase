Directions & Parking

Arriving at University Place

  • Driving & Parking: Located off W.T. Harris Blvd and J.M. Keynes Drive in University Place. Abundant complimentary surface lot parking is available throughout the shopping complex.
  • Boardwalk Access: Park in the central plaza lot and follow the pedestrian signs down to the lakefront boardwalk. The Wine Vault is nestled directly along the waterfront promenade.
  • Charlotte LYNX Blue Line: Easily accessible from the JW Clay Blvd / UNC Charlotte light rail station with a brief walk or rideshare down to the boardwalk.
