# Creative Design Brief: The Derby

## 1. Restaurant Identity & Intelligence
- **Name**: The Derby
- **Cuisine**: Southern Speakeasy & Wood-Fired Steakhouse
- **Tagline**: Bourbon, Prime Steaks & Southern Heritage
- **Location**: 5638 N Tryon St, Charlotte, NC 28213

## 2. Three Concept Directions Evaluated

### Concept A: Speakeasy Leather & Bourbon Salon (SELECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Bespoke Southern Speakeasy & Wood-Fired Steakhouse aesthetics. Primary accent `#d97706`, deep background `#0f172a`.
- **Typography**: Display font `'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif` paired with `'DM Sans', sans-serif`.
- **Signature Feature**: Bourbon & Prime Cut Pairing Guide.
- **Header Strategy**: Brand badge + adaptive navigation drawer activating at 1024px to prevent horizontal crowding.

### Concept B: Modern Southern Grill Room (REJECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Rejected for lack of distinct regional identity.

### Concept C: Classic Horse & Track Tavern (REJECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Rejected as too generic for The Derby.

## 3. Art Direction Rationale
- **Color Logic**: Accent `#d97706` for callouts with `#0f172a` background.
- **Typography Logic**: Hierarchy using `'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif` for titles and `'DM Sans', sans-serif` for legibility.
- **Responsive Navigation**: Adaptive breakpoint at 1024px tested across 1440px to 360px with zero overflow.
