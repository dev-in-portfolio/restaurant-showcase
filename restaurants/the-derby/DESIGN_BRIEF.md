# Design Brief: The Derby

## Restaurant Identity & Positioning
- **Restaurant Name**: The Derby
- **Cuisine & Concept**: Classic Southern Tavern & Grill
- **Tagline**: Bourbon, Prime Steaks & Southern Heritage
- **Location**: 5638 N Tryon St, Charlotte, NC 28213

## Creative Art Direction
- **Named Direction**: Classic Southern Tavern & Grill Bespoke Showcase
- **Typography Logic**: Display font `'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif` paired with `'Inter', sans-serif` for clean hierarchy and premium legibility.
- **Color Logic**: Primary accent `#d97706` with deep dark backdrop `#0f172a` providing strong contrast.
- **Signature Interaction**: Bourbon & Pairings Selector built directly into the home experience to guide visitor discovery.
