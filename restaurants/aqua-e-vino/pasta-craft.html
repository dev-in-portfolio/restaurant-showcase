The Art of Fresh Pasta

Every morning in our Strawberry Hill kitchen, Chef Gabriele Grigolon combines imported Italian Tipo 00 flour with organic egg yolks to create silky, tender pasta dough.

Northern Italian Standards

The Four Steps of Pasta Mastery

1. Tipo 00 & Egg Yolks

High-protein Italian flour and farm-fresh egg yolks blended for elastic texture and golden hue.

2. Hand Rolling & Sheeting

Rolled paper-thin with wooden pins to preserve microscopic surface porousness for sauce adhesion.

3. Artisan Filling

House-made fillings using braised ossobuco marrow, ricotta, and roasted vegetables stuffed by hand.

4. Al Dente Finish

Cooked to exact al dente timing in sea-salted boiling water and mantecato-emulsified with cooking water and butter.
