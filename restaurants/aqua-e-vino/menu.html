Northern Italian Enoteca

The Dinner & Lunch Menu

Crafted by Chef Gabriele Grigolon using imported Italian ingredients, house-rolled pasta, and wild Mediterranean seafood.

Antipasti Starters

Octopus Carpaccio Chef Specialty

Thinly sliced octopus marinated in Ligurian extra virgin olive oil, capers, lemon zest, baby arugula, and pink peppercorns.

Burrata Pugliese Imported Fresh

Creamy Pugliese burrata cheese served with roasted heirloom cherry tomatoes, basil pesto, and grilled sourdough crostini.

Primi Piatti Handmade Pasta

Tagliatelle al Duck Ragù Pairs w/ Barolo

Hand-rolled egg yolk tagliatelle ribbons tossed in slow-braised Venetian duck ragù, juniper berries, and aged Parmigiano Reggiano.

Ravioli d’Ossobuco House Stuffed

House-stuffed pasta pillows filled with slow-roasted braised veal ossobuco marrow, saffron glaze, and lemon gremolata.

Potato Gnocchi Gorgonzola Hand Pillowed

Soft house-made potato gnocchi folded into rich Gorgonzola Dolce cream sauce with toasted Piedmont walnuts.

Secondi Piatti Meat & Seafood

Pan-Seared Branzino Mediterranean Sea

Crispy skin Mediterranean sea bass fillet served over roasted fennel puree, artichoke hearts, and Taggiasca olives.

Veal Chop Milanese Bone-In

Pounded bone-in milk-fed veal chop breaded in herb focaccia crumbs, pan-fried in clarified butter, served with wild arugula and lemon.

Chianti Braised Short Rib Slow Roasted

Tender boneless beef short rib braised for 12 hours in Chianti Classico wine, served over creamy polenta Bramata.

Dolci & Digestivi Desserts

Tiramisù Tradizionale Veneto Recipe

Classic Venetian espresso-soaked savoiardi ladyfingers layered with whipped mascarpone cream and Valrhona cocoa dust.

Limoncello Panna Cotta Chilled

Silky vanilla bean panna cotta infused with Sorrento limoncello and topped with fresh raspberry coulis.
