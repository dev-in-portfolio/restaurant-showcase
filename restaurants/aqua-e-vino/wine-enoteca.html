Italian Terroir

The Enoteca Cellar

Our wine program highlights artisanal Italian growers and indigenous grape varieties from Veneto, Piedmont, Tuscany, and Friuli-Venezia Giulia.

Barolo & Bold Reds Piedmont & Tuscany

Barolo DOCG (Piedmont) Pairs w/ Duck Ragù

Nebbiolo grape producing aromas of dried rose petals, tar, and dark cherry with structured tannin and elegant acidity.

Amarone della Valpolicella Veneto Special

Rich full-bodied red made from dried Corvina grapes with notes of dark chocolate, fig, and black cherry liqueur.

Brunello di Montalcino Tuscan Sangiovese

Aged 100% Sangiovese Grosso featuring earthy truffle, tobacco leaf, and ripe plum complexity.

Whites & Sparklings Friuli & Veneto

Prosecco Superiore DOCG Valdobbiadene

Artisanal extra dry sparkling wine from hillside vineyards in Valdobbiadene, crisp with green apple and white floral notes.

Pinot Grigio delle Venezie Pairs w/ Branzino

Single-estate Northern Italian white wine with vibrant minerality, pear skin aromatics, and bright acidity.

Friulano Colli Orientali Indigenous Grape

Native white wine from Friuli displaying delicate almond finish, white peach, and stony mineral undertones.
