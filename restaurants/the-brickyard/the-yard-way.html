House Rules, Painted On Brick

The Yard Way.

Every bar says it's a neighborhood bar. The ‘Yard puts its rules where everybody can read them — six lines that explain exactly how this room runs.

The Door Never Charges.

"NEVER a cover, ALWAYS a crowd" isn't a slogan somebody invented for a sign — it is the operating rule the bar prints on its own pages. Walk up broke and thirsty-curious; the only money that matters is your tab.

No Reservations. Ever.

The Brickyard doesn't take them — not for high-tops, not for the patio, not for game day. First in the door picks their seat; that's the whole system.

Closed When Others Open.

Monday and Tuesday the lights stay off so Wednesday through Sunday can run hot: five nights, every one of them ending at 2 AM.

Weekends Start At Two.

Saturday and Sunday doors open at 2 PM — afternoon daylight on the rooftop, golden hour on the patio, and a full arc into the late set.

Every Screen Works.

TVs throughout the house are there for viewing all sporting events. If you care about a score, this room cares with you.

The Stairs Go Somewhere.

A rooftop bar over South Tryon is not a given for a neighborhood joint — it's the ‘Yard's ace. Take it.
