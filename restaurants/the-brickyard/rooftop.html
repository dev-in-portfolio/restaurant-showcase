Up Top · Out Back · In The Sun

The Rooftop Is The Move.

Two open-air rooms in one bar: the rooftop bar over South Tryon for golden-hour pours, and the patio out back when the night wants to keep going. Both come with the same house rule as everywhere else at the ‘Yard — never a cover.

The Rooftop Bar

Above the South Tryon corridor with the whole South End afternoon spread out around you. Weekend doors open at 2 PM — early enough that the rooftop is yours before the block fills up.

The Patio

Street level and easygoing, out back of the bar room. It carries the night: bands inside, fresh air outside, and nobody has to choose between them.

Golden-Hour Orders

Pacifico by daylight, High Life by dusk, Fireball when the band starts. The full board lives on the kitchen & bar page.
