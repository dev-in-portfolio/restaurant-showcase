OUR STORY & CRAFT PHILOSOPHY

Rock 'n' roll attitude, artisanal burgers, and Uptown Charlotte's finest whiskey collection.

Uptown Charlotte Roots

WE PUT MORE COWBELL IN THE QUEEN CITY

Founded in 2012 at 201 North Tryon Street (Suite 1010 on 5th Street), Cowbell Burger & Whiskey Bar set out with an unapologetic mission: elevate the humble American hamburger to culinary greatness while pouring the world's most sought-after whiskeys.

We rejected cookie-cutter pre-formed beef and commercial fryer oil. Instead, our chef team engineered a proprietary whole-muscle grind of choice chuck, rich brisket, and tender short rib, cooked in cast iron with custom seasonings, served alongside potatoes twice-fried in golden duck fat.

Step through our doors and you enter an upscale rock lounge draped in dark mahogany, amber backlighting, and plush velvet booths—a high-energy culinary sanctuary for Charlotte foodies and night owls.

The Proprietary Grind

Our beef is ground fresh daily from whole cuts of American choice chuck for body, brisket for rich buttery fat content, and short rib for intense umami depth.

The 150+ Whiskey Vault

From hard-to-find Pappy Van Winkle and Blanton's allocations to peaty Islay single malts and high-rye craft distillers, our whiskey catalog is unmatched in Uptown.

Late Night Until 2 AM

We proudly keep our kitchen firing late—serving full gourmet burgers, duck fat truffle fries, and boozy shakes until midnight Sunday-Thursday and 2:00 AM on weekends.
