🍾 1920s Mixology & Hand-Crafted Spirits

Bootlegger Roots, Liquid Gold.

The craft of the 1920s speakeasy demanded ingenuity, boldness, and deep respect for balanced flavor. At Prohibition, our bartenders revive classic American recipes using small-batch whiskeys, Carolina moonshines, hand-pressed citrus, and torch-smoked oak woods.

Speakeasy Mastery

The 3 Pillars of Prohibition Cocktail Craft

1. Smoked Whiskey Craft

Infusing small-batch bourbons with aromatic smoke from charred oak barrels, bourbon chips, and wild rosemary to create our signature Old Fashioneds.

2. Carolina Moonshines

Partnering with artisanal regional distilleries to serve authentic North Carolina mountain moonshines in mules, smashes, and neat flights.

3. Hand-Cut Crystal Ice

Freezing and cutting dense crystal ice blocks that chill spirits to near-freezing temperatures without rapid water dilution.
