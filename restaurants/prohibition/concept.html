🍾 The Prohibition Story

Roaring Twenties, Center City Soul.

Prohibition was created to capture the mystery, rebellious excitement, and craft cocktail artistry of the 1920s. Built with rustic reclaimed barnwood, antique copper stills, and plush leather booths at 200 N Tryon Street, we have stood as an Uptown nightlife landmark for over a decade.

The Values of Prohibition

Authentic Nostalgia

Honoring the clandestine spirit of the Roaring Twenties with reclaimed historic woods and Prohibition-era recipes.

Electrifying Energy

Transitioning seamlessly from sophisticated happy hour cocktails into an energetic, music-driven late-night celebration.

Uptown Distinction

Anchoring Center City Charlotte’s entertainment district on 5th and Tryon with unmatched hospitality and views.
