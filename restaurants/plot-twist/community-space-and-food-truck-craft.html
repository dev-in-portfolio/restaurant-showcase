🏛 Industrial Garage Lounge & Dog-Friendly Patio

Converted Garage, Open Courtyard.

Once a mid-century auto mechanics garage, Plot Twist has been reimagined as an inviting, light-filled neighborhood sanctuary. Massive industrial roll-up doors open seamlessly to an outdoor courtyard patio, setting the stage for weekend food truck pop-ups, remote work, and evening drinks.

The 3 Pillars of the Plot Twist Space

1. Converted Garage Architecture

Preserving original concrete floors, exposed metal beams, and roll-up garage doors paired with plush velvet lounge seating and warm ambient lighting.

2. Rotating Food Truck Pop-Ups

Welcoming Charlotte’s best mobile food trucks on Thursday through Sunday to serve street tacos, artisan pizzas, and gourmet sliders right in our courtyard.

3. Dog-Friendly Neighborhood Haven

An inclusive outdoor patio welcoming pets of all sizes with fresh water bowls, shaded tables, and a relaxed Plaza Midwood neighborhood vibe.
