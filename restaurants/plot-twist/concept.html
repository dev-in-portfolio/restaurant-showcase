🏛 The Plot Twist Story

Midwood Soul, Modern Sanctuary.

Plot Twist was created to reimagine what a neighborhood bar can be. Founded as a woman-led "third space" in Plaza Midwood, we set out to build an inclusive daytime coffee lounge and evening cocktail sanctuary where everyone—from espresso aficionados to zero-proof drinkers and cocktail lovers—finds their seat.

Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of Plot Twist

Inclusive Hospitality

Creating an intentionally warm, unpretentious environment where guests of all backgrounds feel equally celebrated.

Thoughtful Balance

Offering equal artistry to coffee, alcoholic spirits, and zero-proof botanical creations so no guest ever feels left out.

Local Collaboration

Partnering with local Charlotte roasters, herbalists, artists, and mobile food trucks to foster a rich community ecosystem.
