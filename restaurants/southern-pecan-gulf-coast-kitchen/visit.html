Arrival Directives

Phillips Place Parking & Accessibility

Arriving at 6706-C Phillips Place Court is seamless and stress-free:

  • Complimentary Surface & Deck Parking: Abundant free surface parking is available throughout Phillips Place, alongside a covered multi-level parking deck just steps away.
  • Evening Valet Service: Convenient valet parking is stationed directly in the central roundabout of Phillips Place Court for effortless dinner arrivals.
  • Curbside & Wheelchair Access: Seamless level curb transitions and wide double doors offer full ADA accessibility from parking to dining table.
Private Gatherings

Private Dining Room & Courtyard Patio

Host your next milestone dinner, rehearsal feast, or executive gathering:

  • Private Dining Room: Elegant enclosed dining space accommodating up to 40 guests with custom coastal menus and audiovisual setup.
  • Courtyard Patio Dining: Enjoy al fresco Lowcountry meals under towering oaks and gas lanterns in Phillips Place's charming plaza.
  • Whole Southern Pecan Pies: Handcrafted whole pecan pies and party platters available for holiday and celebration pickup. Call (704) 749-2949 to place orders.
