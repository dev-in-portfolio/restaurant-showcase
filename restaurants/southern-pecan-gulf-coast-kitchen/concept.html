Phillips Place Village • Charlotte SouthPark

A Symphony of Southern Coastlines.

Southern Pecan Gulf Coast Kitchen was founded to bring the genuine spirit, rich flavors, and timeless hospitality of the Gulf of Mexico and Carolina Lowcountry to Charlotte. Located in the picturesque courtyard of Phillips Place, we celebrate the shared culinary lineage connecting New Orleans, Mobile, and Charleston.

Core Values

The Foundations of Southern Pecan

What anchors our scratch kitchen and dining room in SouthPark.

Value 01

Authentic Scratch Cooking

We make our stocks from simmered roasted seafood, whip fresh remoulade by hand, bake our cornbread and pies from raw ingredients, and fry only to order.

Value 02

Uncompromising Hospitality

True Southern hospitality is heartfelt and generous. From welcoming smiles at the host stand to knowledgeable table service, guests are treated like family.

Value 03

Phillips Place Sanctuary

Surrounded by European-style architecture, bubbling fountains, and majestic oak trees, our dining room and courtyard patio offer an elegant dining escape.

Gathering Place

SouthPark's Gathering Table

Whether you are celebrating a milestone anniversary, hosting business colleagues over a leisurely lunch, or enjoying a casual weeknight bowl of gumbo with family, Southern Pecan provides the gracious warmth and exceptional food that make every meal memorable.
