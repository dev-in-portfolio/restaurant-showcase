From-Scratch Gulf Coast Heritage.

Welcome to Southern Pecan Gulf Coast Kitchen, located at 6706-C Phillips Place Court in Charlotte’s SouthPark district. Drawing inspiration from New Orleans, coastal Alabama, and the Carolina Lowcountry, our kitchen crafts authentic regional classics from scratch daily—from Charleston shrimp and creamy stone-ground grits to dark-roux seafood gumbo, blackened redfish, and our signature warm Southern pecan pie.

Heritage Roux

Dark Mahogany Seafood Gumbo

Built upon a patient 45-minute hand-stirred flour and oil roux, our gumbo is laden with wild Gulf shrimp, lump crabmeat, tender okra, and smoked andouille sausage over rice.

Cajun-Creole Soul

Slow-Simmered Seafood & Andouille Gumbo

A true Louisiana gumbo cannot be rushed. Our kitchen slowly toasts flour in pure oil until it reaches the precise deep mahogany hue of roasted pecans. Simmered with rich seafood stock, holy trinity aromatic vegetables, gulf shrimp, and charred andouille sausage, it delivers a masterclass in coastal Southern flavor.

Dessert Heritage

Handmade Southern Pecan Pie & Cobbler

End your meal with our signature dessert tradition. Every pie is handcrafted in our pastry station using whole halved Georgia pecans, real butter, and pure cane syrup baked until set with a delicate caramelized top. Enjoy alongside warm peach cobblers and sticky toffee butter cakes.
