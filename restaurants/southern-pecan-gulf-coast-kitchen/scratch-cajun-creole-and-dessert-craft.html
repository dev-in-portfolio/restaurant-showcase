Patient Kitchen Disciplines • Namesake Bakery

The Sacred Roux, The Warm Pie.

Two iconic culinary traditions define our identity at Southern Pecan Gulf Coast Kitchen: the patient, constant figure-eight stirring of a forty-five-minute dark mahogany gumbo roux, and the morning baking ritual of our namesake Southern Pecan Pie in Phillips Place.

Kitchen Techniques

The Craft of Gumbo & Pastry

Time, temperature, and technique in every pot and pan.

Technique 01

The 45-Minute Dark Roux

Equal parts unbleached flour and clean oil stirred continuously over medium heat until reaching the deep dark shade of dark chocolate and roasted pecans.

Technique 02

The Creole Holy Trinity

Finely diced yellow onions, green bell peppers, and crisp celery are plunged into the smoking hot roux to halt cooking and release aromatic sweetness.

Technique 03

Georgia Pecan Custard

Whole halves of selected Georgia pecans are suspended in rich pure cane syrup, dark brown sugar, and melted butter custard, baked in hand-crimped crust.

A Meal In A Bowl

Seafood, Okra & Andouille Harmony

Every ladle of our Seafood & Andouille Gumbo tells the story of Louisiana coastal camps and plantation kitchens. Tender okra provides natural body, while wild Gulf shrimp, lump blue crab, and peppery smoked sausage impart savory depth that lingers through the final spoonful.
