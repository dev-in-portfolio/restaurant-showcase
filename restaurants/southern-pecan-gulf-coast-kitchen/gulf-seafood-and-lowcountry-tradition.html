The Four Pillars of Our Seafood Craft

Techniques perfected across generations along the Southern coast.

Pillar 01

Wild Gulf Brown & White Shrimp

We exclusively source wild-caught shrimp from coastal Gulf waters, prized for their firm texture, natural sweetness, and authentic snap when seared in cast iron.

Pillar 02

Heirloom Stone-Ground Grits

Milled slowly on granite stones to preserve the germ and natural corn sweetness, simmered for forty minutes with heavy cream, butter, and sharp aged white cheddar.

Pillar 03

Cast-Iron Blackening

Redfish fillets are brushed with clarified butter, dredged in our house-blended eleven-spice blackened rub, and seared on scorching cast iron to seal in succulent juices.

Fryer Discipline

Cornmeal Crust & House Comeback Sauce

Southern seafood frying is an art of restraint. We coat tender select Gulf oysters and Carolina catfish in seasoned stone-ground yellow cornmeal before flashing them in clean oil at 360 degrees. The result is a feather-light golden crust that lets the brininess of the oyster and sweetness of the fish shine through.
