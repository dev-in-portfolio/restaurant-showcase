Stir-Fry Philosophy

3 Principles of Bonzai Wok Noodles

1. Intense High Heat

Flash-searing noodles at extreme temperatures locks in flavor and prevents soggy texture.

2. Scratch Sauce Reductions

House-brewed tamarind glaze, sweet dark soy sauce, and crushed chili garlic paste prepared fresh daily.

3. Fresh Aromatics

Fresh Thai sweet basil, kaffir lime, crushed garlic, and scallions added at the final second of tossing.
