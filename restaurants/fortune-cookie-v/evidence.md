# Evidence & Design Ledger for Fortune Cookie V

## Creative Brief
- **Client Identity:** Fortune Cookie V, located at 7320 The Plaza Ste A, Charlotte, NC 28215 in East Charlotte (Newell area).
- **Concept:** Premier fast-casual and takeout Chinese kitchen specializing in authentic New York-style Chinese classics, sizzling high-heat wok cooking (Wok Hei), generous combination platters, handmade crispy egg rolls, golden crab rangoon, General Tso's chicken, beef and broccoli, house special fried rice, and catering pans.
- **Target Audience:** East Charlotte residents, lunch patrons, busy families ordering dinner takeout, office groups ordering catering pans.
- **Design Language:** Imperial Crimson #C02626, Wok Flame Gold #F59E0B, Jade Green #10B981, Deep Charcoal #111827, and Warm Light #F9FAFB. Modern Chinese takeout aesthetics with glowing wok heat accents, responsive drawer navigation, and interactive office catering calculator.

## Claim Ledger

- **Menu Offerings Claim:** General Tso's chicken, sesame chicken, honey garlic chicken wings, beef with broccoli, house special lo mein, Singapore mei fun, crab rangoon cheese wontons, steamed dumplings, hot and sour soup, and fortune cookies with every order.
- **Catering Claim:** Half and full catering pans of fried rice, noodles, and wok entrees for office luncheons, family reunions, and game day parties.

## Add-On Preservation
- **Interactive Wok Feast & Catering Calculator:** Allows users to choose guest headcount (10 to 200 people), select party tiers (Classic Takeout Buffet, Wok Master Feast, Imperial Dragon Banquet), add extra half/full pans of Lo Mein or Fried Rice, add extra platters of Crab Rangoon / Egg Rolls, and generate real-time pricing and pre-filled email orders.
- **Interactive Category Filter:** Live tab switching across All, Chef's Specialties, Sizzling Wok Entrees, Fried Rice & Noodles, Appetizers & Wings, and Soups & Healthy Steamed on menu.html.
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Expanding answers covering custom spice levels, MSG preferences, dietary accommodations, catering lead times, and pickup on contact.html.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- **Namespace:** .fcv-* exclusively used across HTML, CSS, and JS.
- **Distinct Aesthetic:** Vibrant imperial crimson and wok gold theme, high contrast cards, bold callouts, and clean layout uniquely differentiating from Mediterranean lounges, Mexican fondas, and artisanal bakeries.
