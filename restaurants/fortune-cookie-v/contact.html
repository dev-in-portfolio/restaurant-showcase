Pickup Guidelines

Fast & Easy Takeout

1. Phone Orders: Most standard lunch and dinner orders are hot and ready within 10 to 15 minutes of calling.

2. Walk-in Counter: Feel free to walk in and order at our front register. Our rapid wok line ensures you won't wait long.

3. Large Catering Orders: For party pans feeding over 20 guests, please order at least 2 to 4 hours in advance, or 24 hours for weekend luncheons.

Dietary & Custom Spice Requests

We are happy to accommodate dietary requests such as no MSG, steamed preparation, low sodium, extra sauce, or customized spice levels from mild to super fiery.
