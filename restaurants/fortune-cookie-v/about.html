Our Story

Bringing New York Quality Down South

The story of bringing authentic northern and Cantonese wok flavors to Charlotte, North Carolina.

When our family opened Fortune Cookie V at 7320 The Plaza in the Newell neighborhood of Charlotte, our mission was clear: create an authentic New York style neighborhood Chinese kitchen where neighbors could find generous combination platters, sizzling hot wok entrees, and golden crispy egg rolls seven days a week.

Unlike buffet concepts or steam table cafeterias, every single order at Fortune Cookie V starts only when the kitchen ticket prints. Our wok master fires the intense 700°F burner, heats the seasoned carbon steel, and flash-cooks each meal in under 3 minutes. This keeps meats tender, vegetables bursting with crunch, and sauces rich with caramelized flavor.

We are honored to serve our East Charlotte community—from hardworking hospital and warehouse staff grabbing a fast lunch combo to neighborhood families picking up dinner on Friday night. Thank you for your continued loyalty and support!
