The Art of Wok Hei

Breath of the Wok: 700°F Intense Flame

In authentic Chinese wok cookery, true flavor comes from "Wok Hei" — the distinct smoky aroma imparted by tossing fresh ingredients over jet-engine burners at temperatures exceeding 700 degrees Fahrenheit. This instant sear seals in natural juices, keeps vegetables crisp, and caramelizes our handcrafted sauces.

From our signature General Tso's reduction to our slow-simmered bone broths and hand-wrapped crab rangoons, we honor classic New York Chinatown recipes that have satisfied hungry families for generations.
