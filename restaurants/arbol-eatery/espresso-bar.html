Single Origin & Latin Craft

The Espresso & Beverage Program

Ethically sourced Latin American beans, velvety steamed milks, house-made horchata foams, and fresh tropical fruit smoothies.

Single-Origin Latin Heritage Coffee

At Arbol Eatery, our coffee beans are directly sourced from generational family-run farms in Colombia, Guatemala, and Costa Rica. Roasted to a medium-dark profile, our espresso highlights notes of dark cocoa, toasted hazelnut, and sweet panela sugarcane.

We craft our signature syrups and milk blends in-house, from our three-milk sweetened condensed reduction for the Tres Leches Latte to our scratch rice-and-cinnamon Horchata cold brew foam.

Looking for non-coffee refreshments? We whisk ceremonial-grade Japanese organic matcha and blend whole-fruit tropical smoothies with pure coconut water.

Espresso Craft

Tres Leches Latte

Sweetened condensed milk, evaporated milk, rich double espresso, steamed milk, and cinnamon dust. Available hot or over crystal ice.

Slow Brew

Horchata Cold Brew

Steeped for 20 hours in cold mountain water, topped with sweet house horchata cream foam for the perfect afternoon energy boost.

Ceremonial Matcha
