# Evidence & Brand Ledger: Arbol Eatery

## Creative Brief
- **Brand Identity:** Arbol Eatery is Uptown Charlotte's premier Latin-inspired artisanal cafe, espresso bar, and corporate lunch eatery, situated in the lobby concourse of Carillon Tower at 227 W Trade Street.
- **Atmosphere & Concept:** Sunlit botanical sanctuary combining the warmth of a Latin espresso bar with fast-casual scratch culinary excellence. Features rich terracotta and forest canopy tones, fragrant roasted specialty coffee, and handcrafted breakfast and lunch offerings.
- **Culinary Focus:** Vibrant Latin American comfort foods reimagined with fresh, wholesome ingredients: mojo-braised pork, adobo chicken, grilled steak with chimichurri, fresh avocado, artisan Cuban bread, organic grains, and house-made salsas.
- **Key Offerings:**
  - Signature Pressed Paninis & Sandwiches: Traditional Cubano Panini (mojo pork, ham, Swiss, mustard, pickles), Chicken Torta Panini (adobo chicken, avocado mash, black beans, queso fresco), La Carolina (pulled pork, chipotle BBQ, slaw), Chimichurri Steak Panini, Roasted Veggie & Hummus Wrap.
  - Breakfast & Morning Bowls: Uptown Breakfast Burrito, Avocado & Poached Egg Toast on sourdough, Tropical A?ai Bowls, Greek Yogurt & Granola Berry Parfaits.
  - Nutrient-Dense Grain Bowls & Salads: Mojo Chicken & Quinoa Bowl, Latin Caesar Salad with roasted corn and cotija, Chimichurri Steak Power Bowl.
  - Specialty Latin Espresso Bar: Tres Leches Latte, Dulce de Leche Macchiato, Horchata Cold Brew, Ceremonial Grade Matcha, Mango Passionfruit Smoothies.
  - Uptown Corporate Catering: Executive Panini Box Lunches, Morning Breakfast Burrito Platters, Coffee Cambros, and Afternoon Sweet Trays.
- **Operating Hours:** Monday-Friday 7:00 AM - 3:00 PM; Saturday-Sunday Closed (weekend private catering available).
- **Location:** 227 W Trade St, Suite 125 (Carillon Tower), Charlotte, NC 28202. Phone: (704) 930-2320.

## Claim Ledger
1. **Carillon Tower Uptown Anchor:** Located at 227 W Trade St Suite 125, serving Uptown Charlotte's financial district, corporate towers, and nearby cultural venues with rapid morning breakfast and lunchtime dining.
2. **Authentic Latin-Inspired Recipes:** Highlighting house-made mojo marinades, fresh chimichurri, pickled red onions, slow-braised meats, and pressed Cuban bread.
3. **Specialty Latin Espresso Program:** Signature coffee beverages crafted with single-origin espresso beans, including the fan-favorite Tres Leches Latte, Dulce de Leche Macchiato, and Horchata Cold Brew.
4. **Corporate Catering Specialization:** Tailored office box lunches, breakfast burrito platters, and coffee services with direct delivery to Uptown Charlotte office towers.
5. **Interactive Corporate Catering Calculator:** Providing immediate, transparent estimates for corporate meeting planners ordering executive lunches, breakfast trays, and beverage services.

## Add-On Preservation
- **Interactive Uptown Corporate Catering Calculator:** Dynamically calculates costs for breakfast boxes, panini lunches, grain bowl platters, coffee cambros, and delivery fees with itemized subtotals and one-click quote requests.
- **Interactive Menu Filter System:** Category-based filtering across Breakfast, Paninis & Sandwiches, Bowls & Salads, and Specialty Espresso & Smoothies.
- **Responsive Drawer Navigation:** Mobile navigation drawer with aria attributes, touch responsiveness, and clean keyboard accessibility.
- **Uptown Ordering & Visit Guide:** Operating hours, pedestrian and skywalk access directions, parking tips, and contact channels.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- **Namespace:** Dedicated `.arb-*` CSS prefix ensuring complete style encapsulation.
- **Visual Distinction:** Earthy botanical modern Latin aesthetic blending forest canopy green (`#15803d`), terracotta clay (`#ea580c`), warm sunshine amber (`#facc15`), and light linen parchment (`#fefce8`).
- **Typography:** Refined display serif headings (`Cinzel` / `Playfair Display`) paired with clean modern body typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) celebrating artisanal craftsmanship and hospitality.
- **Zero Placeholder Words & Zero Emojis:** Pure factual menu data, accurate pricing, and zero placeholder text throughout.
