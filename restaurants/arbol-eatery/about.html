The Story of Arbol Eatery

Rooted in authentic Latin culinary traditions, Arbol Eatery was created to bring warmth, craft espresso, and wholesome scratch dining to Uptown Charlotte.

An Urban Oasis in Carillon Tower

"?rbol" means tree in Spanish?a symbol of deep roots, nourishment, community shade, and continuous growth. Located at 227 West Trade Street in Uptown Charlotte's iconic Carillon Tower, Arbol Eatery was designed as a welcoming sanctuary for professionals, commuters, and city residents.

We believe fast-casual daytime dining should never mean compromising on scratch craftsmanship. That is why our pork is slow-roasted in citrus mojo, our salsas are blended fresh each morning, and our coffee is pulled from ethically traded single-origin Latin American espresso beans.

Whether you are picking up a quick Tres Leches Latte before a morning meeting or catering an executive lunch for fifty colleagues, our team delivers the vibrant flavors and genuine hospitality of Latin America.
