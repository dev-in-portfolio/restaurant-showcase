Distilled Spirits Bottled On-Site

Oaklore Four Grain Bourbon Flagship Spirit

Handcrafted mash bill of NC yellow corn, soft winter wheat, malted barley, and rye. Aged in deeply charred American white oak casks with notes of warm vanilla, butterscotch, and toasted pecan.

NC Straight Rye Whiskey Award Winner

Bold 95% rye grain bill distilled in copper pots and matured in new oak barrels. Crisp baking spice, cracked black pepper, dried apricot, and a long, warm honey finish.

Oaklore Botanical Citrus Gin Copper Distilled

Vapor-infused with North Carolina juniper, fresh orange peel, coriander, cardamom, and mountain angelica root. Bright, crisp, and beautifully balanced.

Blackberry Infused Craft Bourbon Seasonal Spirit

Oaklore bourbon steeped with sun-ripened Carolina blackberries and raw cane syrup. Luscious, velvety berry sweetness balanced by rich charred oak.

Smoked Oak Old Fashioned Signature Cocktail

Oaklore Four Grain Bourbon, demerara syrup, Angostura and orange bitters, presented under a cloche filled with aromatic applewood smoke and expressed orange peel.

Charred Cask Black Walnut Manhattan Barrel-Aged

NC Straight Rye Whiskey, sweet vermouth, and black walnut bitters rested in small oak barrels for 30 days. Served up with Luxardo cherries.

Distiller's Spirits Tasting Flight Tasting Flight
