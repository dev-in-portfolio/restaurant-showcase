🎆 Small-Cask Maturation & Smoked Aromatics

The Art of Barrel Cocktails

Aging complete cocktails in small charred oak barrels transforms great drinks into extraordinary experiences. At Oaklore, we rest our Old Fashioneds, Manhattans, and seasonal creations in 5-gallon American oak casks, allowing the spirits, vermouths, and botanical bitters to harmonize into velvety perfection.

Mixology Principles

The 3 Pillars of Oaklore Cocktails

1. Micro-Cask Barrel Aging

Resting batched cocktails in 5-gallon charred oak barrels for 30 to 45 days, softening spirit heat and extracting vanilla oak lactones.

2. Torched Applewood & Herbs

Infusing cocktails under glass cloches with fragrant torched applewood and smoking sprigs of fresh rosemary to elevate the sensory nose.

3. Crystal Clear Ice Spheres

Serving craft drinks over dense, hand-cut clear ice spheres that melt slowly without over-diluting the whiskey's natural proof.
