The Alchemy of Grain to Glass

Every drop of spirit produced at Oaklore Distilling Co. honors traditional distillation principles while celebrating North Carolina agriculture. From mashing local non-GMO heirloom grains to meticulous copper pot cuts and aging in heavy-char white oak barrels, quality is never rushed.

Distillery Philosophy

The 3 Pillars of Oaklore Distillation

1. NC Farm-Grown Grains

Partnering with regional agricultural growers for non-GMO corn, wheat, malted barley, and rye that impart genuine Carolina terroir.

2. Small-Batch Copper Runs

Distilling in custom copper pot stills and taking narrow heart cuts to capture rich aromatics while discarding harsh heads and tails.

3. Heavy Char White Oak Casks

Maturating whiskey in virgin American white oak barrels charred to level 3 and 4, developing deep butterscotch, vanilla, and baking spice notes.
