📖 The Stories Behind the Oak

Lore Told Over Great Spirits

The name "Oaklore" marries two profound traditions: the transformative power of charred white oak barrels and the timeless lore of stories shared around a fire. Located at 11136 Monroe Rd in Matthews, we created a distillery where fine craft spirits bring neighbors and friends together.

Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of Oaklore Distilling

Authentic Storytelling

Honoring the heritage of North Carolina distillers and fostering an inviting space where guests forge new memories and share their own lore.

Agricultural Craft

Respecting the land and local Carolina grain farmers by keeping every stage of mashing, fermenting, and distilling honest and transparent.

Patio Community

Creating a welcoming Matthews gathering haven with cozy outdoor fire pits, live Saturday acoustic music, and approachable hospitality.
