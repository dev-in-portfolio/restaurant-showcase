North Carolina Burger Roots

The Story of Barley & Burger

From its inception in Rocky Mount to Uptown Charlotte's premier food hall, our mission is simple: uncompromised chef-driven burgers and welcoming hospitality.

From Rocky Mount to Uptown Charlotte

Barley & Burger was founded by restaurateurs Travis and Kristi Ellis, along with hospitality visionary Brandon Clarke and New York Times best-selling author Etaf Rum. What began as a neighborhood craft burger and brew house in Rocky Mount, North Carolina quickly earned a cult following for its uncompromising quality and creative pop-culture burger builds.

Recognizing Charlotte's vibrant culinary appetite, the team brought their signature flat-top craft to Monarch Market at 101 N. Tryon Street under the Barley & Burger Smashed banner.

Here in the bustling heart of Center City at Trade and Tryon, we specialize in high-velocity, precision-smashed double burgers, crispy buttermilk chicken, and hand-spun frozen custard shakes for downtown office workers, urban residents, and visitors alike.
