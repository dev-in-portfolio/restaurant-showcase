# Evidence & Brand Ledger: Barley & Burger Smashed

## Creative Brief
- **Brand Identity:** Barley & Burger Smashed is Uptown Charlotte's premier chef-driven gourmet smashed burger destination, located inside the bustling Monarch Market food hall at Trade & Tryon.
- **Founding Roots:** Originally founded in Rocky Mount, NC by culinary enthusiasts Travis and Kristi Ellis alongside Brandon Clarke and best-selling author Etaf Rum, bringing high-craft gourmet burgers, hand-breaded chicken, and loaded sides to Charlotte.
- **Signature Culinary Technique:** Proprietary custom beef blend consisting of Certified Black Angus, brisket/short rib, and chuck seared on a screaming-hot flat-top grill to develop a signature caramelized, ultra-crispy lacy crust while sealing in rich, savory juices.
- **Menu Offerings:**
  - Signature Double Smashed Burgers: All American, Truffle Shuffle (truffle aioli & caramelized portobello), Ghostbuster (ghost chipotle & pepper jack), Marty McFly (Carolina chili, slaw, mustard), Rocky Balboa (B&B secret sauce & cherry peppers), Maverick (A.1. reduction & french onion straws), Big Kahuna (grilled pineapple & teriyaki).
  - Buttermilk Crispy Chicken: Crispy Bird, Hot Nashville Cayenne Bird, Hand-Breaded Buttermilk Tenders (4pc/6pc) with craft scratch sauces.
  - Sides & Loaded Potatoes: Seasoned Waffle Fries, Golden Fried Pickle Spears with buttermilk ranch, Beer-Battered Onion Rings, Loaded Smashed Fries with warm beer cheese and bacon bits.
  - Hand-Spun Custard Shakes: Vanilla Bean, Salted Caramel Pretzel, Chocolate Fudge Brownie, Oreo Cookies & Cream.
  - Corporate & Office Catering: Custom Smashed Burger & Slider Box Bundles with waffle fries and dips for Uptown Charlotte law firms, banks, and creative agencies.
- **Operating Hours:** Sunday-Thursday 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM; Friday-Saturday 11:00 AM - 10:00 PM.
- **Location:** 101 N. Tryon St (Inside Monarch Market), Charlotte, NC 28246. Phone: (980) 309-0830.

## Claim Ledger
1. **Signature Flat-Top Smashed Technique:** Hand-pressed double beef patties smashed razor-thin on cast-iron grills for the iconic crispy lacy edges, served on toasted sweet golden brioche buns.
2. **Proprietary Triple-Cut Beef Blend:** Crafted exclusively from Certified Black Angus chuck, short rib, and brisket for unmatched depth of flavor and tenderness.
3. **Pop-Culture-Inspired Gourmet Recipes:** Cleverly themed, chef-crafted recipes (Truffle Shuffle, Ghostbuster, Marty McFly, Rocky Balboa, Maverick) utilizing scratch-made aiolis, reductions, and glazes.
4. **Monarch Market Uptown Anchor:** Prime center-city location at Trade and Tryon, serving fast-casual walk-in dining, mobile order pickup, and office box lunch catering.
5. **Interactive Office Burger & Slider Box Calculator:** Providing instant transparent cost estimates for corporate event planners ordering lunch bundles, slider party platters, and craft dessert shakes.

## Add-On Preservation
- **Interactive Corporate Lunch & Slider Box Calculator:** Configures headcounts, meal tiers (Classic Smash Bundle, Premium Burger Box, Executive Slider Feast), side selections, milkshakes, and delivery options with live subtotal, service gratuity, and instant quote submission.
- **Interactive Menu Filter System:** Real-time filtering by category (All Items, Smashed Burgers, Crispy Chicken, Loaded Sides, Shakes & Brews).
- **Responsive Drawer Navigation:** Mobile drawer navigation with accessible keyboard and touch controls.
- **Uptown Ordering & Pickup Hub:** Operating schedules, location details, Monarch Market stall map guide, and direct online ordering links.
