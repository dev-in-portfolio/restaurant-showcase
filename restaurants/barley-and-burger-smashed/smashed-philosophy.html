The Art of the Flat-Top

The Smashed Philosophy

Why we smash: extreme heat, proprietary triple-cut beef, and intense Maillard reaction create the ultimate burger experience.

The Lacy Edge & Caramelized Crust

A true smashed burger isn't just flattened?it's engineered for maximum surface browning. When our custom ground beef hits our screaming 450°F cast-iron flat-top, we use heavy stainless steel presses to smash the patty ultra-thin in the first ten seconds.

This technique drives intense contact with the searing surface, accelerating the Maillard reaction to create an unmistakable crispy, caramelized lace around the perimeter while sealing in the savory interior juices.

Double patties ensure the perfect meat-to-cheese ratio, melting American or Swiss cheese into every nook and cranny before being tucked into a warm, butter-toasted golden brioche bun.

The Meat

Triple-Cut Custom Blend

We blend Certified Black Angus chuck for rich beef flavor, brisket for savory marbling, and short rib for buttery tenderness. Freshly ground daily, never frozen.

The Bun

Golden Toasted Brioche

Our sweet, pillowy brioche buns are custom-baked by artisan bakery partners, toasted with clarified butter to hold up to the juiciest double-patty stacks.

The Sauces

Scratch Craft Sauces

From our signature B&B Comeback Sauce to White Truffle Aioli, Ghost Pepper Chipotle, and A.1. Steakhouse reduction, every condiment is whipped in-house.
