## Original Audit Weakness

Nefelie’s serves as a cornerstone of Charlotte’s historic Brevard Court pedestrian alleyway, beloved for its authentic Greek family recipes, generous portions, and vibrant outdoor courtyard patio. However, the restaurant lacked a dedicated multi-page website showcasing its authentic Greek gyros, tavern smash burgers, late-night weekend hours, or game day patio seating guide. This 6-page concept provides an energetic, high-converting digital presence.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Authentic Greek Kitchen Specialties:** Shaved spiced lamb and beef gyros with cucumber garlic tzatziki on grilled pita, char-grilled chicken souvlaki skewers, golden baked spanakopita, and Greek village salads.
2. **American Tavern Comforts:** Double smash burgers with grilled onions, jumbo crispy wings in Greek herb and lemon pepper sauces, and Greek loaded feta fries.
3. **Brevard Court Cobblestone Patio:** Historic European-style brick alleyway seating, string lights, local craft draft beers, and pre-game patio energy for Charlotte Knights and Panthers games.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Aegean Blue & Greek Olive Gold Palette:** Aegean Blue (`#1e40af`), Santorini Cobalt (`#0f172a`), Greek Olive Gold (`#d97706`), and Mykonos White (`#f8fafc`).
2. **Mediterranean Tavern Architecture:** Gyro breakdown anatomy, souvlaki platter highlights, and Brevard Court game day itinerary.
3. **Bold Classical Typography:** Cinzel Mediterranean serif display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for game day kickoff timing.
