Uptown Charlotte Heritage

The Magic of Brevard Court

Connecting Latta Arcade and the city's favorite sports destinations.

The Historic Pedestrian Alley Est. 1914

Originally constructed as an open-air extension of the historic Latta Arcade cotton trading center, now a vibrant brick dining thoroughfare.

Romare Bearden Park Proximity Direct Access

Step directly out of the alleyway into the 5.4-acre urban park featuring city skyline views, waterfalls, and live concert series.

Draft Craft Beer Program Pints • Pitchers

Rotating local North Carolina IPAs, crisp lagers, Greek imported Mythos beer, and refreshing hard seltzers on tap.

Covered Weather Seating Heated in Winter

Equipped with large market umbrellas for summer shade and commercial patio heaters during crisp autumn football weekends.
