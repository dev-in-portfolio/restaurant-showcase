Bar Discipline

The 3 Pillars of Room Service Mixology

1. The Hotel Lobby Revival

Channeling the sophisticated, unhurried atmosphere of grand European and Manhattan hotel bars where every drink is served with intention.

2. Precision Crystal Ice

Carving directional crystal ice blocks into spears and rocks to ensure minimal dilution and optimal chilled spirit integration.

3. Scratch Bitters & Cordials

Simmering seasonal botanicals, whole citrus rinds, and whole spices in-house to craft customized bitters, shrubs, and aromatic oils.

Signature Recipe Spotlight

The Bellhop Old Fashioned

Our hallmark pour begins with bonded small-batch rye whiskey, elevated by in-house demerara syrup slow-simmered with Madagascar vanilla and charred cinnamon. Stored over custom hand-carved ice cubes, we express fresh orange citrus oils and crown each glass with a brandied Luxardo cherry.
