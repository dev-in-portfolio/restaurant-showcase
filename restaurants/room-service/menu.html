Hotel Cocktails Shaken & Stirred

The Bellhop Old Fashioned Signature Stir

Small-batch bonded rye whiskey, rich demerara syrup, Angostura and orange house bitters, expressed with flame-singed orange oil and Luxardo cherry.

Midnight Check-In Gin Fizz Botanical Silk

Artisanal dry gin, fresh pressed Meyer lemon juice, elderflower liqueur, club soda, and silky aquafaba foam dusted with edible violet petals.

Penthouse Espresso Martini After Dark

Madagascar vanilla bean infused vodka, freshly pulled Camp North End espresso, dark coffee liqueur, and grated Ecuadorian dark chocolate.

Velvet Keycard Spritz Aperitivo

Select Venetian bitter aperitivo, chilled dry prosecco, sparkling soda, and Castelvetrano green olive garnish served over hand-cut ice spears.

Sandwiches & Bites From the Kitchen

House Pastrami Reuben House Classic

Thick-cut grilled peppered pastrami, melted Gruyère cheese, tangy house-fermented sauerkraut, and Russian dressing pressed on artisan marbled rye.

Whipped Truffled Feta & Pita Shareable Dip

Smoked Turkey & Brie Panini Pressed Melt
