Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of Room Service

Unhurried Hospitality

Creating an elevated sanctuary where guests feel pampered like hotel VIPs while retaining the relaxed, welcoming warmth of a local bar.

Camp North End Integration

Thriving inside the historic Keswick District of Charlotte's premier creative campus, collaborating with neighboring artists, makers, and innovators.

Culinary & Spirit Craft

Treating both our cocktail shakers and sandwich press with the highest culinary discipline, balancing flavor, texture, and visual elegance.

Community & Culture

A Gathering Space For Charlotte

From vinyl listening sessions and community book trivia nights to relaxed afternoon co-working with iced lattes and hot pastrami melts, Room Service is designed as Camp North End's living room. Join us whenever you seek fine spirits and unpretentious comfort.
