The 3 Pillars of Room Service Bites

1. The Reuben Heritage

Layering seasoned peppered pastrami, Swiss and Gruyère cheeses, house kraut, and Russian dressing pressed on thick marbled rye bread.

2. Whipped Truffle Feta

Whipping rich Mediterranean sheep's milk feta with black truffle oil, wildflower honey, and pistachios served with warm za'atar pita.

3. Cocktail Complementarity

Balancing rich melted cheeses, cured meats, and savory dips against spirit-forward cocktails, citrus sours, and crisp natural wines.
