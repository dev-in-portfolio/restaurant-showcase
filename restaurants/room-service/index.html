The Boutique Hotel Philosophy

Hotel Lounge Living Without The Room Key

Conceived as an homage to vintage boutique hotel lobbies, Room Service brings together travelers, local creators, and evening wanderers in an intimate, dim-lit parlor. Settle into plush velvet banquettes beneath warm brass pendants, listen to analog vinyl selections, and enjoy unhurried hospitality in Charlotte’s most vibrant creative district.
