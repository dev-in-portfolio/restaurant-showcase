🏛 The Littl Madeleine Story

A Vision of Mindful Baking.

Littl Madeleine was born from pastry artist Sadie Noh's deep commitment to wholesome, organic craftsmanship. Starting in 2019 under Millbridge Cake, Sadie's pursuit of balanced, less-sweet French-Korean confections led to opening our boutique pastry salon at 11318 N Community House Rd in Ballantyne.

Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of Littl Madeleine

Purity of Ingredients

Refusing artificial flavorings, high-fructose corn syrups, or chemical food colorings in favor of 100% certified organic pantry staples.

Artisanal Small Batches

Baking limited quantities throughout the morning and afternoon so every guest receives warm, crisp-edged madeleines at their peak freshness.

Mindful Gifting

Packaging our confections in custom embossed gift boxes, transforming simple baked goods into memorable tokens of gratitude and love.
