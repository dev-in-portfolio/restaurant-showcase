🍵 Multi-Grain Roasting & Organic Espresso

Roasted Grains, Silky Cream.

To complement our delicate madeleines, our beverage program celebrates heritage Korean sips alongside organic espresso. Our signature Misugaru latte blends stone-ground roasted barley, black beans, and brown rice with raw honey for an earthy, naturally energizing morning elixir.

Beverage Craft

The 3 Pillars of Littl Madeleine Drinks

1. Stone-Ground Misugaru

Roasting and milling whole Korean grains including black soybeans, barley, and sesame into a fine powder rich in natural fiber and nutty aroma.

2. Fair-Trade Organic Coffee

Pulling sweet, clean espresso from certified organic coffee beans, paired with house-simmered whole Madagascar vanilla syrups.

3. Fluffy Japanese Shokupan Sandos

Crafting morning egg salad sandos on pillowy Japanese milk bread to provide the ultimate savory companion to your morning tea or latte.
