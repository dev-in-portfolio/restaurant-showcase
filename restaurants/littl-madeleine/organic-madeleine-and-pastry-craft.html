The Art of the Fluffy Shell

Baking the perfect French madeleine is an exercise in precision. Founded by pastry artisan Sadie Noh, Littl Madeleine embraces a Korean "less-sweet" philosophy. We use certified organic flour, rich cultured butter, organic cage-free eggs, and raw honey to create delicate cakes where nuanced tea and citrus flavors shine.

Baking Principles

The 3 Pillars of Littl Madeleine Baking

1. 100% Organic Sourcing

Using certified organic unbleached pastry flours, organic unrefined sugars, pasture-raised eggs, and farm-fresh dairy with zero artificial extracts.

2. Korean "Less-Sweet" Ethos

Restraining refined sugars to allow natural aromatics—like bergamot tea, roasted misugaru, Meyer lemon zest, and brown butter—to take center stage.

3. Traditional French Metal Molds

Baking in heavy French scalloped pans at precise high oven temperatures to ensure the iconic madeleine "hump" rises tall and crisp.
