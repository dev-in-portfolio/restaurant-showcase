Artisanal Craft Mixology

Cocktail mixology at Crisp is an elevated sensory experience. Our mixologists infuse small-batch bourbons with charred hickory wood smoke, express fresh citrus oils, and craft botanical syrups in-house daily.

Mixology Excellence

The 3 Pillars of Crisp Cocktail Craft

1. Real Wood Smoke Infusion

Our smoked old fashioneds are cloched and smoked live with real charred hickory & applewood shavings.

2. Small-Batch Bourbon Program

Curated selection of single-barrel North Carolina and Kentucky bourbons & straight rye whiskies.

3. House-Made Botanical Syrups

In-house syrups simmered with demarara sugar, lavender, elderflower, rosemary, & dark Luxardo cherries.
