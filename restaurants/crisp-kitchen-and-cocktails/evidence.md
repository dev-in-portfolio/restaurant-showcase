## Original Audit Weakness

Crisp Kitchen & Cocktails relies on basic ordering links (`crispclt.com`). Located at 132 Brevard Court in historic Brevard Court, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its craft mixology program, double-crisp Southern frying technique, late-night cocktail lounge vibe, and historic Brevard Court courtyard setting.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Brevard Court Location:** 132 Brevard Court, Charlotte NC 28202 (Historic Brevard Court in Uptown Charlotte near Romare Bearden Park).
2. **Signature Southern Fare:** Honey-butter glazed crispy hot chicken, fried green tomatoes with whipped pimento cheese, braised short ribs over stone-ground grits, and truffle parmesan frites.
3. **Craft Mixology Lounge:** Smoked old fashioneds, botanical gin spritzes, barrel-aged craft cocktails, and small-batch bourbon selections.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Sleek Modern Sans Typography:** Clean modern display sans (*DM Sans*) paired with refined body sans (*Outfit*) and mixology ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Midnight Emerald & Amber Copper Palette:** High-fashion nocturnal lounge palette anchored in midnight emerald (`#081C15`), deep botanical green (`#1B4332`), warm amber copper (`#F59E0B`), golden honey glow (`#FBBF24`), and soft silk ivory (`#FAF7F2`).
3. **"The Double-Crisp Southern Frying & Smoked Bourbon Craft Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`crispy-southern-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing double-crisp hot chicken alongside smoked bourbon cocktails and Southern small plates.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Courtyard Hooligans` — British soccer pub with Unbounded display fonts and Stadium Green/Gold.
2. `Costa del Sol` — Honduran coastal kitchen with Lora display fonts and Honduran Terracotta/Teal.
3. `Coquette` — Parisian haute couture brasserie with Playfair Display fonts and Parisian Burgundy.

### Divergent Choices for Crisp Kitchen & Cocktails

- **Hero Composition:** Modern Midnight Emerald Lounge & Craft Mixology Vault split-hero layout: left side features a luminous neon cocktail crest & copper stamp badge (`"CRAFT KITCHEN & COCKTAIL LOUNGE • BREVARD COURT UPTOWN"`), sleek modern sans display (*DM Sans*), midnight emerald & amber copper palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of honey-butter hot chicken, truffle frites, fried green tomatoes, and a smoking old fashioned cocktail.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Crisp Vault Cards** (`crisp-vault-card`) and **Brevard Lounge Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`crisp-header`, `lounge-brand`, `emerald-hero-stage`, `neon-stamp-badge`, `crisp-vault-card`, `crisp-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
