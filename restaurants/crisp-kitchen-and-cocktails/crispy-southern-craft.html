Southern Frying Science

The Art of Double-Crisp Frying

Achieving the ultimate shatter crunch requires precision. Our chicken undergoes a 24-hour spiced buttermilk soak, followed by a double-dredge flour coating and dual-stage temperature frying for golden perfection.

Frying Process

The 3 Pillars of Crisp Frying Craft

1. 24-Hour Spiced Buttermilk Soak

Whole fresh chicken is steeped in cultured buttermilk infused with cayenne, paprika, garlic, & black pepper.

2. Double-Dredge Flour Shell

Seasoned cornstarch & flour double-dredging creates micro-crags that seal in juice while frying ultra-crispy.

3. Habanero Honey-Butter Glaze

Brushed hot out of the fryer with raw wildflower honey melted into sweet cream butter & habanero pepper reduction.
