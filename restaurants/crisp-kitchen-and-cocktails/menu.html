Southern Kitchen Plates Mains & Bites

Honey-Butter Crispy Hot Chicken House Specialty

Double-dredged buttermilk fried chicken thigh & drumstick, drizzled with habanero wildflower honey-butter, served with dill pickles & house ranch.

Fried Green Tomatoes & Pimento Cheese Southern Classic

Cornmeal crusted green tomato slices stacked with house whipped sharp cheddar pimento cheese & sweet red pepper jam.

Braised Short Ribs & Grits Chef Feature

24-hour braised beef short rib served over creamy stone-ground Carolina white cheddar grits with natural jus glaze.

Truffle Parmesan Frites Shareable Side

Golden thin-cut skin-on fries tossed in black truffle oil, aged parmesan reggiano cheese, parsley, & garlic aioli dipping sauce.

Craft Mixology & Spirits Signature Drinks

Smoked Hickory Old Fashioned Signature Smoke

Small-batch bourbon, angostura & orange bitters, demarara syrup, served over clear sphere ice infused with real hickory wood smoke.

Empress Botanical Spritz Craft Gin

Black Cherry Bourbon Sour Lounge Favorite

Valrhona Espresso Martini Late-Night Drink
