The Spirit of Omakase

The Japanese phrase Omakase translates to “I leave it up to you.” At Kuya Omakase 2.0, our master chefs take you on an intimate flavor journey, pairing pristine cuts of seasonal seafood with house-brushed nikiri soy, grated fresh wasabi, and citrusy yuzu zest.

  • The Shari Foundation: Short-grain Koshihikari sushi rice seasoned with artisanal red and white vinegars.
  • Flame-Torched Aburi: Lightly seared with a blowtorch to render natural fish oils and create smoky sweetness.
  • Artful Garnishes: House-pickled ginger, toasted sesame, tobiko caviar, and microgreens.
