The Foundation

The Anatomy of Perfect Nigiri

At Kuya Omakase 2.0, nigiri is never simply raw fish over cold rice. It is a precise architectural harmony of texture, temperature, and seasoning:

  • The Shari (Sushi Rice): Koshihikari rice polished, cooked to exact firmness, and fanned while folding in seasoned vinegars so each grain stays distinct.
  • The Single Slice: Sliced across the grain in one continuous motion to preserve moisture and tenderness without tearing delicate muscle fibers.
  • The Balance of Wasabi: A measured dab of wasabi tucked between fish and rice to elevate natural umami without masking clean sweetness.

Sushi Etiquette

How to Savor Omakase Nigiri

To enjoy each piece at its absolute apex of flavor and texture, our chefs recommend:

  • Eat in One Bite: Nigiri is sized to be enjoyed whole so the fish, rice, and seasoning blend simultaneously.
  • Fingers or Chopsticks: In Japanese tradition, eating nigiri with clean fingers is celebrated, allowing you to feel the warmth of the shari.
  • Pre-Seasoned Perfection: Because our chefs brush each piece with customized nikiri soy, additional soy sauce dipping is unnecessary.
