## Original Audit Weakness

Kuya Omakase 2.0 is one of the centerpiece culinary concepts inside Monarch Market, delivering high-end sushi bar craftsmanship in an approachable, energetic Uptown environment. However, its previous online presence lacked a dedicated multi-page website explaining the sushi chef&#39;s knife craft, omakase progression, poke customization, and food hall bar seating options. This 6-page responsive web experience provides an elevated, high-converting digital portal.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Chef-Guided Omakase & Nigiri Craft:** Precision knife cuts of fresh tuna, salmon, yellowtail, unagi, and scallops paired with seasoned warm sushi rice (shari).
2. **Signature Specialty Rolls & Poke:** High-impact flavor combinations including the Black Mamba, Green Lantern, Musashi, and fresh poke bowls with house marinades.
3. **Monarch Market Luxury Food Hall:** Premier Uptown Charlotte location at One South Tryon featuring counter-height bar stools, cocktail bar synergy, and indoor-outdoor dining.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Midnight Indigo & Wasabi Green Palette:** Deep Midnight Indigo (`#0b132b`), Slate Navy (`#1c2541`), Wasabi Leaf Green (`#16a34a`), Salmon Roe Amber (`#ea580c`), Rice Paper Ivory (`#faf9f6`), and Clean Silver (`#e2e8f0`).
2. **Omakase Flight & Knife Technique Architecture:** Curated tasting sequence cards, fish provenance breakdowns, and step-by-step poke building guides.
3. **Contemporary Luxury Typography:** Cormorant Garamond serif display headers paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for precise pricing and fish descriptions.
