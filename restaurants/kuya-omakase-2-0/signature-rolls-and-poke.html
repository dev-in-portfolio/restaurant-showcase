Signature Rolls & Poke Creations

Bold fusion maki, crisp tempura accents, and vibrant Hawaiian-style poke bowls tossed with house shoyu and yuzu dressings.

Contemporary Flavors

The Craft of Specialty Maki

Our maki rolls combine traditional knife technique with modern textures and vibrant house-made sauces. Every roll is balanced with precision so the fresh seafood shines:

  • Black Mamba Roll: Spicy tuna core draped in savory BBQ unagi eel, ripe avocado, black tobiko, and unagi reduction.
  • Musashi Roll: Crunchy soft-shell crab tempura, spicy crab salad, and flame-seared salmon with scallion ginger oil.
  • Green Lantern Roll: Yellowtail, spicy serrano peppers, fresh cilantro, avocado sheet, and yuzu lime splash.
Fresh & Energizing

Vibrant Poke Bowls

For a wholesome, high-protein lunch or dinner on the go, our poke bowls feature sushi-grade fish cubed fresh to order and tossed in chef marinades:

  • Signature Ahi Poke: Diced bigeye tuna tossed in toasted sesame shoyu over sushi rice with avocado, seaweed salad, and spicy mayo.
  • Salmon Citrus Poke: Scottish salmon cubes with cucumber ribbons, pickled ginger, masago, and yuzu ponzu reduction.
  • Custom Combinations: Choose your protein, base of sushi rice or organic greens, and fresh toppings.
