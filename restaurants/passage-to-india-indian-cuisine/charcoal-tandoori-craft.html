🔥 800°F Intense Charcoal Heat

The Magic of the Clay Tandoor

The heart of North Indian cooking is the traditional clay tandoor. At Passage to India, we utilize natural hardwood charcoal that reaches temperatures upwards of 800°F. Long steel skewers suspended directly over glowing coals create a rapid sear that traps savory juices inside while imparting an unmistakable smoky aroma.

Tandoori Principles

The 3 Pillars of Passage to India Tandoor Roasting

1. 24-Hour Cultured Marinade

Submerging cuts in whole milk yogurt infused with freshly pounded ginger, garlic, Kashmiri chili, and toasted garam masala.

2. 800° Charcoal Radiant Heat

Roasting vertically over live hardwood charcoal, creating crisp blistered edges and deeply caramelized flavors.

3. Sizzling Cast-Iron Finish

Serving every order piping hot on a smoking cast-iron platter layered with sweet caramelized onions and fresh lemon wedges.
