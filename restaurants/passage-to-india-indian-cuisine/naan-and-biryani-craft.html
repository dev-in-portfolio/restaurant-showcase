The 3 Pillars of Passage to India Heritage Cooking

1. Wall-Slapped Clay Naan

Stretching leavened dough and slapping it against curved clay walls, puffing into light, chewy, garlic-brushed perfection in under 90 seconds.

2. Hyderabadi "Dum" Slow Simmer

Sealing aged basmati rice and marinated meats in heavy pots with dough to trap aromatic spice vapors until every grain absorbs deep flavor.

3. Scratch Samosa Folding

Hand-rolling delicate pastry cones filled with spiced Yukon potatoes, sweet peas, roasted cumin, and coriander before flash-crisping.
