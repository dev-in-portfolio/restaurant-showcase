## Original Audit Weakness

Chaat ’N’ Dosa relies on basic template pages (`chaatndosanc.com`) and Toast Tab online ordering links (`toasttab.com`). Located at 8624 Camfield Street in Ballantyne, Charlotte NC, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its authentic South Indian dosa griddle craftsmanship, crunchy street chaat spices, thali meal platters, and event catering.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Camfield Street Ballantyne Location:** 8624 Camfield Street, Suite C, Charlotte NC 28277 (Ballantyne / South Charlotte).
2. **South Indian Dosa Griddle Craft:** Paper thin crispy dosas (Mysore masala, Bengaluru benne, paneer butter masala), idli, vada, and uttapam.
3. **Indian Street Chaat & Thalis:** Samosa chaat, bhel puri, pani puri, pav bhaji, South Indian thali meals, and sweet mango lassi.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Royal Saffron & Spice Typography:** Regal display serif (*Playfair Display*) paired with clean modern sans (*Outfit*) and griddle technical mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Saffron & Mint Chutney Palette:** Vibrant South Indian palette anchored in deep saffron red (`#6B1214`), golden turmeric (`#E09F25`), tamarind amber (`#C75D18`), mint chutney green (`#2A6A4E`), and soft banana leaf cream (`#FAF7F0`).
3. **"The Golden Dosa Griddle & Crunchy Chaat Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`dosa-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing griddle-crisped dosas alongside crunchy savory chaat snacks.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Carmella’s Pizza Grill` — New York red-checkered pizzeria with Bitter/Plus Jakarta Sans fonts.
2. `Caribbean Hut` — Sunny Caribbean island kitchen with Outfit/Playfair Display fonts.
3. `Café Audire` — Mid-Century modern walnut analog vinyl lounge with Playfair/Outfit fonts.

### Divergent Choices for Chaat ’N’ Dosa

- **Hero Composition:** South Indian Saffron Spice & Golden Dosa Vault split-hero layout: left side features a turmeric curry stamp badge (`"SOUTH INDIAN VEGETARIAN STREET FOOD • BALLANTYNE"`), regal display typography (*Playfair Display*), deep saffron red & golden turmeric palette, and right side features a banana-leaf framed hero image of a rolled paper dosa with sambar and chutneys.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Thali Vault Cards** (`thali-vault-card`) and **South Indian Griddle Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`dosa-header`, `chaat-brand`, `griddle-hero-stage`, `saffron-stamp-badge`, `thali-vault-card`, `chaat-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
