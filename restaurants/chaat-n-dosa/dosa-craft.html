The Dosa Preparation Process

Overnight Fermentation

Stone-ground rice and black gram lentils fermented naturally for 24 hours to develop light crisp texture and tangy flavor.

High-Heat Flat Griddle

Poured and circular-swirled thinly on seasoned iron griddles with pure ghee or vegetable oil until golden brown.
