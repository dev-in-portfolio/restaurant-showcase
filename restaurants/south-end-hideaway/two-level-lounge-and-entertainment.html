Multi-Level Social Architecture

Two Levels Of Energy & Games.

South End Hideaway was conceived to give Charlotte a versatile, multi-dimensional playground. Spread across two distinct floors with two fully staffed bars, four tournament-grade slate pool tables, steel-tip dart throwing lanes, and elevated mezzanine booths, there is always room to relax, play, or dance.

Venue Architecture

The Anatomy of South End Hideaway

Two distinct atmospheres unified under one roof on Bland Street.

Level 01

Main Entertainment Floor

The ground floor serves as the pulsing heartbeat of the venue, featuring our primary 40-foot full bar, open dance floor, resident DJ booth, and high-top social tables.

Level 02

Mezzanine Lounge

Overlooking the main floor, the second-story mezzanine offers plush lounge seating, dedicated cocktail service, panoramic views, and semi-private group hangouts.

Recreation

Four Slate Pool Tables

Centrally located on the entertainment floor, four regulation slate billiard tables are open for casual racks and friendly competition from afternoon through late night.

Weekend Nights

Resident DJs & State-of-the-Art Acoustics

As darkness falls across South End, Hideaway transitions into a high-energy dance destination. Our rotating lineup of Charlotte's finest resident DJs spins curated sets spanning throwback hip-hop, deep house, and modern club anthems through an immersive surround-sound acoustic rig.
