Getting Here

Lynx Light Rail & Parking Directives

Arriving at South End Hideaway is effortless via transit or vehicle:

  • Lynx Blue Line (Bland Street Station): The light rail platform is located directly across the street (under 100 feet away), connecting you directly to Uptown, NoDa, and LoSo.
  • Neighborhood Parking: Designated on-street parking is available along Bland Street, with nearby paid parking decks on Camden Road and Tryon Street.
  • Rideshare Drop-Off: Curbside pickup and drop-off is recommended at the intersection of Bland Street and Tryon Street for quick access.
Private Gatherings

Mezzanine VIP Lounges & Venue Buyouts

Host your next birthday, corporate happy hour, or celebration at South End Hideaway:

  • Mezzanine Lounge Buyouts: Reserve our elevated second-floor lounge for private groups of 15 to 60 guests with dedicated bar service.
  • Full Two-Level Buyouts: Exclusive buyout access across both floors, including all four pool tables, two full bars, and DJ booth for up to 300 attendees.
  • Food Truck Catering Packages: Customized taco platters, sesame fries, and craft cocktail bars curated through our YUMBII partnership. Call (704) 910-6710 to plan.
