Culinary Collaboration • YUMBII Food Truck

Asian Flavors, Street Taco Soul.

Great nightlife demands great food. South End Hideaway proudly hosts an exclusive culinary residency with the celebrated YUMBII food truck, serving up boundary-pushing Asian-Mexican fusion street tacos, marinated ribeye bulgogi, slow-braised carnitas, and world-famous sesame fries late into the night on Bland Street.

Kitchen Philosophy

The Asian-Mexican Fusion Marriage

Where traditional taqueria techniques intersect with Korean marinade traditions.

Profile 01

Sesame Ribeye Bulgogi

Choice ribeye beef sliced razor thin and steeped in a garlic, soy, pear, and sesame marinade before flash-searing on high heat for caramelized perfection.

Profile 02

Citrus Braised Carnitas

Heritage pork shoulder braised for twelve hours in Mexican citrus, cumin, and Mexican oregano until pull-apart tender, topped with pickled onions and salsa verde.

Profile 03

Famous Sesame Fries

Double-fried crispy Idaho potatoes immediately tossed in pure toasted sesame oil and fine sea salt, served with our signature spicy sriracha dipping crema.

Pairing Disciplines

Balancing Spice With Craft Cocktails

The rich umami and spice of YUMBII's street tacos pair naturally with our bar program. From the crisp agave acidity of our Neon Velvet Margarita to the bittersweet caramel depth of our Smoked Old Fashioned, every cocktail is formulated to enhance each bite.
