Social Philosophy

The Hideaway Principles

How we approach nightlife and daytime socializing in South End.

Principle 01

Day-To-Night Versatility

We reject the notion that a bar must be only one thing. By afternoon we are a sunlit pool and beer hall; by night, an electrifying cocktail and DJ lounge.

Principle 02

Inclusive Gathering

No stiff velvet ropes or intimidating cover charges. Everyone who walks through our doors is treated to warm Southern hospitality and friendly service.

Principle 03

Local Craft Collaborations

From our food residency with YUMBII to featuring Charlotte-based DJs and regional North Carolina microbreweries, community partnership is in our DNA.

The Corridor Anchor

South End's Premier Destination

Situated directly along the rail trail corridor at 332 W Bland Street, South End Hideaway anchors one of Charlotte's most walkable and vibrant neighborhoods. Drop in after work, start your weekend bar crawl, or finish your night surrounded by good music and good people.
