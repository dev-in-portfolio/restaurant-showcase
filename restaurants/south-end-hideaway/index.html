South End Entertainment Corridor • Steps from Light Rail

Two Sprawling Levels. Zero Pretense.

Welcome to South End Hideaway, Charlotte’s dynamic two-level social club located at 332 W Bland Street. Featuring two expansive bars, four tournament pool tables, steel-tip darts, an exclusive culinary residency with the YUMBII food truck serving Asian-Mexican fusion tacos, and nightly rotating DJs, South End Hideaway is built for long nights and casual afternoon gatherings.
