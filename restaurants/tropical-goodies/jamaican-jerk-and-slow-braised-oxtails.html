Island Tradition • Uncompromising Spice

Scotch Bonnet Heat, Five-Hour Braise.

The heart of Jamaican cuisine beats in two legendary preparations: open-grate jerk and slow-braised oxtails. At Tropical Goodies, we never rush the clock or dilute the spices. We grind whole Jamaican pimento berries, blend fresh scotch bonnet chiles, and let cast-iron braising pots simmer until meat reaches melt-in-your-mouth tenderness.

The Two Giants

Secrets of Caribbean Kitchen Mastery

Time, seasoning, and heat working in total harmony.

Mastery 01

Authentic Wet Jerk Rub

We grind Jamaican allspice berries (pimento), fresh scotch bonnet peppers, scallions, ginger root, nutmeg, and thyme sprigs into a rich wet marinade that penetrates deep into the poultry.

Mastery 02

Five-Hour Oxtail Reduction

Selected beef oxtails are seasoned, browned, and simmered for five continuous hours with sweet onions and butter beans until natural gelatin melts into a thick, glossy brown gravy.

Mastery 03

Coconut Rice & Peas

We cook long grain rice directly in unsweetened rich coconut milk steeped with red kidney beans, crushed garlic, and fresh sprigs of island thyme, providing the ideal bed for rich stews.

The Classic Platter

Slow-Braised Oxtails with Butter Beans

True Jamaican oxtail is famous worldwide because it cannot be hurried. When simmered gently with large creamy butter beans, the meat absorbs the fragrance of pimento, thyme, and caramelized garlic while yielding a luxurious gravy that blankets every spoonful of rice & peas. It is comfort on the grandest scale.
