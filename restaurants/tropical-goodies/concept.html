Historic West End • Culinary Anchor

A West Charlotte Landmark Of Island Flavor.

Tropical Goodies was founded on a simple, enduring premise: to serve real, uncompromised Jamaican cooking alongside the soulful comfort of the American South. On Lasalle Street, our kitchen has become a beloved gathering point for families, workers, churchgoers, and travelers seeking authentic island spices, huge portions, and genuine Queen City warmth.

Enduring Values

The Values of Tropical Goodies

Authenticity, generous hospitality, and deep neighborhood pride.

Value 01

Authentic Island Heritage

We never compromise on the foundational ingredients of Jamaican cuisine—pimento berries, scotch bonnet chiles, fresh thyme, and coconut milk—cooking strictly from heritage family recipes.

Value 02

Soulful Generosity

Every plate leaving our kitchen is packed with care and abundance. We believe food should nourish the spirit, fill the stomach, and leave guests completely satisfied.

Value 03

West Charlotte Pride

For years, Tropical Goodies has proudly anchored the Lasalle Street corridor, supporting community milestones, family celebrations, and local events with warm island spirit.

The Experience

Fresh Food & Welcoming Service

Whether you're stopping by during a busy lunch break for a quick jerk chicken box or picking up weekend dinner trays of oxtails and macaroni for a Sunday family dinner, our staff greets every customer with genuine friendliness and prompt service.
