Effortless Arrival

On-Site Parking & Transit

Visiting Tropical Goodies on Lasalle Street is simple and direct:

  • Free Surface Parking: Dedicated customer parking spaces are available directly on-site in front of and alongside our building.
  • Highway Proximity: Located just minutes from I-77 (Exit 12 Lasalle St / Atando Ave) and Beatties Ford Road.
  • CATS Bus Transit: Conveniently accessible via CATS Route 7 and Route 22 bus lines along Lasalle St.
Call-Ahead Pickup

Fresh Caribbean Platters to Go

Take home generous Caribbean and soul food dinners with ease:

  • Call Ahead: Dial (704) 399-5036 to order your oxtails, jerk chicken, curry goat, or wing platters in advance.
  • Insulated Packaging: All hot gravies, plantains, and rice & peas are securely sealed to remain steaming hot until you arrive home.
  • Family Trays: Catering pans available for Sunday dinners and celebrations with 24 hours advance notice.
