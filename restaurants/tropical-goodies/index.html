House Signature

Slow-Braised Oxtails & Butter Beans

Our oxtails are the crown jewel of Tropical Goodies. Cut thick and marinated overnight in traditional Jamaican seasonings, they are braised slowly until the meat easily pulls away from the bone. Simmered with plump butter beans and draped in an unctuous, deeply savory reduction, it is the ultimate comfort meal over rice & peas.

Caribbean Heritage

Traditional Curry Goat & Warm Roti

Tender bone-in goat meat slow-simmered in vibrant Jamaican yellow curry powder, roasted garlic, fresh garden thyme, and diced potatoes. Served with warm, flaky layered roti flatbread or fragrant coconut rice, this classic represents deep roots in Caribbean celebratory cooking.
