Curries & Soul Sides
Jamaican Curry Goat, Warm Roti & Baked Mac
Kitchen Fusion

The Harmony of Island Spice & Southern Soul

Hand-kneaded dough, aromatic turmeric curries, and comforting homestyle sides.

Tradition 01

Jamaican Yellow Curry

We toast turmeric, coriander, fenugreek, and cumin in oil before braising bone-in goat meat with garlic and scotch bonnet peppers, producing an intensely fragrant, vibrant yellow curry.

Tradition 02

Hand-Stretched Roti

Our roti is folded and cooked hot on the griddle until blistered and tender, peeling apart in delicate, buttery layers that readily soak up thick curry sauces.

Tradition 03

Four-Cheese Baked Mac

A Southern staple crafted with heavy cream, melted sharp cheddar, and mozzarella, baked slowly until the top forms a golden-brown bubbling crust.

Island Refreshers

Fresh Jamaican Sorrel with Ginger

No Caribbean feast is complete without a tall glass of ice-cold Sorrel. Made from steeped deep-crimson hibiscus flowers boiled with fresh crushed ginger root, allspice berries, and pure cane sugar, it delivers an invigorating tart-sweet kick that cools down scotch bonnet heat.
