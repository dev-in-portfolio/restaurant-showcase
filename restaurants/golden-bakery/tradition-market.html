Established 1981 • 40+ Years of Service

Honoring Ancient Roots, Nourishing Charlotte.

Since 1981, Golden Bakery has stood as a welcoming cornerstone for Charlotte’s Middle Eastern and Mediterranean communities. What began as an artisan brick-oven bakery has grown into a beloved halal market and gathering place.

Heritage & Community

The Four Pillars of Golden Bakery

Rooted in authenticity, certified halal integrity, and generous Mediterranean hospitality.

1981 Pioneer

Charlotte Legacy

One of Charlotte’s earliest Middle Eastern establishments, introducing authentic stone-hearth pita and za’atar manakish to North Carolina over 40 years ago.

Aleppo Recipes

Levantine Craft

Preserving the centuries-old culinary secrets of Aleppo: stone-ground whole spices, unbleached flours, pure ghee, and artisanal fermentation methods.

Full Market

Halal Groceries

Our aisles feature certified halal meats, imported Lebanese olive oils, Mediterranean cheeses (Akkawi, Nabulsi, Halloumi), fragrant teas, and bulk grains.

Certified Halal & Alcohol-Free

A Pure Gathering Space for Families

We take deep pride in maintaining 100% certified halal standards across all meats and bakery items in our alcohol-free, family-friendly dining room and grocery market. Come for a quick warm lunch, stock your home pantry, or pick up fresh sweets for holiday gatherings.
