The Story Behind Ten58

Named in tribute to Thomas Davis's #10 jersey at the University of Georgia and #58 with the Carolina Panthers.

The Man Behind #58

Resilience, Community & Sports Excellence

Thomas Davis spent 14 unforgettable seasons with the Carolina Panthers, earning 3 Pro Bowl selections, First-Team All-Pro honors, and the prestigious 2014 Walter Payton NFL Man of the Year award for his relentless philanthropy.

Known across the NFL as one of the most resilient and beloved linebackers in football history, Thomas founded Ten58 Sports Bar & Lounge to give Charlotte sports fans a welcoming, high-energy venue that matches the intensity of the stadium.

Located right across from Truist Field and down the street from Bank of America Stadium, Ten58 is where fans, players, and Queen City community members unite over great food and memorable sports moments.
