# Evidence & Design Ledger: Ten58 Sports Bar & Lounge

## Creative Brief
- **Client / Business:** Ten58 Sports Bar & Lounge
- **Address:** 430 W 4th St, Charlotte, NC 28202 (Uptown Charlotte, directly across from Truist Field & Bank of America Stadium)
- **Phone:** (980) 207-1058
- **Email:** events@ten58clt.com
- **Operating Hours:** Monday - Thursday: 12:00 PM - 12:00 AM; Friday: 12:00 PM - 1:00 AM; Saturday: 11:30 AM - 1:00 AM; Sunday: 11:30 AM - 12:00 AM
- **Founder & Concept:** Founded by Carolina Panthers legend and Walter Payton NFL Man of the Year Thomas Davis (honoring his #10 at Georgia and #58 with the Panthers). An elevated, high-energy Uptown Charlotte sports lounge featuring 30+ 4K ultra-HD screens, stadium sound, VIP party booths, craft cocktails, local craft drafts, and an elevated scratch pub menu (Touchdown TD Wings, Sick 'Em Collard Green Dip, Carolina Crawfish Dip, Ten58 Stadium Nachos, Tackling Tacos, Comeback Lump Crab Cakes, Man of the Year Smash Burger).
- **Design Philosophy:** Carolina stadium luxury meets electric Uptown nightlife. Panther blue, deep charcoal stadium steel, championship metallic silver, and energetic touchdown gold accents. High-contrast typography, interactive Tailgate & Watch Party calculator, table booking inquiry system, and zero emojis.

## Claim Ledger
- **Thomas Davis Championship Heritage:** Curated by #58 himself, celebrating athletic dedication, community uplift, and ultimate game day hospitality.
- **Stadium-Proximity Sports Destination:** Situated right at 430 W 4th St across from Truist Field and steps from Bank of America Stadium for the premier pre-game and post-game watch experience.
- **Scratch-Kitchen Gameday Comforts:** Beyond standard bar food; featuring house-smoked wings in 6 signature glazes, slow-braised collard green dip, authentic Carolina crawfish dip, and custom double-patty smash burgers.
- **Tailgate Party Platters & VIP Booth Experiences:** Full service game day VIP booth reservations and half-pan/full-pan catering packages for corporate suites and tailgate gatherings.
