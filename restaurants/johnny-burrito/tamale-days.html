The Tamale Day Survival Guide

Tips for securing your tamales before the steamers run dry.

Arrive Early (11:00 AM – 12:15 PM) Recommended

Steamers open right at 11:00 AM. While we make hundreds of tamales every Tuesday and Friday, popular varieties often sell out before 1:00 PM.

Call Ahead For Large Office Orders (704) 371-4448

Ordering for the team? Call in your tamale count by 10:30 AM to ensure your order is set aside hot and ready for pickup.

Smothered Red Sauce Guarantee Included

Every tamale order comes drenched in warm house-simmered red enchilada gravy and a sprinkle of authentic cotija cheese.

Take-Home Half Dozen Packs $24.00

Take home a 6-pack of hot tamales with a pint of red chile gravy for dinner with family or friends.
