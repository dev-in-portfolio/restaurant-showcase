How It Works

The Anatomy of Being a Rookie

What to expect when you visit Johnny Burrito for the very first time.

Step 1 • Speak Up

Call Out Your Rookie Status

As you reach the front of the line, proudly declare “I’m a rookie!” to Johnny or the line crew. You’ll be met with immediate cheers and personalized guidance.

Step 2 • The Keepsake

Receive Your Rookie Card

You’ll be handed an official Johnny Burrito Rookie Card—a souvenir badge of honor kept in wallets and on office bulletin boards across Uptown.

Step 3 • Custom Build

Master The Assembly Line

Our team will walk you through your choice of 13-inch tortillas, beans, grilled meats, and scratch roasted salsas, ensuring your first burrito is unforgettable.
