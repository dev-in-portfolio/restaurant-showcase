# Evidence & Verification Ledger: Johnny Burrito

## Creative Brief
- Business Name: Johnny Burrito
- Location: 301 S Tryon St, Concourse Level (Under Two Wells Fargo Atrium, down escalators to the right), Charlotte, NC 28202
- Phone: (704) 371-4448 | Fax Orders: (704) 371-4449
- Operational Hours: Monday - Friday: 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM; Saturday - Sunday: Closed

Famous for customizable "walk the line" jumbo burritos, tortilla bowl nacho salads, scratch-made tamales on Tuesdays and Fridays (steamed in real corn husks, selling out fast), vibrant five-tier house salsa bar (Pico de Gallo up to El Diablo), creamy queso, hand-mashed guacamole, cash discount ("CASH-EESH"), and frozen fruit slushies.

- Visual Aesthetic: Vibrant California-Baja street taqueria palette (warm terracotta `#c2410c`, sunny avocado lime `#65a30d`, fire-roasted habanero `#dc2626`, maize gold `#f59e0b`, deep charcoal slate `#1e293b`, and warm parchment `#fefce8`), bold display typography (`Paytone One` + `Plus Jakarta Sans`), high-energy badges, and interactive line-customization tools.
