The Johnny Assembly Line Process

How we build your custom burrito in under 45 seconds.

Step 1: Choose Your Tortilla & Rice Included

Select Jalapeño Cheddar, Spinach, Chipotle, or Flour. We steam it hot and add a generous scoop of cilantro-lime long-grain rice.

Step 2: Slow-Cooked Beans & Meats Included

Choose seasoned Black Beans or Pinto Beans, followed by Grilled Flank Steak, Citrus Chicken, or Pork Carnitas.

Step 3: Fresh Cheese, Salsas & Guacamole Included

Shredded Monterey jack cheese, sour cream, fresh pico de gallo, roasted corn salsa, and fresh hand-mashed guacamole.

Step 4: The Master Foil Wrap Heavyweight

Tightly rolled and wrapped in heavy aluminum foil to keep it steaming hot and easy to eat at your desk or plaza table.
