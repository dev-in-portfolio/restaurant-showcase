The 3 Pillars of Panda's Den Starters

1. Hand-Pinched Dumplings

Hand-wrapping each dumpling with seasoned pork, fresh minced ginger, and scallions before steaming tender or pan-frying crisp.

2. Real Crab & Cream Cheese

Stuffing crispy wonton skins with real crabmeat, scallions, and sweet cream cheese, fried golden for the perfect sweet-savory crunch.

Accompanying every starter with our signature seasoned ginger soy, house sweet & sour, and fiery hot Chinese mustard.
