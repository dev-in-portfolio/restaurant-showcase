Classic 8-Ball & Timeless Soundtracks

Chalked Cues & Analog Grooves.

A real dive bar needs two things above all else: a table that rolls true and music that sets the soul at ease. Since 1990, the backroom pool area at The Sanctuary Pub has served as NoDa's community rec room, where neighbors chalk cues, call their shots, and play along to an eclectic jukebox soundtrack until 2:00 AM.

Game Room Traditions

The Code of The Sanctuary Pool Room

Honor, camaraderie, and friendly competition around our regulation tables.

Rule 01

Chalkboard Sign-Up

Put your name on the board to call the next game. Winner stays on the table; loser shakes hands and puts their name back up. Simple, democratic, and honest.

Rule 02

Meticulous Table Care

Our slate beds are balanced regularly, re-felted with durable tournament cloth, and vacuumed daily so every bank and cut roll straight without wobbles.

Rule 03

The Jukebox Soundtrack

Our vintage music box is packed with classic punk, Motown soul, 70s rock, delta blues, and local North Carolina indie releases that keep the room vibrating.

Sunday Tournaments

Neighborhood 8-Ball & Cold Pitchers

On Sunday afternoons, our backroom transforms into a friendly local 8-ball showdown. Neighborhood residents, hospitality industry workers, and seasoned sharpshooters compete for modest cash pots, free beer pitchers, and local bragging rights. All skill levels are warmly embraced.
