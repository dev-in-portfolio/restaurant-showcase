NoDa’s Original Neighborhood Haven.

Welcome to The Sanctuary Pub, Charlotte’s quintessential neighborhood dive bar at 507 East 36th Street. Established in 1990 in the heart of NoDa, we are a welcoming haven for artists, musicians, night owls, and loyal regulars. Regulation pool tables, retro jukebox grooves, ice-cold pints, bourbon shot specials, and fresh tavern pizza—served without pretense every single night until 2:00 AM.
