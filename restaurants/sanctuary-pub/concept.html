Established 1990 • Thirty-Six Years of NoDa

A Living Room For The Neighborhood.

Before the high-rises and trendy bistros arrived, North Davidson Street was a collection of retired textile mills and bohemian artist lofts. In 1990, The Sanctuary opened its doors at 507 East 36th Street to give that emerging creative community an unpretentious refuge. More than three decades later, our mission has not wavered: provide an honest, affordable sanctuary for everyone.

The Sanctuary Creed

Three Truths That Define Our Bar

Why The Sanctuary remains Charlotte’s most enduring dive.

Truth 01

All Are Welcomed

Whether you arrive in paint-splattered overalls, construction gear, a business suit, or gym shorts, everyone gets the same respectful, welcoming dive bar greeting.

Truth 02

Steadfast Simplicity

We never chase fleeting nightlife fads or try to be an upscale gastropub. We take pride in being a real dive bar: cold beer, honest pours, pool tables, and loud jukeboxes.

Truth 03

365-Day Reliability

When other venues close for holidays or quiet weekdays, The Sanctuary remains lit and open until 2:00 AM every single night of the calendar year.

Community Anchor

The Front Porch of East 36th Street

Our street-facing outdoor patio provides the quintessential NoDa people-watching perch. On balmy Carolina evenings, grab a cold cider or pint and watch artists, musicians, and dog walkers stroll past the beating heart of Charlotte's premier creative district.
