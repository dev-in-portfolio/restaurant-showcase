Getting Here

Light Rail, Parking & Transit

Arriving at 507 East 36th Street is straightforward whether by foot, rail, or car:

  • Lynx Blue Line (36th Street Station): The light rail platform is located just two blocks east, offering a simple, safe 3-minute walk directly to our doors.
  • Neighborhood Street Parking: Plentiful free and metered street parking is available along East 36th Street and surrounding residential avenues.
  • NoDa Public Parking Lots: Multiple public surface lots and garages are situated within a two-block radius on North Davidson.
Gatherings & Parties

Patio Celebrations & Pool Nights

Looking to celebrate a birthday, team achievement, or casual neighborhood gathering?

  • Dog-Friendly Outdoor Patio: Our covered street-side patio welcomes well-behaved leashed dogs and their human companions for open-air drinks.
  • Large Group Hangouts: For parties of 10 or more, feel free to give us a heads up at (704) 333-0346 so our team can have tavern pizzas and pitchers ready.
  • No Rental Fees: We do not charge stuffy room fees—just bring your crew, order your drinks at the bar, and enjoy the night.
