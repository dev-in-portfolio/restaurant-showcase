# China Bowl Charlotte - Evidence & Research Ledger

## Creative Brief
China Bowl is a cherished neighborhood Chinese-American kitchen and wok house located at 3101 The Plaza in Charlotte, NC. Known for its intense high-flame wok cooking ("wok hei"), generous combination platters, and crispy appetizers, China Bowl serves signature General Tso's chicken, wok-charred house special fried rice, sesame crispy beef, golden crab rangoon, and Singapore curry mei fun. The design aesthetic features rich vermilion dragon red, warm imperial gold, lustrous dark charcoal, crisp cream backgrounds, and interactive family feast calculators.
