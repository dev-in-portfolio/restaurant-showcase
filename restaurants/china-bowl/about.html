About China Bowl

Our Promise

High-Heat Wok Hei & Honest Quality

Located at 3101 The Plaza in East Charlotte, China Bowl has been a culinary staple for neighbors seeking authentic wok-tossed favorites made with care and speed.

We believe the secret to unforgettable Chinese takeout lies in three fundamentals: roaring high-flame wok cooking that seals in flavor, fresh crisp produce, and handcrafted sauces made in-house every morning.
