Wood-Fired Pizza & Smash Burgers

Great craft beer demands extraordinary food. Our open kitchen features 72-hour sourdough pizzas baked at 800°F, flat-top double smash burgers, and hickory smoked wings designed to pair with our 40+ craft tap lines.

Kitchen Craft

The 3 Pillars of Distro Kitchen Excellence

1. 72-Hour Sourdough Pizza Crust

Long-fermented sourdough dough baked in an 800°F stone oven for a charred, bubbly, light crust.

2. Flat-Top Crispy Smash Burgers

Beef patties smashed paper-thin on a white-hot griddle for maximum caramelization & crispy edges.

3. Hickory Smoked Wings & Sauces

Low-smoked chicken wings flash-fried crispy & tossed in scratch spicy IPA garlic buffalo sauce.
