The 4-Brewery Collective Taproom

Distro Beer Hub unites four of North Carolina's most celebrated craft breweries under one roof at 2412 Distribution St. Sample rotating IPAs, pastry stouts, wild sours, and crisp lagers from Heist, Divine Barrel, DSSOLVR, & Incendiary.

Brewery Collective

Our 4 North Carolina Craft Brewery Partners

1. Heist Brewery (Charlotte NC)

Renowned for juicy haze IPAs, fruited sours, & barrel-aged imperial stouts poured fresh from NoDa & Camp North End.

2. Divine Barrel Brewing (Charlotte NC)

NoDa neighborhood favorites specializing in crisp Czech lagers, West Coast IPAs, & rich pastry stouts.

3. DSSOLVR (Asheville NC)

Surreal craft beers from Western NC featuring mixed-fermentation sours, wild ales, & unrefined hop bombs.

4. Incendiary Brewing (Winston-Salem NC)

Bold Triad craft pioneer known for intense double IPAs, fruited sours, & innovative small-batch fermentations.
