Southern Meats & Scratch Sides

Southern Entrees Steam Table Meats

Crispy Southern Fried Chicken #1 Diner Classic

Fresh hand-breaded bone-in chicken fried golden brown to order, offering extra-crispy seasoned skin and succulent juicy meat. Served with your choice of two or three sides.

Country Style Steak in Onion Gravy Fork-Tender Beef

Braised tender cubed beef steak slow-simmered in rich homemade brown gravy loaded with sweet caramelized onions. Perfect over rice or mashed potatoes.

Smothered Center-Cut Pork Chops Southern Staple

Two juicy seasoned pork chops pan-fried and smothered in savory pan gravy with sweet bell peppers and onions.

Crispy Fried Flounder Fillets Seafood Special

Tender white flounder dredged in yellow cornmeal spice batter, fried golden and served with scratch tartar sauce and lemon wedges.

Scratch Sides & Breakfast Vegetables & Breads

Four-Cheese Baked Mac & Cheese Scratch Side

Elbow macaroni folded in sharp cheddar, monterey jack, and heavy cream custard, baked until bubbling with a golden brown crust.

Slow-Simmered Collard Greens Seasoned Greens

Fresh hand-washed collard greens braised low and slow with smoked turkey aromatics, apple cider vinegar, and pepper flakes.

Sweet Potato Casserole Souffle Sweet Side

Mashed local sweet potatoes whipped with brown sugar, butter, and cinnamon, finished with a crunchy toasted pecan topping.

Country Sausage Egg & Cheese Biscuit Sunrise Breakfast

Hot jumbo buttermilk biscuit split and stacked with savory pork sausage patty, folded farm egg, and melted American cheese.
