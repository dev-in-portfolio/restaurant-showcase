🍲 Southern Steam Table Tradition

The Art of the Meat & Three

The Southern meat-and-three is more than a meal—it is a cornerstone of Carolinas culinary culture. At Niki's Food Shop, our hot steam table is filled fresh every morning with comforting meats, simmering gravies, and farm-fresh Southern vegetables prepared from scratch.

Culinary Tradition

The 3 Pillars of Niki's Steam Table

1. Scratch-Simmered Gravies

Rich brown onion gravies and silky chicken drippings simmered slowly on the stovetop to coat tender steaks and fluffy rice.

2. Hand-Cut Southern Vegetables

Fresh collard greens, green beans, cabbage, and candied yams seasoned with traditional spices, smoked meats, and love.

3. Daily Golden Breads

Every platter comes complete with your choice of warm buttery cornbread squares or fresh yeast rolls to mop up every drop of gravy.
