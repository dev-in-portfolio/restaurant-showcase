🌮 Peruvian Sánguche Heritage

The Art of Peruvian Sánguches

In Lima, a sanguchería is more than a restaurant—it's a neighborhood meeting post. At La Lima Sangucheria, we slow-braise pork belly in aromatic stock, flash-fry it for golden crackling skin, & layer it on fresh crusty bread with fried sweet potato & lime-soaked red onions.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Peruvian Sánguche Craft

1. Double-Cooked Chicharrón

Pork belly is simmered in seasoned broth until tender, then fried in its own rendering for crisp skin.

2. Lime Salsa Criolla

Thinly sliced red onions soaked in ice water, tossed with key lime juice, fresh cilantro, & rocoto peppers.

3. Artisanal Crusty Bread

French-style pan francés rolls baked fresh with a thin crackling crust & pillowy soft crumb.
