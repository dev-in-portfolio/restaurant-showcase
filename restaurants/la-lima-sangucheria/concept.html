Peruvian Heritage & Pride

La Lima Sangucheria was born out of love for the bustling sangucherías of Lima, Peru. Located at 131 E Park Ave in South End Charlotte, we bring authentic Peruvian street food culture to Queen City with bold flavors, warm hospitality, & scratch-made sauces.
