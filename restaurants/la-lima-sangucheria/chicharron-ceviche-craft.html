🥤 Fresh Lime Ceviche & Yucca

The Art of Lime Ceviche

Peruvian ceviche is world-renowned for its intense citrus brightness. At La Lima Sangucheria, fresh ocean fish isflash-cured in pure key lime juice, tossed with red onions & rocoto pepper, and served alongside sweet potato & giant choclo corn kernels.

Kitchen Craft

The 3 Pillars of Ceviche & Side Artistry

1. Leche de Tigre Marinade

Fresh lime juice blended with fish fumet, garlic, ginger, cilantro, & spicy Aji Limo peppers.

2. Giant Peruvian Choclo Corn

Oversized Peruvian corn kernels boiled in anise water for a sweet, nutty contrast to tart lime ceviche.

3. Creamy Aji Huancaina Sauce

Golden sauce made from queso fresco, yellow Aji Amarillo peppers, & evaporated milk served over crispy yucca.
