Directions to Suite 30

How To Find Halfpenny’s Cafe

  • From S. Tryon Street: Enter Two Wells Fargo Center at 301 S Tryon St and take the central glass escalators or elevators down to the lower plaza level.
  • From Overstreet Mall: Walk across the indoor skywalk into Two Wells Fargo and follow the atrium signage down to Suite 30.
  • Office Catering Drops: We deliver breakfast biscuit boxes, hot coffee urns, and deli lunch platters directly to office floors throughout Uptown towers.
