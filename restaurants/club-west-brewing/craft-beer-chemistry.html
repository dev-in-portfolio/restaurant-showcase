The Science of Craft Fermentation

Brewing Chemistry

The 3 Pillars of Club West Brewing

1. Double Dry-Hopping

Fresh Mosaic and Citra hop pellets are added directly to the fermenters cold to extract aromatic essential oils without added bitterness.

2. Water Chemistry Tuning

We adjust local Charlotte water profiles with calcium sulfate and chloride to match Pacific Northwest and Bavarian mineral levels.

3. Cold Conditioning

Lagers and ciders are cold-conditioned for up to 6 weeks to ensure crisp carbonation and clean flavor separation.
