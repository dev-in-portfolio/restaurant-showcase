Craft Beer Tap List & Taproom Eats

Freshly poured West Coast IPAs, fruit ciders, crisp lagers, smash burgers, and warm pretzel bites in South End.

Draft Beer & Cider Fresh From Fermenter

Flagship West Coast IPA 7.2% ABV

Bold West Coast India Pale Ale dry-hopped with Mosaic & Citra for bright grapefruit, resinous pine, and clean bitter finish.

Peach Yuzu Hard Cider 5.6% ABV

Crisp hard apple cider infused with sweet North Carolina peaches and vibrant, tart Japanese yuzu juice.

Citrus Valencia Wheat Ale 5.2% ABV

Unfiltered wheat ale brewed with Valencia orange peel, coriander, and tangerine puree for smooth sunshine flavor.

Blood Orange Lime Mexican Lager 4.8% ABV

Ultra-crisp light Mexican-style lager conditioned with fresh blood orange juice and key lime zest.

Club West Light Lager 4.2% ABV

Easy-drinking American light lager brewed with 2-row Pilsner malt for clean refreshment on warm patio afternoons.

Taproom Eats & Cocktails Scratch Comfort

Warm Pretzel Bites & Beer Cheese House Specialty

Soft salted Bavarian pretzel bites served warm with house IPA sharp cheddar cheese dip & brown mustard.

South End Double Smash Burger Craft Burger

Two seared Angus beef smash patties with melted American cheese, caramelized onions, pickles, & taproom sauce on brioche.

Loaded Brewery Garlic Fries Shareable

Crispy fries tossed in garlic herb butter, cotija cheese, scallions, and served with house smoked chipotle aioli.

Hawkins Street Mule (Cocktail) Craft Cocktail

Local vodka, spicy ginger beer, fresh lime juice, and a splash of peach yuzu cider served in copper mug.
