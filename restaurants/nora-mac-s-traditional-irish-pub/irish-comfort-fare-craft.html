The Art of Irish Comforts

True Irish public house cuisine is grounded in slow braises, rich gravies, and comforting textures. At Nora Mac's, our recipes follow time-honored Dublin traditions: slow-cooking ground lamb in stout reductions, hand-piping buttery colcannon potatoes, and frying fresh Atlantic cod to crisp golden perfection.

Culinary Principles

The 3 Pillars of Nora Mac's Kitchen

1. Stout-Braised Savory Gravies

Simmering premium meats with fresh herbs, root vegetables, and draught Guinness to build deep, savory gravies that warm the soul.

2. Harp Lager Crisp Batters

Whisking ice-cold Irish Harp lager into light flour batters for wild Atlantic cod, creating a delicate, shattering golden crust.

3. Butter-Rich Colcannon & Boxty

Mashing Yukon Gold potatoes with braised cabbage, Kerrygold butter, and cream, and griddling traditional Irish potato boxty cakes fresh.
