🍺 The 119.5-Second Dublin Pour

The Perfect Imperial Pint

Pouring an authentic pint of Guinness is an act of patient craftsmanship. At Nora Mac's, our draught lines are maintained to Dublin brewery specifications—poured at 45 degrees, rested for the signature nitrogen cascade, and topped off to form a velvety, dome-crested head in a full 20oz Imperial glass.

The 3 Pillars of Celtic Beverage Service

1. The Two-Stage Nitrogen Pour

Filling to 75% at a 45-degree angle, resting 119.5 seconds to settle the cascade, then pushing the tap forward for a proud creamy dome.

2. True 20oz Imperial Glassware

Refusing smaller American 16oz pints—serving every stout, ale, and cider in authentic British & Irish 20oz Imperial glassware.

3. Rare Pot Still Irish Whiskeys

Curating a world-class collection of triple-distilled Irish whiskeys, peated malts, and single pot still expressions from Cork to Dublin.
