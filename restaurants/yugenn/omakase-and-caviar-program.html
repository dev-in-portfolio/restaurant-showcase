The Pillars of Our Tasting Menu

Sustainable Luxury

Polanco Oscietra Caviar

Sustainably harvested in the Rio Negro of Uruguay. Our Grand Reserve sturgeon pearls are buttery, nutty, and velvety, accompanied by potato pavé and house chips ($125).

Sensory Indulgence

The Signature Uni Spoon

Hokkaido sea urchin, bluefin otoro, cured salmon ikura, quail egg yolk, truffle ponzu, and negi ($20). An explosion of ocean salinity, rich umami, and luscious texture in a single bite.

French-Japanese Union

Potato Foie Ve & Wagyu

Hudson Valley seared foie gras with potato pavé ($38) and Miyazaki A5 beef tartare on dashi butter toast with caviar reflect our kitchen’s seamless dialogue between classical French technique and Japanese ethos.

The Chef’s Omakase Counter

Seated directly before Chef Boi and Chef Chris, guests at the intimate omakase counter witness the precision of Japanese yanagiba knives, flame-kissed aburi searing, and bespoke sake pairings. With limited seatings available each evening, reservations are released in monthly allotments on Tock.
