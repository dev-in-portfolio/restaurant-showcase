Seating & Attire

Reservations: Main dining tables and Omakase Counter booked via Tock. Walk-ins welcomed for cocktails and sushi in our lounge bar. Smart casual to elevated evening attire requested.

Book Your Evening

Reservations via Tock

To ensure a seamless dining experience, we strongly recommend reserving your table or counter seat ahead of time:

  • Dining Room Tables: Reserve tables for parties of two to eight through the official YUGENN Tock reservation portal.
  • Omakase Counter: Experience our multi-course chef tasting menu seated directly in front of Chef Boi and Chef Chris. Released monthly.
  • Lounge Walk-Ins: Our cocktail bar and lounge offer first-come, first-served seating for drinks and small plates.
Celebrations & Corporate

Private Events & Lounge Buyouts

From private corporate dinners to celebratory birthday gatherings and full restaurant buyouts:

  • Custom Tasting Menus: Tailored omakase progressions and passed specialty maki for exclusive receptions.
  • Dedicated Beverage Pairings: Curated flights of rare Junmai Daiginjo sakes and signature craft cocktails.
  • Inquiries: Contact our events coordinator by emailing info@yugenn.co or calling (980) 209-0811.
