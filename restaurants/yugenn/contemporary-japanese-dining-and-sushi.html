Modern Maki • Aburi Nigiri • Robata Hot Plates

Culinary Theatre, Bold Artistry.

YUGENN redefines modern Japanese dining in Charlotte. Blending centuries-old Edomae cutting traditions with avant-garde culinary creativity, our kitchen serves 24-karat gold leaf maki, butter-poached lobster rolls, and wok-seared lobster fried rice.

Modern Craft

The Signatures of YUGENN Sushi

Layered complexity, balanced acidity, and stunning presentation.

Luxe Composition

Gold Leaf & Lobster Maki

From our Kin No Hamachi ($22) with snow crab and 24k gold leaf to the Homard Supreme ($26) with butter-poached lobster claws and volcanic black salt, each roll is a curated visual and sensory masterpiece.

Torch & Glaze

Flame-Kissed Aburi

Our aburi nigiri features high-fat cuts like Ora King salmon and hamachi belly lightly kissed with open torch flame to render natural oils, finished with lime zest, nikiri glaze, and chili garlic crisp.

Hot Plates

Lobster Fried Rice & Collars

Beyond sushi, our hot kitchen excels: wok-charred Lobster Fried Rice ($26) with dashi butter and puffed forbidden rice, and slow-grilled Hamachi Kama ($13) with crispy skin and juicy rich meat.

South End Nightlife

Dining Meets Sensory Nightlife

YUGENN bridges elevated fine dining with late-night South End sophistication. Enjoy curated vinyl and downtempo soundtracks, plush banquette booths, and inventive Japanese craft cocktails like the Roku Gin Vice & Vine ($22) until midnight on weekends.
