Sensory Precision, Japanese Soul.

YUGENN brings an elevated energy to Charlotte’s South End, where contemporary Japanese dining meets transcendent culinary craft. Helmed by Executive Chef Boi Phomsopha and Executive Sous Chef Christopher Nguyen, YUGENN pairs pristine Toyosu market sashimi imports with Polanco Oscietra caviar service, Miyazaki A5 Wagyu, and rare Junmai Daiginjo sakes.
