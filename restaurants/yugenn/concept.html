The Philosophy of Yūgen (幽玄) • South End

Profound Grace, Mysterious Beauty.

In Japanese aesthetics, Yūgen describes a profound, mysterious sense of the beauty of the universe—grace too deep for words. At YUGENN, this philosophy guides every detail, from the selection of sashimi-grade bluefin to the curated design, ambient lighting, and bespoke hospitality.

Culinary Leadership

The Chefs Behind the Counter

Over two decades of collective Japanese knife craft, discipline, and vision.

Executive Chef & Partner

Chef Boi Phomsopha

Born in New Orleans and raised in Charlotte from age four, Chef Boi trained under Chef Khoa Nguyen at Chopstix before advancing to lead fine-dining sushi operations. His dishes weave Japanese precision with vibrant Southeast Asian herbs and aromatics.

Executive Sous Chef

Chef Christopher Nguyen

Trained at Charlotte’s acclaimed O-Ku and refined in omakase under Chef Eiji at Hestia and Chef Kap at Miku Asian Kitchen. Nguyen brings deep reverence for traditional Edomae techniques while pushing culinary boundaries.

The Experience

Curated Hospitality

From our custom sake display case to our mother-of-pearl caviar spoons, YUGENN is designed to offer a refined, sensory atmosphere where culinary excellence and late-night South End energy coexist.

South End Dining

An Unrivaled Atmosphere

Step inside our second-floor lounge at 255 W Bland Street to find warm champagne gold finishes, dark textured walls, ambient lighting, and panoramic views of South End. Whether celebrating a milestone with omakase or sipping rare Junmai Daiginjo sakes at the bar, YUGENN leaves an indelible impression.
