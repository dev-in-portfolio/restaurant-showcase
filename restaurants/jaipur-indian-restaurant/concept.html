Charlotte Heritage & Royalty

Established in 1999, Jaipur Indian Restaurant at 5909 South Blvd is one of Charlotte's longest-standing culinary institutions. Inspired by the regal Pink City of Rajasthan, we bring centuries-old royal Mughlai & North Indian cooking traditions to South Charlotte.
