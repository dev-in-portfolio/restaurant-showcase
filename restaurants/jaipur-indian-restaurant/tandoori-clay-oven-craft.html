🍲 800°F Charcoal Clay Tandoor

The Art of Tandoori Roasting

Tandoori cooking relies on intense radiant heat from an 800°F clay oven fired with natural hardwood charcoal. At Jaipur Indian Restaurant, we hand-marinate chicken & lamb in yogurt & aromatic spices, searing juices instantly for a smoky, tender flavor impossible to replicate in ordinary ovens.

Tandoori Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Clay Oven Roasting

1. 24-Hour Yogurt & Spice Cure

Meats are tenderized in cultured yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, Kashmiri chili, & lemon juice to lock in moisture.

2. 800°F Radiant Charcoal Sear

Lowered into the cylindrical clay tandoor on long steel skewers, searing meat exteriors while keeping centers juicy.

3. Clay Wall Naan Slapping

Leavened dough rounds are slapped against red-hot inner clay walls, rising into blistered naan within 60 seconds.
