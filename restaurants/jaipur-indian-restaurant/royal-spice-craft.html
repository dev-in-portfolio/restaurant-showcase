The Alchemy of Indian Spices

Great Indian curry relies on the harmonious blooming of whole spices. At Jaipur Indian Restaurant, we roast whole cardamom pods, cinnamon bark, cloves, & cumin seeds in heavy copper pans before stone-grinding our secret house garam masala.

Spice Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Royal Curry Alchemy

1. Whole Spice Pan Roasting

Whole spices are gently toasted to release essential oils before being stone-ground into custom masala powders.

2. Slow-Simmered Gravy Reduction

Onion, tomato, ginger, & garlic gravies are simmered for hours until fat separates, creating velvety sauce texture.

3. Customized Heat Balancing

Every curry can be tailored to your precise spice preference—Mild, Medium, Hot, or Authentic Indian Heat.
