## Original Audit Weakness

Jaipur Indian Restaurant relies on basic web hosting directory portals. Located at 5909 South Blvd, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its 25+ year Charlotte legacy, 800°F clay oven tandoori art, royal spice blends, and custom wedding & corporate Indian catering.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South Charlotte Location:** 5909 South Blvd, Charlotte NC 28217 (South Charlotte near Tyvola Rd).
2. **North Indian Tandoori & Curry Specialties:** 800°F Clay Oven Tandoori Chicken, Lamb Seekh Kebabs, Velvet Chicken Tikka Masala, Lamb Rogan Josh, Paneer Butter Masala, Saffron Basmati Biryani, Garlic Naan, & Mango Lassi.
3. **Established Legacy:** 1999 (Charlotte's iconic North Indian landmark).
4. **Operating Hours:**
   - Lunch: Mon–Fri 11:00 AM – 2:30 PM | Sat–Sun 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM
   - Dinner: Sun–Thu 5:30 PM – 9:30 PM | Fri–Sat 5:30 PM – 10:00 PM

### Core Design Moves

1. **Majestic Regal Serif Typography:** Elegant serif (*Cormorant Garamond*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and spice vault mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Jaipur Crimson & Saffron Amber Palette:** Regal North Indian palette anchored in deep Jaipur crimson (`#5C061C`), saffron amber gold (`#D97706`), turmeric warm gold (`#EAB308`), and royal warm parchment (`#FFFDF7`).
3. **"The 800°F Clay Oven Tandoori & Royal Curry Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`tandoori-clay-oven-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing clay oven kebabs alongside slow-simmered regional curries & fresh naan.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Italo` — Venetian Renaissance Italian with Bodoni Moda fonts and Barolo Burgundy/Tuscan Gold.
2. `Ilios Crafted Greek` — Aegean Coastal Mediterranean with Cinzel fonts and Deep Aegean Cobalt/Azure.
3. `Honeybear Bake Shop` — Warm Golden Honey Bakery with Playfair Display fonts and Deep Cocoa/Honey Amber.

### Divergent Choices for Jaipur Indian Restaurant

- **Hero Composition:** Royal Pink City Palace & Saffron Clay Oven Tandoori Vault split-hero layout: left side features a Saffron amber stamp badge (`"ROYAL NORTH INDIAN TANDOORI VAULT • SOUTH CHARLOTTE (5909 SOUTH BLVD)"`), majestic regal serif (*Cormorant Garamond*), Jaipur crimson & saffron amber palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of tandoori clay oven specialties & royal curries.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Jaipur Vault Cards** (`jaipur-vault-card`) and South Charlotte culinary highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`jaipur-header`, `jaipur-brand`, `palace-hero-stage`, `saffron-stamp-badge`, `jaipur-vault-card`, `jaipur-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
