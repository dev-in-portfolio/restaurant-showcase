# Guatelinda Restaurante & Panadería - Evidence & Research Ledger

## Creative Brief
Guatelinda Restaurante & Panadería is Charlotte's authentic Guatemalan culinary sanctuary and artisan hearth bakery located at 6016 The Plaza. Serving the community from early morning breakfast through evening family dinner, Guatelinda offers time-honored Mayan stews (pepián de pollo, kak'ik), traditional tamales colorados in banana leaves, handheld chuchitos, savory carne adobada, and a bustling panadería counter brimming with warm conchas, champurradas, and shecas. The design aesthetic weaves Mayan indigo, volcanic terracotta, golden maize, and warm textile motifs with interactive bakery box calculators.

  - Tamales Guatemaltecos (fluffy seasoned corn masa wrapped in banana leaves with savory meat, raisins, and red recado sauce)
  - Chuchitos de Pollo (handheld corn parcels filled with spiced chicken wrapped in dry corn husks)
  - Desayuno Chapín Tradicional (scrambled eggs with tomatoes/onions, fried sweet plantains, refried black beans, queso fresco, crema, and handmade hot corn tortillas)
  - Fresh Pan Dulce Counter (conchas, pan de yema, champurradas, shecas de frijol, guava pastries, atol de elote, and Guatemalan hot coffee).
