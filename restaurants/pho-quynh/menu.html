Bone Broth Phở 24-Hour Simmer

Phở Đặc Biệt (House Combination) #1 Signature

Large steaming bowl of our 24-hour beef bone broth loaded with thin-sliced rare eye round steak, well-done flank, tender brisket, soft tendon, honeycomb tripe, and beef meatballs over fresh rice noodles with basil, bean sprouts, jalapeño, and lime.

Phở Tái Nạm (Rare Steak & Flank) Customer Favorite

Delicate aromatic beef broth with thinly sliced prime rare beef eye round and braised brisket flank that finishes gently cooking in the piping hot soup bowl at your table.

Phở Gà (Saigon Chicken Pho) Clear Broth

Clear, aromatic simmered ginger chicken broth served with tender shredded poached chicken breast, fresh rice noodles, scallions, cilantro, and ginger dipping sauce.

Phở Chay (Vegetarian Tofu Pho) Plant-Based

Fragrant roasted vegetable and star anise broth served with organic fried tofu cubes, shiitake mushrooms, baby bok choy, carrots, and fresh rice noodles.

Bún & Street Bites Saigon Kitchen

Bún Thịt Nướng Chả Giò Vermicelli Star

Char-grilled lemongrass marinated pork slices and crispy pork egg rolls served over cool rice vermicelli noodles, shredded lettuce, cucumber, pickled daikon/carrots, crushed roasted peanuts, and sweet garlic Nước Chấm.

Crispy Bánh Xèo Crepe Sizzling Street Bite

Crispy golden turmeric coconut rice crepe stuffed with plump shrimp, tender pork belly slices, bean sprouts, and scallions, served with green leaf lettuce wraps and house dipping fish sauce.

Chả Giò (Crispy Pork Egg Rolls - 4pc) Crispy Starter

Deep-fried golden rice paper rolls filled with seasoned ground pork, wood ear mushrooms, glass noodles, and minced carrots, served with sweet chili fish sauce.

Cà Phê Sữa Đá (Vietnamese Iced Coffee) Slow Drip

Dark-roasted Robusta coffee slow-dripped through a traditional metal Phin filter directly over rich sweetened condensed milk and poured over crushed ice.
