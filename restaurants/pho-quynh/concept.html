🏛 The Pho Quynh Story

Saigon Roots, Central Ave Soul.

Nestled in the heart of East Charlotte's international dining corridor along Central Avenue, Pho Quynh was founded to share the uncompromised home cooking of South Vietnam. Built on multi-generational family broth secrets, fire-charred aromatics, and honest hospitality.
