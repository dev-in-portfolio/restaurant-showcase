🌱 Sizzling Street Crepes & Slow-Drip Phin Coffee

Street Fire, Slow Drips.

Beyond our steaming noodle bowls, Pho Quynh celebrates the dynamic street food culture of Saigon. From crackling golden turmeric Bánh Xèo crepes packed with Gulf shrimp to dark-roasted Vietnamese iced coffee brewed fresh through traditional stainless steel Phin filters.

Street Craft

The 3 Pillars of Pho Quynh Street Specialties

1. Sizzling Bánh Xèo Crepes

Ladle-pouring rice flour, coconut milk, and ground turmeric into searing hot skillets until paper-thin and shattering crisp around pork belly and shrimp.

2. Lemongrass Marinade Skewers

Marinating pork in crushed lemongrass stalks, shallots, garlic, fish sauce, and honey before flame-grilling over charcoal for intense caramelized char.

3. Authentic Cà Phê Sữa Đá

Slow-dripping dark French-roasted Robusta coffee beans over rich condensed milk, then stirring vigorously and serving over crushed ice.
