🍜 24-Hour Gentle Simmering & Charred Aromatics

The Art of the Golden Broth

True Vietnamese phở cannot be rushed. At Pho Quynh, our soup kettle never sleeps. We gently simmer beef marrow bones, oxtails, and beef knuckles for a full 24 hours. The resulting broth is luminous, deeply restorative, and fragrant with dry-toasted spices.

Culinary Mastery

The 3 Pillars of Pho Quynh Broth Craft

1. 24-Hour Marrow Reduction

Simmering beef knuckles, brisket, and marrow bones with continuous gentle skimming to yield a crystalline broth rich in natural collagen and beef essence.

2. Fire-Charred Botanicals

Charring fresh yellow onions and ginger over open flames before toasting whole star anise, black cardamom pods, and Saigon cinnamon in cast iron pans.

3. Fresh Rice Noodle Blanching

Flash-blanching delicate bánh phở rice noodles to order for thirty seconds, ensuring the perfect springy bite that absorbs the seasoned broth.
