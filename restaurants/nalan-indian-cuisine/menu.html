South Indian Dosas Fermented Crepes

Mysore Masala Dosa #1 Signature Dosa

Crispy crepe smeared inside with fiery red chili garlic Mysore paste, stuffed with spiced potato masala, served with piping hot vegetable sambar & three fresh chutneys.

Ghee Paper Roast Dosa Ultra Thin & Crispy

Extra-large paper-thin crispy crepe roasted with pure aromatic clarified butter (desi ghee), rolled into a dramatic golden cone.

Onion Chili Tomato Uthappam Thick Pancake

Thick, savory fermented rice and lentil pancake topped with caramelized red onions, green chilies, cilantro, and fresh diced tomatoes.

Medu Vada Sambar Dipper (3 Pcs) Traditional Snack

Crispy golden deep-fried savory lentil fritters soaked in spiced lentil vegetable sambar and served with coconut chutney.

Tandoor & Royal Curries Clay Oven & Pots

Tandoori Murgh Chicken Tikka Charcoal Roasted

Boneless tender chicken breast chunks marinated in Greek yogurt, crushed Kashmiri chilies, and garam masala, roasted in our clay tandoor oven.

Hyderabadi Chicken Dum Biryani Royal Dum Pot

Long-grain aged basmati rice layered with spiced bone-in chicken, saffron milk, fried onions, and fresh mint, slow-cooked in a dough-sealed dum pot.
