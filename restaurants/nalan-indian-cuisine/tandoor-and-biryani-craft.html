Hearth Cookery

The 3 Pillars of Tandoor & Biryani Cooking

1. Intense Clay Tandoor Heat

Roasting marinated chicken tikka, seekh kebabs, and paneer over live hardwood charcoal to achieve deeply caramelized, smoky crusts.

2. Dum Pukht Slow-Sealing

Sealing heavy biryani pots with wheat dough so aged basmati rice steams gently in saffron milk, ghee, and succulent spiced chicken juices.

3. Hand-Stretched Tandoori Naan

Slapping hand-kneaded dough straight onto blistering clay oven walls, producing pillowy, blistered naan brushed with fresh garlic butter.
