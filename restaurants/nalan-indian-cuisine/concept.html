🏰 The Legend of King Nala

A Heritage of Royal Gastronomy

In ancient Indian folklore, King Nala was a revered monarch celebrated for his unmatched culinary mastery and profound knowledge of spices—a heritage so legendary that exceptional cooking in India is still called 'Nala Baakam'. At Nalan, we carry this royal standard forward in SouthPark Charlotte.

Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of Nalan Indian Cuisine

Authentic Regional Diversity

Honoring the distinct culinary traditions of South India (Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Andhra) and North India (Punjab, Awadh, Hyderabad).

Scratch Spice Blending

Roasting and stone-grinding whole coriander, cardamom, cumin, star anise, and peppercorns in-house for uncompromised aroma.

Atithi Devo Bhava

Embodying the ancient Sanskrit principle 'Guest is God' through warm, attentive, and generous Indian hospitality.
