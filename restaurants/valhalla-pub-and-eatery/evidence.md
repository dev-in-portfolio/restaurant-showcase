# Evidence & Architecture Brief: Valhalla Pub & Eatery

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: Valhalla Pub & Eatery
- Concept: Authentic Scandinavian-inspired Viking pub and tavern located in historic Brevard Court in Uptown Charlotte. Celebrating Norse culinary heritage with made-from-scratch Norwegian Meatballs with rich cream gravy and lingonberry jam, custom beef-pork Viking Blend Burgers, North Sea Beer-Battered Fish & Chips, hickory-smoked jumbo wings, and a lively pet-friendly open-air cobblestone courtyard patio with 10 rotating craft beer taps.
- Target Market: Uptown sports fans (Charlotte FC and Carolina Panthers pre-game crowds), local office workers, neighborhood residents, and visitors seeking hearty from-scratch pub comfort and outdoor courtyard vibes.
- Location: 317 S Church St, Charlotte, NC 28202 (Brevard Court)
- Primary Contact: (704) 332-3273 | contact@valhallapub.com
- Visual Identity: Nordic Fjord Navy (#1e3a8a), Mead Gold / Runic Amber (#d97706), Weathered Timber Brown (#78350f), Cobblestone Grey (#f3f4f6), and Pure White (#ffffff). Celtic/Norse serif display titles paired with robust, readable modern body typography.
