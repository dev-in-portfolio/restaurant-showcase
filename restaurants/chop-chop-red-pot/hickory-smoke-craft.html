12-Hour Hickory Smoke Process

Low-and-Slow Smokehouse Science

Great Southern barbecue cannot be rushed. We smoke beef briskets and pork shoulders for 12 hours overnight using aged North Carolina hickory wood at 225°F for unmatched tenderness and mahogany bark.

Explore Smoked Meats
Smoker Pillars

The 3 Pillars of Red Pot Smoke

Aged NC Hickory Wood

Naturally seasoned North Carolina hickory logs deliver a sweet, deep smoke flavor without harsh bitterness.

Signature Red Pot Rub

Coarse black pepper, kosher salt, brown sugar, garlic, and smoked paprika form a dark caramelized spice crust.

Cast-Iron Smash Sear

Chopped brisket is seared on flaming flat-tops into smashburger patties to lock in juicy smoky goodness.
