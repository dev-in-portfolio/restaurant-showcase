Steaming Bowls, Crunchy Chicken.

Welcome to Roppongi Ramen Bar, located at 9626 Monroe Road in Southeast Charlotte. We combine the soul-warming discipline of Tokyo ramen counters with the irresistible crunch of Seoul street-style double-fried chicken.
