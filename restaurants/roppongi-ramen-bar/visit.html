Arrival Directives

Free On-Site Surface Lot Parking

Visiting Roppongi at 9626 Monroe Road is completely hassle-free:

  • Generous On-Site Lot: A large, well-lit surface parking lot sits directly in front of the restaurant with ample free spaces at all hours of operation.
  • Matthews & MoRA Proximity: Accessible via Monroe Road and Sardis Road North, just minutes from the Matthews town line and Independence Boulevard.
  • Curbside Takeout Parking: Designated quick-pickup stalls right outside our main glass doors for takeout customers.
Party Platters & Takeout

Korean Fried Chicken Platters & Ramen Kits

Elevate your next gathering, office lunch, or family movie night:

  • Chicken Party Boxes: 20-piece and 40-piece platters of double-fried soy garlic wings and spicy gochujang drumsticks with side pickles and fries.
  • Separated Broth Takeout Packaging: Hot simmered broth is packaged in leak-proof sealed containers separate from cold noodles and fresh toppings to guarantee noodle chew at home.
