Double-Fried Crunch Discipline

The Science of Korean Fried Chicken.

Beyond our simmering ramen kettles, Roppongi is celebrated for authentic Korean street-style fried chicken. By utilizing pure potato starch and a meticulous two-stage frying process, our chicken achieves a paper-thin, shatteringly crisp shell that holds its crunch beneath thick, glossy glazes.

Culinary Technique

The Secret To Ultimate Crunch

Engineered to stay intensely crispy down to the very last bite.

Technique 01

Potato Starch Coating

Instead of heavy flour batters, we dredge marinated chicken in ultra-fine potato starch. When fried, this creates a delicate, glass-like crust that never turns heavy or oily.

Technique 02

The Two-Stage Fry

The first lower-temperature fry gently cooks the meat through while rendering internal fat. A brief rest is followed by a blistering second fry that flashes off residual moisture for unmatched crispness.

Technique 03

Hand-Brushed Scratch Glazes

We toss chicken immediately out of the fryer in reduced soy garlic caramel or fermented gochujang paste with raw honey, garnishing with toasted sesame seeds and crunchy pickled daikon.

Izakaya Small Plates

Handmade Pan-Seared Pork Gyoza

Our gyoza represent the pinnacle of Japanese tavern dining. Minced heritage pork is kneaded with minced scallions, garlic chives, ginger, and sesame oil, wrapped in delicate wheat skins, and seared over blazing cast iron until the base forms a crispy, golden web.
