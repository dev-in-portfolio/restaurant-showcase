Culinary Science & Heritage

The Alchemy of 18-Hour Broth.

Great ramen is not assembled—it is extracted over hours of patient, methodical heat. At Roppongi Ramen Bar on Monroe Road, our stockpots simmer uninterrupted through the night to transform dense pork marrow, roasted chicken bones, kombu kelp, and dried bonito into rich, soul-comforting elixirs.

The Four Pillars of Roppongi Ramen

Every element in the bowl serves a specific textural and aromatic purpose.

Pillar 01

18-Hour Continuous Emulsion

For our Tonkotsu broth, pork femur bones and aromatics are boiled violently for 18 continuous hours. This rapid rolling boil breaks down collagen into rich gelatin, emulsifying fat into a creamy, milky soup base.

Pillar 02

Artisanal Tare Seasoning

The soul of each bowl is its tare—the concentrated seasoning sauce at the bottom of the bowl. We age our shoyu tare with smoked bonito flakes, kombu seaweed, and mirin to deliver deep umami resonance.

Pillar 03

Kansui Alkaline Wheat Noodles

Our wavy noodles are crafted with alkaline mineral salts (kansui), providing the iconic golden hue, springy bite, and resistance to softening even in piping-hot broth until the very last slurp.

Topping Precision

Slow-Braised Chashu Pork & Ajitsuke Tamago

A bowl of ramen is crowned by its toppings. We roll, truss, and sear whole pork bellies before braising them for five hours in sweet sake, soy sauce, and ginger. Each bowl also receives an ajitsuke tamago—a six-minute soft-boiled egg with a jammy golden yolk marinated overnight in sweet soy dashi.
