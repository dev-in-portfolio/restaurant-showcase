The Spirits of the Earth

Agave is more than a spirit—it is centuries of Mexican agricultural heritage and sacred craft. Our cellar houses over 100 curated expressions of artisanal mezcal, small-batch tequila, raicilla, and bacanora distilled by multi-generational maestros mezcaleros across Mexico.

Cellar Mastery

The 3 Pillars of Puerta Agave Program

1. Wild-Harvested Agave Terroir

Highlighting rare agaves like Tobalá, Tepeztate, and Arroqueño matured for up to 25 years before earthen pit roasting and copper distillation.

2. Traditional Copita Service

Serving ancestral pours in authentic clay copitas accompanied by fresh orange wedges and house-blended Oaxacan sal de gusano.

3. Modern Agave Mixology

Balancing smoke, earth, and citrus through craft cocktails like our Agave Old Fashioned and Charcoal Clarified Paloma.
