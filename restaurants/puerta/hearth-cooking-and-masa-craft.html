🍎 Single-Origin Nixtamal & Live Fire

Nixtamalized Masa, Live Oak Fire.

The foundation of true Mexican cuisine is heirloom corn. At Puerta, we import non-GMO heirloom landrace corn from Oaxaca and Jalisco, gently nixtamalizing each kernel in volcanic mineral water before stone-grinding into supple masa for tortillas pressed and puffed fresh to order on our comals.

Culinary Mastery

The 3 Pillars of Puerta Kitchen Fire

1. Daily Nixtamalization

Steeping heirloom corn with slaked lime to unlock rich roasted aroma, vital nutrients, and the signature elastic texture of artisanal masa.

2. Comal-Puffed Tortillas

Hand-pressing every tortilla and searing on blazing flat comals seconds before serving with our braised birria, carnitas, and seafood.

3. 24-Ingredient Mole Negro

Slow-reducing charred chilhuacle chiles, Mexican dark chocolate, plantains, raisins, and roasted spices over 48 hours for depth and complexity.
