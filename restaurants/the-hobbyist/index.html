Signature Libation

The Renowned Espresso Martini

Our Espresso Martini is celebrated across Charlotte for a simple reason: we never use stale pre-batched espresso concentrate. Every cocktail is shaken hard with a freshly pulled double shot of Hatchet espresso, clean vodka, Kahlua, and Madagascar vanilla, yielding a dense, velvety layer of golden crema crowned with roasted coffee beans.
