Lavender Vanilla Cortado $5.00

Equal parts intense espresso and silky steamed milk infused with real French culinary lavender buds and pure Madagascar vanilla bean.

Classic Flat White / Cappuccino $4.75

Precision double shot of Hatchet espresso with silky microfoam whole milk or oat milk, poured with intricate latte art.

18-Hour Single-Origin Cold Brew $4.50

Craft Beer Taps, Flights & Bottle Shop

14 rotating draft lines and over 250 refrigerated retail cans and bottles.

14 Rotating Craft Draft Pints $6.50 – $9.00

Curated draft selection from regional NC craft breweries, European pilsners, juicy hazy IPAs, fruited kettle sours, and robust stouts.

Custom Four-Pour Beer Flight $12.00
