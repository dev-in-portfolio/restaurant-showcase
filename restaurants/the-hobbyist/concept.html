Neighborhood Living Room • Villa Heights

Craft Coffee, Community Connection.

The Hobbyist was born from a simple passion: creating a space that feels like your neighborhood living room, where world-class specialty coffee and exceptional craft beers share equal standing. Located in Villa Heights along the historic North Davidson corridor, we are proud to serve as our community’s third place.

Core Values

The Ethos of The Hobbyist

Guided by quality sourcing, inclusive design, and neighborhood stewardship.

Value 01

Craftsmanship Across All Hours

Whether dialing in a morning single-origin espresso shot or shaking a late-night cocktail, we give the same exacting attention to every beverage poured.

Value 02

A Living Botanical Sanctuary

We believe natural environments elevate focus and conversation. Our space is lush with living tropical plants, natural timber, and warm ambient lighting.

Value 03

Villa Heights Neighborhood Roots

Supporting local North Carolina roasters, regional microbreweries, Charlotte bakeries, and local musicians to enrich our creative community.

Community Hub

Music, Trivia & Weekly Events

From acoustic singer-songwriter sessions on Friday nights to weekend pop-ups with local artisans, The Hobbyist is an active canvas for Charlotte’s creative culture. Join us to discover new music, taste rare beers with guest brewmasters, or simply catch up with neighbors.
