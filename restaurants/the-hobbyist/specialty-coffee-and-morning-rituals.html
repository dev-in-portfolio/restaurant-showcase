Specialty Roasting • Boone NC Origins

Hatchet Roasters, Precision Extraction.

Coffee is not an afterthought at The Hobbyist—it is the foundation of our morning identity. Partnering with Hatchet Coffee in the High Country of Boone, North Carolina, we pull dialed-in espresso shots on commercial Italian machinery, marry them with house-crafted syrups, and pour velvety microfoam with genuine barista artistry.

The Four Tenets of Our Morning Program

Commitment to roast freshness, calibrated grind profiles, and scratch flavor extracts.

Tenet 01

Direct-Trade Roasts

Sourced by Hatchet Coffee from ethical farming collectives across Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, roasted small-batch in Boone, NC, and delivered fresh weekly.

Tenet 02

Scratch Botanical Syrups

We never use artificial bottled flavorings. Our black walnut syrup, French lavender infusions, and bourbon barrel-aged vanillas are brewed from whole ingredients in our kitchen.

Tenet 03

Silky Microfoam

Steamed whole milk from regional family dairies and oat milk from Minor Figures, heated to ideal sweetness (145°F) for glossy, long-lasting latte art.

Daytime Haven

Laptop-Friendly Workspace

From 7:30 AM onward, The Hobbyist is home to Charlotte remote professionals, designers, and creative founders. Outfitted with blazing fast fiber Wi-Fi, abundant electrical outlets along our banquettes, and natural greenery, it is the ideal environment to focus, brainstorm, and take meetings.
