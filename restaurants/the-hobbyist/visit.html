Daytime Focus

Remote Work & Co-Working Guidelines

Directives for working productively at The Hobbyist:

  • High-Speed Connectivity: Complimentary high-speed fiber Wi-Fi is accessible throughout the cafe and patio.
  • Electrical Outlets: Plentiful wall and floor outlets are located adjacent to our banquet seating and perimeter work counters.
  • Community Courtesy: Please use headphones for all audio/video calls, and keep conversational volumes courteous during busy midday hours.
Community Evenings

Live Music & Bottle Tastings

Directives for evening entertainment and weekend pop-ups:

  • Friday Night Acoustic Music: Weekly live performances featuring local Charlotte musicians beginning at 7:00 PM on Friday evenings.
  • Brewery Tap Takeovers: Regular tasting events spotlighting guest brewmasters from across North Carolina and regional craft cideries.
  • Dog Friendly: Well-behaved dogs on leashes are warmly welcomed on our outdoor patio.
