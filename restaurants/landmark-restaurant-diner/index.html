Food Network Feature

Featured on Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives

When host Guy Fieri visited Landmark Diner on Central Avenue, he highlighted our hand-rolled Greek Spanakopita and classic roasted turkey dinner as the gold standard of authentic American diner cooking.

  • Homemade Greek Spanakopita: Crisp, golden layers of butter-brushed phyllo pastry stuffed with fresh spinach, scallions, dill, and imported Greek feta cheese.
  • Roast Turkey & Cornbread Dressing: Slow-roasted whole turkey carved tender and topped with rich giblet gravy, savory dressing, and cranberry sauce.
  • Country Chicken Fried Steak: Tenderized steak breaded golden crisp and smothered in creamy country pepper gravy.

36 Years of Charlotte Hospitality

Comfortable Booths & Friendly Service

Step into Landmark Diner and experience classic diner charm. From our spacious retro vinyl booths to the cheerful clatter of the kitchen and bottomless hot coffee mugs, you are always treated like an old friend.

  • Early 6:00 AM Openings: Perfect for early-morning commuters, construction crews, and breakfast lovers across East Charlotte.
  • Generous Family Portions: Huge triple-decker clubs, hearty hot open-faced roast beef sandwiches, and loaded diner platters.
  • Fast Takeout & Catering: Call ahead for quick pickup of hot breakfasts, lunches, and whole bakery cakes for your office or family party.
