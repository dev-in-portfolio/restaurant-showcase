Walk-In Seating Policy

We operate on a first-come, first-served walk-in basis for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Our spacious dining room features retro booths and flexible tables to seat couples, families, and larger groups quickly.

Planning a family reunion, church group, or sports team outing of 10 or more? Give our manager a call at (704) 532-1153 so we can prepare joined seating.
