## Original Audit Weakness

Landmark Restaurant Diner is one of the most famous roadside culinary institutions in Charlotte, recognized nationally on Food Network and cherished locally for over three decades. However, its previous web presence lacked an organized, responsive multi-page presentation of its breakfast specialties, Greek family dishes, dessert showcase, and Guy Fieri feature history. This 6-page responsive web experience provides a nostalgic, elegant, and high-converting digital storefront.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **All-Day Diner Breakfast:** Stacks of golden fluffy buttermilk pancakes with warm maple syrup, Belgian waffles, griddled French toast, hash browns, and 3-egg specialty omelets.
2. **Authentic Greek & Comfort Classics:** Hand-layered spinach and feta spanakopita in phyllo pastry, carved gyro meat platters, slow-roasted turkey dinners with giblet gravy, and country chicken fried steak.
3. **The Iconic Dessert Case & Food Network Heritage:** 36 years of memories on Central Avenue, Food Network Guy Fieri feature, and towering cakes, pies, and cheesecakes baked fresh daily.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Classic Diner Crimson & Warm Buttercream Palette:** Retro Diner Crimson (`#a82020`), Warm Buttercream (`#fcf7ee`), Toasted Golden Wheat (`#c88d32`), Deep Charcoal (`#221f1d`), and Polished Chrome Accents (`#4b5563`).
2. **Diner Section Visual Architecture:** Distinct all-day breakfast griddle showcase, Greek heritage spotlight, and bakery rotating dessert case gallery.
3. **Nostalgic Diner Typography:** Playfair Display elegant serif display headlines paired with Inter for crystal-clear menu navigation and responsive mobile reading.
