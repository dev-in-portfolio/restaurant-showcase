The Famous Revolving Dessert Case

No trip to Landmark Diner is complete without admiring our glowing, revolving glass dessert showcase near the front counter. Our in-house bakers arrive before sunrise to whip frostings, bake moist cake layers, and roll buttery pie crusts.

  • Four-Layer Chocolate Fudge Cake: Deep cocoa sponge filled with rich fudge icing and chocolate shavings.
  • Southern Red Velvet & Carrot Cakes: Classic recipes with velvety cream cheese frosting and toasted pecans.

A Charlotte Staple Since 1990

From Family Dream to Roadside Legend

Opening its doors in 1990 at 4429 Central Avenue, Landmark Diner was founded on simple Greek-American values: cook everything with generous portions, serve guests with genuine warmth, and never cut corners on quality ingredients.

Over the decades, Central Avenue has grown and evolved, but Landmark Diner remains a steady, comforting beacon for generations of Charlotte families, late-night workers, and weekend breakfast regulars.
