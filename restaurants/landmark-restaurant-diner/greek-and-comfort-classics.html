Greek Family Recipes

Spanakopita, Gyros & Souvlaki Platters

Our Greek heritage is at the heart of our kitchen. We layer delicate phyllo pastry by hand, carve seasoned gyro meat fresh from the rotisserie, and prepare our tzatziki from thick strained yogurt, grated cucumber, garlic, and extra virgin olive oil.

  • Handmade Spanakopita: Golden phyllo crust stuffed with seasoned spinach and imported Greek feta cheese ($14.99).
  • Gyro Dinner Platter: Sliced lamb and beef gyro over grilled pita with tzatziki, Greek salad, and fries ($15.50).
  • Chicken Souvlaki Skewers: Lemon-herb marinated chicken breast char-grilled and served with Greek rice pilaf ($15.50).
