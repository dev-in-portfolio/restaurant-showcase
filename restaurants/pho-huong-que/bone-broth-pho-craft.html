🍜 24-Hour Slow Simmer Heritage

The Purity of Real Pho Broth

Great pho begins and ends with the broth. At Pho Huong Que, our stock pots simmer continuously for 24 hours. We combine prime beef marrow and knuckle bones with charred ginger, caramelized whole onions, toasted star anise, cinnamon bark, and black cardamom, meticulously skimming to achieve crystal-clear purity and deep savory nuance.

Broth Principles

The 3 Pillars of Pho Huong Que Broth Mastery

1. 24-Hour Marrow Extraction

Blanching and slow-simmering high-collagen beef marrow bones for a full day to extract rich gelatin and natural sweetness without shortcuts.

2. Flame-Charred Aromatics

Roasting fresh ginger roots and whole yellow onions over open flames to develop smoky, sweet undertones that harmonize with toasted spices.

3. Garden-Fresh Herb Plates

Serving every steaming bowl with a mountain of fresh Thai basil, crisp mung bean sprouts, jalapeño slices, and fresh-cut limes for custom balance.
