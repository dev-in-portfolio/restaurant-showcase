Traditional Phở 24-Hour Broths

Phở Đặc Biệt (House Special) #1 Best Seller

The ultimate beef pho bowl featuring thinly sliced rare eye round steak, well-done flank, soft beef tendon, and honeycomb tripe over rice noodles in our signature 24-hour slow-simmered star anise beef bone broth.

Phở Tái Nạm (Steak & Flank) Beef Favorite

Tender thinly sliced rare eye round beef that gently cooks in the steaming broth, paired with melt-in-your-mouth braised beef flank, scallions, sweet onions, and fresh cilantro.

Bún Bò Huế (Spicy Hue Soup) Spicy Specialty

Central Vietnamese spicy beef and lemongrass noodle soup with thicker round rice vermicelli noodles, tender sliced beef shank, pork patties, and chili-lemongrass oil broth.

Phở Gà (Free-Range Chicken Pho) Poultry Classic

Shredded free-range white and dark chicken meat in a golden, aromatic chicken bone broth simmered with roasted ginger, coriander, and scallions.

Bún & Street Bites Fresh & Crispy

Bún Thịt Nướng Chả Giò Vermicelli Star

Warm rice vermicelli noodles topped with chargrilled lemongrass marinated pork slices, crispy imperial egg rolls, crushed roasted peanuts, fresh herbs, pickled daikon, and savory nuoc cham dipping sauce.

Crispy Chả Giò Egg Rolls (3) Scratch Appetizer

Golden fried rice-paper wrapped rolls filled with seasoned ground pork, shrimp, wood-ear mushrooms, glass noodles, and shredded carrots, served with sweet chili garlic sauce.

Fresh Gỏi Cuốn Spring Rolls (2) Fresh Rolls

Translucent rice paper wrapped around steamed shrimp, tender pork belly, vermicelli noodles, fresh mint, and crisp green lettuce, served with rich homemade peanut dipping sauce.

Cà Phê Sữa Đá (Iced Coffee) Saigon Classic

Traditional dark roast Vietnamese Robusta coffee brewed through a slow metal drip phin filter over sweet condensed milk, poured over cracked ice.
