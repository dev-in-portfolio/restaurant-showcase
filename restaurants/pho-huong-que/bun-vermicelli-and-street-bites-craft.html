Street Food Mastery

The 3 Pillars of Pho Huong Que Street Craft

1. High-Heat Lemongrass Grilling

Marinating pork and chicken in minced lemongrass, shallots, garlic, fish sauce, and brown sugar before searing over high flames for smoky caramelization.

2. Handcrafted Chả Giò

Rolling seasoned pork, shrimp, glass noodles, and wood-ear mushrooms in thin rice wrappers and flash-frying to shatteringly crisp golden perfection.

3. Phin Metal Filter Drip Coffee

Slowly dripping rich dark French roast coffee over sweetened condensed milk for the ultimate bold, creamy pick-me-up.
