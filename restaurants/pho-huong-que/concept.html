🏛 The Pho Huong Que Heritage

A Heritage of Honest Simmering.

Pho Huong Que was created with a commitment to authentic Vietnamese culinary tradition. Located at 7211 E Independence Blvd, our kitchen honors time-honored techniques passed down through generations—refusing shortcuts, pre-made bases, or artificial powders in favor of genuine 24-hour beef bone reduction.

The Values of Pho Huong Que

Patience in the Kitchen

Allowing marrow bones, roasted spices, and aromatics to extract naturally over 24 hours rather than rushing the simmering process.

Herb Garden Freshness

Providing generous platters of crisp herbs, bean sprouts, and citrus with every soup to honor the cooling, restorative nature of Vietnamese cuisine.

Charlotte Community Anchor

Serving as a beloved gathering spot for Vietnamese families, local East Charlotte workers, and pho enthusiasts seeking genuine soul food.
