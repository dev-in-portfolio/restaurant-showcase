Wine-Forward Culinary Craft • Scratch Kitchen

Scratch Craft, Composed for Wine.

Unlike traditional wine shops that treat food as an afterthought, our kitchen operates as a full-service European bistro. We prepare house rosemary focaccia, roll thin hearth doughs, hand-cut Angus steaks, and curate world-class cured meats and aged cheeses tailored precisely to match the profiles of our 500+ cellar bottles.

Culinary Standards

The Foundations of Our Kitchen

Artisanal charcuterie curation, hearth heat, and scratch precision.

Standard 01

Artisan Meat & Cheese Curation

We slice Prosciutto di Parma, Spanish chorizo, and soppressata to order, paired with cave-aged Manchego and soft triple cream French brie with house focaccia.

Standard 02

Stone-Hearth Blistering

Our flatbreads are baked at 600 degrees directly on stone hearths, achieving an airy, crackling crust capable of supporting delicate toppings without becoming soggy.

Standard 03

Terroir-Driven Seasoning

Every pan reduction, herb chimichurri, and white truffle oil emulsion is seasoned to mirror and balance wine tannins, acidity, and stone fruit aromas.

Signature Presentation

The Rivergate Grand Charcuterie Board

Our grand board is designed for sharing over a great bottle. Presented on a solid ash tasting board, it showcases imported Prosciutto di Parma aged 18 months, smoky Spanish chorizo ribbons, Genoa soppressata, Spanish Manchego, French Brie, house-baked rosemary focaccia crostini, sweet fig jam, and warm citrus-marinated Castelvetrano olives.
