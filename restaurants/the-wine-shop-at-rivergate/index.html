Culinary Bistro, Curated Global Cellar.

Welcome to The Wine Shop at Rivergate, Steele Creek’s beloved culinary wine bar and boutique bottle shop. Combining an expansive library of over 500 hand-selected global wines with a scratch European-American bistro kitchen, we invite you to savor grand charcuterie boards, blistered flatbreads, Angus flatiron steaks, and sommelier tasting flights.

Bistro Highlight

Prosciutto di Parma & Arugula Flatbread

Baked in high-heat ovens until blistered and crisp, our signature flatbread combines crushed San Marzano tomato sauce, whole milk mozzarella, peppery baby arugula leaves, paper-thin ribbons of Prosciutto di Parma, and freshly shaved aged pecorino with a finishing drizzle of aged balsamic.
