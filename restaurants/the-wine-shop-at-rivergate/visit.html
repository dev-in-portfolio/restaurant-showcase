Live Entertainment

Thursday & Sunday Live Jazz Sessions

Experience intimate live acoustic and jazz performances twice weekly:

  • Thursday Jazz Nights: 6:30 PM – 9:00 PM featuring rotating regional jazz pianists, upright bassists, and vocal duos.
  • Sunday Afternoon Wine & Notes: 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM featuring mellow acoustic jazz, light charcuterie, and wine flights.
  • No Cover Charge: Live music is complimentary for all dining and tasting guests.
Al Fresco Dining

Shaded Dog-Friendly Garden Patio

Relax outdoors on our covered bistro terrace in Steele Creek:

  • Four-Legged Friends: Well-behaved leashed dogs are warmly welcomed on our patio with fresh water bowls provided.
  • All-Season Comfort: Outdoor ceiling fans for warm summer evenings and radiant heaters for brisk Carolina autumn nights.
  • Convenient Parking: Plentiful free self-parking located directly in front of the restaurant within the Rivergate complex.
