The Wine Shop at Rivergate houses one of Southwest Charlotte’s most comprehensive wine libraries. With over 500 labels spanning small family domains and legendary world estates, guests enjoy the unique freedom to explore sommelier-led flights at our tasting bar, buy bottles at retail prices, or uncork tableside with their meal.

Cellar Philosophy

A Smarter Way to Enjoy Wine

Transparency, accessibility, and exploration across every vintage.

Privilege 01

Retail Shelf Pricing

Unlike traditional restaurants that apply a 300% markup to wine bottles, our cellar operates on retail shop margins. Buy to take home or enjoy tableside with modest corkage.

Privilege 02

Curated Tasting Flights

Expand your palate through guided three-glass comparative flights, letting you taste across distinct sub-appellations, vintages, or winemaking philosophies without committing to a full bottle.

Privilege 03

Unpretentious Guidance

Our knowledgeable team loves demystifying wine. Whether you are seeking a Tuesday night value pinot noir or a collector Cabernet for an anniversary, we match your taste precisely.

The Perfect Match

Sommelier Pairing Evenings

Every week, our kitchen and wine directors collaborate on seasonal pairings that match our charcuterie selections, blistered flatbreads, and grilled steaks with hand-picked cellar bottles. Join us at our granite bar or shaded outdoor terrace to discover your new favorite vintage.
