Steele Creek Charlotte • Established 2009

A Gathering Place for Wine Lovers & Friends.

Founded in 2009 in Charlotte’s growing Steele Creek neighborhood, The Wine Shop at Rivergate was created to break the traditional restaurant mold. Why should wine lovers have to pay steep restaurant markups to enjoy a great vintage with dinner? By pairing a retail wine cellar with an authentic European bistro kitchen, we created a seamless neighborhood destination where wine is celebrated every day.

Our Story
Steele Creek Wine Bar & Bistro Since 2009
Guiding Values

The Values Behind Our Cellar & Kitchen

Approachability, fair value, scratch cooking, and neighborhood connection.

Value 01

Democratizing Great Wine

We reject the stuffy elitism often found in wine bars. Whether you enjoy a $12 everyday pinot grigio or a rare $150 vintage reserve, every guest receives gracious, attentive service.

Value 02

Scratch Bistro Cooking

From our fresh-baked focaccia dough and hand-carved Angus steaks to house reductions and dressings, our kitchen takes pride in scratch culinary integrity.

Value 03

Steele Creek Anchor

For over fifteen years, we have served as a cherished gathering spot for Steele Creek residents, hosting date nights, anniversaries, business lunches, and dog-friendly patio afternoons.

Live Music Culture

Thursday & Sunday Live Jazz Sessions

Wine and music have always belonged together. Twice a week, our dining room transforms into an intimate jazz salon featuring local acoustic guitarists, jazz pianists, and vocalists, creating Charlotte’s most relaxing evening atmosphere.
