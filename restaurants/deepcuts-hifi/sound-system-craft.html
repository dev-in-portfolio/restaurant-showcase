Acoustic Engineering

Analog Tube & Horn Craft

We reject digital compression and harsh artificial amplification. Every note at DeepCuts flows through hand-wired vacuum tube circuits and vintage acoustic horns.

Audio Architecture

The Four Pillars of Our Sound

1. Hand-Wired Vacuum Tube Amps

Class-A tube amplification delivering rich harmonic warmth and zero listener fatigue even during late-night sets.

2. Vintage Klipsch Horn Speakers

Restored high-efficiency acoustic horn enclosures providing dynamic bass response and crisp, natural vocal presence.

3. Direct-Drive Turntables

Audiophile tonearms paired with Japanese moving-magnet cartridges for pristine groove tracking.

4. Tuned Room Acoustics

Custom acoustic felt diffusers and bass traps designed to eliminate harsh room reflections.
