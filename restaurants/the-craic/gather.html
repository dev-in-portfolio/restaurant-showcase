Gather

The room makes the night. Pick your room.

The Craic was built for gathering: two private dining areas that flex between seated dinners and cocktail-style events — group celebrations, business dinners, and community gatherings in the middle of SouthPark.

Room One

The Seated Dinner

A private dining area set for the long table: birthdays that deserve a room, team dinners that outgrew the booth, family celebrations with a start and an end.

The kitchen's full range — brunch through Irish comfort — works as a pre-set or ordered menu, with the bar program alongside it.

Room Two

The Cocktail Session

The second area opens up for standing-format events — larger, cocktail-style nights where the 31-tap wall does the entertaining and the room keeps the conversation moving.

Well-suited to launches, receptions, and the gatherings that don't fit a seating chart.

How To Book

Start with the bar, not a form.

Room details, availability, and menus are confirmed directly with the venue. Call (980) 219-7195 or reserve a table through the Craic's reservation system, and the team will take it from there.
