craic

Lively conversation, good times, and the feeling of connection — the good times themselves.

That's the word this bar is named for, and it's the standard the room is held to. The Craic is SouthPark's Irish-themed neighborhood bar: elevated comfort food, a craft-driven bar program with 31 taps, daily brunch that runs straight through to a 2 AM close, and an atmosphere that shifts with the table.
