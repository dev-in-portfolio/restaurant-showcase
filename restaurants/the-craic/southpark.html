The Neighborhood

A new regular in one of Charlotte's busiest neighborhoods.

The Craic opened April 15, 2026 in Sharon Square — the walkable dining pocket of SouthPark — filling the space between fine dining and casual cafes with an all-day Irish neighborhood bar.

Apr '26

Opened by hospitality lifers

The team behind The Public House in Uptown — seasoned Charlotte operators — built The Craic as their SouthPark debut, taking over the former Rusty Bucket space.

The Story

Sharon Sq

The walkable corner of SouthPark

4810 Ashley Park Lane sits among the district's dining row — Dilworth Tasting Room and Miró Spanish Grille a stone's throw away, Oak Steakhouse around the corner, hotels within a short walk for visiting crowds.

All Day

The missing middle

SouthPark had steakhouses and cafes — what the neighborhood lacked was a living room: a bar that works for brunch, business meetings, dinner, and late-night drinks in the same week. That's the gap The Craic was built to fill.
