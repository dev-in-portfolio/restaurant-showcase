## Original Audit Weakness

Café Audire relies on social profiles and press announcements (`thepassclt.com`, `facebook.com`). Located at 4090 Raleigh St at The Pass development in Charlotte NC, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its mid-century modern vinyl listening lounge, Sound Burger headphone tables, artisan morning coffee, and evening cocktail program.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **The Pass Raleigh St Hub:** Located in a 1,107-sq-ft mid-century modern space at 4090 Raleigh St in Charlotte's The Pass development (NoDa North / Sugar Creek).
2. **Vinyl Listening Lounge & Sound Burger Hardware:** Curated vinyl record collection and rental portable Audio-Technica Sound Burger record players with studio headphones.
3. **Day-to-Night Beverage Program:** Locally roasted specialty coffee, teas, and fresh bakery by day; retro '60s–'70s craft cocktails, classic martinis, and punch bowls by night.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Mid-Century 1970s Hi-Fi Typography:** Elegant serif display (*Playfair Display*) paired with warm geometric sans (*Outfit*) and turntable technical mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Warm Walnut & Brass Palette:** Rich analog palette anchored in warm walnut wood (`#2B1B12`), vintage brass gold (`#D4A359`), warm mustard amber (`#E68A2E`), soft paper cream (`#F7F2EB`), and velvet burgundy (`#7A2528`).
3. **"The Analog Vinyl Track & Craft Cocktail Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`listening-craft.html` & `menu.html`) pairing vinyl listening sessions with daytime coffee and evening punch bowls.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `DeepCuts HiFi` — Audiophile vinyl black journal layout with Syne/Space Mono fonts.
2. `Caffeto Specialty Coffee` — Modern Andean Colombian espresso roastery with Lora/Outfit fonts.
3. `Brawley’s Beverage` — Craft Taphouse slab layout with roasted stout dark tones and Bitter font.

### Divergent Choices for Café Audire

- **Hero Composition:** Mid-Century Warm Walnut & Turntable Vault split-hero layout: left side features a brass record groove stamp badge (`"THE PASS RALEIGH ST • MID-CENTURY VINYL LOUNGE"`), elegant 1970s warm serif display typography (*Playfair Display*), warm teak wood & vintage amber glow palette, and right side features an arch-framed hero image of the vinyl listening console and espresso.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Listening Vault Cards** (`listening-vault-card`) and **Sound Burger Experience Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`audire-header`, `audire-brand`, `vinyl-hero-stage`, `groove-stamp-badge`, `listening-vault-card`, `audire-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
