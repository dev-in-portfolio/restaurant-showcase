The 3 Pillars of Halal Street Food

1. 100% Zabiha Halal Certified

We strictly source 100% certified Zabiha Halal chicken, lamb, & beef for total dietary peace of mind.

2. Turmeric Basmati Infusion

Long-grain basmati rice is steeped with golden turmeric, whole cardamom, bay leaves, & clarified ghee.

3. High-Speed Plancha Sear

Our grill masters chop meats live on hot steel planchas, creating crispy charred edges that hold sauce perfectly.
