## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Pinnacle Ridge Location:** 9539 Pinnacle Dr, Suite 203, Charlotte NC 28262 (University City Corridor).
2. **21+ Vibe Dining & Steakhouse Entrees:** Smoked Rosemary Old Fashioneds, Dry-Aged Tomahawk Ribeye, Honey Glazed Lamb Chops, Lobster Mac & Cheese, & Truffle Fries.
3. **Weekend Champagne Brunch & Lounge Vibe:** Saturday & Sunday Champagne Brunch (12pm-5pm), strictly enforced business casual dress code, & 2-hour vibe dining experience policy.

### Core Design Moves

1. **High-Contrast Ultra-Modern Serif Typography:** Ultra-modern serif (*Syne*) paired with clean body sans (*Inter*) and lounge VIP mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Deep Obsidian Onyx & Smoked Rose Gold Palette:** High-energy 21+ vibe lounge palette anchored in obsidian onyx (`#050507`), smoked rose gold (`#E11D48`), velvet amethyst (`#7E22CE`), champagne quartz (`#F59E0B`), and pearl white (`#FAFAFA`).
3. **"The Smoked Mixology & Vibe Dining Steakhouse Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`smoked-mixology-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing smoked craft cocktails alongside dry-aged steaks, lamb chops, & weekend champagne brunch.
