🍸 Wood-Smoked Glass Infusion

The Art of Smoked Spirits

Mixology is theater at Fumée. Named after the French word for smoke, we elevate craft cocktails by torching organic rosemary, applewood, & hickory chips under glass cloches, trapping rich aromatic smoke that infuses every sip.

Mixology Science

The 3 Pillars of Fumée Smoked Mixology

1. Botanical Wood Selection

We hand-select organic rosemary sprigs, applewood shavings, & hickory chips for custom smoke profiles.

2. Glass Cloche Vapor Capture

Hand-blown glass domes trap dense cold smoke over icy cocktail glasses, binding subtle smokiness to spirit fats.

3. Table-Side Reveal Ritual

Cocktails are served closed & un-cloched table-side, releasing a fragrant plume of aromatic smoke across your table.
