✨ 21+ Vibe Dining Guidelines

The Art of Vibe Dining

Vibe dining fuses high-end steakhouse gastronomy with ambient nightlife energy. To maintain an elegant lounge atmosphere for all guests, we enforce an upscale business casual dress code, 21+ entry, & a 2-hour dining experience window.

The 3 Pillars of Fumée Vibe Dining

1. Enforced Business Casual Attire

Collared shirts, tailored jackets, & elegant footwear required. Flip-flops, sandals, & athletic gym wear strictly prohibited.

2. Strict 21+ Adult Atmosphere

Valid physical photo ID required at entry for all guests without exception, ensuring a refined mature evening environment.

3. 2-Hour Experience Reservation Window

Tables are allocated a 2-hour experience window to ensure smooth reservation transitions & optimal service flow.
