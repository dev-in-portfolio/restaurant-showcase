Sashimi & Boba Tea

Nothing balances a savory Hawaiian poke bowl like an ice-cold boba milk tea. At King Fish Poke, we brew whole-leaf jasmine teas & simmer sweet taro root daily, pairing chewy Grade-A tapioca boba with our fresh ocean bowls.

The 3 Pillars of Boba & Sashimi Pairing

1. Freshly Brewed Leaf Teas

High-mountain jasmine green tea & black tea leaves steeped in small batches for pure floral flavor.

2. Brown Sugar Simmered Boba

Grade-A tapioca pearls simmered in dark brown sugar syrup every 2 hours to ensure optimal warm, chewy texture.

3. Creamy Taro & Fruit Flavors

Customizable sweet taro milk teas, mango passion fruit green teas, & popping boba toppings.
