🏜 Hawaiian Poke Craft

The Art of Hawaiian Poke

"Poke" means to slice or cut in Hawaiian. At King Fish Poke, we honor the island tradition of slicing pristine, sashimi-grade Ahi tuna & Atlantic salmon into bite-sized cubes, tossing them with green onions, sweet onions, sesame oil, & sea salt right before your eyes.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of King Fish Poke Craft

1. Sashimi-Grade Fish Sourcing

We source 100% wild-caught yellowfin Ahi tuna & sushi-grade salmon inspected daily for peak freshness & color.

2. Precision Hand-Dicing

Every fish loin is precision-cut into 3/4-inch cubes every morning, maintaining pristine moisture & tender texture.

3. Artisanal Island Sauces

House-brewed ponzu shoyu, spicy sriracha mayo, roasted sesame ginger, & sweet unagi glaze crafted from scratch.
