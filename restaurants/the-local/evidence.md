## Original Audit Weakness

The Local stands as one of Uptown Charlotte’s most vibrant neighborhood taverns, beloved by locals and sports fans for its no-frills attitude, 24 HDTV screens, and full kitchen open until 2:00 AM every night of the week. However, the restaurant lacked a dedicated multi-page website showcasing its scratch comfort menu, East Fizz drink varieties, game day schedules for Hornets/Panthers games, or private party bookings. This 6-page concept provides a bold, high-octane digital presence.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Scratch Tavern Comfort Food:** Double smash Angus burgers, slow-smoked brisket grilled cheeses on sourdough, crispy double-fried jumbo wings, and loaded cheese tots.
2. **Home of the East Fizz:** Handcrafted signature vodka seltzers with fresh fruit and citrus juices, alongside 20+ rotating North Carolina draft beers.
3. **Spectrum Center Game Day & 2:00 AM Kitchen:** Premier sports viewing destination with 24 big screens, late-night dining 7 days a week, and post-concert energy.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Neon Amber & Brick Rust Palette:** 5th Street Neon Amber (`#f59e0b`), Uptown Brick Rust (`#9a3412`), Tavern Black Slate (`#0f172a`), and Vintage Brass Gold (`#ca8a04`).
2. **Sports & Nightlife Visual Architecture:** East Fizz cocktail cards, 24 HDTV game day sports hub, and late-night 2:00 AM kitchen countdown.
3. **Punchy Bold Typography:** Syne display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for game tip-off times.
