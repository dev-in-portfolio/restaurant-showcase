🍕 High-Heat Brick Oven Artisanal Science

The Art of Wood-Fired Pizza

Great pizza requires high heat & patient dough fermentation. At Midtown Tavern, our brick oven reaches 800 degrees, stoked with seasoned oak wood to bake crisp, airy crusts with signature leopard charring in under 90 seconds.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Midtown Wood-Fired Pizza

1. 48-Hour Cold Dough Fermentation

Fermenting Italian Caputo 00 flour dough cold for 48 hours for light digestibility & airy crust bubble development.

2. 800° Seasoned Oak Wood Fire

Maintaining 800-degree floor temperatures using kiln-dried hickory & oak wood logs.

3. Artisanal Italian & Local Toppings

Hand-crushing San Marzano plum tomatoes & slicing fresh fior di latte mozzarella daily.
