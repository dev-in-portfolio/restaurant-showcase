The Art of NC Craft Beer

North Carolina is home to world-class craft brewing. At Midtown Tavern, our 24-tap draft wall features fresh IPAs, crisp Pilsners, rich stouts, & seasonal ciders from Charlotte, Asheville, & the Carolinas alongside handcrafted spirits.

Taproom Program

The 3 Pillars of Midtown Taproom Craft

1. 100% Local NC Brewery Focus

Supporting independent Carolinas microbreweries with fresh weekly tap rotation.

2. Glycol-Chilled Tap System

Maintaining line temperatures at a crisp 36 degrees for optimal carbonation & foam head.

3. House Craft Cocktails & Bourbon

Stirring small-batch bourbon Old Fashioneds, fresh citrus margaritas, & seasonal mules.
