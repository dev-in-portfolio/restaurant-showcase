The Four Pillars of Suffolk Punch Brewing

How our brewing team crafts benchmark lagers and IPAs.

Pillar 01

Water Chemistry Profiles

Every beer style begins with tailored water mineralogy. We adjust calcium, sulfate, and chloride ratios to match historic brewing waters—from soft Pilsen to crisp Burton-on-Trent.

Pillar 02

Direct-Farm Hop Selection

We contract directly with Pacific Northwest and European hop growers, hand-selecting high-oil lots of Citra, Mosaic, and Saaz to achieve peak aromatics without vegetal astringency.

Pillar 03

Dedicated Cold Lagering

No rushed fermentations. Flagship lagers like Blue Daisy condition at near-freezing temperatures for six full weeks to achieve brilliant clarity and crisp malt drinkability.

The Flagship Story

Pulp Prescription Hazy IPA

Our most requested beer was engineered to showcase double dry-hopped hop oils at their fruitiest and most delicate. Built on a generous grist of malted oats and white wheat, Pulp Prescription pours a dense glowing golden haze with intoxicating notes of fresh mango pulp, candied grapefruit peel, and citrus blossom.
