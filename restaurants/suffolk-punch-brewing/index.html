South End Rail Trail Anchor • Griffith Street

Craft Brewhouse, Scratch Kitchen.

Welcome to Suffolk Punch Brewing, South End Charlotte's landmark destination for house-crafted beer and elevated dining at 2911 Griffith Street. Positioned directly on the Charlotte Rail Trail next to the New Bern Station, we brew acclaimed flagships like Pulp Prescription Hazy IPA and Blue Daisy Pilsner. Our culinary team delivers an uncompromising scratch kitchen menu—from double smash burgers and hand-stretched pizzas to weekend brunch and espresso coffee.

Culinary Signature

The SPB Double Smash Burger

Our kitchen's pride begins on a screaming-hot flat top. Two Angus beef patties smashed thin for lacy, caramelized edges, layered with melted American cheese, griddled onions, house-brined dill pickles, and secret pub sauce on a toasted brioche bun, paired with golden garlic parmesan frites.

Wood-Fired Crust

Hand-Stretched Tuscan Pizzas

Fermented over 48 hours for an airy, blistered crust, our Tuscan flatbread pizzas are topped with fresh San Marzano tomatoes, buffalo mozzarella, fresh torn basil, and chef creations like shaved prosciutto di Parma drizzled with spicy wildflower hot honey.
