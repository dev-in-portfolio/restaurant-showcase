At Suffolk Punch Brewing, the kitchen is held to the exact same uncompromising standards as the brewhouse. Our culinary team crafts every sauce, pickle, and aioli from scratch. From certified Angus smash burgers and blistered flatbreads to weekend brunch loaded with Carolina shrimp and stone-ground grits, we deliver culinary depth that pairs seamlessly with our beers.

Pantry Principles

The Benchmarks of Our Scratch Kitchen

Why scratch prep elevates the South End brewery dining experience.

Principle 01

House-Ground Angus Beef

Our burger patties are ground fresh daily from whole chuck and brisket primal cuts, smashed hard on high-heat planchas to create crispy Maillard crusts.

Principle 02

48-Hour Slow Dough Fermentation

Our Tuscan-style flatbread pizzas utilize long cold-fermented dough that develops complex wheaty flavor and delicate, bubbly crusts under fierce oven heat.

Principle 03

Carolina Coastal Sourcing

We celebrate regional Carolinian bounty—wild Carolina shrimp, heritage stone-ground grits, and Southern produce sourced directly from regional farm partners.

Weekend Destination

South End's Premier Weekend Brunch

Every Saturday and Sunday morning starting at 10:00 AM, our South End taproom and beer garden transform into the neighborhood's favorite brunch destination. Pair scratch cheddar biscuits, smoked huevos rancheros, and shrimp & grits with morning cold brew or a crisp Blue Daisy Pilsner.
