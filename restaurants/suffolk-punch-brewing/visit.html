Transit & Parking

Arriving at Suffolk Punch Brewing

Multiple convenient ways to reach our South End campus:

  • Lynx Light Rail (Recommended): Disembark directly at the New Bern Station on the Blue Line. We are situated less than 100 feet from the platform.
  • Charlotte Rail Trail: Walk, run, or bike directly into our beer garden along the pedestrian Rail Trail path. Dedicated bike racks available.
  • Designated Customer Parking: Dedicated parking spots are available in our adjacent Griffith Street surface lot, with overflow deck parking nearby.
Outdoor Pavilion

Covered Beer Garden & Dog-Friendly Patio

Our expansive outdoor pavilion is designed for year-round comfort:

  • All-Weather Pavilion: High architectural ceilings equipped with cooling misting fans during summer and radiant heaters in cooler months.
  • Dog-Friendly Environment: Leashed, well-behaved dogs are warmly welcomed in all outdoor beer garden spaces, with water stations provided.
  • Full Table Dining: Full beer drafts, cocktails, coffee, and complete scratch kitchen dining are served directly in the beer garden.
