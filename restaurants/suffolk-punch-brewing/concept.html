South End Heritage • 2911 Griffith Street

Rooted in South End, Built for Community.

Suffolk Punch Brewing was created to be more than a simple taproom. Named after the steadfast English draft horse renowned for strength and gentle temperament, our brewery campus was envisioned as South End Charlotte's central living room—a vibrant nexus where world-class brewing, chef-driven dining, morning coffee, and neighborhood gathering merge seamlessly along the Rail Trail.

Core Values

The Identity of Suffolk Punch

What drives our hospitality, brewing integrity, and community role.

Value 01

Craft Without Compromise

Whether formulating a crisp Czech pilsner or a seasonal hazy double IPA, we invest in premier raw ingredients and patient lagering to brew beer at its highest potential.

Value 02

Culinary Partnership

We reject the notion that breweries should rely on outside food trucks. Our scratch culinary team produces chef-driven cuisine matched intentionally to our taplist.

Value 03

Open-Door Hospitality

From runners stopping off the Rail Trail for iced espresso to family weekend brunches and late-night toasts, we welcome all segments of our city with genuine warmth.

Urban Integration

On The Charlotte Rail Trail

Our location directly adjacent to the Rail Trail and Lynx New Bern Station allows guests to commute sustainably on foot, by bike, or via light rail. Enjoy an afternoon in our covered open-air beer garden pavilion surrounded by greenery and urban energy.
