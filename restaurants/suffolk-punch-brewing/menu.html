House-Brewed Craft Beers & Flights

Brewed on site on Griffith Street. Available by the pint, 4-pour flight, or 4-pack cans to go.

Pulp Prescription Hazy IPA $7.50 Pint / 6.8% ABV

Our flagship New England style IPA double dry-hopped with Citra and Mosaic; intensely aromatic notes of ripe mango, pink grapefruit, and velvety mouthfeel.

Blue Daisy Bohemian Pilsner $7.00 Pint / 5.2% ABV

Traditional European-style lager conditioned over six weeks; noble Saaz hops provide delicate floral spice backed by clean crackery pilsner malt snap.

Daydream Bavarian Hefeweizen $7.00 Pint / 5.4% ABV

Authentic unfiltered German wheat ale fermenting with expressive yeast character delivering natural notes of ripe banana, sweet clove, and airy breadiness.

Hyde Park Amber Ale $7.00 Pint / 5.8% ABV

Rich toasted specialty caramel malts balanced with earthy British hops; smooth, easy-drinking medium body with subtle biscuit sweetness.

Custom 4-Pour Brewhouse Tasting Flight $14.00 Flight

Sample any four draft selections pulled fresh from our tap wall, served in our signature forged-iron tabletop tasting cradle.

South End Nitro Stout $7.50 Pint / 6.2% ABV

Poured on nitrogen for a cascading creamy foam head; roasted cacao nibs, dark baker's chocolate, and cold-brew coffee finish.

Chef-Driven Scratch Kitchen

Made fresh from scratch daily with premium meats and produce.

The SPB Double Smash Burger $16.50

Two smashed Certified Angus beef patties, caramelized griddled onions, sharp American cheese, house-brined dill pickles, and secret pub sauce on toasted brioche with garlic parmesan frites.

Mojo Pork Tostones $14.00

Crispy twice-fried green plantain tostones topped with tender citrus-braised mojo pulled pork, pickled red onions, fresh cilantro aioli, and crumbled queso fresco.

Blackened Ahi Tuna Wonton Nachos $17.00

Crisp wonton chips piled with blackened rare sesame ahi tuna, shelled edamame, wakame seaweed salad, sriracha aioli, unagi sauce drizzle, and scallions.

Hot Honey Fried Chicken Biscuit $14.50

Buttermilk brined crispy fried chicken thigh, habanero hot honey drizzle, and house pickle chips sandwiched in a fresh scratch-baked cheddar scallion biscuit.

Carolina BBQ Shrimp & Grits $19.00

Jumbo wild-caught Carolina shrimp, smoky andouille sausage, and sweet corn relish simmered in charred tomato gravy over creamy yellow stone-ground grits.

Garlic Parmesan Brewhouse Frites $8.00

Hand-cut crispy russet potatoes tossed in roasted garlic confit oil, sea salt, rosemary, and aged grated parmesan, served with roasted garlic aioli.

Hand-Stretched Tuscan Flatbread Pizzas

Slow-fermented dough hand-stretched thin and baked to blistering perfection.

Prosciutto, Fig & Hot Honey Flatbread $17.00

Aged prosciutto di Parma, sweet black mission fig jam, creamy shredded mozzarella, crumbled gorgonzola cheese, fresh baby arugula, and habanero chili hot honey.

Classic Margherita Flatbread $15.00

San Marzano crushed tomato pomodoro, fresh sliced buffalo mozzarella, extra virgin olive oil, cracked black pepper, and whole torn garden basil leaves.

Smoked Fennel Sausage & Sweet Pepper $16.50

House Italian fennel pork sausage, fire-roasted sweet bell peppers, caramelized onions, melted fontina cheese, and crushed red chili flakes.

Wild Mushroom & Truffle Cream $16.50

Roasted cremini and shiitake mushrooms, garlic herb ricotta, shredded mozzarella, fresh thyme, and white truffle oil drizzle.
