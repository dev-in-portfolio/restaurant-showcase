🌱 Durban Culinary Tradition

The Legend of Bunny Chow

Bunny Chow is South Africa's most famous and beloved street food treasure. Originating in Durban among Indian immigrant communities, this hearty dish hollows out fresh bakery sourdough loafs and packs them with rich, aromatic slow-simmered spiced curries. At Oh My Soul, we bring this timeless culinary comfort to Charlotte, made 100% plant-based.

The 3 Pillars of Oh My Soul Comfort Fare

1. Roasted Cape Malay Spices

Whole-toasting coriander, cumin, turmeric, fenugreek, and cardamom to unlock fragrant warmth and authentic depth of flavor.

2. Sourdough Loaf Bowls

Using crusty, hollowed bakery sourdough loaves that soak up the spiced curry gravy without getting soggy, served with fresh carrot sambals.

3. 100% Compassionate Kitchen

Crafting deeply satisfying, flavorful dishes entirely free from animal products—proving plant-based food can be hearty, indulgent, and soulful.
