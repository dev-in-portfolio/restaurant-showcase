🌳 Outdoor Garden & Living Heritage

Under the 100-Year Oak

Step into an urban garden oasis in the heart of NoDa. Our shaded outdoor patio wraps around a magnificent 100-year-old oak tree, offering a peaceful sanctuary where dogs are welcome, drinks are poured fresh from our outdoor bar, and community gathers under twinkling evening lights.

The 3 Pillars of Our Patio Experience

1. The Living Heritage Oak

A sprawling 100-year-old canopy providing natural cool shade during warm summer afternoons and a magical canopy lit by fairy lights at dusk.

2. Full Outdoor Cocktail Bar

Serving fresh cold-pressed botanical cocktails, South African rooibos spritzes, local Charlotte craft beers, and organic vegan wines.

3. Dog-Friendly Hospitality

Welcoming four-legged family members with fresh water bowls, cozy seating corners, and dedicated patio space for community events.
