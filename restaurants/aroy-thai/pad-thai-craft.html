The Art of Wok Seared Noodles

Authentic Thai stir-fried noodles require intense wok heat to lock in aromatics and create smoky 'wok hei' char on rice noodles.

Wok Cooking Principles

The Four Pillars of Aroy Noodle Craft

1. Scratch Tamarind Glaze

Simmered in-house from pulp tamarind, palm sugar, and fish sauce for balanced sweet-tart taste.

2. Screaming Hot Woks

High BTU burners create caramelization on broad rice noodles without overcooking vegetables.

3. Fresh Aromatics

Freshly crushed garlic, Thai bird’s eye chilies, sweet basil, and scallions tossed at peak heat.

4. Roasted Peanut Finish

Finished with crisp bean sprouts, fresh lime wedges, and house-roasted crushed peanuts.
