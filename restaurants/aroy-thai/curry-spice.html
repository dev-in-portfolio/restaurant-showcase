House Spice Scale Custom Heat

Level 1 — Mild Gentle Warmth

Subtle lemongrass and galangal aromatics without chili heat. Great for kids and sensitive palates.

Level 2–3 — Medium / Spicy Standard Heat

Traditional heat level. Balanced kick from crushed red peppers and fresh Thai chilies.

Level 4–5 — Very Spicy / Thai Hot Authentic Fiery

For true chili enthusiasts. Whole bird’s eye chilies and extra chili oil. We recommend pairing with sweet Thai Iced Tea!
