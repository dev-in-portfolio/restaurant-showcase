Natural Wines by the Glass & Bottle

Low-intervention, biodynamic, and organic wines served fresh at cellar temperature.

Domaine Bobinet Pétillant Naturel Glass $13.00 | Bottle $42.00

Loire Valley, France. 100% Cabernet Franc pét-nat. Naturally sparkling with wild strawberry, mineral salinity, fine bubbles, and zero dosage.

Meinklang 'Kontakt' Skin-Contact Orange Glass $14.00 | Bottle $46.00

Burgenland, Austria. Biodynamic Grüner Veltliner & Welschriesling macerated on skins for two weeks. Notes of dried apricot, chamomile, and gentle tannins.

Ruth Lewandowski 'Feints' Chilled Red Glass $14.50 | Bottle $48.00

Mendocino County, California. Co-fermented Arneis, Dolcetto, and Barbera. Pomegranate, fresh herbs, and refreshing high-toned fruit served with a slight chill.

Occhipinti 'SP68' Rosso Glass $16.00 | Bottle $54.00

Sicily, Italy. Biodynamic Frappato and Nero d'Avola grown on calcareous sand. Bright red currants, wild thyme, black pepper, and volcanic energy.

Curated 4-Pour Tasting Flights

Four 2.5-ounce pours served on custom oak barrel staves ($18.00 each).

The Living Earth Natural Flight $18.00

Explore the spectrum of natural winemaking: wild pét-nat sparkler, skin-contact orange, light-bodied chilled red, and an unfiltered volcanic red.

The Old World Terroir Flight $18.00

A deep dive into European heritage vineyards across France, Italy, and Austria, highlighting low-intervention farming and distinct mineral soil profiles.

The Bold & Structured Red Flight $18.00

For lovers of darker fruit and spice: four organic, un-oaked full-bodied reds with velvety mouthfeel and notes of blackberry, cedar, and crushed cocoa.

The Crisp & Saline White Flight $18.00

High-acid, refreshing whites from coastal and alpine regions: Muscadet, Basque Txakolina, Alto Adige Pinot Grigio, and dry Austrian Riesling.
