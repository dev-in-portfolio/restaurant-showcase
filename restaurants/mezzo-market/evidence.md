# Mezzo Market - Evidence & Research Ledger

## Creative Brief
Mezzo Market is Plaza Midwood's vibrant neighborhood culinary anchor located at 2212 The Plaza in Charlotte, NC. Blending an artisan Mediterranean deli, a wood-fired hearth pizzeria, and a curated specialty grocery market, Mezzo Market serves slow-braised lamb shank sandwiches, handmade Georgian khachapuri, flaky Balkan burek, blistered sourdough pizzas, and house-blended mezze platters. The design aesthetic radiates Mediterranean market warmth with sun-baked terracotta, olive green, warm stone, natural linen textures, clean editorial typography, and interactive culinary calculators.
