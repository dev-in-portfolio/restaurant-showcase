About Mezzo Market

Rooted in the coastal markets of the Mediterranean and the historic hearths of the Balkans, created for Plaza Midwood.

Our Philosophy

Artisanal Craft, Real Ingredients & Neighborhood Soul

Mezzo Market was born from a passion for the lively markets of Southern and Eastern Europe, where buying groceries, sharing a warm wood-fired slice, and sipping a glass of natural wine happen under one welcoming roof.

Located at 2212 The Plaza, we ferment our sourdough pizza dough for 72 hours, roll fresh phyllo pastry for our daily burek, and slow-braise lamb shanks with fragrant Mediterranean herbs. We believe great food should be unpretentious, deeply flavorful, and shared with neighbors.

Explore Our Kitchen Visit Us in Plaza Midwood

72-Hour Sourdough Fermentation

Our pizza dough is cold-fermented for three days using Italian Caputo flour for an airy, easily digestible, and blistered crust.

Old-World Phyllo Rolling

Hand-stretched paper-thin phyllo pastry baked golden and flaky every morning for our traditional Balkan bureks.

Direct Artisan Importers

We partner directly with small family producers in Greece, Italy, and Spain to bring rare olive oils, cheeses, and wines to Charlotte.
