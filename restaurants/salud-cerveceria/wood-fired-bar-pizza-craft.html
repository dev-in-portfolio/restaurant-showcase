The Discipline of Crispy Bar Pizza.

At Salud Cerveceria, our pizza is a study in precision. Inspired by traditional northeastern bar-style pies and elevated with Latin culinary perspective, our pizzas feature ultra-thin, crackery crusts that stay rigid and crispy under bubbling cheeses and savory sauces.

Kitchen Discipline

The Anatomy of Our Bar-Style Crust

Engineered for maximum crunch, charred flavor, and structural integrity.

Pillar 01

48-Hour Cold Fermentation

Our dough is mixed from high-gluten unbleached flour, pure filtered water, and minimal yeast, resting in refrigerated retarders for two full days to develop deep lactic acid complexities and effortless crispiness.

Pillar 02

700-Degree Blistering Heat

Pies are baked directly on scorching deck stones. The intense bottom heat vaporizes moisture in seconds, locking in a crispy crackery undercarriage and blistering the crust perimeter with flavorful char.

Pillar 03

Latin-Influenced Flavor Profiles

We honor our heritage through inventive combinations: charred poblano crema bases, fire-roasted sweet corn, salty cotija cheese crumbles, and hot honey drizzles that pair seamlessly with craft beer.

Pairing Harmony

Crispy Crust & Cold Craft Beer

Bar pizza was historically invented in working-class taverns to be held in one hand while holding a cold beer in the other. At Salud, that ritual reaches its apex: the charred savory crunch of our thin crust cuts effortlessly through the tropical hops of our hazy IPAs and the crisp carbonation of our Mexican-style lager.
