Wood-Fired Pizza, Trailblazing Beer.

Welcome to Salud Cerveceria at 3306-C North Davidson Street in the heart of Charlotte's historic NoDa arts district. Founded by Jason and Daria Glunt, Salud is a nationally acclaimed culinary home combining crispy, charred-crust bar-style pizzas with in-house brewed lagers, wild sours, morning espresso, and world-class craft beer culture.

Scratch Starters & Subs

Mamacita Meatballs & San Marzano Sauce

Our famous Mamacita Meatballs are rolled by hand from seasoned beef and pork, braised slowly in sweet San Marzano tomato marinara sauce, and topped with melted mozzarella, grated pecorino, and fresh sweet basil. Served with warm crusty bread for table-sharing.
