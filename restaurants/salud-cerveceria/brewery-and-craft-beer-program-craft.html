Fermentation Artistry in NoDa

Pioneering Brews, Curated Bottles.

Salud began in 2012 as an ambitious craft bottle shop and evolved into one of the country’s premier independent breweries. Today, our upstairs and downstairs draft systems pour fresh in-house lagers, intensely hopped IPAs, and mixed-culture sours alongside the world’s most sought-after guest barrels.

Cellar Mastery

The Foundations of Salud Beer

Rooted in heritage, driven by precision, and celebrated across the craft beer world.

Style 01

Clean Heritage Lagers

Brewed with flaked maize, premium two-row barley, and traditional noble hops. Cold-fermented and lagered for weeks until ultra-crisp, refreshing, and clean on the palate.

Style 02

Aromatic Hazy IPAs

Massive dry-hop additions during active fermentation impart intense aromas of passionfruit, ripe peach, and citrus zest without harsh lingering bitterness, finishing lush and pillowy.

Style 03

Mixed-Fermentation Sours

Our barrel program utilizes native yeasts, lactic acid cultures, and French oak casks to produce complex, vinous farmhouse ales and fruited sours of exceptional depth.
