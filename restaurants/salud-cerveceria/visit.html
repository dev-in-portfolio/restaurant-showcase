Arrival Directives

Transit & Parking Options in NoDa

Getting to Salud Cerveceria at 3306-C North Davidson Street:

  • LYNX Blue Line Light Rail: The 36th Street Station is just a 4-minute walk (0.2 miles) away, offering direct rail transit to and from Uptown and South End.
  • Street Parking: Metered and free parallel parking is available along North Davidson Street and adjoining cross streets throughout NoDa.
  • Public Parking Decks: The 36th Street parking deck and multiple paid surface lots are located within two blocks of our front entrance.
Private Events & Group Orders

Upstairs Mezzanine Buyouts & Pizza Platters

Host your next celebration at Salud Cerveceria:

  • Upstairs Taproom Lounge: Available for private and semi-private buyouts, complete with private bar service, shuffleboard, and flexible seating for up to 80 guests.
