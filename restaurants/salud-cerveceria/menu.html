Wood-Fired Bar-Style Pizzas

Slow-fermented thin crust baked directly on blazing stone decks until crispy and blistered. Vegan cheese available on request.

Hootie Hoo Margherita $16

Crushed San Marzano tomato sauce, fresh buffalo mozzarella, aged pecorino romano, cold-pressed olive oil, and fresh garden basil on a charred thin crust.

Latin Lingo Specialty Pie $18

Spicy charred poblano crema base, seasoned grilled chicken breast, fire-roasted sweet corn, whole-milk mozzarella, pickled red onions, crumbled cotija, and fresh cilantro.

Return of the G (White Pie) $17

Garlic olive oil base, sautéed cremini and roasted oyster mushrooms, caramelized tender zucchini, shredded mozzarella, and cracked black pepper.

The Elote Pizza $17

Fire-roasted sweet corn, garlic herb crema, shredded mozzarella, cotija cheese, chili lime dusting, fresh cilantro, and green scallions.

The NoDa Cup & Char Pepperoni $17

Rich red tomato sauce, shredded mozzarella, crispy cup-and-char pepperoni bowls, hot wildflower honey drizzle, and dried Sicilian oregano.

Plain Jane Cheese Pie $14

San Marzano tomato marinara, whole-milk mozzarella, fontina cheese blend, aged parmesan, and extra virgin olive oil.

Mamacita Meatballs, Subs & Starters

Made from scratch daily using slow-simmered sauces, prime meats, and local bakery rolls.

Mamacita Meatballs $12

Hand-rolled beef and pork meatballs simmered slowly in rich San Marzano tomato marinara, topped with melted mozzarella and fresh basil, served with crusty bread.

Player’s Ball Meatball Sub $15

Mamacita meatballs, house red sauce, melted provolone, whole-milk mozzarella, and house basil pesto on a toasted Italian roll with kettle chips.

Call of Da Wild Chicken Sub $15

Herb-roasted chicken breast, crispy applewood bacon, melted mozzarella cheese, crisp shredded lettuce, and house buttermilk ranch on a toasted hoagie.

Wood-Fired Pepperoni Rolls $11

Fluffy yeast dough rolls stuffed with sliced pepperoni, garlic butter, and mozzarella cheese, baked golden brown and served with warm marinara dipping sauce.

In-House Brews & Tasting Flights

Brewed directly on-site at Salud Cerveceria. Available by the glass or in curated 4-beer tasting flights.

Salud Mexican-Style Craft Lager (5.0%) $7.00

Crisp, refreshing, straw-colored lager brewed with flaked maize and German noble hops. Clean, dry finish with gentle floral hop character.

Queen City Hazy IPA (6.8%) $8.00

Double dry-hopped with Citra, Mosaic, and Galaxy hops. Murky golden pour overflowing with aromas of ripe mango, passionfruit, and juicy pineapple.

Wild Fermented Farmhouse Sour (5.8%) $9.00

Fermented with native yeasts and aged in oak barrels with North Carolina stone fruits. Bright tart acidity with complex vinous funk.

Curated 4-Beer Tasting Paddle Flight $14.00

Choose any four 5oz pours from our draft lines, served on a custom wooden flight paddle with thick creamy foam heads and informative tasting notes.
