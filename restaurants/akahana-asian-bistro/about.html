About Akahana

Dedicated to authentic Pan-Asian cuisine, precise sushi preparation, and warm neighborhood hospitality in Plaza Midwood.

Our Vision

Craftsmanship Across Asian Culinary Traditions

Located at 1308 The Plaza, Akahana Asian Bistro was established to provide Plaza Midwood neighbors with a welcoming gathering place offering the finest elements of Japanese, Vietnamese, Thai, and Chinese gastronomy under one roof.

Our sushi chefs slice pristine raw fish daily, while our kitchen simmers aromatic bone broths for 14 hours and tosses fresh wok dishes over high flames. We take pride in generous portions, exceptional freshness, and fast, friendly service for both dine-in and takeout.
