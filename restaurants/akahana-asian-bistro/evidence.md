# Akahana Asian Bistro - Evidence & Research Ledger

## Creative Brief
Akahana Asian Bistro is Plaza Midwood's premier neighborhood Asian fusion destination located at 1308 The Plaza in Charlotte, NC. Combining an expansive craft sushi & sashimi bar with comforting pan-Asian culinary classics, Akahana serves freshly rolled specialty maki, steaming Vietnamese beef pho, Japanese udon bowls, fragrant Thai red curries, and sizzling wok dishes. The design system features rich imperial crimson, bamboo jade, crisp clean whites, elegant slate cards, and intuitive interactive calculators.
