What You Will Find at Cedars

Carefully sourced culinary staples from Lebanon, Turkey, Greece, Egypt, and Jordan.

Aromatic Spices

Whole & Ground Spices

Authentic wild mountain za'atar, tart crimson sumac, Turkish Aleppo pepper, whole green cardamom pods, Lebanese 7-spice blend, and pure saffron threads.

Oils & Syrups

Olive Oils & Floral Waters

First cold-pressed extra virgin olive oils, tart pomegranate molasses, pure Lebanese orange blossom water, Damascus rose water, and tahini pastes.
