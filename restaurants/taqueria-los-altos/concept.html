East Charlotte Community • 8625 Monroe Rd

Family Pride, Uncompromising Flavors.

Taqueria Los Altos was founded on a simple promise: cook food exactly the way it is made in the homes and roadside fondas of Los Altos de Jalisco. Located on the bustling Monroe Road corridor near the Matthews line, our kitchen is a labor of love, bringing families, neighbors, and workers together over steaming plates of authentic Mexican cuisine.

Core Values

The Foundations of Taqueria Los Altos

The culinary commitments and community spirit that guide our family.

Value 01

Authentic Recipe Integrity

We never compromise on authentic ingredients. From dried guajillo and árbol chiles to fresh Mexican cotija cheese and ripe avocados, our dishes are unadulterated and true to Mexico.

Value 02

Scratch Daily Preparation

Our meats are butchered and marinated in-house, our beans are slow-cooked daily from dry pintos, and our corn tortillas and salsas are made fresh continuously throughout the day.

Value 03

Welcoming Hospitality

Whether you are stopping in for a quick three-taco lunch special or bringing the entire family for a weekend caldo de res feast, we greet you with the warmth of family.

Monroe Road Corridor

An East Charlotte Neighborhood Anchor

Monroe Road is one of Charlotte’s most vibrant multicultural corridors. Taqueria Los Altos is proud to serve this diverse community, providing affordable, hearty, and authentic meals seven days a week.
