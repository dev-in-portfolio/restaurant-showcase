Transit & Parking

Arriving on Monroe Road

Visiting our taqueria is fast and convenient:

  • Plaza Surface Parking: Generous free parking spaces are situated directly in front of Suite D across the plaza surface lot.
  • Curbside Takeout Bays: Convenient curbside parking spaces allow quick in-and-out access for carryout and online orders.
  • Corridor Access: Effortless connection from Independence Blvd (Hwy 74), Sardis Road North, and central Matthews.
Party Feasts & Events

Taco Catering Directives

Guidelines for your group gatherings:

  • Taco Bar Feasts: Complete taco bar packages with choice of meats (carnitas, carne asada, al pastor, chicken), double corn tortillas, fresh cilantro, onions, lime, rice, and beans.
  • Scratch Salsa Bar: Gallon and pint containers of our house-roasted salsa verde, chile de árbol salsa roja, and fresh pico de gallo with tortilla chips.
  • Advance Notice: We request 24 hours advance notice for catering orders exceeding 25 guests. Call (704) 910-3979 to order.
