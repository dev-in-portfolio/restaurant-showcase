Authentic Jalisco Recipes

Street Tacos, Plancha Grills & Caldos.

Every dish at Taqueria Los Altos is prepared with traditional Jalisco techniques: freshly pressed corn tortillas, tender slow-braised meats, searing flat-top plancha grills, and vibrant roasted salsas made from scratch daily.

Street Tacos, Plancha Grills & Platillos

Tacos Callejeros (Street Tacos)

Served on warm double corn tortillas with chopped cilantro, diced onion, and fresh lime.

Tacos de Carnitas Michoacán $3.50 ea

Pork shoulder slow-braised for hours with citrus and spices until tender, crisped on the plancha, topped with fresh cilantro and white onion.

Tacos de Carne Asada $3.75 ea

Flank steak marinated in citrus and garlic, seared hard on the high-heat plancha, and finely diced with cilantro, onion, and fresh lime.

Tacos al Pastor con Piña $3.50 ea

Pork marinated in guajillo chile and achiote spice paste, griddled with sweet charred pineapple chunks, cilantro, and diced onion.

Tacos de Chorizo Casero $3.50 ea

Spicy Jalisco-style house-ground Mexican pork sausage seared to a savory crisp on the plancha with cilantro and diced onion.

Tacos de Pollo Asado $3.25 ea

Chicken breast steeped in citrus juice, cumin, and Mexican oregano, grilled juicy and chopped fine with fresh cilantro and lime.

Tacos de Lengua de Res $4.25 ea

Tender beef tongue simmered gently with bay leaf and aromatics until melt-in-your-mouth soft, finished on the flat-top with onions and cilantro.

Plancha Grill & Platillos Típicos

Complete dinner plates served with Mexican red rice, refried pinto beans, and warm tortillas.

Carne Asada Especial $16.95

Marinated thin-cut steak seared with charred whole spring onions (cebollitas), accompanied by Mexican red rice, refried beans, fresh avocado, and warm tortillas.

Chori Pollo Los Altos $15.50

Juicy grilled chicken breast topped with house Mexican chorizo and smothered in creamy melted queso blanco, served with rice and refried beans.

Fajitas Mixtas al Sarten $18.00

Sizzling platter of marinated steak, tender chicken, and shrimp sautéed with sweet bell peppers and onions, served with sour cream, guacamole, and tortillas.

Enchiladas Verdes Tradicionales $13.95

Three rolled corn tortillas filled with shredded chicken, submerged in tangy tomatillo salsa verde, cotija cheese, shredded lettuce, crema, and sliced avocado.

Caldo de Res con Verduras $14.50

Hearty homestyle Mexican beef soup simmered with bone-in beef shank, sweet corn, cabbage, carrots, zucchini, and chayote, served with cilantro and lime.

Menudo Rojo de Res (Weekends) $14.00

Traditional Mexican honeycomb tripe soup prepared in a fragrant red guajillo chile broth with hominy, served Saturday and Sunday with fresh oregano and onion.

Scratch Salsas & Traditional Beverages

Roasted salsas and refreshing Mexican drinks.

Agua Fresca de Horchata Casera $4.00

Traditional sweet Mexican rice milk beverage infused with Mexican canela cinnamon and vanilla, served iced.

Agua Fresca de Jamaica $4.00

Brewed hibiscus flower tea, lightly sweetened and refreshing with a tart, cranberry-like finish.

Mexican Glass Bottle Sodas $3.50

Imported Mexican Coca-Cola and Jarritos fruit sodas sweetened with 100% natural cane sugar.

Salsa Verde & Roja Trio Complimentary

House-roasted tomatillo salsa verde, fiery chile de árbol salsa roja, and fresh cilantro-lime pico de gallo served with warm tortilla chips.
