Highlands of Jalisco • Flat-Top Plancha

Centuries of Fire, Highland Traditions.

Los Altos de Jalisco is famous throughout Mexico for its rich agricultural heritage, pastoral livestock culture, and legendary roadside fondas. At Taqueria Los Altos on Monroe Road, we bring this vibrant culinary lineage to life every day, searing premium meats on our blistering plancha and honoring authentic cooking methods passed down through generations.

Culinary Standards

The Benchmarks of Our Jalisco Kitchen

Techniques that distinguish authentic regional cooking.

Standard 01

High-Heat Flat-Top Plancha

Maintaining cooking surfaces at over 450°F enables immediate searing, caramelizing meat surfaces while keeping the centers tender and full of natural juices.

Standard 02

Charred Cebollitas Cambray

We griddle whole Mexican green spring onions directly alongside our steaks until the outer skins blister sweet and tender, serving them as an essential aromatic companion.

Standard 03

Slow-Braised Carnitas

Our pork is simmered gently with Mexican citrus, garlic, and bay leaf until meltingly tender, then finished on the flat-top for irresistible crispy caramelized edges.
