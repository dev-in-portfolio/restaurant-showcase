East Charlotte • 8625 Monroe Road

Handmade Corn Tacos, Sizzling Plancha Grills.

Welcome to Taqueria Los Altos, East Charlotte’s authentic home for the regional culinary heritage of Los Altos de Jalisco. Located at 8625 Monroe Road, our kitchen crafts double-corn-tortilla street tacos loaded with slow-braised carnitas and plancha carne asada, sizzling skillets with charred cebollitas, and tangy tomatillo enchiladas verdes.

The Masa

Handmade Corn Tortillas

Pressed from pure nixtamalized corn masa and warmed on the plancha, yielding a fragrant, flexible double tortilla base that never tears under hearty fillings.

The Fire

Plancha Hearth Searing

Our high-heat flat-top plancha sears marinated flank steak, spicy house-ground chorizo, and citrus chicken while caramelizing sweet spring cebollitas.

Plancha Specialty

Carne Asada con Cebollitas

Our traditional Carne Asada plate features tender, seasoned flank steak seared hard on the plancha and topped with blistered green spring onions. Accompanied by Mexican red rice, slow-cooked refried beans with melted cheese, fresh avocado slices, and hot handmade corn tortillas.

Classic Mexican Comfort

Enchiladas Verdes con Aguacate

Three soft corn tortillas filled with shredded chicken or slow-simmered beef, bathed in our scratch roasted tomatillo salsa verde, and topped with shredded lettuce, crumbly cotija cheese, cool crema mexicana, and fresh sliced avocado.
