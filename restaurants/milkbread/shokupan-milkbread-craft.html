🍩 Tangzhong Method Science

The Art of Shokupan Milkbread

Japanese shokupan milkbread is famous worldwide for its unbelievable softness & delicate sweetness. At milkbread, we begin each dough batch with a cooked Tangzhong roux starter—locking in moisture so our donuts & buns stay impossibly soft, light, & pillowy.

Bakery Science

The 3 Pillars of Milkbread Donut Craft

1. The Tangzhong Roux Starter

Pre-cooking flour & milk into a thick starter roux gelatinizes starches, allowing dough to absorb & hold maximum moisture.

2. European Cultured Butter & Milk

Enriching dough with high-fat European butter, organic whole milk, & fresh egg yolks for a rich, tender crumb.

3. Scratch Vanilla Bean Glaze

Dipping hot, freshly fried donuts into real Madagascar vanilla bean glaze while warm for a translucent sugar shell.
