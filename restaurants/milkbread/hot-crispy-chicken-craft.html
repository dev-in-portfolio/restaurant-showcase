🍗 Buttermilk Crispy Chicken Science

The Art of Hot Crispy Chicken

Fried chicken requires contrast—a shattering crispy outer crust protecting juicy, flavorful tender chicken inside. At milkbread, our chicken is marinated in spiced buttermilk, dredged in seasoned flour, & fried to order before being paired with soft milkbread buns or biscuits.

Kitchen Craft

The 3 Pillars of Milkbread Crispy Chicken

1. 24-Hour Spiced Buttermilk Brine

Brining fresh boneless chicken thighs in garlic, black pepper, & buttermilk for tender, flavorful meat.

2. Double-Dredged Shatter Crust

Hand-dredging chicken twice in spiced flour to create craggy, extra-crunchy exterior nooks & crannies.

3. Scratch Milkbread Buns & Biscuits

Baking fresh shokupan buns & flaky milkbread biscuits continuously throughout the day for warm assembly.
