Fiesta Maya Mexican Restaurant relies on third-party delivery platforms and basic social pages. Located at 3507 David Cox Rd in North Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its sizzling lava stone molcajete craft, hand-pressed corn tortilla process, craft tequila cantina program, and family fiesta dinner reservation directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **North Charlotte Location:** 3507 David Cox Rd, Charlotte NC 28269 (North Charlotte / University City Corridor).
2. **Mayan & Authentic Mexican Entrees:** Sizzling Volcán Molcajete Bowl (arrachera steak, jumbo grilled shrimp, chorizo, panela cheese), Fajitas Mayas, Birria Tacos, Enchiladas Suizas, & Carnitas Michoacán.
3. **Cantina Bar & Fresh Agave:** Fresh lime craft margaritas, scratch guacamoles served in lava stone molcajete, jumbo horchata, & warm churros with cajeta caramel.

### Core Design Moves

1. **High-Contrast Display Serif Typography:** Dramatic high-contrast display serif (*Rozha One* / *Playfair Display*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and cantina mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Deep Obsidian & Mayan Jade Palette:** High-energy Mayan cantina palette anchored in deep obsidian (`#090A0F`), Mayan jade emerald (`#047857`), agave sunburst (`#EAB308`), molcajete terracotta (`#B45309`), and warm parchment (`#FFFBEB`).
3. **"The Lava Stone Molcajete & Tequila Cantina Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`molcajete-stone-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing sizzling volcanic stone bowls alongside authentic tacos, sizzling fajitas, & craft agave margaritas.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Felix Empanadas` — Warm Vibrant Argentine Street Food with Plus Jakarta Sans fonts and Terracotta Rust/Chimichurri Emerald.
2. `Exotica Indian Kitchen & Bar` — Royal Indian Fine Dining with Cinzel Decorative fonts and Imperial Navy/Copper Gold.
3. `Everybody Eatz` — Bold Southern Soul Food with Outfit fonts and Cajun Charcoal/Electric Amber.

### Divergent Choices for Fiesta Maya Mexican Restaurant

- **Hero Composition:** Opulent Mayan Stone & Sizzling Molcajete Vault split-hero layout: left side features a polished jade emerald stamp badge (`"NORTH CHARLOTTE MAYAN & MEXICAN CANTINA • 3507 DAVID COX RD"`), high-contrast display serif (*Rozha One*), deep obsidian & jade emerald palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of sizzling arrachera steak tacos, molcajete guacamole, fresh lime margarita, & tortilla chips.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Fiesta Maya Vault Cards** (`maya-vault-card`) and North Charlotte cantina highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`maya-header`, `maya-brand`, `mayan-hero-stage`, `jade-stamp-badge`, `maya-vault-card`, `maya-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
