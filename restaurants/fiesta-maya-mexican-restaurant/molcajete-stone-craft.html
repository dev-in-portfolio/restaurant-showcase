The Art of Molcajete Cooking

The crowning jewel of our kitchen is the Volcán Molcajete. Hand-carved from ancient Mexican volcanic basalt stone, each bowl is heated to 500°F to keep your arrachera steak, chorizo, & panela cheese sizzling at your table.

Molcajete Science

The 3 Pillars of Fiesta Maya Molcajete Craft

1. Hand-Carved Volcanic Basalt

Authentic Mexican basalt lava stone porous structure retains high heat & seasons meats with minerals.

2. 500°F Lava Stone Heat Seal

Preheated in specialized ovens so green tomatillo salsa bubbles continuously at your table for over 30 minutes.

3. Charred Scallion & Panela Crust

Panela cheese melts against the hot stone walls while green cebollitas scallions char into sweet perfection.
