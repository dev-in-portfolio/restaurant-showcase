Cantina Principles

The 3 Pillars of Fiesta Maya Mixology

1. 100% Blue Agave Spirits

We strictly pour authentic Jalisco Blue Weber agave tequilas & artisanal Oaxaca mezcals—zero artificial syrups.

2. Squeezed Daily Persian Limes

Fresh lime juice pressed continuously behind the bar for vibrant citrus acidity in every margarita.

3. Scratch Agave & Habanero Rims

House-blended Tajín chili lime salt, smoked volcanic salt, & habanero pepper rims for elevated spice contrast.
