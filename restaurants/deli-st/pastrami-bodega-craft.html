🥪 House Smoking Science

The Craft of House Pastrami

Real deli pastrami requires patience. We cure beef brisket in an aromatic spice brine for 7 days, coat it in cracked black pepper & toasted coriander, and smoke it low-and-slow over hickory wood for 12 hours.

Smoking Process

The 3 Pillars of Deli St Pastrami Craft

1. 7-Day Aromatic Spice Brine

Beef brisket steeps for a full week in sea salt, brown sugar, garlic, bay leaves, & juniper berries.

2. Cracked Pepper & Coriander Crust

Crusted heavily in freshly cracked black peppercorns & pan-roasted coriander seeds before smoking.

3. 12-Hour Hickory Low Smoke

Smoked at 225°F over North Carolina hickory logs until tender, juicy, & deeply aromatic.
