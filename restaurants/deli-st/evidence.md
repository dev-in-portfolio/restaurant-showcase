## Original Audit Weakness

Deli St relies on basic social links & basic ordering (`delistclt.com`). Located at 2801 Central Ave in Plaza Midwood, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its urban bodega deli culture, house-smoked deli meats, artisanal sandwich layering craft, and Plaza Midwood community vibe.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Plaza Midwood Location:** 2801 Central Ave, Charlotte NC 28205 (Plaza Midwood at Central Ave & N Kilborne Rd).
2. **Artisanal Bodega Sandwiches:** Stacked house-smoked pastrami on rye with swiss & deli mustard, turkey pesto paninis, chopped cheese bodega subs, vegan eggplant parm subs, and avocado egg hash browns.
3. **Craft Beverages & Bodega Vibe:** Nitro cold brew on tap, ceremonial matcha lattes, craft sodas, and neighborhood bodega deli counter atmosphere.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Punchy Urban Display Typography:** Bold urban display sans (*Bricolage Grotesque*) paired with refined body sans (*Outfit*) and deli ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Bodega Charcoal & Mustard Yellow Palette:** High-energy urban bodega palette anchored in deep bodega charcoal (`#121820`), mustard yellow (`#EAB308`), sriracha red (`#DC2626`), fresh basil green (`#16A34A`), and parchment paper white (`#F8FAF6`).
3. **"The House-Smoked Pastrami & Bodega Layering Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`pastrami-bodega-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing stacked deli sandwiches alongside bodega breakfast subs and specialty nitro espresso.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Curry Junction` — Regal Himalayan Saffron & Tandoori Copper with Marcellus fonts and Royal Plum/Saffron.
2. `CupLux Coffee Drive-Thru` — Modern vibrant drive-thru with Plus Jakarta Sans fonts and Freedom Navy/Yellow.
3. `Crisp Kitchen & Cocktails` — Modern midnight emerald lounge with DM Sans fonts and Amber Copper.

### Divergent Choices for Deli St

- **Hero Composition:** Urban Plaza Midwood Bodega & Artisanal Sandwich Vault split-hero layout: left side features a bright mustard yellow bodega stamp badge (`"PLAZA MIDWOOD CRAFT BODEGA & DELI • 2801 CENTRAL AVE"`), punchy urban display sans (*Bricolage Grotesque*), deep bodega charcoal & mustard yellow palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of stacked house-smoked pastrami on rye with swiss, pickles, and nitro cold brew.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Deli St Vault Cards** (`delist-vault-card`) and **Plaza Midwood Bodega Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`delist-header`, `bodega-brand`, `charcoal-hero-stage`, `mustard-stamp-badge`, `delist-vault-card`, `delist-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
