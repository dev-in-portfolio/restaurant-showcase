The 3 Pillars of Deli St Bodega Layering

1. Live Flat-Top Chopped Cheese

Fresh ground beef & sweet yellow onions seared and chopped right on the flat-top with melted American cheese.

2. Signature Bodega Mayo Sauce

In-house bodega sauce whipped with garlic, smoked paprika, fresh herbs, & apple cider vinegar.

3. Toasted Hero & Panini Bread

Local sourdough & Italian sub rolls toasted with garlic butter for a crispy outer shell and soft interior.
