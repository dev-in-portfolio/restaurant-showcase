Our Wok Craft & Uptown Story

Rooted in time-honored Chinese cooking traditions and shaped by the vibrant rhythm of Uptown Charlotte.

The Soho Bistro Philosophy

High-Flame Wok Cooking in the Heart of Charlotte

Located at 214 North Tryon Street, Soho Bistro was created to provide Charlotte residents, business leaders, and visitors with an elevated yet accessible Pan-Asian dining experience.

At the heart of our kitchen is the mastery of Wok Hei — the elusive "breath of the wok." When fresh ingredients encounter extreme heat in seasoned carbon-steel woks, flavors caramelize instantaneously, locking in crisp textures, tender moisture, and smoky undertones.

We make our dumplings by hand daily, prepare all sauces in small batches from scratch, and source premium proteins to ensure every plate meets our exacting standard.

Our Kitchen Standards

The 4 Pillars of Soho Bistro Quality

How our culinary team ensures exceptional flavor across every dish.

1. High-Heat Wok Hei

Intense searing at over 600 degrees creates smoky complexity that cannot be replicated with conventional cookware.

2. Scratch Sauces

Our General Tso's glaze, honey walnut citrus cream, and spicy Szechuan chili oils are blended in-house without preservatives.

3. Hand-Folded Dim Sum

Every potsticker and wonton is hand-rolled and stuffed each morning by our specialized dim sum culinary staff.

4. Uptown Speed & Care

Whether dining leisurely or grabbing an express lunch, our front-of-house team delivers warm, attentive, and efficient hospitality.
