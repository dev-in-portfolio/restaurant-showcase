# Evidence & Design Ledger: Soho Bistro

## Creative Brief
- **Client / Business:** Soho Bistro
- **Address:** 214 N Tryon St #110, Charlotte, NC 28202 (Uptown Charlotte)
- **Phone:** (704) 333-5189
- **Email:** info@sohobistroclt.com
- **Operating Hours:** Monday - Friday: 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM; Saturday: 4:30 PM - 9:30 PM; Sunday: Closed
- **Concept & Cuisine:** Uptown Charlotte's premier modern Chinese and Pan-Asian wok bistro on North Tryon Street, serving high-flame wok classics including Crispy Sesame Chicken, Honey Walnut Shrimp, Tangerine Peel Beef, Moo Shu Pork with handmade crepes, Singapore Curry Mei Fun, Beef Chow Fun, handmade pan-fried dumplings, crab wontons, steaming hot & sour soup, weekday express lunch combinations, and corporate office catering platters.
- **Design Philosophy:** Modern urban Asian bistro aesthetics combining imperial lacquered red, sleek midnight onyx, warm lantern gold, and crisp porcelain tones. Clean geometric cards, vibrant flavor badges, interactive corporate catering calculator, and zero emojis.

## Claim Ledger
- **High-Flame Wok Cooking (Wok Hei):** Master chefs executing traditional high-heat wok searing to lock in crisp vegetable textures, tender proteins, and complex smoky wok aroma.
- **Uptown Executive Lunch Tradition:** Fast, reliable 15-minute express weekday lunch combinations served with golden spring roll, choice of fried or steamed rice, and hot soup for Uptown business professionals.
- **Handmade Dim Sum & Starters:** Fresh pork and chicken potstickers, crispy golden crab wontons, and steamed edamame made fresh daily.
- **Corporate Catering & Office Buffets:** Comprehensive half-pan and full-pan wok platters, individually packaged executive bento boxes, and prompt Uptown desk/suite delivery.
