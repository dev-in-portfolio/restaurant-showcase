Volume Up, Pretenses Out.

VINYL is an unpretentious neighborhood bar in Charlotte’s South End, built around good music, an expertly curated cocktail program, and elevated street food. Consistently named one of the best bars in Charlotte, VINYL brings nostalgia to life with a giant wrap-around bar, resident DJs spinning nightly, and honest Queen City hospitality.

Kitchen Craft

Bar Food Turned Up a Level

Forget standard fried bar snacks. Our kitchen crafts vibrant culinary creations from scratch: grilled stuffed ribeye roll ups seared in beef fat, roasted cauliflower with bacon lardons and soft eggs, blistered shishito peppers with chili crisp aioli, and crispy fish & chips with salt-and-vinegar potatoes.

Industrial-Chic Lounge

Charlotte’s Only Live Flap Board

Step inside our South End haven to find rich brick walls, Biggie Smalls wallpaper, an iconic David Bowie mural, and Charlotte's only operating mechanical split flap board clicking through currently playing tracks. Roll-up garage doors open directly to South Tryon Street for effortless indoor-outdoor energy.
