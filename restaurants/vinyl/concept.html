Opened April 2022 • A B-Side Production

Your Neighborhood Bar, Remastered.

VINYL opened in April 2022 to answer a calling in South End: a genuine neighborhood haven where legendary rock and hip-hop spill through open garage doors, where bartenders shake world-class cocktails with zero snobbery, and where everyone feels at home.

Core Identity

The Elements of Our Space

London gastropub intimacy meets New York edge in the heart of South End.

The Sonic Vibe

Music You Can Feel

Inspired by the warmth of 1970s vinyl records and timeless artists—from Bowie to Bey and Bjork to Biggie. Our space features Biggie Smalls wallpaper, a Bowie mural, and vintage album art.

Unique Feature

Live Mechanical Flap Board

The only operating split-flap board in Charlotte, clicking dynamically throughout the night to display song titles, upcoming DJ sets, and kitchen specials above the bar.

Industry Credibility

USBG Guildhouse

VINYL is an official Founding Guildhouse of the United States Bartenders’ Guild, committed to ongoing bartender education, mentorship, and hospitality leadership.

The Creative Team

Collaborative Queen City Talent

VINYL’s identity is brought to life by passionate Charlotte hospitality leaders: General Manager Chris Singleton, acclaimed Culinary Director Vince Giancarlo, award-winning Music Director Justin Aswell, Chef de Cuisine Jacob Hujer, and Spirited Awards nominee Beverage Director Henry Schmulling.
