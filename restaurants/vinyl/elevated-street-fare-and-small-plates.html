Chef-Driven Gastropub Fare • South End

High-Fidelity Flavors, Late Night.

Under the culinary direction of Vince Giancarlo (Johnson & Wales, Beat Bobby Flay, Supperland) and Chef de Cuisine Jacob Hujer (Culinary Institute of America), VINYL transforms traditional Americana bar food into globally inspired, scratch-crafted dishes that redefine late-night dining.

Ingredient-First Philosophy

The Culinary Philosophy of VINYL

Local partnerships, whole-animal grinding, and uncompromising technique.

Signature Smash

House-Ground Ribeye & Brisket

We grind our own prime ribeye and brisket blend in-house for the signature VINYL Burger ($17), smashing patties hard on steel griddles to achieve caramelized crispy edges and juicy richness.

Local Bakery Roots

Artisan Bread Partnerships

Great sandwiches require exceptional bread. We partner with Charlotte’s own Golden Grains Bakery for custom brioche buns and Verdant Bread for naturally fermented sourdough and focaccia.

Late-Night Respect

Kitchen Open Until 1:00 AM

No frozen fryer shortcuts. Our kitchen serves our complete menu of chef-crafted burgers, roll ups, and hot garlic bread pizzas until 1:00 AM Monday through Wednesday and 10:00 PM Thursday through Sunday.

Weekend Daytime Ritual

Weekend Vinyl Brunch

On Saturdays and Sundays starting at 10:00 AM, our garage doors roll open for South End’s favorite music-infused brunch. Enjoy hot honey chicken biscuits, biscuits & gravy, steak & eggs featuring porcini-rubbed Nolan Ryan Ranch prime ribeye, and house bloody marys while DJs spin sunny soulful records.
