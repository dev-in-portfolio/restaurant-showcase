Open Daily, Spun Nightly.

Hospitality Options

Atmosphere & Policy

Vibe: Walk-ins welcome at the bar and communal tables. Table reservations available via Resy. Dog-friendly outdoor patio on South Tryon. Dress up or dress down—all are welcome.

Private Gatherings

Lounge Buyouts & Celebrations

Host your next event surrounded by great music and craft hospitality:

  • Corporate Receptions & Mixers: Full lounge buyouts or semi-private section reservations with custom cocktail menus and passed small plates.
  • Birthdays & Celebrations: Reserved booths, chef-curated charcuterie and flatbread displays, and dedicated DJ shoutouts.
  • Inquiries: Contact Hospitality Coordinator Alley Neyman by calling (980) 237-3893 or emailing admin@vinylclt.com.
Getting Here

Sidewalk Patio, Parking & Rail Trail Access

Visiting VINYL is seamless whether walking, taking transit, or driving:

  • Sidewalk Patio: Shaded outdoor cafe seating along South Tryon Street, welcoming well-behaved leashed dogs.
  • LYNX Light Rail: Located just a brief walk from the Bland Street and East/West Boulevard LYNX Blue Line stations.
  • Parking Options: Building deck parking and surrounding surface lots available throughout South End.
