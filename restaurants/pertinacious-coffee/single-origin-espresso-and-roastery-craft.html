The Science of the Perfect Cup

Great specialty coffee begins at origin and culminates at the bar. We partner with ethical farming cooperatives in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Central America, roasting in small batches to preserve terroir and dialing in each extraction to the tenth of a gram every single morning.

Roastery Principles

The 3 Pillars of Pertinacious Roasting Mastery

1. Ethical Micro-Lot Sourcing

Purchasing direct-trade coffees from farmers who practice sustainable, shade-grown agricultural methods and pay equitable living wages.

2. Small-Batch Roast Profiling

Developing roast curves that enhance inherent regional flavors—from jasmine and bergamot in Ethiopian washed beans to brown sugar in Colombian naturals.

3. Dialed-In Morning Calibration

Testing water mineral content, burr grind sizes, and water temperatures to ensure every shot pulls with rich golden crema and clean balance.
