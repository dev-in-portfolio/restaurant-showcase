Precision Espresso Micro-Lot Roast

Single-Origin Double Shot Pure Extraction

A double espresso extracted at 9 bars of pressure from rotating seasonal single-origin micro-lots, highlighting bright citrus, stone fruit sweetness, and chocolate finish.

Australian Style Flat White (6oz) Barista Favorite

Double shot of espresso married with velvety, micro-foamed whole milk (or organic oat milk) for an ultra-creamy, coffee-forward cup.

Traditional Cortado (4oz) Balanced Ratio

Equal parts rich espresso and silky steamed milk served in a classic Gibraltar glass to cut acidity while honoring the origin profile.

18-Hour Slow Steep Cold Brew Crisp & Bold

Coarsely ground specialty beans steeped in cold filtered water for 18 hours, resulting in an exceptionally smooth, low-acid iced coffee with chocolate notes.

Botanical Lattes & Tea House Syrups

Brown Sugar Cardamom Latte #1 Signature Drink

Double espresso and velvety oat milk infused with our house-simmered dark brown sugar syrup steeped with freshly ground green cardamom pods.

Bourbon Madagascar Vanilla Latte Scratch Vanilla

Real whole Madagascar vanilla beans slow-simmered with organic cane sugar, combined with espresso and steamed milk, dusted with raw turbinado sugar.

Ceremonial Grade Uji Matcha Latte Japanese Matcha

First-harvest ceremonial green tea powder from Uji, Kyoto, whisked with hot water and micro-foamed milk with a touch of wildflower lavender honey.

Artisan Morning Pastries Daily Bakery

Freshly baked butter croissants, seasonal fruit galettes, almond scones, and vegan gluten-free morning muffins delivered fresh daily from local Charlotte bakeries.
