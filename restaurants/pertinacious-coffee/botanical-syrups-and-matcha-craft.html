🌿 Scratch Botanical Infusions & Mobile Bar

Whole Botanicals, Zero Shortcuts.

We believe flavored lattes should enhance espresso rather than mask it with artificial corn syrups. Our house-made syrup lab simmers raw organic botanicals, crushed cardamom pods, and whole vanilla beans in small weekly batches. We also bring our mobile espresso cart directly to Charlotte weddings and private events.

Syrup & Beverage Lab

The 3 Pillars of Pertinacious Beverage Craft

1. 100% Whole Food Ingredients

Simmering crushed green cardamom, whole split Madagascar vanilla pods, organic cane sugar, and raw honey with zero chemical flavorings.

2. First-Harvest Uji Matcha

Whisking shade-grown ceremonial Japanese green tea powder to order for rich antioxidant vibrancy, natural sweetness, and silky texture.

3. Mobile Espresso Cart Catering

Equipped with commercial dual-group machines to provide full-service specialty espresso bars at Queen City weddings, parties, and corporate galas.
