🏛 The Pertinacious South End Story

Tenacity in Every Roast.

To be "pertinacious" is to hold firmly and stubbornly to an opinion, purpose, or course of action. At Pertinacious Coffee, that unwavering determination is poured into our craft. Located at 1200 S Graham St inside South End's creative hub, we refuse shortcuts in roasting, extraction, and whole-ingredient syrup creation.

Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of Pertinacious Coffee

Persistent Quality

Refusing commercial pre-packaged concentrates and stale mass roasts in favor of freshly roasted micro-lot coffees and scratch syrups.

Community Hospitality

Creating an inviting, inclusive space where coffee newcomers and espresso purists alike feel genuinely celebrated and welcomed.

Sustainable Farmer Support

Honoring the hardworking coffee producers across the globe by maintaining direct relationships and highlighting bean origin on every bag.
