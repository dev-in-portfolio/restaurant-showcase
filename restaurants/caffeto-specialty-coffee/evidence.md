## Original Audit Weakness

Caffeto Specialty Coffee relies on third-party Toast Tab links (`toasttab.com`) and basic social profiles (`caffetospecialtycoffee.com`). Located at 3012 N Davidson St in NoDa, it lacks a high-craft digital showcase highlighting its single-origin Colombian bean origins, direct-trade espresso roasts, signature pandebono baking, and mobile trailer culture.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **NoDa Davidson St & Cornelius Locations:** Permanent coffee trailer at 3012 N Davidson St, Charlotte NC 28205 (NoDa Arts District) and Lake Norman expansion at 20910 Torrence Chapel Rd, Cornelius NC.
2. **Direct-Trade Single-Origin Colombian Roast:** Colombian microlot specialty beans, cold brews, cardamom lattes, strawberry & mango matcha, and coconut lemonade (*Limonada de Coco*).
3. **Fresh Baked Pandebono & Pastries:** Warm Colombian pandebono (cheese bread), cachitos, croissants, danishes, and fresh-ground retail bags.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Botanical Colombian Typography:** Warm editorial serif display (*Lora*) paired with modern organic sans (*Outfit*) and technical coffee mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Terracotta & Panela Palette:** Rich Andean roastery palette anchored in deep roasted espresso (`#23120A`), terracotta clay (`#B85B35`), Andean mint (`#3E6B5A`), warm panela sugar (`#F4E6D2`), and crisp oat milk cream (`#FDFBF7`).
3. **"The Single-Origin Roast & Pandebono Bake Matrix":** Asymmetrical grid layout (`roast-craft.html` & `menu.html`) pairing specialty espresso beverages with warm Colombian bakery treats.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Babaloo Coffee Club` — Warm golden Cuban retro espresso vibe with brown background.
2. `Buona Vita Pub & Pizzeria` — Tuscan hearth terra cotta brick tavern with Playfair serif display.
3. `DeepCuts HiFi` — Audiophile vinyl black journal layout with Syne/Space Mono fonts.

### Divergent Choices for Caffeto Specialty Coffee

- **Hero Composition:** Modern Andean Colombian Espresso Bar split-hero layout: left side features an organic terracotta stamp badge (`"COLOMBIAN DIRECT TRADE • NODA N DAVIDSON"`), sleek botanical serif display typography (*Lora*), deep roast & warm cane sugar palette, and right side features an arch-framed hero image of poured latte art and pandebono.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Roaster Specialty Cards** (`roaster-card`) and **NoDa Community Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`caffeto-header`, `colombian-brand`, `andean-hero-stage`, `clay-stamp-badge`, `roaster-card`, `caffeto-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
