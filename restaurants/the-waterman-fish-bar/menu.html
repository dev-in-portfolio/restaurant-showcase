Fresh Raw Bar & Chargrilled Oysters

Flown in daily from Atlantic cold waters and the Gulf. Shucked fresh to order.

Fresh Shucked Boutique Oysters $16.00 / $30.00

Half dozen or full dozen raw oysters served on crushed ice with shallot red wine mignonette, spicy house cocktail sauce, grated horseradish, and lemon wedges.

$1.50 Happy Hour House Oysters $1.50 Each

Our celebrated weekday special. Freshly shucked raw house oysters on ice, available dine-in only Monday through Friday from 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM.

Chargrilled Carolina Oysters (Half Dozen) $18.00

Broiled over an open flame until bubbling hot, topped with roasted garlic herb butter, shredded parmesan cheese, and toasted herb breadcrumbs.

Chilled Jumbo Shrimp Cocktail $15.00

Five colossal wild Atlantic shrimp poached in Old Bay citrus court bouillon, chilled and served with fiery horseradish cocktail sauce and charred lemon.

Coastal Entrees, Sandwiches & Fryer Baskets

Scratch-prepared coastal favorites and fresh seafood specialties.

The Classic Maine Lobster Roll $26.50

Sweet cold-water lobster knuckle and claw meat, lightly dressed in lemon-chive aioli or served warm with drawn butter, tucked in a griddled split brioche bun with sea salt kettle chips.

Crispy Beer-Battered Fish & Chips $18.95

Flaky North Atlantic whitefish fillet fried in light beer batter, served with hand-cut fries, scratch sweet caper tartar sauce, fresh lemon, and warm hush puppies.

Voodoo Crispy Shrimp $14.95

Jumbo crispy fried wild shrimp tossed in a sweet and spicy sriracha glaze, sprinkled with toasted sesame seeds and fresh scallions, served with lime.

Blackened Atlantic Salmon $22.50

Fresh salmon fillet dusted in spicy Creole blackening seasoning, cast-iron seared crisp, served over garlic roasted seasonal vegetables and lemon herb compound butter.

Cajun Seafood Pasta $21.95

Sautéed wild shrimp and tender sea scallops tossed in a rich, spicy Cajun cream sauce with penne pasta, blistered tomatoes, and toasted garlic baguette.

Lowcountry Seafood Gumbo $9.50 / $14.50

Rich dark roux stewed with Gulf shrimp, smoked andouille sausage, tender okra, and sweet bell peppers, served over steamed long-grain white rice.

Topside Rooftop Cocktails & Coastal Drafts

Handcrafted for warm Charlotte evenings and rooftop sunsets.

The Waterman Painkiller $13.50

Topside Coastal Spritz $12.00

Aperol, sparkling dry prosecco, fresh ruby red grapefruit juice, and sparkling soda water garnished with a grapefruit wedge.

Carolina Coastal Craft Drafts $6.50 – $8.50
