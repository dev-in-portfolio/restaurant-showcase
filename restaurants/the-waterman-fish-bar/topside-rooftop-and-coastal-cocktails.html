Second-Story Open-Air Deck • South End Skyline

Rooftop Breezes, Tropical Libations.

Ascend the stairs to Topside, The Waterman's breezy second-story rooftop lounge. Outfitted with teak loungers, coastal botanical accents, and panoramic views of the South End skyline, Topside is Charlotte's favorite destination for golden hour frozen painkillers, effervescent spritzes, and sunset seafood baskets.

Bar Craft

The Elements of the Topside Experience

Tropical flavor profiles, crisp effervescence, and open-air Carolina hospitality.

The Signature

The Waterman Painkiller

Authentic Pusser's British Navy rum shaken with rich cream of coconut, fresh pineapple, and orange juice, topped with fresh-grated nutmeg over pebble ice.

The Sunset

Botanical Spritzes

Effervescent aperitifs combining Italian bitter cordials, fresh citrus reductions, sparkling prosecco, and soda water to pair perfectly with raw oysters.

The Draught

Carolina Craft Taps

Refreshing coastal lagers, easy-drinking kolsches, and juicy hazies sourced from independent North Carolina microbrewers along the coast and mountains.

All-Weather Comfort

Year-Round Open-Air Dining

Whether cooling off under summer ceiling fans with an icy cocktail or staying toasty during autumn evenings under our overhead heaters and clear weather enclosures, Topside is engineered for comfort throughout all four seasons in Charlotte.
