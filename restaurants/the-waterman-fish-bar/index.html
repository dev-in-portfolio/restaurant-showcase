Welcome to The Waterman Fish Bar, South End’s favorite neighborhood seafood sanctuary. From boutique Carolina oysters shucked over crystal ice at our bustling raw bar to golden Maine lobster rolls, crispy beer-battered fish and chips, and tropical craft libations atop our second-story open-air rooftop, we bring authentic coastal tavern spirit to South Boulevard.

Signature Specialty

Classic Maine Lobster Roll

We source sweet knuckle and claw lobster meat directly from cold North Atlantic waters. Gently tossed in light lemon-herb mayo or warmed in melted butter, then packed generously into a buttered, griddled split-top brioche bun and paired with house sea salt kettle chips.

Tavern Classic

Beer-Battered Fish & Chips

Thick fillets of flaky North Atlantic whitefish dipped in light, crisp beer batter and fried golden crisp. Served piping hot alongside hand-cut seasoned fries, scratch sweet caper tartar sauce, fresh lemon, and warm hush puppies.
