Atlantic Provenance • Daily Shucking

Cold Water Brine, Hand-Shucked Precision.

Oysters are the beating heart of The Waterman. We partner with sustainable boutique mariculture farms along the North Carolina Outer Banks, Pamlico Sound, and the Virginia Capes. Our shuckers inspect every bivalve, serving them glistening on crushed ice with house red wine mignonette, fresh horseradish, and lemon wedges.

The Foundations of Our Raw Bar

Strict cold-chain logistics, regional farmer partnerships, and culinary balance.

Foundation 01

Carolina Farm Sourcing

We champion regional North Carolina oyster growers, highlighting seasonal harvests with distinct salinity profiles ranging from ocean-briny to buttery-sweet.

Foundation 02

Hand-Shucked to Order

No pre-shucking or holding. Every oyster is popped at our raw bar counter right before your eyes, preserving the precious liquor inside the deep cup.

Foundation 03

$1.50 Weekday Happy Hour

Charlotte's premier oyster ritual: $1.50 house oysters every Monday through Friday from 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM for dine-in guests at the bar or patio.

Open Flame Flavor

Chargrilled Carolina Oysters

For guests who prefer cooked oysters or crave bubbling richness, our chargrilled oysters are legendary. Cooked over direct heat until the shells smoke, each oyster is bathed in rich roasted garlic butter, aged parmesan cheese, fresh herbs, and crispy breadcrumbs. Served bubbling hot with grilled crusty French bread for dipping.
