Weekday Ritual

$1.50 Oyster Happy Hour Directives

Guidelines for enjoying Charlotte's premier weekday raw bar special:

  • Days & Hours: Monday through Friday from 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM promptly.
  • Dine-In Exclusive: Available exclusively for dine-in guests seated at the raw bar, main dining room, or patio. Takeout is not permitted for happy hour pricing.
  • Accompaniments: Served chilled over fresh crushed ice with house red wine shallot mignonette, cocktail sauce, grated horseradish, and lemon wedges.
Rooftop Protocol

Topside Rooftop Directives & Parking

Essential guidance for visiting our second-story rooftop deck:

  • Seating Policy: Topside operates primarily on a walk-in, first-come, first-served basis. Full food and beverage service is available at all rooftop high-tops and bar seats.
  • Dedicated Parking: A complimentary guest parking lot is located directly on-site adjacent to the building off South Boulevard. Street parking is also available along surrounding side streets.
  • Transit Accessibility: Located within easy walking distance of the LYNX Blue Line New Bern Light Rail Station.
