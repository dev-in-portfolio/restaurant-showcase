Honoring Coastal Traditions • Founded 2018

Born of Saltwater, Built for South End.

The term "waterman" refers to the generational coastal fishermen who make their living on the open waters of North Carolina’s Outer Banks and the Chesapeake Bay. Founded in 2018 along South Boulevard, The Waterman Fish Bar was built to honor these hardworking maritime stewards by serving clean, unpretentious seafood in a vibrant neighborhood setting.

Core Principles

The Values Guiding The Waterman

Commitment to fishery sustainability, scratch cooking, and communal warmth.

Principle 01

Sustainable Shell Recycling

Every oyster shell consumed at The Waterman is collected and returned to North Carolina coastal waters through shell recycling programs to rebuild living reefs.

Principle 02

Fresh Cold-Chain Logistics

We bypass lengthy middleman warehousing. Our seafood arrives daily on ice directly from trusted coastal docks and boutique certified mariculture farms.

Principle 03

Welcoming Neighborhood Hub

Whether coming off the light rail for a quick dozen oysters or celebrating milestones atop the Topside rooftop, our team delivers warm Carolina hospitality.

Expanding Footprint

From South End to Lake Norman & CLT Airport

Following the overwhelming success of our original South End location, The Waterman has expanded its coastal footprint with a second lakeside location in Cornelius at Lake Norman, as well as a bustling traveler's oasis inside Charlotte Douglas International Airport (Concourse C, Gate 13).
