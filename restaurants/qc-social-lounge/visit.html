Admission

Age & ID Requirements

QC Social Lounge is strictly a 21+ establishment for all operating hours. A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (Driver's License, State ID, Military ID, or Passport) is required at entry. Digital photos or paper copies are not accepted.

Atmosphere Standards

Nightlife Dress Code Directives

To preserve our boutique atmosphere, QC Social Lounge enforces an upscale fashionable dress code at the discretion of door hosts:

  • Encouraged: Chic nightlife attire, collared shirts, blazers, fashionable fitted denim, upscale sneakers, and dresses.
  • Prohibited: Athletic jerseys, gym shorts, sweatpants, flip flops, work boots, plain white undershirts, and unauthorized headwear.
Transit & Parking

Uptown Parking & Transit Options

Convenient parking and transit access surrounds our 300 N College St address:

  • 7th Street Station Parking Deck: Located directly across the street at 225 E 6th St. Ample covered multi-level parking.
  • Center City Surface Lots: Paid surface lots available along N College St and E 7th St.
  • LYNX Blue Line Light Rail: Disembark at 7th Street Station for an immediate 1-minute walk to our alley entrance.
  • Rideshare Drop-off: Set destination to 300 N College St for curb drop-off directly adjacent to the alley walkway.
