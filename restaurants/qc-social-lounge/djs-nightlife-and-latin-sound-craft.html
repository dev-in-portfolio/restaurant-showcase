Each evening at QC Social Lounge delivers a distinct musical journey engineered for high-energy movement.

Technical Specifications

Concert-Grade Sound & Intelligent Lighting

We believe great nightlife demands uncompromising fidelity. QC Social Lounge features custom acoustic baffling to prevent harsh flutter echoes, dual high-excursion subwoofers delivering chest-thumping low-end warmth without ear fatigue, and a synchronized DMX lighting rig featuring moving-head spotlights, amber strobes, and perimeter LED washes.
