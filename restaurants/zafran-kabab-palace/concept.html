Heritage • Pure Spices • Charlotte NC

Royal Traditions, Uncompromising Halal.

At Zafran Kabab Palace, we honor centuries of culinary artistry passed down through generations of master ustaads in Lahore, Karachi, and Peshawar. Our name 'Zafran' (Saffron) represents purity, luxury, and the golden spice that graces our royal banquet dishes.

Zabiha Halal

Every cut of beef, goat, lamb, and chicken used in our kitchen is 100% certified Zabiha Halal. We maintain strict separation and uncompromising standards for peace of mind.

In-House Milling

Freshly Ground Spices

We roast and grind whole coriander, cumin, star anise, mace, and nutmeg in small weekly batches to ensure potent essential oils and deep fragrant aromas in every curry.
