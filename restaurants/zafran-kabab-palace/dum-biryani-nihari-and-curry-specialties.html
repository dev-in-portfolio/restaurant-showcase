The Hallmarks of Our Stews & Rice

Slow simmering unlocks the deepest flavors of bone marrow, whole cardamom, and saffron.

8-Hour Simmer

Royal Beef Nihari

Tender cuts of bone-in beef shank slow-cooked in a deeply spiced marrow broth scented with fennel, dry ginger, and star anise ($21.99). Served with hot ginger juliennes and lime.

Iron Wok Mastery

Peshawari Lamb Karahi

Cooked in heavy iron karahis without heavy onion paste. We flash-cook tender bone-in lamb with fresh tomatoes, ginger root, green chilies, and coarse black pepper ($24.99).

Aged Basmati

Mutton Dum Biryani

Sealed with dough to trap the aromatic steam. Saffron-dyed basmati rice absorbs the savory juices of spiced tender goat meat ($16.15), served with cooling raita.

Classic Comfort

Chicken Tikka Masala & Butter Chicken

Our butter chicken ($18.99) and chicken tikka masala ($19.10) start inside the 800-degree clay oven. The smoky char on the chicken pieces pairs perfectly with our rich, silky tomato-butter gravy and aromatic crushed fenugreek leaves.
