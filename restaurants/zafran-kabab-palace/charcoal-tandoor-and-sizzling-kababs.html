800° Charcoal Tandoor • 100% Zabiha Halal

Smoky Charcoal, Tender Skewers.

The heartbeat of Zafran Kabab Palace is our authentic clay tandoor. Fueled by natural hardwood lump charcoal reaching temperatures exceeding 800 degrees, our skewers flash-roast in minutes, sealing in rich natural juices while searing a tantalizing smoky crust on the exterior.

Bihari Tradition

Raw Papaya & Mustard Oil

For our famous Beef Bihari ($18.99), tender beef strips are tenderized naturally with green raw papaya enzymes, pungent mustard oil, roasted crushed coriander, and warm allspice.

Ludhiana & Lahore

Hung Curd & Kashmiri Chili

Used on our Chicken Tikka and Tandoori Lamb Chops ($28.99). Strained yogurt locks in moisture while vibrant Kashmiri red chilies provide a rich crimson color without blistering heat.

Mughlai Luxury

Malai Cream & Cardamom

Our Chicken Malai Boti ($16.99) is soaked in heavy dairy cream, green cardamom powder, white pepper, and fresh garlic for an ultra-mild, velvety melt-in-the-mouth texture.

The Palace Mix Grill Platter

Designed for shared family feasts ($45.89 / serves 2-3). A sizzling mountain of juicy chicken tikka boti, tender lamb chops, spicy beef seekh kababs, and beef bihari served over a bed of fragrant spiced Afghani pulao rice with cool mint-coriander raita chutney.
