Stadium Experience

Game Day Viewing Directives

Directives for enjoying sporting events at The Lodge:

  • First-Come Seating: Bar and high-top seating is first-come, first-served. We recommend arriving 45 minutes before kickoff for Carolina Panthers games and marquee college football Saturdays.
  • Broadcast Sound: Main audio is piped through our surround system for premier local games, with individual table audio available upon request for secondary games.
  • Group Bookings: Parties of 8 or more can request booth reservations by calling (704) 544-5226 at least 24 hours in advance.
Culinary Traditions

Weekly Specials & Traditions

Directives for our celebrated South Charlotte weekly food features:

  • Meatloaf Monday: Our homestyle beef and pork meatloaf with brown sugar glaze, garlic mashed potatoes, and gravy ($15.95) served all day Monday while supplies last.
  • Thursday Rib Night: Full racks of hickory-smoked St. Louis ribs served with fries and slaw at special neighborhood pricing.
  • Sunday Morning Kickoff: Doors open early at 10:00 AM every Sunday for early NFL action, bloody marys, and brunch specials.
