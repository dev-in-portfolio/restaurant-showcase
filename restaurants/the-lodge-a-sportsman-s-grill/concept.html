Colony Place Anchor • South Charlotte

Mountain Heritage, South Charlotte Community.

The Lodge was conceived as an escape to the Blue Ridge mountains without leaving South Charlotte. Inspired by historic Appalachian hunting lodges and sports taverns, we built a hearth with natural stone, rough timber beams, and warm leather where neighbors can gather for unpretentious, scratch-made tavern food and championship sports.

Core Values

The Ethos That Drives The Lodge

Commitment to scratch cooking, genuine Southern hospitality, and neighborhood camaraderie.

Value 01

Scratch Kitchen Integrity

From our fresh-ground burger patties and smoked ribs to handmade pimento cheese and ranch dressings, we never rely on frozen industrial shortcuts.

Value 02

Authentic Lodge Warmth

A comfortable rustic atmosphere with comfortable leather booths, stone fireplaces, and an expansive bar where regulars are greeted by name.

Value 03

Charlotte Sports Culture

Hosting local alumni chapters, fantasy football drafts, Panthers game watches, and family team banquets with enthusiastic game-day service.

Community Gatherings

Heated Patio & Bar Seating

Whether sitting by our outdoor heated fireplace on a crisp autumn evening with a bourbon neat or gathering ten friends along the main horseshoe bar to watch March Madness, The Lodge offers versatile dining spaces suited for every occasion.
