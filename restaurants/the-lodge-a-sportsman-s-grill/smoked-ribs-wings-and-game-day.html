The Elements of Our Game Day Kitchen

Low temperatures, clean hardwood smoke, and double-fried crispy textures.

Element 01

Hickory Hardwood

We use only native North Carolina hickory logs to generate sweet, aromatic smoke without the acrid bitterness of inferior charcoal pellets.

Element 02

Brown Sugar Glaze

Basted in the final 30 minutes of smoking with our house sauce, caramelizing the natural sugars into a sticky, mahogany bark that locks in juiciness.

Element 03

Double-Fried Wings

Jumbo chicken wings are rendered slowly first, then flash-fried at high heat upon order to achieve shattering crispness before tossing in sauce.
