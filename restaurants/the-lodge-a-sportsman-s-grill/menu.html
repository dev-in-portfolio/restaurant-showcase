Hearty Sportsman Fare

Scratch Craftsmanship, Generous Mountain Portions.

Every dish at The Lodge is built around generous portions, bold seasoning, and authentic scratch preparation. From our Carolina pasture-raised bison burgers to hickory-smoked pork ribs and fresh bacon-wrapped pimento jalapeno poppers, our kitchen fuels game day gatherings and family dinners across South Charlotte.

The Sportsman Carolina Bison Burger $17.95

Lean pasture-raised Carolina ground bison patty seared medium on the flat-top, topped with melted sharp cheddar, caramelized sweet onions, applewood bacon, and lodge burger sauce on a toasted brioche bun.

The Hearth Angus Burger $15.50

Half-pound fresh Angus beef patty grilled to order with aged Wisconsin cheddar, crisp leaf lettuce, vine-ripe tomato, kosher dill pickles, and shaved red onion on a toasted potato roll.

Pimento Bacon Jam Burger $16.50

Fresh Angus patty crowned with house-made sharp pimento cheese, smoky slow-cooked bacon onion jam, and crispy fried tobacco onions on a toasted brioche bun.

Blackened Mahi Fish Sandwich $16.95

Gulf mahi fillet dusted in Cajun blackening spices, seared cast-iron crisp, served with zesty Creole remoulade, shredded lettuce, and ripe tomato on a toasted brioche roll.

Pesto Grilled Chicken Wrap $14.95

Herb-marinated grilled chicken breast, fresh basil walnut pesto, roasted red peppers, and melted aged provolone rolled in a toasted garlic herb flour tortilla.

Black Bean Garden Veggie Burger $14.50

Smokehouse Ribs, Wings & Daily Specials

Slow hickory-smoked meats and game day tavern classics.

Slow-Smoked St. Louis Ribs (Full Rack) $26.95

Dry-rubbed pork ribs smoked low and slow over hickory hardwood, basted in sweet tangy Carolina barbecue glaze, served with french fries and house coleslaw. (Half Rack available for $18.95).

Jumbo Crispy Chicken Wings (10 / 20 Wings) $15.95 / $28.95

Fried to order with a crisp crust and juicy meat. Choice of sauce: House Dry Rub, Classic Buffalo, Honey Habanero, Garlic Parmesan, or Sweet Teriyaki, served with celery and blue cheese.

Famous Meatloaf Monday Special $15.95

Thick cut of homestyle beef and pork meatloaf baked with savory herbs, glazed in brown sugar tomato reduction, served over roasted garlic red-skin mashed potatoes with brown gravy.

Blackened Fish Tacos (3 Tacos) $15.50

Warm flour tortillas stuffed with blackened fish, shredded cabbage slaw, fresh pico de gallo, cotija cheese, and cilantro lime crema, served with tortilla chips and house salsa.
