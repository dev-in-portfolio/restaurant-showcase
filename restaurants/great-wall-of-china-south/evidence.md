## Original Audit Weakness

Great Wall of China South relies on basic template delivery links and third-party portal listings. Located at 6666 Carmel Rd in South Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its authentic high-heat wok craft, house specialty recipes, family dining room, and private party catering.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Carmel Commons Location:** 6666 Carmel Rd, Charlotte NC 28226 (Carmel Commons / South Charlotte).
2. **Authentic Cantonese, Hunan & Sichuan Entrees:** General Tso's Chicken, Crispy Sesame Beef, Peking Duck, House Special Lo Mein, Steamed Pork Dumplings, Szechuan Crispy Shredded Beef, & Hand-Folded Wontons.
3. **Dining Room Hours:** Mon-Thurs 11:30am-9pm, Fri 11:30am-9:30pm, Sat 12pm-9:30pm, Sun 11:30am-9pm.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Classic Serif Typography:** Elegant classic serif (*Playfair Display*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and wok kitchen mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Imperial Jade Onyx & Crimson Red Palette:** Rich oriental culinary palette anchored in imperial jade onyx (`#0C130F`), imperial crimson red (`#B91C1C`), imperial gold (`#D97706`), bamboo green (`#15803D`), and silk parchment cream (`#FDFBF7`).
3. **"The Wok Hei & Dim Sum Gastronomy Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`wok-hei-cantonese-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing 700°F wok-hei Cantonese entrees alongside handcrafted steamed pork dumplings & Peking duck.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `El Veneno` — Urban Mexican Street Tacos with Unbounded fonts and Dark Obsidian/Habanero Red.
2. `Dee’s Vegan To Go` — 100% Plant-Based Comfort with Plus Jakarta Sans fonts and Forest Charcoal/Kale Emerald.
3. `Curry n Cake` — Indian Street Food & Fusion Bakery with Fraunces fonts and Saffron Charcoal/Royal Amber.

### Divergent Choices for Great Wall of China South

- **Hero Composition:** Classic Cantonese & Sichuan Wok Gastronomy Vault split-hero layout: left side features an imperial crimson red stamp badge (`"CLASSIC CANTONESE & SICHUAN WOK CRAFT • 6666 CARMEL RD"`), classic serif (*Playfair Display*), jade onyx & crimson red palette, and right side features an elegant framed hero image of sizzling wok dishes, dim sum, & crispy sesame beef.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **GW Vault Cards** (`gw-vault-card`) and Carmel Commons culinary highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`gw-header`, `gw-brand`, `jade-hero-stage`, `dragon-stamp-badge`, `gw-vault-card`, `gw-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
