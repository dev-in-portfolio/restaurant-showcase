🧱 700°F Wok Hei Flame Sear

The Art of Wok Hei Searing

True Cantonese stir-fry requires the elusive "breath of the wok" (Wok Hei). At Great Wall of China South, our chefs toss meats & vegetables over 700°F jet flame burners, caramelizing natural sugars in seconds.

Wok Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Wok Hei Cooking

1. 700°F High-Heat Sear

Jet burners heat hand-pounded iron woks past 700°F, vaporizing oil droplets & infusing smoky wok aroma.

2. Velvet Meat Marinade

Beef, chicken, & pork are tenderized with rice wine & cornstarch, locking in juiciness during high-speed searing.

3. Flash-Tossed Aromatics

Fresh scallions, ginger root, garlic, & dark soy are bloomed in hot oil for seconds before glazing the dish.
