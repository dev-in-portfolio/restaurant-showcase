🍲 Málà Szechuan Spice Craft

The Power of Szechuan Chili

For lovers of bold heat, our kitchen uses authentic Szechuan red peppercorns & roasted facing-heaven chili peppers. This creates the coveted *Málà* (numbing & spicy) flavor profile that defines southwestern Chinese gastronomy.

Spice Science

The 3 Pillars of Szechuan & Hunan Heat

1. Numbing Peppercorn (Má)

Hand-crushed red Szechuan peppercorns release essential oils that create a gentle numbing citrus tingling sensation.

2. Chili Bean Paste (Toban Djan)

Fermented broad beans & chili peppers form the deep, savory umami backbone of our spicy Szechuan glazes.

3. Hunan Dry Chili Sear

Hunan dishes feature whole roasted chili pods, garlic, & black beans for direct, fiery pepper heat.
