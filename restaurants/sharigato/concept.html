Gratitude For Rice • Tokyo Counter Soul

The Spirit of Shari & Arigato.

In Japanese culinary tradition, the true master sushi chef is judged not by the fish, but by the "shari"—the seasoned, body-temperature rice that forms the foundation of every bite. Our name unites "Shari" with "Arigato" (gratitude), celebrating the sacred grain, the farmers of Japan's Ariake Sea, and the diners who gather around our 22nd Street wooden counter.

The Sharigato Philosophy

Three Principles That Anchor Our Counter

Mindful pacing, direct chef-to-guest connection, and ingredient reverence.

Principle 01

Demystifying Sushi

We eliminate the stiffness and pretension often associated with high-end omakase. Sitting at our counter offers an unpretentious, accessible, and deeply interactive dining experience.

Principle 02

Intimate Wooden Sanctuary

Constructed with warm hinoki cedar surfaces, soft architectural lighting, and minimalist acoustics, our dining room removes outside distractions so you can focus on pure sensory taste.

Principle 03

Rooted In Charlotte

Located in the vibrant 22nd Street / Area 15 creative corridor between Optimist Park, Belmont, and NoDa, we are proud to introduce Charlotte to genuine Japanese hand-roll culture.

Wabi-Sabi Aesthetics

Beauty In Simplicity & Momentary Perfection

A hand roll exists in its ideal state for only thirty seconds. This fleeting culinary moment embodies the Japanese concept of "ichi-go ichi-e"—treasuring an unrepeatable encounter. Each roll is crafted by hand, accepted with gratitude, and enjoyed immediately.
