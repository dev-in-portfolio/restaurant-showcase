Optimist Park / Area 15 • Handed One By One

Warm Shari, Shattering Nori.

Welcome to Sharigato, Charlotte’s dedicated Japanese hand-roll bar at 420 East 22nd Street. Our name honors "Shari" (the soul of sushi rice) and "Arigato" (gratitude). Rooted in the Tokyo temaki tradition, our hand rolls are crafted and passed directly across the wooden counter one by one, to be enjoyed within seconds while the toasted seaweed remains intensely crisp.

The Sharigato Standard

The 30-Second Rule Of Temaki

Uncompromising rice temperature, toasted Japanese seaweed, and intimate pacing.

The Foundation

Warm Seasoned Shari

We use premium short-grain Koshihikari rice, seasoned with traditional red vinegar (akazu). Kept at human body temperature, the warm grains contrast exquisitely against cold fish.

The Crunch

Toasted Ariake Sea Nori

Sourced from Japan’s legendary Ariake Sea, our nori is toasted to order until shatteringly crisp, imparting an earthy umami aroma that dissolves cleanly on the palate.

The Rhythm

Passed One At A Time

Never served all at once on a platter. Each roll is placed directly into your hand or on your cedar stand as soon as it is rolled, so you experience peak crispness every bite.

Signature Seafood

Salmon Roe (Ikura) & Bluefin Toro

From glistening wild salmon roe that bursts with rich ocean brine to fatty Bluefin tuna belly (toro) paired with scallions and Osetra caviar, our seafood is flown in fresh from top Japanese and domestic fish purveyors.
