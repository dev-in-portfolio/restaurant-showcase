Transit & Parking

Convenient Customer Parking & Transit

Visiting our 22nd Street counter is seamless with multiple parking and transit options:

  • Area 15 Dedicated Surface Lot: Complimentary surface parking spaces directly adjoining 420 E 22nd Street designated for restaurant guests.
  • Neighborhood Street Parking: Plentiful free curbside parking along East 22nd Street and neighboring cross streets.
  • Lynx Blue Line Light Rail: The 25th Street Station is located just a short 5-minute walk from our front door.
Exclusive Events

Private Counter Buyouts & Temaki Kits

Host an unforgettable private dining experience at Sharigato:

  • Full Counter Buyout (Up to 16 Guests): Reserve our entire cedar counter for private celebrations, corporate dinners, or milestone events with customized multi-course omakase menus and sake pairings.
