Omakase-Style Progression

The Choreography of Curated Sets.

Dining at Sharigato is an intentionally choreographed journey. Our multi-roll sets are designed to guide your palate from lighter, citrus-accented proteins through opulent, fatty cuts, finishing with warm, sweet glazed unagi eel. Paired with chilled artisan sakes and Japanese whiskies, it is a complete sensory tasting.

Tasting Discipline

The Arc of The Hand-Roll Set

Carefully structured to prevent palate fatigue and build flavor depth.

Movement 01

Clean & Vibrant

The tasting opens with lean, energetic flavors—our signature spicy tuna with sesame and wild King salmon seasoned with aromatic citrus yuzu kosho.

Movement 02

Rich & Decadent

The mid-course introduces luxurious textures: tender Maine lobster poached in yuzu butter and rich Bluefin toro belly crowned with imported Royal Osetra caviar.

Movement 03

Sweet & Grounding

The finale concludes with grilled freshwater eel (unagi) brushed with rich caramelized reduction and toasted sesame, providing a comforting, savory-sweet finish.

Sake Pairings

Curated Junmai Daiginjo & Japanese Whiskies

Our beverage program is specifically curated to complement the mineral qualities of nori and rich seafood oils. From crisp, melon-scented Dassai 45 Junmai Daiginjo served chilled in carafes to Japanese highballs and earthy roasted hojicha green tea, each sip refreshes the palate between rolls.
