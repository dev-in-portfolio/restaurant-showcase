How Corporate Catering Works

Fast, reliable service backed by 30 years of Uptown experience.

Step 1: Place Your Order (704) 344-1975

Call us with your headcount, sandwich selections, and desired delivery or pickup time. 24-hour advance notice is recommended for large groups.

Every boxed lunch is clearly labeled with the sandwich name, number, and recipient's name or dietary requirements (gluten-free, vegetarian, dairy-free).

All platters and boxes come complete with heavyweight napkins, cutlery, serving tongs, and extra condiments for seamless meetings.
