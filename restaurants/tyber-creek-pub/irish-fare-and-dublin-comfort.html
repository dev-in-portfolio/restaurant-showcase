A great Irish pub starts with a kitchen that takes pride in every plate. At Tyber Creek, we prepare proper comfort food made fresh to order—from golden beer-battered cod and curry-dipped chips to the authentic Dublin spice bag and griddled toasties.

The Pillars of Our Kitchen

Time-tested tavern recipes and iconic Dublin takeaway cravings.

Dublin Takeaway Craze

The Dublin Spice Bag

Born in Dublin's late-night chippers, our spice bag combines crispy fried chicken chunks and thick chips tossed with sautéed peppers, sweet onions, and a punchy salt-and-chili spice blend.

North Atlantic Quality

Beer-Battered Cod

Fresh, flaky Icelandic cod fillets dipped in house craft beer batter and fried to a blistered golden crunch, served with thick-cut chips and tangy house tartar sauce.

Irish Comfort

Curry Chips & Toasties

Thick golden chips served with a pot of aromatic mild Irish curry sauce, alongside griddled toasties packed with smoked ham, melted cheddar, and sweet onions.

Late-Night Fare

Freshly Prepared for Late-Night South End

Our kitchen remains open late into the evening so you never have to settle for fast food after a concert, match, or evening out in South End. Gather around a table with a basket of piping hot wings, a double smash burger, or a spice bag with cold pints.
