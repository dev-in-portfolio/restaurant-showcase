The Perfect Pour, Match Day Spirit.

For over a quarter-century, Tyber Creek Pub has been Charlotte’s spiritual home for European football, rugby, and genuine pub conviviality. Situated on South Mint Street within walking distance of Bank of America Stadium, we open early on weekends for Premier League kickoffs with authentic imperial pints.

The Gold District Tavern

Historic Mahogany, Fresh Energy

When Tyber Creek relocated from South Boulevard to 1501 S Mint Street, our beloved 25-year-old original mahogany bar was lovingly dismantled and reassembled in our new space. The familiar heart and soul of Tyber Creek remains untouched, surrounded by an expanded outdoor patio and vibrant Gold District neighborliness.
