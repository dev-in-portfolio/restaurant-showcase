Established 1999 • Relocated to Gold District

New Address, Timeless Spirit.

Since 1999, Tyber Creek Pub has stood as an authentic cornerstone of Charlotte’s hospitality landscape. When urban growth required a new home, we meticulously preserved our legendary solid mahogany bar, vintage signage, and Irish soul, reopening at 1501 S Mint Street to write the next chapter of South End history.

Enduring Traditions

Values That Define Tyber Creek

Authentic craftsmanship, neighborhood loyalty, and genuine human warmth.

Pillar 01

Preserving the Bar

Our original 1999 mahogany bar top—where countless Charlotteans celebrated engagements, watched historic cup finals, and shared evening pints—was carefully moved and reinstalled as the beating heart of our Mint Street home.

Pillar 02

Real Community

In an era of corporate chain bars and cookie-cutter taprooms, Tyber Creek remains an independent public house where bartenders know your name, your pint, and your club.

Pillar 03

Match Day Legacy

We were pouring pints for early morning soccer matches long before it became fashionable. That dedication to authentic supporters' culture lives on every weekend.

The Gold District Chapter

Historic Roots in South End's Creative Enclave

Our location at 1501 S Mint Street places Tyber Creek squarely in Charlotte's bustling Gold District. With enhanced outdoor seating, ample room for live music and events, and easy walking distance to Bank of America Stadium, we are proud to continue serving Charlotte for the next twenty-five years.
