Getting Here

Patio Dining, Parking & Stadium Walk

Enjoy our Gold District tavern with seamless neighborhood access:

  • Outdoor Sidewalk Patio: Spacious shaded patio seating along South Mint Street, pet-friendly for leashed dogs.
  • Bank of America Stadium Walk: Direct walking route down Mint Street to Carolina Panthers football games and Charlotte FC soccer matches.
  • Transit & Parking: Street parking along Mint and surrounding Gold District streets, and walking distance from the LYNX Blue Line Bland Street and Carson stations.
