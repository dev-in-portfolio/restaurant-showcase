Proper Pub Fare & Specialties

Hearty Irish pub traditions and modern tavern favorites.

Icelandic Cod & Chips $18.00

A pub favorite — fresh fillet of Icelandic cod dipped in crisp beer batter and fried to perfection. Served with thick-cut chips, house tartar sauce, and lemon wedge.

The Dublin Spice Bag $14.00

A proper Irish pub classic with a fiery twist — crispy chicken bites and golden chips tossed with sautéed onions, mixed peppers, and a punchy Chinese salt-and-chili spice blend.

Tyber Double Smash Burger $16.00

Two juicy beef patties smashed on a screaming hot griddle for crispy, caramelized edges, layered with American cheese, sweet onions, lettuce, tomato, pickles, and signature Smash Sauce on a toasted potato roll with chips.

Tyber Fried Chicken Sandwich $15.00

Crispy fried chicken breast topped with creamy, tangy Southern Gold sauce, crisp lettuce, tomato, and pickles on a toasted potato bun.

Irish Curry Chips $10.00

Crispy golden fries served with a warm, mildly spiced traditional Irish pub curry sauce for dipping or pouring to your taste.

Crispy Golden Wings $14.00

Crispy fried chicken wings tossed in choice of Buffalo or BBQ sauce. Served with cool buttermilk ranch or tangy blue cheese and crisp carrot sticks.

The Sticky Situation $8.00

Double cheeseburger, crisp dill pickles, Thai peanut sauce, and spicy strawberry jam. A crave-worthy late-night favorite.

Tavern Handhelds & Toasties

Served warm with seasoned potato crisps.

Corned Beef Reuben $16.00

Slow-cooked corned beef, melted Swiss cheese, tangy sauerkraut, and whole-grain mayonnaise on toasted marbled rye with seasoned crisps.

Dublin Ham & Cheese Toastie $14.00

Shaved ham, melted sharp cheddar cheese, sweet sliced onion, and honey mustard griddled golden and served with crisps.

Tavern Grilled Cheese & Tomato Soup $15.00

Melted American and sharp cheddar cheeses pressed on thick hearth focaccia, served with a bowl of rich tomato soup for dipping.

Curry Chicken Salad Sandwich $16.00

Poached chicken breast tossed with mild curry dressing, celery, crisp lettuce, and ripe tomato with potato crisps.

Hearth-Baked Flatbreads

Crisp, bubbly hearth crusts made for sharing around the pub table.

Fig & Country Ham Flatbread $15.00

Country ham, creamy goat cheese spread, mission figs, aged balsamic reduction, and toasted walnuts.

Artisan Pepperoni Flatbread $15.00

Rich marinara sauce, whole milk mozzarella, and crisp cupping pepperoni finished with fresh oregano.

Classic Margherita Flatbread $14.00

House tomato marinara, fresh melted mozzarella curd, and torn garden basil on a blistered crust.

Draft Beers & Ciders

Proper 20oz imperial pints poured with patience.

Guinness Irish Stout (Nitro) Imperial Pint

The legendary Dublin dry stout poured slow with its iconic cascading ruby-black body and velvety dense head.

Harp Irish Lager Imperial Pint

Classic crisp golden lager brewed with Irish barley, delivering a clean malt profile and smooth refreshing finish.

Smithwick’s Irish Red Ale Imperial Pint

Ireland’s oldest operating ale recipe, balanced with roasted barley sweetness, caramel notes, and gentle bitterness.

Magners Irish Cider Over Ice

Crisp, refreshing hard cider pressed from 17 varieties of Irish apples in Clonmel, Ireland.

Legion Juicy Jay East Coast IPA Draft Pint

Charlotte’s celebrated flagship hazy IPA bursting with juicy tropical citrus, mango, and pine hop notes.

Wicked Weed Pernicious IPA Draft Pint

North Carolina craft legend dry-hopped with high amounts of American hops for a resinous, tropical bite.
