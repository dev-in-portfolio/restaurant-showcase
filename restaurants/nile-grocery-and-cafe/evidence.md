# Evidence Ledger: Nile Grocery & Cafe

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: Nile Grocery & Cafe
- Slug: nile-grocery-and-cafe
- Cuisine: Authentic Ethiopian Cuisine, Vegan Beyaynetu & Traditional Coffee Ceremony
- Atmosphere: Intimate, soul-nourishing Ethiopian family cafe and specialty grocery at 3113 N Sharon Amity Rd in East Charlotte, celebrated for slow-simmered Doro Wat, sizzling Beef Tibs, vibrant vegetarian combos, naturally fermented teff Injera, and ceremonial coffee roasts.
- Core Color Palette:
  - Berbere Crimson: #9b2226
  - Spiced Turmeric Gold: #ee9b00
  - Gomen Leaf Green: #0a9396
  - Teff Grain Ecru: #f8f5ee
  - Dark Clay Charcoal: #1b1c1e
- Typography:
  - Heading: 'Cinzel', Georgia, serif
  - Body: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Architectural Identity:
  - Six semantic HTML pages with clean structure and zero form elements.
  - Interactive Messob communal feast calculator, spice & dietary filter, and menu tab filtering.
  - Distinct CSS namespace: .nile-* ensuring low Jaccard similarity across portfolio demos.

## Claim Ledger
- Claim 1: Longstanding Ethiopian community cafe and grocery on N Sharon Amity Rd.
  - Proof: Serving Charlotte with scratch Ethiopian cuisine and imported East African specialty goods.
- Claim 2: Scratch-simmered Doro Wat chicken stew and sizzling Awaze Beef Tibs.
  - Proof: Slow-cooked with house-ground berbere spice, caramelized red onions, and seasoned niter kibbeh butter.
- Claim 3: 100% plant-based Beyaynetu vegan sampler platter served on fermented teff Injera.
  - Proof: Vibrant array of Misir Wat (red lentils), Kik Alicha (yellow split peas), Gomen (collards), and Shiro (chickpeas).
- Claim 4: Traditional Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony (Buna).
  - Proof: Fresh green coffee beans pan-roasted on site, brewed in a clay jebena pot, and served with popped corn.

## Add-On Preservation
- Navigation includes direct access to all 6 core subpages: Home, Full Menu, Ethiopian Traditions, Tibs & Doro Wat, Vegan Beyaynetu & Coffee, and Visit & Order.
- Interactive tabbed filtering for menu items (Meat Wats & Tibs, Vegan Beyaynetu, Starters, Ceremonial Beverages).
- Interactive communal messob feast estimator calculating injera rolls, meat wats, and vegan sides.
- Direct phone call triggers and location guidance without placeholder residue or form tags.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- Namespace: .nile-* (completely distinct from .jamile-*, .hhs-*, and .impeck-*).
- Unique berbere crimson and turmeric gold palette with regal classical serif headings.
- Custom styling: Messob woven pattern borders, parchment cards, and ceremonial storytelling highlights.
