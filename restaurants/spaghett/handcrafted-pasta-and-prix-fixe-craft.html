Flour, Farm Eggs & Bronze Dies

The Geometry of Fresh Pasta.

Pasta is not merely a vehicle for sauce; it is the soul of the table. At Spaghett, we mill, hydrate, knead, extrude, and hand-cut pasta shapes each morning. From delicate egg yolk rich tagliatelle to bronze-die penne with porous exteriors, our pasta honors centuries-old Italian geometry paired with Carolinian farm bounty.

Culinary Standards

The Four Pillars of Spaghett Pasta

Techniques that distinguish authentic artisan pasta from commercial factory noodles.

Standard 01

Bronze-Die Extrusion

Unlike slick Teflon dies, our solid bronze extrusion plates create a textured, matte pasta surface that grips sauces, emulsified butter, and braising liquids effortlessly.

Standard 02

Pasture-Raised Egg Dough

For our rolled ribbon pastas and filled shapes, we use vibrant golden yolks from pasture-raised North Carolina hens and Italian Tipo 00 soft wheat flour for silky elasticity.

Standard 03

The Four-Course Harmony

We structure dining in the Italian tradition: opening with delicate antipasti, followed by a dedicated pasta course, a roasted meat or fish entrata, and finishing with dolci.

Micro-Seasonality

Rooted In Carolinian Agriculture

While our pasta techniques are deeply Italian, our ingredients belong to the Carolinas. We partner directly with regional growers and coastal fishermen to source sweet summer corn for agnolotti, bitter chicories, foraged chanterelles, and fresh flounder, changing our prix fixe offerings as the seasons turn.
