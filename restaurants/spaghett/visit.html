Arrival Directives

Fourth Ward Parking & Neighborhood Access

Fourth Ward is a historic, leafy residential neighborhood with specific parking considerations:

  • Street Parking: Metered and public curbside parking is available along West 10th Street, North Pine Street, and North Poplar Street. Please observe posted residential signage.
  • Rideshare Recommendation: Because street parking can fill quickly during peak evening hours, we strongly recommend arriving via Uber, Lyft, or Uptown light rail.
  • Uptown Surface Lots: Public paid surface lots are located within two short blocks along North Graham Street and 9th Street.
Tock Booking Directives

Dining Room Tables & Bar Walk-Ins

How to secure your seat at Spaghett:

  • Tock Reservations: Dining room reservations for our four-course prix fixe ($75/person) release two weeks in advance on Tock. Pre-payment or deposit may apply.
  • Bar Counter Walk-Ins: Our intimate wooden bar counter is reserved exclusively for walk-in guests on a first-come basis, offering à la carte pasta, cocktails, and amaro.
  • Dietary Accommodations: Vegetarian and pescatarian options are seamlessly integrated into our prix fixe progression. Please note severe allergies upon booking.
