Victorian Architecture • Spirit-Driven Mixology

Spirits Of Italy, Historic Walls.

Nestled within the 1885 Queen Anne Victorian walls of the historic Young-Morrison House, Spaghett offers an intimate escape from modern urban hustle. Led by nationally acclaimed beverage director Amanda Britton, our bar program celebrates the depth of Italian botanical amari, aperitivo cocktails, and natural wines.

Mixology Director

Amanda Britton's Vision

Known across the Southeast for inventive, precise mixology, Amanda Britton crafts balanced aperitivo and digestivo cocktails that pair seamlessly with rich pasta courses.

Historic 1885 Home

Young-Morrison House

Originally constructed in 1885, the Young-Morrison House stands as one of Fourth Ward's architectural treasures with original heart-pine floors and period molding.

Italian Bitters

The Amaro Library

From gentian-heavy alpine liqueurs to citrus-forward Sicilian amari, our curated flights provide a fascinating geographic tour of Italy's herbal bitter culture.

Walk-In Welcome

The Intimate Bar Counter

While dining room tables require advance Tock reservations, our warm wooden bar counter reserves seats nightly for walk-in neighborhood guests. Join us for a quick glass of chilled Nebbiolo, a bowl of fresh pasta, or an unhurried post-work digestivo.
