1885 Victorian Landmark • Uptown Charlotte

A Living Ode To Italian Craft.

Spaghett was conceived to bring a rare intimacy and artisanal devotion to Uptown Charlotte. Set within the Queen Anne Victorian architecture of the Young-Morrison House, our thirteen-table dining room was intentionally designed to revive the warmth of an authentic Italian trattoria where every dish is prepared with reverence for the table.

Core Principles

The Foundations of Spaghett

What defines our approach to hospitality and cooking in Fourth Ward.

Principle 01

Unhurried Intimacy

With only thirteen tables, dinner at Spaghett is never rushed. Each party is invited to settle into the rhythm of the meal across multiple harmonious courses.

Principle 02

Handmade Purity

We make our pasta from scratch every morning, hand-extruding through bronze dies and pinching filled shapes, pairing them with slowly braised farm stocks and ragùs.

Principle 03

Neighborhood Soul

Surrounded by Victorian homes, gas lanterns, and tree-canopied brick sidewalks, we take pride in anchoring Fourth Ward as a culinary home for our community.

Hospitality Heritage

A Table Set For Conversation

Food tastes best when shared in a room rich with character and history. At Spaghett, soft candle glow reflects off antique brick and heart pine floors, while the aroma of simmering garlic and rosemary welcomes guests home.
