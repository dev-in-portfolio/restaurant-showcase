Historic Young-Morrison House • Fourth Ward

Hand-Rolled Pasta, Intimate Evenings.

Welcome to Spaghett, an intimate thirteen-table craft pasta house and cocktail salon situated within the historic 1885 Young-Morrison House at 224 West 10th Street. Centered around a hyper-seasonal four-course Italian dining progression—Primi, Pasta, Entrata, and Dolci—paired with an acclaimed amaro and cocktail salon led by Amanda Britton, we celebrate traditional Italian craft rooted in Carolinian agriculture.

Sauce Mastery

Slow-Simmered Pomodoro & Hand-Folded Shapes

Great Italian cuisine relies on the highest quality ingredients treated with patience. Our penne and rigatoni are tossed in slow-simmered San Marzano tomato pomodoro, infused with roasted garlic, sweet basil, and finished with freshly shaved aged pecorino romano cheese.

Spirited Aperture

The Art of the Italian Aperitivo

Begin your evening at our cozy bar with a chilled Paper Plane, a botanical Fourth Ward Negroni, or our playful namesake Spaghett cocktail. Our cellar features an extensive library of bitter herbal amari from across northern and southern Italy.
