Brewhouse Philosophy

The Foundations of Our Draft Program

Carefully balanced malt bills, expressive yeast strains, and vibrant hop oils.

Pillar 01

Escape Plan West Coast IPA

Our flagship IPA balances a crisp two-row malt grist with heavy whirlpool and dry hop charges of Citra, Mosaic, and Simcoe for dazzling notes of pine, citrus, and ripe mango.

Pillar 02

Kettle Sours & Real Fruit

Our Cruise to Barbados sour program utilizes pure lactobacillus kettle fermentation infused with toasted coconut and tropical passionfruit for bright, refreshing acidity.

Pillar 03

Patient German Lagering

With Slow Punch Helles and Hidden City Schwarzbier, we allow extended cold lagering to produce brilliantly bright, snappy lagers with refined biscuit malt depth.

Interactive Tasting

The Four-Pour Flight Experience

We designed our taproom to be a place of sensory discovery. Our custom wooden flight paddles allow guests to create their own four-beer tasting flight, labeled on individual chalkboard slats. Whether comparing different hop varieties side-by-side or pairing lagers with warm appetizers, our staff provides expert guidance.
