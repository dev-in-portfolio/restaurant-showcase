800 Grandin Road • Founded 2018

Rooted in Wesley Heights, Brewed for Everyone.

When Town Brewing Company opened its doors in 2018, the goal was simple: to create a genuine neighborhood anchor in historic Wesley Heights. Housed in a repurposed industrial warehouse with soaring ceilings and exposed brick, our brewery is designed as an open, welcoming gathering space where neighbors connect over extraordinary beer and scratch-made food.

Our Story
Wesley Heights Neighborhood Brewery Since 2018
Core Values

The Principles of Town Brewing

Commitment to authentic brewing, community connection, and neighborly warmth.

Value 01

Neighborhood Anchor

We take pride in our Wesley Heights location, creating a family-friendly and pet-friendly environment where all Charlotteans feel at home.

Value 02

Craft Without Pretension

While we obsess over hop chemistry and brewing metrics, our taproom staff is friendly, welcoming, and passionate about helping guests find their next favorite pint.

Value 03

Full Culinary Integration

We believe beer and food are meant to elevate each other. Our scratch kitchen creates dishes that pair harmoniously with our rotating draft line.

Social Connection

Community Clubs & Events

Town Brewing is more than a place to drink—it’s an active community hub. From our weekly Tuesday night run club that winds through Wesley Heights to trivia competitions, local live music, and pop-up makers markets, our taproom brings Charlotte together.
