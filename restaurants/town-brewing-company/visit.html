Dog-Friendly Patio Directives

Guidelines for visiting our outdoor lawn and patio with your four-legged companions:

  • Leash Policy: Dogs must remain on standard non-retractable leashes at all times while on the patio and brewery lawn.
  • Pet Amenities: Fresh water dog bowls and shaded picnic benches are stationed across the grounds.
  • Ordering: Guests are welcome to order at the outdoor walk-up window or use table QR codes for direct mobile ordering to your picnic bench.
Community Happenings

Weekly Events & Run Club

Join our active weekly brewery traditions in Wesley Heights:

  • Tuesday Run Club (6:30 PM): 1-mile, 3-mile, and 5-mile neighborhood loops through Wesley Heights. Runners receive $5 core draft pints post-run.
  • Wednesday Trivia Night (7:00 PM): Free-to-play pub trivia with taproom gift card prizes and draft specials.
  • Sunday Board Game Sessions: Family and friend gaming sessions with complimentary access to our taproom game library.
