Wesley Heights • 800 Grandin Road

Innovative Pours, Artisan Flatbreads.

Welcome to Town Brewing Company, Wesley Heights’ neighborhood living room. Located inside a restored historic industrial space along Grandin Road, we craft bold West Coast IPAs, vibrant fruited sours, and ultra-crisp lagers, paired with a full scratch kitchen serving wood-fired style flatbread pizzas, loaded beer cheese nachos, and warm jumbo soft pretzels.
