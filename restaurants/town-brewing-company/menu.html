Wesley Heights Brewhouse & Kitchen

Craft Beers on Tap, Wood-Fired Flatbreads.

At Town Brewing Company, we believe world-class beer deserves equally craveable food. Our scratch kitchen prepares hot, hand-stretched flatbreads, legendary Texas Red's nachos, and jumbo soft pretzels to pair with our rotating 16 taps of fresh IPAs, sours, and lagers.

Wesley Heights Craft Beers on Draft

Brewed on-site at 800 Grandin Road. Available in pints, 10-oz pours, flights, and 4-pack cans to go.

Escape Plan West Coast IPA $7.00 / Pint

Our flagship American IPA brewed with Citra, Mosaic, and Simcoe hops. Crystal-clear amber hue with vibrant aromas of resinous pine, pink grapefruit, and tropical mango. 6.7% ABV.

Cruise to Barbados Coconut Sour $7.50 / Tulip

Tropical fruited kettle sour brewed with real toasted coconut, key lime purée, and passionfruit. Tangy, zesty, and refreshing with an island fruit finish. 4.8% ABV.

Slow Punch German Helles Lager $6.50 / Pint

Traditional golden Bavarian lager brewed with Munich malts and noble Hallertau hops. Clean biscuit malt backbone with crisp, refreshing carbonation. 4.8% ABV.

Hidden City Dark Black Lager $7.00 / Pint

Traditional German Schwarzbier with rich roasted malt flavors of dark chocolate and mild espresso, yet drinking extraordinarily light, dry, and crisp. 5.2% ABV.

Custom Four-Pour Craft Flight $12.00

Your choice of four 5-oz draft pours served on a handcrafted wooden flight paddle with custom tasting notes.

Artisan Flatbread Pizzas

Hand-stretched dough baked hot with charred edges and premium toppings.

"Hot N' Bothered" Flatbread $14.50

Hand-stretched crust topped with slow-simmered tomato marinara, melted mozzarella, spicy pepperoni slices, house pickled jalapeños, and a drizzle of hot honey.

"The Big Chicken" Flatbread $15.00

Grilled seasoned chicken breast, melted Wisconsin cheddar and mozzarella, smoky bourbon BBQ sauce, thinly sliced red onion, and house buttermilk ranch drizzle.

Classic Margherita Flatbread $13.50

San Marzano tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella medallions, roasted garlic herb oil, sliced ripe Roma tomatoes, and fresh torn basil leaves.

Taproom Starters & Sharables

Designed for communal benches and sharing around pints.

Texas Red's Beer Cheese Nachos $14.00

Crisp corn tortilla chips smothered in house Escape Plan beer cheese, seasoned black beans, roasted corn salsa, pickled jalapeños, and cilantro lime crema. (Add chicken or beef +$4).

Jumbo Warm Soft Pretzel $11.00

Baked Bavarian pretzel sprinkled with coarse salt, served warm with house-made beer cheese dip and tangy stone-ground German mustard.

Sizzling Meatball Skillet $12.50

Three tender beef and pork meatballs slow-simmered in San Marzano marinara, baked in a cast-iron skillet with bubbly melted mozzarella and toasted garlic bread.

Freshly Popped Popcorn $5.00

Made-to-order warm popcorn tossed in your choice of White Truffle Herb, Smoked White Cheddar, or Sea Salt.

Curated North Carolina Craft Cocktails

Handcrafted using spirits from regional North Carolina distilleries.

Wesley Heights Mule $12.00

North Carolina craft vodka, spicy craft ginger beer, fresh lime juice, and Angostura bitters served over crushed ice in a copper mug.

Town Old Fashioned $13.00

Small batch straight bourbon whiskey, raw cane turbinado syrup, aromatic bitters, and a flamed orange peel twist.
