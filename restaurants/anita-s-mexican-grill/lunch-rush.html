The Yorkmont Lunch Rush

We operate exclusively during the weekday lunch hour (Mon–Fri 10:30 AM – 3:00 PM). Here is how to get in, get fed, and get back to work without wasting a minute of your lunch break.

Insider Lunch Tips

Beat The Queue

1. Early Bird (10:30 - 11:15 AM)

Arrive right at opening to get hot fresh batch meats with zero wait time. Ideal for shift workers and early lunch takers.

2. Call-Ahead Phone Orders

Call (704) 329-0321 before 11:30 AM to place your pickup order for express counter collection.

3. Late Afternoon (1:45 - 2:45 PM)

Post-peak hours offer easy parking in the Yorkmont lot and quick seating for relaxed dining.
