Transit & Parking

Dedicated Central Avenue Surface Parking

Visiting Sabores de Oaxaca offers an easy and convenient arrival in East Charlotte:

  • Complimentary Surface Parking: Large dedicated commercial plaza surface lot located directly outside our storefront. Ample open spaces with no meters or fees.
  • Central Corridor Location: Situated directly on Central Avenue (NC-27) between Eastway Drive and Sharon Amity Road, minutes from Plaza Midwood and Eastland.
  • Curbside Counter Pickup: Short-term storefront spaces available for rapid order pickup.
Family Feasts & Celebrations

Oaxacan Party Platters & Catering

Bring the extraordinary flavors of Oaxaca to your next family reunion, birthday, or office lunch:

  • Tlayuda Party Trays: Multi-cut giant tlayudas packed with tasajo, cecina, and chorizo for easy sharing.
  • Mole Negro Stew Buffets: Half and full pans of Mole Negro with chicken, Mexican rice, black beans, and fresh tortillas.
  • Direct Orders: Call our kitchen team at (980) 207-2581 with 24 hours advance notice for party trays.
