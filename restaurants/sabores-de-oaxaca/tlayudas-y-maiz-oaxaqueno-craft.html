Comal Toasted Heirloom Maize

The Architecture of The Tlayuda.

Known affectionately as the 'Oaxacan pizza,' a genuine tlayuda is an intricate culinary canvas. Measuring 14 inches across and toasted over hot griddles until crisp and pliable, it unites unadulterated native corn, savory pork asiento, avocado-leaf perfumed beans, and mountains of hand-pulled quesillo.

The Four Essential Layers of an Authentic Tlayuda

Every element follows the traditional culinary laws of the Central Valleys of Oaxaca.

Layer 01

14-Inch Comal-Toasted Corn

Unlike standard soft tortillas, tlayuda tortillas are pressed thin from white or yellow heirloom corn, par-cooked on a clay comal, and toasted until crisp yet chewy enough to fold without breaking.

Layer 02

Asiento & Hoja de Aguacate

The hot tortilla is brushed with unrefined asiento (the savory, browned toasted sediments from pork carnitas) and a thick spread of pureed black beans simmered with dried avocado leaves for an unmistakable herbal aroma.

Layer 03

Hand-Shredded Quesillo

Authentic Quesillo Oaxaqueño is a kneaded string cheese produced using the pasta filata curd method. Stretched into thin ribbons, it melts into luscious, buttery threads that bind the entire tlayuda together.

The Trifecta of Meats

Tasajo, Cecina Enchilada & Chorizo

To top our tlayudas, we prepare traditional Oaxacan meats from scratch: thinly sliced center beef cured in sea salt and air-dried (tasajo), tender pork cutlets rubbed in crushed guajillo paste (cecina enchilada), and house-ground spiced chorizo fragrant with allspice and Mexican oregano. Enjoy them individually or combine all three for a legendary feast.
