Gastronomic Crown Jewel

Authentic Mole Negro Oaxaqueño

Mole Negro is celebrated worldwide for its astonishing flavor depth. Our recipe balances charred Mexican chilies, dark stone-ground cacao, whole almonds, sesame seeds, thyme, and Mexican oregano. Simmered unhurriedly until velvet-smooth and glossy black, it is poured over tender poultry and served with traditional white rice and warm tortillas.

Ancestral Morning & Dessert

Hot Chocolate Oaxaqueño & Pan de Yema

In Oaxaca, chocolate is not a candy—it is an ancient ritual. We blend roasted cacao beans, sweet sugar cane, whole almonds, and fragrant Mexican canela (cinnamon). Whisked vigorously with a hand-carved wooden molinillo to create an airy, aromatic froth, it is served alongside airy, anise-scented pan de yema (egg yolk sweet bread) for dunking.
