Las Tlayudas Tradicionales de Oaxaca

Giant 14-inch crispy toasted corn tortillas smeared with asiento, refried black beans scented with avocado leaf, shredded cabbage, sliced tomatoes, fresh avocado, and generous threads of artisanal Oaxacan quesillo.

Tlayuda con Tasajo $18

Giant crispy tlayuda loaded with thinly sliced, tender salt-cured Oaxacan beef (tasajo) grilled over open griddle, layered with rich quesillo and avocado.

Tlayuda con Cecina Enchilada $18

Thin ribbons of tender pork marinated in guajillo chili paste, garlic, and vinegar, seared crisp and draped across the melted quesillo and bean foundation.

Tlayuda con Chorizo Oaxaqueño $17

Crumbled artisanal Oaxacan pork sausage seasoned with ground clove, cinnamon, dried chilies, and herbs, rendered juicy and browned over the hot comal.

Tlayuda Mixta de Tres Carnes $22

The ultimate Oaxacan market feast: a towering tlayuda crowned with all three traditional meats—tasajo cured beef, cecina enchilada pork, and spiced chorizo.

Tlayuda Sencilla / Vegetariana $15

Toasted corn tortilla prepared without asiento, layered with rich black beans simmered with avocado leaves, double quesillo cheese, cabbage, sliced tomatoes, and avocado.

Los Moles & Guisados Oaxaqueños

Simmered for hours following ancestral regional formulas. Served with traditional Mexican white rice, refried black beans, and warm handmade tortillas.

Mole Negro de Oaxaca con Pollo $21

Our signature 30-ingredient black mole made from charred chilhuacle chilies, dark Mexican cacao, plantains, raisins, almonds, and warm spices, smothered over tender bone-in chicken.

Mole Coloradito con Costilla de Cerdo $20

Rich, brick-red Oaxacan mole balancing sweet plantains, sesame seeds, ancho chilies, and ripe tomatoes, simmered gently around tender pork ribs.

Enmoladas de Pollo Oaxaqueñas $18

Four fresh corn tortillas dipped in warm Mole Negro, rolled with shredded chicken breast, and finished with crumbled queso fresco, raw white onion rings, and toasted sesame seeds.

Estofado de Res Tradicional $21

Slow-braised tender beef shank in an intricate tomato-tomatillo gravy flavored with cinnamon, green olives, capers, raisins, and pickled jalapeños.

Antojitos Callejeros & Tortas

Street market delicacies made to order from freshly nixtamalized corn masa and bakery-fresh Mexican telera rolls.

Memelas Oaxaqueñas (Trio) $14

Three thick hand-pressed masa cakes pinched along the borders, spread with savory asiento, pureed black beans, stringy quesillo, and fresh salsa verde.

Torta de Pierna Oaxaqueña $14

Crispy Mexican telera roll loaded with citrus-marinated slow-roasted pork leg, melted quesillo string cheese, creamy avocado slices, pickled jalapeño, and tomato.

Sopes con Tinga de Pollo (Trio) $14

Handmade masa rounds with raised edges topped with refried beans, shredded chicken stewed with chipotle and sweet onions, shredded lettuce, crema, and cotija cheese.

Tacos Dorados de Barbacoa $13

Four rolled corn tortillas stuffed with slow-steamed seasoned beef barbacoa, fried until golden crisp, served over shredded cabbage with avocado salsa.

Bebidas Ancestrales & Pan Dulce

Artisanal stone-ground hot chocolate, ancient indigenous beverages, and daily fresh bakery.

Chocolate Oaxaqueño Caliente $6.00

Stone-ground cacao with Mexican cinnamon and almonds, whisked hot with a traditional wooden molinillo in a clay bowl until deeply frothy.

Pan de Yema Tradicional $3.50

Airy, golden sweet Mexican egg yolk bread with subtle notes of anise seed and sesame crust, baked fresh daily for dipping into hot chocolate.

Tejate de los Valles Centrales $6.50

Ancient pre-Hispanic cold beverage whipped from roasted maize, fermented cacao, mamey sapote seeds (pixtle), and edible flor de cacao foam.

Agua de Horchata con Tuna y Melón $5.00

Chilled rice milk steeped with Mexican cinnamon, crowned with diced sweet cantaloupe melon and bright crimson prickly pear (tuna) coulis.
