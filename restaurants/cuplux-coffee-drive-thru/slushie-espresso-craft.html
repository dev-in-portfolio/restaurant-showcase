☕ Blended Espresso Extraction

The Art of Slushie Coffee

Blending espresso without diluting rich coffee flavor requires technique. Our Slushie Coffee uses freshly pulled double espresso shots, house sweet cream, and custom micro-ice blending for a velvety texture that stays thick to the last sip.

Blending Process

The 3 Pillars of Slushie Coffee Craft

1. Freshly Pulled Double Espresso

We pull fresh double-shots of dark specialty espresso blend right into the blender jar for full aromatic crema.

2. House-Made Sweet Cream Base

In-house cream simmered with real Madagascar vanilla beans and cane sugar creates a rich, non-separating texture.

3. Salted Caramel Drizzle Crown

Finished with a dollop of fresh whipped cream and house salted caramel glaze drizzled inside the cup wall.
