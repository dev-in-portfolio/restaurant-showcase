🍦 Waffle Cone Iron Baking

The Art of Hand-Rolled Cones

Nothing compares to the aroma of freshly baked waffle cones. We mix real butter, vanilla, & brown sugar into our batter, press it on hot irons right behind the counter, & hand-roll each cone while piping hot.

Baking & Churning

The 3 Pillars of Elizabeth Waffle Craft

1. Real Vanilla & Butter Batter

Mixed fresh daily with pure vanilla extract, sweet cream butter, & dark brown sugar for golden caramel flavor.

2. Hot Iron Press & Hand Roll

Poured onto hot waffle irons and rolled around wood mandrels while still warm & pliable.

3. Low-Overrun Small-Batch Churning

Our ice cream is slow-churned with minimal air inclusion for a dense, silky, ultra-creamy mouthfeel.
