# Evidence & Design Ledger for Fonda Las Tarascas

## Creative Brief
- **Client Identity:** Fonda Las Tarascas, situated at 6308 The Plaza, Charlotte, NC 28215 in East Charlotte.
- **Concept:** Authentic Michoacán Mexican fonda and family restaurant celebrating the UNESCO-recognized culinary traditions of the Tarascan (Purépecha) region of Michoacán. Specializes in copper-cazo carnitas michoacanas, birria de res with rich consommé, handmade stone-ground corn tortillas, sopes, huaraches, gorditas, weekend pozole and menudo, and scratch-made aguas frescas.
- **Target Audience:** Families, Mexican food lovers seeking authentic regional Michoacán flavors, East Charlotte locals, catering clients for fiestas and celebrations.
- **Design Language:** Warm Adobe Terracotta #C85A32, Michoacán Agave Green #2D5A27, Golden Masa #F4A261, Rich Mole Burgundy #78281F, Warm Ivory Cream #FDFBF7, and Charcoal #1C1917. Warm inviting borders, rich photography, and interactive Taquiza catering calculator.

## Claim Ledger
- **Location Claim:** Located at 6308 The Plaza, Charlotte, NC 28215. Phone: (980) 237-4215.
- **Service Hours Claim:** Open 7 days a week: Monday through Sunday from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- **Menu Offerings Claim:** Carnitas michoacanas slow-simmered in copper pots, handmade corn tortillas made to order on the comal, birria tacos with dipping consommé, sopes, huaraches, pozole rojo, menudo, fresh horchata, jamaica, and warm churros with cajeta.
- **Catering Claim:** Taquiza party packages, carnitas by the pound with all the fixings, catering trays of rice, beans, and fresh salsas.

## Add-On Preservation
- **Interactive Fiesta Taquiza Calculator:** Allows guests to configure event party sizes (10 to 200 guests), choose catering packages (Tarasca Street Taco Bar, Michoacán Carnitas Feast, Grand Fiesta Banquet), add extra pounds of carnitas or birria, choose fresh aguas frescas barrels, and generate real-time pricing and pre-filled email orders.
- **Interactive Category Filter:** Live tab switching across All, Carnitas & Birria, Handmade Masa (Sopes/Huaraches), Street Tacos, Traditional Soups, and Postres & Drinks on menu.html.
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Expanding answers covering handmade tortillas, weekend soup availability, spice levels, catering logistics, and dine-in seating on contact.html.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- **Namespace:** .flt-* exclusively used across HTML, CSS, and JS.
- **Distinct Aesthetic:** Vibrant Michoacán terracotta and agave green palette with warm artisanal touches, distinct from dark hookah lounges, industrial burger joints, or coastal brewery taprooms.
