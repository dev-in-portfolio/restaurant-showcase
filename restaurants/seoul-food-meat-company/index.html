South End & Mill District • Hickory Smoked

Southern Smoke, Seoul Soul.

Welcome to Seoul Food Meat Company, Charlotte’s premier Korean-Southern fusion smokehouse. Across our South End and Mill District campuses, we bring together the patient discipline of 14-hour hickory-smoked barbecue with the fiery, sweet, and umami-rich flavors of Korean street culture. Served family-style on butcher paper trays with bao buns, double-fried wings, and ice-cold soju.
