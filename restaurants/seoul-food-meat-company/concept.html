Born in South End • Expanded to Mill District

A Bold Marriage of Two Great Cultures.

Barbecue in North Carolina is sacred. Korean dining culture is equally legendary for its communal warmth, bold fermented spice, and endless small dishes. Seoul Food Meat Company was born from a singular passion: what happens when you smoke classic American cuts over Carolina hickory, but glaze, season, and serve them with unapologetic Korean soul?

The Seoul Food Philosophy

Three Principles That Guide Our Smokehouse

Uncompromising pit discipline, communal dining joy, and playful hospitality.

Principle 01

Pure Hickory Smoke

We never use artificial liquid smoke or electric shortcuts. All of our briskets, burnt ends, and pork butts are smoked in real offset wood pits burning dry North Carolina hardwood logs.

Principle 02

Communal Butcher Trays

Barbecue is best enjoyed together. We serve meats by the half-pound on shared metal trays so groups can craft custom sliders with warm bao buns, house kimchi, and pickled radish.

Principle 03

Unapologetic Fun

From our high-energy karaoke rooms to outdoor lawn bowling, fenced dog runs, and late-night pocha cocktails, we believe dining out should feel like a celebration every single day.

Two Vibrant Campuses

South End Roots & Mill District Expansion

What started as a beloved neighborhood joint on South Church Street has expanded to include our sprawling 30,000-square-foot Mill District campus on East 26th Street. Both locations offer ample indoor seating, outdoor turf games, and dedicated private event spaces.
