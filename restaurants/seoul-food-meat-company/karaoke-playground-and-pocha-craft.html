Seoul Food Meat Company was created as a high-energy gathering ground for friends, families, and team celebrations. Across our South End and Mill District campuses, dining is just the beginning. The Ultimate Social Playground

Everything you need for an unforgettable night out in Charlotte.

Singing Rooms

Private Karaoke Suites

Boutique soundproof karaoke rooms equipped with premium audio systems, dual wireless mics, thousands of domestic and international hits, and full food and bottle service.

Outdoor Fun

Adult Outdoor Playground

Sprawling outdoor turf patios featuring giant Connect Four, table tennis, billiards, foosball tables, and fire pits designed for laid-back socializing with food and drinks.

Canine Friendly

Fenced Dog Parks

Both campuses boast dedicated off-leash dog park areas with agility features and seating so you can enjoy your barbecue while your four-legged companions run and socialize safely.

Late-Night Drinking Culture

Seoul Food Pocha: Soju Carafes & Street Bites

Inspired by Korea's famous street tent bars (pojangmacha), Seoul Food Pocha at our South End location offers an electric late-night lounge experience. Sip chilled carafes of green apple, peach, and yogurt soju, Asian craft beers, and creative cocktails alongside piping-hot street snacks until 2:00 AM on weekends.
