Arrival Directives

Free Campus Parking & Transit

Arriving at either Seoul Food Meat Company location is hassle-free:

  • South End Campus (1400 S Church St): On-site customer surface lot directly in front of the building, plus adjacent neighborhood street parking and Bland Street Lynx Blue Line station two blocks away.
  • Mill District Campus (421 E 26th St): A massive private 100+ stall surface parking lot dedicated exclusively to restaurant patrons with well-lit walkway access.
  • Rideshare Zones: Designated drop-off and pick-up zones at main campus entrances for weekend nightlife.
Tailgates & Large Parties

BBQ Party Trays & Tailgate Catering

Feed your crew with our signature Korean-Southern BBQ feast:

  • Smoked Meats By The Pound: Hickory smoked brisket, fire pork belly, and pulled pork packed warm with extra bao buns, sauces, and kimchi.
  • Wing Party Boxes: 24-piece, 48-piece, and 100-piece double-fried wing boxes tossed in soy garlic or spicy Korean BBQ glazes.
  • Catering Orders: Call (980) 299-5143 in advance to coordinate pickup or delivery for your office event or game-day party.
