The Art of Agave & Drinks

Fresh masa tacos require vibrant, refreshing drink pairings. At MAS Tortilleria, our agave bar features small-batch Oaxacan mezcals, 100% blue agave reposado tequilas, fresh key lime margaritas, & house-blended cinnamon rice horchata.
