## Original Audit Weakness

MAS Tortilleria relies on basic online ordering portals and social media pages. Located at 1018 Jay St Suite 110 in FreemoreWest, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its authentic heirloom corn nixtamalization, daily fresh tortilla milling, party catering, and FreemoreWest directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **FreemoreWest Location:** 1018 Jay St, Suite 110, Charlotte NC 28208 (FreemoreWest / Wesley Heights near Enderly Park & Jay St).
2. **Tortilleria & Taqueria Specialties:** Hand-Milled Heirloom Corn Nixtamalized Masa Tortillas (Birria de Res Consomé Tacos, Slow-Braised Suadero Tacos, Al Pastor Tacos, House Quesadillas, Fresh Guacamole & Masa Chips, Hand-Wrapped Tamales, House Horchata, & Agave Cocktails).
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 910-6010; Mon Closed | Tue–Thu 10:30 AM – 8:30 PM | Fri–Sat 10:30 AM – 9:30 PM | Sun 10:30 AM – 3:30 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Warm Artisan Display Typography:** Clean modern sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) paired with tech body sans (*Inter*) and Oaxaca tortilleria mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Volcanic Clay & Heirloom Maize Palette:** Authentic Mexican tortilleria palette anchored in deep volcanic clay terracotta (`#7C2D12`), heirloom maize gold (`#EAB308`), agave green (`#15803D`), and soft sun-baked agave parchment (`#FFFBEB`).
3. **"The Heirloom Corn Nixtamalization & Taqueria Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`heirloom-nixtamal-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing hand-milled corn tortillas alongside slow-braised taco fillings & craft agave drinks.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Lorenzo’s Pizzeria` — Brooklyn Stone Deck with Outfit fonts and Deep Forest Pine/Terracotta Orange.
2. `LILA Restaurant & Lounge` — Tokyo Midnight Velvet with Cormorant Garamond fonts and Velvet Midnight Plum/Champagne Gold.

### Divergent Choices for MAS Tortilleria

- **Hero Composition:** Oaxaca Earth & Heirloom Corn Tortilleria Vault split-hero layout: left side features an Heirloom Masa Mill stamp badge (`"HEIRLOOM CORN NIXTAMAL TORTILLERIA • FREEMOREWEST CHARLOTTE NC"`), warm artisan sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*), volcanic clay & heirloom maize gold palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of fresh nixtamal tacos & agave cocktails.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **MAS Vault Cards** (`mas-vault-card`) and FreemoreWest dining highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`mas-header`, `mas-brand`, `clay-hero-stage`, `masa-mill-badge`, `mas-vault-card`, `mas-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
