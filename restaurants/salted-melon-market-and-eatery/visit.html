Transit & Parking

Worthington Garage & Rail Trail Access

Arriving at our South End flagship is simple and seamless:

  • Worthington Deck Parking: Convenient parking garage located directly behind 100 W Worthington Avenue with retail validation available.
  • Rail Trail & LYNX Light Rail: Directly accessible by foot and bike from the Charlotte Rail Trail, or via East/West Boulevard light rail station (3-minute walk).
  • Phillips Place Parking: At our SouthPark location, enjoy hundreds of complimentary open-air surface parking spaces directly in the shopping center courtyard.
