Kitchen Discipline

The Four Tenets of Our Kitchen

Every plate celebrates unhurried scratch preparation and wholesome farm sourcing.

Tenet 01

Pasture-Raised Farm Eggs

We exclusively crack certified pasture-raised farm eggs with vibrant golden yolks, gently poaching them for our artisan avocado toasts or scrambling them soft for breakfast burritos and breakfast bagel sandwiches.

Tenet 02

Scratch Vinaigrettes & Sauces

We never buy commercial dressings loaded with preservatives or high-fructose corn syrup. We whip our own lemon-herb tahini, buttermilk green goddess, and black pepper Caesar using extra virgin olive oil and fresh herbs.

Tenet 03

Fresh Griddle Searing

From our crispy lacy-edge prime smash burgers to seared Atlantic salmon fillets, our proteins are cooked to order over high heat on seasoned flat-top planchas to preserve maximum juiciness and flavor.

Colorful Nutrition

Vibrant Market Bowls & Seasonal Produce

Our market bowls are engineered for both culinary delight and sustained energy. We combine warm ancient grains like quinoa or pan-seared potato gnocchi with tender baby spinach, roasted vine tomatoes, and crisp seasonal vegetables tossed in vibrant vinaigrettes. Perfect for a quick, wholesome lunch along South End's Rail Trail.
