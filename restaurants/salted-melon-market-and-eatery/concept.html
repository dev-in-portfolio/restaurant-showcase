Neighborhood Market Evolution

Redefining How Charlotte Eats Every Day.

Salted Melon Market & Eatery was born from a simple realization: you shouldn't have to choose between quick convenience and culinary excellence. We created an open, sunny neighborhood gathering place where high-grade culinary ingredients, third-wave espresso, artisan grocery items, and warm hospitality live seamlessly under one roof.

Core Values

The Four Pillars of Salted Melon

Guided by food integrity, community energy, and multi-daypart hospitality.

Pillar 01

Everyday Gourmet Access

We reject the notion that gourmet dining should be reserved only for special occasions. We bring chef-driven techniques and pure ingredients into your everyday breakfast, lunch, and dinner routine.

Pillar 02

Seamless Multi-Part Living

Grab a cold brew and avocado toast on your morning commute, pick up a healthy grain bowl for lunch, or meet friends for seated dinner and cocktails on our South End Rail Trail patio.

Pillar 03

Neighborhood Market Roots

With our flagship at 100 W Worthington in South End and our second outpost at Phillips Place in SouthPark, we are deeply embedded in the fabric of Charlotte's most dynamic neighborhoods.

South End & Phillips Place

Two Distinct Homes, One Shared Standard

Whether you're stepping into our sunlit South End flagship along the Rail Trail or browsing the relaxed courtyard setting at Phillips Place in SouthPark, you will find the exact same dedication to friendly service, scratch cooking, and thoughtful food curation that defines the Salted Melon experience.
