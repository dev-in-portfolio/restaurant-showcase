Everyday Gourmet, Morning to Night.

Welcome to Salted Melon Market & Eatery, South End Charlotte’s all-day culinary destination at 100 W Worthington Avenue. We merge an artisanal scratch kitchen with a third-wave specialty espresso bar, craft cocktail lounge, and curated neighborhood grocery. From sunrise avocado toasts and nourishing market bowls to prime smash burgers and evening seated dining, we make exceptional food accessible every day.

Morning Craft

Artisan Avocado Toast & Farm Poached Eggs

Our morning staple starts with thick slices of country sourdough toasted golden brown, smeared with crushed California Hass avocados, topped with marinated heirloom cherry tomatoes, shaved aged parmesan, and two tender poached farm eggs with rich liquid yolks. Finished with a drizzle of cold-pressed olive oil and flaky sea salt.
