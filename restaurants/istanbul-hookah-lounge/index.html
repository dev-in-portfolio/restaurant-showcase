Historic Ambience

Uptown Charlotte's Hidden European Cobblestone Alley

Located in Brevard Court—Charlotte's beloved pedestrian walkway originally built in the 1920s—Istanbul Hookah Lounge provides both intimate indoor booth seating adorned with Turkish mosaic lamps and an open-air alleyway patio under glowing string lights.

Just steps from Romare Bearden Park and Truist Field, it is the premier late-night sanctuary to unwind after a night in Center City or gather with friends for deep conversation.
