# Evidence & Design Ledger for Istanbul Hookah Lounge

## Creative Brief
- **Client Identity:** Istanbul Hookah Lounge, situated at 138 Brevard Ct, Charlotte, NC 28202 in the historic Brevard Court cobblestone alleyway in Uptown Charlotte.
- **Concept:** Premier authentic Turkish and Mediterranean hookah lounge offering artisan shisha blends, custom hand-carved fresh fruit heads, ice-tip cooling hoses, 100% natural coconut coals, sand-brewed Turkish coffee, traditional Turkish teas, Mediterranean mezze, and handcrafted baklava.
- **Target Audience:** Uptown nightlife patrons, private celebration groups, couples, craft shisha enthusiasts, and after-dinner socializers looking for an exotic, relaxing atmosphere.
- **Design Language:** Deep Ottoman Midnight #0B0E14, Royal Sapphire #111827, Warm Turkish Gold #E5A93C, Spice Crimson #C84B31, and Glowing Ember #FF6B35. Rich ambient lighting, subtle ornate borders, responsive mobile drawer, and interactive shisha mixology & VIP cost calculator.

## Claim Ledger
- **Location Claim:** Located at 138 Brevard Ct in Uptown Charlotte's historic Brevard Court (French Quarter) across from Romare Bearden Park. Phone: (704) 333-5175.
- **Service Hours Claim:** Open Tuesday-Wednesday 2:00 PM to 12:00 AM (Midnight), Thursday-Saturday 2:00 PM to 2:00 AM, Sunday 2:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Closed Mondays.
- **Hookah & Flavors Claim:** Premium tobacco brands (Al Fakher, Starbuzz, Fumari, Tangiers), organic natural coconut charcoal, fresh fruit head upgrades (fresh pineapple, grapefruit, orange bowls), and frozen ice tips.
- **Catering & Drinks Claim:** Traditional sand-brewed Turkish coffee with Turkish delight (lokum), Rize black tea, apple mint tea, Mediterranean mezze platters (hummus, pita, dolmas, falafel), and pistachio baklava.

## Add-On Preservation
- **Interactive Shisha & VIP Experience Calculator:** Allows users to choose their guest party size (2 to 30 guests), select hookah package tiers (Bosphorus Classic, Sultan's VIP, Grand Bazaar Celebration), add fresh fruit heads, add ice tips, select tea/coffee service, and receive real-time price calculations with pre-filled email reservations.
- **Interactive Category Filter:** Live tab switching across All, Signature Hookahs, Fruit Bowls & Upgrades, Turkish Teas & Coffee, and Mediterranean Mezze & Sweets on menu.html.
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Expanding answers covering age requirements (21+), Brevard Court parking, coal management, fruit bowl longevity, and group bookings on contact.html.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- **Namespace:** .ihl-* exclusively used across HTML, CSS, and JS to prevent global style contamination.
- **Distinct Aesthetic:** Ottoman-inspired dark luxury theme with ornate gold filigree accents, rich jewel tones, and ambient ember glows, uniquely differentiating from pizzeria, brewery, burger, and cafe designs.
