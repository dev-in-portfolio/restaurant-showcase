Parking & Uptown Navigation

Brevard Court is a pedestrian-only brick walkway. There are multiple convenient parking decks and transit options within 2 blocks:

Closest Parking Decks:

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Guidelines

In strict compliance with North Carolina regulations, all guests must be 21 years of age or older. A valid, unexpired government-issued physical photo ID (Driver's License, State ID, or Passport) is required for entry at all times.
We exclusively use 100% natural compressed coconut shell charcoal. Our coals contain zero chemical accelerants or sulfur, providing a completely odorless, clean burn that highlights the pure notes of the tobacco blend.
Our hand-carved pineapple and grapefruit heads typically last between 2.0 to 2.5 hours with consistent dense clouds, as the natural fruit moisture prevents the tobacco from drying out while continuously infusing fresh tropical juices into the airflow.
Walk-ins are always warmly welcomed for both patio and indoor seating based on availability. For weekend peak nights (Thursday through Saturday after 9:00 PM) and groups of 4 or more, we strongly recommend booking a VIP table in advance.
