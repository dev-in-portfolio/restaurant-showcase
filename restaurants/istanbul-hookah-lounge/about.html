Our Heritage & Vision

The Istanbul Lounge Story

Transplanting the timeless hospitality, aromas, and social warmth of the Bosphorus to the heart of Uptown Charlotte.

From the Bosphorus to Brevard Court

In Turkey, hookah lounges and coffeehouses have served as central cultural gathering points for centuries. They are sanctuaries where time slows down, stories are exchanged, and friends connect over fragrant smoke and warm tea.

When we established Istanbul Hookah Lounge at 138 Brevard Court, we fell in love with the cobblestone alleyways that mirror the European feel of old Istanbul. We brought authentic brass hookahs, handcrafted mosaic lamps, sand-brewing apparatus, and the highest grade shisha tobaccos to create a unique haven in Center City Charlotte.

Guiding Principles

The Traditions We Uphold

Uncompromised Cleanliness

Every stem, glass base, silicone hose, and bowl is thoroughly sanitized between uses with commercial steam-grade equipment to ensure pure, unadulterated flavor.

Attentive Hospitality

Turkish hospitality means guests never have to search for fresh coals. Our hosts proactively manage coal temperatures and airflow throughout your stay.

A Welcoming Gathering Space

Whether you visit for an after-dinner Turkish coffee on the patio or celebrate a milestone with a full VIP section, our lounge offers a comfortable and respectful atmosphere.
