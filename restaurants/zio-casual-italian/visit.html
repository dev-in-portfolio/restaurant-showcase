Outdoor Trattoria

The Herb Garden Patio Seating

Our garden patio is one of the most romantic dining experiences in Charlotte:

  • First-Come Basis: Garden patio tables are seated on a walk-in, first-come basis weather permitting.
  • Aromatic Setting: Dine among living planter boxes of rosemary, sweet basil, and oregano beneath soft cafe lighting.
  • Heated in Shoulder Seasons: Radiant heaters extend outdoor comfort during crisp spring and autumn evenings.
Effortless Arrival

Parking & Neighborhood Access

Visiting Zio in historic Myers Park is simple:

  • On-Site Private Lot: Complimentary customer parking is located directly beside and behind our building on Middleton Drive.
  • Street Parking: Ample, free residential street parking is permitted along Middleton Drive.
  • Call-Ahead Takeout: Dial (704) 344-0101 to have your grilled pizzas, toasted ravioli, and pastas hot and ready for quick pickup.
