House Classic

Spaghetti & Hand-Rolled Meatballs

Our jumbo Italian meatballs are hand-rolled each morning using prime ground chuck, seasoned pork, pecorino cheese, fresh garlic, and Italian breadcrumbs. Braised in our signature San Marzano marinara and served over al dente pasta with freshly shaved Parmigiano-Reggiano, it is Myers Park’s favorite comfort plate.

Signature Antipasti

Crispy Toasted Ravioli

A St. Louis-inspired Italian treasure brought to North Carolina: four-cheese pasta pillows breaded in seasoned Italian breadcrumbs, fried until golden and crisp, dusted with aged pecorino, and served with a ramekin of warm, simmering marinara sauce. The quintessential starter for every dinner table.
