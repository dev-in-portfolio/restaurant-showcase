Open-Flame Grate • Artisan Fermentation

Direct Heat, Blistered Perfection.

At Zio, we do not bake pizzas on conveyor belts or inside standard ovens. We cook our hand-stretched dough directly on roaring open flame grates. The immediate contact produces a crisp, smoky crust with golden leopard spotting that gives every bite an unforgettable crunch.

The Three Searing Stages

How Zio Perfects the Grilled Pizza

Carefully calibrated heat, dough tension, and pristine Italian ingredients.

Stage 01

48-Hour Cold Ferment

Our dough is mixed with imported Italian flour, extra virgin olive oil, and sea salt, then cold-fermented for two full days to build rich aroma and complex, airy gluten structure.

Stage 02

Direct Grate Sear

The stretched round is brushed with garlic oil and laid flat over open fire grates. In seconds, air pockets billow up and blister with smoky, charred grill marks.

Stage 03

Hearth Oven Finish

Flipped and topped with crushed San Marzano tomatoes, buffalo mozzarella, and fresh garden herbs, the pizza finishes under intense top heat until the cheese bubbles.
