Charlotte Thai Pioneers • Established 1995

Three Decades of Royal Thai Tradition.

When Thai House opened its original dining room in 1995, Charlotte’s international food landscape was in its infancy. For thirty-one years, our kitchen has remained dedicated to introducing the Queen City to authentic Thai culinary culture: the balance of fresh lemongrass, galangal, and bird's eye chilies, paired with warm Southeast Asian graciousness.

Heritage Foundations

The Values That Guide Thai House

Hospitality principles preserved through generations of family stewardship.

Heritage 01

Authentic Recipe Integrity

We refuse shortcuts or westernized syrups. Our pastes are compounded from fresh herbs, and our broths simmer for hours to achieve pure Southeast Asian depth.

Heritage 02

Family-Style Dining

Thai dining is inherently communal. We encourage ordering dishes to share—curries, wok noodles, stir-fries, and rice—bringing families and colleagues closer together.

Heritage 03

Community Loyalty

Serving Charlotte across Cotswold, University City, and South Charlotte for 31 years, watching second and third-generation patrons return to our dining tables.

Southeast Asian Ambience

Warm Dining Rooms in Cotswold & University

Step inside our dining rooms to find tranquil teakwood decor, Thai silk accents, and soft ambient lighting. Whether joining us for a swift weekday business lunch or an extended multi-course celebratory family dinner, our staff greets every guest with heartfelt Thai hospitality.
