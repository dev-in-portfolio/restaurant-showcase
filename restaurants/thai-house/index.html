Charlotte Thai Institution • Established 1995

Royal Thai Curries, Wok-Fired Pad Thai.

Welcome to Thai House, Charlotte’s foundational Thai culinary home since 1995. With flagship dining rooms in Cotswold (110 S Sharon Amity Rd) and University City (230 E W.T. Harris Blvd), we prepare time-honored Royal Thai curries simmered in rich coconut milk, wok-seared tamarind Pad Thai with jumbo shrimp, and flame-grilled chicken satay.

Signature Dish

Royal Green Curry with Jasmine Rice

Our Kaeng Khiao Wan begins with crushed fresh green chilies, lemongrass, shallots, garlic, and galangal. Gently bloomed in coconut cream until the aromatic oils rise, then simmered with tender chicken, bamboo shoots, kaffir lime leaves, and red chili slivers, served with fragrant steamed jasmine rice.

Street Food Craft

Flame-Grilled Chicken Satay

Marinated in coconut milk, turmeric, coriander, and lemongrass, our chicken skewers are flame-grilled over high heat and generously coated in our warm, house-made roasted peanut sauce on a banana leaf platter.
