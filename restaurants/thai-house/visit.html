Takeout & Catering Directives

Guidelines for enjoying Thai House at home or at the office:

  • Advance Ordering: Order online or call ahead for rapid pickup. During peak dinner hours (6:00 PM – 8:00 PM), please allow 25 to 35 minutes for curry and wok preparation.
  • Dedicated Pickup Counter: Both locations feature separate pickup stations near the entrance so you can grab your order without waiting for table service.
  • Office & Party Catering: Large family-style trays of Pad Thai, curries, and satay skewers are available for corporate luncheons and private gatherings.
Spice Customization

Thai Spice Level Guide

Every curry, stir-fry, and noodle dish can be adjusted to your desired heat:

  • Level 1 (Mild): Warm, aromatic spices with minimal heat—ideal for children and gentle palates.
  • Level 2 (Medium): A pleasant, lingering warmth that complements the rich coconut milk.
  • Level 3 (Hot): Bold and pronounced chili heat crafted with fresh Thai bird's eye chilies.
  • Level 4 & 5 (Thai Fiery): Genuine Bangkok street heat with intense roasted chili flakes. For experienced chili enthusiasts only.
