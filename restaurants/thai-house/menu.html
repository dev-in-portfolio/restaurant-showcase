Royal Thai Curries

Simmered in rich coconut milk and served with fragrant steamed Jasmine Rice. Choice of Chicken, Pork, Tofu, or Beef (Shrimp +$3.00).

Classic Green Curry (Kaeng Khiao Wan) $16.95

Aromatic green chili paste simmered in coconut milk with crisp bamboo shoots, Thai eggplant, red bell peppers, kaffir lime leaves, and sweet Thai holy basil.

Panang Curry $16.95

Rich and creamy coconut curry scented with ground peanuts, fresh kaffir lime leaves, sweet red bell peppers, and tender green string beans.

Royal Masaman Curry $16.95

Southern Thai mild spiced curry simmered with tender potato wedges, whole roasted peanuts, sweet onions, cinnamon, and whole cardamom pods.

Traditional Red Curry (Kaeng Phet) $16.95

Spicy red chili paste simmered in silky coconut milk with bamboo shoots, bell peppers, zucchini, and fresh fragrant Thai basil.

Golden Yellow Curry (Kaeng Kari) $16.95

Gentle yellow curry infused with turmeric and cumin seeds, simmered with sweet potatoes, Spanish onions, and tender carrots.

Roasted Duck Red Curry $21.95

Crispy boneless roast duck simmered in red coconut curry with sweet pineapple chunks, cherry tomatoes, and fresh Thai basil leaves.

Wok Noodles & Fried Rice

Wok-seared over intense flames to achieve signature smoky wok-hei flavor.

Classic Shrimp Pad Thai $17.50

Stir-fried thin rice noodles in house tamarind sauce with jumbo shrimp, farm-fresh eggs, pressed bean curd, preserved radish, crisp bean sprouts, scallions, and crushed peanuts.

Pad See Ewe (Sweet Soy Noodles) $15.95

Broad flat rice noodles charred in dark sweet soy sauce with tender sliced chicken or pork, farm eggs, and crisp Chinese broccoli.

Drunken Noodles (Pad Kee Mao) $15.95

Wide flat noodles tossed with fresh minced garlic, spicy bird's eye chilies, sweet onions, bell peppers, tomatoes, and aromatic Thai holy basil.

Traditional Basil Fried Rice $15.50

Fragrant jasmine rice wok-fried with farm eggs, garlic, bird's eye chilies, sweet onions, bell peppers, and crispy Thai basil leaves.

Tropical Pineapple Fried Rice $16.50

Jasmine rice tossed with golden pineapple tidbits, toasted whole cashews, sweet raisins, eggs, scallions, and fragrant yellow curry powder.

Pad Woon Sen (Glass Noodles) $15.95

Delicate mung bean glass noodles stir-fried with eggs, wood ear mushrooms, cabbage, celery, carrots, and sweet onions in light garlic soy.

Crispy Thai Spring Rolls (4 Pieces) $8.95

Tom Yum Goong (Spicy Lemongrass Soup) $9.50 / $16.50
