The Italian Wine Vault

Italian cuisine comes alive when paired with native regional wines. Our floor-to-ceiling glass wine cellar features rare Barolo, Brunello di Montalcino, Chianti Classico Gran Selezione, & Bolgheri Super Tuscans served via Coravin system.

Cellar Standards

The 3 Pillars of Our Wine Vault

1. Piedmont Barolo & Nebbiolo

Earthy, floral Nebbiolos with firm tannins that cut through rich braised veal Bolognese & black truffle pastas.

2. Tuscan Sangiovese & Super Tuscans

Iconic Tuscan Cabernet & Sangiovese blends featuring Sassicaia, Tignanello, & Brunello di Montalcino vintage reserves.

3. Coravin Precision Preservation

Argon gas needle insertion allows guests to enjoy rare 20-year-old vintage cellar bottles by the single glass.
