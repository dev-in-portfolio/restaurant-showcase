🍝 Caputo 00 Flour & Bronze Dies

The Science of Fresh Pasta

True Italian pasta is an art of moisture balance & gluten development. At Italo, we knead imported Italian Caputo 00 flour with golden egg yolks daily, extruding dough through heavy bronze dies to create micro-porous surfaces that capture every drop of black truffle butter & slow-cooked ragù.

Pasta Craftsmanship

The 3 Pillars of Hand-Rolled Italian Pasta

1. Italian Caputo 00 & Egg Yolk Dough

Milled wheat flour blended with high-fat farm yolks for silky texture, vibrant yellow color, & springy bite.

2. Heavy Bronze Die Extrusion

Extruded through antique bronze dies, creating a rustic chalky finish that clings to emulsified sauces.

3. Precision Al Dente Boiling

Flash-boiled in salted copper kettles for exactly 120 seconds, then finished directly inside the simmering pan sauce.
