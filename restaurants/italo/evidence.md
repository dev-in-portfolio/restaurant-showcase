## Original Audit Weakness

Italo relies on basic third-party reservation widgets (Resy). Located at 100 N Tryon St in Uptown Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its hand-rolled pasta craft, high-end Italian wine vault, private tower dining, and culinary roots.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Uptown Charlotte Tower Location:** 100 N Tryon St, Charlotte NC 28202 (inside Bank of America Corporate Tower at corner of Trade & College St).
2. **Italian Trattoria & Pasta House Specialties:** Hand-Shaped Tagliatelle al Tartufo (Black Truffle Butter), Slow-Braised Veal & Beef Rigatoni Bolognese, Squid Ink Chitarra with Wild White Shrimp, Wood-Fired Mediterranean Sea Bass, Charred Octopus Carpaccio, Super Tuscan Wine Vault, & Traditional Tableside Tiramisu.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 340-9499; Tuesday – Saturday 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM (Sunday & Monday Closed).

### Core Design Moves

1. **High-Fashion Roman Serif Typography:** Elegant serif (*Bodoni Moda*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and sommelier cellar mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Barolo Burgundy & Florentine Tuscan Gold Palette:** High-end Italian palette anchored in rich Barolo burgundy (`#4A0E17`), Florentine Tuscan gold (`#D4AF37`), Carrara marble white (`#FAFAF9`), and deep espresso walnut (`#1A0C0E`).
3. **"The Hand-Rolled Pasta & Barolo Wine Vault Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`hand-crafted-pasta-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing hand-shaped pastas alongside Super Tuscan wines & wood-fired seafood.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Ilios Crafted Greek` — Aegean Coastal Mediterranean with Cinzel fonts and Deep Aegean Cobalt/Azure.
2. `Honeybear Bake Shop` — Warm Golden Honey Bakery with Playfair Display fonts and Deep Cocoa/Honey Amber.
3. `Home Brew Taproom & Tunes` — Industrial Craft Beer with Syne fonts and Industrial Iron/Copper.

### Divergent Choices for Italo

- **Hero Composition:** Venetian Renaissance & Barolo Burgundy Wine Vault split-hero layout: left side features a Tuscan gold stamp badge (`"ARTISANAL PASTA HOUSE & WINE BAR • UPTOWN CHARLOTTE (100 N TRYON ST)"`), high-fashion Roman serif (*Bodoni Moda*), Barolo burgundy & Tuscan gold palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of handmade pasta & stone oven trattoria dishes.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Italo Vault Cards** (`italo-vault-card`) and Uptown Charlotte trattoria highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`italo-header`, `italo-brand`, `trattoria-hero-stage`, `florentine-stamp-badge`, `italo-vault-card`, `italo-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
