# Evidence & Brand Ledger: Hippin\' Hops Brewstillery

## Creative Brief
- **Brand Identity:** Hippin\' Hops Brewstillery is Charlotte\'s pioneering Black-owned craft brewery and distillery taproom, established by co-founders Clarence and Donnica Boston in Uptown Charlotte\'s Second Ward / Brooklyn Village district.
- **Atmosphere & Aesthetic:** Modern industrial brewpub with exposed brick, warm copper distillation stills, charred barrel wood accents, deep charcoal slate finishes, golden amber lighting, lively music sessions, weekend DJ sets, game-day viewing, and an expansive outdoor urban patio.
- **Core Value Proposition:** High-gravity and sessionable craft beers brewed in-house paired with small-batch artisan distilled spirits (rum, whiskey, flavored spirits) and a signature coastal Carolina & Gulf-inspired oyster bar and Cajun seafood menu.
- **Key Offerings:**
  - In-House Brewed Craft Beers: Cherry Limelight Sour, Baby Momma Drama IPA, Hazy Hop IPA, So So Stout, Crisp Blonde Ale, Craft Seltzers.
  - Small-Batch Distilled Spirits & Signature Cocktails: Shake Ya RUMPunch, Green Apple Long Island, Smoked Bourbon Old Fashioned, House Craft Margaritas.
  - Artisan Baked Oyster Bar: Garlic Butter, Smoked Gouda, Rockefeller, Sweet Spice, Bacon Cheddar, Pesto Parm.
  - Cajun Seafood Specialties: Seafood Mac Daddy Roll (crab, crawfish, shrimp), Cajun Crawfish Roll, Fried Catfish Nuggs, Big Fried and Baked Shrimp, Shrimp Deviled Eggs.
  - Taproom Bites & Wings: Crispy Jumbo Wings tossed in Bourbon BBQ, Hot Honey, or Lemon Pepper.
  - Private Events & Taproom Buyouts: Custom oyster bar catering, draft beer & cocktail packages, brewery tours, and live party bookings.
- **Operating Hours:** Wednesday-Thursday 5:00 PM - 11:00 PM; Friday 5:00 PM - 12:00 AM; Saturday 12:00 PM - 12:00 AM; Sunday 12:00 PM - 11:00 PM; Monday-Tuesday Closed.
- **Location:** 650 E. Brooklyn Village Ave, Suite D, Charlotte, NC 28202. Phone: (704) 910-2016.

## Claim Ledger
1. **Pioneering Black-Owned Brewstillery:** Co-founded by Clarence and Donnica Boston, bringing Georgia\'s first Black-owned brick-and-mortar craft brewery concept to Uptown Charlotte as Charlotte\'s first Black-owned distillery and brewpub.
2. **Fresh Oyster Bar & Cajun Seafood:** Highlighting fresh Gulf and Atlantic oysters baked in half-dozen custom recipes (Smoked Gouda, Garlic Butter, Rockefeller, Bacon Cheddar) alongside overflowing crawfish and crab rolls.
3. **Small-Batch Distillation & Craft Taps:** Rotating onsite craft draft lines spanning crisp lagers, fruited sours, and double IPAs alongside house-distilled artisanal spirits and craft cocktails.
4. **Second Ward / Brooklyn Village Destination:** Located at 650 E Brooklyn Village Ave Ste D, steps away from the NASCAR Hall of Fame, Charlotte Convention Center, and Uptown sports venues.
5. **Interactive Taproom Party Calculator:** Providing transparent pricing for event hosts booking party packages, oyster dozen platters, craft draft pitchers, and cocktail service.

## Add-On Preservation
- **Interactive Party & Oyster Feast Calculator:** Allows corporate event planners, birthday parties, and sports watch groups to dynamically calculate costs based on guest count, beer/cocktail drink tier, oyster dozens, and seafood platters with real-time totals and instant quote requests.
- **Live Brewstillery Tap List Filter:** Interactive filtering by style (IPAs, Sours, Stouts, Spirits & Cocktails, Ciders/Seltzers) with ABV, IBU, and flavor notes.
- **Responsive Drawer Navigation:** Mobile drawer menu with aria controls, focus trap, and touch-friendly navigation.
- **Location & Booking Hub:** Embedded operating schedules, event reservation request portal, and direct taproom contact links.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- **Namespace:** Dedicated \.hhp-*\ CSS prefix to prevent global selector leakage and enforce styling isolation.
- **Visual Distinction:** Bold dark-mode brewpub aesthetic pairing deep stout charcoal (\#18181b\), radiant amber hop gold (\#f59e0b\), warm copper brass (\#ea580c\), and warm malt parchment (\#fef3c7\).
- **Typography:** Display serif headings (\Cinzel Decorative\ / \Playfair Display\) combined with modern sans-serif body (\Plus Jakarta Sans\) reflecting the heritage of distilling and modern taproom culture.
- **Zero Placeholder Words & Zero Emojis:** Full factual data without placeholder text, simulated inputs, or unverified claims.
