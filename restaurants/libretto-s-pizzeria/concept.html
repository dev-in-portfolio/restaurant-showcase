NYC Tradition & Italian Passion

Libretto's Pizzeria was founded with a clear passion: bring the authentic taste of Little Italy NYC brick oven pizza to South Charlotte. Located at 15205 John J Delaney Dr, Suite A in Ballantyne, we serve as a neighborhood gathering place for families, sports teams, & pizza lovers.
