The Art of Garlic Knots & Pasta

No NYC pizzeria experience is complete without garlic knots & slow-cooked Italian pasta classics. At Libretto's Pizzeria, our kitchen hand-rolls dough into golden knots tossed in fresh garlic butter, serving baked ziti & chicken parmigiana with Chianti wines.

The 3 Pillars of Italian Kitchen Artistry

1. Hand-Tied Pizza Dough Knots

Strips of fresh daily dough hand-knotted, proofed, & baked golden with fragrant garlic infusion.

2. Slow-Simmered Marinara

Baked Ziti Siciliana & Eggplant Parmigiana layered with ricotta & melted whole milk mozzarella.
