🍕 550°F Volcanic Stone Deck Baking

The Art of Brick Oven Pizza

The hallmark of true NYC pizzeria pizza is the heat of a stone deck. At Libretto's Pizzeria, our pizzas are baked directly on 550°F volcanic stone tiles. This intense conductive heat creates a crisp lower crust, airy interior pockets, & caramelized cheese edges.

The 3 Pillars of NYC Brick Oven Excellence

1. 48-Hour Cold Fermentation

Spring wheat flour dough rested 48 hours to develop rich complex yeast flavor & easy digestibility.

2. Crushed San Marzano Tomatoes

Italian plum tomatoes hand-crushed with sea salt, garlic, & extra virgin olive oil for bright sweet acidity.
