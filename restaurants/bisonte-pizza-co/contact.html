Common Questions & Answers

Buffalo pizza features a dough with more height and airiness than a thin NY slice, baked in a deck pan so the bottom remains crisp while the interior stays tender. The sauce is slightly sweeter to complement generous whole-milk mozzarella and thick cup-and-char pepperoni.
Beef on Weck is an authentic Western New York sandwich made of slow-roasted thin-sliced beef dipped in savory beef au jus and served on a freshly baked kummelweck roll topped with caraway seeds and coarse kosher salt. It is traditionally accompanied by atomic horseradish.
