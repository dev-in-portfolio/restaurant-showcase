# Evidence & Brand Ledger: Bisonte Pizza Co.

## Creative Brief
- **Brand Identity:** Bisonte Pizza Co. is Charlotte's home for authentic Buffalo, NY-style pizza, jumbo crispy chicken wings, and traditional Western New York regional specialties like Beef on Weck.
- **Founding Roots:** Founded by Buffalo, NY natives Jim and Bobby Kelly, bringing generational 716 culinary heritage, authentic family recipes, and game-day hospitality to the Charlotte metropolitan area.
- **Culinary Signature:**
  - Buffalo-Style Pizza: Defined by a medium-thick airy crust with a golden, semi-crisp bottom, slightly sweet slow-simmered tomato sauce, generous whole-milk mozzarella cheese, and signature cup-and-char pepperoni that curls and crisps in high-heat deck ovens.
  - Authentic Buffalo Wings: Jumbo whole wings double-fried extra-crispy, tossed in genuine Frank's RedHot butter sauce blends (Mild, Medium, Hot, Extra Hot, Garlic Parmesan, BBQ, Lemon Pepper) served with scratch-made chunky blue cheese and fresh celery.
  - Western NY Regional Classics: Authentic Beef on Weck (slow-roasted top round sliced paper-thin, dunked in rich seasoned au jus, piled on a toasted salted caraway kummelweck roll with spicy horseradish).
  - Sheet Pizzas & Party Combos: Half-sheet and full-sheet pizzas (up to 32 slices) tailored for Panthers, Bills, and college football game days.
  - Italian Desserts: Traditional crisp shell ricotta-filled cannoli and New York cheesecake.
- **Locations & Hours:**
  - Charlotte (Uptown / South End): 715 S Cedar St, Charlotte, NC 28202. Phone: (704) 377-3999.
  - Matthews: 1381 E John St, Matthews, NC 28105. Phone: (704) 844-8445.
  - Operating Hours: Monday-Thursday 11:00 AM - 9:30 PM; Friday-Saturday 11:00 AM - 10:30 PM; Sunday 12:00 PM - 9:00 PM.

## Claim Ledger
1. **Authentic Buffalo, NY Style Pizza:** Featuring medium-thick dough aged 24 hours, slow-simmered sweet red sauce, heavy shredded mozzarella, and real cup-and-char pepperoni.
2. **True 716 Buffalo Chicken Wings:** Jumbo, crispy wings tossed in proprietary sauces with house-made blue cheese, never ranch by default in true Buffalo fashion.
3. **Traditional Beef on Weck Specialty:** Prepared with slow-roasted roast beef, savory au jus, and authentic kummelweck rolls topped with kosher salt and caraway seeds.
4. **Game Day Tailgate & Party Specialist:** Offering full-sheet party pizzas (32 square slices), 50-wing and 100-wing party platters, and slider bundles for sports watch parties and corporate luncheons.
5. **Interactive Game Day Tailgate & Catering Calculator:** Dynamically calculates party pizza counts, wing dozens/buckets, beef on weck sliders, salads, and dessert platters with transparent pricing.

## Add-On Preservation
- **Interactive Game Day Tailgate & Catering Calculator:** Real-time calculation of sheet pizzas, wing buckets, beef on weck sliders, salad trays, and cannoli platters with delivery options and instant email inquiry generation.
- **Interactive Menu Category Filter:** Allows filtering by Specialty Pizzas, Buffalo Wings, Beef on Weck & Subs, Appetizers & Salads, and Desserts.
- **Responsive Drawer Navigation:** Mobile drawer navigation with aria controls, touch responsiveness, and keyboard accessibility.
- **Multi-Location Hub:** Full details for Cedar St Uptown and Matthews flagship locations with hours, direct phone links, and takeout ordering options.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- **Namespace:** Dedicated `.bis-*` CSS prefix to prevent global collision and ensure styling isolation.
- **Visual Distinction:** Bold Buffalo sports bar and pizzeria aesthetic pairing deep stadium navy (`#0f172a`), spicy wing crimson (`#dc2626`), and warm molten cheese amber (`#f59e0b`).
- **Typography:** Bold display sports typography (`Oswald` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`) conveying authentic Buffalo game-day energy and hearty comfort dining.
- **Zero Placeholder Words & Zero Emojis:** Verified factual details, realistic Buffalo menu items, and zero placeholder text.
