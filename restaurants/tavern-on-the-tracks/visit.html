Transit & Parking

Arriving at Tavern on the Tracks

Navigating South End to visit us:

  • Light Rail Transit: We are located directly behind the Bland Street LYNX Blue Line station. Walk directly off the platform onto our patio in less than 60 seconds.
  • Rail Trail Pedestrian Access: Easy walk or bike ride along the Charlotte Rail Trail with bicycle racks available nearby.
  • Neighborhood Parking: Metered street parking along South Tryon Street, Camden Road, and paid public parking decks throughout South End. Rideshare drop-off is convenient on Tryon.
Sports & Watch Parties

Game Day Directives

Guidelines for enjoying game days at the Tavern:

  • Seating Policy: All indoor booths, bar stools, and outdoor patio tables are seated strictly first-come, first-served. We do not accept game-day reservations.
  • Buffalo Bills Watch Parties: During NFL Sundays, the tavern fills quickly. We recommend arriving 60 to 90 minutes before kickoff to secure prime seating with game audio.
  • Patio Seating: Our covered outdoor patio is dog-friendly and heated during cooler fall and winter months.
