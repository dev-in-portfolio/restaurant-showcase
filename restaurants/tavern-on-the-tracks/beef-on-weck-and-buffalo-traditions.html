Western New York Heritage • Kummelweck Craft

The Kummelweck Roll, Carved Rare Roast Beef.

While Buffalo is globally renowned for its chicken wings, ask any Western New Yorker about their true culinary soul food, and they will point you to Beef on Weck. At Tavern on the Tracks, we honor this 140-year-old Upstate New York tradition with absolute fidelity right in South End Charlotte.

Culinary Rules

The Four Tenets of True Beef on Weck

You cannot substitute ingredients when making an authentic Buffalo classic.

Tenet 01

The Kummelweck Crust

The roll must be brushed with egg wash and encrusted with coarse kosher pretzel salt and whole caraway seeds, delivering an unmistakable crunch, aroma, and savory punch.

Tenet 02

Paper-Thin Rare Beef

Top round beef is slow-roasted whole until juicy medium-rare, then shaved wafer-thin and piled high in generous ripples across the soft crumb of the bottom bun.

Tenet 03

The Au Jus Dip & Horseradish

The top half of the bun is submerged directly into hot, rich beef au jus before topping, finished with pure freshly-grated spicy white horseradish that clears the sinuses.

A Buffalo Home in the Queen City

When Tavern on the Tracks opened in 2002, Charlotte was welcoming thousands of New York transplants seeking warmer weather but missing home. For over two decades, our dining room and bar has been the undisputed gathering place on Sundays, bringing generations together over Labatt Blue and Beef on Weck.
