South End Charlotte • Established 2002

Twenty-Four Years on the South End Tracks.

When Tavern on the Tracks first unlocked its doors in 2002 at 1411 South Tryon Street, South End was an industrial corridor of textile mills and quiet warehouses. Through two decades of unprecedented growth and the arrival of the LYNX Blue Line light rail, our tavern has remained steadfast: an unpretentious, welcoming haven serving authentic Buffalo comfort food, icy beers, and genuine community hospitality.

Core Values

The Foundations of Tavern on the Tracks

The neighborhood values that have anchored our pub since day one.

Value 01

Buffalo Culinary Authenticity

We never compromise on tradition. From sourcing real caraway-and-salt kummelweck rolls to frying un-breaded wings and serving Labatt Blue, we deliver genuine Western New York flavors.

Value 02

Neighborhood Inclusivity

No velvet ropes or pretentious cocktail attitudes. Whether you arrive in running gear off the Rail Trail or dressed for a night out, everyone has a place at our bar.

Value 03

Electric Sports Passion

We are proud to be the heart of Charlotte's Buffalo Bills and sports community, creating an unbeatable, high-energy atmosphere every weekend of the year.

Community Roots

A South End Living Room

While trendy cocktail bars and upscale apartments have filled South End, locals know that Tavern on the Tracks is where memories are forged. Stop by after work for a cold pint, meet neighbors on the patio, or bring out-of-town guests to experience our legendary Beef on Weck.
