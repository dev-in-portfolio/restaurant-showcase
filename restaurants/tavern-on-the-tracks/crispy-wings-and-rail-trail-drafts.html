Wing Integrity

The Anatomy of True Buffalo Wings

What sets our wings apart from ordinary sports bars.

Technique 01

Never Breaded, Pure Crisp

Authentic Buffalo wings must never be coated in flour or batter. High-temperature frying renders the skin naturally blistered and crunchy while locking in meat juiciness.

Technique 02

The Legendary Gold Rush

Our proprietary house glaze blends clover honey, tangy mustard, hickory barbecue, and cayenne heat, creating a complex sweet-spicy coating that has won fans across Charlotte.

Technique 03

Chunky House Blue Cheese

We make our blue cheese dressing from scratch with real Danish blue cheese crumbles, mayonnaise, buttermilk, and spices—thick enough to hold to the celery sticks.

The Rail Trail Tapline

Cold Pints on the Rail Trail

Situated right beside the LYNX light rail tracks, our covered outdoor patio is one of South End’s favorite social spots. Whether catching a summer breeze with your pup, watching the trains roll by, or cheering on the Carolina Panthers and Buffalo Bills, cold beer flows daily.
