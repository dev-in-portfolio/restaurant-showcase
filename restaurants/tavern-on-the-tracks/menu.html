Buffalo Specialties & Famous Sandwiches

Tender, slow-roasted roast beef sliced thin and dipped in hot au jus, piled high onto an authentic roll crusted with kosher salt and caraway seeds, served with freshly grated hot horseradish.

Buffalo Chicken Finger Sub $14.50

Crispy breaded chicken breast tenders tossed in medium Buffalo sauce, melted provolone cheese, cool blue cheese dressing, shredded lettuce, and tomato on a toasted sub roll.

Tracks Smash Cheeseburger $13.95

Two fresh ground chuck smash patties griddled on the flat-top with American cheese, caramelized onions, dill pickle chips, and house secret burger sauce on a butter-toasted brioche bun.

Classic Italian Meatball Grinder $13.50

Jumbo handmade beef and pork meatballs simmered in rustic Italian marinara sauce, smothered in melted whole-milk mozzarella cheese on a toasted garlic grinder roll.

Rail Trail Turkey & Bacon Club $13.50

Thinly sliced smoked turkey breast, crispy thick-cut Applewood bacon, sharp cheddar cheese, lettuce, tomato, and garlic herb mayonnaise on toasted artisan sourdough.

Grilled Philly Ribeye Cheesesteak $14.95

Shaved ribeye steak griddled with sweet onions, green bell peppers, and melted white American cheese on a warm Amoroso hearth-baked roll.
