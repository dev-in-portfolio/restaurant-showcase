The Science of Craft Drafts

Great taprooms require meticulous cold chain management and passionate brewery curation. At Home Brew Taproom & Tunes, we pour 24 rotating draft lines from independent Carolina microbreweries at a crisp 36°F.

Taproom Science

The 3 Pillars of Draft Quality

1. Weekly Line Recirculation

Our draft lines are acid-cleaned & recirculated weekly to eliminate beer stone & preserve pure hop aroma.

2. Direct-Draw Cold Vault

Kegs rest inside a dedicated walk-in cooler 5 feet from the taps, preventing warm fobbing or carbonation loss.

3. Carolina Brewery Curation

We partner directly with Union County, Charlotte, & Blue Ridge microbreweries to pour fresh limited keg drops.
