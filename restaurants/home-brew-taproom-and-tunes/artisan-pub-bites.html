🍾 Artisan Pretzels & Stone Flatbreads

The Craft of Pub Bites

Great craft beer deserves exceptional savory snacks. At Home Brew Taproom & Tunes, we bake jumbo Bavarian pretzels fresh daily, serving them warm with house amber beer cheese & spicy mustard alongside stone-baked flatbreads.

The 3 Pillars of Artisan Taproom Snacks

1. Warm Bavarian Pretzels

Authentic lye-dipped pretzel twists baked golden, brushed with real butter, & salted with coarse sea salt.

2. Amber Beer Cheese Dip

White cheddar, smoked gouda, & garlic reduction folded into local amber ale for maximum rich umami dip.

3. High-Heat Stone Flatbreads

Thin, crispy dough crusts topped with prosciutto, hot honey, buffalo mozzarella, & fresh basil baked at 650°F.
