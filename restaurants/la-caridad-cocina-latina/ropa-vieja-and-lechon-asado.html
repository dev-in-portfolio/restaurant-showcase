Dominican Roasting Art

Pernil Asado al Horno

In the Dominican Republic and Cuba, no feast is complete without Pernil. We start by scoring whole pork shoulders and rubbing them with our house-ground adobo: crushed garlic cloves, sour orange juice (naranja agria), dried oregano, black pepper, and sea salt.

After marinating for a full 24 hours to let the citrus tenderize the meat to the bone, it roasts low and slow for five hours before receiving a high-heat finish to achieve blistered, shatteringly crisp pork crackling skin.

  • Whole bone-in roast for maximum flavor retention
  • Signature crisp cuerito cracklings on every platter
  • Available as individual dinner combo or full celebration tray
Cuban National Treasure

Ropa Vieja Tradicional

Named for its resemblance to colorful shredded garments, Ropa Vieja is the crown jewel of Cuban homestyle cuisine. We gently simmer premium flank steak with aromatics until it separates into juicy ribbons.

The shredded beef is then folded into a simmering sofrito base of sweet red and green bell peppers, Spanish yellow onions, garlic, Spanish pimento olives, cumin, and dry white wine. The result is a savory, subtly tangy sauce that coats every single fiber of beef.

  • 100% prime flank steak hand-pulled for authentic texture
  • Simmered with pimento olives and sweet bell pepper sofrito
  • Served traditionally over fluffy white rice with black bean sauce
