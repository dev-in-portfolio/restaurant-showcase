The Wooden Pilon Tradition

The Craft of Authentic Mofongo

Mofongo is an indigenous Caribbean masterpiece combining African, Taino, and Spanish culinary traditions. Green plantains are sliced into thick wheels, fried until golden, and transferred immediately into a wooden pilon (mortar and pestle).

We mash the hot plantains with freshly minced garlic, sea salt, olive oil, and crispy chicharron cracklings, shaping the mixture into a savory dome served with a cup of piping hot seasoned beef broth.

  • Mashed fresh to order for optimal fluffy texture
  • Choice of toppings: Garlic Shrimp (Ajillo), Pernil, or Crispy Pork Chunks
  • Served with rich au jus broth for dipping or pouring
