Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about our kitchen, dining format, and preparation methods.

What type of cuisine does La Caridad serve? +

We specialize in authentic Caribbean Latin cuisine with deep roots in Dominican and Cuban cooking. Our offerings include slow-roasted meats, stewed chicken and beef, savory mofongo, seasoned rice varieties, and handmade tropical desserts.

Do you have a daily steam table for fast takeout? +

Yes! We feature a daily hot steam counter stocked every morning with fresh Pernil, Pollo Guisado, Ropa Vieja, Rabo Encendido (oxtail), Arroz con Gandules, Moro de Habichuelas, and hot sides for lightning-fast takeout service.

Can I place catering orders for weekend events? +

Absolutely. We prepare full and half catering pans for birthdays, corporate events, and holidays. We recommend placing large tray orders 24 to 48 hours in advance by calling us at (704) 537-8890.
