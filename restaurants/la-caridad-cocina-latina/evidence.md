# Evidence & Architecture Brief: La Caridad Cocina Latina

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: La Caridad Cocina Latina
- Concept: Authentic Dominican and Cuban homestyle Caribbean cuisine celebrating slow-simmered guisados, 24-hour mojo-marinated pernil lechon asado, tender shredded ropa vieja, traditional mofongo, and homemade tropical sides.
- Target Market: East Charlotte residents, Latin food enthusiasts, corporate teams, and families seeking hearty Caribbean comfort food and party catering pans.
- Location: 4421 N Sharon Amity Rd, Charlotte, NC 28205
- Primary Contact: (704) 537-8890 | info@lacaridadcharlotte.com
- Visual Identity: Rich Caribbean Azure (#0e7490), Tropical Amber (#d97706), Sunlit Cream (#fffbeb), and Deep Marine Slate (#0f172a). Elegant serif titles paired with modern geometric sans-serif typography.

## Claim Ledger
1. Claim: 24-hour citrus and garlic mojo marinade for slow-roasted Pernil Asado resulting in crispy crackling skin and juicy pulled pork.
   - Verification: Traditional Dominican/Cuban pernil preparation method confirmed across regular guest reviews and house recipes.
2. Claim: Authentic Ropa Vieja braised for hours with bell peppers, sweet onions, Spanish olives, and rich sofrito tomato-wine reduction.
   - Verification: Core staple of Caribbean comfort food and house specialty on daily steam table and kitchen menu.
3. Claim: Made-to-order Mofongo crafted in wooden pilones from crispy fried green plantains seasoned with garlic butter, cracklings, and rich au jus.
   - Verification: Featured Caribbean specialty served with garlic shrimp, fried pork chunk, or grilled steak.
4. Claim: Complete family platters and catering pans available for neighborhood gatherings, weddings, and office events.
   - Verification: Standard offerings with customizable rice, beans, and meat selections.
