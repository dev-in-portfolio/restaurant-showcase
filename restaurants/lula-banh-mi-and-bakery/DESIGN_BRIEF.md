# Design Brief: Lula Bánh Mì + Bakery

## Restaurant Identity & Positioning
- **Restaurant Name**: Lula Bánh Mì + Bakery
- **Cuisine & Concept**: Vietnamese Street Food & Bakery
- **Tagline**: Premium Culinary Craftsmanship in Charlotte, NC
- **Location**: Charlotte Metro Area, NC

## Creative Art Direction
- **Named Direction**: Vietnamese Street Food & Bakery Bespoke Showcase
- **Typography Logic**: Display font `'Syne', sans-serif` paired with `'Inter', sans-serif` for clean hierarchy and premium legibility.
- **Color Logic**: Primary accent `#6366f1` with deep dark backdrop `#0f172a` providing strong contrast.
- **Signature Interaction**: Lula Bánh Mì + Bakery Flavor Explorer built directly into the home experience to guide visitor discovery.
