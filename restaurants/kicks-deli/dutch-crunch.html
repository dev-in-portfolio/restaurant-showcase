The Dutch Crunch Bread Guide

Baking Heritage

What Makes Dutch Crunch Legendary?

Known in the Netherlands as tijgerbrood (tiger bread) and popularized across Northern California delis, Dutch Crunch gets its signature look and explosive texture from a special rice flour, yeast, sesame oil, and sugar paste applied to the dough just before baking.

As the bread rises and expands in high-temperature ovens, the gluten-free rice paste dries faster than the underlying dough, cracking into an intricate golden mosaic pattern that provides irresistible crunch and toasted savory aroma.

At Kicks Deli, we bake our Dutch Crunch fresh every morning inside Monarch Market so that every sandwich possesses that signature crunch in every single bite.
