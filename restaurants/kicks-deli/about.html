The Story Behind Kicks Deli

Born at the crossroads of classic streetwear aesthetic and obsessive sandwich craftsmanship in Uptown Charlotte.

The Kicks Philosophy

Fresh Bread, Fresh Drops, Fresh Flavor

Located at 101 North Tryon Street inside Monarch Market, Kicks Deli was founded on a simple realization: Charlotte was missing the legendary Dutch Crunch sandwiches that define great West Coast deli culture.

Drawing inspiration from sneaker drops and hip-hop heritage, we named our creations after the classics — like "The One," a retro Italian sub stacked with precision cold cuts and dressed in house balsamic glaze.

Whether you are grabbing a quick desk lunch during a busy banking workday or fueling up on the weekend at Monarch Market, Kicks Deli delivers pure sandwich perfection.

Deli Standards

The 4 Rules of the Kicks Kitchen

How we ensure every sandwich hits like a grail drop.

1. Daily Baked Bread

Our signature Dutch Crunch rolls and hoagies are baked fresh daily for peak crackle and texture.

2. Whole Muscle Meats

Top-tier whole-muscle meats sliced fresh to order never pre-packaged or held under lights.

3. Scratch Dressings

From our aged balsamic reductions to garlic herb aioli, all spreads are mixed in-house.

4. Speed with Care

Lightning-fast turnaround for Uptown lunch rushes without ever cutting corners on build quality.
