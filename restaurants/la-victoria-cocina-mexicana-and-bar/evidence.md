## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Ballantyne Location:** 16139 Lancaster Hwy, Charlotte NC 28277 (Ballantyne / South Charlotte).
2. **Hacienda Mexican Specialties:** Signature Victoria Enchiladas Suizas, Sizzling Fajitas Supreme, Carne Asada Tampiqueña with cheese enchilada & poblano rajas, Tableside Guacamole, Craft Tequila Margaritas, Hand-Pressed Corn Tortillas, & Ballantyne Patio Dining.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 540-3058; Mon–Thu 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM | Fri–Sat 11:00 AM – 10:30 PM | Sun 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Regal Classical Display Typography:** Regal display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and Ballantyne luxury mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Royal Midnight Emerald & Sunlit Gold Palette:** Upscale hacienda palette anchored in deep royal midnight emerald (`#064E3B`), warm sunlit gold (`#D97706`), imperial amber (`#F59E0B`), and warm ivory cream (`#FAFAF9`).
3. **"The Victoria Hacienda & Tequila Bar Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`hacienda-tequila-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing signature enchiladas suizas alongside handcrafted tequila margaritas & Ballantyne patio dining.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `La Unica Bar & Grill` — Music Factory Neon Cantina with Space Grotesk fonts and Deep Obsidian Void/Fiery Coral.
2. `La Poblanita Mexican Restaurant` — Puebla Hacienda Terracotta with Fraunces fonts and Deep Puebla Terracotta/Agave Amber.
3. `La Lima Sangucheria` — Peruvian Andean Sun with Syne fonts and Deep Andean Red/Lime Green.

### Divergent Choices for La Victoria Cocina Mexicana & Bar

- **Hero Composition:** Ballantyne Gold Hacienda & Spanish Colonial Courtyard Vault split-hero layout: left side features an Imperial Laurel stamp badge (`"MEXICAN COCINA & TEQUILA LOUNGE • BALLANTYNE CHARLOTTE NC"`), regal classical serif (*Cinzel*), royal midnight emerald & sunlit gold palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of craft margaritas & hacienda dining.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **La Victoria Vault Cards** (`victoria-vault-card`) and Ballantyne dining highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`victoria-header`, `victoria-brand`, `regal-hero-stage`, `laurel-stamp-badge`, `victoria-vault-card`, `victoria-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
