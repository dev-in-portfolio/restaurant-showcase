Charlotte's Original Soccer Pub

Nestled in historic Brevard Court—a 1920s cobblestone pedestrian alleyway in Uptown Charlotte across from Romare Bearden Park—Courtyard Hooligans has served as Charlotte's official soccer sanctuary for over a decade.
