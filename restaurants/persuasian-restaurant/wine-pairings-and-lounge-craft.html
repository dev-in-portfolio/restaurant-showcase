🍷 Boutique Vineyards & Asian Flavor Pairings

Curated Wine, Elevated Flavors.

Pairing wine with Asian cuisine is an art form. Traditional bold reds can overwhelm subtle aromatics, while overly oaky whites clash with spice. At Persuasian, we curate high-acid, aromatic whites, elegant low-tannin reds, and sparkling varietals that elevate every wok-seared dish.

Cellar Philosophy

The 3 Pillars of Persuasian Wine Curation

1. High-Acid Aromatic Whites

Highlighting crisp New Zealand Sauvignon Blancs, dry Rieslings, and French Chenin Blancs that cut through rich sesame glazes and chili oils.

2. Earthy, Low-Tannin Reds

Featuring Willamette Valley Pinot Noirs and Spanish Garnachas that marry harmoniously with savory soy broths and pan-seared sea bass.

3. Botanical Craft Cocktails

Shaking artisanal cocktails infused with real lychee nectar, freshly grated ginger, Japanese yuzu, and fragrant Thai basil.
