Chef's Wok & Dim Sum Scratch Crafted

Szechuan Pork Dumplings (6) #1 Starter

Handmade delicate dough pockets filled with ginger-seasoned Berkshire pork and scallions, gently boiled and bathed in our signature spicy roasted Szechuan chili oil sauce.

Hong Kong Chilean Sea Bass Chef's Signature

Pan-seared Chilean sea bass fillet served over steamed baby bok choy and jasmine rice, drizzled with ginger-scallion reduction and aged sweet soy broth.

Crispy Sesame Chicken Fusion Favorite

Tender pieces of crispy flash-fried chicken breast tossed in a luscious honey-ginger sesame glaze with steamed broccoli florets and toasted white sesame seeds.

Traditional Street Pad Thai Noodle Classic

Wok-tossed thin rice noodles with jumbo shrimp, chicken, eggs, fresh bean sprouts, and scallions in tangy tamarind sauce, topped with crushed peanuts and lime.

Small Plates & Cellar Curated Pairings

Crispy Crab Rangoon (4) Hand-Pinched

Hand-folded wontons stuffed with real lump crab meat, cream cheese, and scallions, fried crispy and served with house sweet chili apricot sauce.

Boutique Sauvignon Blanc (NZ) White Pairing

Marlborough, New Zealand. Crisp notes of lemongrass, passionfruit, and bright acidity that perfectly cleanses the palate alongside spicy Szechuan dishes.

Willamette Valley Pinot Noir (OR) Red Pairing

Oregon. Silky red cherry, subtle clove spice, and earthy undertones harmonizing beautifully with pan-seared sea bass and sesame chicken.
