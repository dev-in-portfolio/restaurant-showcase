🏛 The Persuasian Dilworth Story

A Vision of Asian Elegance.

Persuasian was born from decades of hospitality passion by veteran Charlotte restaurateurs Jenny and Phong. Having previously operated beloved Queen City culinary staples including Zen Fusion, they established Persuasian at 2214 Park Road to provide Dilworth neighbors with a cozy, sophisticated dining room celebrating Asian street craft and refined wine culture.

The Values of Persuasian Restaurant

Neighborhood Warmth

Treating every guest like an old friend, whether joining us for a quick weeknight dinner, an anniversary date night, or a glass of wine at the bar.

Uncompromising Freshness

Sourcing fresh seafood, tender meats, crisp local produce, and whole spices without relying on frozen shortcut ingredients.

Harmonious Balance

Honoring the core principles of Asian cuisine—sweet, sour, salty, spicy, and umami—in seamless harmony with our wine selections.
