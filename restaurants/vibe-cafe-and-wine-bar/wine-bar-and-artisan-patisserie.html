Curated Pours, Delicate Sweets.

As the afternoon sun lowers over East Boulevard, Vibe Café gracefully transforms into an intimate European wine lounge. Sip estate-bottled Old World wines, crisp prosecco spritzes, and artisan cocktails paired with multi-layered Medovik honey cake and delicate French patisserie.

The Patisserie Collection

Slavic Masterpiece

Medovik Honey Cake

Eight ultra-thin honey biscuits layered with whipped cultured cream that soften over twenty-four hours into a velvety, harmonious cake crowned with tart fresh raspberries.

French Heritage

Mille-Feuille Napoleon

Delicate, caramelized sheets of puff pastry stacked with luscious Madagascar vanilla bean pastry cream, offering a satisfying crisp crunch in every bite.

Classic Elegance

Espresso Tiramisu

Airy ladyfingers saturated in freshly pulled Italian espresso, layered with whipped mascarpone cream, and finished with a rich cocoa dust.

Wines by the Glass & Bottle

Our wine list spotlights independent European vineyards from Italy, France, Spain, and Greece. Whether you prefer a crisp Pinot Grigio with our Burrata Caprese or a bold Tuscan red with our Beef Stroganoff, our team will help you find the ideal pour.
