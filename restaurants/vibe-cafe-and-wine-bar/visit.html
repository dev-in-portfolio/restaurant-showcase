Dilworth Celebrations

European Catering & Event Platters

Elevate your corporate gatherings, birthday celebrations, or intimate dinner parties with our authentic European catering options:

  • Patisserie Dessert Trays: Assorted authentic Medovik honey cake slices, French macarons, Napoleon puff pastry squares, and miniature pavlovas.
  • Brunch & Savory Platters: Golden syrniki cottage cheese pancakes with berry preserves, smoked salmon bagel displays, and artisan crêpe platters.
  • Hot Comfort Platters: Pelmeni meat dumplings tossed in herb butter, stuffed cabbage rolls, and crispy cheburek turnovers. Call (980) 207-1480 or email info.vibecafe@gmail.com for catering inquiries.
Guest Comfort

Sidewalk Patio & Convenient Parking

Visiting Vibe Café is easy and relaxing:

  • Sidewalk Patio: Shaded outdoor cafe seating along tree-lined East Boulevard, welcoming well-behaved leashed pets.
  • On-Site Parking: Free dedicated customer parking lot directly serving 1315 East Boulevard commercial center.
  • Neighborhood Stroll: Conveniently located within walking distance of Freedom Park and Dilworth boutiques.
