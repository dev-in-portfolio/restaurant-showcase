Culinary Heritage • Dilworth Charlotte

Scratch Craft, Old World Soul.

At Vibe Café & Wine Bar, our kitchen bridges the warmth of Eastern European home cooking with the breezy elegance of Mediterranean bistros. From pillowy cottage cheese syrniki to handcrafted dumplings and slow-simmered beet borscht, every dish is made from scratch with genuine love.

Eastern European Brunch

Golden Syrniki

Made from traditional farmer's curd cheese (tvorog), our cottage cheese pancakes are delicately pan-seared to a golden exterior while remaining custardy and soft inside, finished with sweet berry preserves and chilled sour cream.

Hand-Folded Heritage

Siberian Pelmeni

Tender pasta dough rolled paper-thin and hand-pinched around seasoned minced beef and pork, boiled gently and tossed in rich melted butter, fresh dill, and accompanied by tangy sour cream.

Slow-Simmered Comfort

Traditional Borscht

Our ruby-red beet soup is built on layers of caramelized onions, shredded cabbage, carrots, potatoes, and garlic simmered in a savory broth, served piping hot with crusty hearth bread.

Mediterranean Warmth

Roman Flatbreads & Crisp Cheburek

Alongside our Slavic comfort soups and dumplings, our ovens bake crisp Roman flatbreads topped with prosciutto di Parma, wild arugula, and aged balsamic glaze, as well as golden, blistered cheburek—crisp turnover pastries bursting with seasoned meat juices.
