Our Story

Core Values

The Spirit of Our Table

The principles guiding every espresso pulled and every plate served.

Principle 01

Heartfelt Hospitality

We greet every guest like family arriving at our dinner table. Whether you are popping in for a quick morning latte or staying for an intimate multi-course dinner, you are warmly welcomed.

Principle 02

Scratch Craftsmanship

No shortcuts, no artificial flavorings. Our borscht is simmered slowly from whole beets, our dumplings are pinched by hand, and our honey cakes are layered from scratch with pure honey.

Principle 03

Neighborhood Gathering

Dilworth is a close-knit, historic community. We are honored to serve as a beloved neighborhood anchor where neighbors bump into friends and make new connections.

East Boulevard Charm

Connected to Charlotte’s Historic Dilworth

Situated in the heart of Dilworth along picturesque East Boulevard, Vibe Café & Wine Bar is just moments from Freedom Park, boutique shops, and beautiful residential avenues. Relax on our outdoor patio or retreat inside to our calming, art-filled dining room.
