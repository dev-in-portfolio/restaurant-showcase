🥪 Hand-Stretched Flour Tortillas

The Art of Baleadas Catrachas

The baleada is the undisputed icon of Honduran street gastronomy. At Lempira Restaurant, we knead & hand-stretch fresh wheat flour dough for every order, searing them on hot griddles before filling with savory red beans, mantequilla cream, queso duro, & tender carne asada.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Authentic Baleada Craft

1. Hand-Stretched Wheat Dough

Wheat flour, milk, & oil kneaded soft, rested, & hand-stretched thin for pillowy tender tortillas.

2. Honduran Frijoles Refritos

Small red Honduran beans cooked with garlic, onions, & lard before mashing into velvet-smooth refried beans.

3. Mantequilla & Queso Duro

Imported Honduran cultured mantequilla sour cream & sharp dry-cured queso duro blanco cheese.
