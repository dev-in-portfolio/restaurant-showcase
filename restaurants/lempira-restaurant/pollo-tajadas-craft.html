🌮 Green Banana Tajadas & Fried Chicken

The Art of Pollo con Tajadas

Famous along the North Coast of Honduras, Pollo chuco (Pollo con Tajadas) combines crispy fried chicken with paper-thin fried green banana chips. At Lempira Restaurant, we slice raw guineos verdes daily & fry them to golden perfection before layering on cabbage slaw & chismol.

Kitchen Craft

The 3 Pillars of Pollo con Tajadas Artistry

1. Guineos Verdes Tajadas

Unripened green bananas sliced paper-thin & fried extra crispy for a savory, crunchy starch base.

2. Spiced Catracho Fried Chicken

Fresh chicken marinated in mustard, garlic, oregano, & cumin, double-dredged for shatter-crisp crunch.

3. Chismol & Pink Sauce

Diced tomatoes, peppers, onions, & cilantro tossed with lime juice, topped with creamy garlic pink sauce.
