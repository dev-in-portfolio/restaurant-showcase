Honduran Baleada & Tajadas Menu

Baleadas Catrachas, Pollo con Tajadas, Carne Asada Tipica, & horchata de jícaro at 5906 South Blvd.

Baleadas & Entrees Catracho Classics

Baleada Especial con Carne Asada #1 House Special

Large hand-stretched flour tortilla folded with refried Honduran red beans, mantequilla cream, grated queso duro, scrambled eggs, & grilled marinated skirt steak.

Pollo con Tajadas (Chicken & Banana Chips) Customer Fav

Crispy spiced Honduran fried chicken quarter served over crispy green banana tajada chips, pickled red onions, cabbage salad, & signature pink sauce.

Carne Asada Tipica Platter Traditional Platter

Char-broiled marinated steak served with sweet fried ripe plantains, Honduran refried beans, mantequilla, avocado, queso fresco, & warm corn tortillas.

Tajadas con Chicharrón Crispy Pork

Crispy fried pork belly chicharrón served over green banana chips, shredded cabbage, tomato salsa, & pickled habanero onions.

Soups & Drinks Fresh Drinks

Sopa de Caracol (Honduran Conch Soup) Coastal Soup

Rich coconut milk broth simmered with tender Caribbean conch, green plantains, yucca, sweet corn, cilantro, & fresh lime wedges.

Sopa de Res Rib Eye Beef Soup Hearty Soup

Slow-cooked beef short rib broth with corn on the cob, cabbage, chayote squash, yucca, & cilantro, served with white rice.

Horchata de Jícaro House Brew Honduran Drink

Traditional Honduran beverage brewed from ground jícaro seeds, rice, cinnamon, nutmeg, & vanilla bean served ice-cold.

Fresh Maracuyá & Tamarindo Juices Tropical Juice

Hand-pressed tropical passion fruit or sweet-tart tamarind juice served over crushed ice.
