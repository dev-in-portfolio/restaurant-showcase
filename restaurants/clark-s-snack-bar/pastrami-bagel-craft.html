Artisanal Deli Technique

The Science of Montreal Bagels & Pastrami

Unlike fluffy New York bagels, Montreal-style bagels are hand-rolled, poached in honey water for subtle sweetness, heavily coated in toasted sesame seeds, and baked crisp. Paired with hot steamed pastrami, it creates the ultimate deli bite.

Craft Steps

The 3 Pillars of Clark’s Pastrami

1. Honey-Water Poaching

Bagel dough is boiled in honey-sweetened water, forming a thin shiny crust before baking.

2. Spiced Black Pepper Rub

Beef brisket is brine-cured, rubbed with crushed coriander and coarse black pepper, and smoked.

3. Steam Cabinet Finishing

Smoked pastrami is sliced thick and held in a steam cabinet to render fat for juicy tenderness.
