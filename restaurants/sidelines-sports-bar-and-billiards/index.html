Charlotte’s Premier Sports Haven.

Welcome to Sidelines Sports Bar & Billiards, Charlotte’s iconic inclusive sports lounge located at 4544 South Boulevard. Anchoring the Lower South End corridor for over eighteen years, Sidelines features four tournament-grade slate pool tables, competitive steel-tip dart lanes, wall-to-wall HDTV sports broadcasts, ice-cold drafts, and a relaxed, welcoming community atmosphere where every sports fan belongs.
