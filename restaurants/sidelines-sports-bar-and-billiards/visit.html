Getting Here

On-Site Parking & Transit Access

Arriving at 4544 South Boulevard is hassle-free:

  • Complimentary Surface Parking: Extensive free, dedicated on-site parking is available directly in the private shopping center lot in front of our doors.
  • Lynx Blue Line (Scaleybark Station): The light rail station is located roughly a half-mile away, providing quick connections to South End and Uptown.
  • Rideshare Pickup: A dedicated curbside turnaround allows for safe, seamless Uber and Lyft drop-offs and late-night pickups.
Groups & Socializing

Outdoor Patio & Team Watch Parties

Planning an event, alumni watch party, or recreational league social?

  • Covered Outdoor Patio: Enjoy fresh Carolina air and outdoor drinks on our comfortable, covered patio space.
  • Team & League Gatherings: For sports leagues or watch party groups of 8 or more, call us at (704) 525-2608 in advance so our crew can prepare table groupings.
  • Pitchers & Snack Platters: Enjoy group-friendly draft beer pitcher specials, soft pretzel platters, and flatbread pizzas during games.
