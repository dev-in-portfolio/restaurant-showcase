Recreational Standards

The Sidelines Game Room Disciplines

Carefully maintained equipment ensuring level rolls and true dart trajectories.

Standard 01

Simonis Tournament Cloth

All four regulation slate tables are outfitted with high-speed Simonis cloth that prevents ball roll resistance, ensuring every delicate English spin and bank shot behaves true.

Standard 02

Steel-Tip Cork Lanes

Our dedicated dart throwing lanes feature official regulation distance oches, bristle cork dartboards, and chalkboard scoring for casual games and local league matches.

Standard 03

Free Pool Nights

We celebrate community play with dedicated free pool nights every week, inviting players of all skill levels to rack up balls and enjoy casual competition without table fees.

League Play

Weekly 8-Ball & 9-Ball Tournaments

Looking to test your skills against South Charlotte's friendliest cueists? Sidelines hosts regular 8-ball and 9-ball amateur tournaments and league nights. Win prizes, earn local bragging rights, or simply enjoy high-caliber billiards alongside fellow sports fans.
