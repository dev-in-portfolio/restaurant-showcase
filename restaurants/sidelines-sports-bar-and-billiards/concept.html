Established 2007 • Eighteen Years of Sports Fandom

A Home For Every Sports Fan.

In 2007, Sidelines Sports Bar & Billiards opened its doors at 4544 South Boulevard with a pioneering vision: build an inclusive, relaxed sports bar where the LGBTQ+ community, loyal allies, and neighborhood residents could enjoy live broadcasts and championship billiards together in a warm, welcoming environment.

Core Values

The Foundations of Sidelines

What sets our Lower South End sports lounge apart.

Value 01

Inclusive Community Hub

We take immense pride in offering an ultra-friendly, mature, and safe space where sports enthusiasts of all backgrounds connect over a shared passion for the game.

Value 02

Grassroots Team Sponsor

For over eighteen years, Sidelines has sponsored local recreational softball, kickball, bowling, and billiards teams throughout the greater Charlotte metropolitan area.

Value 03

Calm, Mature Atmosphere

Unlike loud, chaotic mega-clubs, Sidelines prioritizes conversational comfort, attentive bartending, and a relaxed environment where you can actually talk and play.

Neighborhood Anchor

Lower South End's Living Room

From afternoon pints on our outdoor patio to late-night 8-ball racks under ambient game lights, Sidelines offers an enduring sense of belonging in Charlotte's evolving Lower South End. Stop by any day of the week and experience the hospitality that has sustained us for nearly two decades.
