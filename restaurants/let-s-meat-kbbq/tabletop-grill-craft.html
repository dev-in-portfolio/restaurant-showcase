The 3 Pillars of KBBQ Tabletop Mastery

1. Downdraft Smokeless Tech

High-suction perimeter vents capture smoke instantly before it reaches clothes or dining room air.

2. Asian Pear Marinades

Fresh grated Asian pears naturally tenderize beef fibers while caramelizing into sweet savory glazes on the grill.

3. Interactive Ssang Wrap Ritual

Wrap grilled meats in crisp green lettuce with ssamjang chili paste, pickled radish, & raw garlic cloves.
