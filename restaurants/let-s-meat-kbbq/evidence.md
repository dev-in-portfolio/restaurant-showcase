Let’s Meat KBBQ relies on basic third-party directory listings and social media pages. Located at 1400 S Church St Suite B in South End, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its all-you-can-eat tabletop grilling ritual, Korean soju bar program, corporate group dining directives, and South End rules.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South End Location:** 1400 S Church St, Suite B, Charlotte NC 28203 (South End Charlotte off Church St & Gold District).
2. **Korean BBQ Specialties:** All-You-Can-Eat Tabletop KBBQ (Thinly-Sliced Chadolbaegi Beef Belly, Prime Galbi Short Ribs, Spicy Samgyeopsal Pork Belly, Garlic Beef Bulgogi, Honey Soy Chicken, Kimchi Stew, Corn Cheese, Banchan, & Korean Soju Cocktails).
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (980) 299-4389; Mon–Thu 11:30 AM – 11:00 PM | Fri–Sat 11:30 AM – 12:00 AM Midnight | Sun 11:30 AM – 11:00 PM. Walk-in only policy (no reservations).

### Core Design Moves

1. **Futuristic Display Typography:** Space grotesque display sans (*Space Grotesk*) paired with clean tech body sans (*Inter*) and South End industrial mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Charcoal Obsidian & Fiery Ember Palette:** High-energy KBBQ lounge palette anchored in deep charcoal obsidian (`#111827`), fiery ember red (`#EF4444`), electric flame orange (`#F97316`), and crisp steel silver (`#F9FAFB`).
3. **"The Tabletop KBBQ Grill & Soju Lounge Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`tabletop-grill-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing tabletop smokeless grill meats alongside handcrafted Korean soju cocktails & banchan sides.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Lempira Restaurant` — Honduran Caribbean Coast with Outfit fonts and Deep Sapphire Blue/Banana Gold.
2. `La Victoria Cocina Mexicana & Bar` — Ballantyne Gold Hacienda with Cinzel fonts and Deep Royal Emerald/Sunlit Gold.
3. `La Unica Bar & Grill` — Music Factory Neon Cantina with Space Grotesk fonts and Deep Obsidian Void/Fiery Coral.

### Divergent Choices for Let’s Meat KBBQ

- **Hero Composition:** Korean Tabletop Grill & Seoul Cyber-Industrial Vault split-hero layout: left side features an Ember Stamp badge (`"KOREAN TABLETOP BBQ & SOJU LOUNGE • SOUTH END CHARLOTTE NC"`), futuristic bold display sans (*Space Grotesk*), charcoal obsidian & fiery ember palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of sizzling Kalbi BBQ short ribs.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **KBBQ Vault Cards** (`kbbq-vault-card`) and South End dining highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`kbbq-header`, `kbbq-brand`, `grill-hero-stage`, `ember-stamp-badge`, `kbbq-vault-card`, `kbbq-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
