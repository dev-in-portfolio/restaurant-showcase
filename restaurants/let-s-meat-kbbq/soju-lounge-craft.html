🍶 Korean Soju & Anju Lounge

The Art of Korean Soju Bar

In Korea, drinking soju with friends is incomplete without anju—flavorful savory dishes designed to pair with spirits. At Let's Meat KBBQ, enjoy ice-cold flavored Jinro sojus, Cass beers, & signature cocktail buckets alongside sizzling corn cheese & kimchi jjigae.

The 3 Pillars of Soju & Anju Craft

1. Chilled Jinro Fruit Soju

Green Grape, Fresh Strawberry, Peach, & Grapefruit sojus served at 32°F for smooth tabletop toasts.

2. Korean Somaek Beer Bombs

The classic pairing of ice-cold Cass lager beer with a shot of classic Jinro Chamisul soju.

3. Savory Anju Pairings

Sizzling corn cheese, spicy tteokbokki rice cakes, & extra-crispy kimchi scallion pancakes.
