Sustainable Carolina Mariculture

From Core Sound Waters To Your Table.

Unlike standard seafood houses that rely on anonymous distributors, Sea Level NC is founded on a direct aquaculture partnership in Sea Level, North Carolina. Our proprietary Sea Level Salts are nurtured in floating mesh cages where the brisk oceanic currents of Core Sound produce bivalves of unparalleled purity and sweetness.

The Anatomy of Sea Level Salts

Carefully managed from microscopic oyster seed to cold-water harvest.

Environment

Core Sound Terroir (Merroir)

Farmed between the North Carolina mainland and the Cape Lookout National Seashore. Pristine Atlantic tides flush the sound twice daily with clean, nutrient-dense ocean water.

Aquaculture

Floating Mesh Cages

Oysters are grown in surface floating cages tumbled naturally by waves. This prevents muddy bottom sediment contact, producing clean, smooth shells with deep cups and plump meats.

Raw Bar Standard

Artisan Hand-Shucking

Our oyster shuckers pop the rear hinge cleanly without tearing the delicate mantle, sever the adductor muscle, and preserve 100% of the natural salinity liquor in every shell.

Weekday Happy Hour Tradition

$1.50 House Oysters Every Monday Through Friday

Between 2:00 PM and 6:00 PM on weekdays, our Uptown raw bar transforms into Charlotte's favorite afternoon retreat. Slurp freshly shucked house oysters for just $1.50 each alongside chilled glasses of Muscadet, sparkling cava, or our signature dirty oyster martini.
