Maritime Roots • Urban Refuge

From Sea Level, NC To Uptown Charlotte.

Sea Level, North Carolina is a tiny, storied fishing hamlet in Carteret County along the pristine shoreline of Core Sound. For generations, families there have looked to the waters for their livelihood. We founded Sea Level NC at 129 East 5th Street to bring that authentic coastal life and marine honesty directly into the heart of Charlotte’s urban skyline.

The Sea Level Philosophy

Three Principles That Anchor Our Hospitality

Direct coastal partnership, culinary transparency, and welcoming community warmth.

Principle 01

Direct Fisherman Relationships

By partnering directly with oyster farmers in Sea Level, NC and commercial trap watermen in Maine, we cut out long supply chains, guaranteeing our seafood reaches Uptown within hours of harvest.

Principle 02

An Urban Coastal Sanctuary

Surrounded by glass towers and corporate headquarters, our dining room and raw bar offer a relaxed nautical escape where business lunches, happy hours, and date nights feel genuinely unhurried.

Principle 03

Sustainable Stewardship

We recognize that our craft depends on the health of our oceans. We support marine conservation, recycle every single oyster shell for reef restoration, and champion sustainable fisheries.

Reef Restoration

Recycling Every Single Shell Back To The Sound

Every oyster shell consumed at Sea Level NC is collected and returned to coastal restoration programs. These recycled shells form vital artificial reefs along the North Carolina coast, providing essential habitat for future generations of spat to settle, filter ocean waters, and protect shorelines from erosion.
