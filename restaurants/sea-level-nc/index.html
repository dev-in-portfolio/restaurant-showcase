Core Sound Farm-To-Fork • Uptown Charlotte

Pristine Oysters, Carolina Coast.

Welcome to Sea Level NC, located at 129 East 5th Street in Uptown Charlotte. We bring the briny purity of North Carolina’s Outer Banks directly to the Queen City. From our proprietary Sea Level Salts oysters harvested from Core Sound waters to warm buttered Maine lobster rolls and Carolina shrimp and grits, we serve responsibly sourced coastal seafood daily.

Authentic Maine Lobster Rolls On Brioche

We believe in pure lobster meat without fillers or celery distractions. Sourced from sustainable North Atlantic traps, whole claws and sweet knuckles are gently warmed in melted sweet cream butter or folded lightly with fresh lemon juice, tarragon, and sea-salt mayo inside a griddled split-top bun.
