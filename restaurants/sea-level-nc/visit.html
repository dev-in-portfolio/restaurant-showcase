Arrival Directives

Truist Tower Garage Parking Validation

Visiting Uptown Charlotte is stress-free when you dine at Sea Level NC:

  • Adjacent Parking Garage: Park in the covered Truist Tower parking garage located directly adjacent to the restaurant on 5th Street.
  • Generous Validation Hours: We validate up to 2 hours of parking for arrivals before 6:00 PM, and up to 3 hours of parking for arrivals after 6:00 PM.
  • Validation Process: Simply present your physical parking ticket or digital garage scan to our host stand upon arrival or departure.
