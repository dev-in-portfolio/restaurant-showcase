Earthen Guisados & Hand Masa

Slow-braised birria, mole poblano, barbacoa de chivo, handmade comal tortillas, & house drinks at 5130 N Tryon St.

Earthenware Guisados Slow Simmered

Birria de Res con Consomé #1 House Specialty

Tender beef brisket slow-cooked 8 hours in guajillo & ancho chili broth, served in earthenware bowl with hot rich consomé dipping broth.

Mole Poblano de Guajolote Traditional Craft

Roasted turkey tenderloin smothered in rich artisanal mole poblano sauce made from 20 ingredients including dark chocolate, toasted sesame, & chilies.

Barbacoa de Chivo en Agave Weekend Specialty

Young goat meat slow-steamed inside maguey agave leaves with avocado leaves & spices until fall-apart tender, served with garbanzo consomé.

Slow-Crisped Carnitas Michoacán Michoacán Style

Pork shoulder copper-kettle braised in orange peel, lard, & Mexican cinnamon, crisped golden & served with pickled jalapenos.

Cazuela de Chicharrón en Salsa Verde Clay Pot Stew

Crisp pork cracklings simmered in bubbling hot clay cazuela with roasted tomatillo salsa verde, serrano peppers, & pinto beans.

Comal Tortillas & Drinks Scratch Prepared

Handmade Comal Corn Tortillas Pressed Fresh

Order of 4 thick yellow corn masa tortillas hand-pressed & puffed fresh on iron comales per order.

Fresh Table-Side Guacamole Cazuela Fresh Avocados

Ripe avocados mashed with lime, sea salt, cilantro, diced onions, & tomatoes, served in clay bowl with warm tortilla chips.

Creamy Horchata de Arroz Milk House Drink

Traditional rice & almond milk beverage steeped with cinnamon sticks, vanilla, & cane sugar over ice.

Chilled Tart Agua de Tamarindo Refreshing

Pressed natural tamarind pulp beverage balanced with raw cane sugar & crushed ice.

Warm Cinnamon Churros con Cajeta Sweet Dessert

Crisp fried churro pastry dusted with cinnamon sugar, served with warm goat-milk caramel cajeta dipping sauce.
