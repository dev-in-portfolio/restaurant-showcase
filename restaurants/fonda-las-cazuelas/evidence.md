## Original Audit Weakness

Fonda Las Cazuelas relies on basic older web layout. Located at 5130 N Tryon St in Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its authentic earthenware cazuela slow-simmering heritage, hand-pressed corn masa process, traditional family recipes since 2008, and corporate catering order directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **North Tryon Location:** 5130 N Tryon St, Charlotte NC 28213 (Sugar Creek / North Tryon Corridor).
2. **Traditional Clay-Pot Guisados:** Birria de Res con Consomé, Mole Poblano de Guajolote, Barbacoa de Chivo, Carnitas Michoacán, & Cazuela de Chicharrón en Salsa Verde.
3. **Handmade Comal Tortillas & Sweets:** Fresh masa corn tortillas pressed per order, horchata de arroz milk, & warm cinnamon sugar churros with cajeta caramel.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Rich Organic Display Serif Typography:** Warm organic serif (*Fraunces*) paired with clean body sans (*Inter*) and kitchen mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Terracotta Rust & Chilmole Green Palette:** High-energy Mexican earthenware palette anchored in terracotta rust (`#C2410C`), chilmole green (`#15803D`), corn gold (`#D97706`), warm silk cream (`#FFFBEB`), and earthen charcoal (`#1C1917`).
3. **"The Earthenware Cazuela & Slow-Simmered Guisado Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`cazuela-simmer-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing clay-pot stews alongside handmade corn tacos, mole poblano, & traditional horchata.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Flip-a-Lo’s` — High-Velocity Buffalo Wing with Space Grotesk fonts and Midnight Charcoal/Cayenne Red.

### Divergent Choices for Fonda Las Cazuelas

- **Hero Composition:** Earthen Clay-Pot & Traditional Mexican Guisado Vault split-hero layout: left side features a rich terracotta stamp badge (`"TRADITIONAL MEXICAN CLAY-POT GASTRONOMY • EST. 2008 • 5130 N TRYON ST"`), warm organic serif (*Fraunces*), terracotta rust & chilmole green palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of bubbling clay cazuela stews, hand-pressed corn tortillas, & horchata.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Fonda Las Cazuelas Vault Cards** (`fonda-vault-card`) and North Tryon culinary highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`fonda-header`, `fonda-brand`, `earthen-hero-stage`, `terracotta-stamp-badge`, `fonda-vault-card`, `fonda-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
