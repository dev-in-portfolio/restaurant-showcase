🏘 Family Owned & Operated Since 2008

North Tryon Culinary Legacy

Fonda Las Cazuelas opened its doors at 5130 N Tryon St in 2008 with a single mission: to bring the warmth & authentic flavors of traditional Mexican family kitchens to Charlotte. Named after the traditional clay cooking pots, we serve dishes rooted in heritage.
