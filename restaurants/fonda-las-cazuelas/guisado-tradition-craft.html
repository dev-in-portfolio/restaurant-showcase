Heritage of Mexican Guisados

In Mexican gastronomy, a guisado represents love, patience, & family history. Every morning at 5:00 AM, our kitchen toasts dried guajillo & ancho chilies on hot comales, blending them into braising stocks for beef birria, turkey mole poblano, & pork carnitas.

Guisado Heritage

The 3 Pillars of Fonda Guisados

1. Comal-Toasted Dried Chilies

Ancho, guajillo, pasilla, & mulato chilies are dry-roasted on iron comales to release smokey natural oils before steeping.

2. Artisanal Mole Poblano Blend

Over 20 distinct ingredients—chocolate, raisins, pumpkin seeds, cinnamon, garlic, & cloves—ground smooth for mole poblano.

3. Maguey Agave Steam Braising

Barbacoa de chivo is wrapped in roasted maguey agave fronds & steamed overnight for authentic Mexican feast flavor.
