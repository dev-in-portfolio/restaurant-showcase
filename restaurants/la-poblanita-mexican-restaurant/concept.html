Puebla Roots & Pride

La Poblanita Mexican Restaurant was founded in 2009 to bring authentic Puebla home cooking to Charlotte, NC. Located at 1925 Westinghouse Blvd, our family kitchen combines traditional Mexican dining with a celebrated candy store & artisanal piñata market.
