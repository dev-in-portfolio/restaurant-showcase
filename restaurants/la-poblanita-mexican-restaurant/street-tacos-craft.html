The Art of Street Tacos

Great tacos start with great tortillas. At La Poblanita Mexican Restaurant, we press stone-ground corn masa by hand throughout the day, searing them on a blazing hot comal before layering them with Al Pastor pork, Birria de Res, or Carne Asada.

Kitchen Craft

The 3 Pillars of Street Taco Artistry

1. Stone-Ground Corn Masa

Nixtamalized white corn flour mixed fresh daily, hand-balling masa & pressing on heavy iron presses.

2. Vertical Trompo Spit Roasting

Pork shoulder stacked on vertical trompo with achiote, pineapple, & spices, sliced directly into warm tortillas.

3. Molcajete Salsa Bar

Fresh roasted tomatillo salsa verde, smoky arbol salsa roja, pickled habanero onions, & key lime wedges.
