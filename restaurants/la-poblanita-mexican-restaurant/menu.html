Puebla Specialties Scratch Recipes

Signature Mole Poblano Chicken #1 House Special

Slow-simmered bone-in chicken leg quarters smothered in authentic 20-ingredient dark chocolate & ancho chili mole, served with Mexican rice, refried beans, & warm handmade tortillas.

Birria de Res Tacos with Consommé (3) Customer Fav

Crispy guajillo-dipped corn tortillas stuffed with slow-braised beef brisket & melted Oaxaca cheese, served with rich beef dipping consommé, cilantro, & lime.

Traditional Chiles Rellenos (2) Classic Dish

Roasted Poblano peppers stuffed with queso fresco, light egg batter fried, & served under savory tomato caldillo sauce.

Handmade Sopes de Carnitas (3) Thick Masa

Thick fried corn masa cakes topped with refried beans, slow-roasted pork carnitas, shredded lettuce, crema, queso fresco, & salsa verde.

Street Tacos & Drinks Handmade Tortillas

Tacos Al Pastor with Pineapple (3) Taco Special

Achiote-marinated pork roasted on vertical spit, seared on flat top with fresh pineapple chunks, diced onions, & cilantro on handmade corn tortillas.

Carne Asada Street Tacos (3) Char-Grilled

Char-grilled marinated skirt steak on double handmade corn tortillas with fresh guacamole, onions, cilantro, & house salsa roja.

Fresh Table-Side Guacamole & Chips Fresh Starter

Ripe Haas avocados mashed with sea salt, lime juice, jalapeños, tomatoes, & cilantro, served with warm tortilla chips.

Scratch Rice Horchata Agua Fresca Ice-Cold Drink

House-ground long-grain rice milk spiced with Mexican cinnamon sticks, vanilla, & sweetened whole milk served ice-cold.
