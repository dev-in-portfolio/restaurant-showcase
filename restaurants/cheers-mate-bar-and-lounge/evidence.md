# Evidence & Creative Brief: Cheers Mate Bar & Lounge

## Creative Brief
- **Brand Identity**: Cheers Mate Bar & Lounge (also operating in conjunction with the Varsity Lounge brand) is a high-energy urban sports bar, late-night eatery, and weekend brunch destination located at 521 N College St in Uptown Charlotte. Celebrated for gourmet smash sliders (2AM beef, crispy fried chicken, pulled pork, vegan Impossible), jumbo seasoned wings, loaded fries, vibrant craft cocktail mixology, and high-definition sports watch parties.
- **Target Audience**: Charlotte sports fans and supporters attending Panthers, Hornets, and Charlotte FC matches; Uptown weekend brunch lovers seeking chicken & waffles and mimosa towers; late-night hospitality professionals and social crowds looking for elevated comfort food and cocktails.
- **Design Philosophy**: High-energy electric lounge aesthetic combining deep midnight obsidian (`#0b0f19` / `#111827`), glowing neon amber/gold (`#f59e0b`), and electric sports cobalt (`#2563eb`). Sharp geometric badges, dark-mode elevated surfaces, interactive menu tab filtering, and party catering calculators.
- **Key Differentiators**: Gourmet slider flights with vegan Impossible options; weekend brunch party experience (Sat & Sun 11am-4pm); late-night kitchen service; private lounge and VIP section reservations.
