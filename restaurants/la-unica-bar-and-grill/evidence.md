## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Music Factory Location:** 900 North Carolina Music Factory Blvd, Charlotte NC 28206 (AvidXChange Music Factory in Uptown Charlotte).
2. **Mexican Cantina & Nightlife Specialties:** Signature Sizzling Skillet Fajitas (Steak, Chicken, Shrimp, & Trio), Tequila Margaritas & Craft Cocktails, Street Tacos Al Pastor, Carne Asada, Chimichangas, Queso Fundido with Chorizo, & Music Factory Nightlife Lounge directives.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (980) 875-9077; Mon–Wed 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM | Thu–Fri 11:00 AM – 2:00 AM | Sat 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM | Sun 4:00 PM – 1:00 AM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Modern Grotesque Display Typography:** Tech-grotesque display sans (*Space Grotesk*) paired with clean body sans (*Inter*) and Music Factory nightlife mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Obsidian Void & Fiery Coral Palette:** Nightlife cantina palette anchored in deep obsidian night void (`#0F172A`), fiery agave coral (`#F97316`), electric lime neon (`#84CC16`), and crisp silver glow (`#F8FAFC`).
3. **"The Sizzling Fajitas & Tequila Lounge Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`fajita-skillet-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing cast-iron fajita skillets alongside handcrafted tequila margaritas & late-night concert dining.
