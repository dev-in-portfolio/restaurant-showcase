The Art of Sizzling Fajitas

Nothing signals Mexican cantina celebration like the sound & aroma of a sizzling fajita skillet. At La Unica Bar & Grill, we marinate prime meats in citrus, garlic, & Mexican spices before searing them over open flames & serving them on preheated cast-iron skillets.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Sizzling Fajita Craft

1. Citrus-Chili Marinade

Fresh orange juice, lime, cumin, Mexican oregano, & garlic tenderize prime skirt steak & chicken.

2. Open-Flame Sear

Meats are char-broiled over open mesquite flames to lock in juices before slicing across the grain.

3. Screaming Cast-Iron

Cast-iron skillets heated to 500°F caramelize onions & peppers, delivering table-side sizzle & aroma.
