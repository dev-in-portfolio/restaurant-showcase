## Original Audit Weakness

CHNO Coffee Co. relies on basic Square ordering links (`chnocoffeeco.square.site`). Located at 4612 Gilead St in LoSo Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its chemical caffeine lab identity, cereal milk latte alchemy, local pastry pairings, and LoSo neighborhood community vibe.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Élan LoSo Gilead Street Location:** 4612 Gilead St, Charlotte NC 28217 (Élan LoSo / Lower South End).
2. **Single-Origin Guatemala Roasts & Cereal Milk Alchemy:** Antigua Guatemala single-origin espresso (roasted by Giddy Coffee), "Gramma Deb" oatmeal cream pie matcha lattes, and Cap'n Crunch cereal milk lattes.
3. **Zero-Proof Caffeine Boosters & Shushees:** Caffeine-free fruit "shushees", plant-based energy infusions, and local artisanal morning pastries.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Cyber Tech Caffeine Lab Typography:** Futuristic tech display sans (*Space Grotesk*) paired with clean modern body sans (*Outfit*) and chemical lab technical mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Obsidian & Neon Cyan Palette:** High-contrast chemical lab palette anchored in dark obsidian (`#0F1117`), electric neon cyan (`#00F0FF`), electric magenta (`#FF007A`), cereal gold (`#F5A623`), and chalk white (`#F4F6FB`).
3. **"The Chemical Caffeine Formula & Nostalgia Brew Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`caffeine-lab-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing Guatemalan espresso science alongside cereal milk lattes.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Chez Marie Pâtisserie` — Parisian high-fashion pâtisserie with Cormorant Garamond/Outfit fonts.
2. `Cheat’s Cheesesteaks` — High-octane Philly cheesesteak joint with Outfit/Plus Jakarta Sans fonts.
3. `Chaat ’N’ Dosa` — South Indian saffron spice & paper dosa layout with Playfair/Outfit fonts.

### Divergent Choices for CHNO Coffee Co.

- **Hero Composition:** Modern Chemical Caffeine Lab & Nostalgic Cereal Latte Vault split-hero layout: left side features a neon cyan caffeine formula stamp badge (`"C8H10N4O2 CAFFEINE LAB • LOWER SOUTH END (LOSO)"`), cyber tech display typography (*Space Grotesk*), obsidian & neon cyan palette, and right side features a sunlit hero image of an artisanal cereal milk matcha espresso latte.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Formula Vault Cards** (`formula-vault-card`) and **Caffeine Lab Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`chno-header`, `caffeine-brand`, `lab-hero-stage`, `cyan-stamp-badge`, `formula-vault-card`, `chno-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
