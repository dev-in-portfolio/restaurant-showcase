Guatemala Espresso & Chemistry

CHNO stands for Carbon, Hydrogen, Nitrogen, and Oxygen—the exact chemical elements that make up C8H10N4O2 (Caffeine). We roast single-origin Antigua Guatemala coffee beans for balanced acidity, body, and sweetness.
