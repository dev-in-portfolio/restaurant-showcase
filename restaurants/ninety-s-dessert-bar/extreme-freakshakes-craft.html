🎮 Shake Engineering & Cereal Nostalgia

The Art of the Extreme Shake

At Ninety's Dessert Bar, an extreme freakshake is an edible sculpture of 90s nostalgia. Every creation starts with thick artisan ice cream infused with classic cereal milks, poured into custom frosted-rim mason jars, and crowned with freshly blowtorched s'mores, giant cookies, and retro candies.

Freakshake Engineering

The 3 Pillars of Ninety's Freakshakes

1. Cereal Milk Infusion Base

Steeping real whole milk in nostalgic childhood cereals like Cinnamon Toast Crunch and Fruity Pebbles before hand-spinning.

2. Structural Frosting Rims

Coating mason jar rims in thick chocolate hazelnut spread, marshmallow fluff, and birthday cake frosting rolled in crunchy toppings.

3. Blowtorched Pastry Crowns

Topping each shake with full baked goods—toasted campfire s'mores skewers, frosted Pop-Tarts, and jumbo wafer straws.
