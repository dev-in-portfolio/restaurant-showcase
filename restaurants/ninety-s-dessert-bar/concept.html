📺 90s Saturday Mornings & Pure Joy

A Love Letter to the Radical 1990s

Ninety's Dessert Bar was created to transport you back to the golden era of Saturday morning cartoons, neon windbreakers, arcade high scores, and big bowls of sugary cereal. Located on the University Boardwalk, we combine radical 90s nostalgia with over-the-top culinary dessert artistry.

Pure Nostalgic Joy
