The BYOF Philosophy

How BYOF Works at Two Buck Saloon

Support your favorite local restaurants and food trucks while enjoying cold drinks with us.

Rule 1

Order From Anywhere

Grab pizza from Belmont, tacos from Central Avenue food trucks, burgers, or order delivery straight to the saloon bar counter. Any food is 100% welcome.

Rule 2

We Provide The Supplies

Just ask your bartender for paper plates, napkins, or extra forks. We keep a full stash behind the bar for our dining patrons.

Rule 3

Pair With Cold Drinks

Grab a cold $2 PBR, a craft draft, or our $5 Buck Shot to wash down your meal while you watch the game or shoot a round of free pool.

Neighborhood Synergy

Supporting Charlotte Local Eats

Rather than running a limited bar kitchen, our BYOF model celebrates Charlotte’s incredible culinary scene. Neighborhood regulars frequently coordinate group pizza orders, barbecue tailgates, and taco nights right on our spacious pine tables.
