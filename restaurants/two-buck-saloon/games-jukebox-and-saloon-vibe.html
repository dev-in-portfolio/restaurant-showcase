The Entertainment Lineup

Everything you need for a memorable night with friends.

Complimentary Billiards

Free Pool Tables

Regulation billiard tables equipped with fresh felt, balanced cue sticks, and chalk. Winner stays on, with lively friendly competition every night of the week.

Precision Throwing

Steel-Tip Darts

Traditional sisal bristle dartboards with chalk scoreboards for Cricket and 501. Bring your own tungsten set or borrow house darts from behind the bar.

Digital Music

TouchTunes Jukebox

Play millions of songs from your phone or the wall console. From classic rock and 90s grunge to outlaw country and hip hop, you set the evening vibe.

Game Day Hub

Carolina Sports on Every Screen

Catch the Carolina Panthers, Charlotte Hornets, Charlotte FC, and college football matchups on our high-definition screens. With budget-friendly drink buckets and plenty of table room, Two Buck Saloon is Charlotte's favorite unpretentious tailgate spot.
