Know Before You Go

Saloon House Rules & Etiquette

  • Age Restriction: 21+ only at all times. Please have a valid government-issued photo ID ready at the door or bar.
  • 100% BYOF Welcome: Carry in food from any restaurant or food truck. We have napkins and paper plates ready for you.
  • Free Games: Pool tables and steel-tip darts are complimentary. Respect fellow players and keep cue sticks on the table.
  • Parking: Ample free street parking and adjacent lot spaces available at all locations.
