The Ultimate Soup Dipper

No gourmet grilled cheese experience is complete without its soulmate: our famous scratch velvet tomato soup. Rich, creamy, and seasoned with roasted garlic and fresh sweet basil, it delivers the quintessential warmth for dipping butter-toasted melts alongside golden tater tots.

The 3 Pillars of Papi Queso Dippers & Sides

1. Velvet Slow-Simmered Soup

Simmering crushed Italian plum tomatoes with caramelized aromatics and sweet cream for a velvety, dip-ready texture.

2. Truffle & Parm Crispy Tots

Frying barrel-shaped tater tots to a deep golden crunch, tossed in fragrant white truffle oil and snowy grated parmesan.

3. Scratch Signature Dips

Blending house garlic herb mayo, spicy chipotle aioli, and molten cheese sauces for extra dunking joy.
