Full Melt Menu

The Iconic "Pig Mac", "Lil Cheesy", Velvet Tomato Soup, & Truffle Tots at 1115 N Brevard St.

Signature Melts Toasted Sourdough

The "Pig Mac" #1 Signature Melt

Hickory-smoked slow-cooked pulled pork shoulder, creamy sharp cheddar mac & cheese, and house sweet-pickled red onions on golden butter-toasted artisan sourdough.

The "Lil Cheesy" Artisan Classic

The quintessential three-cheese grilled cheese: sharp yellow cheddar, creamy fontina, and aged provolone melted to molten perfection on buttered sourdough bread.

Texas Cheese Steak Melt Hearty Beef

Thin-sliced seasoned ribeye steak, caramelized sweet onions, pickled jalapeños, and smothered pepper jack and cheddar cheeses on crusty bread.

The Classic "Chop Cheese" NYC Inspired

Griddled seasoned ground beef chopped with sweet onions, melted American and cheddar cheese, crisp shredded lettuce, ripe tomato, and special sauce.

Soup Dippers & Tots Comfort Sides

Famous Velvet Tomato Soup Dipper Melt Essential

Slow-simmered San Marzano tomatoes blended with heavy sweet cream, roasted garlic, fresh basil, and a touch of black pepper. Served in a dipping crock.

Truffle Parmesan Tater Tots Loaded Side

Extra crispy golden tater tots tossed in white truffle oil, finely grated aged parmesan reggiano, and fresh chopped parsley.

Queso Smothered Tots Cheesy Side

Golden crispy tots smothered in molten house cheese sauce, pickled jalapeño rings, and chopped bacon crumbles.

Scratch Dips & Sauces Flavor Boosters

Papi's secret special burger sauce, roasted garlic herb mayo, spicy chipotle aioli, and extra cups of melted warm queso.
