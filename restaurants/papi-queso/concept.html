🚚 From Food Truck to Food Hall Landmark

Born on Wheels, Rooted in Optimist Hall.

Papi Queso began as a grassroots mobile food truck revolutionizing Charlotte's street food scene with audacious, chef-driven gourmet grilled cheese melts. Today, as an anchor stall inside Optimist Hall, we continue to push the boundaries of comfort food with uncompromising craft and hospitality.

The Values of Papi Queso

Chef-Driven Comfort

Applying classical culinary techniques, slow-smoking meats in-house, and sourcing premium cheeses to elevate humble grilled cheese into an art form.

Optimist Hall Energy

Contributing to the lively culinary culture of Optimist Hall, serving hundreds of hungry locals, families, and visitors every single day.

Community Connection

Proudly rooted in Charlotte, celebrating local bakeries, craft beer pairings, and loyal cheese lovers across the Queen City.
