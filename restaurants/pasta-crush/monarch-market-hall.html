Center City Hub

The Monarch Market Atmosphere

Monarch Market at One South Tryon brings together the best independent food concepts in Charlotte across an open, multi-level architectural dining hall. Pasta Crush serves as the cornerstone Italian pasta destination:

  • Skillet to Table in 4 Minutes: Watch our chefs flash-sauté your pasta bowl right in front of you.
  • Craft Beverage Pairing: Pair your pasta with draft beers, Italian wines, and cocktails from Monarch Market's Aster Lounge.
  • Diverse Seating Options: High-top counter seats, spacious communal tables, and covered outdoor terrace seating.
Getting Here

Location & Center City Access

Steps from Uptown towers, cultural museums, and light rail transit.

1. Trade & Tryon Center

Located at 101 North Tryon Street inside One South Tryon, at the absolute core of Charlotte's financial and arts district.

2. LYNX Light Rail

Two blocks from both the Charlotte Transportation Center (CTC/Arena) and 7th Street LYNX Blue Line rail stations.

3. Parking Decks

Convenient parking available in the One South Tryon garage and nearby Center City parking decks with direct elevator access.
