## Original Audit Weakness

Pasta Crush delivers a high-speed, craveable artisan pasta concept to the dense corporate core of Uptown Charlotte. However, its previous online footprint lacked a dedicated multi-page website illustrating the build-your-own sauce combination matrix, explaining the fresh semolina skillet-saut&#233; technique, and highlighting food hall dining seating. This 6-page responsive web experience provides a warm, high-converting digital storefront.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Skillet-Saut&#233;ed Fresh Pasta Bowls:** Fresh semolina pasta tossed to order over high heat in woks and skillets with silky emulsified sauces and grated aged parmesan.
2. **Signature & Global Fusion Sauces:** High-impact flavor crossovers including Cajun Cream Shrimp, Indian Butter Chicken Tikka Masala Alfredo, and rich Truffle Pesto.
3. **Monarch Market Center City Vibe:** Energetic urban food hall setting at Trade and Tryon with quick counter service, validated parking options, and communal lounges.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Tuscan Tomato Red & Semolina Cream Palette:** Deep Tuscan Red (`#b91c1c`), Parmesan Gold (`#eab308`), Basil Leaf Green (`#15803d`), Semolina Cream (`#fdfbf7`), and Dark Slate (`#1e293b`).
2. **Custom Pasta Builder Architecture:** Step-by-step pasta shape, sauce profile, protein, and vegetable mix-in visual breakdown.
3. **Warm Modern Italian Typography:** Fraunces editorial serif display headlines paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for clear pricing and ingredient customization.
