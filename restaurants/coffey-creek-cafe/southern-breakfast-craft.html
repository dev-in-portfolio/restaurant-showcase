39 Years of Morning Craft

The Art of Southern Breakfast

Since 1987, Coffey Creek Café has been cracking fresh eggs and grilling country ham before sunrise. We believe a real Southern breakfast needs stone-ground buttered grits, crispy bacon, and warm biscuits.

The 3 Pillars of Coffey Creek Mornings

1. Fresh Cracked Triple Eggs

We crack grade-A eggs directly on the hot flat top grill for sunny-side up, over easy, or scrambled perfection.

2. Carolina Livermush Tradition

Authentic North Carolina livermush sliced thick and pan-seared until crispy on the outside and tender inside.

3. Buttered Stone-Ground Grits

Slow-simmered white corn grits whipped with real butter and seasoned for authentic Southern comfort.
