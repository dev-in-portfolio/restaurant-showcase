Smoked Daily Over Hardwood Coals

Wood-Cooked Meats, Scratch Southern Sides.

At Sweet Lew’s BBQ, we cook meat the way North Carolinians have for generations—with nothing but split white oak, hickory, and pecan wood coals. Our meats are smoked fresh each morning and served until the cutting boards are bare. All combo platters include two scratch sides and golden sweet corn hushpuppies.
