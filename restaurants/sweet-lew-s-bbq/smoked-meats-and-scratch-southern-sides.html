Great barbecue begins at the smoke pits, but it is completed in the scratch kitchen. At Sweet Lew’s BBQ, our 15-hour Texas-style briskets and St. Louis cut pork ribs are paired with sides made fresh from scratch every single morning—slow-simmered collard greens, baked mac & cheese, and crispy sweet corn hushpuppies served hot with whipped honey butter.

Kitchen Principles

The Sideboard is Never an Afterthought

Fresh Southern sides made from scratch with zero shortcuts.

Side 01

Real Potlikker Collard Greens

We clean fresh leafy greens by hand, simmering them slowly with cider vinegar, smoked pork trimmings, and chili flakes to create nutrient-dense, flavorful potlikker.

Side 02

Golden Fried Sweet Corn Hushpuppies

No premade frozen mix. We combine stone-ground cornmeal, sweet corn kernels, buttermilk, and scallions, frying them fresh to order with honey butter.

Side 03

Scratch-Cooked Banana Pudding

We make our vanilla bean custard on the stovetop, layering it in mason jars with freshly sliced ripe bananas, fluffy cream, and crisp Nilla wafers.

The Sweet Lew Sampler Experience

Can't decide between pork, brisket, and ribs? The Sweet Lew Sampler brings the entire smokehouse to your table. Piled onto butcher paper with two scratch sides and warm hushpuppies, it lets you experience the distinctive smoke profiles of whole pork shoulder, peppery brisket, and caramelized spare ribs all at once.
