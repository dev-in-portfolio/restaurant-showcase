Transit & Parking

Arriving at Sweet Lew’s BBQ

Visiting our Belmont smokehouse is easy and hassle-free:

  • Free On-Site Surface Lot: Dedicated customer parking spots are located right in front of and beside our building at 923 Belmont Avenue.
  • Street Parking: Additional free, unmetered neighborhood street parking is available along Belmont Avenue and adjoining side streets.
  • Quick Access: Located just five minutes from Uptown Charlotte and directly between NoDa and Plaza Midwood.
Smokehouse Experience

Outdoor Seating & Takeout Guidance

What to expect when dining with us:

  • Red Picnic Benches: Enjoy your smoked meats outside under the North Carolina sky at our shaded wooden picnic tables.
  • Family Takeout & Poundage: Order pulled pork and beef brisket by the half-pound or pound, along with pints of mac & cheese and collards for quick dinner pickup.
  • Dog-Friendly Outdoor Area: Leashed dogs are welcome in our outdoor patio seating space.
