Real Hardwood Coals, True Carolina BBQ.

Welcome to Sweet Lew’s BBQ, Charlotte’s standard-bearer for authentic wood-cooked North Carolina barbecue. Pitmaster Lewis Donald burns white oak, hickory, and pecan down to glowing embers in outdoor burn barrels, shoveling live coals by hand under whole pork shoulders, 15-hour beef briskets, and St. Louis spare ribs. No gas, no electric shortcuts—just pure smoke, heritage technique, and scratch-made Southern sides seven days a week.

Carolina Classic

NC Chopped Pork with Red Slaw

Our chopped pork begins with whole heritage pork shoulders cooked low and slow over hickory coals for 12 to 14 hours. Pulled and chopped by hand, it is seasoned with our cider vinegar, crushed red pepper, and spice dip, then piled high on a soft potato bun topped with crisp, freshly dressed coleslaw.

Pitmaster Ribs

St. Louis Spare Ribs & Potato Salad

Rubbed with brown sugar, cracked black pepper, and paprika, our St. Louis cut pork spare ribs smoke until bite-tender with clean bone pullback and caramelized bark. Pair with our house-made mustard dill potato salad or sweet corn hushpuppies with honey butter.
