Historic Belmont Neighborhood • 923 Belmont Ave

Restoring North Carolina's Endangered Craft.

When Pitmaster Lewis Donald opened Sweet Lew’s BBQ at 923 Belmont Avenue, his mission was clear: bring back genuine wood-cooked barbecue to Charlotte. Fewer than five percent of modern barbecue restaurants still burn whole logs down to coals. By transforming an old neighborhood service station into a pitmaster haven, Sweet Lew's created a vibrant community living room dedicated to smoke, craft, and authentic Southern hospitality.

Core Values

The Foundations of Sweet Lew’s BBQ

How we uphold North Carolina barbecue culture without compromise.

Value 01

Pure Wood Integrity

No pellets, gas burners, or artificial liquid smoke. We burn real logs down to coals every day, upholding the culinary purity of North Carolina barbecue history.

Value 02

Neighborhood Roots

Located on Belmont Avenue, we are proud to serve as an affordable, welcoming gathering spot for long-time residents, workers, families, and barbecue pilgrims alike.

Value 03

Scratch Craftsmanship

From our vinegary Eastern mop sauce and Alabama white dip to fresh hushpuppies and sweet tea, every single element is prepared by hand in our Belmont kitchen.

From Service Station to Smokehouse

A Beacon on Belmont Avenue

The cinderblock building at 923 Belmont Avenue once tuned engines and changed tires. Today, its garage bays and outdoor yard are filled with the sweet, unmistakable perfume of burning hickory and white oak. Pull up a red picnic bench and join our barbecue family.
