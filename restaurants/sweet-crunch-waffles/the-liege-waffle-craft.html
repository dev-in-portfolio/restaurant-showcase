Waffle Taxonomy

Liège vs. Brussels vs. American Waffles

Most Americans are familiar with breakfast waffles made from aerated baking powder batter poured onto non-stick irons. Belgian waffle tradition is vastly richer and divided into two historic masters:

  • Brussels Waffles: Rectangular, light, and airy with deep pockets, made from liquid egg white batter and dusted with powdered sugar.
  • American Waffles: Round, pancake-adjacent batter requiring maple syrup to add flavor and moisture.
  • Liège Waffles (Our Specialty): Irregularly shaped oval dough studded with pearl sugar. Dense, chewy, and intensely caramelized with sweet pocket bursts. No syrup needed.
The Secret Ingredient

The Magic of Belgian Pearl Sugar

Why regular granulated sugar can never recreate authentic Liège crunch.

1. High Compression Clusters

Belgian pearl sugar (sucre perlé) is formed by heavily compacting refined beet sugar crystals into dense rounded pebbles. Because of their density, they resist dissolving into the moist dough during fermentation.

2. Heat Resistance & Melting Point

When the dough ball is placed onto our seasoned 375°F cast-iron press, the outer pearls melt against the iron grates into liquid golden caramel, coating every ridge in a glossy, crunchy candy shell.

3. The Intact Sugar Pocket

The pearls embedded deeper inside the dough melt only partially, leaving delightful tiny sugar crunches hidden within the soft, buttery brioche crumb for a dynamic dual texture.

Bakery Tips

How to Enjoy at Peak Texture

A fresh Sweet Crunch waffle straight from the iron is an extraordinary sensory experience. Because the caramelized sugar glaze hardens slightly as it cools, the exterior becomes delightfully brittle while the center remains moist.

  • At The Market: Enjoy immediately while walking the aisles or seated at community tables with an espresso.
  • Taking Boxes Home: Store in our ventilated bakery box at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze up to 1 month.
  • The Toaster Method: Warm for 3-4 minutes in a toaster oven at 350°F to reactivate the fragrant butter and sugar glaze.
