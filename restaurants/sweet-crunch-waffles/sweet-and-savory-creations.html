Sweet Artistry

The Sweet Spectrum

Our sweet specialty waffles are crafted to balance the caramelized pearl sugar dough with bright fruit acidity, velvety nut spreads, and airy creams:

  • Berry Loaded: Sliced tart strawberries, plump blueberries, and warm Nutella that melts into every waffle pocket.
  • Cookies n' Cream: Triple-threat combination of dark chocolate fudge, Oreo crunch, and creamy Belgian speculoos cookie butter.
  • S'mores Fireside: Graham cracker dust and warm melted chocolate topped with roasted marshmallow fluff.
Market Collaboration

The Savory Revolution: Brie & Hot Honey

Being located inside The Market at 7th Street gives us direct access to Charlotte's premier artisan food vendors. Our signature savory creation is a direct partnership with Orrman's Cheese Shop:

  • Melted French Brie: Silky, earthy French Brie cheese melted directly over the hot caramelized waffle grid.
  • Hickory Smoked Bacon: Generous layers of thick-cut, crisp savory bacon crumbles.
  • Chili-Infused Hot Honey: A generous drizzle of spicy-sweet clover honey that ties the rich cheese and sweet dough together seamlessly.
