Uptown Charlotte Food Hub

Inside The Market at 7th Street

Located inside Uptown's historic non-profit community food hall. A gathering place for local culinary artisans, coffee lovers, and food enthusiasts.

Community & Culture

Charlotte's Premier Urban Food Incubator

The Market at 7th Street (formerly 7th Street Public Market) operates as a mission-driven culinary hub that nurtures emerging local food entrepreneurs while providing Uptown Charlotte with farm-fresh produce, artisanal baked goods, and diverse global cuisines.

  • Support Local Business: Every waffle purchased directly supports a local Charlotte small business and the non-profit market mission.
  • Artisan Synergy: Order a fresh waffle at Sweet Crunch, grab a specialty pour-over or cortado from the coffee bar, and browse local cheeses and gourmet provisions.
  • Welcoming Public Seating: Spacious indoor communal seating, sunny outdoor patios, and fast guest Wi-Fi.
Easy Access

Getting Here is Effortless

Enjoy 90 minutes of free parking or ride Charlotte's premier transit line directly to our door.

1. 90-Min Validated Parking

Pull into the 7th Street Station Parking Garage (located directly above the market at 224 E. 7th St.). Bring your ticket inside, and any vendor will validate it for 90 minutes free.

2. LYNX Blue Line Light Rail

Exit at the 7th Street Station light rail stop. The train platform connects directly to the market's covered patio entrance, making it the easiest breakfast stop on your commute.

3. Uptown Walkability

Located in the First Ward neighborhood, just 3 blocks from Tryon Street, Levine Center for the Arts, First Ward Park, and the Charlotte Rail Trail.
