90-Minute Validated Garage Parking
7th Street Station Garage

Park with ease directly above the market at the 7th Street Station Parking Garage (entrances on 7th St. and 8th St.). Bring your parking ticket to our counter when ordering for 90 minutes of free validated parking.

Rapid Light Rail Access
LYNX 7th Street Station

Directly connected to the light rail platform. Step off the train and straight onto our covered market patio for an easy morning or afternoon treat.

Ordering Options
Walk-Up & Boxed Takeout

Walk up to our counter inside the market hall. Individual waffles are pressed hot in 3-4 minutes. Multi-pack boxes are packed fresh for home reheating.

Validated Parking Summary

Remember to bring your paper parking garage ticket inside to our counter. Validation applies to the 7th Street Station garage for up to 90 minutes every day of the week.
