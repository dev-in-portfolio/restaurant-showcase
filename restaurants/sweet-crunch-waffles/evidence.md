## Original Audit Weakness

Sweet Crunch Waffles operates one of the highest-rated sweet and savory breakfast concepts in Uptown Charlotte, drawing heavy pedestrian foot traffic from office towers, local residents, and 7th Street light rail commuters. However, its web presence previously relied only on a third-party Square checkout page without an immersive multi-page brand home explaining the science of Liège yeast dough vs. American batter waffles, detailing savory collaborations with Orrman's Brie, and highlighting 90-minute validated garage parking. This 6-page responsive web experience provides an elegant, mouthwatering digital showcase.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Authentic Belgian Liège Yeast Dough:** Made from brioche-style dough with yeast and imported pearl sugar (sucre perle) that melts into crunchy caramelized crusts rather than batter-based American waffles.
2. **Specialty Sweet & Savory Combinations:** The famous "Berry Loaded" with fresh berries and Nutella, and the savory "Bacon, Bacon" made with creamy French Brie from neighboring Orrman's Cheese Shop and hot honey.
3. **The Market at 7th Street Experience:** Situated in Uptown Charlotte's premier market hall with validated 90-minute parking, direct rail transit, outdoor patio seating, and artisan coffee pairings.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Warm Belgian Caramel & Pearl Vanilla Palette:** Deep Belgian Caramel (`#b45309`), Warm Amber Glaze (`#d97706`), Dark Cocoa Truffle (`#29180d`), Whipped Vanilla Cream (`#fffdf9`), Warm Dough Cream (`#fbf5ea`), and Wild Berry Accent (`#e11d48`).
2. **Artisan Waffle Craft Anatomy:** Visual step-by-step breakdown of brioche yeast fermentation, pearl sugar caramelization chemistry, and cast-iron press heat transfer.
3. **Warm Editorial Typography:** High-contrast Fraunces serif display headlines paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and Space Mono for accurate pricing and market hours.
