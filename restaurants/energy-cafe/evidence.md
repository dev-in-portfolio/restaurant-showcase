- **Concept & Offerings:** Uptown Charlotte's premier fuel-focused health food cafe with dual flagship locations on South Tryon Street. Powering financial executives, legal professionals, and urban athletes with clean, nutrient-dense nutrition: warm protein bowls, signature Popeye Wraps, fresh rotisserie chicken, crisp grain salads, high-performance smoothies (The Hulk, Java Diesel, Recharge), Brazilian superfruit açai bowls, chia parfaits, avocado toast, and corporate healthy box lunch catering.
