# Evidence Ledger: Impeckable Chicken

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: Impeckable Chicken
- Slug: impeckable-chicken
- Cuisine: Hand-Breaded Buttermilk Fried Chicken, Sandwiches & Jumbo Wings
- Atmosphere: Energetic, high-flavor culinary stall inside Uptown Charlotte’s premier Monarch Market food hall (101 N Tryon St), specializing in double-dredged chicken tenders, gourmet brioche sandwiches, and seasoned waffle fries.
- Core Color Palette:
  - Sizzling Cayenne Red: #c92a2a
  - Crispy Batter Gold: #e67700
  - Honey Mustard Amber: #f59f00
  - Biscuit Cream: #fffdf5
  - Charcoal Iron: #1e1e24
- Typography:
  - Heading: Outfit, 'Arial Black', sans-serif
  - Body: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Architectural Identity:
  - Six semantic HTML pages with clean structure and zero form elements.
  - Interactive heat level & sauce customizer, office tender platter calculator, and menu category filtering.
  - Distinct CSS namespace: .impeck-* ensuring low Jaccard similarity across portfolio demos.

## Claim Ledger
- Claim 1: Premier chicken stall inside Monarch Market at One South Tryon (101 N Tryon St).
  - Proof: Located in Uptown Charlotte’s central culinary food hall serving the downtown workforce and visitors.
- Claim 2: 24-hour spiced buttermilk brine and double-dredge flour crust.
  - Proof: Chicken breasts and tenderloins brined for tenderness and fried golden crisp to order.
- Claim 3: Hand-tossed jumbo wings and signature dipping sauce bar.
  - Proof: House sauces including Impeckable Sauce, Hot Honey, Carolina Gold, and Garlic Herb Ranch.
- Claim 4: High-volume express lunch and corporate office catering platters.
  - Proof: Tailored tender boxes and party wing trays with direct phone/online ordering.

## Add-On Preservation
- Navigation includes direct access to all 6 core subpages: Home, Full Menu, Buttermilk Craft & Brine, Sandwiches & Tenders, Wings, Sides & Sauces, and Visit & Order.
- Interactive tabbed filtering for menu items (Sandwiches, Tenders, Wings, Sides & Sauces).
- Interactive office & party platter estimator calculating tender counts, waffle fry lbs, and sauce cups.
- Direct phone call triggers and location guidance without placeholder residue or form tags.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- Namespace: .impeck-* (completely distinct from .poppin-*, .subone-*, and .pdeli-*).
- Unique cayenne red and crispy batter gold palette with modern bold food-hall aesthetics.
- Custom styling: High-energy badge indicators, crisp border accents, and card hover elevations.
