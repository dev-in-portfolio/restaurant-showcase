🍖 Slow-Simmered Comfort Heritage

The Art of Soul Food Craft

True soul food takes time. We slow-braise our oxtails for hours until tender enough to melt off the bone into rich brown gravy, paired with scratch baked five-cheese mac and cheese & sweet yam cornbread.

Soul Food Principles

The 3 Pillars of Everybody Eatz Soul Craft

1. Slow Herb Braising

Beef oxtails & turkey legs simmered low and slow with fresh thyme, allspice, garlic, & sweet onions.

2. Five-Cheese Baked Macaroni

Sharp cheddar, gouda, & Monterey jack folded into egg & heavy cream custard baked golden brown.

3. Smoked Turkey Pot Liquor Greens

Fresh collard greens slow-steeped with smoked turkey leg & tangy cider vinegar for savory depth.
