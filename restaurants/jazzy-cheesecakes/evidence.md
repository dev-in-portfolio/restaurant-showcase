## Original Audit Weakness

Jazzy Cheesecakes relies on basic third-party online ordering apps (Toasttab). Located at 9402 Northlake West Dr in Northlake Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its artisan graham-crust baking craft, cheesecake-in-a-jar delivery subscriptions, cheesecake of the month club, and corporate dessert catering platters.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Northlake Charlotte Bakery Location:** 9402 Northlake West Dr, Ste 102, Charlotte NC 28216.
2. **Handcrafted Gourmet Cheesecake Specialties:** Red Velvet Tuxedo, Strawberry Crunch Lava, Sweet Potato Pecan Pie, Banana Pudding Caramel, Peach Cobbler Cheesecake, Cheesecake Mason Jars, Mini Bites, & Cheesecake Milkshakes.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (980) 859-7859; Mon–Fri 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM | Sat 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM | Sun 11:00 AM – 7:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Stylish Jazz Serif Typography:** Bold jazz serif (*Syne*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and bakery timer mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Velvet Magenta Plum & Raspberry Pink Palette:** Gourmet dessert palette anchored in deep velvet plum (`#4C0519`), hot raspberry pink (`#BE185D`), caramel amber gold (`#F59E0B`), and cream vanilla parchment (`#FFFDF5`).
3. **"The Gourmet Cheesecake Jar & Party Platter Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`cheesecake-baking-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing whole artisanal cheesecakes alongside portable cheesecake jars & party dessert trays.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Jaipur Indian Restaurant` — Royal Pink City Palace with Cormorant Garamond fonts and Deep Crimson/Saffron.
2. `Italo` — Venetian Renaissance Italian with Bodoni Moda fonts and Barolo Burgundy/Tuscan Gold.
3. `Ilios Crafted Greek` — Aegean Coastal Mediterranean with Cinzel fonts and Deep Aegean Cobalt/Azure.

### Divergent Choices for Jazzy Cheesecakes

- **Hero Composition:** Velvet Magenta Jazz Lounge & Gourmet Dessert Vault split-hero layout: left side features a Magenta Glow stamp badge (`"GOURMET HANDCRAFTED CHEESECAKE BAR • NORTHLAKE CHARLOTTE NC"`), stylish jazz serif (*Syne*), velvet plum & hot raspberry palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of artisanal whole cheesecakes & dessert jars.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Jazzy Vault Cards** (`jazzy-vault-card`) and Northlake dessert highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`jazzy-header`, `jazzy-brand`, `lounge-hero-stage`, `magenta-stamp-badge`, `jazzy-vault-card`, `jazzy-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
