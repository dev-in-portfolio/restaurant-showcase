🏛 The Platform Story

Wesley Heights Roots, Modern Vision.

Born inside the historic industrial walls of Station West on Berryhill Road, Platform Coffee + Kitchen was founded to provide Charlotte with a true third place. Evolving from a neighborhood coffee bar into a full specialty micro-roastery and scratch kitchen, we serve as an anchor for the West End's growing community.

Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of Platform Coffee

Ethical Direct Sourcing

Honoring the hard work of coffee farmers worldwide through transparent, equitable sourcing partnerships and precision roasting.

Inclusive Hospitality

Creating an open, welcoming environment where everyone—from daily commuters to remote workers and neighborhood families—feels at home.

Culinary Passion

Refusing standard pre-packaged cafe food in favor of fresh seasonal ingredients, locally sourced eggs, and scratch-baked breads.
