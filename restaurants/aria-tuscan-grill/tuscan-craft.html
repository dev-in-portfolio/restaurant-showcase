Tuscan Wood-Fired Craft

At the heart of Aria is our open kitchen, anchored by a wood-burning oven that sears pizzas at 500°F and roasts savory prime meats.

Technique & Tradition

The Four Pillars of Aria Craft

1. High-Heat Wood Searing

Hardwood logs generate intense 500°F heat for blistered, airy pizza crusts and charred octopus.

2. Caramelized Gnocchi

House potato gnocchi seared on flat-top skillets until golden-brown crust forms on both sides.

3. Slow-Braised Duck Ragù

Venetian-style duck simmered for 8 hours with red wine, mirepoix, and herbs for deep savory depth.

4. Open Kitchen Experience

Watch our chefs roll pasta, toss pizzas, and flame-sear steaks live from your dining room table.
