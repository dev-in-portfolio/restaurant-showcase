Dining Etiquette

Reservation Directives & Seating

Guidelines for securing your table at Mi Cariño:

  • Advance Booking: Table reservations are released 30 days in advance via the Resy platform. Booking ahead is highly encouraged for weekend seatings.
  • Bar Seating: Our full menu and complete 200+ agave collection are available at the mezcal bar on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Pacing: Table reservations are paced for 90 minutes (parties of 1-4) or 120 minutes (parties of 5+) to ensure relaxed culinary enjoyment.
Payment Protocol

Cashless Establishment Directives

Information regarding payment handling at Mi Cariño:

  • Accepted Methods: We operate strictly as a cashless establishment. We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
  • Tab Management: Bar tabs may be transferred seamlessly between the mezcal bar and dining room tables upon seating.
  • Large Parties: Itemized check splits are supported for up to four separate payment cards per table.
