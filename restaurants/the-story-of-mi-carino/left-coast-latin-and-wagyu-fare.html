Butcher Cuts & Coastal Roots

Left Coast Heritage, Wagyu Precision.

"Left Coast Latin" is our culinary compass. It bridges the generational traditions of coastal Mexico—from Baja seafood stalls and Yucatan pibil smokehouses to Nayarit shrimp waters—with California's hyper-seasonal farm ethos and prime Texas Akaushi Wagyu butchery.

Kitchen Tenets

The Pillars of Our Cocina

Butchery, heirloom masa, and vibrant coastal raw preparations.

Pillar 01

Texas Akaushi Wagyu

Renowned for supreme intramuscular marbling and buttery tenderness, our steaks are seared intensely over high heat and rested with roasted garlic bone marrow butter.

Pillar 02

Baja-Style Raw Bar

Citrus-cured daily catches, wild Mexican shrimp, charred habanero broths, and fresh mango leche de tigre crafted with precise acidity and crisp tostada crunch.

Pillar 03

Slow-Braised Pibil

Pork belly and heritage duck marinated in Seville citrus, Mexican oregano, and Yucatán achiote, wrapped in banana leaves and slow-cooked for 12 hours.

Antojito Moderno

Truffle Street Corn Dip Alchemy

Street corn is one of Mexico’s greatest gifts to gastronomy. At Mi Cariño, we char sweet corn on the plancha, fold in velvety white truffle oil, crumbled salty cotija cheese, fresh lime juice, and house Mexican crema. Served piping hot with crispy freshly fried tortilla chips, it is an irresistible table starter.
