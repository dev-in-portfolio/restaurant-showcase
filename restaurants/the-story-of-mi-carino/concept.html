1440 South Tryon • Founded 2023

Warmth, Affection, Latin Soul.

In Spanish, "Mi Cariño" translates to "My Affection" or "My Darling"—a term of intimate endearment reserved for those we hold closest to our hearts. Founded in 2023 in Charlotte's South End, our restaurant was conceived as a vibrant expression of that love: poured into ancestral mezcals, heirloom corn nixtamalization, and unforgettable hospitality.

Core Values

The Values Driving Mi Cariño

Reverence for ancestral traditions, ingredient integrity, and heartfelt gathering.

Value 01

Agave Stewardship

We partner directly with family palenques across Oaxaca, Jalisco, and Michoacán, honoring the master distillers who protect multi-generational agave biodiversity.

Value 02

Uncompromising Quality

From certified Texas Akaushi Wagyu to wild Mexican blue shrimp and cold-pressed fresh juices, we refuse to compromise on ingredient excellence.

Value 03

Heartfelt Hospitality

Every detail of our candlelit cantina—from the greeting at the host stand to the copita pours at midnight—is crafted to make guests feel deeply cherished.

South End Energy

At the Epicenter of Charlotte Culture

Situated in the historic 1440 South Tryon corridor, The Story of Mi Cariño is part of the creative heartbeat that defines South End. Just steps from the Charlotte Rail Trail and LYNX Bland Street Station, our lounge offers an immersive sanctuary away from the bustling city streets.
