Ancestral Distillation • 200+ Bottles

Earth, Smoke, Agave Terroir.

The mezcalería at The Story of Mi Cariño is a tribute to the sacred spirits of Mexico. Housing more than 200 rare, small-batch, and ancestral bottles—from wild Tobalá and Tepeztate agaves to rare Jaliscan raicillas and Sonoran bacanoras—we invite guests on a guided journey through Mexico’s diverse microclimates.

Agave Disciplines

The Anatomy of Our Mezcalería

Wild harvests, pit roasting, and ceremonial copita service.

Tradition 01

Wild Agave Varieties

Featuring rare wild agaves that mature for up to 25 years before harvest—including high-altitude Tobalá, limestone-loving Tepeztate, and majestic Arroqueño.

Tradition 02

Ancestral Clay Distillation

Honoring master mezcaleros who roast piñas in underground conical stone pits, crush agave by tahona wheel, and distill in handcrafted clay pot stills.

Tradition 03

Ceremonial Copita Flights

Our single-village flights are presented in hand-thrown Oaxacan clay copitas with fresh sweet orange half-moons and house chili sal de gusano.

Pairing Symphony

Agave & Wagyu Harmony

Mezcal is gastronomy’s most versatile pairing partner. The smoky minerality of artisanal Espadín cuts through the lush, marbled richness of our Akaushi Wagyu ribeye, while the herbaceous floral notes of wild Tobalá enhance the lime and mango in our ceviches.
