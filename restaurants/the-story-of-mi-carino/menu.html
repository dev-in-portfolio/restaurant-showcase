Left Coast Latin Entrees & Wagyu

Butcher-cut prime meats and hearth-seared coastal Mexican specialties.

Akaushi Wagyu Ribeye $46.00

Seared 14-ounce Texas Akaushi Wagyu ribeye steak, roasted bone marrow garlic butter, charred scallion chimichurri, and crispy duck-fat fingerling potatoes.

Camarones Kora $28.00

Jumbo wild Mexican blue shrimp seared in smoky Nayarit-style chile piquín reduction, golden garlic chips, heirloom corn rice, and warm handmade tortillas.

Pork Belly Pibil $26.00

Berkshire pork belly slow-braised in banana leaves with citrus achiote marinade, seared crispy, served with pickled red habanero escabeche and heirloom corn tortillas.

Cast-Iron Branzino a la Talla $32.00

Butterflied whole Mediterranean sea bass painted half in smoky guajillo chile adobo and half in fresh parsley chimichurri, served with charred lime and herb salad.

Raw Bar & Chef Antojitos

Engineered for sharing and pairing with artisanal mezcals.

Ceviche Tropicoso $18.00

Fresh wild catch cured in passionfruit citrus leche de tigre, ripe mango, creamy diced avocado, shaved Persian cucumber, and pickled red onions on corn tostadas.

Truffle Street Corn Dip $15.00

Charred sweet summer corn off the cob, aromatic white truffle oil, crumbled aged cotija cheese, cilantro lime crema, and warm house-fried tortilla chips.

Aguachile Negro de Camarón $19.00

Wild Mexican shrimp cured in charred habanero lime broth, shaved crisp cucumbers, watermelon radish, Hass avocado, and heirloom blue tostadas.

Crispy Duck Carnitas Tacos $16.50

Three handmade corn tortillas piled with crispy duck carnitas, roasted sesame salsa macha, diced white onion, and fresh micro-cilantro.

Mezcalería Signature Libations

Over 200 agave bottles, hand-carved ice, and single-village distillates.

Smoked Grapefruit Paloma $16.00

Artisanal Mezcal Espadín, fresh ruby red grapefruit juice, lime, blue agave nectar, and black volcanic salt rim in a faceted crystal highball glass.

Oaxaca Old Fashioned $17.00

Single estate reposado tequila, wild Tobalá mezcal, raw agave nectar, Mexican mole bitters, and flame-expressed orange peel over carved crystal ice.

Single-Village Agave Flights $24.00 – $36.00

Three 1-oz neat pours of rare artisanal mezcals served in traditional clay copitas with fresh orange wheels and Oaxacan sal de gusano.

Popcorn Flan & Salted Caramel $11.00

Silky sweet corn flan topped with buttery popcorn crunch, roasted pepitas, and a warm sea salt piloncillo caramel reduction.
