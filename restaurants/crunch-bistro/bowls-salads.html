4 Steps to Your Custom Bowl

Endless combinations tailored to your dietary goals and taste preferences.

Step 1: The Base

Pick Your Green or Grain

Choose crisp Romaine, organic baby spinach, tender baby kale, peppery arugula, or steaming warm organic tri-color quinoa.

Step 2: 40+ Fresh Toppings

Market Vegetables & Seeds

Add fire-roasted corn, black beans, edamame, grape tomatoes, seedless cucumbers, sliced almonds, pumpkin seeds, and crumbled cheeses.

Step 3 & 4: Protein & Dressing
