🍓 The Crown Jewel of Antioquia Gastronomy

The Art of Bandeja Paisa

Bandeja Paisa is the legendary feast of Colombia's coffee-growing Antioquia region. At Medellín Colombian Restaurant, our kitchen crafts every element from scratch—scoring & deep-frying pork belly into crunchy chicharrón, braising red kidney beans for 6 hours, & grilling tender carne asada.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Medellín Bandeja Craft

1. Double-Fried Chicharrón Crackling

Pork belly skin scored into accordion strips, boiled with salt, & deep-fried until bubbly & crunchy.

2. 6-Hour Hogao Red Bean Stew

Red kidney beans simmered low with scallions, tomatoes, garlic, cumin, & savory pork trotter broth.

3. Griddle-Seared Carne Asada

Thinly sliced skirt steak marinated in garlic & lime, seared fast on high-heat flat top grills.
