## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South Charlotte Location:** 5909 South Blvd, Suite J, Charlotte NC 28217 (Archdale Marketplace on South Blvd near Archdale Dr).
2. **Paisa Colombian Specialties:** Signature Bandeja Paisa (Crispy Chicharrón, Grilled Carne Asada, Colombian Chorizo, Fried Egg, Rice, Red Beans, Sweet Plantain, & Arepa), Sancocho de Gallina Hen Soup, Crispy Beef Empanadas, Arepas de Choclo with Queso Campesino, Fresh Pandebono Pastries, & Aguardiente Cocktails.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 910-0009; Email `info@medellinclt.com`; Open Daily 10:00 AM – 12:00 AM Midnight.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Expressive Display Typography:** Warm expressive serif (*Playfair Display*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and Paisa bakery mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Emerald Jungle & Mango Gold Palette:** Vibrant Paisa palette anchored in deep emerald jungle green (`#064E3B`), golden mango yellow (`#EAB308`), Caribbean sky blue (`#0284C7`), and soft warm cream (`#FFFBEB`).
3. **"The Bandeja Paisa & Colombian Bakery Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`bandeja-paisa-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing 9-item Bandeja Paisa platters alongside fresh pandebono pastries & aguardiente cocktails.

- **Hero Composition:** Paisa Valley & Emerald Sunlit Hacienda Vault split-hero layout: left side features a Paisa Heritage Stamp badge (`"AUTHENTIC PAISA GASTRONOMY & COLOMBIAN BAKERY • SOUTH BLVD CHARLOTTE NC"`), warm expressive serif (*Playfair Display*), deep emerald jungle & golden mango palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of authentic Bandeja Paisa.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Medellín Vault Cards** (`medellin-vault-card`) and Archdale Marketplace highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`medellin-header`, `medellin-brand`, `emerald-hero-stage`, `paisa-seal-badge`, `medellin-vault-card`, `medellin-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
