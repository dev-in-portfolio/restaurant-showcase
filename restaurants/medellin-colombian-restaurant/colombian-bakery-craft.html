The Art of Paisa Bakery

The aroma of fresh pandebono & dark Colombian coffee fills our bakery every morning. At Medellín Colombian Restaurant, our baker kneads cassava starch & queso costeño cheese to bake hot pandebono rolls every hour, grilling sweet corn Arepas de Choclo to order.

Bakery Craft

The 3 Pillars of Medellín Colombian Bakery

1. Hourly Baked Pandebono

Naturally gluten-free cassava flour kneaded with salty white cheese & cornmeal for chewy golden rolls.

2. Sweet Corn Arepas de Choclo

Fresh tender yellow corn ground sweet & griddle-cooked with melted white campesino cheese slices.

3. Dark-Roast Colombian Coffee

Rich single-origin Colombian Arabica coffee beans brewed fresh for tinto & steaming café con leche.
