Driving From Charlotte

Easy Drive From Greater Charlotte

  • Via US-74 East / Monroe Expressway: Take US-74 E or the Monroe Bypass into Monroe, exit toward historic downtown, and turn onto S. Stewart Street.
  • Downtown Walking: Located within comfortable walking distance of Monroe’s shops, historic courthouse, and downtown restaurants.
  • Private Event Inquiries: Looking to host a birthday party, corporate mixer, or wedding rehearsal? Call our taproom team directly.
