12-Hour Cuban Simmer

The Legend of Ropa Vieja

Literally translating to 'old clothes', Ropa Vieja is Cuba’s national culinary treasure—flank steak slow-braised until tender enough to shred by hand into rich sofrito sauce.

Culinary Mastery

The Four Steps of Azucar Braising

1. Citrus Mojo Marinade

Prime beef flank steak steeped overnight in garlic, sour orange juice, oregano, and ground cumin.

2. Sofrito Reduction

Slow-sauteed Spanish onions, sweet bell peppers, garlic cloves, tomato reduction, and white wine broth.

3. Hand Shredding

Pulled into thin silky ribbons to absorb every drop of rich red wine gravy.

4. Congrí & Maduros Pairings

Served alongside black bean congrí rice, sweet fried plantains, and soft garlic yuca.
