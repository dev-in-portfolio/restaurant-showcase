The 3 Pillars of Cheat’s

Thinly Shaved Ribeye

Custom thinly sliced beef ribeye cooked fast on high-heat flat-top griddles, chopped and seasoned for juicy tender steak bites.

Cooper Sharp American

Renowned for its silky melt and sharp aged cheese profile, Cooper Sharp coats every strand of meat and onion.

Philadelphia Amoroso Rolls

Directly sourced hearth-baked Amoroso rolls—soft, airy interior with just enough crust resistance to hold the steak juices.
