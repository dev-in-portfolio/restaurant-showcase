🥧 Inclusive Cocktail Engineering

The Science of Twin Mixology

For too long, non-drinkers were relegated to sugary fruit juices or plain club sodas. At No Proof, we revolutionized cocktail engineering: crafting each recipe with a spirit-based formula and an identical zero-proof botanical twin that mirrors its mouthfeel, acidity, and aromatic depth.

Cocktail Alchemy

The 3 Pillars of Parallel Craft

1. Custom Botanical Distillates

Distilling whole herbs, roots, citrus peels, and oak wood to extract complex bitter and aromatic notes without alcohol.

2. Identical Glassware & Ice

Serving every cocktail—alcoholic or zero-proof—in the exact same crystal coupes, smoked highballs, and hand-carved clear ice blocks.

3. Zero Social Compromise

Creating an inclusive nightlife sanctuary where mindful drinkers, designated drivers, and spirit lovers celebrate side by side.
