A New Era in Charlotte Nightlife

No Proof was founded on a simple yet revolutionary truth: great nights out shouldn't require alcohol, nor should enjoying spirits isolate you from mindful friends. Located at 201 W Worthington Ave, we built an inclusive haven where everyone shares the exact same elevated cocktail experience.
