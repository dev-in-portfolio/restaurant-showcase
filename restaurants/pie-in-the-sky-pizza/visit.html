How To Find Us in Brevard Court

  • From S. Tryon Street: Walk into the historic glass-canopy Latta Arcade (next to 320 S Tryon) and exit straight into the open-air Brevard Court alleyway.
  • From S. Church Street: Enter Brevard Court directly across the street from Romare Bearden Park and walk 50 feet down the cobblestone path.
  • Stadium & Park Walking Distance: 2-minute walk from Truist Field (Charlotte Knights) and 5 minutes from Bank of America Stadium.
