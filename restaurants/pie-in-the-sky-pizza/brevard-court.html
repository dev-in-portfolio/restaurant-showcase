Historic Latta Arcade • Brevard Court Alleyway

Cobblestone Charm, Cold Craft Pours.

Brevard Court is one of Charlotte’s most unique urban treasures. Connecting Tryon Street through the glass-roofed 1914 Latta Arcade to Church Street, this historic pedestrian alley offers European cafe charm and vibrant Uptown energy.

The Charm of Brevard Court

Where historic architecture meets lively sports energy and al fresco dining.

1914 Heritage

Historic Latta Arcade

Originally constructed in 1914 for Charlotte’s cotton merchants, the glass skylight arcade leads directly to our cobblestone open-air courtyard.

Local Craft Taps

Carolina Beer Bar

We feature a rotating selection of local North Carolina craft beers on draft, including crisp lagers, juicy IPAs, and seasonal ciders.

Stadium Proximity

Game Day Hub

Located directly across Church Street from Romare Bearden Park, we are the prime pre-game stop for Charlotte Knights baseball and Panthers football.

Al Fresco Uptown Dining

Under The Court Lights

On warm Charlotte evenings, grab a high-top table under the string lights with friends. Share a fresh 18-inch Sky Master pie, a basket of crispy garlic parmesan wings, and pints of local beer while soaking in the sounds of Uptown.
