# Creative Design Brief: AJ Family Restaurant

## 1. Restaurant Identity & Intelligence
- **Name**: AJ Family Restaurant
- **Cuisine**: Comfort Country Diner & Breakfast House
- **Tagline**: Hearty Home-Cooked Meals & Warm Southern Hospitality
- **Location**: 4807 Albemarle Rd, Charlotte, NC 28205

## 2. Three Concept Directions Evaluated

### Concept A: Vintage Country Diner & Biscuit House (SELECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Bespoke Comfort Country Diner & Breakfast House aesthetics. Primary accent `#ea580c`, deep background `#18181b`.
- **Typography**: Display font `'Outfit', sans-serif` paired with `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif`.
- **Signature Feature**: Southern Breakfast Plate Customizer.
- **Header Strategy**: Brand badge + adaptive navigation drawer activating at 1024px to prevent horizontal crowding.

### Concept B: Modern Family Table (REJECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Rejected for lack of distinct regional identity.

### Concept C: Classic Southern Breakfast Joint (REJECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Rejected as too generic for AJ Family Restaurant.

## 3. Art Direction Rationale
- **Color Logic**: Accent `#ea580c` for callouts with `#18181b` background.
- **Typography Logic**: Hierarchy using `'Outfit', sans-serif` for titles and `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif` for legibility.
- **Responsive Navigation**: Adaptive breakpoint at 1024px tested across 1440px to 360px with zero overflow.
