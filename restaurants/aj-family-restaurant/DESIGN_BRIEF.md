# Design Brief: AJ Family Restaurant

## Restaurant Identity & Positioning
- **Restaurant Name**: AJ Family Restaurant
- **Cuisine & Concept**: Comfort Country Diner
- **Tagline**: Hearty Home-Cooked Meals & Warm Hospitality
- **Location**: 4807 Albemarle Rd, Charlotte, NC 28205

## Creative Art Direction
- **Named Direction**: Comfort Country Diner Bespoke Showcase
- **Typography Logic**: Display font `'Outfit', sans-serif` paired with `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif` for clean hierarchy and premium legibility.
- **Color Logic**: Primary accent `#ea580c` with deep dark backdrop `#18181b` providing strong contrast.
- **Signature Interaction**: Breakfast Plate Customizer built directly into the home experience to guide visitor discovery.
