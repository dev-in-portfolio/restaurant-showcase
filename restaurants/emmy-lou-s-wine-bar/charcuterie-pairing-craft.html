The Art of Charcuterie Pairing

A great wine needs the perfect partner. We slice paper-thin San Daniele prosciutto, hand-cut aged Dutch gouda & creamy French brie, & pair them with marcona almonds, fresh figs, & warm rosemary flatbreads.

Pairing Principles

The 3 Pillars of Charcuterie Craft

1. Premium Imported Charcuterie

Prosciutto di Parma, spicy calabrese salami, & coppa aged to perfection and sliced fresh per order.

2. Artisanal Cheese Curations

Aged raw-milk gouda, double-cream French brie, gorgonzola, & manchego selected for rich texture.

3. House-Baked Flatbreads & Jams

Warm rosemary olive oil flatbreads, scratch sourdough crostini, organic fig jam, & Spanish marcona almonds.
