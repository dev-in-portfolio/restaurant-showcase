## Original Audit Weakness

Emmy Lou's Wine Bar relies on basic social links & basic website (`emmylouswinebar.com`). Located at 2400 Park Rd in Dilworth, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its intimate sommelier wine curation, artisanal charcuterie pairing craft, relaxed Dilworth neighborhood patio lounge, and private wine tasting directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Dilworth Location:** 2400 Park Rd, Suite H, Charlotte NC 28203 (Dilworth Shopping Center in Dilworth Charlotte).
2. **Sommelier Wine Curation:** Curations of Old World & New World wines by the glass or bottle, sparkling champagnes, orange wines, and sommelier tasting flights.
3. **Artisanal Charcuterie & Small Plates:** Custom charcuterie boards with imported prosciutto, salami, aged gouda, double cream brie, marcona almonds, truffle flatbreads, & burrata crostini.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Sophisticated Editorial Serif Typography:** Sophisticated editorial serif (*Cormorant Garamond*) paired with refined body sans (*Outfit*) and cellar mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Velvet Bordeaux & Champagne Gold Palette:** High-energy sommelier wine lounge palette anchored in deep velvet bordeaux (`#4C0519`), champagne gold (`#F59E0B`), rich pinot noir red (`#881337`), warm oak mahogany (`#27120A`), and soft linen cream (`#FAF5F0`).
3. **"The Sommelier Pour & Artisanal Charcuterie Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`wine-charcuterie-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing wine flights alongside imported cheese & cured meat charcuterie boards and warm artisan flatbreads.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Elizabeth Creamery` — Warm Sweet Shop with Fraunces fonts and Strawberry Velvet/Waffle Gold.
2. `Distro Beer Hub` — Industrial Craft Beer Hall with Syne fonts and Industrial Charcoal/Hops Amber.
3. `Deli St` — Urban Plaza Midwood Bodega with Bricolage Grotesque fonts and Bodega Charcoal/Mustard Yellow.

### Divergent Choices for Emmy Lou's Wine Bar

- **Hero Composition:** Modern Dilworth Velvet Wine Bar & Charcuterie Vault split-hero layout: left side features a polished champagne gold stamp badge (`"DILWORTH ARTISANAL WINE BAR & CHARCUTERIE • 2400 PARK RD"`), sophisticated editorial serif (*Cormorant Garamond*), deep velvet bordeaux & champagne gold palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of red wine pours, a rich charcuterie board with brie & prosciutto, and grilled flatbread in front of the brick wine wall.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Emmy Lou's Vault Cards** (`emmy-vault-card`) and Dilworth wine lounge highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`emmy-header`, `wine-brand`, `bordeaux-hero-stage`, `champagne-stamp-badge`, `emmy-vault-card`, `emmy-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
