The 3 Pillars of Emmy Lou's Wine Curation

1. Boutique Family Vineyards

Sourced exclusively from low-yield, family-owned estates practicing sustainable & organic viticulture.

2. Precision Temperature Cellaring

Reds kept at a steady 58°F and whites chilled to 48°F so every varietal opens with vibrant bouquet & aroma.

3. Guided Sommelier Tasting Flights

Weekly curated 3-pour flights highlighting emerging wine regions, natural orange wines, & rare vintages.
