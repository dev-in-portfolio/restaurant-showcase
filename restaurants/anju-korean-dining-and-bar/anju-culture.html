Cultural Heritage

The Art of Anju

In Korea, drinking is never done without food. "Anju" (안주) refers to the rich spectrum of dishes specially created to complement soju, beer, and rice wine.

Pillars of Korean Drinking Food

Sensory Harmony

Bold Spicy & Umami Notes

Fermented gochujang, garlic, and kimchi create intense flavors that cut through crisp soju and cold lagers.

Sizzling & Savory Textures

Crispy Kimchi Pajeon pancakes and double-fried garlic chicken offer satisfying crunch alongside craft beverages.

Communal Hot Pots

Simmering bowls of Budae Jungol encourage lingering conversation and pour-for-pour hospitality late into the night.
