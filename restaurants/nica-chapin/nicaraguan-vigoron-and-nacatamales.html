The Great Weekend Ritual

The Craft of the Giant Nacatamal

In Nicaragua, weekends begin with a steaming Nacatamal enjoyed alongside fresh bread and hot coffee. Unlike smaller tamales found across Mesoamerica, the Nacatamal is an all-inclusive feast wrapped in fresh plantain and banana leaves.

Our masa is ground from white heirloom corn cooked with lard, garlic, bitter orange juice, and rich achiote paste. Each bundle is layered with marinated pork shoulder, seasoned rice, potato wheels, sweet peppers, olives, raisins, and aromatic spearmint leaves before simmering for four unhurried hours.

  • Weighs over one full pound per serving
  • Slow-steamed in double layers of banana leaves for distinctive aroma
  • Available fresh every weekend and by advance catering order
Granada Street Tradition

Vigor?n Granadino

Invented in 1914 under the shade of the mango trees in Granada's Central Park, Vigor?n is the ultimate interplay of textures and temperatures.

Piping hot, buttery-soft boiled yuca root forms the base. It is topped with generous chunks of crackling crisp pork skin and meat (chicharr?n de faja), and crowned with a cool, crunchy cabbage and tomato slaw tossed with sour orange juice and chile congo.

  • Served traditionally on a fresh green plantain leaf
  • Crispy double-cooked pork chicharrones
  • Tangy house-made curtido with sour orange vinaigrette
