National Cultural Patrimony

Pepi?n de Pollo Ancestral

Recognized as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Guatemala, Pepi?n dates back to the Kaqchikel Maya civilization. Its signature depth comes from patient dry-toasting on a hot clay comal.

We toast raw pumpkin seeds (pepitoria), sesame seeds (ajonjol?), cinnamon bark, dried guaque and pasa chilies, and charred corn tortillas. These are stone-ground with roasted tomatoes, tomatillos, and onions into a velvety recado that simmers with tender chicken, chayote (guisquil), and green beans.

  • Deep, smoky, nutty flavor profile unique to Guatemala
  • Served with warm handmade corn tortillas and steamed rice
  • Prepared fresh daily using imported Guatemalan spices
Highland Comfort

Hilachas de Res Chapinas

Originating from the department of Baja Verapaz, Hilachas ("threads") is one of Guatemala's most comforting family dishes.

Premium flank steak is gently simmered until it easily shreds into fine ribbons. It is then braised in a vibrant sauce of roasted tomatoes, sweet peppers, tomatillos, and mild guajillo chili, accompanied by tender potato cubes and sweet carrots.

  • Slow-braised beef ribbons that absorb the rich tomato broth
  • Naturally thickened with ground corn and vegetables
  • Paired with seasoned black beans and hand-patted corn tortillas
