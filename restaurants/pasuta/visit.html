Home Kitchen Guide

Cooking Fresh Raw Pasta at Home

Directives for preparing our take-home half-pound raw pasta boxes:

  • Boiling Water: Bring 4 quarts of water to a vigorous rolling boil. Add 1 generous tablespoon of sea salt.
  • Quick Cooking Time: Drop in fresh pasta gently: 2 to 2.5 minutes for Tagliatelle; 2.5 to 3 minutes for Bucatini and Spaghetti; 3.5 minutes for Rigatoni. Do not overcook!
  • Emulsion Secret: Always reserve 1/2 cup of starchy boiling water before straining. Toss noodles directly into your warm sauce pan with the starchy water and 1 tablespoon of butter for 30 seconds to create restaurant-grade silkiness.
