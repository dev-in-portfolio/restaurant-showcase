Bronze-Die Extrusion • Daily Production

Semolina Purity, Microscopic Sauce Ridges.

Unlike industrial commercial pasta extruded through slick Teflon dies that produce slippery, sauce-repelling noodles, Pasuta extrudes our pasta using traditional heavy bronze dies. The bronze surface creates microscopic ridges and texture that grab our silky Wafu soy-butter and bolognese sauces.

Noodle Profiles

The Four Fresh Pasta Cuts

Each shape is tailored for specific sauce viscosities and mouthfeel.

Cut 01

Bronze Bucatini

Thick hollow-center strands that draw savory butter broths, dashi emulsions, and miso sauces directly into the core with every bite.

Cut 02

Egg Tagliatelle

Silky wide flat ribbons rolled with fresh eggs and durum wheat, offering luxurious chew that cradles our fermented black bean bolognese.

Cut 03

Rigatoni Rigate

Bold ridged tubes engineered to trap substantial pieces of ground meat, wild mushrooms, and melted Parmigiano-Reggiano.

Take-Home Raw Pasta Boxes

Want to cook fresh restaurant-quality pasta in your home kitchen? Pick up half-pound boxes of raw bucatini, tagliatelle, or spaghetti at our market counter. In boiling salted water, our fresh noodles cook to perfect al dente tenderness in under three minutes.
