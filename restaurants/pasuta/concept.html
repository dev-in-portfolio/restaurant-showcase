Two Culinary Capitals, One Handcrafted Bowl.

"Pasuta" (パスタ) is how the word "pasta" is spoken across the noodle counters of Tokyo, Osaka, and Kyoto. Located inside Charlotte’s innovative Urban District Market food hall, Pasuta was created to introduce Queen City food lovers to Japan’s vibrant, decades-old culture of Wafu pasta.

The Guiding Values

The Craft Philosophy Behind Pasuta

Authentic fusion anchored in culinary reverence, scratch production, and food hall camaraderie.

Philosophy 01

Scratch Craftsmanship

We never cut corners with pre-packaged dry pasta or jarred base sauces. Every noodle is extruded in-house, and every sauce is finished à la minute in hot saute pans.

Philosophy 02

Culinary Respect

True fusion is not novelty; it is deep mutual understanding. We honor both the Italian discipline of al dente texture and the Japanese obsession with balanced umami.

Philosophy 03

Mill District Energy

Nestled among NoDa creatives and Charlotte beer enthusiasts, our open kitchen brings theater and approachable artisan food to everyday dining.

Food Hall Synergy

Urban District Market Community

Urban District Market is a dynamic gathering hub in Charlotte’s Mill District, uniting global culinary stalls, shared beer and cocktail bars, local retail, and outdoor gaming plazas. Pasuta fits perfectly into this tapestry, providing satisfying, high-energy comfort food for lunch breaks, casual dates, and weekend family outings.
