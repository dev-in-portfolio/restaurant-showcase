Japanese Wafu Pasta • Urban District Market

Tokyo Umami, Italian Pasta Craft.

Welcome to Pasuta, Charlotte’s dedicated Japanese Wafu pasta bar located inside Urban District Market at 2315 North Davidson Street. We unite traditional Italian bronze-die pasta craftsmanship with quintessential Japanese flavors: spicy mentaiko cod roe, sweet white miso butter, savory fermented black bean bolognese, and toasted shredded nori seaweed.

House Favorite

Creamy Miso Mushroom Butter

Our Miso Mushroom Butter pasta showcases earthy Japanese shimeji beech mushrooms and sweet caramelized cabbage sauteed with crispy bacon. Gently folded with sweet white Kyoto miso butter and splash of heavy cream, creating a rich sauce that clings to every twist of pasta.

Flour & Semolina

Handmade Fresh Pasta Daily

We believe great pasta requires pristine technique. Mixed from non-GMO durum semolina and pure mountain water, our noodles are cut fresh every morning to preserve the delicate spring and chew prized by both Italian nonnas and Tokyo pasta masters.
