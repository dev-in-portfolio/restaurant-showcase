Wafu Pasta Selection

Custom Extrusion, Tokyo Umami Emulsions.

Every pasta dish begins with your choice of our fresh extruded noodles: Bucatini, Spaghetti, Tagliatelle, or Rigatoni. Each sauce is cooked to order in individual saute pans to achieve silky emulsification, finished with fresh nori, shiso herbs, or Parmigiano-Reggiano.
