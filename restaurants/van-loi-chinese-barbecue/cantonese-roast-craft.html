The Art of Siu Mei Roasting

From whole air-dried ducks basted in maltose syrup to blistered crackling pork belly and open-flame char siu.

The 24-Hour Duck Process

Air-Drying, Maltose Glaze & Vertical Ovens

True Cantonese roast duck requires patience and strict temperature control. Each duck is carefully cleaned, seasoned inside with five-spice powder, star anise, ginger, and garlic, then scalded with boiling water to tighten the skin.

  • Maltose & Rice Vinegar Glaze: Basted by hand to create the signature glossy mahogany sheen and paper-crisp bite.
  • Air-Drying Stage: Hung in temperature-controlled drying racks for up to 12 hours so the skin dehydrates perfectly before roasting.
  • Vertical Roasting: Suspended over intense radiant heat in specialized ovens so the fat renders cleanly into the bottom trough.

Crackling Skin Precision

Heo Quay: The Secret to the Perfect Crunch

Our crispy roast pork belly (Heo Quay / Siu Yuk) is renowned across Charlotte for its incredible textural contrast: an ear-shattering crispy skin on top, a luscious ribbon of tender fat, and fragrant five-spice seasoned meat below.

  • Skin Pricking & Salt Crust: Thousands of micro-punctures are made in the skin before applying a coarse sea salt crust to extract moisture.
  • High-Heat Blistering: The skin puffs and blisters evenly without burning, resulting in a honeycomb crackling texture.
  • Carved with Cleaver Precision: Sliced to order into bite-sized square cubes that retain their crunch long after leaving the counter.

Core Kitchen Principles

The Four Pillars of Van Loi Roasting

1. Fresh Daily Roasts

We never serve day-old roast meats. Every duck, pork belly, and char siu strip is roasted fresh in our ovens every morning.

2. Whole Spices Only

We grind whole star anise, cinnamon bark, cloves, and Sichuan peppercorns in-house for vibrant, complex aromas.

3. Master Cleaver Craft

Our roast masters use heavy wood chopping blocks and sharp carbon steel cleavers to chop each cut cleanly through the bone.

4. Pure Natural Juices

We capture the seasoned juices rendering from inside the cavity of the roast ducks to create our signature dipping sauce.
