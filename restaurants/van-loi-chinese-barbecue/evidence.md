## Original Audit Weakness

Van Loi Chinese Barbecue is widely regarded as Charlotte's undisputed gold standard for authentic Cantonese roast meats and celebratory whole roast suckling pigs. However, its previous digital footprint was minimal and fragmented across basic web directory listings, lacking a dedicated multi-page showcase detailing its five-spice roasting craft, combination rice and noodle platters, whole duck/pig holiday pre-order guidelines, and location directions. This 6-page responsive web experience gives Van Loi an authentic, visually stunning, and high-converting digital storefront.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **The Golden Hanging Roast Window (Siu Mei):** Whole hanging Cantonese roast ducks glistening with maltose glaze, crisp pork bellies with blistered crackling skin, and caramelized red char siu pork.
2. **Combination Rice & Egg Noodle Platters:** Steaming bowls of wonton egg noodles and jasmine rice loaded with chopped roast duck, crispy pork, soy chicken, and ginger scallion sauce.
3. **Celebratory Whole Roasts & Holiday Pre-Orders:** Custom-roasted whole suckling pigs and golden roast ducks prepared for Lunar New Year, weddings, and family banquets.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Imperial Red & Honey Glaze Palette:** Deep Imperial Red (`#991b1b`), Roast Amber Gold (`#d97706`), Warm Bamboo Cream (`#fcf8f2`), Rich Dark Soy (`#18120f`), and Jade Emerald Accents (`#047857`).
2. **Roast Meat Butcher Block Visual Architecture:** Hanging display highlights, dual-meat combination cards, and custom catering order guides.
3. **Artisan Chinese Typography:** Cinzel elegant serif display headlines paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for clear roast meat weight, pricing, and timing.
