Springy Wonton Noodles

Roast Duck & Wonton Egg Noodle Soups

Our noodles are the traditional thin Cantonese egg noodles blanched quickly in boiling water and shocked in cold water for an authentic springy "al dente" bite.

  • Roast Duck Wonton Noodle Soup ($14.50): Hand-wrapped shrimp and pork wontons, carved roast duck, baby bok choy, and scallions in rich bone broth.
  • Two-Meat Dry Tossed Lo Mein ($14.99): Noodles tossed in dark oyster sauce and aromatic shallot oil, served with roast meats and a side soup.
  • Crispy Pork Noodle Soup ($13.50): Thick cubes of crispy pork belly over hot noodles with fried garlic and cilantro.
