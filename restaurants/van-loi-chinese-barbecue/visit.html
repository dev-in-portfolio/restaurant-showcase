How to Order

Butcher Counter Ordering

1. Ordering by the Pound

Point to your preferred roast in the window. Our butcher will weigh the meat, chop it fresh on the butcher block, and package it hot with dipping juices.

2. Individual Meal Platters

Choose two or three meats over jasmine rice or egg noodle soup for a fast, piping hot lunch or dinner to go.

Holiday & Banquet Pre-Orders:
