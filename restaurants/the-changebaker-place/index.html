East Charlotte · Central Avenue Open Daily 7:30 AM – 6:00 PM Next Door To Manolo's Bakery

Half coffee shop, civic living room.

The ChangeBaker Place is Manolo Betancur's community café on Central Avenue — Colombian espresso at the counter, Manolo's Bakery pastries in the case, gelato in the back, and a room built for neighbors, students, and organizers who need somewhere to sit and get to work.

Reel 02 · The Corner

A former hair salon, reborn as a café.

Suite B at 4405 Central used to be a hair salon. Now the espresso machine hums where the dryers were, the pastry case faces the same Central Avenue light, and Manolo's Bakery — open since long before — stands a few steps away. East Charlotte's corridor keeps growing around it: the family's next concepts, Artisen Gelato and Higher Grounds, are slated for the Eastland Yards build-out.
