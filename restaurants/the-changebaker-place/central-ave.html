Reel 06 · Central Avenue

One block of Central, told through coffee.

The ChangeBaker Place isn't a standalone shop — it's the newest stop on a family footprint that East Charlotte already navigates by. Here's the corridor as the café sits in it.

Stylized concept map — not to scale. Distances and addresses verified against public listings.

  1. 01

    Manolo's Bakery

    The institution that started it: Latin pastries and breads on Central Ave, nationally noticed in November 2025 when the neighborhood rallied around it. Its ovens supply the café's pastry case next door.

  2. 02

    The ChangeBaker Place — Suite B

    March 2026: a former hair salon becomes a café with Colombian espresso, gelato, sandwiches, laptop tables, and reservable meeting rooms tied to By Immigrant Hands.

  3. 03

    Eastland Yards — the next chapter

    Two more Betancur concepts, Artisen Gelato and Higher Grounds, were approved as early tenants for the city's Eastland Yards retail build-out — extending the family's footprint beyond Central Avenue.
