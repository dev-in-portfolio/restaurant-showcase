Reel 03 · The Counter

Four cases, one counter.

The ChangeBaker Place keeps its menu the way the room keeps its promises: Colombian coffee at the espresso bar, Manolo's Bakery pastry in the case, gelato in the back, and sandwiches for the tables that stay all afternoon. The cases rotate — the counter staff will tell you what's in them today.
