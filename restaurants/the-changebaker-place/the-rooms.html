Reel 04 · The Rooms

Built for the people who stay.

The ChangeBaker Place was laid out for working, studying, and gathering: cozy seating, laptop-friendly tables, and small meeting rooms that local groups can reserve. Part café, part neighborhood war room — caffeine and community work under one roof.

The Open Room

Bar seating along the counter, comfortable chairs, and a large communal dining table — the one regulars describe as feeling like a grandmother's house. Open all day, no booking needed, laptop welcome.

The Meeting Rooms

Small rooms set aside for groups — study circles, neighborhood committees, nonprofit planning, the kinds of meetings that outgrow a coffee table. Local groups reserve them through the café.

Reserve By Email

The Gathering Space

The room doubles as home base for the bakery's outreach and nonprofit work with By Immigrant Hands, and it hosts community gatherings alongside the daily coffee service.

How reservations work

  • Step one — write to info@thechangebaker.com with your group and date.
  • Step two — the café gets back to you and confirms the room.
  • Good to know — the open room and counter never need a reservation; walk in any day from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM.
  • While you meet — the counter runs espresso, pastries, gelato, and sandwiches the whole time.

Rates, room capacities, and availability are confirmed directly by the café when you inquire.
