Taiwanese Specialties & Handcrafted Noodle Soups

Slow-simmered bone broths and authentic Taiwanese market staples.

Taiwanese Braised Beef Noodle Soup (Niu Rou Mian) $16.95

Chewy wheat noodles in rich aromatic beef bone broth simmered with star anise, cassia bark, ginger, rock sugar, and aged soy, topped with tender braised beef shank chunks, baby bok choy, and scallions.

Three Cup Chicken (San Bei Ji) $17.50

Boneless chicken simmered in a piping hot clay pot with equal measures of toasted sesame oil, premium soy sauce, and Shaoxing rice wine, finished with thick ginger slices, whole garlic cloves, and fresh holy basil.

Taiwanese Crispy Pork Chop over Rice $15.50

Five-spice marinated bone-in pork loin cutlet fried extra crispy, served over seasoned jasmine rice with braised minced pork sauce (lu rou), hard-boiled soy egg, and pickled mustard greens.

Sichuan Dan Dan Noodles $12.95

Handcrafted noodles tossed in a rich roasted sesame and peanut paste with spiced minced pork, chili oil, scallions, sui mi ya cai (pickled greens), and crushed roasted peanuts.

Beef Tendon & Shank Combination Noodle Soup $18.50

Slow-braised melt-in-your-mouth beef tendon paired with tender shank slices in our signature twelve-hour five-spice beef broth with fresh greens and scallions.

Taiwanese Minced Pork Rice (Lu Rou Fan) $13.95

Slow-cooked pork belly cubes braised in sweet soy, five spice, fried shallots, and rice wine over steaming jasmine rice with braised egg and pickled radish.

Master Wok Poultry, Beef & Seafood

Wok-tossed over high heat, served with steamed Jasmine Rice.

Crispy General Tso's Chicken $15.95

Crispy golden battered chicken morsels glazed in a savory-sweet garlic ginger sauce with whole roasted red chili pods and fresh steamed broccoli florets.

Mongolian Flank Steak $17.95

Tender marinated flank steak slices wok-charred with sweet yellow onions, whole garlic, and fresh green scallions in a rich hoisin dark soy glaze.

Authentic Szechuan Mapo Tofu $14.95

Silken bean curd and minced beef simmered in spicy fermented broad bean paste (doubanjiang) dusted with tongue-tingling roasted Sichuan peppercorn powder.

Crispy Honey Walnut Shrimp $18.95

Jumbo lightly battered shrimp coated in a delicate sweet honey cream glaze, topped with house candied walnuts and fresh steamed broccoli florets.

Kung Pao Chicken $15.95

Diced chicken breast wok-fired with roasted whole peanuts, chili peppers, zucchini, and celery in a savory, tangy Sichuan chili sauce.

Chinese Eggplant in Spicy Garlic Sauce $14.50

Tender purple Chinese eggplant braised in sweet and tangy garlic chili sauce with wood ear mushrooms and chopped scallions.

Handmade Dumplings, Dim Sum & Appetizers

Rolled, pleated, and filled by hand daily in our kitchen.

Handmade Pork & Napa Cabbage Dumplings (8 Pieces) $10.50

Steamed or pan-fried dumplings filled with seasoned pork and crisp napa cabbage, served with aged Zhenjiang black vinegar and house chili oil.

Steamed Pork Xiao Long Bao (6 Pieces) $11.50

Delicate pleated flour pouches filled with hot savory pork broth and seasoned meat, served with fresh ginger shreds and black vinegar.

Crispy Scallion Pancake (Cong You Bing) $7.50

Flaky layered pan-fried flatbread studded with fresh chopped green scallions, served with sweet garlic dipping sauce.

Traditional Hot & Sour Soup $4.50 / $8.95

Classic northern broth with wood ear mushrooms, silken tofu, bamboo shoots, egg ribbon, aromatic white pepper, and aged black vinegar.
