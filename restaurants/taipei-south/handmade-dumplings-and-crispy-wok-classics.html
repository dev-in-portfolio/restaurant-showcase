The Craft of Handmade Jiaozi

Why handmade dumplings stand far above frozen factory imitations.

Skill 01

Hand-Rolled Wrappers

Our dough is rolled thin at the edges while maintaining a thicker center, ensuring the dumpling holds its shape and locks in rich meat juices during steaming.

Skill 02

Seasoned Pork & Napa

Fresh ground pork is whipped with minced ginger, scallions, sesame oil, and crunchy napa cabbage, creating a delicate, juicy filling packed with natural flavor.

Skill 03

Zhenjiang Black Vinegar

Served with authentic aged Zhenjiang aromatic black vinegar and chili crisp, cutting through the savory richness with malty acidity and subtle sweetness.

Wok Mastery

Crisp Glaze & Fresh Broccoli

Our wok stations reach intense heat, allowing our chefs to seal battered chicken morsels in seconds while keeping the chicken tender and juicy inside. Tossed with fiery roasted red chili peppers and garlic sauce, this iconic dish is a beloved staple on our South Charlotte menu.
