Taiwanese National Treasure • Niu Rou Mian

Twelve-Hour Bone Stock, Tender Braised Beef Shank.

In Taiwan, Beef Noodle Soup is an obsession—the national dish celebrated in international festivals and night markets from Taipei to Kaohsiung. At Taipei South, our broth simmers around the clock with roasted beef marrow bones, aged Shaoxing wine, star anise, ginger, and fermented bean paste, delivering deep soul-warming richness to Charlotte noodle lovers.

The Four Pillars of Authentic Niu Rou Mian

What gives our Taiwanese broth its signature depth and comforting aroma.

Pillar 01

Twelve-Hour Simmer

Roasted beef marrow bones simmer overnight with charred ginger, scallion bundles, star anise, cassia bark, and Sichuan peppercorns to extract pure collagen and rich umami.

Pillar 02

Marbled Beef Shank

We select thick center-cut beef shank laced with natural sinew. Slow braising in rock sugar and dark soy transforms the tough connective tissue into succulent, melt-in-your-mouth tenderness.

Pillar 03

Chewy Wheat Noodles

Our wide wheat noodles are cooked to an al dente chew, holding the hot five-spice broth and topped with crisp baby bok choy and sharp chopped scallions.

Taiwanese Night Market Soul

Three Cup Chicken & Pork Chops

Beyond our beef noodle soup, our menu features beloved Taiwanese comforting classics: San Bei Ji (Three Cup Chicken) bubbling hot in heavy clay pots with caramelized garlic and Thai basil, and our crispy five-spice pork chops served over jasmine rice with braised minced pork gravy.
