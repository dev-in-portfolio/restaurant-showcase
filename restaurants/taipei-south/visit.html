Fast & Fresh Lunch

Weekday Lunch Specials

Directives for our popular South Charlotte midday lunch service:

  • Schedule: Served Monday through Friday from 11:30 AM to 3:00 PM.
  • Complete Meals: Each lunch combination is served with your choice of steamed jasmine rice or pork fried rice, plus an egg roll, crab rangoon, or cup of soup (Hot & Sour or Egg Drop).
  • Business Quickness: Prepared within 10 to 15 minutes of ordering to accommodate busy work schedules.
Celebration & Feasts

Family & Corporate Banquets

Directives for hosting group gatherings and family feasts:

  • Large Round Tables: Our dining hall features circular tables with rotating lazy susans, seating parties of 8 to 12 guests comfortably.
  • Family-Style Courses: We recommend selecting 4 to 8 shared dishes—combining our Taiwanese beef noodle soup, handmade dumplings, clay pot chicken, and whole steamed fish.
  • Reservations: Recommended for parties of 6 or more during Friday and Saturday dinner service. Call (704) 542-8038 to book.
