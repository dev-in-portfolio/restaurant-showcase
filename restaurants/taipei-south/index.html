Taiwanese Beef Noodles, Master Wok Classics.

Welcome to Taipei South, South Charlotte’s premier full-service Taiwanese and regional Chinese sit-down dining landmark. Located at 10106 Johnston Road in the McMullen Creek corridor, we prepare authentic Taiwanese Beef Noodle Soup (Niu Rou Mian) simmered for twelve hours with five-spice aromatics, handmade pork jiaozi dumplings, and sizzling wok-fired culinary favorites.

Authentic Taiwanese & Chinese Heritage

The Broth

Slow-Braised Niu Rou Mian

Simmering beef bone broth for twelve hours with star anise, cassia bark, ginger, rock sugar, and aged soy, ladled over chewy wheat noodles and tender beef shank.

Wok Classic

Crispy General Tso's Chicken

Our General Tso's chicken begins with tender chicken thighs cut into generous bite-sized morsels, tossed in light starch, and wok-crisped to golden perfection. Glazed in a deeply balanced sweet-spicy ginger garlic sauce studded with roasted whole red chili peppers and served over steamed jasmine rice with crisp broccoli florets.
