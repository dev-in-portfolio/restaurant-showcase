South Charlotte Institution • 10106 Johnston Road

Taiwanese Tradition, South Charlotte Community.

Nestled in South Charlotte’s McMullen Creek corridor along Johnston Road, Taipei South was created to provide a true sit-down culinary haven for Taiwanese street delicacies and regional Chinese banquet specialties. In an era dominated by rushed takeout counters, we offer spacious dining room tables, traditional lazy susans, and leisurely hospitality.

Core Philosophy

The Values That Guide Taipei South

Commitment to authentic recipes and generous Chinese dining room hospitality.

Value 01

Authentic Regional Ingredients

We source premium Sichuan peppercorns, aged Shaoxing wine, fermented spicy bean paste, and authentic Taiwanese five-spice to ensure our flavors mirror the markets of Taipei.

Value 02

Family Banquet Culture

Our dining room is arranged for family gatherings, business banquets, and celebratory feasts, complete with large round tables and sharing platters.

Value 03

Generous Neighborhood Care

Welcoming South Charlotte neighbors, Ballantyne professionals, and visiting international guests with swift lunch service and warm evening dining.

Full-Service Hospitality

Dining Room, Full Bar & Banquets

Whether enjoying a bowl of Taiwanese beef noodles at our full-service bar with a cold Tsingtao beer or gathering ten friends around a round banquet table for Peking duck and Three Cup Chicken, Taipei South is designed for comfort and celebration.
