Sizzling Granite Stone Bowls

The Magic of Dolsot Bibimbap

Dolsot translates to 'stone pot'. Heated over open flames to 450°F, our granite bowls sizzle sesame oil against rice, creating a golden, crunchy rice layer known as Nurungji.
