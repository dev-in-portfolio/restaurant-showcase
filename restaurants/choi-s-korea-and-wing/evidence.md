## Original Audit Weakness

Choi’s Korea & Wing relies on basic ordering templates (`choiskoreawing.com`, `chownow.com`). Located at 808 E Arrowood Rd, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its double-fry Korean wing glazing technique, hot stone dolsot sizzle, dedicated vegan Korean menu, and family kitchen roots.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Arrowood Road Charlotte Location:** 808 E Arrowood Rd, Charlotte NC 28217 (Arrowood / South Charlotte).
2. **Double-Fried Korean Wings & Glazes:** Soy Garlic glazed wings, Sweet & Spicy Yangnyeom wings, Honey Butter wings, and crisp pickled daikon radish.
3. **Traditional Korean Kitchen & Vegan Menu:** Sizzling beef bulgogi, dolsot bibimbap, jajangmyun black bean noodles, kimchi jjigae, and dedicated vegan/gluten-free Korean dishes.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Bold Modern Korean Asian Typography:** Heavy modern sans display (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and kitchen technical mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Hanok Mahogany & Crimson Chili Palette:** Vibrant Korean palette anchored in hanok mahogany (`#261516`), crimson chili red (`#8B191B`), sesame gold (`#D89028`), toasted cream (`#FAF6F0`), and crisp pickled white (`#FFFFFF`).
3. **"The Double-Fried Wing Glaze & Hot Dolsot Stone Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`korean-wing-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing extra-crispy double-fried wings alongside hot stone bowl bibimbap.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `CHNO Coffee Co.` — Modern chemical caffeine lab with Space Grotesk/Outfit fonts.
2. `Chez Marie Pâtisserie` — Parisian high-fashion pâtisserie with Cormorant Garamond/Outfit fonts.
3. `Cheat’s Cheesesteaks` — High-octane Philly cheesesteak joint with Outfit/Plus Jakarta Sans fonts.

### Divergent Choices for Choi’s Korea & Wing

- **Hero Composition:** Double-Fried Korean Glaze & Sizzling Dolsot Vault split-hero layout: left side features a crimson chili stamp badge (`"DOUBLE-FRIED KOREAN WINGS & DOLSOT • ARROWOOD RD"`), bold modern sans display typography (*Plus Jakarta Sans*), hanok mahogany & crimson chili palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of soy garlic & yangnyeom glazed wings with pickled radish.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Dolsot Vault Cards** (`dolsot-vault-card`) and **Korean Wing Glaze Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`choi-header`, `korea-brand`, `glaze-hero-stage`, `chili-stamp-badge`, `dolsot-vault-card`, `choi-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
