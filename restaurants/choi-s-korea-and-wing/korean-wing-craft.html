Double-Frying Method

Double-Fried Glazed Wing Science

Unlike American wings with heavy batter, authentic Korean fried chicken relies on potato starch and two separate frying stages to achieve a paper-thin, glass-like crunch that seals in juice.

Explore Wing Menu
Craft Steps

The 3 Steps to Korean Wing Perfection

1. The Initial Rend Fry

Wings are lightly dusted in potato starch and fried at a low temperature to render out fat and cook the meat thoroughly.

2. The High-Heat Flash Fry

After resting, wings are flash-fried at high heat to transform moisture into an ultra-thin, crackling shell.

3. Hand-Brushed Glaze

Wings are individually brushed with house-made Soy Garlic or Gochujang Yangnyeom glaze so every bite is evenly coated.
