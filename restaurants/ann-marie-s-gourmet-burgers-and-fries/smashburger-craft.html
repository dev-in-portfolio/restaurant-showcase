Griddle Precision

The Art of the Smash

We smash fresh 100% USDA beef balls on a screaming hot 500°F steel griddle. The intense pressure creates maximum surface contact for lacy, caramelized crispy crusts.

Anatomy of Flavor

Four Pillars of a Great Smash

1. High Heat Searing

Smashed on 500°F flat-tops to induce rapid Maillard reaction, forming deep savory caramelized crusts.

2. Lacy Crispy Edges

The thin outer edges sear into ultra-crispy beef lace while the interior stays tender and juicy.

3. Gooey Melted Cheese

Real American or sharp cheddar cheese draped over hot patties right on the griddle to melt instantly.

4. Butter-Toasted Brioche

Rich bakery brioche buns toasted in clarification butter to hold the juices and sauce without getting soggy.
