🍝 Bronze-Die Extrusions & Hardwood Embers

The Alchemy of Fire & Dough

At Laurel Park, our open kitchen merges ancient Mediterranean pasta techniques with the intense heat of American hardwood hearths. Every morning, our chefs knead durum semolina pasta dough, while our wood grills burn seasoned white oak for rich char and tenderness.

Culinary Craftsmanship

The 3 Pillars of Laurel Park Kitchen Craft

1. Daily In-House Extrusion

Passing fresh semolina dough through custom Italian bronze dies creates a rough, porous pasta texture that captures every drop of rich emulsion.

2. Oak Wood Hearth Grilling

Searing 35-day dry-aged steaks and Atlantic sea bass over live hardwood coals seals in natural juices while imparting clean smokiness.

3. Scratch Stock Reductions

Simmering 48-hour roasted bone reductions, fresh garden herbs, and Parmigiano rinds into unctuous glazes with deep complexity.
