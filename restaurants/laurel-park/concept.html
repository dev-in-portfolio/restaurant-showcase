🏛 The Laurel Park Story

Approachable Elegance.

Laurel Park was conceived to fill a vital niche in SouthPark dining: offering the rigorous culinary standards of high-end metropolitan dining within a warm, lively, and unpretentious neighborhood bistro setting. Located at 4201 Congress St, we bridge Mediterranean soul with American wood hearth fire.

The Values of Laurel Park

Warm Hospitality

Creating an energetic, welcoming sanctuary where casual weeknight dinners and celebratory milestones receive equally meticulous service.

Scratch Honesty

Rejecting shortcuts across every station—from fresh daily pasta extrusion to long-simmered sauces, house syrups, and wood-fired meats.

SouthPark Community Hub

Serving as SouthPark's go-to gathering space for power lunches, lively veranda cocktail hours, family brunches, and private banquets.
