Culinary Principles

The Pillars of Authentic Taste

We source authentic Mexican regional ingredients to preserve genuine street flavors.

Nixtamal Masa

Pressed on Demand

We never use packaged store-bought tortillas. Every corn tortilla is hand-pressed from fresh masa directly onto hot comales right before serving.

Artisanal Dairy

Queso Oaxaca Real

Imported Mexican string cheese (quesillo) that melts into stretchy ribbons inside our quesadillas fritas and giant tortas.

Rare Delicacies

Huitlacoche & Flor

Authentic Mexican delicacies: fresh flor de calabaza (squash blossoms) and savory huitlacoche (corn truffle) sautéed with wild epazote.

Hidden Inside Rodriguez Supermarket

An Authentic Market Taqueria

Just like the bustling market stalls of Mexico City's Mercado de San Juan or La Merced, our taqueria counter operates inside the Rodriguez Supermarket on Eastway Drive. Pull up a stool, smell the sizzling comal, and enjoy tacos made fresh in front of your eyes.
