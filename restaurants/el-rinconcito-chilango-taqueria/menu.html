Tacos al Comal & Cazo

Served on double hand-pressed fresh corn tortillas with cilantro, onions, lime, and house salsa.

Taco de Suadero (CDMX Classic) $3.50

Slow-simmered beef rose meat in traditional copper cazo, chopped and seared crisp on the comal. Juicy and rich.

Taco Al Pastor de Trompo $3.25

Achiote and guajillo marinated pork sliced hot from the vertical rotisserie spit with fresh grilled pineapple.

Taco Campechano $3.75

The Mexico City street favorite: savory suadero beef, spiced longaniza pork sausage, and crispy chicharrón cracklings.

Taco de Tripa Dorada $3.75

Slow-cooked beef tripe flash-crisped on the flat-top comal until golden, shatteringly crisp, and flavorful.

Quesabirrias con Consomé (3 Tacos) $13.50

Braised beef chuck folded in chili-dipped corn tortillas with melted Oaxaca cheese, cilantro, onions, and hot beef dipping broth.

Taco de Lengua o Cabeza $3.75

Steamed tender beef tongue or cheek meat, seasoned lightly with sea salt and garlic. Incredibly soft.

Giant Street Tortas & Pambazos

Served on warm toasted Telera bread with refried beans, avocado, and jalapeños.

Torta Cubana Chilanga $14.50

The monster street sandwich: breaded beef milanesa, smoked ham, fried frankfurters, fried egg, queso blanco, avocado, beans, and pickled peppers.

Torta de Milanesa de Res o Pollo $12.50

Crispy golden breaded steak or chicken cutlet with melted Oaxaca cheese, avocado, fresh tomato, and chipotle mayo.

Pambazo Tradicional al Guajillo $11.50

Telera roll dipped in red guajillo chili sauce and griddled crisp, stuffed with spicy chorizo and potatoes, shredded lettuce, crema, and cotija cheese.

Torta de Pastor con Queso $12.00

Trompo-carved al pastor pork, melted Oaxaca cheese, grilled pineapple, beans, and avocado on warm bread.

Quesadilla Frita de Flor de Calabaza $5.00

Quesadilla Frita de Huitlacoche $5.50
