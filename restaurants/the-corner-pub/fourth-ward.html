The Neighborhood Board

The pub that belongs to its corner.

Fourth Ward is Uptown Charlotte's historic residential quarter — porches and parks a few blocks from the towers — and The Corner Pub is its neighborhood bar: city vibes, neighborly feel, and an established crowd of regulars who don't need a menu to order.

  1. 7th & Graham — the address as identity

    The pub sits right on the intersection, which is why the name works. You don't give directions to The Corner Pub; you point at the corner.

  2. Blocks from both ballparks

    Bank of America Stadium and the Knights' ballpark are within blocks — the pub absorbs every home game's pre- and post-game crowd.

  3. Skyline in the windows

    Uptown's towers rise around the low historic ward — the view from the bar and the outdoor seats is the city itself.

  4. The regulars' economy

    A 4.5-star Google rating across roughly 460 reviews, with bartenders praised by name — the kind of staff continuity that keeps a corner pub alive.

Make a Fourth Ward night of it

The corner works as the anchor: start on the pub's outdoor seats when the weather's right, walk to an Uptown show or a game, and close the loop back at the corner — the bar runs until 2 AM on late nights.

Nearby, all within a short walk: Fourth Ward's historic streets and pocket parks, Old Settlers' Cemetery's quiet square two blocks south, and the restaurants of the 7th Street corridor.
