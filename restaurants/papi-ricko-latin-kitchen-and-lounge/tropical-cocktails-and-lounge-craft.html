🍹 Agave Spirits & Nightlife Culture

Craft Agave, Late-Night Vibras.

Step from the sunny covered patio into our high-energy cantina lounge. We shake fresh tropical agave cocktails, pour premium tequilas and smoky mezcals, and turn up the energy every weekend with live DJ sets, VIP bottle service, and late-night street food.

The 3 Pillars of Papi Ricko Nightlife

1. 100% Agave & Mezcal Focus

Highlighting artisanal blanco, reposado, and añejo tequilas paired with smoky Oaxacan mezcals for depth and nuance.

2. Scratch Fruit Reductions

Brewing fresh hibiscus tea, pressing passionfruit and guava purées, and squeezing fresh limes for all house cocktails.

3. VIP Table & DJ Nights

Hosting weekend "Papi Fridays", Latin Urban DJ sets, and VIP bottle service sections on Central Avenue.
