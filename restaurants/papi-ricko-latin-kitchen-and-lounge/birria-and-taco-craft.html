🌮 Slow-Simmered Jalisco Tradition

The Craft of Quesabirria

At Papi Ricko, our birria is a multi-step labor of love. We slow-braise premium beef chuck and shank for 8 hours with toasted dried guajillo and ancho chilis, aromatic Mexican spices, and fresh herbs until the meat shreds with a fork, producing a concentrated, flavor-packed dipping broth.

Culinary Principles

The 3 Pillars of Papi Ricko Birria Mastery

1. 8-Hour Slow-Braise

Simmering whole cuts of beef in a rich adobo of guajillo, ancho, cumin, Mexican cinnamon, and garlic until unbelievably tender.

2. Chili-Dipped Tortillas

Dipping double corn tortillas into the seasoned spiced broth before searing them on the chrome flat top with creamy Chihuahua cheese.

3. Rich Dipping Consomé

Serving every order with a bowl of piping hot, fat-kissed broth garnished with fresh cilantro and finely diced sweet white onion.
