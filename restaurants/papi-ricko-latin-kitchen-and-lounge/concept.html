🏛 The Tacos Rick-O Evolution

Born on Wheels, Reborn in Midwood.

Chef Ricky Ortiz gained cult acclaim across Charlotte with his renowned *Tacos Rick-O* food truck. With Papi Ricko Latin Kitchen & Lounge at 1226 Central Ave, he has brought his signature birria, street tacos, and infectious hospitality to a permanent, high-octane cantina and nightlife lounge.

The Values of Papi Ricko

Authentic Street Craft

Staying true to traditional Latin slow-braising, scratch adobo pastes, and hand-pressed corn tortillas for uncompromising flavor.

Infusing every service with vibrant Latin music, friendly smiles, and an electric atmosphere where everyone feels like family.

Anchor of Central Avenue nightlife, collaborating with local DJs, hosting vibrant themed parties, and serving late-night street food.
