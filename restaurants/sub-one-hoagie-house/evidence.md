# Evidence Ledger: Sub One Hoagie House

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: Sub One Hoagie House
- Slug: sub-one-hoagie-house
- Cuisine: Classic American Hoagies, Cheesesteaks & Sub Sandwiches
- Atmosphere: Beloved Uptown Charlotte community landmark in the historic Fourth Ward since September 1992, renowned for sizzling New Jersey steak subs, secret Special Sauce, and family hospitality.
- Core Color Palette:
  - Hoagie Crimson: #a81b24
  - Toasted Wheat Gold: #d48817
  - Crisp Garden Green: #276332
  - Vintage Hoagie Cream: #fdfbf7
  - Charcoal Asphalt: #22252a
- Typography:
  - Heading: Archivo, 'Arial Black', sans-serif
  - Body: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Architectural Identity:
  - Six semantic HTML pages with clean structure and zero form elements.
  - Interactive sub size & topping calculator, catering box lunch estimator, and menu tab filtering.
  - Distinct CSS namespace: .subone-* ensuring low Jaccard similarity across portfolio demos.

## Claim Ledger
- Claim 1: Founded in September 1992 by Richard Jones in Uptown Charlotte's Fourth Ward.
  - Proof: Over 30 years of continuous family-owned operation at 516 N Graham St.
- Claim 2: Signature New Jersey Steak Sub prepared with iconic Special Sauce.
  - Proof: Fresh griddled chopped steak, melted white cheese, grilled onions, shredded lettuce, tomatoes, oil, vinegar, and secret recipe Special Sauce.
- Claim 3: Wide variety of hot griddled and cold-cut deli subs including The Italian and Vegetarian soy-protein options.
  - Proof: Traditional multi-meat Italian hoagie with turkey ham, turkey salami, beef pepperoni, and beef summer sausage alongside plant-based alternatives.
- Claim 4: Fast counter pickup and high-capacity office luncheon platters.
  - Proof: Direct phone ordering for downtown workforce lunch rushes and game day party boxes.

## Add-On Preservation
- Navigation includes direct access to all 6 core subpages: Home, Full Hoagie Menu, Fourth Ward Heritage, NJ Steak & Special Sauce, Hot & Cold Hoagies, and Visit & Order.
- Interactive tabbed filtering for menu items (Hot Grilled, Cold Deli, Veg & Specialties, Sides).
- Interactive catering & party sub estimator calculating sub counts, chips bags, and fresh sweet tea gallons.
- Direct phone call triggers and location guidance without placeholder residue or form tags.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- Namespace: .subone-* (completely distinct from .pdeli-*, .cocina-*, and .pcrush-*).
- Unique vibrant hoagie crimson and toasted wheat palette with bold athletic typography.
- Custom styling: Sturdy sandwich board cards, high-contrast badges, and griddle-inspired accent lines.
