Transit & Parking

Arriving at SWIRL Dessert Bar

Visiting our South End confectionery parlor is convenient and accessible:

  • Customer Surface Parking: Free customer parking spaces are available directly in our front surface lot facing South Boulevard.
  • Neighborhood Street Parking: Additional free street parking can be found along neighboring cross streets throughout South End.
  • Lynx Light Rail: Easily reachable from the New Bern and East/West Boulevard stations along the Blue Line.
Confectionery Guidance

Parlor Seating & Celebration Orders

Tips for visiting and custom pre-orders:

  • Counter Flow: Step up to the glass customizer bar, select your components from the menu board, and watch your cupcakes piped live.
  • Late-Night Crowd: Peak evening rush occurs between 7:30 PM and 9:30 PM after South End dinner hours. Counter seating is first come, first served.
  • Giant Cookie Cakes: 10-inch cookie cakes and multi-dozen party packs can be pre-ordered online or by phone for celebrations.
