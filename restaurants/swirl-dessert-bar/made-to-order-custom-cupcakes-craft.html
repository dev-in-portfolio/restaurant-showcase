The Made-to-Order Confectionery Revolution

Say Goodbye To Stale Display Cases.

Traditional bakeries bake cupcakes in the early morning and let them sit behind dry refrigerated glass for hours or even days. At SWIRL Dessert Bar, we reinvented the cupcake from the bottom up. We keep warm, moist cake bases in small batches, coring them live, piping whipped scratch buttercreams to order, and garnishing them with premium toppings right before your eyes.

The Four Steps to Your Signature SWIRL

Over 10,000 possible flavor combinations assembled in seconds.

Phase 01

Small-Batch Cake Bases

Baking around the clock ensures our bases—Madagascar vanilla, rich chocolate, buttermilk red velvet, or funfetti—are tender, springy, and never refrigerated.

Phase 02

Injected Core Fillings

Every cupcake is precision-cored and filled with your choice of warm dark chocolate fudge, marshmallow fluff, sweet tart raspberry compote, or sea salt caramel.

Phase 03

Live Buttercream Piping

Our decorators pipe fluffy swirls of real-butter frosting to order—cream cheese, peanut butter, Oreo crumb, or classic vanilla—guaranteeing peak velvet texture.

Dessert as an Interactive Art Form

Visiting SWIRL is an experience designed for all ages. Watch our confectioners swirl your frosting, drizzle warm sauces, and tumble crumbles over your creations.
