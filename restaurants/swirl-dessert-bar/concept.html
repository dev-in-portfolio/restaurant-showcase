Born in South End • 2822 South Boulevard

Culinary Theater, Sweet Memories.

SWIRL Dessert Bar was born out of a simple realization: the traditional bakery model was outdated. Cupcakes should be moist, freshly iced, and tailored to the exact tastes of the person eating them. By combining interactive counter customization with high-quality ingredients and a vibrant late-night parlor atmosphere, SWIRL established a beloved South End institution where dessert is an unforgettable experience.

Core Values

The SWIRL Philosophy

How we approach confectionery craft and neighborhood hospitality.

Value 01

Freshness Above All

We never compromise on texture. By piping scratch buttercreams and injecting cores live to order, we guarantee every bite is soft, light, and delicious.

Value 02

Interactive Personalization

Dessert should reflect individual cravings. With dozens of cake, core, frosting, and topping permutations, guests have total freedom to express their creativity.

Value 03

Late-Night Community

South End comes alive at night. We remain open until 10:00 PM on weeknights and 11:00 PM on weekends, offering a welcoming evening dessert lounge.

South End Energy

On South Boulevard

Situated in the heart of South End, SWIRL Dessert Bar is just blocks from local breweries, restaurants, and apartments. Drop in after dinner for a treat, meet friends for a milkshake date, or celebrate milestones with our giant cookie cakes.
