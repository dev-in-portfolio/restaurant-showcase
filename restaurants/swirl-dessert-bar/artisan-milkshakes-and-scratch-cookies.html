The Hallmarks of Our Loaded Menu

Why our shakes and cookies stand out in Charlotte's dessert scene.

Shake Craft 01

Real Ice Cream & Purees

We never use premixed syrup bases. Our shakes start with rich ice cream, folded with real fruit compotes, fresh espresso, and authentic bakery items.

Bakery Craft 02

Jumbo Cookie Dough Portions

Our cookies weigh in generously with crisp, caramelized butter rims and luscious, under-baked centers that stay soft and gooey hours after baking.

Confection Craft 03

Custom Cookie Cakes

Forget boring grocery store cakes. Our 10-inch scratch chocolate chip cookie cakes are piped with custom buttercream designs for birthdays and celebrations.

Evening Pairing

The Ultimate Cookie & Shake Combo

Pair a warm salted chocolate chip cookie fresh from the oven with a hand-spun "Tommy" cereal milk shake or a "Made to Last" cold brew espresso shake. Whether dining in our parlor lounge or walking the Rail Trail corridor, it is the quintessential South End dessert ritual.
