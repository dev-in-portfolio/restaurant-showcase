The Science of the Jumbo Wing

At Picasso's Sports Café, wing perfection is non-negotiable. We source fresh, never-frozen jumbo chicken wings, flash-fry them at 375°F to achieve an ultra-crisp exterior that seals in natural juices, and spin each batch to order in over ten signature scratch sauces.

Wing Standards

The 3 Pillars of Picasso’s Wing Mastery

1. Fresh, Never-Frozen Size

Insisting on meaty, plump jumbo wings that stay tender and juicy rather than shrinking or drying out under high frying heat.

2. 10+ Scratch-Blended Sauces

Simmering real aged cayenne peppers, pure honey, roasted garlic, tropical mango habanero purée, and Jamaican jerk spices in-house daily.

3. Scratch Bleu Cheese & Celery

Accompanied by house-whipped chunky Danish bleu cheese dressing, creamy buttermilk ranch, and ice-cold fresh celery and carrots.
