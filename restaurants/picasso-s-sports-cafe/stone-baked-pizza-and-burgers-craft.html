🍕 550°F Stone Deck Baking & Angus Charbroil

Stone-Deck Crust, Angus Beef.

Great sports food goes beyond wings. We ferment our pizza dough daily, stretch it by hand, and bake our 16-inch specialty pies on blistering stone decks. Pair that with our juicy half-pound Angus steakhouse burgers charbroiled to order, and your game day feast is complete.

The 3 Pillars of Picasso’s Kitchen Craft

1. Daily Fermented Pizza Dough

Mixing flour, yeast, and olive oil for a slow 24-hour fermentation that produces an airy, chewy, crisp crust under high deck heat.

2. 100% Whole Milk Mozzarella

Shredding premium whole milk mozzarella cheese that melts into creamy, golden-flecked pools without burning.

3. Half-Pound Fresh Angus Beef

Pressing fresh, never-frozen Angus chuck into thick half-pound patties seasoned with our signature steakhouse spice blend.
