Gyros & Souvlaki Spit-Roasted & Flame-Grilled

Traditional Pork Gyro Pita #1 House Specialty

Vertical spit-roasted pork shoulder shaved crisp into char-grilled pita with red onion, ripe tomatoes, fresh fries, & garlic tzatziki.

Flame-Grilled Chicken Souvlaki Skewer Wrap Popular

Oregano olive oil marinated chicken breast skewer char-broiled over flame, wrapped in pita with house tzatziki & greens.

Rosemary Garlic Lamb Souvlaki Platter Premium Skewer

Tender leg of lamb skewers grilled over open flame, served with lemon oregano potatoes, Greek village salad, & whipped feta.

Crispy Falafel Tahini Gyro Vegetarian

House-made chickpea herb falafel fried crispy, wrapped in pita with cucumber salad, pickled turnip, & creamy lemon tahini.

Meze & Desserts Made Fresh Daily

Spicy Whipped Feta Dip (Tirokafteri) Favorite Dip

Creamy Greek sheep's feta whipped with roasted red peppers, olive oil, & cayenne, served warm with pita triangles.

Authentic Greek Village Salad (Horiatiki) No Lettuce

Vine-ripened tomatoes, crisp cucumbers, kalamata olives, red onion, & thick block of Greek feta drizzled in extra virgin olive oil.

Greek Lemon Oregano Roasted Potatoes Classic Side

Yukon gold potatoes slow-roasted in chicken stock, fresh lemon juice, Greek oregano, & garlic butter.

Honey Pecan Baklava Flaky Pastry Sweet Finish

Layers of buttered phyllo dough stuffed with crushed walnuts & pecans, soaked in orange blossom honey syrup.
