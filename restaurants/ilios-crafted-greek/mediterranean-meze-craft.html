The Craft of Greek Meze

Every traditional Greek meal begins with meze. At Ilios Crafted Greek, we whip Greek sheep's feta with roasted red peppers, strain real Greek yogurt for garlic cucumber tzatziki, & toss authentic lettuce-free village salads.

Meze Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Greek Meze

1. Whipped Sheep's Feta (Tirokafteri)

Imported Greek P.D.O. feta whipped silky smooth with sweet roasted peppers & cayenne pepper.

2. Strained Tzatziki Yogurt

Thick strained whole-milk yogurt folded with salted grated cucumber, garlic, fresh dill, & extra virgin olive oil.

3. Authentic Village Horiatiki

Ripe vine tomatoes, crisp cucumbers, kalamata olives, & thick feta slab—zero filler lettuce leaves.
