## Original Audit Weakness

Ilios Crafted Greek relies on basic third-party online ordering widgets. Located at 1514 S Church St in South End, it lacks an owned digital showcase highlighting its Aegean culinary heritage, flame-grilled souvlaki craft, multi-location Charlotte footprint, and Greek gyro family catering platters.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South End Flagship Location:** 1514 S Church St, Charlotte NC 28203 (South End / Gold District).
2. **Aegean Mediterranean & Greek Specialties:** Vertical Spit-Roasted Pork & Chicken Gyros, Flame-Grilled Lamb Souvlaki Skewers, Whipped Feta & Tzatziki Meze Dips, Authentic Village Salad (*Horiatiki*), Crispy Lemon Oregano Potatoes, & Honey Pecan Baklava.
3. **Multi-Location Footprint:** South End, Stonecrest at Piper Glen, Oakhurst, Davidson, & Fort Mill SC.
4. **Hours of Operation:** Daily 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM (7 days a week).

### Core Design Moves

1. **Crisp Mediterranean Architectural Serif Typography:** Classic serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and Aegean kitchen mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Aegean Cobalt & Cycladic Marble White Palette:** Mediterranean coastal palette anchored in deep Aegean cobalt (`#0F2C59`), Aegean sky azure (`#0284C7`), terracotta sun gold (`#EAB308`), and Cycladic marble white (`#F8FAFC`).
3. **"The Aegean Spit-Roast Gyro & Meze Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`aegean-souvlaki-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing spit-roasted gyros & flame-grilled souvlaki alongside Mediterranean meze dips & Greek village salads.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Honeybear Bake Shop` — Warm Golden Honey Bakery with Playfair Display fonts and Deep Cocoa/Honey Amber.
2. `Home Brew Taproom & Tunes` — Industrial Craft Beer with Syne fonts and Industrial Iron/Copper.
3. `Ho Ho Cherry House` — Traditional Chinese & Wok with Cormorant Garamond fonts and Cherry Lacquer/Crimson.

### Divergent Choices for Ilios Crafted Greek

- **Hero Composition:** Aegean Coastal Mediterranean Sun & White Cycladic Stone Vault split-hero layout: left side features an Aegean azure blue stamp badge (`"AEGEAN MEDITERRANEAN & GREEK SOUVLAKI • SOUTH END (1514 S CHURCH ST)"`), crisp architectural serif (*Cinzel*), Aegean cobalt & azure blue palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of Mediterranean souvlaki platters & Greek tzatziki.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **ICG Vault Cards** (`icg-vault-card`) and South End Aegean highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`icg-header`, `icg-brand`, `aegean-hero-stage`, `azure-stamp-badge`, `icg-vault-card`, `icg-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
