🍽 Vertical Spit-Roast & Charcoal Sear

The Art of Aegean Souvlaki

Authentic Greek street food demands patient vertical spit roasting and intense open-flame searing. At Ilios Crafted Greek, we hand-stack pork & chicken shoulders in wild oregano & olive oil marinades, shaving juicy crisp meat directly onto char-grilled pitas.

Souvlaki Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Aegean Gyro Craft

1. Wild Oregano & EVOO Marinade

Meats are steeped for 24 hours in cold-pressed Greek extra virgin olive oil, garlic, lemon, & mountainous wild oregano.

2. Vertical Rotisserie Crisping

Hand-stacked meat cones spin continuously past radiant vertical heaters, rendering fat & creating crispy outer edges.

3. Flame-Grilled Souvlaki Skewers

Pork loin, chicken breast, & lamb skewers are char-broiled over open flames, locking in smoky charcoal flavor.
