🥦 Scratch-Made Vegan Gastronomy

The Science of Plant Comfort

Comfort food doesn't need animal products to taste soul-satisfying. At Dee’s Vegan To Go, we harness rich cashew creams, nutritional yeast, slow-braised jackfruit, & smoked paprika to create rich vegan mac, ribs, & collards.

Comfort Recipe Pillars

The 3 Pillars of Dee’s Plant-Based Cooking

1. Cashew & Yeast Cheese Base

We blend raw organic cashews & nutritional yeast into ultra-creamy cheese sauce without artificial additives.

2. Slow-Braised Jackfruit Ribs

Young green jackfruit absorbs rich hickory molasses BBQ sauce, mimicking tender pulled pork texture naturally.

3. Smoked Paprika Southern Braise

Our collard greens get deep smoky complexity from Spanish paprika & garlic instead of pork meat.
