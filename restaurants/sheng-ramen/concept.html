Culinary Integrity • Ballantyne Charlotte

Scratch Craft In Every Steaming Bowl.

Sheng Ramen was established with an unbending standard: to serve regional Japanese ramen prepared completely from scratch, without powdered concentrates or artificial shortcuts. By simmering whole pork bones for sixteen hours and honoring traditional seasoning tares, we provide South Charlotte with a comforting, authentic noodle destination.

Core Commitments

The Sheng Kitchen Creed

The three pillars guiding our daily prep and customer care.

Commitment 01

Real Bone Simmering

We never use commercial flavor powders or shortcuts. Every kettle of broth is filled with fresh pork bones and aromatic vegetables, simmered overnight until thick and savory.

Commitment 02

Hand-Crafted Toppings

From our tender rolled Berkshire pork belly chashu torched to order to our jammy marinated ramen eggs, each topping is prepared fresh daily in our Ballantyne kitchen.

Commitment 03

Welcoming Community Table

We designed our dining space to be warm, contemporary, and relaxed—welcoming families, solo noodle lovers, and coworkers for an effortless, delicious meal.

Ballantyne Dining

A Gathering Place in South Charlotte

Situated in the bustling Ballantyne Commons area, Sheng Ramen provides a peaceful retreat from the busy workday. Stop by for our swift weekday lunch sets or enjoy a leisurely weekend dinner complete with gyoza, karaage, and imported Japanese refreshments.
