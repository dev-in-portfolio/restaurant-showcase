Rich Broths, Artisan Noodles.

Welcome to Sheng Ramen, South Charlotte’s destination for authentic Japanese ramen craftsmanship and izakaya favorites. Located at 15201 John J Delaney Drive in Ballantyne, our kitchen simmers rich tonkotsu pork bone broths for sixteen continuous hours, paired with custom springy alkaline noodles, torched Berkshire chashu, and scratch pan-fried gyoza.

Crunch & Flavor

Double-Fried Japanese Chicken Karaage

Bite-sized chicken thigh marinated in ginger, garlic, sake, and premium soy sauce, lightly dusted in potato starch and double-fried for an intensely crunchy exterior and succulent center. Served with Japanese Kewpie mayo and lemon wedge.
