At Sheng Ramen in Ballantyne, our kitchen maintains rolling kettles of marrow-rich pork bones for sixteen continuous hours, breaking down collagen into a velvety broth that coats every strand of our custom-formulated springy alkaline wheat noodles.

Culinary Standards

The Sheng Four-Pillar Architecture

How our kitchen constructs harmony across every element of the ramen bowl.

Pillar 01

16-Hour Emulsion

Pork femur and neck bones are soaked, blanched clean, then kept at a continuous rolling boil for sixteen hours to emulsify bone marrow and gelatin into a velvety, creamy broth.

Pillar 02

Kansui Mineral Salts

Our noodles are mixed with alkaline water (kansui) containing potassium and sodium carbonate, creating the signature golden hue, elasticity, and toothsome chew.

Pillar 03

Kuro Mayu Black Garlic Oil

Garlic cloves are slowly scorched through multiple stages until intensely aromatic and charred, blended with sesame oil to provide a deep, earthy roasted aroma.

Artisan Toppings

Torched Chashu & Ajitsuke Tamago

Every bowl is crowned with rolled Berkshire pork belly chashu that is braised tender in sweet mirin and soy sauce, then individually seared with a culinary blowtorch right before presentation. Paired with a seasoned soft-boiled ramen egg with a jammy, golden liquid yolk, each slurp delivers rich, multi-layered satisfaction.
