Signature Ramen Bowls

Served steaming hot with fresh alkaline noodles, ajitsuke egg, and premium toppings.

Tonkotsu Original Ramen $14.95

Rich 16-hour simmered pork bone broth, torched Berkshire pork chashu, soft ajitsuke tamago egg, menma bamboo shoots, wood ear mushrooms, green onions, and nori.

Spicy Black Garlic Tonkotsu $15.95

Creamy pork bone broth infused with roasted black garlic oil (mayu), house chili tare, double chashu pork belly, seasoned ground pork, and soft egg.

Savory fermented red and white miso broth, sweet corn kernels, melted Hokkaido butter pat, wavy springy noodles, chashu pork or organic crispy tofu, and bean sprouts.

Tokyo Shoyu Ramen $13.95

Seafood Champon Ramen $16.95

Japanese Curry Ramen $14.95
