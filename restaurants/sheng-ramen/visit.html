Easy Arrival

Complimentary Plaza Parking

Visiting our Ballantyne restaurant is completely stress-free:

  • Ample Surface Parking: Over two hundred free, well-lit surface parking stalls are situated directly in front of our dining room doors.
  • Accessible Stalls: Designated ADA handicap parking spaces are located immediately adjacent to our entrance walkway.
  • Quick Pickup Stalls: Designated short-term parking stalls allow for rapid takeout pickup without circling the plaza.
Takeout & Catering

Two-Tier Ramen Packaging & Party Trays

Enjoy our restaurant-quality ramen and izakaya favorites wherever you are:

  • Separated Broth Packaging: All takeout ramen bowls are packed with broth and fresh noodles in separate thermal compartments to prevent soggy noodles during transit.
  • Izakaya Catering Trays: Large-format 30-piece gyoza platters, crispy chicken karaage boxes, and steamed bao bun trays designed for corporate lunches and family gatherings.
  • Advance Notice: Please give our kitchen 24 hours advance notice for catering orders exceeding ten people by calling (704) 919-0118.
