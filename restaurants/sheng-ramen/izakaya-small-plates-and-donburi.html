The Hallmarks of Our Small Plates

Every appetizer is prepared with precision heat, balance, and presentation.

Technique 01

Hanetsuki Gyoza Lace

Our dumplings are pan-fried in cast iron with a starch slurry that forms a crisp, delicate lace skirt connecting the dumplings, providing textural contrast against juicy pork fillings.

Technique 02

Double-Fry Karaage

Chicken thigh pieces are ginger-marinated and fried twice—first at moderate temperature to cook through gently, then flash-fried at high heat to achieve supreme crunchiness without greasiness.

Technique 03

Sweet Soy Donburi Tare

Our rice bowls feature slow-reduced sweet unagi and mirin sauce drizzled over hot sushi rice, complemented by caramelized pork chashu or crispy panko chicken katsu.

Perfect Pairings

Cold Sapporo & Piping Hot Karaage

Nothing complements a lively dinner in Ballantyne like a chilled glass of Japanese draft beer paired with piping hot chicken karaage or takoyaki octopus balls. Share a few plates with family and friends before diving into your signature ramen bowls.
