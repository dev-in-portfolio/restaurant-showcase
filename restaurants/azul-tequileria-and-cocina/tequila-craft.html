The 100% Blue Agave Tequila Cellar

We stock only 100% Weber Blue Agave tequilas distilled using traditional masonry ovens and copper pot stills. Discover our signature tasting flights below.

Curated Tasting Board

Our Four Agave Flight Expressions

01

Highland Blanco Pure Harvest Flight

Unaged 100% blue agave bottled straight after distillation. Bright herbal aromas, fresh citrus zest, cracked black pepper, and clean mineral finish.

Pairing:
Cocktail Mexicano
02

Reposado Oak Barrel Cask Flight

Aged 2 to 12 months in white oak barrels. Smooth notes of toasted vanilla, roasted agave heart, light cinnamon spice, and buttery finish.

Pairing:
Birria Tacos
03

Añejo & Reserve Bourbon Cask Flight

Aged 1 to 3 years in charred bourbon barrels. Deep amber hue with rich chocolate, dried fruit, warm oak, and silky velvet mouthfeel.

Pairing:
Carne Asada
04

Oaxacan Underground Pit-Roasted Mezcal Flight

Artisanal Espadín and Tobalá agaves roasted underground in stone pits. Earthy, rich, complex smoky finish served with orange slices and sal de gusano.

Pairing:
Churros con Cajeta
