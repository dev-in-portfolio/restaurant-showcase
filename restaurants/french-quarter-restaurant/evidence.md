# Evidence & Creative Brief: French Quarter Restaurant

## Creative Brief
- **Brand Identity**: French Quarter Restaurant is an iconic Uptown Charlotte culinary landmark established in 1986, located at 321 S Church St directly anchoring the entrance to historic Brevard Court. Renowned for authentic New Orleans Cajun & Creole heritage, hearty homemade comfort food, famous Salt & Pepper chicken wings, and vibrant open-air courtyard patio dining.
- **Target Audience**: Uptown corporate professionals seeking rapid, hearty weekday lunches; Charlotte sports enthusiasts heading to Bank of America Stadium and Truist Field; evening socializers wanting craft brews, Cajun pastas, and courtyard cocktails.
- **Design Philosophy**: Deep Mardi Gras-inspired regal palette (French navy, rich royal purple `#2e1065`, antique brass/gold `#d97706`, spiced cayenne `#b91c1c`, and warm parchment `#fdfbf7`), architectural cast-iron accents reflecting New Orleans French Quarter balconies, clean modern typography, responsive multi-column layouts, and interactive menu filtering.
- **Key Differentiators**: Over 35 years of continuous family hospitality in Brevard Court; scratch-simmered Louisiana Gumbo & Creole; signature Salt & Pepper wings; iconic Monte Cristo sandwich; pet-friendly courtyard seating.
