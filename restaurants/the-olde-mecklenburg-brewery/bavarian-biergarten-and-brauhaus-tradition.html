8-Acre LoSo Canopy • Munich Communal Tables

Shaded Oak Biergarten, Bavarian Hospitality.

In Munich, a biergarten is not merely an outdoor patio—it is an egalitarian public living room where all generations gather beneath shaded chestnut trees to share conversation, cold lager, and hearty food. Our 8-acre campus in Charlotte’s Lower South End captures this spirit completely, shaded by massive oak trees and bordered by our traditional Brauhaus Festhalle.

Bavarian Traditions

The Elements of the OMB Biergarten

Rooted in Munich history, community warmth, and open-air German fellowship.

Tradition 01

Communal Oak Tables

No velvet ropes or isolated booths. Authentic long German timber tables welcome groups to sit together, meet neighbors, and clink glasses of Copper.

Tradition 02

Family & Dog Friendly

A spacious, fenced park setting welcoming leashed four-legged companions and families with open grass areas and outdoor games.

Tradition 03

The Brauhaus Festhalle

When cooler weather arrives, our cavernous timber-beamed German hall provides cozy fireplace seating, live brass bands, and full table service.

Culinary Heritage

Authentic Bavarian Kitchen

We believe great German beer demands authentic German food. Our scratch culinary team prepares hand-pinched sausages simmered in Copper Altbier, savory sweet currywurst with crisp pommes frites, and golden schnitzels served with warm bacon-dressed German potato salad.
