Ingolstadt Bavaria 1516 • Zero Adjuncts

Four Ingredients, Uncompromising Purity.

In 1516, Duke Wilhelm IV of Bavaria decreed the Reinheitsgebot—the world's oldest food and consumer safety law. At The Olde Mecklenburg Brewery, we preserve this five-century-old brewing philosophy with religious discipline. We believe world-class beer requires no shortcuts, no rice, no corn syrup, and no preservatives.

The Four Ingredients of OMB Craft Beer

Sourced directly from Bavarian family farms and filtered for absolute pristine clarity.

Element 01

Malted Two-Row Barley

Imported directly from certified German maltings, providing rich biscuit notes, copper color, and clean fermentable sugars without harsh tannins.

Element 02

Bavarian Noble Hops

Hallertau Mittelfrüh and Tettnang hops grown in southern Germany, delivering subtle floral aromas and refined, crisp bitterness rather than palate-scorching acidity.

Element 03

Pure Water & Yeast

Purified municipal water balanced with brewing salts to replicate Bavarian aquifer profiles, paired with pure single-strain German yeast cultures.

Lagering Method

Horizontal Cold Lagering Tanks

Unlike modern industrial breweries that rush beer out the door in ten days, OMB utilizes horizontal lagering tanks imported from Germany. By storing our beers near freezing temperatures for four to eight weeks, yeast and particulate settle naturally under gentle hydrostatic pressure, creating an exceptionally smooth, crisp beer without artificial filtration aids.
