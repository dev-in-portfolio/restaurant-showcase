Reinheitsgebot Brews & Bavarian Kitchen

Pure German Lagers, Scratch Brauhaus Fare.

Every beer on tap at The Olde Mecklenburg Brewery is brewed under the strict discipline of the German Reinheitsgebot of 1516. Paired with scratch-made Bavarian classics—from giant lye-dipped pretzels and beer-simmered bratwursts to golden pork schnitzels—our menu delivers authentic Munich flavor to the Queen City.

Reinheitsgebot 1516 Craft Beers on Tap

Brewed strictly with pure water, malted barley, Bavarian noble hops, and yeast. Poured in traditional half-liters, full liters, or flights.

Copper Düsseldorf-Style Altbier $6.75 / Half Liter

Charlotte's most iconic craft beer. An authentic top-fermented Düsseldorf-style amber ale with rich malt complexity, notes of toasted bread crust, and a crisp, clean Bavarian hop finish. 4.8% ABV.

Captain Jack Munich-Style Pilsner $6.75 / Half Liter

Crisp, refreshing German lager brewed with two-row Munich malts and noble Hallertau hops. Golden straw color with floral hop aromas and snappy bitterness. 4.9% ABV.

Hornet's Nest Bavarian Hefeweizen $6.75 / Half Liter

Traditional unfiltered Bavarian wheat beer poured with a towering pillowy white head. Naturally fermented with yeast strains yielding subtle banana and clove esters. 5.4% ABV.

70 Knots New England Style IPA $7.25 / Pint

Hazy, juicy American IPA brewed with pure German two-row malt and dry-hopped generously for bursts of tropical citrus, ripe mango, and grapefruit aromas. 6.8% ABV.

Mecktoberfest Märzen Amber Lager $7.00 / Half Liter

International gold-medal winning traditional German Märzen lager brewed with toasted Munich and Vienna malts for deep amber color and toasted bread aromatics. 5.4% ABV.

Custom Four-Pour Brauhaus Flight $12.00

Your choice of four 5-ounce pours from our active draft taps, served on a custom handcrafted wooden bier paddle with tasting notes.

Traditional German Brauhaus Fare & Sausages

Authentic recipes cooked from scratch in our Charlotte kitchen.

OMB Signature Bratwurst Platter $16.50

Two grilled Bavarian pork bratwursts simmered in Copper Altbier, served with warm braised sauerkraut, pan-roasted German red potatoes with bacon, and sweet imported Bavarian mustard.

Classic Currywurst & Pommes $14.50

Sliced grilled German sausage smothered in our signature spiced curry tomato ketchup, served with a generous mountain of golden french fries and European mayonnaise.

Crispy Pork Schnitzel $18.95

Center-cut pork loin pounded thin, dredged in seasoned breadcrumbs and pan-fried golden crisp, served with braised sweet red cabbage, warm potato salad, and fresh lemon wedges.

The OMB Brauhaus Burger $15.95

Angus beef patty grilled medium with melted sharp Wisconsin cheddar, caramelized onions, smoked bacon, and Copper beer glaze on a toasted pretzel bun with fries.

Bavarian Biergarten Sharables & Starters

Designed for sharing around long biergarten tables.

Giant Bavarian Soft Pretzel (Laugenbrezel) $11.50

Authentic Bavarian jumbo pretzel baked fresh with a lye-dipped crust and coarse salt, served with warm house-made Copper beer cheese dip and sweet Bavarian mustard.

German Sausage Trio Board $16.00

Grilled Bratwurst, Knackwurst, and Smoked Kielbasa sliced and served with cornichons, braised sauerkraut, spicy German mustard, and warm rye bread.

Brauhaus Fried Pickles $9.50

Thick-cut kosher dill pickle chips dredged in seasoned flour, flash-fried crispy, and served with house-made buttermilk herb dip.

Bavarian Beer Cheese & Chips $8.50

Silky warm dip made with sharp cheddar and Copper Altbier, served with crispy salted kettle potato chips.
