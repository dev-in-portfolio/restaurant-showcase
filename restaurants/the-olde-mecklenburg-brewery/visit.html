Open-Air Seating

Biergarten Directives & Pet Policy

Guidelines for enjoying our outdoor tree-canopied grounds:

  • Communal Open Seating: Outdoor biergarten tables are first-come, first-served. Guests are encouraged to share open table ends with fellow patrons.
  • Dog Friendly: Well-behaved dogs on standard non-retractable leashes are warmly welcomed throughout all outdoor biergarten areas. Water bowls are stationed throughout the grounds.
  • Table Service vs. Walk-Up: Food and drink in the outdoor biergarten can be ordered from any walk-up kiosk, beer station, or mobile QR code. Indoor Brauhaus dining rooms feature full table service.
Annual Celebrations

Seasonal Festivals & Celebrations

Directives for our iconic Queen City German festivals:

  • Spring Maibaum (May): Traditional Bavarian Maypole raising festival featuring live folk music, spring Maibock beer tapping, and family crafts.
  • Mecktoberfest (September): Charlotte's largest German celebration spanning three weekends, showcasing authentic Märzen lager, Bavarian brass bands, and stein holding competitions.
  • Weihnachtsmarkt (December): Traditional European Christmas market featuring artisan craft huts, fire pits, spiced warm Glühwein, and festive holiday lights.
