Charlotte's Original Craft Brewery • Est. 2009

Pure German Heritage, Copper Altbier.

Welcome to The Olde Mecklenburg Brewery, the foundation of Charlotte craft brewing. Since 2009, we have strictly adhered to the 1516 German Reinheitsgebot, brewing world-class German lagers and ales with only pure water, two-row malt, Bavarian hops, and yeast. Gather beneath the majestic tree canopy of our 8-acre LoSo biergarten for fresh-poured steins, warm Laugenbrezeln, and scratch-made bratwurst platters.

Biergarten Essential

Authentic Bavarian Laugenbrezeln

Baked fresh throughout the day, our giant Bavarian soft pretzels feature a mahogany-brown lye-dipped crust, coarse pretzel salt crystals, and a tender, steaming interior. Served alongside warm house-made craft beer cheese dip made with our flagship Copper ale and sweet imported Bavarian mustard.

Precision Craft

Polished Copper Brewhouse

Our custom German-engineered brewhouse utilizes traditional decoction mashing and horizontal lagering tanks imported directly from Germany. This patient brewing process gives our flagship Copper Altbier and Captain Jack Pilsner unmatched clarity, smooth malt depth, and clean drinkability.
