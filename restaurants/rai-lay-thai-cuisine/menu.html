Wok Noodles & Curries Kitchen Craft

Rai Lay Pad Thai #1 Signature

Thin rice noodles wok-seared with jumbo tiger prawns, eggs, fresh bean sprouts, Chinese chives, pressed tofu, and house sweet-tangy tamarind sauce, finished with crushed roasted peanuts and fresh lime.

Drunken Noodles (Pad Kee Mao) Fiery Wok

Wide rice noodles flash-fried over intense heat with sliced chicken breast, bird's eye chilies, bell peppers, sweet yellow onions, and aromatic Thai holy basil in savory dark soy glaze.

Southern Massaman Beef Curry Slow-Braised

Tender chunks of beef chuck braised low and slow in aromatic massaman coconut curry with roasted baby potatoes, pearl onions, and toasted whole peanuts with jasmine rice.

Panang Salmon Curry Coastal Hero

Pan-seared Atlantic salmon fillet resting in rich, nutty panang coconut curry infused with julienned kaffir lime leaves, bell peppers, and fresh green beans.

Specialties & Drinks Bar & Sweet

Crispy Duck Tamarind Chef Feature

Half roasted duck with crispy skin glazed in sweet and sour tamarind reduction, served alongside steamed baby bok choy and fragrant jasmine rice.

Tom Yum Goong Lemongrass Soup Hot & Sour

Classic fragrant hot and sour broth steeped with jumbo shrimp, straw mushrooms, galangal, lemongrass stalks, and fresh lime juice.

Traditional Thai Iced Milk Tea House Brew

Slow-steeped spiced Thai black tea poured over crushed ice and topped with rich sweet condensed milk and evaporated milk cream.

Mango Sticky Rice Classic Dessert

Warm sweet sticky rice infused with coconut milk and pandan, served with ripe sliced honey mango and toasted sesame seeds.
