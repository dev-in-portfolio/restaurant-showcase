Southern Thailand’s coastlines are renowned for extraordinary seafood preparations and vibrant refreshments. Indulge in crispy roasted duck with tangy tamarind glaze, pan-seared salmon curries, lemongrass hot pots, handcrafted tropical cocktails, and our legendary Thai milk tea.

The 3 Pillars of Rai Lay Seafood & Bar

1. Crispy Duck Mastery

Marinating whole ducks in aromatic five-spice and honey, roasting slow, and crisping skin before bathing in sour-sweet tamarind sauce.

2. Andaman Seafood Pairings

Featuring tiger prawns, Atlantic salmon, and tender calamari wok-tossed with fresh ginger, Thai basil, and sweet bell peppers.

3. House-Brewed Thai Teas

Steeping traditional Ceylon black teas with star anise and cardamom, poured over crushed ice and sweetened with velvety condensed milk.
