🌴 The Rai Lay Story

Krabi Spirit, Queen City Soul.

Rai Lay Thai was named in honor of Railay Beach—a secluded peninsula in Southern Thailand accessible only by traditional long-tail boats, famed for its towering limestone peaks and vibrant culinary street markets. We bring that same sense of coastal escape and authentic Thai warmth to South End Charlotte.
