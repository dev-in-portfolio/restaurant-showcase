The Sommelier Vision

Under the guidance of proprietor and sommelier Josh Villapando, Assorted Table strips away pretense to celebrate great winemaking, rare sakes, and community gathering.

Philosophy & Curation

The Four Pillars of Assorted Table

1. Small-Scale Independent Estates

We prioritize organic, biodynamic, and family-owned vineyards over mass commercial labels.

2. Accessible Wine Education

No jargon, no intimidation. Our staff loves helping beginner wine drinkers and seasoned collectors alike.

3. Japanese Sake & Bitters Archive

Charlotte’s most expansive collection of artisanal Junmai Ginjo sakes and small-batch cocktail bitters.

4. Urban Food Hall Hub

Embedded in The Market at 7th Street to foster vibrant community tastings, pop-up events, and light rail convenience.
