Handcrafted Cantina Cocktails

Fresh Lime Margaritas & Jalisco Cantaritos

At Santa Fe Mexican Restaurant, our cantina elevates classic Mexican cocktails with 100% Blue Agave tequilas, freshly squeezed Persian limes, and natural fruit purees.

  • House Gold & Silver Margaritas: Shaken with fresh citrus juice and served on the rocks or frozen with seasoned chili-lime Tajín rims.
  • Cantarito de Jalisco: Traditional clay cup filled with reposado tequila, fresh grapefruit, orange, lime, and Mexican squirt soda.
  • Cervezas & Micheladas: Ice-cold Modelo Especial, Corona, Dos Equis, Pacifico, and Victoria drafted or bottled with house-spiced Clamato.
  • Aguas Frescas Caseras: Freshly brewed Horchata, sweet tart Agua de Jamaica (hibiscus), and Tamarindo made fresh each morning.
