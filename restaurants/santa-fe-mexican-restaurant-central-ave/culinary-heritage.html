Rooted in Tradition

Our Culinary Heritage & Values

A family passion for slow-cooked Mexican comfort food, hand-roasted chiles, and true community hospitality on Central Avenue.

From Scratch Every Morning

The Fire, The Stone, and The Salsas

At Santa Fe Mexican Restaurant, our kitchen doors open early every day at 8:00 AM so our cooks can begin charring roma tomatoes, roasting serranos and poblanos over open flame, and slow-braising our meats.

We never use shortcuts or pre-packaged spice mixes. Our marinades rely on whole Mexican oregano, ground cumin, whole allspice, achiote paste, fresh Mexican cinnamon, and citrus juices.

What We Stand For

The Four Pillars of Santa Fe

1. Authenticity First

We honor regional Mexican recipes from Jalisco and Michoacán, serving real street tacos on double corn tortillas with cilantro, onions, and spicy salsas.

2. Daily Freshness

From avocados mashed tableside for guacamole to freshly pressed tortillas and morning-brewed horchata, fresh is our daily standard.

3. Generous Portions

We believe no one should leave hungry. Every platter is piled high with seasoned meats, charro beans, Spanish rice, and warm tortillas.

4. Family Hospitality

From the moment you walk through our doors at 5111 Central Ave, you are welcomed as family into our vibrant dining room.

Morning Comfort Food

Breakfast Served Daily Starting at 9:00 AM

Start your morning in East Charlotte with a hot plate of traditional Mexican breakfast. Enjoy crisp corn tortillas simmered in salsa verde or roja, topped with sunny-side-up eggs, crumbled queso fresco, and thinly sliced grilled cecina steak.

  • Chilaquiles Verdes con Cecina: Hand-cut fried tortilla chips tossed in tangy green tomatillo sauce with steak, crema, and eggs.
  • Huevos Rancheros: Two fried eggs over soft corn tortillas smothered in warm ranchero salsa, served with refried beans and potatoes.
  • Huevos con Chorizo: Scrambled eggs mixed with our savory house Mexican chorizo sausage and warm flour tortillas.
