The Art of the Sizzle

Citrus-Marinated Meats & Blistered Peppers

Our fajitas start with 24-hour marinades crafted from fresh orange juice, Mexican oregano, cumin, garlic, and cracked pepper. Char-grilled over open flames and served over a searing bed of sweet bell peppers and caramelized onions.

  • Fajitas Santa Fe Trio: Marinated arrachera skirt steak, tender chicken breast, and tiger shrimp.
  • Fajitas Texanas: Steak, chicken, shrimp, and crumbled Mexican chorizo sausage for extra richness.
  • Accompaniments Included: Homemade guacamole, fresh pico de gallo, sour cream, Mexican rice, charro beans, and warm corn or flour tortillas.

Taqueria Excellence

Street Tacos Served the Authentic Mexican Way

No shredded yellow cheese or hard shells here. Our tacos follow traditional street recipes: doubled warm corn tortillas, cilantro, freshly diced white onions, lime wedges, and house-crafted salsas.

  • Al Pastor con Piña: Spiced pork roasted on the vertical spit with sweet roasted pineapple chunks.
  • Carne Asada: Tender citrus-marinated steak seared on high heat on the plancha.
  • Birria de Res: Crisped on the griddle with melted cheese and served with rich spiced dipping consommé.
  • Carnitas Michoacanas: Crispy pork confit simmered with orange and Mexican herbs until fall-apart tender.

Centuries-Old Tradition

Molcajete Caliente: A Volcanic Feast

Our signature Molcajete is served in a volcanic basalt rock mortar heated directly over high flames to 500 degrees Fahrenheit. The stone retains heat throughout your meal, keeping the rich roasted chipotle sauce bubbling at your table.

  • Grilled Arrachera & Pechuga: Skirt steak and chicken breast grilled over flame.
  • Tiger Shrimp & Chorizo: Sautéed jumbo shrimp and house pork sausage.
  • Queso Panela & Nopales: Grilled fresh panela cheese slabs and tender cactus paddles that absorb the fiery roasted salsa.
  • Family Sized: Generously portioned to share between 2 to 3 guests.
