## Original Audit Weakness

Santa Fe Mexican Restaurant on Central Avenue is an essential culinary pillar for authentic Mexican cuisine in East Charlotte, serving everything from morning chilaquiles to late-night sizzling fajitas. However, their digital footprint was previously limited to basic online ordering links without a dedicated custom multi-page website highlighting their volcanic molcajetes, scratch-made salsas and marinades, cantina cocktail program, or group party catering services. This 6-page responsive web experience gives Santa Fe a vibrant, modern, and high-converting digital storefront.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Sizzling Molcajetes & Cast-Iron Fajitas:** Heated volcanic basalt stone mortars bubbling with marinated arrachera steak, chicken, tiger shrimp, grilled cactus (nopales), and panela cheese in spicy roasted chipotle salsa.
2. **Authentic Double-Corn Street Tacos:** Slow-roasted Al Pastor with sweet pineapple, braised Birria de Res with rich consommé, and crisp Carnitas Michoacanas.
3. **Fresh-Squeezed Cantina Cocktails:** 100% Blue Agave tequilas, fresh Persian lime margaritas, Jalisco clay cantaritos, and scratch-brewed Horchata, Jamaica, and Tamarindo.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Warm Terracotta & Agave Palette:** Rich Terracotta Red (`#b83a1b`), Deep Agave Green (`#245842`), Warm Maize Gold (`#d9822b`), and Warm Linen Cream (`#faf7f2`).
2. **Culinary Focus Visual Cards:** Sizzling cast-iron skillet banners, volcanic stone spotlight, and authentic taqueria cards.
3. **Warm Modern Typography:** Playfair Display elegant serif display headlines paired with Inter for clear, readable menus and mobile-friendly navigation.
