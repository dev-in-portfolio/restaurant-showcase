The Santa Fe Margarita Standard

We believe a great margarita starts with real ingredients. Our house sour mix is crafted daily using fresh Persian limes, lemons, and pure organic agave nectar—never bottled syrups or powders.

  • Margarita de la Casa (Rocks or Frozen): 100% Agave Tequila, triple sec, fresh lime juice, and salted rim ($8.99 / Jumbo $14.50).
  • Spicy Jalapeño Cucumber Margarita: Silver tequila, muddled crisp cucumber, fresh jalapeño slices, and Tajín chile-lime rim ($11.50).
  • Tropical Fruit Puree Margaritas: Mango, Passion Fruit, Strawberry, and Guava ($10.50).
  • Top Shelf Cadillac Margarita: Don Julio Blanco, Grand Marnier float, freshly squeezed lime, and coarse sea salt ($14.99).
