🏛 Decades of East Charlotte Tradition

A Charlotte Diner Tradition

For decades, Parkway House Family Restaurant has served as a welcoming meeting place for generations of Charlotte families. Located in the East Town Shopping Center at 5821 E W.T. Harris Blvd, our diner is defined by friendly greetings, steaming cups of hot coffee, and honest comfort food made from scratch.
