🥞 Scratch-Mixed Morning Comfort

The Art of All-Day Breakfast

Breakfast is served all day, every day at Parkway House. We mix our buttermilk pancake batter from scratch every morning, griddle golden hash browns crispy, and hand-bread our tender country fried steaks before smothering them in house sausage gravy.

Breakfast Standards

The 3 Pillars of Parkway House Morning Griddling

1. Real Buttermilk Batter

Whisking whole eggs, cultured buttermilk, real butter, and pure vanilla for light, fluffy, golden-edged pancakes.

2. Cast-Iron Skillet Sizzle

Layering seasoned hash browns, breakfast meats, grilled onions, and melted cheeses in searing skillets topped with eggs.

3. Scratch Country Gravy

Simmering seasoned pork sausage drippings with whole milk, flour, and cracked black pepper for authentic Southern country gravy.
