Decades on Providence Road, One Fiery Wok at a Time.

Taipei Express has stood proudly at 731 Providence Road through decades of Charlotte growth and transformation. In a dining landscape often swept by fleeting culinary trends, our commitment has remained unwavering: serve honest, piping-hot Chinese and Taiwanese wok cuisine prepared fresh to order with generous portions and warm family hospitality.

Core Values

The Foundations of Taipei Express

How we uphold wok integrity and community connection.

Value 01

Authentic Wok Mastery

We never use steam tables or pre-cooked entrees. Every plate is fired to order in high-heat woks, ensuring food arrives at your table or home steaming hot.

Value 02

Eastover Neighborhood Anchor

Serving Eastover, Myers Park, and Elizabeth, we have welcomed generations of Charlotte families, doctors from nearby hospitals, and local business neighbors.

Value 03

Consistent Value & Generosity

We believe in hearty portions at fair prices. Our daily lunch specials and dinner combos provide substantial, nutritious meals crafted from quality ingredients.

A Neighborhood Tradition

Family Table to Takeout Bag

Whether gathering at our Providence Road booths for family dinner or picking up takeout on the drive home from work, Taipei Express delivers the familiar comfort of sizzling woks, fragrant jasmine rice, and warm dumplings. Thank you for making us a part of your family table.
