Historic Eastover • 731 Providence Road

High-Heat Wok Hei, Time-Honored Flavor.

Welcome to Taipei Express, Eastover Charlotte’s beloved destination for authentic Chinese and Taiwanese wok cuisine. Located on Providence Road, our kitchen harnesses roaring steel woks at 600 degrees to impart authentic "breath of the wok" flavor. From crisp General Tso’s chicken and hand-pleated pork potstickers to wok-seared beef with broccoli and comforting lo mein, we have served Charlotte families for decades.
