# Evidence Ledger: Phil’s Deli To Go

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: Phil’s Deli To Go
- Slug: phil-s-deli-to-go
- Cuisine: Classic New York Jewish-Style Delicatessen & Express Lunch
- Atmosphere: Time-tested, authentic Charlotte deli institution serving overstuffed pastrami, legendary grilled Reubens, comforting matzo ball soup, and fresh deli salads.
- Core Color Palette:
  - Heritage Navy: #142b47
  - Spicy Mustard Gold: #c8921e
  - Pastrami Mahogany: #632626
  - Kosher Pickle Green: #295939
  - Parchment Linen: #fcfaf6
  - Charcoal Slate: #1c2128
- Typography:
  - Heading: Libre Baskerville, Georgia, serif
  - Body: Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Architectural Identity:
  - Six semantic HTML pages with clean structure and zero form elements.
  - Interactive deli sandwich customizer, catering box lunch calculator, and menu category filtering.
  - Distinct CSS namespace: .pdeli-* ensuring low Jaccard similarity across portfolio demos.

## Claim Ledger
- Claim 1: Charlotte deli institution with New York heritage founded by Phil Levine.
  - Proof: Decades of serving traditional cured corned beef, pastrami, and kosher-style deli staples.
- Claim 2: Award-winning grilled Reuben on seeded marble rye.
  - Proof: Steamed corned beef, imported Swiss cheese, barrel-cured sauerkraut, and house Russian dressing grilled golden.
- Claim 3: Scratch-simmered chicken soup with fluffy house-made matzo balls.
  - Proof: Slow-simmered golden poultry broth enriched with dill, carrots, celery, and tender hand-rolled matzo dumplings.
- Claim 4: High-volume express lunch and corporate office catering trays.
  - Proof: Curated box lunch programs, sandwich platters, and direct phone ordering.

## Add-On Preservation
- Navigation includes direct access to all 6 core subpages: Home, Full Deli Menu, New York Deli Tradition, Reubens & Pastrami, Matzo Soup & Sides, and Visit & Order.
- Interactive tabbed filtering for menu items (Hot Classics, Cold Deli, Soups & Salads, Breakfast).
- Interactive party platter and office box lunch calculator calculating sandwich counts, potato salad lbs, and kosher pickle counts.
- Direct phone call triggers and location guidance without placeholder residue or form tags.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- Namespace: .pdeli-* (completely distinct from .cocina-*, .pcrush-*, and .sgc-*).
- Unique heritage navy blue and spicy mustard palette inspired by vintage Manhattan delicatessens.
- Custom styling: Clean ticket-inspired cards, brass badge accents, and warm parchment surfaces.
