🍗 The Spanish Tapas Tradition

The Art of Iberian Tapas

In Spain, dining is a social celebration meant to be shared through small, intensely flavorful tapas. At Miro Spanish Grille, our tapas menu celebrates Spain's premier ingredients—from acorn-fed Jamón Ibérico de Bellota to sizzling garlic shrimp in olive oil.

Iberian Sourcing

The 3 Pillars of Miro's Tapas Kitchen

1. 100% Acorn-Fed Jamón Ibérico

Cured for 36 months in the mountainous regions of Spain, our Jamón Ibérico possesses unmatched nutty sweetness and melt-in-your-mouth fat marbling.

2. Sizzling Cazuela Cookery

Serving hot tapas like Gambas al Ajillo bubbling straight to your table in traditional terracotta cazuela dishes infused with garlic and olive oil.

3. Artisanal Manchego & Membrillo

Pairing raw sheep's milk Manchego cheeses aged up to 12 months with quince membrillo, Marcona almonds, and rustic sourdough.
