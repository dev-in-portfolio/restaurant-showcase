🥘 The National Dish of Spain

The Art of Valencian Paella

True Spanish paella is a masterwork of heat control, quality grain, and aromatic aromatics. At Miro Spanish Grille, every paella is cooked to order in authentic wide polished steel pans, blending Spanish Bomba rice with toasted saffron threads and fresh Atlantic seafood.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Miro's Paella Craft

1. Valencian Bomba Short-Grain Rice

Imported Bomba rice absorbs three times its volume in saffron-infused stock while remaining firm and distinct without turning mushy.

2. Hand-Harvested Spanish Saffron

Infusing crushed La Mancha saffron threads into our slow-simmered shellfish broth for unmistakable golden hue and floral earthiness.

3. Achieving the Perfect Socarrat

Finishing the paella over high flame during the final minutes to develop the coveted caramelized, savory crispy rice layer on the pan bottom.
