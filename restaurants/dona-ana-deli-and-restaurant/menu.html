Guisados & Asados del Día

Served with rice, beans, and sweet fried plantains (maduros).

Pernil Asado Dominicano $13.50

Slow-roasted pork shoulder marinated in sour orange, garlic, and oregano, roasted until falling off the bone with shatteringly crisp skin (cuerito).

Pollo Guisado Criollo $12.50

Tender bone-in chicken slow-simmered in rich sofrito tomato sauce with sweet bell peppers, onions, and Dominican oregano.

Chivo Liniero Guisado $15.50

Tender bone-in goat stewed in traditional northwest Dominican spices, Scotch bonnet peppers, garlic, and dark red wine sauce.

Sancocho Dominicano (Fin de Semana) $14.50

Traditional rich Dominican stew of beef, pork, chicken, yuca, green plantain, and corn, served with white rice and fresh avocado.

Desayuno & Mangú Tradicional

Served all morning starting at 7:00 AM.

Mangú con Los Tres Golpes $10.50

Mashed green plantains with butter, topped with red pickled onions, fried Dominican salami, golden queso de freír, and two sunny eggs.

Desayuno con Longaniza $11.50

Mangú con los tres golpes accompanied by seasoned, crispy-fried pork longaniza sausage and avocado.

Mofongos en Pilón

Mashed green plantains with roasted garlic and chicharrón, served with hot savory broth.

Mofongo de Chicharrón $13.50

Fried green plantains mashed in a wooden pilón with roasted garlic, olive oil, and crispy pork cracklings. Served with dipping broth.

Mofongo con Camarones al Ajillo $16.50

Traditional garlic plantain mofongo topped with plump jumbo shrimp sautéed in garlic butter and white wine sauce.

Morir Soñando Tradicional $4.50

Classic Dominican blended drink of fresh orange juice, cold evaporated milk, and pure cane sugar.

Jugo Natural de Chinola $4.00

Freshly made natural passion fruit juice served chilled over crushed ice.
