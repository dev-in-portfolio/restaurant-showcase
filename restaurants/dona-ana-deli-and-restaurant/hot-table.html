The Hot-Table Rotation

Served steaming hot all day with generous portions for lunch and dinner.

Signature Roast

Pernil Asado

Pork shoulder slow-roasted for 8 hours in garlic, oregano, and sour orange with perfectly blistered, glass-crisp pork skin (cuerito).

Slow Simmered

Pollo Guisado Criollo

Juicy chicken pieces caramelized in raw cane sugar and braised in rich tomato sofrito with bell peppers and Spanish olives.

Weekend Specialty

Sancocho Dominicano

Dominican national soup packed with seven types of meat, yuca root, plantains, calabaza squash, and sweet corn on the cob.

Rice, Beans & Plantain Sides

Every hot-table platter includes your choice of freshly cooked sides.

Moro de Gandules (Pigeon Pea Rice) Included

Long-grain rice simmered with pigeon peas, coconut milk, cilantro, and garlic sofrito.

Arroz Blanco con Habichuelas Rojas Included

Steamed white rice smothered in rich stewed Dominican red silk beans (habichuelas guisadas).

Plátanos Maduros Fritos Included

Ripe sweet yellow plantains fried golden with caramelized edges.

Yuca Hervida con Cebollitas Included

Tender boiled cassava root drizzled with olive oil, garlic mojo, and pickled red onions.
