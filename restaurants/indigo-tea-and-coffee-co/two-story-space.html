From Pop-Up To Brick & Mortar

The Journey of Founder Reba Cox

Building a Black-owned community coffee brand rooted in care and flavor.

The South End Pop-Up Origins Est. 2021

Reba launched Indigo as a weekend pop-up at 412 W Palmer Street, quickly drawing loyal crowds for her homemade brown butter and coquito syrups.

Historic Fourth Ward Home 644 N Church St

The permanent flagship in Fourth Ward transformed an historic building into a vibrant, multi-level gathering space for Uptown residents and professionals.

Inclusive Community Programming Events

Indigo frequently hosts local book clubs, creative workshops, open-mic poetry evenings, and seasonal evening cocktail takeovers.

High-Speed WiFi & Coworking Complimentary

Fast fiber internet across both floors with dedicated power stations, making Indigo one of Uptown's favorite remote work destinations.
