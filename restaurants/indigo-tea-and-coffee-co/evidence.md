## Original Audit Weakness

Indigo Tea & Coffee Co. represents a premier gathering space in Uptown Charlotte's Fourth Ward, renowned for its viral scratch-made seasonal syrups, serene two-story brick architecture, and welcoming community vibe. However, their digital footprint relied heavily on social media posts without a responsive multi-page website showcasing their complete beverage menu, syrup origin stories, tea steeping guide, or parking validation directions. This 6-page concept delivers an elegant, high-converting digital home.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **House-Made Signature Syrups:** Brown Butter Sage, Banana Bread, Coquito, Smoked Vanilla Bean, and Cardamom Rose.
2. **Ceremonial Matcha & Loose-Leaf Teas:** Uji Kyoto ceremonial matcha, Lavender London Fog, Jasmine Blossom White Tea, and Hibiscus Tisanes.
3. **Two-Story Fourth Ward Sanctuary:** Mezzanine lounge, botanical work spaces, community coffee tables, and Ascher Garage parking validation.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Deep Indigo Navy & Matcha Sage Green Palette:** Deep Indigo Navy (`#1e1b4b`), Matcha Sage Green (`#4d7c0f`), Golden Honey Cream (`#fde68a`), and Parchment Alabaster (`#fcfbf8`).
2. **Botanical Sanctuary Visual Architecture:** Loose-leaf origin tags, syrup formulation cards, and mezzanine coworking layouts.
3. **Warm Modern Typography:** Syne display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for water temperature and steeping times.
