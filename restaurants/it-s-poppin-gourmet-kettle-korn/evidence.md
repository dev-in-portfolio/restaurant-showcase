- **Handcrafted Fruit Waters:** Freshly squeezed lemons and limes muddled with ripe strawberries, pure cane sugar, and ice with unlimited free refills at the counter.
- **Custom Event Favors & Popcorn Bars:** Full-service popcorn buffet displays and custom-branded individual favor bags for weddings, school graduations, baby showers, and corporate gifting.
