Handcrafted Fresh Fruit Waters

Made to order with real fruit, fresh citrus juices, and raw cane sugar—never artificial syrups or powders.

Pure Refreshment

The Ultimate Popcorn Pairing

Nothing cuts through the richness of our cheese, chocolate, or savory popcorn like an ice-cold, naturally infused fruit water.

We start with whole fresh California strawberries, Florida lemons, and Persian limes, muddle them gently to release their essential juices and aromas, and blend them with ice and pure filtered water. Sweetened lightly with pure raw cane sugar or available unsweetened upon request.
