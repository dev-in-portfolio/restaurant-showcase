Born in Charlotte

How It All Started

It's Poppin! Gourmet Kettle Korn was founded on a simple belief: snacks should be fun, extraordinarily delicious, and made with genuine heart.

From our home inside The Market at 7th Street in Uptown Charlotte, we pop over 60 unique flavor recipes in small batches daily, using non-GMO mushroom kernels that yield giant, spherical, crunch-packed bites.
