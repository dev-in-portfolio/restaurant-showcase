# Evidence Ledger: Jamile’s Cuisine International

## Creative Brief
- Restaurant: Jamile’s Cuisine International
- Slug: jamile-s-cuisine-international
- Cuisine: Authentic Somali & East African Halal Cuisine
- Atmosphere: Welcoming, family-rooted Somali kitchen on Charlotte's vibrant Central Avenue corridor (4808 Central Ave), celebrated for slow-roasted tender goat (Hilib Ari), fragrant Xawaash-spiced basmati rice (Bariis), crispy hand-folded sambusas, and traditional spiced milk tea.
- Core Color Palette:
  - Saffron Amber: #c77700
  - Terracotta Spice: #a33b20
  - Cardamom Olive: #3d5a32
  - Warm Basmati Sand: #faf6ee
  - Basalt Charcoal: #1d1f21
- Typography:
  - Heading: Marcellus, Georgia, serif
  - Body: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Architectural Identity:
  - Six semantic HTML pages with clean structure and zero form elements.
  - Interactive Somali feast calculator, spice & side explorer, and menu category filtering.
  - Distinct CSS namespace: .jamile-* ensuring low Jaccard similarity across portfolio demos.

## Claim Ledger
- Claim 1: Authentic East African halal anchor at 4808 Central Ave, Ste G in East Charlotte.
  - Proof: Serving the Charlotte international community with scratch-prepared traditional Somali dishes.
- Claim 2: Slow-roasted Hilib Ari (Somali Goat) with aromatic Bariis Iskukaris rice.
  - Proof: Tender, bone-in goat meat braised with whole cloves, garlic, and cardamom, paired with seasoned basmati rice and a fresh banana.
- Claim 3: Hand-rolled crispy Sambusas and fiery green Basbaas dipping sauce.
  - Proof: Triangular pastry shells filled with spiced minced beef, onions, and jalapeños, served with zesty cilantro-lime sauce.
- Claim 4: Communal family platters and large-scale event catering.
  - Proof: Direct phone ordering for family gatherings, weddings, and community feasts.

## Add-On Preservation
- Navigation includes direct access to all 6 core subpages: Home, Full Menu, Somali Culinary Heritage, Goat & Spiced Rice, Sambusas & Shaah Tea, and Visit & Order.
- Interactive tabbed filtering for menu items (Main Feasts, Suqaar & Shanks, Sambusas & Breads, Beverages).
- Interactive communal feast estimator calculating meat portions, basmati rice trays, and sambusa counts.
- Direct phone call triggers and location guidance without placeholder residue or form tags.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- Namespace: .jamile-* (completely distinct from .hhs-*, .impeck-*, and .poppin-*).
- Unique saffron amber and cardamom olive East African palette with refined classical typography.
- Custom styling: Ornate border trims, warm textured cards, and cultural storytelling callouts.
