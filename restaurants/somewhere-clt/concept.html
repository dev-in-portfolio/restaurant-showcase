Redefining The Social Night.

Somewhere CLT was born from a simple desire: to give Charlotte adults a stylish, mature social sanctuary where they can unwind with top-shelf drinks, premium hookah, and great music without the chaos of college clubs. Located at 2815 The Plaza, we cultivate genuine connection in an atmosphere of refined comfort.

Our Commitments

The Foundations of Somewhere CLT

The values that guide our hospitality and nightlife curation.

Commitment 01

Mature Sophistication

Our 25+ door policy ensures a relaxed, mature crowd where patrons can dress with style, converse comfortably, and celebrate life's milestones with poise.

Commitment 02

Culinary Respect

We reject the notion that night clubs only serve frozen snacks. Our kitchen cooks with fresh meats, house spice rubs, and scratch-blended sauces late into the evening.

Commitment 03

Impeccable Service

From our security team at the door to our bottle hosts and shisha attendants, every team member is dedicated to making your evening smooth, safe, and memorable.

The Plaza Energy

Connecting Midwood & Villa Heights

Situated in a historic arts and culture neighborhood, Somewhere CLT serves as a gathering nexus for Charlotte creators, professionals, and night owls. Step into our world and experience nightlife the way it was always meant to be.
