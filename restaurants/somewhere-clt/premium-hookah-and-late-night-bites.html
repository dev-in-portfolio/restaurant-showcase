The Shisha Difference

The Craft of Pure Smoke

How we guarantee dense, flavor-forward clouds without throat bite.

Standard 01

100% Coconut Husk Coals

Zero chemical accelerants or instant-light toxins. Our coals burn consistently clean and odorless at optimal temperatures for over ninety minutes.

Standard 02

Fresh Citrus Fruit Heads

Our mixologists carve ripe grapefruits, pineapples, and naval oranges by hand to serve as the shisha bowl, infusing natural juice vapors into every draw.

Standard 03

Hygienic Silicone Hoses

Every customer receives single-use wrapped mouth tips and thoroughly sanitized medical-grade silicone hoses for an immaculate, hygienic experience.

Culinary Signature

Smoked Turkey Ribs & Hot Honey Glazes

While many lounges treat food as an afterthought, Somewhere CLT takes barbecue seriously. Our signature turkey ribs are seasoned with savory dry rubs, smoked until melt-in-your-mouth tender, and caramelized on the grill with a dark bourbon brown sugar glaze. Pair them with jumbo wings tossed in hot honey BBQ or lemon pepper butter.
