Arrival Directives

On-Site Parking & Rideshare Access

Arriving at 2815 The Plaza is straightforward and convenient:

  • Dedicated Paved Parking Lot: Private on-site parking is situated directly adjacent to our building along The Plaza for guest use.
  • Well-Lit Street Parking: Additional free curbside parking is available along The Plaza corridor and nearby cross streets.
  • Designated Rideshare Turnout: Seamless Uber and Lyft pickup and drop-off staging right in front of the venue entrance.
VIP Experiences

Velvet Booths & Courtyard Patio

Elevate your weekend celebration or private group night out:

  • Plush VIP Booths: Accommodates 6 to 15 guests with dedicated bottle service hostesses, sparkler bottle presentations, and plush velvet seating.
  • Open-Air Courtyard Patio: Enjoy fresh evening air, string lights, and heaters with full hookah and cocktail service outdoors.
  • Birthdays & Celebrations: Call (704) 465-5509 or email somewhereplaza@gmail.com to reserve your section and custom bottle packages.
