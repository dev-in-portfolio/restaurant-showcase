Welcome to Somewhere CLT, Charlotte's premier 25+ cocktail lounge and social club located at 2815 The Plaza. Master Shisha Preparation

Precision Heat & Custom Citrus Bowls

A superior hookah session demands meticulous temperature control. At Somewhere CLT, our shisha specialists carefully calibrate natural coconut coals over stainless chimney screens to ensure clean, dense vapor and pure flavor fidelity without harshness or burnt notes. Upgrade any session with freshly carved grapefruit or pineapple heads.
