🍪 6oz Cookie Science

The Art of Gourmet Cookies

Baking a 6-ounce cookie requires precision temperature science. At La Bonbonniere Bakery, Chef Valentina López ages our cookie dough for 24 hours to develop rich caramel tones, baking at 410°F to form a delicate golden shell surrounding a warm, molten chocolate center.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of 6oz Cookie Craft

1. 82% Fat European Butter

High-butterfat European cream creates unparalleled richness, tenderness, & a deep golden cookie crust.

2. 24-Hour Cold Dough Maturation

Chilling dough for 24 hours allows starches to hydrate & sugars to caramelize into rich butterscotch flavor notes.

3. Belgian Chocolate Chunks

Hand-cut blocks of Callebaut dark & milk chocolate create thick pools of molten cocoa in every bite.
