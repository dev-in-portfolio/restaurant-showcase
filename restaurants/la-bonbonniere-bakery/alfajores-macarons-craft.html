Alfajores & Macarons

Blending South American heritage with classic French patisserie technique. At La Bonbonniere Bakery, our dulce de leche alfajores melt on your tongue, while our French macarons feature crisp almond feet & silky chocolate ganache centers.

Pastry Craft

The 3 Pillars of Alfajores & Macaron Artistry

1. Slow-Cooked Dulce de Leche

Whole milk & cane sugar cooked low-and-slow for 6 hours until thick, golden, & deeply caramelized.

2. Melt-in-Your-Mouth Shortbread

Cornstarch-infused shortbread dough creating delicate, crumbly cookies that vanish on your tongue.

3. Aged Meringue Macaron Shells

Aged egg whites folded with super-fine almond flour to produce smooth macaron domes with perfect ruffled feet.
