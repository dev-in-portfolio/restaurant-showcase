Patisserie Roots & Passion

La Bonbonniere Bakery was created by pastry chef Valentina López to share the magic of classic French patisserie & South American dessert traditions. From our bakery counter at 320 S Tryon St in Uptown Charlotte, we bake pure joy every single morning.

The Values of La Bonbonniere Bakery

Pure Natural Ingredients

No artificial preservatives, hydrogenated oils, or artificial flavors—only real butter, cane sugar, & fine cocoa.
