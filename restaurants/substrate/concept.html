Optimist Park Roots • 512 East 15th Street

Demystifying Wine, Building Community.

Substrate was founded on a simple conviction: natural wine does not belong exclusively to upscale fine-dining rooms. Inspired by casual Italian and Spanish enotecas, we built an unpretentious sanctuary in Optimist Park where anyone can pull up a stool, listen to a classic soul LP on vinyl, and enjoy honest fermentations without gatekeeping.

Core Values

The Spirit of Substrate

What defines our approach to natural wine and neighborhood hospitality.

Value 01

Zero Pretension

No sommelier jargon or tasting exam pressure. We talk about wine in plain, approachable terms, encouraging guests to find what tastes good to them.

Value 02

Highbrow Meets Lowbrow

We happily pour an Austrian natural orange wine next to an $8 Miller High Life Spaghett cocktail or a tin of Spanish sardines with saltines.

Value 03

Neighborhood Living Room

From our dog-friendly outdoor patio to our cozy indoor wooden booths, Substrate is a space designed for human conversation and connection.
