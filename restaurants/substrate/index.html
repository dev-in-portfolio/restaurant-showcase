Natural Wine, Analog Sound.

Welcome to Substrate, Charlotte's unpretentious "blue-collar enoteca" situated at 512 East 15th Street. We demystify low-intervention winemaking with an eclectic rotating list of skin-contact orange wines, pet-nats, chilled glou-glou reds, and artisanal draft vermouth poured over ice. Anchored by an all-analog vinyl record sound system, gourmet conservas, and an open BYOF policy, we invite you to settle into our indoor listening lounge or leafy outdoor courtyard.
