Living Fermentations • Low-Intervention

Grapes, Native Yeast & Zero Bullshit.

Wine should be alive, expressive, and democratic. At Substrate, our cellars feature natural, biodynamic, and organic wines made by growers who work without synthetic chemicals, industrial enzymes, or heavy sulfites. Alongside our bottle wall, our Spanish vermut on draft revives European aperitif traditions with fresh citrus and olives.

Cellar Philosophy

The Foundations of Substrate Pours

Why minimal intervention creates more compelling, authentic wine.

Principle 01

Organic & Biodynamic Soil

We champion small vignerons practicing regenerative agriculture, avoiding artificial pesticides, and promoting living biodiversity in their vineyard soils.

Principle 02

Native Yeast Fermentation

Every bottle on our shelves ferments spontaneously using indigenous ambient yeasts from the grape skins rather than lab-engineered cultured flavor strains.

Principle 03

Draft Spanish Vermut

We bring Madrid and Barcelona aperitif culture to Charlotte, pouring fortified botanical vermut directly from the tap over ice with Spanish olives and orange.

Maceration Craft

The Joy of Skin-Contact Orange Wine

Orange wine is not made from citrus; it is white wine vinified like red wine, with the grape skins remaining in contact with the juice for weeks or months. This ancient technique produces a glowing amber tint, herbal aromatics, and savory tea-like tannins that pair remarkably well with our artisan charcuterie and conservas.
