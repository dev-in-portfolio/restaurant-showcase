Transit & Parking

How to Reach Substrate

Arriving at our 15th Street enoteca is simple:

  • Adjacent Surface Lot: Free dedicated customer parking spaces are available directly beside the building at 512 East 15th Street.
  • Street Parking: Plentiful free street parking runs along East 15th Street and neighboring Belmont residential streets.
  • Lynx Light Rail Access: We are a short walk from both the Parkwood and 25th Street Lynx Blue Line stations.
Patio & Food Guidelines

Courtyard Patio & Bring Your Own Food (BYOF)

What to expect when hanging out at Substrate:

  • Dog-Friendly Patio: Well-behaved dogs on leashes are always welcome in our open-air courtyard patio under string lights.
  • Bring Your Own Food (BYOF): Because we specialize in wine, vermouth, and bar conservas rather than full-course entrees, guests are welcome to bring outside food or order delivery directly to our address.
  • Vinyl Turntable Requests: Have a record you love? Ask our resident selector behind the bar—we often spin guest LPs during casual early evening sets.
