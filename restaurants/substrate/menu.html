Natural Wines, Draft Vermut & Bites.

At Substrate, wine is approached with curiosity and without gatekeeping. Explore minimal-intervention pet-nats, orange wines, and chilled reds poured alongside vermut on tap, the classic Miller High Life Spaghett, and Spanish tinned fish. Outside food is warmly welcomed.

Natural Wine, Draft Vermut & Conservas

Natural & Low-Intervention Wines

Organic, biodynamic, native-yeast fermented. Glasses poured daily; full retail wall available.

Skin-Contact Orange Wine $14.00 Glass / $50 Bottle

Macerated white grapes with extended skin contact; complex amber hue with dried apricot, chamomile, and savory black tea tannins.

Pétillant Naturel (Pet-Nat) $13.50 Glass / $48 Bottle

Naturally effervescent ancestral method sparkling wine; crisp, cloudily unfiltered, bursting with orchard apple and lively mineral acidity.

Chilled Glou-Glou Red $12.50 Glass / $45 Bottle

Bright, crushable, low-tannin Gamay or Frappato served slightly chilled; juicy tart cherries, brambly raspberries, and snappy finish.

Unfiltered Coastal White $12.00 Glass / $42 Bottle

Minimal-sulfite seaside white wine from northern Spain or coastal Portugal with salinity, lemon peel, and crushed oyster shell notes.

Artisanal Vermouth & High-Low Bar

Traditional European aperitifs and playful neighborhood favorites.

Spanish Vermut on Tap $9.00

House red vermut pulled cold from draft, served over an oversized hand-cut ice rock with a plump Spanish Gordal olive and fresh orange wheel.

The Spaghett $8.00

The high-low tavern icon: ice cold Miller High Life pony bottle doctored with Italian Aperol and fresh squeezed lemon juice.

Alpine Amaro Highball $11.00

Braulio or Amaro Nonino stirred with premium effervescent club soda, served tall with expressed lemon zest.

Underberg Digestif Service $5.00

The iconic 20ml herbal digestive bottle in traditional paper wrap, served cold with fresh citrus slice.

Conservas, Charcuterie & BYOF

Spanish tinned seafood, cured meats, and an open welcome to outside takeout.

Ramón Peña Galician Wild Sardines $14.00

Tender wild-caught Galician sardines hand-packed in olive oil, served in tin with salted European butter, saltines, and lemon wedge.

Matiz Spiced Mussels in Escabeche $12.00

Plump coastal Spanish mussels cured in tangy smoked paprika vinegar escabeche, accompanied by crackers and toothpicks.

Enoteca Charcuterie & Cheese Slate $18.00

Thinly sliced jamón serrano, calabrese salami, aged manchego cheese, pickled guindilla peppers, toasted nuts, and crackers on slate.

Marinated Spanish Gordal Olives $6.00

Jumbo buttery green olives tossed in cold-pressed olive oil, cracked coriander, orange zest, and chili flakes.

Bring Your Own Food (BYOF) $0.00

No outside food corkage fee. Pick up tacos, pizza, or noodles from neighboring Optimist Park / Belmont spots and dine right here.
