Flat-Top Comal Sear Technique

The Science of The Comal Sear

A "comal" is a smooth, heavy flat-top griddle used in authentic Mexican street cooking. Searing hand-pressed heirloom corn tortillas on a scorching comal locks in moisture, creates a blistered texture, and unleashes nutty corn aromas.

Sear Technique

The 3 Pillars of Comal Taco Craft

1. In-House Heirloom Masa Pressing

We press non-GMO blue and yellow corn masa dough into thin tortillas continuously throughout the day.

2. Scorching Flat-Top Temperature

Tortillas are tossed onto a 500°F metal comal plate to puff up in seconds, creating a soft pliable pouch.

3. High-Heat Meats & Salsas

Carne asada and pork carnitas are seared hot on the comal and paired with scratch salsa verde and salsa roja.
