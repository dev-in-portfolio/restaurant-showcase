## Original Audit Weakness

Comal Taco Co. relies on basic food hall directory pages (`comaltaco.com`, `optimisthall.com`). Located at 1115 N Brevard St inside Optimist Hall, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its flat-top comal tortilla searing technique, Arizona-style cheese crisp heritage, Optimist Hall food hall vibe, and Sonoran street food craft.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Optimist Hall Mill District Location:** 1115 N Brevard St, Ste 11, Charlotte NC 28206 (Optimist Hall).
2. **Executive Chef Andres Kaifer & Sonoran Heritage:** Hand-pressed heirloom corn tortillas cooked on a scorching hot flat-top comal grill.
3. **Signature Offerings:** Charcoal-seared carne asada, birria tacos with dipping consommé, Arizona-style Sonoran cheese crisps, house queso blanco, Mexican Coke, and hot churros.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Avant-Garde Mexican Display Typography:** Contemporary Mexican display sans (*Syne*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and street stall ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Scorched Comal Charcoal & Habanero Palette:** High-energy palette anchored in scorched comal charcoal (`#141110`), sun-dried habanero amber (`#E65100`), Sonoran terracotta (`#C84B31`), fresh cilantro lime (`#2E7D32`), and heirloom masa cream (`#FCF8F2`).
3. **"The Flat-Top Comal Sear & Sonoran Cheese Crisp Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`comal-tortilla-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing heirloom corn comal tacos alongside Arizona cheese crisps and sides.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Coffey Creek Café` — 1987 Southern diner with Lora/Outfit fonts.
2. `Club West Brewing` — Modern brewery taproom with Outfit/Plus Jakarta Sans fonts.
3. `Clark’s Snack Bar` — Retro 70s diner with Space Grotesk/Outfit fonts.

### Divergent Choices for Comal Taco Co.

- **Hero Composition:** Modern Sonoran Comal Sear & Optimist Hall Taco Vault split-hero layout: left side features an electric terra-cotta & comal char stamp badge (`"SONORAN COMAL TACOS • OPTIMIST HALL STE 11"`), avant-garde display typography (*Syne*), scorched charcoal & habanero palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of hand-pressed heirloom corn tacos with seared carne asada, birria consommé, and Sonoran cheese crisp.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Comal Vault Cards** (`comal-vault-card`) and **Optimist Hall Stall Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`comal-header`, `taco-brand`, `sear-hero-stage`, `masa-stamp-badge`, `comal-vault-card`, `comal-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
