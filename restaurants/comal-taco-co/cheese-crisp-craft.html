Arizona Sonoran Specialty

Sonoran Cheese Crisps & Birria Dip

Unique to Arizona and Sonoran Mexican culinary tradition, the "Cheese Crisp" is an open-faced flour tortilla brushed with butter and toasted ultra-crispy under high heat with melted Oaxaca cheese and diced roasted green chilis.

Cheese Crisp & Birria Highlights

Blistered Flour Tortilla Base

Large flour tortillas are toasted open-faced until the edges turn golden brown and shatteringly crisp.

Melted Oaxaca & Jack Cheese

Grated Mexican Oaxaca cheese melts smoothly over roasted Anaheim green chilis and scallions.

Chili Oil Birria Consommé

Our beef birria is simmered for 8 hours in guajillo broth; tortillas are dipped in chili oil before flat-top searing.
