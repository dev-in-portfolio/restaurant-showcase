# Design Brief: Matthews Social House

## Restaurant Identity & Positioning
- **Restaurant Name**: Matthews Social House
- **Cuisine & Concept**: Modern American Social House
- **Tagline**: Craft Cocktails, Small Plates & Community Vibes
- **Location**: 104 E Matthews St, Matthews, NC 28105

## Creative Art Direction
- **Named Direction**: Modern American Social House Bespoke Showcase
- **Typography Logic**: Display font `'Outfit', sans-serif` paired with `'Inter', sans-serif` for clean hierarchy and premium legibility.
- **Color Logic**: Primary accent `#3b82f6` with deep dark backdrop `#0b1329` providing strong contrast.
- **Signature Interaction**: Social Small-Plates Planner built directly into the home experience to guide visitor discovery.
