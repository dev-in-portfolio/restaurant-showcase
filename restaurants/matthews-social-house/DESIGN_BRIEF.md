# Creative Design Brief: Matthews Social House

## 1. Restaurant Identity & Intelligence
- **Name**: Matthews Social House
- **Cuisine**: Modern American Social House & Craft Cocktails
- **Tagline**: Craft Cocktails, Small Plates & Community Vibes
- **Location**: 104 E Matthews St, Matthews, NC 28105

## 2. Three Concept Directions Evaluated

### Concept A: Historic Downtown Social Lounge (SELECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Bespoke Modern American Social House & Craft Cocktails aesthetics. Primary accent `#3b82f6`, deep background `#0b1329`.
- **Typography**: Display font `'Outfit', sans-serif` paired with `'Inter', sans-serif`.
- **Signature Feature**: Social Small-Plates & Cocktail Planner.
- **Header Strategy**: Brand badge + adaptive navigation drawer activating at 1024px to prevent horizontal crowding.

### Concept B: Contemporary American Bistro (REJECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Rejected for lack of distinct regional identity.

### Concept C: Neighborhood Tap & Small Plates (REJECTED)
- **Visual Tone**: Rejected as too generic for Matthews Social House.

## 3. Art Direction Rationale
- **Color Logic**: Accent `#3b82f6` for callouts with `#0b1329` background.
- **Typography Logic**: Hierarchy using `'Outfit', sans-serif` for titles and `'Inter', sans-serif` for legibility.
- **Responsive Navigation**: Adaptive breakpoint at 1024px tested across 1440px to 360px with zero overflow.
