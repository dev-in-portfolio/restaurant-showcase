## Original Audit Weakness

Hickory & Heart relies on basic third-party online ordering portals and shopping center directory listings. Located at 10822 Providence Rd in South Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its low-and-slow hickory smokehouse craft, custom pit-master smoking process, family catering packs, and community roots.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Promenade on Providence Location:** 10822 Providence Road, Suite 900, Charlotte NC 28277 (Ballantyne / South Charlotte).
2. **Slow-Smoked Southern BBQ Specialties:** Pit-Smoked Texas Beef Brisket, Hickory Pulled Pork, St. Louis Cut Pork Ribs, Smoked Turkey Breast, Fried Okra, & Jalapeño Mac & Cheese.
3. **Hours of Operation:** Tue-Sun 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Monday Closed).

### Core Design Moves

1. **Heavy Slab Serif Display Typography:** Heavy slab serif (*BioRhyme*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and pit master mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Hickory Charcoal & Smoked Amber Palette:** High-warmth pitmaster palette anchored in hickory charcoal (`#16120E`), smoked amber (`#D97706`), campfire rust red (`#B91C1C`), pitmaster copper (`#CA8A04`), and oat parchment (`#FAF6F0`).
3. **"The Pitmaster & Low-and-Slow Smoke Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`pitmaster-hickory-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing 14-hour hickory smoked brisket & pulled pork alongside Southern scratch sides & family feast platters.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Henrietta’s` — High-End Bistro with Bodoni Moda fonts and Midnight Navy/Champagne Gold.
2. `Halal Street Food` — NYC Halal Cart with Outfit fonts and Cart Onyx/Canary Yellow.
3. `Great Wall of China South` — Classic Cantonese Wok with Playfair Display fonts and Jade Onyx/Crimson Red.

### Divergent Choices for Hickory & Heart

- **Hero Composition:** Rustic Hickory Smokehouse & Southern Pitmaster Vault split-hero layout: left side features a smoked amber stamp badge (`"HICKORY SMOKEHOUSE • PROMENADE ON PROVIDENCE"`), heavy slab serif (*BioRhyme*), hickory charcoal & smoked amber palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of smoked brisket, pulled pork, & fried okra.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **HH Vault Cards** (`hh-vault-card`) and Promenade on Providence smokehouse highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`hh-header`, `hh-brand`, `smoke-hero-stage`, `hickory-stamp-badge`, `hh-vault-card`, `hh-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
