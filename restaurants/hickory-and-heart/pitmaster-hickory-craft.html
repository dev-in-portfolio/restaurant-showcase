🔁 100% Hickory Hardwood Log Smoke

The Science of Hickory & Fire

Real Southern barbecue requires patience, clean burning logs, and constant pit vigilance. At Hickory & Heart, our pitmasters burn seasoned hickory hardwood logs overnight, holding pit temps strictly between 225°F and 250°F.

Smokehouse Science

The 3 Pillars of Hickory Pitmaster Craft

1. Clean Blue Hickory Smoke

We burn cured hickory logs until sweet, translucent blue smoke envelopes the meat without harsh creosote bitterness.

2. Coarse Rub & Dark Bark

Hand-rubbed 16-mesh black pepper & sea salt rendered into a thick, crunchy mahogany bark over 14 hours of smoking.

3. Deep Pink Smoke Rings

Nitrogen dioxide from wood combustion reacts with myoglobin in the meat, creating our signature 1/4-inch ruby smoke ring.
