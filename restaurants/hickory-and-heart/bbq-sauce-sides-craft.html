Carolina BBQ Traditions

The 3 Regional Carolina BBQ Sauces

1. Western NC Sweet Tomato

Rich tomato base simmered with dark molasses, apple cider vinegar, brown sugar, & warm spices—perfect for brisket.

2. Eastern NC Vinegar Pepper

Tangy cider vinegar steeped with crushed red pepper flakes, black pepper, & sea salt—the ultimate match for pulled pork.

3. South Carolina Gold Mustard

Savory yellow mustard reduction blended with honey, apple vinegar, & smoked paprika for zesty tangy heat.
