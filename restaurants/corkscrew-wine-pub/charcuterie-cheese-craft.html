Artisan Charcuterie

The perfect wine companion is a thoughtfully arranged charcuterie board. At Corkscrew Wine Pub, we slice Prosciutto di Parma & Spanish chorizo to order, paired with farmstead cheeses, raw North Carolina honeycomb, & warm bakery crostini.

The 3 Pillars of Artisan Charcuterie Boards

1. Hand-Sliced Salumi & Cured Meats

Prosciutto di Parma aged 18 months, Spanish chorizo picante, & peppered Genoa salami sliced thin to melt in your mouth.

2. Farmstead & Import Cheeses

Aged Dutch Gouda, Spanish Manchego, French double-cream Brie, & creamy Vermont goat cheese wheels.

3. Sweet & Savory Accoutrements

Raw honeycomb, French fig jam, pickled cornichons, marcona almonds, & warm sea salt olive oil crostini.
