Wine Science

The 3 Pillars of Sommelier Flight Pairing

1. Terroir-Driven Selection

We select wines that reflect their origin soil, elevation, & climate—from volcanic Sicilian reds to coastal Oregon Pinot Noir.

2. Argon Gas Preservation

Every open bottle is preserved with inert argon gas, ensuring every glass poured tastes as fresh as a newly pulled cork.

3. Interactive Flight Notes

Every flight includes custom tasting note cards guiding you through aromas, acidity, tannins, & food pairings.
