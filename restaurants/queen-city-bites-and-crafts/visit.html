Pedestrian & Parking Guidance

Pedestrian Walkway: Brevard Court is a pedestrian-only brick arcade connecting S Tryon St and S Church St across from Romare Bearden Park.

Parking: Convenient paid parking available in the 300 S Tryon garage and Truist Center parking deck.
