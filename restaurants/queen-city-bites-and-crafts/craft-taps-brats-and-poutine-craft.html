Beer in Hand, Artisan Fare.

Great beer deserves exceptional food. At QCBC, our 20 rotating draft lines are curated weekly from the finest breweries across North Carolina and beyond, paired with pork bratwursts simmered in local IPA and authentic Canadian-style cheese curd poutine.

Culinary Mastery

The 3 Pillars of QCBC Craft & Fare

1. The 20-Tap Rotation

Maintaining 20 fresh keg lines with a strict cold chain, ensuring peak hop aroma and carbonation for every pour.

2. Beer-Braised Sausages

Simmering premium artisanal bratwursts in local craft ale with sweet onions before crisping over high-heat cast iron.

3. Real Quebec Poutine

Hand-cutting fresh potatoes, frying twice for crunch, and topping with squeaky white cheddar curds and piping hot beef gravy.
