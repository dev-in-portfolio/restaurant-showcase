🍔 Lacey Edge Smash Burgers & Cobblestone Patio

Crispy Edges, Alleyway Vibe.

Brevard Court is one of Uptown Charlotte's rare architectural treasures. Dine al fresco along the historic pedestrian cobblestone arcade enjoying lacey-edged certified Angus smash burgers, loaded fries, and ice-cold craft pints just steps from Romare Bearden Park and Truist Field.

The 3 Pillars of QCBC Burgers & Patio

1. The Smashed Crust

Pressing Angus beef patties paper-thin on 450° griddles to develop deeply caramelized, savory lacey edges while locking in natural juiciness.

2. Cobblestone Patio Dining

Open-air tables lining the historic pedestrian walkway of Brevard Court under warm festoon lights away from automotive traffic.

3. Uptown Gameday Energy

Prime pre-game gathering destination for Charlotte FC matches, Knights baseball games, Panthers tailgates, and stadium concerts.
