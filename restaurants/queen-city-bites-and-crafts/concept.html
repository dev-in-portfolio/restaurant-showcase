🍺 The QCBC Story

Historic Court, Queen City Heart.

Queen City Bites & Crafts was established to celebrate Charlotte's independent craft brewing renaissance in the heart of its most historic pedestrian walkway. Nestled at 135 Brevard Court, our taproom preserves the European cobblestone charm of the 1920s while pouring the best modern brews in North Carolina.

Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of Queen City Bites & Crafts

Historic Preservation

Honoring the rich architectural heritage of 1920s Brevard Court as a welcoming pedestrian retreat in Center City.

North Carolina Craft

Championing independent Tar Heel brewers by rotating seasonal kegs and educating guests on regional craft styles.

Unpretentious Tavern Spirit

Creating an inclusive, friendly neighborhood pub where locals, sports fans, and travelers gather as friends.
