Directions to Plaza

How To Find Nirvana II

  • From S. Tryon Street: Enter Two Wells Fargo Center at 401 S Tryon St and take the central glass escalators or elevators down to the lower plaza atrium.
  • From Overstreet Mall: Walk through the enclosed skybridge into Two Wells Fargo and take the escalator down to the food court level.
  • Corporate Thali Catering: We package individual hot thali boxes (biryani, 2 curries, naan, and raita) for office meetings across Uptown Charlotte.
