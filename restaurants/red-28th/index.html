Charlotte's Original Multicultural Sanctuary

Words, Warmth & Artisanal Smoke.

Founded on the timeless spirit of Harlem Renaissance living-room salons, Red@28th is Charlotte's original multicultural literary lounge and hookah bar. Now anchored in University City at 3509 David Cox Road, we bring together floor-to-ceiling books, glowing coconut charcoals, craft libations, and scratch comfort kitchen plates in a warm, welcoming community haven.

Craft Charcoal & Precision Packing

Pure Heat, Smooth Billowing Clouds

We reject synthetic quick-lighting chemical tablets. At Red@28th, every hookah is individually built using premium grade French, German, and Middle Eastern shisha blends, heated with glowing natural coconut shell cubes, and managed with dedicated tongs to deliver rich, cool, unburned flavor for up to two hours.
