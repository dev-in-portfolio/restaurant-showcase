Admission Policy

Age & ID Requirements

Red@28th welcomes all patrons during early evening hours. After 8:00 PM, entry is strictly 21+. All guests must present a valid, physical government-issued photo ID (Driver's License, State ID, Military ID, or Passport). Digital photos not accepted.

Weekly Programming

Spoken Word, Soul & Game Nights

Our stage is dedicated to creative expression and community joy throughout the week:

  • Wednesday Open Mic Poetry: Charlotte's premier spoken word circle hosted by resident poets and acoustic musicians. Signups begin at 7:30 PM.
  • Friday R&B & Neo-Soul Nights: Feel-good soundtrack spun by guest DJs blending 90s slow jams, contemporary R&B, and Afrobeat rhythms.
  • Sunday Board Game & Chill Sessions: Spades tournaments, chess matches, Scrabble, and acoustic soul with happy hour hookah pricing.
Amenities & Access

Outdoor Patio & Convenient Parking

Enjoy open-air relaxation and stress-free arrival at our University City space:

  • Spacious Outdoor Patio: Weather-protected patio equipped with lounge furniture and string lights for outdoor hookah smoking in mild weather.
  • Free Dedicated Surface Parking: Ample well-lit free parking directly in front of the venue on David Cox Road. No valet fees or parking garage hassles.
  • Group Accommodations: Large sectional booth seating and tables available for birthdays, book clubs, and creative team gatherings.
