Precision Shisha Standards

Pure Coconut Coals, Velvet Clouds.

Hookah preparation at Red@28th is elevated to an art form. We deliberately shun synthetic quick-lighting coals that taint tobacco flavor with chemical fumes. Instead, every pipe is engineered with 100% natural compressed coconut shell cubes, precision heat management, and fresh fruit bowls.

Craftsmanship & Purity

The Red@28th Hookah Standard

Designed for long-lasting, flavorful smoke sessions that never burn harsh or leave an unpleasant chemical aftertaste.

Pillar 01

100% Natural Coconut Charcoal

Manufactured from compressed Indonesian coconut shells with zero sulfur, nitrates, or chemical accelerants. Burns completely odorless and tasteless at a steady, consistent temperature that vaporizes shisha molasses without scorching the tobacco.

Zero Chemical Accelerants
Pillar 02

Hand-Carved Fresh Fruit Heads

Elevate your session by substituting traditional clay bowls with hand-carved fresh fruit. Whole pineapples, grapefruits, or watermelons are hollowed to hold the shisha, naturally saturating every pull with real organic fruit nectar and natural aromatic oils.

Pineapple • Grapefruit • Melon
Pillar 03

Sub-Zero Ice Cooling Chambers

Equipped with specialized freezable ice-pack hose handles and crushed ice bases. As the vapor passes through sub-zero chambers, it cools dramatically, condensing into ultra-dense, silky smooth clouds that are gentle on the throat.

Sub-Zero Condensed Vapor
Social Connection

The Communal Circle of Conversation

In traditional Middle Eastern and North African cultures, hookah is never rushed; it is an invitation to slow down, share stories, and connect deeply with friends. At Red@28th, we pair this historic practice with the literary salon traditions of the Harlem Renaissance—encouraging lively discourse, poetry, board games, and shared laughter around the glow of the coals.
