Scratch Soul Cooking & Mixology

Crisp Wings, Soulful Plates.

Unlike conventional hookah lounges that offer thawed frozen snacks, Red@28th operates an authentic scratch comfort kitchen. From whole double-fried jumbo chicken wings spun in our house-simmered honey hot glaze to Angus beef sliders and literary-themed craft cocktails, our food is designed to feed both stomach and soul.

Culinary Standards

Crafted For Sharing & Savoring

Every dish is cooked fresh to order using premium ingredients, bold Southern seasoning, and house-made sauces.

Wing Craft

Double-Fried Jumbo Wings

Fresh, never-frozen jumbo party wings brined for tenderness, dredged in seasoned flour, and double-fried for maximum crunch. Spun in our house-simmered Honey Hot, Lemon Pepper Garlic Butter, Sweet Chili, or Bourbon BBQ.

Comfort Sides

Loaded Red Waffle Fries

Thick-cut golden waffle fries dusted in our signature smoky paprika rub, smothered in bubbling melted cheddar-jack cheese, crispy smoked applewood bacon bits, scallions, and drizzled with spicy chipotle ranch.

Griddle Sliders

Certified Angus Beef Sliders

Three smashed Certified Angus Beef patties seared with sweet caramelized onions on toasted buttered brioche buns, melted sharp cheddar, crunchy kosher pickles, and our signature tangy red lounge remoulade.

Literary Cocktails

Libations Named for Literary Legends

Our beverage program pays tribute to the titans of African American literature and poetry. Enjoy the Langston Hughes Spiked Lemonade (blackberry-infused vodka and fresh southern lemonade), the bold Baldwin's Old Fashioned (bourbon with walnut bitters and brown sugar), or Zora's Tropical Rum Punch featuring dark Caribbean rums and exotic fruit juices.
