Good To Know

Guest Guidelines & Policies

No reservations are needed or taken. As a 1,700 sq ft neighborhood cocktail den, all seating is open walk-in style.

No food is served at All Purpose Bar. We encourage guests to eat at neighboring restaurants in NoDa before or after their visit.

We feature an equal-footing Zero-Proof menu crafted with distilled botanicals, shrubs, and custom mixers by Larry Suggs.
