Story & Vision

An East Coast Cocktail Den

Occupying 1,700 square feet on North Davidson Street, All Purpose Bar was built to combine classic East Coast tavern character with contemporary Southern hospitality.

Hospitality Roots

Founded by Larry Suggs

After years of shaping cocktail programs across Charlotte, mixologist Larry Suggs created All Purpose Bar as a place where high-level liquid craft meets approachable neighborhood culture.

The Space

Located in the former Protagonist Beer space in NoDa, transformed into an intimate 1,700 sq ft cocktail sanctuary with low lighting and warm wood finishes.

The Ethos

No pretense, no dress codes, no velvet ropes. Just expert bartenders who love making great drinks for great people.

The Community

Designed as a staple for NoDa locals and Charlotte cocktail enthusiasts alike, offering a cozy evening spot Wednesday through Sunday.
