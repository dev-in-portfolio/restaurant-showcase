Neighborhood Integration

The NoDa Night Out

We focus 100% on drinks. Because we have no kitchen in-house, we encourage our guests to enjoy NoDa's incredible food scene before or after visiting us for cocktails.

How To Experience APB

Crafting Your Evening Itinerary

1. Pre-Dinner Aperitif

Stop by right when we open at 6:00 PM for a crisp Zero-Proof Spritz or highball to stimulate your palate before dinner down North Davidson Street.

2. Dine in the Arts District

Explore NoDa's eclectic dining options—from wood-fired pizza and tacos to fine Southern dining—just steps from our front door.

3. Post-Dinner Nightcap

Return for a spirit-forward Old Fashioned or digestif in our low-lit, cozy atmosphere to wind down your evening until midnight.
