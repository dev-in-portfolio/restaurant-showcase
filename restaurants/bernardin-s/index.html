An Historic Setting

THE RATCLIFFE & THE GREEN

Housed on the street level of the storied Ratcliffe building at 435 South Tryon Street, Bernardin's offers one of the most picturesque dining settings in the Carolinas.

Our garden terrace gazes directly out onto The Green—Uptown's literary-themed urban park dotted with bronze statues, sculpted fountains, and shaded oak canopies. Inside, crisp white linens, soft ambient candlelight, and quiet banquettes set the stage for romantic evenings, executive deal-making, and memorable milestones.
