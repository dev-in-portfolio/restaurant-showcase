Landmark Heritage

THE HISTORIC RATCLIFFE

Originally established in the early 20th century as the renowned Ratcliffe's Flowers florist shop, this landmark building at 435 South Tryon Street has long stood as a symbol of Charlotte sophistication.

When the property was preserved and integrated into the modern Ratcliffe boutique tower, Bernardin's made this storied address its home. Today, we continue that heritage by offering an atmosphere of serene grandeur directly overlooking the whimsical brick walkways and fountains of The Green.

Our culinary team approaches every service as a performance of precision—balancing classical French technique, exotic Asian seasonings, and the freshest American farm and ocean bounties.

White Glove Hospitality

Our service captains and sommeliers anticipate every need with discreet elegance, providing an environment where guests can relax completely and focus on their companions.

Global Wine Cellar

Our cellar houses over 300 selections from legendary estates in Bordeaux, Burgundy, Tuscany, Napa Valley, and Rioja, alongside rare boutique small-producer vintages.

The Green Garden Terrace

Dine alfresco under the stars or enjoy a sunny lunch on our private stone terrace directly overlooking the lush manicured lawns and sculptures of The Green park.
