Transit & Parking

Abundant Free Arboretum Parking

Visiting Roz Grill offers one of the easiest dining arrival experiences in South Charlotte:

  • Sprawling Surface Parking: Hundreds of complimentary surface parking stalls located directly outside our storefront. No garages, paid meters, or validation steps.
  • Major Intersection: Located at the junction of Providence Road (NC-16) and Pineville-Matthews Road (NC-51), easily reachable from I-485 exit 57.
  • Curbside Quick Pickup: Designated 15-minute quick-pickup parking bays directly in front of the center for takeout customers.
Large Gatherings & Parties

Family Feasts & Office Catering

Feeding a corporate team, family celebration, or game-day party? Roz Grill provides large-format Mediterranean catering:

  • Kebab Party Trays: Platters of grilled lamb, shish tawook chicken, and kofta over double saffron basmati rice with warm pita.
  • Mezze Sampler Platters: Large bowls of silky hummus, baba ghanoush, muhammara, and fresh pita bread.
  • Direct Orders: Call our team at (704) 910-1936 24 hours in advance for large orders.
