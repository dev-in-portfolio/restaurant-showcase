Cultural Heritage & Vision

A Gathering Place For Genuine Flavor.

Roz Grill was founded with a singular purpose: to bring the authentic taste of Mediterranean and Middle Eastern family dining to South Charlotte. We believe that true hospitality begins with honest food—prepared from scratch, served with warmth, and rooted in culinary traditions that have brought communities together for centuries.

Core Values

The Four Pillars of Roz Grill

Guided by family warmth, culinary authenticity, and the generous spirit of Mediterranean culture.

Pillar 01

Authenticity Over Shortcuts

We never buy canned dips, frozen falafel dough, or pre-cooked meats. We soak dried chickpeas, roast fresh peppers, whip garlic daily, and bake pita fresh throughout service.

Pillar 02

Certified Halal Integrity

We respect our Muslim guests and dietary commitments with 100% certified halal sourcing across all beef, lamb, and poultry. Everyone can dine with complete confidence and peace of mind.

Pillar 03

Community Hospitality

From our open dining room at The Arboretum to large catered corporate lunches across Ballantyne and SouthPark, we treat every customer like an honored guest in our own home.

A South Charlotte Staple

Proudly Rooted In The Arboretum

Situated at 8200 Providence Road, Roz Grill serves neighbors from across South Charlotte, Matthews, Weddington, and Waxhaw. Whether stopping in for a quick fresh-pressed juice and falafel wrap or gathering the entire family for a weekend sultan kebab feast, we are honored to be your neighborhood Mediterranean kitchen.
