Vegetarian Heritage

Crispy Sesame Falafel & House Dips

Our falafel begins with dried chickpeas soaked overnight, coarsely ground with fresh cilantro, parsley, toasted cumin, and coriander. Formed by hand, coated in golden sesame seeds, and flash-fried until shatteringly crisp on the outside and tender green inside. Served with creamy house tahini and pickled garden vegetables.

Pastry Artistry

Handmade Baklava & Golden Kataifi

Paper-thin sheets of filo pastry layered with clarified butter, filled with freshly crushed Antep pistachios and roasted walnuts, baked until crisp and steeped in fragrant orange blossom honey syrup. Alongside our spun-dough kataifi nests, it delivers a sweet, golden conclusion to your meal.
