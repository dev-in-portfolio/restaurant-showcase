Mastery In Fire

The Four Disciplines of Our Grill

Every skewer reflects traditional technique, precise heat control, and unhurried craftsmanship.

Discipline 01

100% Certified Halal

Every ounce of beef, lamb, and chicken served at Roz Grill is certified hand-slaughtered zabiha halal, ensuring the highest ethical standards, clean processing, and exceptional meat quality.

Discipline 02

24-Hour Yogurt Marinade

Our chicken and lamb rest for a full day in cultured Greek yogurt, cold-pressed lemon juice, fresh garlic, sumac, coriander, and Aleppo chili. The lactic acid gently tenderizes the muscle fibers without breaking down texture.

Discipline 03

Natural Hardwood Charcoals

We refuse gas burners or lava rocks. Our custom skewer trough burns natural oak and lump hardwood coals, producing intense 700-degree radiant heat that sears the exterior while locking in natural savory juices.

Side Dish Perfection

Fragrant Saffron Basmati Rice & Toum

A great kebab requires worthy accompaniments. Our long-grain aged basmati rice is gently toasted in clarified butter, steeped in saffron broth, and infused with cardamom pods until each grain remains separate and fragrant. Served alongside our scratch-whipped Lebanese garlic dip (toum) for a complete feast.
