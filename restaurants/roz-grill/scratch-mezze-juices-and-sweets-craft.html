The Craft of Mezze, Juices & Sweets

Handmade daily without preservatives, artificial extracts, or corn syrup.

Craft 01

Stone-Ground Mezze Dips

Our hummus starts with heirloom chickpeas simmered with baking soda for tender skins, pureed hot with pure sesame tahini and lemon juice. Our muhammara combines roasted sweet peppers, toasted walnuts, and tart pomegranate molasses.

Craft 02

Fresh Cold-Pressed Juices

Our dedicated juice bar presses whole California pomegranates and Florida oranges to order. Our Middle Eastern Limonana blends freshly squeezed lemons with whole spearmint leaves for an ultra-refreshing herbal tonic.

Craft 03

Traditional Honey Baklava

Handcrafted filo pastry brushed sheet-by-sheet with clarified sweet cream butter, packed with finely crushed Turkish Antep pistachios and roasted walnuts, then drenched in warm orange blossom water honey syrup.

The Mediterranean Table

Sharing The Feast With Family

In Middle Eastern culture, meals are social tapestries meant to be shared generously. A full table of warm pita, contrasting cold dips, sizzling hot skewers, and crisp sweet pastries brings people together across cultures and generations. At Roz Grill, we welcome your family to our table.
