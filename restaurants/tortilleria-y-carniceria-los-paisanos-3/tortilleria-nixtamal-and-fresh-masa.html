The Art of Nixtamalization

Honoring the ancient Mesoamerican craft of steeping whole corn kernels in mineral lime and stone-milling fresh dough every morning.

Ancestral Grain Milling

Real Corn, Volcanic Stones, Zero Shortcuts

Most commercial tortillas in modern supermarkets are made from dehydrated industrial corn flour. At Tortilleria Los Paisanos 3, we preserve the ancestral 3,500-year-old nixtamal tradition.

Every afternoon, whole non-GMO dried Mexican corn kernels are steeped in water and natural calcium hydroxide (cal) overnight. This softens the outer pericarp and unlocks essential nutrients like niacin and calcium, producing incomparable natural sweetness and aroma.

  • Milled between hand-carved volcanic volcanic stones (piedras volc?nicas)
  • Pressed and baked in our high-temperature gas oven lines
  • Packed hot in thermal paper by the pound and kilo all day long
