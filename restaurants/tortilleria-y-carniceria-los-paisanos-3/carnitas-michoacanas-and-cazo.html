The Michoac?n Copper Cazo

Pork cuts gently confited in bubbling pure lard, Seville oranges, and secret family seasonings.

Quiroga Michoac?n Style

Simmered Low & Slow for Unmatched Tenderness

Originating in the legendary carnitas capital of Quiroga, Michoac?n, authentic carnitas require heavy hammered copper cazos that distribute heat evenly without scorching the delicate meats.

We submerge whole pork shoulders, rib racks, pork belly, and skin into pure rendered lard seasoned with halved oranges, Mexican Coca-Cola, sea salt, bay leaves, and Mexican cinnamon. Over four hours of gentle simmering, the water evaporates and the exterior caramelizes while the interior remains intensely succulent.

  • Maciza: Premium lean pork shoulder and loin, fork-tender
  • Costilla: Melt-in-your-mouth bone-in rib meat
  • Cuerito: Silky, savory braised pork skin
