Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our tortilleria, meat counter, and hot food station.

Can I buy fresh masa for making tamales at home? +

Yes! We sell fresh stone-ground corn masa prepared specifically for tamales (masa preparada with lard and seasoning, or masa natural unseasoned) by the pound, especially popular during holiday seasons.

How are carnitas sold at Los Paisanos 3? +

You can purchase carnitas by the pound from our hot display case (choose Maciza lean meat, Costilla rib meat, Cuerito skin, or Surtida mixed), served with complimentary hot tortillas, salsa verde, salsa roja, and limes.

Do you have special items on weekends? +

Yes, every Saturday and Sunday morning we prepare traditional Barbacoa de Borrego (slow-roasted lamb), Consom? de Barbacoa, and Menudo Rojo (honeycomb tripe soup) with freshly hand-patted tortillas.
