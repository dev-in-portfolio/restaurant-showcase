Hand-Pressed Masa, Fire-Charred Meats.

Every dish at Three Amigos honors the rich culinary heritage of interior Mexico. Made with fresh ingredients, house-roasted dried chilies, melted Chihuahua cheeses, and fresh-squeezed lime, our recipes offer deep, honest flavor for lunch and dinner.

Aperitivos & Tableside Preparations

House-made starters served with freshly fried crispy tortilla chips.

Tableside Fresh Guacamole $11.50

Fresh Hass avocados hand-mashed in a traditional volcanic stone molcajete with lime juice, sea salt, diced jalapeño, cilantro, and tomato, served with house tortilla chips.

Queso Fundido con Chorizo $10.50

Melted Chihuahua and Oaxaca cheeses baked with house-made spiced Mexican chorizo sausage, served bubbling hot with warm corn tortillas.

Sopes Tradicionales (3) $12.00

Three thick handmade corn masa boats topped with refried pinto beans, shredded chicken tinga or carnitas, crisp shredded lettuce, crema fresca, and queso cotija.

Ceviche de Camarón Estilo Nayarit $13.50

Wild gulf shrimp marinated in fresh key lime juice with diced cucumber, red onion, serrano chiles, and cilantro, served with avocado slices and crisp tostadas.

Especialidades de la Parrilla & Carnes

Char-grilled steak cuts and slow-braised meats served with rice, beans, and tortillas.

Carne Asada a la Parrilla $19.50

Marinated skirt steak char-grilled over open flames, blistered whole scallions (cebollitas), Mexican red rice, refried pinto beans with cheese, fresh avocado slices, and warm corn tortillas.

Bistec a la Mexicana $18.00

Tender sliced sirloin steak sautéed with fresh plum tomatoes, serrano peppers, onions, and fresh cilantro simmered in savory pan drippings with rice and beans.

Carnitas Michoacán Tradicionales $17.50

Slow-braised pork shoulder with citrus, Mexican cinnamon, and Mexican oregano, crisped to order, served with pickled red onions, jalapeños, and warm tortillas.

Sizzling Fajitas Mixtas $21.00

Marinated flank steak, tender chicken breast, and wild gulf shrimp grilled with sweet bell peppers and onions, served with guacamole, sour cream, and pico de gallo.

Enchiladas & Moles Auténticos

Hand-rolled corn tortillas smothered in authentic regional Mexican sauces.

Traditional Enchiladas Suizas $15.50

Three corn tortillas rolled with seasoned chicken tinga, smothered in tangy roasted tomatillo cream sauce, covered in melted Chihuahua cheese, sweet white onions, and cilantro.

Enchiladas de Mole Poblano $16.50

Soft corn tortillas filled with shredded chicken, drowned in complex, velvety 24-ingredient house mole sauce, sprinkled with toasted sesame seeds and queso fresco.

Enchiladas Rojas de la Casa $14.50

Rolled tortillas with seasoned ground beef or queso fresco, smothered in guajillo-ancho chili sauce and melted cheese with Mexican crema.

Enchiladas Verdes con Carnitas $15.50

Three rolled tortillas filled with slow-cooked carnitas, smothered in spicy salsa verde, raw diced onion, and melted Oaxaca cheese.
