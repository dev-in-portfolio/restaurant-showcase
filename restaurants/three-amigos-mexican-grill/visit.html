Arrival Directives

On-Site Parking on Central Avenue

Visiting Three Amigos is effortless with convenient transit and parking options:

  • Free On-Site Surface Lot: Dedicated parking is located directly in front of Suite A along Central Avenue, with additional spaces adjacent to the building.
  • Neighborhood Access: Easily accessible from Uptown Charlotte, Elizabeth, Plaza Midwood, and East Charlotte via Central Avenue.
  • CATS Bus Transit: Conveniently served by the Route 9 Central Avenue bus line with stops nearby.
