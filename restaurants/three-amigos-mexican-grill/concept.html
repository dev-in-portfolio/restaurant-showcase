Plaza Midwood Charlotte • Established 2010

A Central Avenue Legacy of Mexican Flavor.

When Three Amigos Mexican Grill opened its doors on Central Avenue in 2010, the mission was simple: bring authentic interior Mexican dishes and warm cantina hospitality to Charlotte. While much of the city was accustomed to commercial Tex-Mex combo plates, Three Amigos introduced diners to the nuanced flavors of authentic moles, fire-charred carne asada, and freshly mashed volcanic stone guacamole.

Core Values

The Values Guiding Our Cocina & Cantina

Authenticity, scratch craftsmanship, and enduring neighborhood roots.

Value 01

Interior Regional Integrity

We remain loyal to traditional cooking traditions from Michoacán, Puebla, and Jalisco, prioritizing deep marinades and roasted chiles over shortcuts.

Value 02

Scratch Craft Every Morning

From our morning salsa simmering to hand-pressing corn tortillas and slow-braising pork carnitas, our culinary team builds flavor with genuine patience.

Value 03

Plaza Midwood Community Anchor

For more than 15 years, our colorful dining room has served as a meeting ground for Charlotte neighbors, artists, families, and long-time patrons.

Hospitality Tradition

Warmth, Family & Generous Plates

At Three Amigos, we believe great food should always be accompanied by genuine warmth. Whether you are grabbing a quick weekday lunch of street tacos or gathering with friends on Friday night over sizzling fajitas and margaritas, our team treats you like family from the moment you walk through our doors.
