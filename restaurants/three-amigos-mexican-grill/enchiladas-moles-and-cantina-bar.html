The Sauce Kitchen

The Alchemy of Mexican Sauces

Slow-simmered, hand-ground, and simmered with patience.

Pillar 01

Enchiladas Suizas Heritage

Born in historical Mexico City cafes, the dish pairs tart roasted green tomatillos and fresh serrano chilis with rich Mexican crema and melted Chihuahua cheese over soft corn tortillas.

Pillar 02

Hand-Crafted Mole Poblano

Our house mole combines toasted mulato and ancho chiles, Mexican dark chocolate, plantains, almonds, sesame seeds, and cinnamon, simmered for eight hours into a complex, savory masterpiece.

Pillar 03

Pure Agave Cantina Bar

We never compromise with commercial syrups or mixto tequilas. Every cocktail features 100% blue agave spirits, freshly squeezed Persian lime juice, and natural sweeteners.

Agave Excellence

Fresh-Pressed Lime & Reposado Tequila

Our cantina bartenders shake every cocktail to order with ice, straining into chilled glassware rimmed with sea salt or tajín. Enjoy our classic House Margarita, a smoky Oaxacan Mezcalita, or a refreshing Ruby Red Grapefruit Paloma alongside your favorite enchiladas or tacos.
