Open-Flame Grilling • Mexican Asador Tradition

Flames, Citrus & The True Asador.

In Mexican culinary tradition, the parrilla is more than just a grill—it is the hearth where seasoned beef cuts are transformed by fire, smoke, and citrus marinades. At Three Amigos on Central Avenue, our Carne Asada and steak specialties are hand-trimmed, marinated in sour orange and crushed garlic, and seared over blistering heat.

The Parrilla Standard

Crafting Authentic Mexican Steaks

Time-tested seasoning, extreme griddle heat, and classic accompaniments.

Standard 01

Citrus & Garlic Adobo

Every cut of skirt steak is marinated for twelve hours in Seville orange juice, fresh lime, whole garlic cloves, ground cumin, and Mexican oregano to break down muscle fibers and impart deep flavor.

Standard 02

Blistered Cebollitas

We toss whole Mexican green spring onions directly onto the scorching cast iron grate alongside the meat, caramelizing the stems until sweet, tender, and lightly charred.

Standard 03

Scratch Pinto Frijoles

Our refried beans are slow-cooked from dry pinto beans with rendered pork seasonings and whipped until silky, then topped with melted Chihuahua cheese just before serving.

Signature Platter

Bistec a la Mexicana

A beloved staple throughout Central and Southern Mexico, Bistec a la Mexicana features tender sirloin strips quickly sautéed with diced ripe plum tomatoes, sharp onions, and minced serrano peppers. The natural tomato juices reduce with meat drippings into a zesty pan sauce that is poured over seasoned Mexican red rice.
