Our Kitchen Heritage

Roots, Spice & Island Pride

Bringing the authentic taste and warmth of Jamaica to the heart of East Charlotte.

Born From Tradition

The Essence of Jamaican Cooking

At Island Flavors II, every single pot is treated with reverence. Authentic Jamaican cooking is not fast food—it is an art form rooted in time, patience, and uncompromised ingredients.

From our signature hardwood jerk smoker to our slow-simmered cast iron pots of curry goat and oxtail, we make every dish from scratch. We chop fresh scallions, grate whole ginger, crack open fresh scotch bonnet peppers, and blend authentic Jamaican pimento berries daily.

When you dine with us, you taste the genuine warmth, spice balance, and soul of home cooking that Caribbean families have cherished for generations.

01

48-Hour Marination

Our jerk chicken and pork soak in proprietary allspice and scotch bonnet marinades for two full days to infuse deep flavor all the way to the bone.

02

Slow-Simmered Gravies

Our oxtails and brown stew pots simmer slowly for four hours, melting natural collagen into rich, velvety gravies loaded with whole butter beans and herbs.

03

Fresh Island Juices

We brew our homemade sorrel ginger and tropical fruit punch from real hibiscus petals, fresh ginger root, and natural cane sugar daily.
