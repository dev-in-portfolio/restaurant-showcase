## Claim Ledger
- **Pit-Smoked Hardwood Jerk Chicken:** Marinated for 48 hours in scotch bonnet peppers, pimento berries (allspice), thyme, and scallions, then smoked over pimento wood and charcoal for authentic Jamaican jerk flavor.
- **Slow-Braised Oxtail with Butter Beans:** Simmered gently for four hours in rich savory browning gravy until gelatinous, fork-tender, and deeply seasoned.
- **Jamaican Breakfast Tradition:** Serving genuine Kingston morning classics including Ackee & Saltfish, Jamaican callaloo with salt mackerel or cod, and boiled green bananas, fried dumplings (festivals), and hard dough bread.
- **Jerk Salmon Rasta Pasta:** Penne pasta tossed in a creamy coconut parmesan sauce infused with jerk spices, tri-color bell peppers, and topped with pan-seared jerk salmon.
- **Party Catering & Family Feasts:** Comprehensive half-pan and full-pan catering configurations for family reunions, weddings, church events, and corporate luncheons.
