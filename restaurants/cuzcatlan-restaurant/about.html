Nuestra Historia

THE LAND OF PRECIOUS THINGS IN CHARLOTTE

In the indigenous Nahuat-Pipil language, Cuzcatlan (Kuskatan) means "The Place of Precious Jewels or Necklaces"—the ancient ancestral name for the territory that is now El Salvador.

Founded on Central Avenue in East Charlotte, Cuzcatlan Restaurant was born out of a profound passion to preserve authentic Salvadoran homestyle gastronomy. We refused to compromise with frozen shortcuts or industrial shortcuts. We cook exactly as our grandmothers did in San Salvador and Santa Ana.

From the rhythmic clapping of our pupuseras patting fresh masa to the slow simmer of our 12-hour weekend beef shank broths, every meal is prepared with genuine love and Central American hospitality.

Hecho a Mano (Handcrafted)

Our pupusas are never pre-formed or machine-pressed. Each one is hand-stuffed and sealed to order, creating the signature thin crust and molten cheesy center that defines true artisan pupuserias.

The Loroco & Curtido Secret

We source real imported Salvadoran loroco flower buds for our signature cheese pupusas, and naturally ferment our cabbage curtido in small batches daily with Mexican oregano and mild jalapeños.
