THE ART OF THE HANDMADE PUPUSA

At Cuzcatlan Restaurant, we honor the ancient Pipil heritage of Cuzcatlan (the Land of Precious Things). Every single pupusa is hand-patted by our skilled pupuseras right before your eyes, creating a delicate crispy exterior that gives way to a decadent molten center of cheese, seasoned pork, and fragrant loroco flowers.

  • ✓ Masa de Maiz (Nixtamalized Corn) & Masa de Arroz (Rice Flour) Options
  • ✓ Freshly fermented curtido cabbage slaw and warm house tomato relish
  • ✓ Sopa de Res and Sopa de Gallina India served fresh on weekends
  • ✓ Natural Horchata de Morro, Tamarindo, Maracuya, and Guanabana
