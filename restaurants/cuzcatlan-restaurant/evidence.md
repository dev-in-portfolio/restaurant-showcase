# Evidence Document: Cuzcatlan Restaurant

## Creative Brief
- **Business Name:** Cuzcatlan Restaurant (Restaurante Cuzcatlan)
- **Location:** 4800 Central Ave, Charlotte, NC 28205 (East Charlotte International Corridor)
- **Phone:** (704) 568-9161
- **Email:** cuzcatlanrestaurante@gmail.com
- **Hours of Operation:** Monday through Sunday: 9:00 AM - 10:00 PM (Daily 9am-10pm)
- **Concept & Vibe:** Authentic family-owned Salvadoran restaurant famous for freshly hand-patted pupusas (corn and rice masa), traditional yuca con chicharron, rich weekend sopas de res, platanos fritos with Salvadoran crema, and refreshing house-made aguas frescas.
- **Design Aesthetic:** Warm terracotta sun, deep Maya indigo blue, golden maize corn yellow, vibrant cilantro verde, and cream woven textures with modern bilingual clarity.

## Claim Ledger
- **Fact 1 (Address & Neighborhood):** 4800 Central Ave in East Charlotte, NC 28205.

- **Fact 5 (Traditional Beverages):** Horchata de morro salvadorena, tamarindo, jamaica, guanabana, maracuya, and Kolashanpan.

## Add-On Preservation
- **Interactive Pupusa Party Box Builder:** Real-time calculator allowing guests to configure custom boxes of 6, 12, 24, or 50 pupusas with masa choices, fillings, curtido, and salsa packages.
- **Interactive Pupusa Flavor & Masa Explorer:** Interactive visual cards detailing traditional fillings, flavor profiles, and loroco herb origins.
- **Natural Beverage Pairings Selector:** Flavor pairings guide for house-made aguas frescas.
- **Direct Action Channels:** Direct telephone link and direct email inquiry links without standard forms.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- **Palette Uniqueness:** Maya indigo blue (#103b74), terracotta sun (#d95d39), golden maize (#e09f29), cilantro green (#2d7a46), corn cream (#fdfbf7), and deep charcoal (#1f1c19).
- **Typography:** Modern organic display 'Outfit' combined with clean readable 'DM Sans', contrasting sharply against the tavern slab serif and fine-dining fonts of earlier demos.
- **Geometry & Styling:** Dual-tone headers, rounded organic cards (12px and 20px radius), bilingual Spanish/English subtitles, and Latin textile border highlights.
