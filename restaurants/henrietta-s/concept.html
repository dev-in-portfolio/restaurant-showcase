LoSo Roots & Modern Charm

Henrietta’s was born from a desire to create a true neighborhood bistro in Charlotte's thriving Lower South End. Located at 117 Southside Drive adjacent to Rally, our space marries warm hospitality, refined craftsmanship, and unforgettable hearth-cooked meals.
