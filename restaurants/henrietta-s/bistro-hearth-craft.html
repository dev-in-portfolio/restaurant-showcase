🔁 Wood-Fired Hearth Searing

The Philosophy of Farm & Flame

Chef Andres Kaifer believes that exceptional dining begins with live fire and regional ingredients. Our open kitchen centers on a custom wood-fired hearth burning white oak & hickory coals to sear prime meats and roast seasonal coastal seafood.

Hearth Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Henrietta’s Culinary Craft

1. Live Oak Wood Flames

Hardwood embers generate intense 800°F radiant heat, caramelizing protein crusts while keeping interiors tender.

2. Carolinas Producer Sourcing

We partner directly with North Carolina organic farms, heritage livestock raisers, & Outer Banks fishermen.

3. Daily Handmade Pasta

Our culinary team hand-rolls egg pasta & delicate ricotta gnocchi every morning using imported Italian semolina flour.
