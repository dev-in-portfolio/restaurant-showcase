## Original Audit Weakness

Henrietta’s relies on basic third-party reservation links and press highlights. Located at 117 Southside Dr in LoSo, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its seasonal Chef Andres Kaifer culinary craft, wood-fired hearth technique, private dining pavilion, and sommelier wine program.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Lower South End (LoSo) Location:** 117 Southside Drive, Charlotte NC 28217 (Adjacent to Rally).
2. **Chef-Driven Craft Entrees:** Wood-Fired Prime Hanger Steak, Pan-Seared NC Sea Bass, House-Made Ricotta Gnocchi, Artisanal Charcuterie Boards, & Sommelier Wine Pairings.
3. **Hours of Operation:** Mon-Sat 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM, Sun 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **High-Fashion Serif Typography:** High-fashion serif (*Bodoni Moda*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and cellar mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Midnight Navy & Champagne Gold Palette:** High-end bistro palette anchored in midnight navy (`#0B132B`), champagne gold (`#D4AF37`), rosewood burgundy (`#881337`), deep slate (`#1C2541`), and linen parchment (`#FAF8F5`).
3. **"The Chef's Tasting & Wood-Fired Hearth Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`bistro-hearth-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing wood-fired prime meats & coastal seafood alongside house ricotta gnocchi & craft cocktails.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Halal Street Food` — NYC Halal Cart with Outfit fonts and Cart Onyx/Canary Yellow.
2. `Great Wall of China South` — Classic Cantonese Wok with Playfair Display fonts and Jade Onyx/Crimson Red.
3. `El Veneno` — Urban Mexican Street Tacos with Unbounded fonts and Dark Obsidian/Habanero Red.

### Divergent Choices for Henrietta’s

- **Hero Composition:** Contemporary High-End LoSo Bistro & Hearth Gastronomy Vault split-hero layout: left side features a champagne gold stamp badge (`"CHEF ANDRES KAIFER BISTRO • LOWER SOUTH END (LOSO)"`), high-fashion serif (*Bodoni Moda*), midnight navy & champagne gold palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of bistro dining room & wood-fired culinary craft.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Hen Vault Cards** (`hen-vault-card`) and LoSo culinary highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`hen-header`, `hen-brand`, `bistro-hero-stage`, `champagne-stamp-badge`, `hen-vault-card`, `hen-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
