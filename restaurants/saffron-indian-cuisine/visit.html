Transit & Parking

Complimentary Covered Deck & Street Stalls

Arriving at Saffron Indian Cuisine is simple and stress-free:

  • Free Multi-Level Parking Deck: A large, clean, well-lit covered parking deck is located directly adjacent to Ayrsley Town Boulevard. No fees or validation tickets required.
  • Curbside Street Stalls: Plentiful 2-hour parallel street parking available right in front of our entrance for swift lunch arrivals and takeout pickups.
  • Highway Proximity: Located just 1 minute off I-485 Exit 1 (South Tryon Street), easily accessible from Ballantyne, Fort Mill, and Charlotte Douglas Airport.
Corporate & Social Buffets

Office Catering & Wedding Buffets

Planning a corporate luncheon, medical conference, or private celebration? Saffron provides full-service Indian catering trays:

  • Royal Biryani Trays: Half-pan and full-pan portions of Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani and Vegetable Dum Biryani with raita.
  • Curry & Tandoori Combinations: Large chaffing-dish setups of Chicken Tikka Masala, Paneer Tikka Masala, and Tandoori Chicken.
  • Direct Booking: Contact our banquet manager at (980) 297-7722 with 24 to 48 hours notice for customized catering packages.
