Tandoori Clay Mastery

Blistered Tandoori Chicken & Mint Raita

Our tandoori chicken marinates for 24 hours in whole-milk yogurt, fresh lime juice, crushed fenugreek leaves (kasoori methi), and house-roasted garam masala. Placed into the cylindrical clay tandoor, the intense radiant heat seals in juices while crisping the spice crust. Served sizzling with raw red onions, lemon, and cool cucumber mint raita.

Baked To Order

Garlic & Saffron Butter Naan

Nothing compares to Indian bread served seconds after it leaves the clay oven. Stretched by hand into tear-drop rounds, our naan bakes directly against the ceramic tandoor wall, forming delicate air pockets and charred blister bubbles before being brushed with melted ghee, minced garlic, and fresh cilantro.
