Culinary Principles

The Craft of Curries & Clay Wall Naan

Handmade daily without artificial thickeners, starches, or canned gravies.

Craft 01

Caramelized Onion Gravy (Bhuna)

The secret to our curries is the slow browning of finely chopped Spanish onions for over two hours. As the natural sugars caramelize to deep mahogany, ginger, garlic, and fresh tomato paste are introduced to create a rich, silky foundation without heavy starches.

Craft 02

Clay-Wall Naan Physics

Our naan dough rests overnight to develop airy gluten structures. When hand-stretched and slapped onto the vertical 800-degree ceramic tandoor wall with a cloth pad, trapped steam expands rapidly, creating charred golden blisters and pillowy crumb.

Craft 03

Ancient Plant-Forward Legacy

India boasts the world's most sophisticated vegetarian culinary heritage. From slow-simmered Punjabi Channa Masala and creamy Dal Makhani to fresh spinach Palak Paneer, our vegetarian and vegan entrées are prepared in dedicated vegetarian cookware.

The Royal Feast

Chicken Tikka Masala: A Global Masterpiece

Our Chicken Tikka Masala is the gold standard of Indian dining in Charlotte. We marinate chicken breast in spiced yogurt, char-roast it over hot coals until smoky, and finish it in a velvety simmer of vine-ripened tomatoes, sweet cream, pure butter, ginger julienne, and crushed fenugreek leaves. Every bite honors royal Mogul heritage.
