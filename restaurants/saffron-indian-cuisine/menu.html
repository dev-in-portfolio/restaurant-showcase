Royal Curries & Simmered Gravies

All curries served with complimentary fragrant saffron basmati rice. Spice levels customized to your preference: Mild, Medium, Hot, or Desi Spicy.

Chicken Tikka Masala $18

Tandoor-charred chicken breast morsels simmered in a velvety tomato-cream gravy with caramelized onions, freshly ground coriander, garam masala, and fragrant fenugreek leaves.

Lamb Rogan Josh $21

A regal Kashmiri specialty: tender boneless lamb slow-braised for hours in rich yogurt, browned onions, whole black cardamom, fennel seeds, and sun-dried Kashmiri chilies.

Classic Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani) $19

Shredded tandoori chicken simmered in a silky, mildly sweet sauce made with vine-ripened tomatoes, sweet cream, pure butter, honey, and crushed kasoori methi.

Goan Chicken Vindaloo $18

Fiery Portuguese-influenced Goan curry cooked with tender chicken, tender red potatoes, malt vinegar, crushed whole spices, and toasted red chilies.

Traditional Goat Curry $22

Bone-in young goat pieces slow-cooked in a robust home-style brown gravy with ginger slivers, garlic, ground turmeric, and aromatic star anise.

Coastal Prawn Korma $22

Jumbo tiger prawns simmered gently in a fragrant white korma sauce infused with crushed cashews, coconut cream, green cardamom, and golden saffron threads.

Tandoori Clay Oven Specialties

Marinated for 24 hours in spiced yogurt and roasted over hot charcoal in our cylindrical clay tandoor. Served on a sizzling platter with mint raita.

Saffron Tandoori Chicken $19

Bone-in chicken legs and thighs scored and marinated in whole milk yogurt, lemon juice, Kashmiri chili, ginger, and roasted tandoori masala, charred over hot coals.

Chicken Malai Tikka $18

Supreme tender chicken breast cubes marinated in heavy cream, soft cream cheese, green cardamom, white pepper, and roasted garlic, gently roasted for melt-in-your-mouth succulence.

Tandoori Lamb Chops $26

Prime center-cut lamb chops marinated in raw papaya, roasted cumin, malt vinegar, and crushed black pepper, flame-seared medium rare with blistered exterior.

Paneer Tikka Shashlik $17

Handmade Indian farmer's cheese cubes skewered with bell peppers and Spanish onions, marinated in ajwain seed yogurt and charred crisp along the edges.

Vegetarian & Vegan Specialties

Celebrating centuries of plant-forward Indian cooking. Prepared in dedicated vegetarian cookware.

Paneer Tikka Masala $17

Char-grilled tandoori paneer cubes folded into our signature spiced tomato-cream tikka sauce with sautéed bell peppers and sweet onions.

Punjabi Channa Masala (Vegan) $15

Tender chickpeas simmered in a dark, tangy Punjabi gravy flavored with dried mango powder (amchur), roasted cumin, ginger julienne, and black tea leaves.

Dal Makhani Royal $16

Whole black lentils and red kidney beans slow-simmered overnight on low embers with fresh cream, butter, and mild spices for an impossibly creamy texture.

Aloo Gobi Matar (Vegan) $15

Golden potato cubes, fresh cauliflower florets, and sweet green peas stir-fried dry with toasted cumin seeds, fresh ginger, turmeric, and fresh cilantro.

Tandoor Naan & Fragrant Dum Biryanis

Stretched and slapped onto clay walls fresh to order; biryanis slow-steamed under dough seals.

Fresh Garlic Naan $4.50

Fluffy leavened flatbread baked on the clay wall, generously topped with minced roasted garlic, fresh cilantro, and brushed with melted butter.

Saffron Butter Naan $4.00

Traditional white flour naan infused with saffron butter and baked to a golden puff with delicate char marks.

Hyderabadi Chicken Dum Biryani $18

Long-grain saffron basmati rice layered with spiced marinated chicken, fried onions, mint, and saffron milk, sealed and slow-steamed (dum pukht). Served with raita and mirchi ka salan.

Vegetable Dum Biryani $16

Seasonal vegetables, paneer cubes, and basmati rice infused with whole warm spices, caramelized shallots, and fragrant rosewater.
