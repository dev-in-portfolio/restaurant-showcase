# Evidence & Design Ledger for Tima African Restaurant

## Creative Brief
- **Client Identity:** Tima African Restaurant, located at 4438 The Plaza, Charlotte, NC 28215 in East Charlotte.
- **Concept:** Premier authentic West African restaurant specializing in Senegalese, Ivorian, and Guinean cuisine. Signature specialties include Thieboudienne (Ceebu Jën national dish with broken jasmine rice and seasoned fish), wood-charred Dibi Lamb with caramelized mustard onions, Yassa lemon-onion chicken, rich Mafé peanut butter stew, steamed fermented cassava attiéké with whole crispy tilapia, and fresh hibiscus Bissap.
- **Target Audience:** African diaspora community, adventurous food explorers seeking authentic West African flavors, local families, and event hosts ordering large-scale catering for celebrations and gatherings.
- **Design Language:** Deep Sahel Earth #12100E, Senegalese Amber Gold #E0A926, Savanna Crimson #B93822, Baobab Green #2E6038, Warm Ochre #D97724, and Sand Cream #FAF7F2. African textile-inspired subtle geometry, warm golden glowing accents, mobile drawer, and interactive West African Event Catering Calculator.

## Add-On Preservation
- **Interactive West African Feast Calculator:** Allows guests to configure event party sizes (10 to 200 guests), choose feast tiers (Teranga Street Box, Dakar Royal Banquet, Grand West African Celebration), add extra pounds of Dibi Lamb, add trays of Jollof Rice, add barrels of Bissap juice, and generate real-time pricing and pre-filled email orders.
- **Interactive Category Filter:** Live tab switching across All, National Dishes (Thieboudienne & Jollof), Charred Dibi & Grills, Slow-Simmered Stews (Mafé & Yassa), and Appetizers, Sides & Drinks on menu.html.
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Expanding answers covering spicy customization, gluten-friendly cassava and rice options, catering order lead times, and halal-certified meats on contact.html.

## Cross-Demo Diversity
- **Namespace:** .tim-* exclusively used across HTML, CSS, and JS.
- **Distinct Aesthetic:** Rich West African amber gold, savanna crimson, and baobab green color palette, with bespoke typography and layout distinctly differing from European lounges, Latin fondas, and American burger/pizza showcases.
