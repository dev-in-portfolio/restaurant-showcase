Our Roots & Journey

The Tima Family Story

Sharing the timeless warmth, aromas, and culinary richness of West Africa with the Queen City.

Bringing Dakar to Charlotte

Food in West Africa is an act of love and community. Families gather around large shared platters, enjoying rich flavors built from hours of patient simmering, hand-pounded spices, and wood-charred grills.

Tima African Restaurant was founded at 4438 The Plaza to preserve and celebrate these vibrant traditions. We cook from scratch each morning, preparing fresh pots of Thieboudienne, grilling savory Dibi lamb cutlets, and stewing rich peanut Mafé just like in our mothers' kitchens back in Dakar and Abidjan.

Core Principles

The Standard of Our Kitchen

Uncompromising Authenticity

We never cut corners on ingredients. From broken jasmine rice to African tamarind, scotch bonnet peppers, and palm oil, our pantry uses genuine ingredients.

Halal & Fresh Quality

All meats are 100% certified Halal, carefully butchered and marinated with garlic, ginger, and Dijon mustard before hitting the open flame.

Community Gathering Hub

Our dining room is a welcoming hub for people of all backgrounds to connect, celebrate cultural milestones, and enjoy generous hospitality.

Traditional Refreshment

House-Brewed Bissap & Pastelles

Pair any dish with our refreshing ruby-red hibiscus Bissap juice or zesty ginger pineapple drink, accompanied by crispy handmade fish pastelles with spicy tomato dip.
