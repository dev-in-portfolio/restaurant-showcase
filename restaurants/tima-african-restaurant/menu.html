Tima African Menu

Experience traditional Senegalese national dishes, charcoal-grilled meats, slow-cooked peanut stews, crispy whole fish, and artisanal juices.

Thieboudienne (Ceebu Jën Rouge) .99

Senegal's iconic national dish. Red broken jasmine rice slow-cooked in spiced tomato and herb broth, accompanied by seasoned whole fish, braised cassava, carrots, cabbage, and spicy tamarind relish.

Senegalese Classic Generous Plate
Ceebu Yapp (Beef Jollof Rice) .50

Fragrant caramelized onion-tomato jollof rice simmered with tender braised beef chunks, roasted sweet peppers, whole garlic cloves, and a side of spicy scotch bonnet puree.

Customer Favorite Tender Beef
Ceebu Guinar (Chicken Jollof Rice) .50

Savory spiced West African jollof rice served with seasoned roasted chicken leg quarter, braised vegetables, and sweet fried plantains.

Jollof Rice Roasted Chicken
Dibi Lamb (Dibi Agneau) .99

Charcoal-grilled marinated lamb cutlets seasoned with Dijon mustard and Maggi spices, piled high with sweet caramelized onions. Served with choice of fried plantains or French baguette.

Signature Dish Charcoal Grilled
Dibi Chicken (Dibi Poulet) .50

Grilled bone-in chicken charred over wood fire, tossed in seasoned Dijon mustard onion relish. Served with spicy dipping sauce and fried plantains.

Wood Charred Dijon Onions
Grilled Whole Tilapia & Attiéké .99

Whole fresh tilapia scored, spiced with ginger and garlic, fried extra crispy, and served with steamed fermented cassava couscous (attiéké) and diced tomato onion relish.

Ivorian Icon Whole Fish
Mafé (Peanut Butter Beef Stew) .50

Rich, velvety peanut butter and tomato stew simmered for hours with tender beef chunks, sweet potatoes, and carrots. Served over a generous mound of white jasmine rice.

Comfort Food Peanut Stew
Yassa Poulet (Lemon Onion Chicken) .50

Marinated grilled chicken braised in a luscious sauce of slow-caramelized onions, freshly squeezed lemon juice, garlic, and Dijon mustard. Served with jasmine rice.

Tangy & Savory Lemon Onion Gravy
Supu Kandja (Okra & Seafood Palm Stew) .50

Traditional West African red palm oil stew packed with fresh minced okra, smoked fish, shrimp, and tender beef cubes. Deep, savory, and richly satisfying.

Okra & Palm Oil Smoked Seafood
Pastelles (Senegalese Fish Pies - 4 pc) .50

Crispy handmade golden pastry turnovers stuffed with seasoned minced fish, garlic, and scallions. Served with our signature spicy tomato-onion dipping sauce.

Top Appetizer Spicy Tomato Dip
Alloco (Sweet Fried Plantains) .00

Ripe yellow plantains sliced and fried to caramelized golden perfection. Crisp on the outside and velvety sweet inside.

Sweet & Crisp Vegetarian
House-Brewed Bissap (Hibiscus Juice) .50

Freshly steeped organic hibiscus flowers sweetened with pure cane sugar and infused with garden mint and vanilla essence. Served ice-cold.

House Brewed Hibiscus & Mint
Ginger Pineapple Juice (Djimbre) .50

Pressed fresh ginger root blended with sweet pineapple juice and lime. Zesty, fiery, and deeply revitalizing.
