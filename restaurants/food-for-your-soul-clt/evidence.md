## Original Audit Weakness

Food For Your Soul CLT relies on basic ordering templates and social pages. Located at 2200 Thrift Rd in Charlotte, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its 12-hour gravy simmering process, secret seasoned cornmeal fish dredge, Sunday soul dinner tradition, and corporate office catering directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Thrift Road Location:** 2200 Thrift Rd, Charlotte NC 28208 (Wesley Heights / Enderly Park Corridor).
2. **Smothered Soul Entrees:** Smothered Pork Chops in Brown Onion Gravy, Crispy Golden Fried Catfish Fillets, Hand-Breaded Fried Chicken, Slow-Cooked Collard Greens with Smoked Turkey, & Four-Cheese Baked Mac & Cheese.
3. **Scratch Sides & Sweets:** Sweet Yam Casserole, Skillet Cornbread, Peach Cobbler with Warm Cinnamon Syrup, & Sweet Iced Tea.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Rich Warm Editorial Serif Typography:** Warm editorial serif (*Playfair Display*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and kitchen mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Cast-Iron Charcoal & Burnt Molasses Palette:** High-energy Southern soul palette anchored in cast-iron charcoal (`#110E0C`), burnt molasses amber (`#B45309`), fried bronze (`#D97706`), collard emerald (`#15803D`), and buttermilk parchment (`#FFFDF5`).
3. **"The Cast-Iron Skillet & Smothered Gravy Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`soul-gravy-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing smothered chops alongside cornmeal catfish, four-cheese mac, & peach cobbler.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Fonda Las Cazuelas` — Earthenware Clay Pot with Fraunces fonts and Earthen Charcoal/Terracotta Rust.
2. `Flip-a-Lo’s` — High-Velocity Buffalo Wing with Space Grotesk fonts and Midnight Charcoal/Cayenne Red.
3. `Fiesta Maya Mexican Restaurant` — Opulent Mayan Stone with Playfair Display fonts and Deep Obsidian/Mayan Jade.

### Divergent Choices for Food For Your Soul CLT

- **Hero Composition:** Warm Southern Heritage & Slow-Smothered Soul Food Vault split-hero layout: left side features a rich bronze-gold stamp badge (`"AUTHENTIC SOUTHERN SOUL FOOD • 2200 THRIFT RD • WESLEY HEIGHTS"`), rich warm editorial serif (*Playfair Display*), cast-iron charcoal & burnt molasses palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of golden fried chicken, mac & cheese, collards, & peach cobbler.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Food For Your Soul Vault Cards** (`soul-vault-card`) and Wesley Heights culinary highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`soul-header`, `soul-brand`, `molasses-hero-stage`, `bronze-stamp-badge`, `soul-vault-card`, `soul-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
