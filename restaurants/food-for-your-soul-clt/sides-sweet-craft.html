🧀 Scratch Southern Baking & Sides

The Art of Southern Sides

In a real Southern soul kitchen, the sides are just as essential as the meat. We bake our four-cheese macaroni with real cream & sharp cheddar, simmer collards with smoked turkey legs, & bake fresh peach cobblers daily.

Side Craft

The 3 Pillars of Food For Your Soul Sides

1. Four-Cheese Baked Custard

Sharp cheddar, smoked gouda, monterey jack, & parmesan folded into heavy cream macaroni & baked until bubbly golden.

2. Smoked Turkey Collard Broth

Hand-cleaned fresh greens simmered slow with smoked turkey leg stock, apple cider vinegar, & hot pepper flakes.

3. Butter Lattice Peach Cobbler

Sweet peaches baked under golden flaky butter pastry crust, finished with warm brown sugar & vanilla cinnamon glaze.
