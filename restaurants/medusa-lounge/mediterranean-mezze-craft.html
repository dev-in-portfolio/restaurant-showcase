🍓 Ancient Culinary Tradition

The Art of Mediterranean Mezze

Mezze is more than a meal—it is a Mediterranean ritual of sharing, conversation, & vibrant flavors. At Medusa Lounge, our culinary team hand-prepares hummus, sears fresh halloumi cheese, & grills lamb kebabs over natural charcoal briquettes daily.

Culinary Science

The 3 Pillars of Medusa Mezze Craft

1. Open Charcoal Flame Grilling

Charbroiling marinated lamb, chicken, & steak kebabs over natural hardwood coals for smoky depth.

2. Scratch Hummus & Dip Whipping

Blending chickpeas, roasted garlic, sesame tahini, & extra virgin olive oil smooth every afternoon.

3. Authentic Greek & Levantine Spices

Importing wild oregano, sumac berries, za'atar, Aleppo pepper, & Cretan extra virgin olive oils.
