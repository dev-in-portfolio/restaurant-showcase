## Original Audit Weakness

Medusa Lounge relies on basic social media profiles and directory listings. Located at 2509 N Davidson St in NoDa, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its upscale Mediterranean culinary offerings, artisanal cocktail program, VIP table lounges, and NoDa directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **NoDa Location:** 2509 N Davidson St, Charlotte NC 28205 (NoDa / North Davidson district).
2. **Mediterranean Lounge Specialties:** Signature Mediterranean Mezze Platters (Charbroiled Lamb Kebabs, Creamy Hummus Trio with Garlic Herb Pita, Grilled Halloumi Cheese, Crisp Crispy Falafel Bowls, Honey Baklava, Craft Velvet Cocktails, & Premium Hookah Blends).
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 533-1345; Mon–Thu 4:00 PM – 12:00 AM | Fri–Sat 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM | Sun 4:00 PM – 12:00 AM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Sleek Luxury Display Typography:** Luxury display serif (*Cormorant Garamond*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and Mediterranean lounge mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Obsidian Midnight & Gold Palette:** Upscale Mediterranean lounge palette anchored in deep obsidian midnight void (`#09090B`), lustrous Mediterranean gold (`#F59E0B`), royal amethyst violet (`#7E22CE`), and warm champagne cream (`#FAFAF9`).
3. **"The Mediterranean Mezze & Craft Velvet Lounge Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`mediterranean-mezze-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing Mediterranean lamb kebabs alongside craft velvet cocktails & premium hookah offerings.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Medellín Colombian Restaurant` — Paisa Valley & Emerald Sunlit Hacienda with Playfair Display fonts and Deep Emerald/Mango Gold.
2. `MAS Tortilleria` — Oaxaca Earth with Plus Jakarta Sans fonts and Volcanic Clay Terracotta/Maize Gold.
3. `Lorenzo’s Pizzeria` — Brooklyn Stone Deck with Outfit fonts and Deep Forest Pine/Terracotta Orange.

### Divergent Choices for Medusa Lounge

- **Hero Composition:** Mediterranean Midnight Obsidian & Velvet Gold Lounge Vault split-hero layout: left side features a Medusa Serpent Crown stamp badge (`"MEDITERRANEAN MEZZE & CRAFT VELVET LOUNGE • NODA CHARLOTTE NC"`), sleek luxury serif (*Cormorant Garamond*), deep obsidian & gold palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of craft velvet cocktails.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Medusa Vault Cards** (`medusa-vault-card`) and NoDa nightlife highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`medusa-header`, `medusa-brand`, `obsidian-hero-stage`, `serpent-crown-badge`, `medusa-vault-card`, `medusa-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
