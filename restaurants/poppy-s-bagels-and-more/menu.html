Bagels & Breakfast Kettle Craft

The Classic Nova Lox Bagel #1 Signature

Fresh kettle-boiled Everything bagel loaded with velvety whipped scallion cream cheese, thin-sliced cold-smoked Nova Scotia salmon, sliced ripe beefsteak tomato, red onion rings, and brined capers.

The Jersey Taylor Ham Pork Roll NYC Breakfast Icon

Crisp griddled original Taylor Ham pork roll layered with two fried farm eggs and melted American cheese on a toasted plain or egg bagel with salt, pepper, and ketchup (SPK).

Bacon, Egg & Sharp Cheddar Breakfast Favorite

Thick-cut applewood smoked bacon, two scrambled cage-free eggs, and melted Vermont sharp cheddar served warm on a toasted Jalapeño Cheddar bagel.

Baker's Dozen Bagel Box Morning Box

13 fresh-baked kettle-boiled bagels of your choice (Plain, Everything, Sesame, Poppyseed, Asiago, Cinnamon Raisin, Onion) with two 8oz tubs of house-whipped cream cheese.

Hot Deli & Fish Carving Board

Hot NY Pastrami on Seeded Rye Deli Legend

Half-pound of steaming, peppery cured beef pastrami hand-carved hot and served on double-baked seeded rye bread with spicy brown deli mustard and a kosher dill pickle spear.

The Classic Corned Beef Reuben Hot Griddled

Hot cured corned beef brisket piled high with melted imported Swiss cheese, barrel-cured sauerkraut, and tangy Russian dressing pressed on buttered seeded rye.

Smoked Whitefish Salad Platter Appetizing Classic

House-whipped Great Lakes smoked whitefish salad blended with fresh dill and celery, served open-faced on a toasted sesame bagel with tomato, red onion, and cucumber.

Poppy’s House Roasted Turkey Club Deli Favorite

Oven-roasted breast of turkey, crispy applewood bacon, green leaf lettuce, beefsteak tomato, and house herb mayonnaise on your choice of bagel or toasted sourdough.
