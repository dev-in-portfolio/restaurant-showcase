🥐 100% Kettle-Boiled & Stone Hearth-Baked

The Real New York Kettle Method

There are no shortcuts to true New York bagels. Commercial bakeries inject steam into ovens—we do it the century-old way. We kettle-boil our proofed dough rings in malted boiling water before transferring them onto wet cedar baking boards and searing them directly on stone hearth decks.

Bakery Mastery

The 3 Pillars of Poppy’s Bagel & Deli Craft

1. Malted Water Kettle Boiling

Boiling gelatinizes the outer dough starches, locking in internal moisture and giving our bagels their distinctive glossy shine and firm, chewy bite.

2. Stone Deck Hearth Baking

Flipping bagels off soaked cedar planks directly onto blazing hot stone oven decks to create a blistered, crisp bottom and golden top.

3. Steam Table Deli Carving

Holding whole cured pastrami and corned beef briskets in rolling steam cabinets and slicing thick, juicy ribbons fresh to order.
