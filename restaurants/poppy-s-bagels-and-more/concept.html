🏛 The Poppy’s Bagels Story

New York Roots, Charlotte Pride.

Poppy’s Bagels & More was founded in 2010 by a passionate family from New York who missed the unmistakable chew, blistered crust, and rich aroma of real kettle-boiled bagels. Bringing traditional recipes, hearth ovens, and steam-table pastrami to Charlotte, Poppy's has grown into a Queen City breakfast landmark.

Our Guiding Ethos

The Values of Poppy’s Bagels

Authentic Kettle Boiling

Refusing commercial steam injection ovens and staying true to copper kettles, burlap boards, and stone deck hearth baking.

Generous NYC Portions

Piling hot pastrami, corned beef, egg sandwiches, and whipped schmears high, just like the legendary delis of Brooklyn and Manhattan.

Neighborhood Family

Knowing our regulars by name and orders by heart across our Providence Road and South Boulevard community bakeries.
