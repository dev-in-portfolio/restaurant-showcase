The 3 Pillars of Poppy’s Catering Service

1. Premium Nova Scotia Lox

Cold-smoked Atlantic salmon cured with brown sugar and sea salt, thinly sliced and paired with tomatoes, capers, cucumbers, and Bermuda red onion.

2. Executive Bagel Platters

Arranging assortments of sliced kettle bagels, seasonal fruit bowls, hot breakfast wraps, and whipped cream cheese tubs for seamless corporate meetings.

3. Scratch Whipped Schmears

Blending real cream cheese with fresh chives, garden vegetables, diced jalapeños, smoked lox, and honey pecans daily in our bakery kitchen.
