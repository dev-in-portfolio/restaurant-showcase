Our Heritage
Clifton & Sharon • Over 35 Years of Craft
A Culinary Journey

The Freshwater Story

Guided by family tradition, genuine Creole recipes, and scratch kitchen execution.

1980s Roots

Southern Sensation

Clifton and Sharon Freshwater opened their first kitchen in New Jersey, quickly building a devoted following for scratch soul food and Louisiana seafood.

2015 Charlotte Arrival

Fourth Ward Flagship

Encouraged by loyal patrons relocating south, the Freshwaters moved to Charlotte and opened their 516 N Graham St restaurant in Uptown Fourth Ward.

Enduring Mission

Pure Hospitality

Operating under the philosophy of treating every guest like family and cooking every roux from scratch with love and patient attention.

A Little Bit of South, Up North

Transported to the French Quarter

Step inside our dining room on North Graham Street and you are immediately welcomed by the aroma of simmering dark roux, warm peach cobbler, and Louisiana spices. We take pride in being a place where neighbors connect and celebrate life's special moments.
