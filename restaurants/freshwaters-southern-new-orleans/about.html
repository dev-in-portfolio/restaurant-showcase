From New Orleans to the Queen City

The Freshwater Family Tradition

Freshwaters was born from Cliff and Rosa Freshwater's dedication to keeping true Creole recipes alive. In Louisiana and the American South, cooking is not just sustenance—it is a labor of love passed down through family kitchens.

When Cliff perfected his recipe for "New Orleans" Catfish—crispy, golden fillets smothered in a velvety brown sauce studded with sautéed Gulf shrimp—he created a Charlotte culinary landmark. Behind every pot of gumbo is a slow-stirred roux cooked to a deep chocolate brown, and behind every side of collard greens is hours of simmering with smoked turkey and spices.

We take pride in feeding our Fourth Ward neighbors, out-of-town visitors, and generations of loyal families who know that real soul food cannot be rushed.
