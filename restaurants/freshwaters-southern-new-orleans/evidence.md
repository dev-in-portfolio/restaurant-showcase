- **Concept & Cuisine:** Beloved Fourth Ward Charlotte destination combining authentic New Orleans Cajun/Creole culinary traditions with classic Southern soul food. Renowned for crispy golden catfish topped with savory shrimp cream sauce, dark roux Louisiana seafood gumbo, crawfish étouffée, tender BBQ ribs, buttermilk southern fried chicken, scratch-made collard greens, baked macaroni and cheese, candied yams, red beans and rice, and warm homemade peach cobbler.
- **Design Philosophy:** Louisiana French Quarter warmth meets classic Southern hospitality. Deep brick crimson, antique bayou gold, warm cypress green accents, and aged cream parchment paper textures. Evocative food storytelling, high-readability classic typography, zero emojis, and robust interactive catering estimation.

## Claim Ledger
- **Signature "New Orleans" Catfish:** Golden cornmeal-crusted farm-raised catfish fillet smothered in rich, velvety brown seafood pan gravy loaded with plump sautéed Gulf shrimp.
- **Dark Roux Creole Seafood Gumbo:** Traditional Louisiana slow-stirred dark roux simmered with blue crab, Gulf shrimp, smoked andouille sausage, okra, and holy trinity vegetables over fluffy rice.
- **Soul Food Trinity Sides:** Authentic scratch-made sides cooked slowly from family recipes—tender collard greens seasoned with smoked turkey, four-cheese baked macaroni, and spiced brown sugar candied yams.
- **Warm Homemade Peach Cobbler:** Fresh spiced sliced peaches baked under a flaky golden buttermilk biscuit crust, served warm with vanilla bean ice cream.
- **Family Feasts & Bulk Trays:** Half-pan and full-pan catering for church fellowships, family reunions, and corporate luncheons.
