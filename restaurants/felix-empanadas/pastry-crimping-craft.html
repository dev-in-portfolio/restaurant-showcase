🥐 Argentine Dough & Repulgue Fold

The Art of Pastry Crimping

An authentic empanada starts with the crust. We roll our dough fresh daily using sweet cream butter, fill it with rich braised meats, and seal each edge with traditional hand-folded repulgue crimps.

Pastry Science

The 3 Pillars of Felix Pastry Craft

1. Butter Layered Laminated Dough

Mixed with high-protein flour, cold butter, & sea salt, rested for 12 hours so the crust bakes crisp & flaky.

2. Traditional Hand-Repulgue Sealing

Each flavor has a unique edge crimp fold pattern—braised beef humita, chicken tinga, & ham cheese sealed by hand.

3. High-Heat Convection Golden Bake

Baked at high heat until the egg wash turns deep golden amber while sealing inside rich meat juices.
