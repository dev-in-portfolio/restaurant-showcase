## Original Audit Weakness

Felix Empanadas relies on food hall directory pages and basic Toast ordering tab. Located inside Optimist Hall at 1115 N Brevard St, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its hand-crimping dough technique, authentic Argentine chimichurri pairing craft, Optimist Hall food hall vibe, and bulk party platter order directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Optimist Hall Location:** 1115 N Brevard St, Suite 4, Charlotte NC 28206 (Optimist Hall in Mill District / NoDa Charlotte).
2. **Hand-Crimped Argentine Empanadas:** Braised Argentine Beef Humita, Chipotle Braised Chicken Tinga, Ham & Double Cheese, Sweet Corn Poblano, & Guava Cream Cheese.
3. **House-Made Chimichurri & Sweets:** Fresh garlic parsley chimichurri dip, spicy habanero salsa, alfajores dulce de leche cookies, & yerba mate iced tea.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Warm Energetic Display Sans Typography:** Energetic warm display sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) paired with clean body sans (*Inter*) and food hall mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Terracotta Rust & Chimichurri Emerald Palette:** High-energy Argentine street food palette anchored in terracotta rust (`#9A3412`), chimichurri emerald green (`#166534`), toasted pastry gold (`#D97706`), pampa linen (`#FFFBEB`), and charcoal slate (`#1C1917`).
3. **"The Hand-Crimped Pastry & House Chimichurri Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`pastry-crimping-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing hand-folded empanadas alongside house chimichurri dips, sides, & dulce de leche alfajores.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Exotica Indian Kitchen & Bar` — Royal Indian Fine Dining with Cinzel Decorative fonts and Imperial Navy/Copper Gold.
2. `Everybody Eatz` — Bold Southern Soul Food with Outfit fonts and Cajun Charcoal/Electric Amber.
3. `Emmy Lou's Wine Bar` — Dilworth Velvet Wine Bar with Cormorant Garamond fonts and Velvet Bordeaux/Champagne Gold.

### Divergent Choices for Felix Empanadas

- **Hero Composition:** Warm Vibrant Argentine Street Food & Hand-Crimped Empanada Vault split-hero layout: left side features a sunburst terracotta stamp badge (`"OPTIMIST HALL ARGENTINE EMPANADAS • 1115 N BREVARD ST"`), warm display sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*), terracotta rust & chimichurri emerald palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of golden hand-crimped empanadas, fresh green chimichurri dip, and alfajores inside Optimist Hall.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Felix Empanadas Vault Cards** (`felix-vault-card`) and Optimist Hall stall highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`felix-header`, `felix-brand`, `pampa-hero-stage`, `sunburst-stamp-badge`, `felix-vault-card`, `felix-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
