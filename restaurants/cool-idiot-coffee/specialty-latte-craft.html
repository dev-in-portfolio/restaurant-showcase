Dessert Latte Alchemy

The Science of The Cake Pop Latte

We believe coffee should be fun. Our signature Cake Pop Latte combines precision 9-bar espresso extraction with house-infused vanilla bean syrup, velvet oat milk, micro-aerated cold foam, and a real birthday cake pop garnish.

Craft Alchemy

The 3 Pillars of Cool Idiot Lattes

1. Precision Espresso Extraction

We calibrate grind size and water temperature daily to pull balanced 2:1 ratio double shots with dense crema.

2. Micro-Aerated Cold Foam

Heavy cream and oat milk are whipped cold at high velocity to create dense, velvety foam layers that float perfectly.

3. House-Cooked Syrups

From red velvet cocoa reduction to ube vanilla bean syrup, every flavor component is cooked from real ingredients.
