## Original Audit Weakness

Cool Idiot Coffee relies on basic ordering/square pages (`coolidiotcoffee.com`, `square.site`). Operating inside HopFly Brewing at 1327 S Mint St in South End, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its playful coffee culture, signature dessert-inspired espresso drinks, ceremonial matcha craft, and South End morning pop-up energy.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South End S Mint St Location:** 1327 S Mint St, Charlotte NC 28203 (inside HopFly Brewing Co. in South End).
2. **Signature Specialty Drinks:** Cake Pop Latte with cold foam, Red Velvet Latte, Ube Vanilla Cold Foam, Kyoto cold brew, and hand-squeezed flavored lemonades.
3. **Ceremonial Matcha & Single-Origin Beans:** Kyoto ceremonial grade matcha, roasted houjicha, house pastries, and single-origin retail coffee beans.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Neo-Pop Display Typography:** Expressive neo-pop display sans (*Bricolage Grotesque* / *Outfit*) paired with clean body sans (*Outfit*) and barista recipe mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Cake Pop Magenta & Neon Lavender Palette:** Playful high-vibe palette anchored in dark espresso noir (`#120D1A`), vibrant cake pop magenta (`#D81B60`), electric lavender lime (`#7C4DFF`), soft milk foam cream (`#FAF5FF`), and South End mint (`#00E676`).
3. **"The Cake Pop Latte & Ceremonial Matcha Science Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`specialty-latte-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing dessert lattes alongside ceremonial teas and house pastries.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Comal Taco Co.` — Sonoran comal taco stall with Syne/Outfit fonts and Habanero Amber.
2. `Coffey Creek Café` — 1987 Southern diner with Lora/Outfit fonts and Copper Chestnut.
3. `Club West Brewing` — Modern brewery taproom with Outfit/Plus Jakarta Sans fonts and Electric Hops Green.

### Divergent Choices for Cool Idiot Coffee

- **Hero Composition:** Playful Neo-Pop Specialty Coffee & South End Espresso Vault split-hero layout: left side features an electric neon lilac & cake pop stamp badge (`"PLAYFUL SPECIALTY COFFEE • SOUTH END 1327 S MINT"`), neo-pop display typography (*Bricolage Grotesque*), cake pop magenta & electric lavender palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of an iced Cake Pop Latte topped with a cake pop, iced matcha, and red velvet cupcakes.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Idiot Vault Cards** (`idiot-vault-card`) and **HopFly Bar Pop-Up Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`idiot-header`, `pop-brand`, `espresso-hero-stage`, `foam-stamp-badge`, `idiot-vault-card`, `idiot-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
