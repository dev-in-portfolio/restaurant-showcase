Charlotte, North Carolina • Established 2021

A Symphony of Belgian Iron & Southern Soul.

The Wafflery was born from a simple realization: breakfast should bring genuine joy. Founded in Charlotte, we set out to honor two iconic morning traditions under one roof—the artisanal perfection of yeast-leavened Brussels waffles and the comforting, hearty warmth of Lowcountry stone-ground grits and buttermilk biscuits.

Our Story
Handcrafted Breakfast & Inclusive Hospitality
Kitchen Principles

Values That Warm Every Morning

Authentic craftsmanship, community connection, and uncompromising quality.

Value 01

Scratch Batters Daily

We never use commercial powdered mixes. Our batters are blended and proofed from raw flour, eggs, buttermilk, real butter, and pure vanilla every sunrise.

Value 02

Radical Vegan Inclusivity

We believe dietary choices should never mean sacrificing breakfast indulgence. Our scratch plant-based waffles and vegan biscuit melts rival any traditional recipe.

Value 03

Queen City Roots

From our 10th Street flagship to Rozzelles Ferry and Docia Crossing, we are proud to energize Charlotte neighbors, families, and morning commuters every day.

Inclusive Hospitality

Plant-Based Without Compromise

Our kitchen takes dietary inclusivity seriously. We developed a proprietary plant-based waffle batter that crisps to perfection on dedicated irons, paired with pure organic maple syrup, fresh berries, and vegan butter. Combined with folded plant eggs and savory vegan country sausage, our plant-based breakfast options are loved by vegans and omnivores alike.
