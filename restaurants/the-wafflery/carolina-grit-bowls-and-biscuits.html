Heirloom Stone-Ground Corn • Lowcountry Heritage

Carolina Grits, Flaky Golden Biscuits.

In the Carolinas, grits are sacred. At The Wafflery, we source heirloom white corn coarse-ground by regional water-powered mills. We simmer them for hours with rich cream and sharp aged cheddar, creating the velvety canvas for wild shrimp, andouille pan gravies, crispy chicken, and golden crab cakes.

Lowcountry Tenets

The Craft Behind Our Savory Bowls

Heirloom milling, slow reduction pan gravies, and buttery laminated doughs.

Tenet 01

Coarse Stone-Ground Milling

We never use instant or quick grits. Our whole grain white corn grits retain their natural corn germ and sweet earthy flavor, requiring patience and continuous stirring.

Tenet 02

Andouille Sausage Gravy

Rendered Louisiana andouille sausage, bell peppers, garlic, and rich stock are simmered down to create a smoky, peppery glaze that clings perfectly to sweet wild shrimp.

Tenet 03

Laminated Buttermilk Biscuits

Grated frozen butter is folded repeatedly into chilled buttermilk dough to form dozens of flaky, tender layers that rise tall and golden in our morning ovens.

Handheld Excellence

The Fiery Hot Honey Chicken Biscuit

For those craving savory morning heat on the go, our hot honey chicken biscuit is unmatched. A colossal hand-breaded fried chicken tender dipped in house-infused habanero chili honey, balanced with tangy crinkle-cut pickles inside a warm, buttery biscuit.
