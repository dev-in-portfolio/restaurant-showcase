Operating Hours

Weekly Breakfast Schedule

Our kitchen fires up the waffle irons early every morning:

  • Monday – Friday: 7:00 AM – 2:00 PM (Early commuter rush & casual weekday breakfast)
  • Saturday – Sunday: 7:00 AM – 3:00 PM (Extended weekend brunch service with full kitchen menu)
  • Walk-Ins & Ordering: Walk-ins are always welcomed on a first-come basis. Carryout orders can be placed directly at our front counters.
Morning Group Gatherings

Catering & Waffle Bar Pop-Ups

Bring Charlotte's favorite breakfast to your next corporate meeting, family gathering, or weekend event:

  • Build-Your-Own Waffle Bars: Mini Belgian waffle platters with hot fried chicken tenders, fresh fruit toppings, and gourmet flavored syrups.
  • Grit Bowl Chafing Stations: Warm stone-ground Carolina grits with shrimp, andouille gravy, and cheese toppings.
  • Morning Beverage Dispensers: Large-format dispensers of 18-hour cold brew coffee, Southern sweet peach tea, and strawberry lemonade.
