The Elements of Our Waffle Craft

Technique, temperature precision, and pure Southern flavor pairings.

Element 01

Yeast-Leavened Batters

Unlike standard baking powder diners, our traditional Brussels-style batter undergoes slow fermentation to unlock subtle malted vanilla notes and an extraordinarily light texture.

Element 02

Buttermilk Fried Chicken

Our chicken tenders and wings soak in seasoned cultured buttermilk for 24 hours before being dredged in seasoned flour and fried to an audibly crisp, juicy golden finish.

Element 03

House-Crafted Syrups

We simmer our own spiced maples, brown sugar bourbon caramels, and real Georgia peach reductions daily to complement every waffle pocket.

Signature Pairing

The Iconic Chicken & Waffles

A masterclass in Southern balance: steaming golden waffle grids crisp enough to catch every drop of warm spiced syrup, crowned with piping-hot buttermilk fried chicken and a generous quenelle of whipped honey butter. Sweet, savory, buttery, and crunch in every single forkful.
