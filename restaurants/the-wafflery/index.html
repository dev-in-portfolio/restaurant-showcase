Carolina Classic

Signature Shrimp & Grits Bowl

Our hallmark savory centerpiece begins with heirloom Carolina stone-ground white corn grits simmered low and slow with aged cheddar and sweet cream. Topped with wild-caught jumbo shrimp sautéed in savory andouille sausage gravy, crumbled applewood bacon, and chopped garden green onions.

18-Hour Steeped Cold Brew

We brew our custom-roasted coffee beans over eighteen slow hours to achieve a velvet-smooth, chocolate-forward cold brew with minimal acidity. Served over crystal ice or enjoyed alongside fresh-squeezed strawberry lemonade and Southern peach sweet tea.
