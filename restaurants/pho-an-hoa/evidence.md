## Original Audit Weakness

Pho An Hoa (Phở Hoà) on Central Avenue is one of the most beloved and enduring Vietnamese noodle houses in the Queen City, with generations of diners visiting for steaming healing bowls of pho and crispy spring rolls. However, its previous online presence lacked a dedicated, modern multi-page responsive website presenting its slow-simmered bone broth craft, fresh herb pairing traditions, vermicelli bowls, and beverage options. This 6-page responsive web experience gives Pho An Hoa a warm, clean, and high-converting digital storefront.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **18-Hour Simmered Bone Broth:** Clear, fragrant beef marrow bone broth simmered with charred aromatics, star anise, cassia bark, and coriander seeds, served piping hot with fresh rice noodles.
2. **Fresh Herb & Meat Customization:** Sliced rare steak that cooks gently in the hot broth, brisket, meatballs, alongside heaping plates of fresh Thai basil, sawtooth culantro (ngò gai), crisp bean sprouts, lime wedges, and sliced jalapeños.
3. **Vietnamese Street Specialties & Refreshments:** Char-grilled pork over rice vermicelli with sweet-savory nuoc cham, crispy pork and taro egg rolls, shrimp summer rolls, and slow-dripped cà phê sữa đá with boba smoothies.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Herbal Amber & Basil Green Palette:** Deep Broth Amber (`#b45309`), Fresh Basil Green (`#15803d`), Rice Paper White (`#ffffff`), Warm Ginger Cream (`#fcfaf5`), and Dark Cardamom Charcoal (`#1c1917`).
2. **Steaming Broth Showcase Architecture:** Herb pairing guides, pho customization charts, and vermicelli salad bowl spotlights.
3. **Refined Organic Typography:** Cormorant Garamond elegant serif display headlines paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for clear bowl sizes, broth choices, and noodle selections.
