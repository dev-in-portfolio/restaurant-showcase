The Phin Filter Ritual

The Craft of Cà Phê Sữa Đá

True Vietnamese iced coffee cannot be rushed. At Pho An Hoa, each glass is brewed individually using coarse dark-roast coffee with chicory notes, extracted drop-by-drop through a traditional stainless steel phin filter into a layer of silky sweetened condensed milk.

  • Intense Bold Roast: Deep chocolate and roasted nut flavor profiles that provide an exhilarating lift.
  • Velvety Sweetness: Rich condensed milk creates a luxurious mouthfeel that balances the robust dark brew.
  • Crushed Ice Finish: Poured over cracked ice for maximum chill and refreshing contrast.
