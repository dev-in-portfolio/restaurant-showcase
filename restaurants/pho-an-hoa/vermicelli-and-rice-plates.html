The Architecture of Bún Bowls

Vietnamese Bún (rice vermicelli) bowls offer a vibrant alternative to hot soups. Served at room temperature, every bowl is layered carefully to deliver contrasting textures and deep aromatics:

  • The Foundation: Fresh, tender rice vermicelli noodles rested on a bed of shredded crisp green leaf lettuce and cucumber ribbons.
  • The Herbal Crown: Fresh Vietnamese mint, cilantro, and crunchy house-pickled daikon and carrots (Đồ Chua).
  • The Flame-Kissed Meats: Lemongrass-marinated pork shoulder, grilled jumbo shrimp, or golden fried egg rolls (Chả Giò).
  • The Dressing: Drizzled with scallion oil (Mỡ Hành), toasted peanuts, and our house-blended garlic chili fish sauce (Nước Chấm).

Comforting Rice Platters

Traditional Cơm Dĩa Rice Plates

For guests seeking a hearty, warm grain meal, our Cơm plates are centered around fragrant jasmine rice served alongside marinated meats seared to order:

  • Cơm Sưỗn Nướng: Caramelized thin pork chops marinated in shallots, five-spice, and garlic, seared over flames.
  • Sunny Fried Egg Option: Add a crispy-edged fried egg with a rich runny yolk that coats the rice.
  • Complimentary Broth: Every rice platter includes a small cup of our light seasoned broth to sip alongside your meal.
