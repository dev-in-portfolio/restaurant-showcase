Takeout Tips

How to Reheat Takeout Pho at Home

Three simple steps to enjoy steaming, restaurant-quality noodle soup at your kitchen table.

Step 1: Boil the Broth

Pour the beef or chicken broth from its container into a saucepan and bring to a rolling boil over medium-high heat.

Step 2: Prepare the Noodles

Place the rice noodles and cooked meats into your serving bowl. If rare steak was ordered, lay the thin raw slices gently atop the noodles.

Step 3: Pour & Garnish

Ladle the boiling broth directly over the steak slices to cook them instantly. Garnish with basil, culantro, sprouts, and jalapeños.
