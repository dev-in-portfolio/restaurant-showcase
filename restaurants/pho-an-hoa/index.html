Generations of Craft

The Soul of Northern & Southern Pho

At Pho An Hoa, every bowl begins before dawn. We combine prime beef knuckles, marrow bones, and brisket, boiling them slowly for 18 hours with charred yellow onions, mature ginger, star anise, black cardamom, cassia bark, and roasted coriander seeds.

  • Uncompromising clarity achieved through meticulous skimming and skimming
  • No shortcuts: zero artificial powders or concentrated bases
  • Served with paper-thin slices of eye of round steak, tender flank, or savory meatballs
  • Heaping garnish platters of fresh herbs customized to your palate

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our noodle preparation, dine-in, and takeout procedures.

Our beef broth is simmered for a full 18 hours using choice marrow bones, charred yellow onions, ginger, star anise, cinnamon, cardamom, and whole coriander. We hand-skim continuously to ensure a crystal-clear, intensely aromatic soup.

To preserve ideal texture, we separate the piping hot broth into sealed soup containers and package fresh noodles, choice meats, herbs, and condiments into separate compartments so your noodles never become soggy during transport.
