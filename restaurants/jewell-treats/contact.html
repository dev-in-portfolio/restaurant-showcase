Pastry Ordering Guidelines

How To Place Your Order

Because we bake strictly from scratch without preservatives, we ask for advance notice to prepare your bespoke order.

Ordering Deadlines:

- Standard Dozens: At least 24 hours in advance.
- Custom Special Orders / Mini Pies: At least 1 week in advance.
- Weddings & Large Tower Buffets: At least 1 month in advance.

Pickup & Delivery Details

  • 1. Curbside Pickup: Call upon arrival at 301 E 4th Street, and our team will bring your boxed cupcakes directly to your vehicle.
  • 2. Charlotte Delivery: Courier delivery available across Mecklenburg County (delivery fee calculated based on distance).
  • 3. Event Setup: On-site tower assembly and display setup available for weddings and corporate events.
