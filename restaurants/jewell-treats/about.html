A Sweet Inspiration

The Gemstone Collection Story

Jewell Treats was founded on a simple yet revolutionary premise: luxury desserts should look like precious gems, taste extraordinary, and contain only clean, natural ingredients.

Inspired by the natural beauty and vivid hues of fine gemstones—rubies, sapphires, diamonds, and jade—we spent years formulating proprietary cupcake recipes using real fruit compotes, vegetable juices for natural pigmentation, and rich European sweet cream butter.

From our bakery in Uptown Charlotte, we have had the honor of sweetening hundreds of weddings, birthday milestones, corporate galas, and quiet family Sunday dinners across North Carolina.
