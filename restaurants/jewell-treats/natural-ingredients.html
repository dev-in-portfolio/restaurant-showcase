Pure Quality & Wholesome Baking

The All-Natural Philosophy

Why we refuse to use artificial dyes, synthetic extracts, or chemical preservatives in our artisan kitchen.

Real Food, Real Flavor

Nature's Own Color Palette

At Jewell Treats, our gorgeous gemstone hues are derived exclusively from nature. We believe you should never have to ingest petroleum-based artificial dyes (like Red 40 or Yellow 5) to enjoy a stunning dessert.

Our Ruby Red Velvet gets its deep crimson from pure organic beet root extract. Our Strawberry Sapphire uses concentrated fresh California strawberry puree. Our Key Lime Jadeite is tinted naturally with organic spinach chlorophyll and lime zest.

No Chemical Preservatives

Our cupcakes are baked fresh to order and contain zero shelf-life extenders. Best enjoyed fresh within 48 hours.

European Sweet Cream Butter

We use high-fat European-style butter for our cake batters and silky smooth buttercreams, producing an unbeatably rich texture.

Madagascar Bourbon Vanilla

Real vanilla beans and cold-extracted pure Madagascar bourbon vanilla—never synthetic vanillin.

Cage-Free Farm Eggs

Fresh farm-sourced eggs that provide natural structure, rich golden color, and delicate sponge texture.
