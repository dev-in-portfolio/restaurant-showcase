Family-Owned • Scratch Baking • Charlotte NC

Family Tradition, Handmade Daily.

Yummm Donuts was founded with a simple, uncompromising promise: bring authentic, old-school scratch donut baking back to the Charlotte community. Every morning before sunrise, our bakers mix, hand-cut, and fry small batches of fresh yeast dough using real dairy, farm-fresh eggs, and unbleached flour.

Our Core Values

The Values Behind Every Glaze

Commitment to scratch freshness, local warmth, and generous community hospitality.

100% Daily Freshness

Never Day-Old

We never sell yesterday’s donuts. Everything in our display cases was fried that very morning. When we sell out of a specialty flavor, it is retired until tomorrow's fresh 5:00 AM batch.

Honest Sourcing

Real Ingredients

We use real butter, pure vanilla extract, unbleached flours, and pure fruit purées. Our glazes and frostings are mixed in-house to achieve clean, vibrant flavors without artificial aftertastes.

Neighborhood Pride

Tyvola Square Roots

We take immense pride in fueling South Charlotte commuters, healthcare workers, educators, and neighborhood families every morning with warm smiles and piping hot donuts.

Morning Routine

Locally Brewed Coffee & Warm Hospitality

A great donut deserves exceptional coffee. We partner with local roasters to brew smooth, rich medium roast drip coffee and iced cold brew daily. Grab a hot cup and a warm glazed donut on your morning commute down Tyvola Road.
