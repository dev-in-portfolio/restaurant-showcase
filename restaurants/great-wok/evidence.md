# Evidence & Creative Brief: Great Wok

## Creative Brief
- **Brand Identity**: Great Wok is a bustling, high-flame Chinese takeout kitchen and fast-casual dining staple located at 718 W Trade St (Suite M) in Uptown Charlotte's Gateway Village corridor. Celebrated for freshly wok-tossed Cantonese and Sichuan classics, generous lunch combo specials, crispy General Tso's chicken, handmade steamed dumplings, sizzling Singapore Mei Fun, and large-format family catering trays.
- **Target Audience**: Uptown Charlotte workers, Johnson & Wales University students, Gateway Village professionals, and neighborhood residents seeking rapid, piping-hot Chinese comfort classics and generous meal combinations.
- **Design Philosophy**: High-energy culinary aesthetic featuring fiery wok crimson (`#dc2626` / `#991b1b`), golden amber (`#f59e0b`), slate charcoal (`#0f172a`), and clean porcelain white. Clear combo selection badges, interactive lunch/party tray estimator, and mobile-first menu navigation.
- **Key Differentiators**: Traditional high-heat "wok hei" stir fry technique; comprehensive combination platters featuring roast pork fried rice and egg rolls; generous party tray sizing; fast carryout pickup counter on West Trade Street.
