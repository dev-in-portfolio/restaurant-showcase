Vietnamese Pho & Street Specialties

Hand-crafted bone broths, warm French baguettes, fresh herb vermicelli bowls, and Phin-drip iced coffee in Plaza Midwood.

Traditional Pho & Noodle Soups Bone Broth

Phở Bò (Beef Special Pho) House Favorite

24-hour beef bone broth served with rice noodles, tender eye round steak, brisket, meatballs, cilantro, Thai basil, & bean sprouts.

Bún Bò Huế (Spicy Beef Noodle) Spicy Central Viet

Bold lemongrass chili beef broth served with round rice noodles, braised beef shank, pork ham, and fresh mint herbs.

Phở Gà (Poached Chicken Pho) Comfort Broth

Clear 12-hour poached chicken bone broth with shredded free-range chicken, ginger, scallions, and rice noodles.

Vegan Mushroom & Tofu Pho 100% Vegan

Aromatic vegetable and shiitake mushroom broth served with organic fried tofu, king oyster mushrooms, and herbs.

Bánh Mì, Bowls & Drinks Baguettes & Coffee

Crispy Pork Belly Bánh Mì Street Classic

Toasted French baguette spread with French liver pâté, roasted pork belly, pickled daikon & carrots, cucumber, & cilantro.

Lemongrass Chicken Vermicelli Bowl Bún Bowl

Warm rice vermicelli noodles topped with chargrilled lemongrass chicken, crispy egg roll, crushed peanuts, & Nước Chấm sauce.

Fresh Shrimp Summer Rolls (2pcs) Fresh Spring Roll

Rice paper wrapped with poached shrimp, pork, rice vermicelli, lettuce, & mint, served with sweet peanut hoisin dip.

Cà Phê Sữa Đá (Vietnamese Iced Coffee) Phin Brewed

Traditional slow-drip French roast coffee brewed over thick sweetened condensed milk and served over crushed ice.
