## Original Audit Weakness

Cilantro Noodle + Kitchen relies on third-party food delivery links (`cilantronoodle.com`, `doordash.com`). Located at 2001 Commonwealth Ave in Plaza Midwood, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its 24-hour marrow bone pho simmer, Plaza Midwood neighborhood vibe, custom vermicelli bowl builder, and Vietnamese coffee bar craft.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Plaza Midwood Commonwealth Location:** 2001 Commonwealth Ave, Charlotte NC 28205 (Plaza Midwood / Commonwealth).
2. **24-Hour Bone Broth Pho & Bánh Mì:** Slow-simmered beef bone marrow Pho Bò, spicy Bún Bò Huế, crispy pork belly & lemongrass chicken Bánh Mì baguettes.
3. **Vermicelli Bowls & Vietnamese Iced Coffee:** Build-your-own rice & vermicelli bowls, fresh shrimp summer rolls, and Phin-drip Vietnamese iced coffee with sweetened condensed milk.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Modern Vietnamese Botanical Typography:** Contemporary botanical display sans (*Outfit*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and kitchen technical mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Saigon Forest & Anise Gold Palette:** Organic Vietnamese palette anchored in deep forest bamboo green (`#1E4D36`), fresh cilantro green (`#2E8B57`), Saigon golden anise (`#E0A838`), rice paper cream (`#FAF8F3`), and bamboo white (`#FFFFFF`).
3. **"The 24-Hour Marrow Bone Simmer & Bánh Mì Baguette Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`pho-simmer-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing artisanal pho broth chemistry alongside French-Vietnamese baguettes.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Chop Chop Red Pot` — Southern smoked street food joint with Outfit/Plus Jakarta Sans fonts.
2. `Choi’s Korea & Wing` — Korean double-fried wing joint with Plus Jakarta Sans/Outfit fonts.
3. `CHNO Coffee Co.` — Modern chemical caffeine lab with Space Grotesk/Outfit fonts.

### Divergent Choices for Cilantro Noodle + Kitchen

- **Hero Composition:** Modern Vietnamese Street Noodle Bar & Fresh Mint Bamboo Vault split-hero layout: left side features a fresh mint green stamp badge (`"VIETNAMESE PHO & STREET NOODLES • PLAZA MIDWOOD"`), botanical display typography (*Outfit*), deep forest bamboo green & gold palette, and right side features a sunlit framed hero image of steaming beef pho and pork belly bánh mì.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Pho Vault Cards** (`pho-vault-card`) and **Vietnamese Noodle Bar Highlights**.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`cilantro-header`, `viet-brand`, `bamboo-hero-stage`, `mint-stamp-badge`, `pho-vault-card`, `cilantro-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
