LoSo Heritage & Heart

Honeybear Bake Shop was founded by Hannah with a simple vision: to craft the most decadent, half-pound stuffed cookies in the Queen City. From our LoSo workshop at 605 Phillip Davis Dr, we bake small batches using pure brown butter & premium chocolates.
